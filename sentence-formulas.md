# Sentence Formulas

A quick-reference list of sentence formulas common in everyday Spanish conversation. Each formula shows the structure, a short note, and one example. This is a standalone reference, not linked into the lesson/MOC/flashcard system.

## Greetings and introductions

- **Me llamo + [name]** — to give your name
  Me llamo Ana. — My name is Ana.
- **¿Cómo te llamas?** — to ask someone's name
  ¿Cómo te llamas? — What's your name?
- **Mucho gusto** — on meeting someone
  Mucho gusto, soy Carlos. — Nice to meet you, I'm Carlos.
- **¿Qué tal?** / **¿Cómo estás?** — to ask how someone is
  ¿Qué tal, todo bien? — How's it going, all good?

## Wants and preferences

- **Quiero + [infinitive/noun]** — to state a want
  Quiero comer algo. — I want to eat something.
- **Me gustaría + [infinitive]** — softer, more polite want
  Me gustaría hablar contigo. — I would like to talk with you.
- **Me gusta(n) + [noun]** — to state a like
  Me gustan las películas de terror. — I like horror movies.
- **Prefiero + [X] a + [Y]** — to state a preference between two things
  Prefiero el té al café. — I prefer tea to coffee.

## Necessity and obligation

- **Tengo que + [infinitive]** — personal obligation
  Tengo que trabajar mañana. — I have to work tomorrow.
- **Hay que + [infinitive]** — general/impersonal obligation
  Hay que llegar temprano. — One has to arrive early.
- **Necesito + [infinitive/noun]** — a need
  Necesito descansar un poco. — I need to rest a bit.

## Ability and permission

- **Puedo + [infinitive]** — ability or permission
  Puedo ayudarte con eso. — I can help you with that.
- **¿Puedes + [infinitive]?** — asking someone to do something
  ¿Puedes cerrar la puerta? — Can you close the door?
- **¿Se puede + [infinitive]?** — asking general permission
  ¿Se puede fumar aquí? — Is smoking allowed here?
- **Sé + [infinitive]** — knowing how to do something
  Sé nadar muy bien. — I know how to swim very well.

## Plans and intentions

- **Voy a + [infinitive]** — near-future plan
  Voy a estudiar esta noche. — I'm going to study tonight.
- **Pienso + [infinitive]** — stated intention
  Pienso viajar el próximo mes. — I'm planning to travel next month.
- **Estoy pensando en + [infinitive]** — a plan still being considered
  Estoy pensando en cambiar de trabajo. — I'm thinking about changing jobs.

## Opinions and agreement

- **Creo que + [clause]** — stating a belief
  Creo que tienes razón. — I think you're right.
- **Me parece que + [clause]** — stating an impression
  Me parece que va a llover. — It seems to me it's going to rain.
- **¿Qué piensas de + [noun]?** / **¿Qué te parece + [noun]?** — asking for an opinion
  ¿Qué te parece el plan? — What do you think of the plan?
- **Estoy de acuerdo** / **No estoy de acuerdo** — agreeing or disagreeing
  Estoy de acuerdo contigo. — I agree with you.
- **Tienes razón** — conceding a point
  Tienes razón, fue mi error. — You're right, it was my mistake.

## Feelings

- **Estoy + [adjective]** — a current state or feeling
  Estoy cansado hoy. — I'm tired today.
- **Me siento + [adjective]** — how you feel
  Me siento mejor ahora. — I feel better now.
- **Tengo ganas de + [infinitive]** — feeling like doing something
  Tengo ganas de salir. — I feel like going out.

## Making suggestions

- **¿Por qué no + [present tense]?** — a suggestion
  ¿Por qué no vamos al cine? — Why don't we go to the movies?
- **¿Qué tal si + [present tense]?** — a casual suggestion
  ¿Qué tal si comemos afuera? — What if we eat outside?
- **Deberías + [infinitive]** — mild advice
  Deberías descansar más. — You should rest more.

## Asking for clarification

- **¿Qué quieres decir?** — asking what someone means
  ¿Qué quieres decir con eso? — What do you mean by that?
- **No entiendo** — signaling confusion
  No entiendo, ¿puedes repetir? — I don't understand, can you repeat?
- **¿Cómo se dice + [word] en español?** — asking for a translation
  ¿Cómo se dice "deadline" en español? — How do you say "deadline" in Spanish?

## Time and frequency

- **Hace + [time] que + [clause]** — how long something has been happening
  Hace dos años que vivo aquí. — I've lived here for two years.
- **Llevo + [time] + [gerund]** — duration of an ongoing action
  Llevo una hora esperando. — I've been waiting for an hour.
- **A veces / Siempre / Nunca + [verb]** — frequency markers
  A veces como fuera los viernes. — Sometimes I eat out on Fridays.

## Giving reasons

- **Porque + [clause]** — giving a reason
  No fui porque estaba enfermo. — I didn't go because I was sick.
- **Es que + [clause]** — softening an excuse or explanation
  Es que no tuve tiempo. — It's just that I didn't have time.

## Comparisons

- **Más + [adjective] + que** — comparing with "more...than"
  Ella es más alta que yo. — She is taller than me.
- **Menos + [adjective] + que** — comparing with "less...than"
  Este libro es menos interesante que el otro. — This book is less interesting than the other.
- **Tan + [adjective] + como** — comparing equality
  Es tan rápido como tú. — He's as fast as you.

## Politeness

- **Perdón por + [noun/infinitive]** — apologizing
  Perdón por llegar tarde. — Sorry for arriving late.
- **Gracias por + [noun/infinitive]** — thanking
  Gracias por tu ayuda. — Thanks for your help.
- **Disculpa, ¿podrías + [infinitive]?** — a polite request
  Disculpa, ¿podrías repetir eso? — Excuse me, could you repeat that?

## Doubt and certainty

- **Estoy seguro/a de que + [clause]** — expressing certainty
  Estoy seguro de que va a funcionar. — I'm sure it's going to work.
- **No sé si + [clause]** — expressing doubt
  No sé si podré ir. — I don't know if I'll be able to go.
- **Dudo que + [subjunctive]** — expressing strong doubt
  Dudo que llegue a tiempo. — I doubt he'll arrive on time.

## Requests and commands

- **¿Me puedes pasar + [noun]?** — asking someone to hand something over
  ¿Me puedes pasar la sal? — Can you pass me the salt?
- **¿Te importa si + [present tense]?** — a polite request that touches the other person
  ¿Te importa si abro la ventana? — Do you mind if I open the window?
- **Favor de + [infinitive]** — a formal instruction
  Favor de esperar aquí. — Please wait here.
- **[Imperative], por favor** — a direct but polite request
  Cierra la puerta, por favor. — Close the door, please.

## Recent and habitual actions

- **Acabo de + [infinitive]** — something just finished
  Acabo de llegar a casa. — I just got home.
- **Suelo + [infinitive]** — a habit or usual practice
  Suelo levantarme a las seis. — I usually get up at six.
- **Vuelvo a + [infinitive]** — doing something again
  Vuelvo a intentarlo mañana. — I'll try it again tomorrow.
- **Sigo + [gerund]** — an action still in progress
  Sigo esperando su respuesta. — I'm still waiting for their reply.

## Possibility and hypotheticals

- **Puede que + [subjunctive]** — expressing possibility
  Puede que llueva más tarde. — It might rain later.
- **Si + [present], + [present/future]** — a real, likely condition
  Si tienes tiempo, llámame. — If you have time, call me.
- **Si + [imperfect subjunctive], + [conditional]** — a hypothetical condition
  Si tuviera dinero, viajaría más. — If I had money, I would travel more.
- **Ojalá que + [subjunctive]** — a hope or wish
  Ojalá que todo salga bien. — I hope everything turns out well.

## Cause and effect

- **Por eso + [clause]** — stating a result
  Perdí el autobús, por eso llegué tarde. — I missed the bus, that's why I was late.
- **Como + [clause], + [clause]** — giving a reason up front
  Como no tenía hambre, no comí. — Since I wasn't hungry, I didn't eat.
- **Gracias a + [noun]** — crediting a positive cause
  Gracias a tu ayuda, terminé a tiempo. — Thanks to your help, I finished on time.
- **Debido a + [noun]** — a more formal cause
  Debido a la lluvia, cerraron el parque. — Due to the rain, they closed the park.

## Sequencing a story or set of steps

- **Primero..., luego..., después..., finalmente...** — ordering events or steps
  Primero cocino, luego como, después lavo los platos. — First I cook, then I eat, then I wash the dishes.
- **Antes de + [infinitive]** — before doing something
  Antes de salir, cierro las ventanas. — Before leaving, I close the windows.
- **Después de + [infinitive]** — after doing something
  Después de comer, dormimos la siesta. — After eating, we take a nap.
- **Mientras + [clause]** — two things happening at once
  Mientras cocino, escucho música. — While I cook, I listen to music.

## Describing people and things

- **Es un/a + [noun] + que + [clause]** — defining something by what it does
  Es un lugar que me encanta. — It's a place that I love.
- **Se parece a + [noun]** — comparing appearance or similarity
  Se parece mucho a su papá. — He looks a lot like his dad.
- **Está hecho/a de + [material]** — describing what something is made of
  La mesa está hecha de madera. — The table is made of wood.
- **Tiene [X] años** — giving someone's age
  Mi hermana tiene veinte años. — My sister is twenty years old.

## Invitations and small talk

- **¿Te gustaría + [infinitive]?** — extending an invitation
  ¿Te gustaría cenar conmigo? — Would you like to have dinner with me?
- **¿Qué planes tienes para + [time]?** — asking about someone's plans
  ¿Qué planes tienes para el fin de semana? — What plans do you have for the weekend?
- **Hace + [weather condition]** — talking about the weather
  Hace mucho calor hoy. — It's very hot today.
- **Cuánto tiempo sin verte** — greeting someone you haven't seen in a while
  ¡Cuánto tiempo sin verte! — It's been so long since I've seen you!

## Location and directions

- **¿Dónde está + [noun]?** — asking where something is
  ¿Dónde está la estación? — Where is the station?
- **Queda + [direction/distance]** — describing where a place is located
  Queda a dos cuadras de aquí. — It's two blocks from here.
- **Sigue derecho / Dobla a la derecha / Dobla a la izquierda** — giving directions
  Sigue derecho y dobla a la izquierda. — Go straight and turn left.
- **Está entre + [X] y + [Y]** — locating something between two points
  Está entre la farmacia y el banco. — It's between the pharmacy and the bank.

## Expressing surprise or reaction

- **¡No puede ser!** — reacting with disbelief
  ¡No puede ser, qué mala suerte! — No way, what bad luck!
- **¡Qué + [adjective/noun]!** — an exclamation of reaction
  ¡Qué sorpresa verte aquí! — What a surprise to see you here!
- **No me digas** — expressing disbelief conversationally
  ¿En serio? No me digas. — Really? You don't say.
- **Me sorprende que + [subjunctive]** — expressing that something is surprising
  Me sorprende que no haya llamado. — I'm surprised he hasn't called.

## Phone and messaging

- **¿Aló? / ¿Bueno? / ¿Diga?** — answering the phone (varies by country)
  ¿Aló? ¿Quién habla? — Hello? Who's calling?
- **¿Me escuchas?** — checking that a call connection is working
  ¿Me escuchas bien? — Can you hear me well?
- **Te llamo luego** — ending a call or postponing it
  Te llamo luego, estoy ocupado. — I'll call you later, I'm busy.
- **Te mando un mensaje** — referring to texting someone
  Te mando un mensaje cuando llegue. — I'll text you when I arrive.

## Ordering and shopping

- **Quisiera + [noun]** — a polite way to order or request something
  Quisiera un café, por favor. — I would like a coffee, please.
- **¿Me trae + [noun]?** — asking a server to bring something
  ¿Me trae la cuenta, por favor? — Could you bring me the check, please?
- **¿Cuánto cuesta/vale + [noun]?** — asking a price
  ¿Cuánto cuesta esta camisa? — How much does this shirt cost?
- **¿Tiene + [noun] en + [size/color]?** — asking if a store has an item in a variant
  ¿Tiene esto en talla mediana? — Do you have this in medium?

## Change of state

- **Ponerse + [adjective]** — a temporary emotional or physical change
  Se puso muy nervioso antes del examen. — He got very nervous before the exam.
- **Volverse + [adjective]** — a lasting, often gradual change
  Con los años se volvió más paciente. — Over the years he became more patient.
- **Hacerse + [noun/adjective]** — a change through effort or process
  Se hizo médico después de diez años de estudio. — He became a doctor after ten years of study.
- **Quedarse + [adjective]** — the resulting state after an event
  Me quedé sorprendido con la noticia. — I was left surprised by the news.

## Health and symptoms

- **Me duele + [body part]** — describing pain
  Me duele la cabeza. — My head hurts.
- **Tengo + [symptom]** — describing a symptom
  Tengo fiebre y tos. — I have a fever and a cough.
- **No me siento bien** — a general statement of feeling unwell
  No me siento bien, creo que estoy enfermo. — I don't feel well, I think I'm sick.
- **Necesito ver a un médico** — stating a need for medical care
  Necesito ver a un médico pronto. — I need to see a doctor soon.

## Extremes and superlatives

- **El/la más + [adjective] de + [group]** — the most within a group
  Es la más inteligente de la clase. — She's the smartest in the class.
- **Lo peor/mejor es que + [clause]** — highlighting the worst or best part
  Lo peor es que llegamos tarde. — The worst part is that we arrived late.
- **Nada es tan + [adjective] como + [noun]** — an emphatic comparison
  Nada es tan importante como la familia. — Nothing is as important as family.
- **Cada vez más + [adjective]** — an increasing degree over time
  La ciudad está cada vez más cara. — The city is getting more and more expensive.

## Quantity and amount

- **Hay demasiado/a(s) + [noun]** — expressing excess
  Hay demasiado tráfico hoy. — There's too much traffic today.
- **No hay suficiente + [noun]** — expressing insufficiency
  No hay suficiente tiempo. — There isn't enough time.
- **La mayoría de + [noun]** — talking about most of a group
  La mayoría de mis amigos viven aquí. — Most of my friends live here.
- **Un par de + [noun]** — a small, approximate number
  Necesito un par de días más. — I need a couple more days.

## Indifference and lack of preference

- **Me da igual** — expressing indifference
  ¿Vamos al cine o al parque? Me da igual. — Should we go to the movies or the park? I don't mind either way.
- **No me importa + [noun/infinitive]** — expressing something doesn't matter to you
  No me importa el precio. — The price doesn't matter to me.
- **Como quieras** — deferring a decision to someone else
  ¿Comemos aquí o allá? Como quieras. — Should we eat here or there? Whatever you want.
- **Da lo mismo** — expressing that two options are equivalent
  Da lo mismo si vamos hoy o mañana. — It's the same whether we go today or tomorrow.

## Compliments and encouragement

- **Qué bien + [verb/clause]** — praising something someone did or said
  Qué bien cocinas. — You cook so well.
- **Te queda muy bien** — complimenting how something looks on someone
  Ese color te queda muy bien. — That color looks great on you.
- **Tú puedes** — offering encouragement
  Ánimo, tú puedes con esto. — Cheer up, you can do this.
- **Vas muy bien** — reassuring someone about their progress
  Vas muy bien con el español. — You're doing really well with your Spanish.

## Warnings and consequences

- **Ten cuidado con + [noun]** — warning about a danger
  Ten cuidado con el piso mojado. — Be careful with the wet floor.
- **Si no + [present], + [future]** — a warning about what happens without an action
  Si no estudias, no vas a aprobar. — If you don't study, you're not going to pass.
- **No vaya a ser que + [subjunctive]** — a cautionary "just in case"
  Lleva paraguas, no vaya a ser que llueva. — Bring an umbrella, in case it rains.
- **Cuidado, que + [clause]** — a quick warning with a reason attached
  Cuidado, que el suelo está resbaloso. — Careful, the floor is slippery.

## Saying goodbye

- **Nos vemos + [time]** — arranging to see someone again
  Nos vemos el lunes. — See you Monday.
- **Que tengas un buen + [day/time period]** — wishing someone well as you part
  Que tengas un buen fin de semana. — Have a good weekend.
- **Cuídate** — a warm farewell
  Cuídate mucho, hablamos pronto. — Take care, we'll talk soon.
- **Fue un placer + [infinitive]** — closing a conversation politely
  Fue un placer conocerte. — It was a pleasure to meet you.

## Reported speech

- **Dice que + [clause]** — reporting what someone says
  Dice que va a llegar tarde. — He says he's going to arrive late.
- **Me dijo que + [clause]** — reporting what someone told you
  Me dijo que estaba cansada. — She told me she was tired.
- **Según + [person/source]** — attributing information to a source
  Según el pronóstico, va a llover. — According to the forecast, it's going to rain.
- **Me preguntó si + [clause]** — reporting a question someone asked
  Me preguntó si quería ir. — He asked me if I wanted to go.

## Making plans to meet

- **Quedamos en + [time/place]** — confirming an arrangement to meet
  Quedamos en la entrada a las siete. — We agreed to meet at the entrance at seven.
- **¿Nos vemos + [time]?** — proposing a time to meet
  ¿Nos vemos el sábado? — Shall we meet on Saturday?
- **Paso por ti a las + [time]** — offering to pick someone up
  Paso por ti a las ocho. — I'll pick you up at eight.
- **Avísame cuando + [subjunctive]** — asking to be notified of something
  Avísame cuando llegues. — Let me know when you arrive.

## Weather

- **Está + [gerund/adjective]** — describing current weather conditions
  Está lloviendo mucho. — It's raining a lot.
- **Se pronostica + [noun]** — talking about a forecast
  Se pronostica una tormenta para mañana. — A storm is forecast for tomorrow.
- **Hace un día + [adjective]** — describing the day's weather
  Hace un día precioso. — It's a beautiful day.
- **Con este/a + [weather noun]...** — commenting on current conditions
  Con este calor, no quiero salir. — With this heat, I don't want to go out.

## Frustration and complaints

- **Estoy harto/a de + [noun/infinitive]** — expressing you're fed up
  Estoy harto de esperar. — I'm sick of waiting.
- **Ya no aguanto + [noun/infinitive]** — expressing you can't take something anymore
  Ya no aguanto este ruido. — I can't stand this noise anymore.
- **Qué fastidio / qué lata** — a casual complaint about an annoyance
  Qué fastidio, se cortó la luz otra vez. — What a pain, the power went out again.
- **Me molesta que + [subjunctive]** — expressing what bothers you
  Me molesta que lleguen tarde. — It bothers me that they arrive late.

## Asking someone to repeat or slow down

- **¿Puedes repetir, por favor?** — asking for repetition
  ¿Puedes repetir, por favor? No te escuché bien. — Can you repeat, please? I didn't hear you well.
- **Habla más despacio, por favor** — asking someone to slow down
  Habla más despacio, por favor, estoy aprendiendo. — Speak more slowly, please, I'm learning.
- **¿Cómo dijiste?** — a casual way to ask someone to repeat
  ¿Cómo dijiste? No entendí. — What did you say? I didn't understand.
- **En otras palabras...** — asking or offering to rephrase
  En otras palabras, ¿qué quieres decir? — In other words, what do you mean?

## Similarity and difference

- **Es igual que + [noun]** — expressing sameness
  Este modelo es igual que el otro. — This model is the same as the other one.
- **A diferencia de + [noun]** — contrasting with something
  A diferencia de mi hermano, yo prefiero el campo. — Unlike my brother, I prefer the countryside.
- **Se parece a + [noun], pero + [clause]** — noting similarity with a caveat
  Se parece a su madre, pero tiene los ojos de su padre. — He looks like his mother, but he has his father's eyes.
- **Tanto [X] como [Y]** — grouping two things as equally applicable
  Tanto tú como yo sabemos la verdad. — Both you and I know the truth.

## Money and finance

- **Cuesta + [amount]** — stating a price
  Cuesta veinte dólares. — It costs twenty dollars.
- **Me alcanza / no me alcanza para + [noun]** — expressing affordability
  No me alcanza para comprarlo ahora. — I can't afford to buy it right now.
- **Está a buen precio / está caro/a** — commenting on a price
  Este restaurante está muy caro. — This restaurant is very expensive.
- **Voy a ahorrar para + [noun/infinitive]** — talking about saving money
  Voy a ahorrar para el viaje. — I'm going to save up for the trip.

## Alternatives and substitution

- **En vez de + [noun/infinitive]** — proposing an alternative
  En vez de café, voy a tomar té. — Instead of coffee, I'm going to have tea.
- **En lugar de + [noun/infinitive]** — a more formal alternative phrase
  En lugar de quejarte, busca una solución. — Instead of complaining, look for a solution.
- **Mejor + [infinitive/clause]** — proposing a better option
  Mejor salimos temprano. — Let's leave early instead.
- **O + [X] o + [Y]** — presenting a choice between two options
  O vamos ahora o nos quedamos en casa. — Either we go now or we stay home.

## Progress and change over time

- **Poco a poco + [clause]** — describing gradual progress
  Poco a poco estoy mejorando mi español. — Little by little I'm improving my Spanish.
- **Cada vez + [comparative]** — describing an increasing or decreasing trend
  Cada vez tengo menos tiempo libre. — I have less and less free time.
- **Ya casi + [verb]** — nearing completion of something
  Ya casi termino el proyecto. — I'm almost done with the project.
- **Todavía no + [verb]** — something not yet completed
  Todavía no he terminado. — I haven't finished yet.

## Family and relationships

- **Estoy casado/a con + [noun]** — describing a marital relationship
  Estoy casada con un ingeniero. — I'm married to an engineer.
- **Nos llevamos bien/mal** — describing how you get along with someone
  Mi hermano y yo nos llevamos muy bien. — My brother and I get along very well.
- **Se parecen mucho** — noting resemblance between family members
  Los gemelos se parecen mucho. — The twins look a lot alike.
- **Crecí con + [noun]** — describing your upbringing
  Crecí con mis abuelos. — I grew up with my grandparents.
