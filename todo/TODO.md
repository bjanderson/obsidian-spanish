## TD-005 — No lesson on diminutive/augmentative suffixes (_-ito, -illo, -ón, -azo_)

- **Type:** suggestion
- **Created:** 2026-08-14

Diminutives (_-ito/-ita_, _-illo/-illa_) and augmentatives (_-ón/-ona_, _-azo/-aza_)
are extremely common in everyday spoken Latin American Spanish — for size, affection,
politeness/softening (_un momentico_, _ahorita_), and sarcasm — but no lesson in the
outline teaches their formation or pragmatic use. A natural fit is a new B2.3
subsection (pairs well with the existing "Softening Requests" B2.3.2 lesson) or a
dedicated A2.4 vocabulary/word-formation lesson for the basic size/affection sense.

## TD-012 — Reciprocal _se_ (_se abrazan, nos vemos_) never distinguished from reflexive _se_

- **Type:** suggestion
- **Created:** 2026-08-14

A2.2.3 teaches reflexive verbs (_me lavo, se viste_) but never distinguishes the
reciprocal use of the same pronouns (_se abrazan_ = "they hug each other," _nos
vemos_ = "we see each other") — a common point of learner confusion since the surface
form is identical. Suggest a short addition to A2.2.3 (e.g. a new A2.2.3.4) or a B1
cross-reference note contrasting the two readings.

## TD-013 — Directional verb pairs _ir/venir_ and _llevar/traer_ never contrasted

- **Type:** suggestion
- **Created:** 2026-08-14

English speakers commonly confuse these two verb pairs since English "come/bring" and
"go/take" don't map onto Spanish the same way (Spanish orientation is toward the
listener's location, not just the speaker's). No lesson anywhere in the outline
addresses this. Suggest a short A2 lesson (e.g. new A2.4 subsection) contrasting
_ir_ vs. _venir_ and _llevar_ vs. _traer_.

## TD-019 — RAE 2010 orthography updates (_solo/sólo_, demonstrative accents) never mentioned

- **Type:** suggestion
- **Created:** 2026-08-14

The 2010 RAE spelling reform dropped the accent on _solo_ (adverb) and on
demonstrative pronouns (_éste → este_) in most contexts, but older texts/media still
use the pre-reform accents. No lesson anywhere notes this, and a learner reading
older native material could be confused by the discrepancy. Suggest a short note
either extending A1.1.3 (orthographic conventions) or as a C1/C2 register aside about
old vs. current spelling norms, since older published Spanish still uses the accents.

## TD-020 — No audio/pronunciation support anywhere in the vault, despite lessons that are inherently about sound

- **Type:** improvement
- **Created:** 2026-08-14

Every lesson is text-only. This is a real limitation for the whole course, but it's

embedded-audio or TTS-link approach, at minimum for C1.3.3.1 and C1.3.3.2, and
ideally for the phonetics lessons back at A1.1 too.
