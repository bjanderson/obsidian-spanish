## English Translation

Joaquín had been working as a dubbing actor for three years, but he had never had a project as demanding as the new animated series he was recording that week: he alone was voicing four different characters within the same episode, each one with a completely different register.

The first was the butler of a mansion, a character who spoke with an almost exaggerated formality. "Allow me to inform you, sir, that dinner will be served in fifteen minutes," Joaquín read in front of the microphone, taking care with every consonant, lengthening the vowels slightly to convey solemnity. The director, from the control booth, nodded, pleased.

The moment that take ended, he had to turn, without a pause, into the butler's teenage nephew, a rebellious kid who spoke in a completely opposite register. "No way am I putting on that suit, what, do you want me to look like a penguin?" Joaquín blurted out, lowering his tone, speeding up the rhythm, letting his voice crack a little at the end, the way an annoyed teenager's would. The change was so abrupt that even the sound engineer laughed behind the glass.

Afterward he had to dub the family's grandmother, a wise woman who spoke slowly and warmly, and finally the villain of the series, whose voice had to sound cold and calculating, every word delivered with a barely contained contempt. Joaquín moved from one character to another with hardly any time to breathe, adjusting not just the tone of voice, but the entire attitude behind every line.

When the session ended, the dubbing director came over to congratulate him. "What you did today isn't just acting," she told him, "it's mastering the language in all its registers at once: the formal, the colloquial, the tender, the threatening. Few actors manage to jump between those worlds without the effort showing." Joaquín, exhausted but satisfied, took off his headphones and thought that, in a way, dubbing four characters in a single day was like speaking four different languages without ever stopping being, underneath it all, the same language.

That night, while listening to the full recording of the episode, Joaquín could barely recognize his own voice in each character, and he smiled, realizing that was, precisely, the highest compliment he could receive.
