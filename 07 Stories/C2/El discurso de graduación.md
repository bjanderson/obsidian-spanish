## English Translation

Valentina was chosen by her classmates to give the graduation speech, an honor that filled her with pride and, at the same time, with panic. She had never spoken in front of so many people, much less on an occasion as solemn as her engineering department's graduation ceremony.

Her first draft, however, sounded more like a conversation between friends than an official speech. "Well, guys, the truth is these years were crazy, right?" she had written, before showing it to her Communications professor, who handed the paper back with a patient smile. "The content is honest, and that's good," he told her, "but the register doesn't match the occasion. A graduation speech calls for formality, for structures that emphasize what you want to say, not just narrate it."

With that guidance, Valentina rewrote the speech from scratch. Instead of "these years were crazy," she wrote: "It was precisely in the hardest moments of this program that we discovered what we were made of." Instead of simply recounting an anecdote about late study nights, she built the sentence with more rhetorical force: "It was for that reason that, night after night, we chose to stay awake: not out of obligation, but out of the certainty that it was worth it." Every sentence she rewrote gained a solemnity it had lacked before.

The night before the ceremony, she rehearsed the speech in front of the mirror, timing every pause, every emphasis. Her mother, listening from the doorway, interrupted her with tears in her eyes. "That doesn't sound like my daughter talking anymore," she said, "it sounds like someone everyone is going to remember." Valentina smiled, still nervous, but sure she had found the right tone.

On graduation day, she walked up to the podium with trembling hands and the speech memorized. "It was in this very department that we learned not just engineering, but perseverance," she began, with a voice that surprised even those who knew her well. When she finished, the whole auditorium stood up to applaud, and Valentina knew that, thanks to the effort of finding the precise words, she had managed to say exactly what she wanted people to remember about that day.
