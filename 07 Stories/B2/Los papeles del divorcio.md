## English Translation

Elena and Roberto decided to end their marriage after fifteen years together. The decision wasn't easy, but they both knew it was best for everyone.

Together they talked to a lawyer, who explained to them how the legal divorce process worked. She told them they needed to reach an agreement about custody of their son Mateo, and that the process could take several months.

Over the following weeks, Elena and Roberto had several meetings with the lawyer to talk about custody. "I want Mateo to split his time equally between us," Elena said.

Roberto agreed, and the two of them promised to put Mateo first, no matter their own issues.

A month later, the lawyer told them the papers were ready to sign.

The papers were signed by both of them in the lawyer's office. It was a strange moment: they felt relief and sadness at the same time.

On their way out of the office, Roberto told Elena he hoped they could be good parents for Mateo, even though they were no longer a couple. "We always will be," Elena answered, and the two of them smiled at each other with respect.
