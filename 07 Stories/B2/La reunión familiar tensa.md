## English Translation

Every year, the family gathered at Grandma Carmen's house for her birthday. This year, everything felt a little tense, because her children Rosa and Fernando hadn't spoken to each other in five years.

Grandma had asked everyone to come, even though she knew it would be hard. "I want my whole family here," she said, and no one could tell her no.

During dinner, Sofía noticed that her aunt and uncle barely looked at each other. She asked her mother why they didn't speak. "Years ago, Fernando asked Rosa to lend him money, and she doubted he would ever pay it back," her mother explained.

After the birthday cake, Grandma stood up and spoke to everyone: "I hope that someday you two can forgive each other. Life is too short to live with resentment," she said.

Fernando looked at her and, after a long silence, told her: "I'm sorry for not paying back the money on time." Rosa looked at him with tears in her eyes and said: "The money doesn't matter anymore. What matters is that you're here."

Sofía understood that family, even when it had problems, would always find a way to come together again.
