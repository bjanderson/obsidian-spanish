## English Translation

It was the first day of class and Emilia felt very nervous. Her family had moved from Colombia barely a week before, and she didn't know anyone at the new school. She sat down alone, near the window, and looked outside, thinking about the country where she'd been born.

The teacher introduced her to the whole class: "This is Emilia, who comes from Colombia," she said. The class was told that Emilia was new and needed a classmate who could help her get to know the school. No one raised their hand right away, and Emilia felt her heart sink a little.

During recess, Emilia sat alone on a bench in the schoolyard. She watched the other kids playing soccer and laughing together, and she missed her friends from her old school. She thought: "There's no one here who knows me."

One of her classmates, Tomás, walked over and asked if she wanted to play with them. Emilia looked at him, surprised, and said yes. While they played, she told him she missed her country a lot, and the friends she'd left behind.

Tomás listened closely and told her: "You have classmates here who want to get to know you." In the days that followed, Emilia was invited to eat lunch with Tomás's group, and little by little she started to feel like part of the class.

A month later, Emilia already had several friends and laughed with them at every recess. She understood that a new school wasn't just a place to study, but also a place she could belong to. Now, whenever someone new joins the class, Emilia is the first one to walk over and offer her friendship.
