## English Translation

Don Ricardo was eighty years old and decided it was time to write his will. One afternoon, he went to a notary's office.

"You need to decide how you want your property divided," the notary explained to him in a calm voice.

Don Ricardo thought carefully. "The house will be inherited by my oldest daughter, and the money will be split among my grandchildren," he decided.

"If I had written this sooner, my children would already know my wishes," he thought, a little sad.

But he also wanted to leave something more: a legacy of love. He wrote a letter for each grandchild, telling them stories from his life and giving them advice for the future.

The will was signed before the notary that same afternoon. Don Ricardo felt at peace.

Walking out of the office, he thought: "The most important thing I'm leaving my grandchildren isn't my property, but everything I taught them." That legacy, he believed, would be worth more than anything else.
