That night, Valentina talked to her parents. Her father confessed that, as a young man, he too had wanted to be a musician, but he decided to study medicine out of fear. "If I had followed my dream, maybe I would have been happier," he told her, "but I don't want you to make the same mistake."

Listening to her father, Valentina understood she had to follow her own path. She decided to study music at the university, and her parents supported her with all their hearts.
