## English Translation

The company where Marta worked was going through an economic crisis. Her boss explained that there was no longer enough money to pay all the employees.

One afternoon, all the employees were called to an urgent meeting. The director told them the company was going to make several layoffs. The news was met with silence and fear.

Marta was afraid they would lay her off too. "I'm worried there won't be enough work for everyone," she told her friend Carla, who worked on the same team.

For several days, Marta and Carla talked with other coworkers about the situation. "It's sad the company is in this situation," Carla said, "but I hope everything turns out okay."

At the end of the week, Marta was called to the director's office. The director told her that, unfortunately, her job was no longer going to exist.

Marta felt the world crashing down on her, but she tried to stay calm. Her contract was ended that very day.

But Marta didn't give up. Weeks later, she found a new job at another company that needed someone with her experience. "Sometimes," she thought, "the end of one thing is just the beginning of another."
