Renata and Andrés had been together for three years, but last week they ended their relationship. The breakup was hard for both of them.

That night, Renata stayed home alone and felt the loneliness like never before. "If we had talked more, maybe we wouldn't have broken up," she thought, looking at her phone.

She remembered how, over the last few months, Andrés had wanted her to spend more time with him, but she doubted the relationship was meant to last forever. One night, he told her they needed to talk more honestly.

The night of the breakup, Andrés told her he wasn't happy anymore, and Renata realized she felt the same way. They both cried, but they agreed they couldn't keep going as a couple.

The weeks passed, and Renata began to get over the pain. Her friend Sofía listened to her every time she needed to talk and told her: "There's nothing wrong with feeling pain."

Over time, Renata understood that, if she hadn't ended that relationship, she never would have discovered how much she herself was worth.

She decided to forgive Andrés and, even more important, to forgive herself. She no longer felt guilt, only peace.
