## English Translation

When their grandfather don Alberto died, he left an inheritance for his three grandchildren: Pablo, Lucía, and Andrés.

The will was read by a lawyer in front of the whole family. The house and the money were divided equally among the three of them. But the will didn't say who should keep their grandfather's favorite watch.

The three siblings started arguing about who deserved the watch. "Grandpa would have wanted me to have it, because I was his oldest grandson," Pablo said.

"But I was the one who took care of him these last few years," Lucía answered. "If we had talked to him before he died, this wouldn't have happened," Andrés said, tired of the argument.

After hours of arguing, the three of them realized the watch wasn't worth more than their relationship as siblings. They decided to keep the watch somewhere special and share it: each year, one of them would have it.

That night, Pablo thought: "If grandpa could see us now, he'd be proud. His real inheritance wasn't the house or the money, but the love he left between us."
