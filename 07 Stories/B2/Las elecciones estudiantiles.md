## English Translation

Every year, the school held student council elections to choose the new president. Isabela decided to run as a candidate that year, because she had a lot of ideas for improving the school.

Her best friend Valentina helped her with the campaign. Together they made posters and thought about a good speech. But Isabela doubted herself: "I don't think people trust me," she told Valentina.

"I doubt you're the only one who feels afraid," Valentina answered. "But your ideas are good, and that's what matters." Isabela's posters were put up on the school walls by her campaign team.

On the day of the debate, Isabela got up on stage and gave her speech in front of the whole school. She explained her proposal to improve the cafeteria and create more after-school activities.

Her rival, Nico, also gave a good speech. No one knew for sure who would win. Isabela doubted that her proposal was better than Nico's, but she decided to trust herself anyway.

The votes were counted by the teachers at the end of the day. The whole school waited for the result closely, and Isabela waited nervously, holding Valentina's hand.

When the teacher said her name as the new president, Isabela couldn't believe it. She hugged Valentina and laughed with joy. Nico walked over and told her she had done a great job. "I doubted I could win," Isabela told him, "but I think we can work together to improve the school."
