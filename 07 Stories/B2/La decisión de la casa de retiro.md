## English Translation

Doña Elena had lived alone since her husband died, two years ago. Her children Marta and Luis were worried about her health.

One day, they talked to her about a retirement home. "It bothers me that you think I can't take care of myself anymore," Elena said, a bit annoyed.

"If I moved to that place, I'd lose my independence," Elena thought.

Marta told her: "It would make us happy if you considered the option. We're afraid something will happen to you and we won't know."

Elena decided to visit the retirement home one day. She was surprised: there were lots of activities and a community of happy people. She met a neighbor her age who told her that she felt freer there than ever.

That night, Elena thought: "If I had known it would be like this, I wouldn't have hesitated so much." She decided to move to the retirement home, but on one condition: she wanted to decide how to live her life there.

Marta and Luis were overjoyed when Elena told them her decision. "You'll always be independent, Mom," Luis told her, "we just want you to be safe." Elena smiled and understood that her family's love didn't want to take away her freedom, but to protect it.
