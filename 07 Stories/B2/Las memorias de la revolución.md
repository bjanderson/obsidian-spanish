## English Translation

Valentina and Mateo visited their grandfather Ernesto and asked him to tell them a story from when he was young.

"When I was young," Grandpa began, "my country was living through a revolution. No one believed the war would end quickly."

"My parents asked me to stay home and not go outside when the army arrived in our town."

"Many houses were destroyed, and the city was taken by soldiers."

"One night, my family decided to flee the town. We walked for days, and there were moments of great danger."

"People just wanted freedom and peace. I remember a man told us: 'One day this will end, and you'll be free.'"

Valentina hugged her grandfather and told him she would never forget his story. Mateo, who had been silent the whole time, finally spoke: "Thank you for telling us this, Grandpa. Now we understand why peace is so important."
