## English Translation

One rainy night, the family was driving home after eating dinner at their grandparents' house. Carlos was driving slowly because the road was wet.

In an instant, another car crashed into theirs. Everyone felt very scared, but no one was badly hurt.

Carlos called the police, and an ambulance arrived a few minutes later. The family was taken to the hospital so a doctor could check them.

On the way, Ana couldn't stop looking at her kids. "I don't think Mateo is hurt, but I doubt he's completely okay," she thought, seeing that he was very shaken.

The doctor talked with each of them and said they were all fine, just a bit shaken up. Valentina had a small bump on her arm, but nothing more.

That night, the whole family slept in the same room. No one wanted to be alone. Carlos hugged everyone and said: "What matters most is that we're all okay. The car can be fixed, but you all are priceless."
