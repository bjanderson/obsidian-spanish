## English Translation

One day, Marcos got a job offer in another country. It was a great opportunity, but it also brought a big change for the whole family.

That night, Marcos and his wife Paula talked with the kids. "If we accept this offer, we'd have a better life, but we'd leave behind everything we know," Paula said.

Sofía asked if she'd have to leave her friends. Her mother explained that she could make new friends, even though it would be hard at first. Tomás said he didn't want to move, but his father promised they would visit family every year.

The following weeks flew by. The family packed their suitcases and put their things in boxes. On the day they said goodbye, all their friends and relatives came to see them off.

When they arrived at their new home, everything was different: the language, the food, even the weather. The first few months were hard, and sometimes Paula thought: "If we hadn't moved, maybe it would be easier."

But over time, the family started to feel at home. The kids made new friends and learned the language quickly. One night, Tomás told his father that, if he could choose again, he'd choose to come with the whole family. Paula understood that home wasn't a place, but the people you love.
