## English Translation

Lucía has worked at the same company for five years. One day, her boss calls her into his office. "I have important news," he tells her. "I'd like to offer you the team manager position."

Lucía can't believe it. "Me, a manager?" she asks, surprised. "Yes," her boss answers. "I think you'd be very good for the position. You have a lot of experience, and the team trusts you."

That night, Lucía calls her husband to tell him the news. "I'm so glad they're offering you the promotion," he tells her. "I knew you'd get there someday." But Lucía is also a little worried. "I'm afraid it'll be too much responsibility," she admits.

"It's normal to be afraid," her husband tells her. "But I'm also sure you could handle this. It would be a good change for your career." Lucía thinks about it all night.

The next day, Lucía accepts the promotion. "I'm glad you decided to accept," her boss tells her, smiling. "The team is going to be happy to work with you."

Her first days as manager are hard. "I'm afraid the team won't respect me," she tells her friend Marta. "That's normal," Marta answers, "but I think you're a good leader. Give them time."

Over time, Lucía feels more confident in her new position. The team respects her, and she's proud of her work. "I never thought I'd be capable of this," she tells her husband. "But I'm so glad you trust me."
