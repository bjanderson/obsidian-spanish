## English Translation

After forty years of work, don Roberto is finally retiring. He's worked at the same company since he was young, and he's always been a dedicated employee.

On the day of his retirement, his coworkers organize a special party. "You've worked hard your whole life," his boss tells him, "and you've helped a lot of people during that time." Don Roberto smiles, moved.

"When I started here, I never imagined I'd spend my whole career at this company," don Roberto says. "But I've learned so much, and I've met wonderful people." His coworkers listen closely.

Before this day, don Roberto had thought a lot about his retirement. "I'd been afraid of not knowing what to do with my free time," he admits. "But now I'm ready for this new part of my life."

That night, at home, his wife asks him: "How do you feel?" Don Roberto thinks for a moment. "I feel happy. I've given the best of myself at my job, and now I want to enjoy time with my family."

The first days of retirement are strange for don Roberto, because he'd never had so much free time. But he soon starts enjoying his new activities: he reads more, he walks in the park, and he visits his grandchildren every week. "I worked my whole life to reach this moment," he thinks, "and now I can finally enjoy it."
