## English Translation

Mateo has played guitar for two years. One day, he tells his friend Sofía: "I'd like to form a music band with you." Sofía smiles. "That would be amazing! I could sing, and you'd play guitar."

The two of them think about who else could be in the band. "Diego plays drums really well," Sofía says. "We could invite Diego." Mateo calls Diego that afternoon. "Would you like to play with us?" he asks him. Diego answers happily: "Of course! It would be fun to play in a band."

The three friends start practicing every week at Mateo's house. "What songs would we play first?" Diego asks. "We should start with easy songs," Sofía answers. They practice a lot, and every week they play better.

One day, there's a special event at school. "It would be a good idea to play in public," Mateo says, a little nervous. "Should we do it?" Sofía and Diego say yes. "We'd like to show what we've practiced."

The night of the event, the three friends go up on stage. At first, Mateo is scared, but when he starts playing guitar, he feels happy. Sofía sings with a lot of energy, and Diego plays the drums with a great beat.

When the song ends, the whole audience claps. "That was amazing!" Sofía says, very happy. "We should play together again," Diego says. Mateo smiles: "I'd love to. I think we're going to play together a lot more."
