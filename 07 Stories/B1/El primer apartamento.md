## English Translation

Sofía finally rents her first apartment. She's very excited, but also a little nervous. The day she gets the keys, the landlady of the building explains the rules to her.

"Pay the rent on the first day of every month," the landlady tells her, in a serious voice. "Don't make noise after ten at night, because the neighbors go to sleep early. And if there's a problem with the water or the electricity, let me know quickly."

"Understood," Sofía answers. "Can I paint the walls?" she asks. "Don't paint the walls without permission," the landlady answers. "But you can put in your own furniture."

That afternoon, her friend Camila helps her set up the apartment. "Put the bed near the window," Camila suggests. "That way you'll get more light in the morning." Sofía puts the furniture where her friend tells her.

"Don't leave the lights on when you go out," Camila reminds her. "And don't forget to pay the rent on time. The landlady seems strict." Sofía laughs. "You're right. I'm going to be very careful."

That night, Sofía sits on her new bed and looks at her apartment. It's small, but it's hers. "This is my home now," she thinks, happily.
