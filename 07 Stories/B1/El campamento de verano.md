# El campamento de verano

Diego [[ir|va]] a un campamento de [[verano]] en el [[bosque]]. [[llevar|Lleva]] su mochila con [[ropa]] y una linterna. El [[primero-primera-primeros-primeras|primer]] [[día]], [[hacer|hace]] [[nuevo-nueva-nuevos-nuevas|nuevos]] [[amigo-amiga|amigos]]: Marco y Ana. [[junto-junta-juntos-juntas|Juntos]] exploran el bosque y [[nadar|nadan]] en el [[lago]].

La [[primero-primera-primeros-primeras|primera]] [[noche]], Diego [[buscar|busca]] su linterna, pero no la [[encontrar|encuentra]]. "¿Dónde [[estar|está]] mi linterna?" Marco le [[dar|da]] la linterna a Diego. "[[mucho-muchos|Muchas]] gracias," le [[decir|dice]] Diego. "Te la [[dar|doy]] [[mañana]]."

[[ese-esa|Esa]] noche, [[todo-todos|todos]] los [[niño-niña|niños]] se [[sentar|sientan]] [[cerca]] del fuego. El [[maestro-maestra|maestro]] [[contar|cuenta]] una [[historia]] de miedo, y los niños [[escuchar|escuchan]] con [[atención]] en la oscuridad. Ana [[tener|tiene]] un [[poco]] de miedo. "No te preocupes," le dice Diego. "[[yo|Yo]] te [[cuidar|cuido]]."

Al [[día]] [[siguiente]], Ana [[recibir|recibe]] una carta de sus [[padre-madre|padres]]. La [[leer|lee]] y [[sonreír|sonríe]]. "¿Qué [[decir|dice]] la carta?" le [[preguntar|pregunta]] Diego. [[cuando|Cuando]] Diego [[volver|vuelve]] a [[casa]], le [[mostrar|muestra]] la foto a su [[padre-madre|madre]]. "Mis amigos me la [[dar|dieron]]," le [[explicar|explica]]. "[[nunca|Nunca]] voy a [[olvidar]] [[ese-esa|este]] campamento."

## English Translation

Diego goes to a summer camp in the forest. He brings his backpack with clothes and a flashlight. On the first day, he makes new friends: Marco and Ana. Together they explore the forest and swim in the lake.

The first night, Diego looks for his flashlight, but he can't find it. "Where's my flashlight?" he asks, worried. "I have another flashlight," Marco says. "I'll lend it to you." Marco gives the flashlight to Diego. "Thank you very much," Diego tells him. "I'll give it back to you tomorrow."

That night, all the kids sit near the fire. The teacher tells a scary story, and the kids listen closely in the dark. Ana is a little scared. "Don't worry," Diego tells her. "I'll look after you."

The next day, Ana gets a letter from her parents. She reads it and smiles. "What does the letter say?" Diego asks her. "My parents wrote it to me yesterday," Ana answers. "They say they miss me a lot." Diego understands, because he misses his family too.

On the last day of camp, Marco gives Diego a photo of the group. "I'm giving it to you so you'll remember this summer," he tells him. Diego puts it away carefully in his backpack.

When Diego gets home, he shows the photo to his mother. "My friends gave it to me," he explains. "I'm never going to forget this camp."
