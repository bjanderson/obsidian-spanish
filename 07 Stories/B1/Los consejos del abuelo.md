## English Translation

Grandpa Pedro sits in the park with his grandson Mateo, who is fifteen years old. "Grandpa, what advice would you give me for my life?" Mateo asks.

Grandpa smiles and thinks for a moment. "Follow your dreams, but also be patient," he says. "Work hard for the things you want, and don't be afraid to make mistakes."

"Did you make a lot of mistakes, Grandpa?" Mateo asks, curious. "So many," Grandpa answers, laughing. "But every mistake I made taught me something important. Don't give up when things get hard."

"Choose your friends carefully, the ones you spend your time with," Grandpa continues. "True friendship is a treasure that's worth a whole lifetime. And never forget the family that loves you."

Mateo listens closely. "What else, Grandpa?" he asks. "Be honest, always," Grandpa says. "And love the people who deserve it with all your heart."

At the end of the afternoon, Mateo hugs his grandfather. "Thanks for your advice," he tells him. "I'm going to remember it my whole life." Grandpa smiles, happy to share his wisdom with his grandson.
