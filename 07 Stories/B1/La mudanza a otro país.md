## English Translation

Daniel and his wife have decided to move to another country for his new job. It's a hard decision, but it's also an adventure that makes them happy.

"We're going to live in a city where we've never been," Daniel says, "but I think it's going to be a great experience." His wife, who has always wanted to live abroad, is very excited.

Before traveling, they have to prepare a lot of things: the passport, the visa, and the suitcases. Also, they have to say goodbye to the family and friends they've known their whole lives.

"We're going to miss the people we've shared so many moments with," Daniel's wife says, a little sad. "But we know we're going to make new friends too."

On the day of the flight, they arrive at the airport where the whole family is waiting to say goodbye. It's a special moment. Daniel's parents, who have never been far from their children, cry a little.

When they finally arrive at their new home, everything is different: the language, the food, the culture. But Daniel and his wife know that, over time, they're going to feel that this place, where they're going to build their new life, is also their home.
