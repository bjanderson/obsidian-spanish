## English Translation

The Ramírez family goes on vacation by car for the first time. "We've been driving for two hours, and we still have four hours left to get there," the father says, looking at the map.

"Can we stop soon?" the youngest daughter asks. "I've been sitting for a long time, and I'm hungry." The mother laughs. "We're going to stop in the next town, to eat something and rest a little."

After half an hour, they arrive at a small town. They stop for gas and buy food for the road. "This town is really pretty," the older son says, looking at the mountains through the car window.

Back on the highway, the family continues its trip toward the beach. "We've been traveling all day," the mother says, "but it's an amazing trip." The kids look out the window, excited about the ocean they'll soon see.

After many hours, they finally arrive at the beach. "We've been traveling almost ten hours from home," the father says, tired but happy. "But it was worth it, because this place is beautiful."

That night, the whole family walks along the beach, looking at the stars. "This trip is going to be really special," the mother says, hugging her children.
