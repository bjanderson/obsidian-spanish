Camila and Tomás talk about social media after school. "I use social media for two hours every day," Camila says. "I use it to talk with my friends and to watch funny videos."

Tomás thinks differently. "I don't use it for fun, but to study," he answers her. "I use the internet to look up information for my homework, but I don't spend much time on social media."

"Don't you like seeing what your friends post?" Camila asks. "Yes, I like it," Tomás says, "but I think social media can be a problem. A lot of people don't rest because they use their phone all night."

Camila thinks for a moment. "You're right. Sometimes I don't sleep well because I'm looking at my phone," she admits. "But it's also good for connecting with people from other countries. It's not just fun, but a way to learn about the world."

"That's true," Tomás says. "For me, the problem isn't social media, but the time we spend on it." The two friends agree on one thing: it's important to use social media carefully.

That night, Camila decides to set a limit. "I'm going to use my phone for only one hour before bed," she says. "It's not easy, but I want to have more time to read and to exercise."
