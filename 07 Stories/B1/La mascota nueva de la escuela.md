## English Translation

In the teacher's class, there's a new fish. His name is Burbuja, and he lives in a fishbowl near the window. The whole class is very happy about the new pet.

The teacher says: "I want each student to take care of Burbuja for one week." The children listen closely. "It's important that you feed him every day," she explains. "It's also necessary that you clean the fishbowl when it's dirty."

Sofía is the first to have the turn. She wants Burbuja to be healthy and happy. Every morning, before class, she gives him food and checks the water. "It's important that you don't give him too much food," the teacher reminds her. "Fish don't need much food."

On Friday, Sofía sees something strange: the water is a little dirty. She tells the teacher: "I think we need to change the water today." The teacher agrees too, and together they clean the fishbowl carefully.

The following Monday, it's Diego's turn. "I hope you take good care of Burbuja," Sofía tells him. "No problem," Diego answers. "I want Burbuja to be happy in our class," Diego says.

By the end of the month, all the students have taken care of Burbuja. The teacher is proud. "It's important that we work together," she tells them. "Everyone has taken very good care of our pet."
