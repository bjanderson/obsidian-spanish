## English Translation

Nico has a brown dog. His name is Toby. Nico has lived with Toby since he was very small, six years ago. Every day, after school, Nico and Toby walk together through the park near their house.

But this week, something happened. On Monday, Nico opened the garden gate to take out the trash, and Toby ran off. Since that moment, no one has seen him.

"Have you looked in the park?" his father asked him. "Yes, I've looked there twice," Nico answered, sad. "I've also talked to the neighbors, but no one has seen him."

It's been three days since Toby disappeared, and Nico hasn't slept well. His mother wrote her phone number on some papers and put them up all over the neighborhood. His sister called the neighborhood veterinary clinic, but they hadn't seen a brown dog like Toby there either.

On Thursday afternoon, a neighbor knocked on the door. "I found a dog near the market," the man said. "He's been there since this morning, and I think he's your dog."

Nico and his family ran to the market. When Nico saw Toby, he shouted with joy. "Toby! We've finally found you!" The dog ran toward him and licked his face.

Since that day, Nico always closes the garden gate well. "We've learned something very important," he told his sister. "We've never been so worried in our lives."
