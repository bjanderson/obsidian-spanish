## English Translation

Mateo has been looking for work for a month. He's sent his résumé to several companies, but he still hasn't gotten any response. "I doubt they'll call me soon," he tells his sister, a little sad.

"It's important that you keep looking," his sister answers. "It's also necessary that you prepare well for your interviews." Mateo decides to follow her advice and starts preparing answers for the most common questions.

A week later, a company calls him for an interview. Mateo is very nervous. "I doubt I have enough experience," he thinks before walking into the office. But during the interview, he answers calmly and talks about his skills.

At the end of the interview, the boss tells him: "It's possible we'll offer you the position. We're going to call you this week." Mateo leaves the interview happy, but also nervous. "I don't doubt that I did my best," he thinks.

Three days later, Mateo gets a call. "I have good news: we're offering you the position," the boss tells him. Mateo can't believe it. "Thank you so much! I accept the offer," he answers, excited.

That night, Mateo calls his sister. "I got the job!" he tells her. "I knew you'd manage it," she answers, proud. "It's important that you enjoy this moment."
