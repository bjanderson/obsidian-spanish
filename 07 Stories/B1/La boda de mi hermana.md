## English Translation

My sister Camila is getting married in two months, and the whole family is helping plan the wedding. "I want everything to go well," Camila says, a little nervous.

My mother is handling the flowers. "I hope the white flowers arrive on time," she says, looking at her papers. "I hope the weather is good that day, because the ceremony is going to be in the garden."

My father wants to help with the music. "I hope everyone likes the songs I prepared," he says, a little proud. Camila laughs. "I hope you don't only play old songs."

I'm helping invite the family's friends. "I want all the cousins to come," I tell Camila. "I hope we have enough chairs for everyone." Camila hugs me. "Thanks for helping me with this."

The day before the wedding, we decorate the church together. "I hope it doesn't rain tomorrow," my mother says, looking at the gray sky. We all hope the weather changes.

Finally, the day of the wedding arrives. The sun shines, and everything turns out the way Camila wanted. "I hope you'll be very happy," I tell my sister and her new husband. Camila hugs me, crying, very happy. "Thanks for making this day so special."
