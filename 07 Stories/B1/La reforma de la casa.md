## English Translation

The García family wants to renovate their house. "If we had more money, we'd redo the whole kitchen," the father says, looking at the old walls.

"What would you all change?" the mother asks the kids. "I'd add more color to my room," the daughter says. "I'd like a blue color." The son thinks for a moment. "I'd add more space for my books."

"It would be good to have a budget before we start," the father says. "We can't spend all our money on a renovation." The family talks about how much money they have and what they need to buy.

"We could start with the kitchen," the mother suggests. "It would be the most important part of the house." Everyone agrees.

The bathroom also needs changes. "The walls would look better if we painted them white," the older son adds.

After talking a lot, the family makes a decision. "We'd start with the kitchen, and then we'd move on to the bathroom," the father says. "That way we'd have a nicer house, little by little."

The kids are excited. "Could we paint my room first?" the daughter asks. "Of course," the mother answers, smiling. "We're going to make this house a place where everyone wants to live."
