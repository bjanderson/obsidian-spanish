## English Translation

Every month, a group of older friends gets together for their book club. This month, they've read a novel about a small town.

"I think the author wrote this story about his own family," doña Elena says, looking at her book. "I don't think it's just fiction."

Her friend don Manuel thinks differently. "I doubt this story is real," he answers. "I think the author just wanted to create an interesting world."

"What do you all think of the ending?" doña Rosa asks. "I don't think the main character made the right decision," she says. "I think he did," don Manuel answers. "I doubt he had another option in that situation."

The friends discuss each chapter with a lot of energy. "I don't doubt this is one of the best books we've read," doña Elena says. "I'm sure we're going to remember it for a long time."

At the end of the meeting, everyone agrees on one thing: the next book is also going to be very interesting. "I hope the next book is as good as this one," doña Rosa says, smiling. "I doubt we'll find anything better, but we're going to try."
