Before arriving at the university, Valentina had never lived far from her family. She had studied hard to get into the medical program, and she had dreamed of this moment since she was a girl.

The day she arrived at the university, her parents took her to the dorm with two big suitcases. "You've never been so far from home," her mother told her, a little sad. Valentina hugged her and said: "Everything's going to be fine."

During her first week, Valentina has met a lot of new people. She's made a friend, Carolina, who lives on the same floor of the dorm. Together they've explored the campus and found the library and the dining hall.

"Have you taken your first exam yet?" Carolina asks her one day. "Not yet," Valentina answers, "but I've studied a lot this week. I'd never had so much homework before." Carolina laughs. "I hadn't imagined the university would be this hard either."

One night, Valentina calls her family on the phone. "I've learned a lot these past few days," she tells them. "At first I was scared, because I'd never lived alone. But now I feel more confident." Her father smiles. "We knew you were going to be fine."

By the end of the first month, Valentina has already adjusted to her new life. She's made friends, learned to manage her time better, and started to feel like the university is another home.
