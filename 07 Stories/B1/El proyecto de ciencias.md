# El proyecto de ciencias

## English Translation

Valentina and Diego have to do a science project together. Their teacher tells them: "I recommend that you research how plants grow." The two students think of an idea: they're going to put three small plants in different places.

"We need to measure each plant every day," Valentina says. Diego brings the plants to class. One plant gets a lot of water and a lot of sunlight. Another gets little water. The third lives without light.

For two weeks, Valentina and Diego take care of their plants. Every day, they measure the leaves and write down the numbers. "It's important that we're patient," the teacher reminds them. "Experiments need time."

After two weeks, they see the results: the plant with a lot of water and light grew more than the other two. "I recommend we talk about this in class," Diego says. "I want our classmates to understand what we learned."

On presentation day, Valentina and Diego explain their experiment to the whole class. The teacher is very pleased. "You worked hard," he tells them. "I recommend you keep exploring science in the future."
