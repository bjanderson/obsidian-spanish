## English Translation

Marta lives in a small apartment, and her neighbor, Mr. Ruiz, plays music very loudly every night. One night, Marta can't sleep because of the noise.

The next day, Marta decides to talk to him. "Excuse me, Mr. Ruiz," she tells him in a kind voice. "Could you turn down the music at night? I can't sleep." Mr. Ruiz looks at her, a little surprised. "Sorry, I didn't know it bothered you so much," he answers.

"Please, turn off the music after ten," Marta asks him. "And if you need anything, let me know. We can be good neighbors." Mr. Ruiz smiles. "You're right. I promise."

A few days later, Mr. Ruiz knocks on Marta's door. "I'm bringing you this," he tells her, showing her a ladder. "Your husband lent it to me last month, and I still haven't given it back to you." Marta thanks him.

Since that day, Mr. Ruiz turns down his music at night, and he and Marta talk every time they see each other in the building. "In the end, he turned out to be a good neighbor," Marta tells her husband that night.
