# El café de la mañana

[[cada|Cada]] [[mañana]], Diego se [[despertar|despierta]] a las seis. [[el-la-los-las|El]] [[sol]] [[entrar|entra]] por [[el-la-los-las|la]] [[ventana]] [[y]] Diego [[levantar|se levanta]] de la cama.

Diego [[caminar|camina]] a la [[cocina]]. [[hay|Hay]] una taza [[sobre]] la [[mesa]]. Diego prepara café [[con]] [[mucho-muchos|mucho]] [[cuidado]].

[[mientras|Mientras]] el café está listo, Diego corta [[el-la-los-las|el]] [[pan]] [[y]] [[poner|pone]] mermelada [[encima]]. [[el-la-los-las|El]] olor del café [[llenar|llena]] la cocina.

Diego se [[sentar|sienta]] [[en]] [[el-la-los-las|la]] [[silla]] [[y]] [[beber|bebe]] su café. [[leer|Lee]] el [[periódico]] [[y]] [[comer|come]] su pan [[despacio]].

[[después|Después]] del [[desayuno]], Diego se [[vestir|viste]]. Se [[poner|pone]] la [[camisa]] [[y]] los [[zapato|zapatos]]. [[mirar|Mira]] el [[reloj]]: [[ser|son]] las siete.

Diego [[salir|sale]] de [[casa]] [[y]] [[caminar|camina]] al [[trabajo]]. [[el-la-los-las|El]] [[día]] [[empezar|empieza]] [[bien]] [[con]] una [[bueno-buena-buenos-buenas|buena]] taza de café.

## English Translation

Every morning, Diego wakes up at six. The sun comes in through the window and Diego gets up from the bed.

Diego walks to the kitchen. There is a cup on the table. Diego prepares coffee very carefully.

While the coffee is ready, Diego cuts the bread and puts jam on it. The smell of the coffee fills the kitchen.

Diego sits in the chair and drinks his coffee. He reads the newspaper and eats his bread slowly.

After breakfast, Diego gets dressed. He puts on his shirt and shoes. He looks at the clock: it is seven.

Diego leaves the house and walks to work. The day starts well with a good cup of coffee.
