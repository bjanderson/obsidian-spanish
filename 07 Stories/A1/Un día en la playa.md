# Un día en la playa

[[es|Es]] un [[día]] de [[verano]]. [[el-la-los-las|La]] [[familia]] de Sara [[ir|va]] a [[el-la-los-las|la]] [[playa]]. [[hacer|Hace]] [[mucho-muchos|mucho]] [[calor]] [[y]] el [[sol]] brilla [[en]] el [[cielo]] [[azul]].

[[en|En]] la playa, [[hay|hay]] mucha arena. [[el-la-los-las|Los]] [[niño-niña|niños]] [[jugar|juegan]] [[con]] la [[pelota]] [[cerca]] del [[agua]]. El [[mar]] [[tener|tiene]] muchas olas [[grande|grandes]].

Sara [[y]] su [[hermano-hermana|hermano]] [[nadar|nadan]] [[en]] el agua [[frío-fría-fríos-frías|fría]]. El agua [[estar|está]] [[fresco-fresca-frescos-frescas|fresca]] [[y]] [[estar|están]] [[feliz|felices]].

[[su-sus|Su]] [[padre-madre|madre]] [[traer|trae]] [[comida]] [[y]] la [[poner|pone]] [[sobre]] una toalla grande. Su [[padre-madre|padre]] [[jugar|juega]] [[con]] la arena [[junto-junta-juntos-juntas|junto]] a [[el-la-los-las|los]] [[niño-niña|niños]].

[[por|Por]] la [[tarde]], [[hacer|hace]] [[viento]] [[y]] el [[cielo]] se [[llenar|llena]] de [[nube|nubes]]. La familia [[recoger|recoge]] sus [[cosa|cosas]] [[y]] [[caminar|camina]] a [[casa]].

[[al|Al]] final del día, [[todo-todos|todos]] [[estar|están]] [[cansado-cansada-cansados-cansadas|cansados]] [[pero]] [[feliz|felices]]. [[es|Es]] un [[bueno-buena-buenos-buenas|buen]] día [[en]] la playa.

## English Translation

It is a summer day. Sara's family goes to the beach. It is very hot and the sun shines in the blue sky.

At the beach, there is a lot of sand. The children play with the ball near the water. The sea has many big waves.

Sara and her brother swim in the cold water. The water is cool and they are happy.

Their mother brings food and puts it on a big towel. Their father plays with the sand next to the children.

In the afternoon, it gets windy and the sky fills with clouds. The family gathers their things and walks home.

At the end of the day, everyone is tired but happy. It is a good day at the beach.
