# El cumpleaños del abuelo

[[hoy|Hoy]] [[es|es]] el [[cumpleaños]] del [[abuelo-abuela|abuelo]] Miguel. Toda la [[familia]] [[venir|viene]] a su [[casa]] [[para]] celebrar.

Los [[nieto-nieta|nietos]] [[traer|traen]] [[globo|globos]] de [[color|colores]] [[y]] [[poner|ponen]] una [[mesa]] [[grande]] [[con]] un [[pastel]]. El pastel [[tener|tiene]] [[mucho-muchos|muchas]] [[vela|velas]].

[[el-la-los-las|La]] [[abuelo-abuela|abuela]] [[cocinar|cocina]] la comida favorita del abuelo [[con]] [[mucho-muchos|mucho]] [[cuidado]].

[[cuando|Cuando]] el abuelo [[llegar|llega]], toda la familia [[decir|dice]]: "¡[[sorpresa]]!" El abuelo [[sonreír|sonríe]] [[mucho-muchos|mucho]] [[y]] [[estar|está]] [[muy]] [[feliz]].

[[el-la-los-las|La]] familia [[cantar|canta]] "Feliz cumpleaños" [[para]] el abuelo. [[todo-todos|Todos]] [[reír|ríen]] [[y]] [[estar|están]] [[contento-contenta-contentos-contentas|contentos]].

[[después|Después]], el abuelo [[abrir|abre]] sus [[regalo|regalos]] [[con]] [[mucho-muchos|mucho]] [[cuidado]]. Sus nietos [[le-les|le]] [[dar|dan]] un regalo [[especial]]: un [[libro]] [[sobre]] la familia.

El abuelo [[mirar|mira]] el libro [[y]] [[decir|dice]]: "[[este-esta|Este]] [[es|es]] el [[mejor]] cumpleaños de [[mi-mis|mi]] [[vida]]." Toda la familia [[estar|está]] [[feliz]] [[junto-junta-juntos-juntas|junta]].

## English Translation

Today is grandpa Miguel's birthday. The whole family comes to his house to celebrate.

The grandchildren bring colorful balloons and set up a big table with a cake. The cake has many candles.

Grandmother cooks grandfather's favorite food very carefully.

When grandpa arrives, the whole family says, "Surprise!" Grandpa smiles a lot and is very happy.

The family sings "Happy Birthday" to grandpa. Everyone laughs and is happy.

Afterward, grandpa opens his gifts very carefully. His grandchildren give him a special gift: a book about the family.

Grandpa looks at the book and says, "This is the best birthday of my life." The whole family is happy together.
