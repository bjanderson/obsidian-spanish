# Vacaciones en familia

[[el-la-los-las|La]] familia Ramírez [[viajar|viaja]] de [[vacaciones]] a [[el-la-los-las|la]] [[ciudad]] de Cusco. [[llevar|Llevan]] sus maletas [[y]] [[subir|suben]] al [[avión]] [[temprano]] [[por]] la [[mañana]].

## English Translation

The Ramírez family travels on vacation to the city of Cusco. They carry their suitcases and board the plane early in the morning.

After a few hours, they arrive at the airport and take a bus to the city. The father looks at the map and searches for the way to the hotel.

The hotel is near downtown, but the family walks far to see the mountains. They follow the path carefully.

In the afternoon, they walk through the streets of the city. They see stores, people, and a lot of history.

At the end of the day, everyone is tired but happy. They return to the hotel and sleep well.
