## English Translation

Every week, don Carlos goes to the market to buy fruits and vegetables. He walks slowly but with a lot of energy.

At the market, there are many people. Don Carlos looks for fresh fruit and buys three kilos.

A man sells him bread and cheese. Don Carlos asks, "How much is the price?"

"Five coins," says the man. Don Carlos pays with his money and says thank you.

Afterward, don Carlos buys two eggs and a little milk. He carries everything in a bag.

At the end, don Carlos is tired but happy. He walks home with all his food.
