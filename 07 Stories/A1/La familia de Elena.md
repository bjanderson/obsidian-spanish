## English Translation

Elena lives in a house with her husband Pablo and their two children, Ana and Luis. The family is happy and works together every day.

In the morning, Elena cooks breakfast for everyone. Pablo drinks coffee and reads the newspaper at the table.

After eating, Elena cleans the kitchen. Ana and Luis help their mother; they carry the cups to the kitchen.

Pablo takes care of the garden. He cuts the flowers and takes care of the plants very carefully.

In the afternoon, the whole family plays in the yard. The dog runs and the children laugh.

At night, they eat together and talk about the day. Elena and Pablo are happy with their family.
