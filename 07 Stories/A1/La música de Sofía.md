## English Translation

Sofía has a guitar. She likes making music a lot.

Today, her friends come to her house. Diego and Camila arrive with a new song.

In the living room, Sofía plays the guitar. Diego sings the song. Camila dances with joy.

"How fun!" says Camila. Everyone laughs and they are happy.

Afterward, they listen to more music together. They dance and sing all day long.

Sofía is happy. Diego and Camila are her best friends.
