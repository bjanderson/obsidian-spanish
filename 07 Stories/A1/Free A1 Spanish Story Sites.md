# Free A1 Spanish Story Sites

A curated list of free websites with A1-level Spanish reading practice.
Read the stories on each site directly — text is not copied into this vault,
since most of these sites are copyrighted (even when free to read).

## MeloLingua
https://melolingua.com/spanish-stories-for-beginners

Free, no login required. Five A1-level stories (~95-125 words each) with
native audio at three speeds, tap-to-translate glosses, guided shadowing
practice, and comprehension questions. Each story tags its grammar focus
(present tense, reflexive verbs, ser vs. tener, etc.).

## Lenguia
https://www.lenguia.com/stories/spanish

Graded stories from A1-A2 through B1-B2, with translations and interactive
reading features. Browse the "Beginner A1/A2" category. Some features may
be gated behind a free account — check on arrival.

## Snappy Spanish
https://snappyspanish.com/

Free, no login required. A1-level stories with short sentences, slow/clear
native audio, side-by-side Spanish-English translation, and vocabulary
flashcard review.

## Inklingo
https://www.inklingo.app/spanish/stories

Free to read on the website, no login required. Ten A1 stories (plus A0 and
A2) with audio narration, illustrations, level-filtered vocabulary, and
optional tap-to-translate and comprehension quizzes.

## StoryWeaver (Pratham Books)
https://storyweaver.org.in/en/search?query=&language=Spanish&level=1

A large open library of illustrated children's stories, openly licensed
under CC BY. Filter by Language: Spanish and Level: 1 for the simplest
tier. Not written specifically as a language-learner resource, but the
language is genuinely simple (children's early-reader level).
