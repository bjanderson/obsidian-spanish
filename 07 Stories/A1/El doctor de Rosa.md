## English Translation

Rosa doesn't feel well. She has a fever and her stomach hurts. Her mother looks at her carefully.

"Let's go to the clinic," says her mother. Rosa and her mother go to the clinic to see the doctor.

At the clinic, there are many patients. Rosa waits with her mother.

The doctor looks at Rosa and listens to her chest. He says that Rosa has the flu.

The doctor gives Rosa medicine and says, "Drink a lot of water and rest."

Rosa returns home with her mother. She takes the medicine and sleeps a lot.

After a few days, Rosa feels better. She no longer has a fever or a cough. She is healthy again.
