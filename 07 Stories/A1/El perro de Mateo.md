# El perro de Mateo

Mateo [[tener|tiene]] un [[perro]]. El perro se [[llamar|llama]] Rex. Rex [[es|es]] [[grande]] [[y]] [[negro-negra-negros-negras|negro]]. [[a|A]] Mateo [[le-les|le]] [[gustar|gusta]] [[mucho-muchos|mucho]] [[su-sus|su]] perro.

[[cada|Cada]] [[día]], Mateo [[ir|va]] [[al]] [[parque]] [[con]] Rex. [[hacer|Hace]] [[sol]] [[y]] el [[cielo]] [[estar|está]] [[azul]].

[[en|En]] el parque, Mateo tiene una [[pelota]] [[rojo-roja-rojos-rojas|roja]]. "¡Vamos, Rex!" [[decir|dice]] Mateo. Rex [[correr|corre]] [[muy]] [[rápido-rápida-rápidos-rápidas|rápido]].

Mateo [[lanzar|lanza]] la pelota. Rex corre y [[buscar|busca]] la pelota. Rex [[traer|trae]] la pelota a Mateo. "¡[[bueno-buena-buenos-buenas|Buen]] perro!" dice Mateo, y [[reír|ríe]].

[[jugar|Juegan]] con la pelota [[durante]] mucho [[tiempo]]. Mateo y Rex están [[feliz|felices]].

[[después|Después]] de jugar, Mateo y Rex están [[cansado-cansada-cansados-cansadas|cansados]]. [[caminar|Caminan]] a [[casa]]. Rex [[beber|bebe]] [[agua]] en su casa.

[[por|Por]] la [[noche]], Rex [[dormir|duerme]] [[cerca]] de Mateo. Mateo [[lo]] [[mirar|mira]] y [[sonreír|sonríe]]. "[[ser|Eres]] [[mi-mis|mi]] [[mejor]] [[amigo-amiga|amigo]]," dice Mateo.

## English Translation

Mateo has a dog. The dog's name is Rex. Rex is big and black. Mateo likes his dog a lot.

Every day, Mateo goes to the park with Rex. It is sunny and the sky is blue.

At the park, Mateo has a red ball. "Come on, Rex!" says Mateo. Rex runs very fast.

Mateo throws the ball. Rex runs and looks for the ball. Rex brings the ball to Mateo. "Good dog!" says Mateo, and he laughs.

They play with the ball for a long time. Mateo and Rex are happy.

After playing, Mateo and Rex are tired. They walk home. Rex drinks water at his house.

At night, Rex sleeps near Mateo. Mateo looks at him and smiles. "You are my best friend," says Mateo.
