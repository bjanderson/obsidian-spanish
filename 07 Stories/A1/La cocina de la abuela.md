# La cocina de la abuela

[[en|En]] la [[cocina]], la abuela [[cocinar|cocina]] su [[receta]] favorita: [[arroz]] [[con]] [[carne]]. [[primero-primera-primeros-primeras|Primero]], [[cocinar|cocina]] el arroz [[con]] [[agua]] [[y]] [[sal]].

## English Translation

Sofía's grandmother lives in a small, pretty house. Every Sunday, the whole family goes to her house to eat.

In the kitchen, grandmother cooks her favorite recipe: rice with meat. First, she cooks the rice with water and salt.

Afterward, she cooks the meat with oil. The kitchen has a delicious smell.

Sofía and her cousins help their grandmother. They set the table and bring everything to it.

When the food is ready, everyone sits down to eat. Grandmother serves the rice with meat.

"It's delicious, grandma!" says Sofía. Grandmother smiles and is happy.

After eating, the children help clean the kitchen. Grandmother tells stories about her life while they work together.
