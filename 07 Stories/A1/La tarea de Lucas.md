# La tarea de Lucas

Lucas [[tener|tiene]] [[tarea]] [[hoy]]. [[se|Se]] [[sentar|sienta]] [[en]] la [[mesa]] [[con]] [[su-sus|su]] [[cuaderno]] [[y]] su [[lápiz]].

[[primero-primera-primeros-primeras|Primero]], Lucas [[contar|cuenta]] [[número|números]]. [[uno|Uno]], [[dos]], [[tres]], [[cuatro]], [[cinco]]. Cuenta con [[el-la-los-las|los]] [[dedo|dedos]] y [[sonreír|sonríe]]. [[es|Es]] [[fácil]].

[[después|Después]], Lucas [[leer|lee]] [[un-una|un]] [[libro]]. Lee una [[página]] y [[otro-otra-otros-otras|otra]] página. La [[historia]] es [[muy]] [[interesante]].

## English Translation

Lucas has homework today. He sits at the table with his notebook and his pencil.

First, Lucas counts numbers. One, two, three, four, five. He counts on his fingers and smiles. It is easy.

After that, Lucas reads a book. He reads one page and another page. The story is very interesting.

Then, Lucas writes. He writes new words in his notebook. He writes letter by letter, carefully.

His mother watches him and says, "How well you work!" Lucas is happy and says, "I already know how to count, read, and write!"

When he finishes his homework, Lucas puts away his notebook and his pencil. He is content because he learned a lot today.
