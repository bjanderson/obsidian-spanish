# Un paseo por el parque

[[cada|Cada]] [[mañana]], don Roberto [[caminar|camina]] [[por]] el [[parque]] [[cerca]] de su [[casa]]. [[le-les|Le]] [[gustar|gusta]] [[caminar]] [[despacio]] [[y]] [[sentir]] el [[aire]] [[fresco-fresca-frescos-frescas|fresco]].

[[en|En]] el parque, [[hay|hay]] [[mucho-muchos|muchos]] [[árbol|árboles]] [[grande|grandes]] [[y]] [[flor|flores]] de [[color|colores]]. Los [[pájaro|pájaros]] [[cantar|cantan]] [[en]] los árboles.

Don Roberto [[ver|ve]] a su [[vecino|vecina]], doña Carmen. [[ella|Ella]] [[también]] [[caminar|camina]] cada mañana. "¡[[bueno-buena-buenos-buenas|Buenos]] [[día|días]]!" [[decir|dice]] don Roberto. "¡Buenos días!" [[responder|responde]] doña Carmen.

[[caminar|Caminan]] [[junto-junta-juntos-juntas|juntos]] [[y]] [[hablar|hablan]] de su [[vida]] [[y]] su [[familia]]. El [[sol]] brilla [[y]] el [[cielo]] [[estar|está]] [[azul]].

[[después|Después]] de [[caminar]], don Roberto se [[sentar|sienta]] [[en]] un [[banco]] cerca de un árbol. Descansa un poco [[y]] [[mirar|mira]] a los [[niño-niña|niños]] [[que]] [[jugar|juegan]] [[con]] una [[pelota]].

[[por|Por]] la [[tarde]], don Roberto [[volver|vuelve]] a casa. [[estar|Está]] [[cansado-cansada-cansados-cansadas|cansado]] [[pero]] [[tranquilo-tranquila-tranquilos-tranquilas|tranquilo]]. [[le-les|Le]] [[gustar|gusta]] [[mucho-muchos|mucho]] su paseo [[por]] el parque cada [[día]].

## English Translation

Every morning, don Roberto walks through the park near his house. He likes to walk slowly and feel the fresh air.

In the park, there are many big trees and colorful flowers. The birds sing in the trees.

Don Roberto sees his neighbor, doña Carmen. She also walks every morning. "Good morning!" says don Roberto. "Good morning!" answers doña Carmen.

They walk together and talk about their life and their family. The sun shines and the sky is blue.

After walking, don Roberto sits on a bench near a tree. He rests a little and watches the children who play with a ball.

In the afternoon, don Roberto returns home. He is tired but calm. He likes his walk through the park very much every day.
