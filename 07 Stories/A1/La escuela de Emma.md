## English Translation

Emma is fourteen years old and studies at a big school in the city. She likes art a lot, but she also likes history.

Every morning, Emma walks to school with her friend Sofía. The two of them talk about music and their classes while they walk.

In math class, the teacher explains the numbers on the whiteboard. Emma listens carefully because math is difficult for her. Her friend Diego helps her with the problem.

After classes, Emma eats lunch in the courtyard with her friends. They talk and laugh a lot. Emma is very happy with her friends.

In the afternoon, Emma has science class. It is a very interesting class and Emma learns about animals and plants.

At the end of the day, Emma goes back home. She does her math homework with her brother's help. Emma thinks school is difficult, but she likes learning new things with her friends.
