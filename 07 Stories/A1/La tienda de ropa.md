# La tienda de ropa

Camila [[tener|tiene]] quince años [[y]] le [[gustar|gusta]] mucho la [[ropa]] [[nuevo-nueva-nuevos-nuevas|nueva]]. [[hoy|Hoy]] [[ir|va]] [[al]] [[centro]] de la [[ciudad]] con su [[amigo-amiga|amiga]] Ana [[para]] [[comprar]] ropa nueva.

[[en|En]] la [[tienda]], [[haber|hay]] muchas [[camisa|camisas]], [[pantalón|pantalones]] [[y]] [[vestido|vestidos]]. Camila [[mirar|mira]] un vestido [[verde]] [[y]] una [[chaqueta]] [[azul]]. "Me gusta el azul," [[decir|dice]] Camila.

Ana [[encontrar|encuentra]] una camisa [[amarillo-amarilla-amarillos-amarillas|amarilla]] [[y]] una [[falda]] [[gris]]. "¿Qué [[pensar|piensas]]?" [[preguntar|pregunta]] Ana. Camila [[creer|cree]] que la falda es [[muy]] [[bonito-bonita-bonitos-bonitas|bonita]].

Camila [[buscar|busca]] un vestido nuevo [[para]] la [[escuela]]. [[ver|Ve]] un vestido [[rosado-rosada-rosados-rosadas|rosado]] [[y]] un vestido [[morado-morada-morados-moradas|morado]]. No [[poder|puede]] [[decidir]]. [[elegir|Elige]] el vestido rosado [[porque]] le gusta mucho el [[color]].

Ana [[usar|usa]] [[zapato|zapatos]] [[negro-negra-negros-negras|negros]] con su ropa nueva. Camila [[llevar|lleva]] el vestido rosado [[y]] unos zapatos [[blanco-blanca-blancos-blancas|blancos]]. Las dos amigas [[estar|están]] [[muy]] [[contento-contenta-contentos-contentas|contentas]].

Camila [[pagar|paga]] el vestido con su [[dinero]]. El [[precio]] es [[bueno-buena-buenos-buenas|bueno]]. Camila [[salir|sale]] de la tienda [[muy]] [[feliz]] con su ropa nueva.

## English Translation

Camila is fifteen years old and she likes new clothes a lot. Today she goes downtown with her friend Ana to buy new clothes.

At the store, there are many shirts, pants, and dresses. Camila looks at a green dress and a blue jacket. "I like blue," says Camila.

Ana finds a yellow shirt and a gray skirt. "What do you think?" asks Ana. Camila thinks the skirt is very pretty.

Camila looks for a new dress for school. She sees a pink dress and a purple dress. She can't decide. She chooses the pink dress because she likes the color a lot.

Ana wears black shoes with her new clothes. Camila wears the pink dress and white shoes. The two friends are very happy.

Camila pays for the dress with her money. The price is good. Camila leaves the store very happy with her new clothes.
