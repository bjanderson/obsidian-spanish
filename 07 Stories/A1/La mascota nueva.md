# La mascota nueva

## English Translation

Camila wants a new pet. Her family also wants an animal for the house. One day, they go to a pet store.

At the store there are many animals. There are dogs and cats. Some dogs are big and others are small.

Camila looks at a small dog. The dog is brown and has big eyes. Its fur is soft. "I like it a lot!" says Camila.

The family chooses the small dog. Camila thinks of a name for the dog. "Your name is Toby," says Camila.

At home, Toby runs through the whole house and plays with a ball. Camila gives food and water to Toby. Toby eats all the food and drinks all the water.

At night, Toby sleeps near Camila. The whole family is happy with the new pet. Camila smiles and says, "Toby is my best friend."
