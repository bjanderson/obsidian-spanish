## English Translation

Today it is very cold. The family looks at the gray sky through the window. There are many clouds and the wind is strong.

"I think rain is coming," says the father. He looks for coats for the whole family.

The mother looks at the number: the temperature is five degrees. It is a very cold winter day.

The children want to play outside, but they need a lot of clothing. They put on their coats and go out carefully.

In the afternoon, the rain starts and everyone runs home.

At home, the family watches the rain through the window. The weather changes a lot in winter, but the family is happy together.
