# Story Outline

Tracks story ideas for `07 Stories/`, across all CEFR levels (A1-C2). Each idea
lists a target audience so the level's stories cover a range of ages, not just
children.

- **Status** — ✅ written, otherwise blank (idea only).
- Stories live at `07 Stories/<Level>/<Title>.md`, built with the
  `00 Templates` conventions used by `El perro de Mateo.md`, `La música de
  Sofía.md`, and `La tarea de Lucas.md` (vocabulary linked inline, English
  translation below).
- `El pueblo de Ana.md` predates this outline and had no level tag. Classified
  as B2 based on its grammar (pluperfect indicative, present and imperfect
  subjunctive, simple conditional for hypothetical/future-in-past), moved to
  `07 Stories/B2/`, and tagged `B2` to match the A1 stories' convention.
