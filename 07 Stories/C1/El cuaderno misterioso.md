## English Translation

Mateo found the notebook at the bottom of his new backpack, which his mother had bought at a secondhand shop. It had no name on the cover, only a faded drawing of an owl, whose wings covered the whole front. Inside, the pages were full of small, cramped handwriting, with scattered sentences, dates with no year, and drawings of places Mateo didn't recognize. "This isn't mine," he thought, "but I can't return it either, because I don't know whose it is."

He showed the notebook to his best friend, Valentina, whose curiosity about mysteries was even greater than his own. "Look at this page," she said, pointing to a paragraph underlined several times, in which the author talked about a hidden garden behind a dry fountain. "And here's a map," she added, "which looks hand-drawn, not printed." The two children, whose plans for that afternoon had simply been to play at the park, decided to follow the notebook's clues instead.

The map led them to the old plaza in the neighborhood, a place Mateo walked past every day without noticing anything special. There, behind a fountain that had been dry for years, they found a small forgotten garden, surrounded by a low wall whose stones were covered in moss. "This must be the garden the notebook talks about," Valentina whispered, her eyes wide open.

Among the plants, Mateo noticed a stone different from the rest, under which the corner of a metal box was peeking out. They pulled it out carefully and opened it: inside were old photographs, a small key, and a note written in the same handwriting as the notebook. The note, whose message could barely be read because of the passage of time, said: "To whoever finds this garden: thank you for not forgetting it."

Valentina suggested they ask at the neighborhood library, where an older woman worked who knew the history of almost every family in the area. The librarian, to whom they showed the notebook and the oldest photograph, immediately recognized the boy in the picture. "That's my brother," she said, surprised, "who disappeared from our lives when he moved to another city many years ago. This notebook was his."

The woman told them that her brother had secretly tended that garden throughout his childhood, a place whose existence only a few people knew about. Mateo carefully handed the notebook back to her, and she, her voice trembling, thanked them for having found something she thought was lost forever. Before leaving, Mateo and Valentina promised to take care of the garden, which, from that day on, stopped being forgotten.
