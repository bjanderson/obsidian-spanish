# El médico y los consejos

Don Alberto [[tener|tenía]] [[setenta]] años y [[ir|fue]] al [[médico]] para una consulta de rutina. La doctora revisó su [[corazón]] y le [[preguntar|preguntó]] sobre su [[salud]].

La doctora lo examinó con cuidado y le [[dar|dio]] algunos consejos. "Su corazón [[estar|está]] [[bien]], pero [[necesitar|necesita]] hacer [[más]] [[ejercicio]]," le [[explicar|explicó]]. "También [[deber|debe]] [[comer]] [[menos]] sal y [[más]] [[fruta|frutas]] y [[vegetal|vegetales]]."

## English Translation

Don Alberto was seventy years old and went to the doctor for a routine checkup. The doctor checked his heart and asked him about his health.

"How are you feeling, Don Alberto?" the doctor asked. "I feel fine, but sometimes I have pain in my legs after walking," he answered.

The doctor examined him carefully and gave him some advice. "Your heart is fine, but you need to exercise more," she explained. "You should also eat less salt and more fruits and vegetables."

"Do I have to stop eating my favorite desserts?" Don Alberto asked with concern. The doctor laughed. "You don't need to stop eating them completely, but you should eat them less often."

"Every morning, walk for thirty minutes in the park," the doctor continued. "And drink plenty of water during the day."

Don Alberto listened to all the advice carefully. "Thank you, doctor. I'm going to follow your advice," he said. "I want to live many more years with my family."

A month later, Don Alberto returned for the follow-up visit. He was walking faster and no longer had pain in his legs. "Your advice worked very well," he told the doctor with a smile.
