# La fiesta de fin de año

Valentina [[querer|quería]] organizar una [[fiesta]] de fin de año para sus [[amigo|amigos]]. Faltaban dos [[semana|semanas]] para el treinta y uno de [[diciembre]], y ella ya [[tener|tenía]] muchas [[idea|ideas]].

"¿Qué vamos a hacer para la fiesta?" le [[preguntar|preguntó]] Valentina a su amiga Ana. "Primero, [[necesitar|necesitamos]] una lista de invitados," [[responder|respondió]] Ana. "Después, [[poder|podemos]] pensar en la [[comida]] y la [[música]]."

Las dos [[amigo-amiga|amigas]] [[hacer|hicieron]] una lista con los [[nombre|nombres]] de [[todo-todos|todos]] sus amigos. Valentina [[decidir|decidió]] mandar las invitaciones por [[mensaje]]. "Voy a [[escribir]] un mensaje [[especial]] para [[cada]] persona," [[decir|dijo]].

Nicolás, otro amigo, [[llamar|llamó]] a Valentina para [[ofrecer|ofrecer]] su ayuda. "[[poder|Puedo]] [[traer]] la música," [[decir|dijo]]. "Tengo muchas canciones [[nuevo-nueva-nuevos-nuevas|nuevas]] para bailar." Valentina [[estar|estuvo]] muy [[contento-contenta-contentos-contentas|contenta]] con la oferta.

[[cuando|Cuando]] [[llegar|llegó]] el treinta y uno de diciembre, [[todo-todos|todos]] los amigos [[llegar|llegaron]] a la [[casa]] de Valentina. Había [[globo|globos]] de muchos [[color|colores]] y un [[pastel]] [[grande]] en la mesa. La música de Nicolás sonaba [[fuerte]] y [[todo-todos|todos]] bailaban.

## English Translation

Valentina wanted to organize a New Year's Eve party for her friends. There were two weeks left until December thirty-first, and she already had many ideas.

"What are we going to do for the party?" Valentina asked her friend Ana. "First, we need a guest list," Ana answered. "After that, we can think about the food and the music."

The two friends made a list with the names of all their friends. Valentina decided to send the invitations by message. "I'm going to write a special message for each person," she said.

Nicolás, another friend, called Valentina to offer his help. "I can bring the music," he said. "I have a lot of new songs to dance to." Valentina was very happy with the offer.

When December thirty-first arrived, all the friends arrived at Valentina's house. There were balloons of many colors and a big cake on the table. Nicolás's music was playing loudly and everyone was dancing.

At midnight, everyone shouted "Happy New Year!" and hugged each other. Valentina looked at her friends and thought it had been the best party of the year. "Next year," she said, "we're going to have an even bigger party."
