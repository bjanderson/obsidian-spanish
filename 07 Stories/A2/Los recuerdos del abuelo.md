## English Translation

Grandpa Pedro sat down in his favorite chair and called his grandchildren. "Come here, I want to tell you a story from my childhood," he said with a smile.

The grandchildren, Camila and Tomás, sat down near him to listen.

"When I was a child, we lived in a small town," Grandpa began. "There was no television or phones like now. We played in the street all day with the friends from the neighborhood."

"Did you play video games?" Tomás asked. Grandpa laughed. "No, there was none of that. We played soccer with an old ball, or we rode bikes for hours."

"Every Sunday, my mother would make a sweet cake for the whole family," Grandpa continued. "And with the coins I earned helping at the town store, I would buy candy for my brothers."

Camila asked: "Do you miss that time, Grandpa?" Grandpa thought for a moment. "Sometimes I do. Life was simpler back then, but I'm very happy to have my family here now."

At the end, Grandpa hugged his grandchildren and said: "Memories are a treasure. Someday, you're going to tell your own stories to your grandchildren."
