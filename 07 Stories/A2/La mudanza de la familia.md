## English Translation

The Ramírez family was going to move to a new house next month. The new house had more space and a big garden.

"When we live in the new house, we'll have a patio to play in," the mother told her children. Ana and Luis were very excited about the change.

"Will I be able to have my own room?" Ana asked. "Yes," her father answered. "Each of you will have your own room, and you'll be able to paint the walls whatever color you want."

Before moving, the family had to pack all their things into boxes. Luis helped pack his books and his toys. Ana packed her clothes very carefully.

On moving day, a big truck arrived at the house. The workers carried the furniture and the boxes to the truck. The whole family worked together all day.

When they arrived at the new house, everyone helped carry the boxes to the rooms. Ana chose blue for her room, and Luis chose green.

In the future, the family is going to meet their new neighbors and explore the new neighborhood together. Everyone thinks they're going to be very happy in their new house.
