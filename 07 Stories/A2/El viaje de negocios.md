## English Translation

Ricardo worked for a big company and traveled a lot for work. This week, he had to go to another city for a business trip.

Ricardo took a very early flight and arrived at the new city's airport at nine in the morning. He looked for a taxi and gave the driver the hotel's address.

The taxi took him through many different streets. Ricardo looked out the window and saw tall buildings and a lot of people. After twenty minutes, they arrived at the hotel.

At the hotel's front desk, a very kind woman gave him his room key. "Your meeting is at two in the afternoon at the main office," she explained. "It's three blocks from here, near a big park."

Ricardo left his suitcase in the room and went out walking to the office. At first, he got a little lost because the streets were different from the ones in his hometown. But he asked a person on the street, and she showed him the right way.

Ricardo arrived at the meeting on time. His colleagues welcomed him with a smile. At the end of the day, Ricardo thought the trip had gone better than he expected.
