# Un fin de semana en el campo

## English Translation

The Gómez family decided to spend the weekend in the countryside, at the grandparents' farm. The children, Sara and Diego, were very excited because it was their first visit to the farm.

When they arrived, the grandfather took them to see the animals. There were cows, chickens, and horses. Sara had never seen a cow so close, and Diego wanted to ride a horse.

"There aren't animals like this in the city," Sara said. "Everything here is calmer than in the city, but also slower," her grandmother answered with a smile.

In the afternoon, everyone helped pick fruits and vegetables from the garden. Diego learned to gather eggs from the hens.

At night, there were no city lights, and the sky was full of stars. "I've never seen so many stars," Sara said, looking up.

At the end of the weekend, Sara and Diego didn't want to go back to the city. "The countryside is calmer and the air is fresher, but I miss my friends from the city," Diego said. "We can come back another weekend," their mother told him.
