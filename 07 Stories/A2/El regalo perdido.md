# El regalo perdido

En su [[cumpleaños]], su [[abuelo-abuela|abuela]] le [[dar|dio]] un [[regalo]] [[especial]]: un collar [[pequeño-pequeña-pequeños-pequeñas|pequeño]] con una [[estrella]]. ## English Translation

Sofía was eight years old. For her birthday, her grandmother gave her a special gift: a small necklace with a star. Sofía liked the necklace a lot, and she wore it every day.

One afternoon, Sofía was playing in the garden with her sister. It was very hot and the sky was blue. Suddenly, Sofía looked at her neck and the necklace was no longer there.

Sofía felt very sad. She ran to the house and said to her mother: "I lost my necklace! I looked in the garden, but I didn't find it."

Her mother hugged her and said: "Don't cry. Let's look together." Her father and her sister also wanted to help.

The whole family looked all over the house. They looked under the table and near the door. Finally, her sister found the necklace under a chair.

Sofía smiled and hugged her sister. "Thank you! You're the best sister," she said to her. Her family was very happy.

From that day on, Sofía kept the necklace in a special box when she wasn't wearing it.
