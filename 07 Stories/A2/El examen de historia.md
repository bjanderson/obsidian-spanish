## English Translation

Camila and Andrés had a history exam on Friday. Andrés was very nervous because he didn't like studying much. Camila, on the other hand, was very organized and always started studying with plenty of time.

"When are you going to study for the exam?" Camila asked Andrés at the library. "I don't know," he answered. "Maybe Thursday night." Camila smiled and said: "That's very little time. I started three days ago."

Andrés thought his habit was normal, but Camila had a different opinion. "For me, it's easier to study a little each day than to study a lot in one night," she explained. "That way I remember more information and I'm less worried."

That afternoon, Andrés decided to study with Camila at the library. They read the history book together, and Camila explained the hardest dates to him. Andrés understood better when he studied with someone more organized.

On the day of the exam, Andrés was less nervous than before. When the exam ended, he smiled and said to Camila: "I think it went well. Thanks for helping me study a different way."

A week later, the teacher gave them their results. Andrés got a good grade, almost as good as Camila's. "You were right," he told his friend. "Studying a little each day is better than studying everything in one night." From that day on, Andrés changed his study habit.
