## English Translation

Emma's class went on a field trip to the zoo that morning. All the students were very excited. The teacher told them they were going to see many different animals.

When they arrived at the zoo, they first saw the elephants. Emma thought the elephants were huge but very calm.

Then, the group walked to the lion area. Diego liked the lions a lot because they were strong and brave. But Sofía didn't like them as much; she thought the monkeys were more fun because they played all day.

"For me, the lion is the most interesting animal in the zoo," Diego said. "I think differently," Sofía answered. "The monkeys are better because they're always playing."

Emma didn't say anything at first. When they saw the giraffes, she knew which was her favorite animal. "Look how long its neck is," she said. "For me, the giraffe is the prettiest of all."

After seeing the tigers and the turtles, the class ate lunch near a lake. The teacher asked the students which was their favorite animal and why. Each child had a different opinion.

At the end of the day, everyone went back to school tired but happy. Emma thought it had been the best field trip of the year.
