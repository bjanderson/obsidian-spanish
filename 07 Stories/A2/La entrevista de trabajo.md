## English Translation

Valeria had finished college two months ago and was looking for her first job. One day, she saw an ad for a position at a big company downtown.

Valeria sent her résumé, and a week later, they called her for an interview.

On the day of the interview, Valeria put on a blue suit and a white shirt. She was very nervous, but she also felt ready for the challenge.

At the office, a woman, the company's manager, greeted her and asked: "Why do you want to work with us?" Valeria explained that she had a lot of experience working with customers and that she liked that kind of work a lot.

The manager also asked her about her studies and her degree from the university. Valeria spoke calmly about her favorite classes and the projects she did during her studies.

At the end of the interview, the manager smiled and told her: "I liked your résumé and your way of speaking a lot. We're going to call you next week."

Valeria left the office very happy. She thought the interview had gone well.

Five days later, the phone rang. It was the manager. "We have the job for you," she told her. "Welcome to the company!" Valeria felt very happy. All the effort of job hunting finally paid off.
