# La cena de aniversario

Laura y Miguel celebraban [[diez]] años de casados. Miguel reservó una [[mesa]] en un [[restaurante]] [[especial]] para la ocasión.

[[cuando|Cuando]] [[llegar|llegaron]] al restaurante, un mesero los [[llevar|llevó]] a una mesa [[cerca]] de la [[ventana]]. La mesa [[tener|tenía]] una [[vela]] y [[flor|flores]] [[rojo-roja-rojos-rojas|rojas]].

El mesero les [[dar|dio]] el menú y les [[preguntar|preguntó]] qué [[querer|querían]] tomar. Miguel [[pedir|pidió]] una botella de [[vino]] tinto para celebrar. Laura [[pedir|pidió]] [[pescado]], y Miguel [[pedir|pidió]] [[carne]] con [[papa|papas]].

Mientras [[comer|comían]], [[hablar|hablaron]] de los diez años [[junto-junta-juntos-juntas|juntos]]: su [[primero-primera-primeros-primeras|primera]] [[casa]], el nacimiento de sus [[hijo-hija|hijos]], y [[todo-todos|todos]] los viajes que [[hacer|hicieron]] juntos.

Al [[final]] de la [[cena]], el mesero [[traer|trajo]] un [[postre]] [[especial]] con una vela [[pequeño-pequeña-pequeños-pequeñas|pequeña]]. "Feliz aniversario," [[decir|dijo]] con una sonrisa. Laura y Miguel se [[mirar|miraron]] y [[sonreír|sonrieron]].

[[cuando|Cuando]] [[pedir|pidieron]] la [[cuenta]], Miguel le [[dar|dio]] las gracias al mesero por una [[noche]] tan especial. "Diez [[año|años]] pasaron muy [[rápido-rápida-rápidos-rápidas|rápido]]," le [[decir|dijo]] a Laura. "Pero cada año [[contigo]] es [[mejor]] que el anterior."

## English Translation

Laura and Miguel were celebrating ten years of marriage. Miguel had reserved a table at a special restaurant for the occasion.

When they arrived at the restaurant, a waiter led them to a table near the window. The table had a candle and red flowers.

The waiter gave them the menu and asked what they wanted to drink. Miguel ordered a bottle of red wine to celebrate. Laura ordered fish, and Miguel ordered meat with potatoes.

As they ate, they talked about their ten years together: their first house, the birth of their children, and all the trips they took together.

At the end of dinner, the waiter brought a special dessert with a small candle. "Happy anniversary," he said with a smile. Laura and Miguel looked at each other and smiled.

When they asked for the check, Miguel thanked the waiter for such a special evening. "Ten years went by so fast," he told Laura. "But every year with you is better than the one before."
