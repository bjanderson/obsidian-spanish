# Las vacaciones de verano

## English Translation

The Torres family was thinking about their summer vacation. The parents, Carlos and Marta, couldn't decide between going to the beach or the mountains.

"I like the beach more," said Emilia, the older daughter. "We can swim in the sea and sunbathe." Tomás, her younger brother, thought differently. "I prefer the mountains. There are more things to do: hiking, seeing animals, and the air is cooler."

Carlos listened to his children and said: "The beach is hotter than the mountains, but the mountains are calmer than the beach. What if we do both?"

Marta had an idea. "We can spend the first five days at the beach and then five days in the mountains. That way everyone is happy." The whole family agreed with the plan.

At the beach, they swam in the sea and built sandcastles. In the mountains, they hiked through the forest and slept in a small cabin. In the end, Emilia and Tomás said that both parts of the vacation were equally fun.
