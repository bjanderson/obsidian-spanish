## English Translation

Diego and Mateo had been best friends since they were very young. They lived on the same street and played together every day after school. Diego was taller than Mateo, but Mateo ran faster than Diego.

One day, Mateo arrived at Diego's house with sad news. "My family is moving to another city," Mateo said. "My father got a new job."

Diego felt very sad. "When are you leaving?" he asked. "In two weeks," Mateo answered. "The new city is bigger than this one, but it doesn't have a park as nice as the one here."

The two friends spent their last days together. They played soccer in the park, watched movies, and ate ice cream like always. Diego thought these days were less fun than other days, because he knew Mateo was leaving soon.

On the day Mateo moved, Diego helped Mateo carry some boxes to the truck. "I'm going to miss you a lot," Diego told him. "Me too," Mateo answered. "But we can video chat every day. And in the summer, I'm going to visit."

Diego smiled. "That's true. The distance is big, but our friendship is stronger than the distance."

Mateo hugged Diego before getting into the car. "You're my best friend, and that's never going to change."

From that day on, Diego and Mateo talked by video every week. Their friendship wasn't the same as before, but it was still special.
