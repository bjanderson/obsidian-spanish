## English Translation

Patricia and Rodrigo worked together on the same team. They had to finish an important project before Friday, but there was a problem.

Rodrigo didn't finish his part of the project on time. Patricia was very upset because she needed his part to continue with the work. "Why didn't you finish the report?" Patricia asked him, a little annoyed.

"I had a problem with my computer and lost a lot of time," Rodrigo explained. "I'm really sorry. I know it's my fault."

Patricia took a deep breath and thought about how to solve the problem without arguing more. "It's okay," she told him. "We can ask the boss for more time and work together this afternoon."

Together, they talked to the boss and explained the situation. The boss listened calmly. "Thank you for telling me the truth," he said. "You can have one more day to finish the project."

That afternoon, Patricia and Rodrigo worked together without any problems. They learned that it's better to talk about a problem than to stay silent. In the end, they turned in the project on time, and the boss was very happy with their work.
