## English Translation

Fernanda had lived in a small town her whole life. When she got a new job in the city, she had to move.

The first day in the city, Fernanda felt a lot of noise. Cars and buses were passing by all the time, and the streets were always full of people. In her town, everything was much calmer.

Her new apartment was smaller than her house back in the town, but it was close to everything: work, stores, and the metro. In the town, she needed her car for everything, but in the city she could walk or use public transportation.

At first, Fernanda missed her family and her friends a lot. She also missed the fresh air and the silence of the countryside. The city had more people, but she felt lonelier than before.

Over time, Fernanda got to know her neighbors and made new friends at work. She started enjoying the restaurants, museums, and parks in the city. Little by little, she got used to the fast pace of city life.

After six months, Fernanda thought differently about the city. "There are more opportunities here than in my town," she told her mother on the phone. "But sometimes I miss the peace and quiet back there. Both lives have something good."
