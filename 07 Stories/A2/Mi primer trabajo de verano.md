## English Translation

Lucía wanted her first summer job. She looked in many stores downtown until she found an ad in a clothing store: "Looking for a summer helper."

Lucía filled out an application, and two days later, the store owner called her for an interview. Lucía was very nervous, but she answered every question calmly.

"You can start on Monday," the owner told her with a smile. Lucía felt very happy. Finally, she had her first job.

On the first day, Lucía learned to fold clothes and show customers the new styles. At first, it was hard to remember where everything was, but over time she learned faster.

One afternoon, a customer was looking for a shirt in a specific color. Lucía helped her find the right size, and the customer left very satisfied. The owner saw everything and told her: "You're very kind with the customers."

At the end of the summer, Lucía received her last paycheck and felt very proud of her work. She earned her own money for the first time and learned a lot about work and responsibility.
