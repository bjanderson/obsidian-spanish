## English Translation

Doña Elena received an invitation to her high school reunion. Forty years had passed since she finished her studies there.

On the day of the reunion, Elena arrived at the room where she used to study when she was young. At first, she didn't recognize anyone. Everyone was much older than in her memories.

Suddenly, a woman came up to her and said: "Elena? It's me, Beatriz!" Elena looked at her in surprise and then smiled. "Beatriz! I can't believe it," she said, and the two of them hugged with great joy.

Her old classmates began arriving one by one. Elena recognized some by their smile, others by their voice. Together, they remembered their favorite teachers and the jokes they used to make in class.

"Do you remember when the math teacher gave us so much homework?" Beatriz asked, laughing. "How could I forget that!" Elena answered. Everyone laughed together, remembering those years of their youth.

At the end of the night, Elena looked at her classmates and felt a wave of emotion. "Even though so many years have passed, we're still the same family," she said. Everyone agreed and promised to see each other again next year.
