# Restrictive vs Non Restrictive Relative Clauses (El cual, La cual, Los cuales) — Conversational Sentences

## Adding extra info about someone already identified

Non-restrictive clause with *el cual/la cual*, set off by a pause — the person is
already clear before the clause starts.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Mi vecino, el cual trabaja en el banco, me ayudó con el trámite. | My neighbor, who works at the bank, helped me with the paperwork. | *El cual* refers back to *mi vecino* (masc.). |
| La profesora, la cual estudió en España, nos enseña gramática avanzada. | The teacher, who studied in Spain, teaches us advanced grammar. | *La cual* agrees with *la profesora*. |
| Los dueños, los cuales viven en otro país, alquilan la casa por internet. | The owners, who live in another country, rent the house online. | Plural masc. antecedent. |
| Nuestra vecina, la cual acaba de mudarse, todavía no conoce a nadie. | Our neighbor, who just moved in, doesn't know anyone yet. | Recent-move context, common in small talk. |

## Referring back through a preposition

*El cual* forms after simple or compound prepositions, common when talking
about places, tools, or reasons.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Esa es la puerta por la cual entramos anoche. | That's the door through which we came in last night. | Simple preposition *por*. |
| El parque, cerca del cual vivimos, tiene una laguna enorme. | The park, near which we live, has a huge pond. | Compound preposition *cerca de*. |
| No encuentro el motivo por el cual se enojó. | I can't find the reason why he got upset. | *Por el cual* = "why/for which." |
| Ese es el banco en el cual abrí mi cuenta. | That's the bank where I opened my account. | *En el cual* referring to a place. |
| Son los amigos con los cuales fuimos de viaje. | Those are the friends we went on the trip with. | Plural after *con*. |

## Clarifying which noun the clause points to

When two possible antecedents of different genders appear, *el cual/la cual*
removes the ambiguity that *que* would leave.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Hablé con el hermano de mi jefa, el cual acaba de llegar de Chile. | I talked with my boss's brother, who just got back from Chile. | *El cual* = the brother, not the boss. |
| Conocí a la hija del gerente, la cual estudia medicina. | I met the manager's daughter, who is studying medicine. | *La cual* = the daughter, not the manager. |
| Vimos al hijo de la directora, el cual trabaja en otra ciudad. | We saw the director's son, who works in another city. | Same disambiguation pattern with a different pairing. |
| Saludé a la asistente del doctor, la cual conocía a mi mamá. | I said hi to the doctor's assistant, who knew my mom. | *La cual* points to the assistant. |

## Talking about events with extra detail attached

Non-restrictive clauses describing a meeting, trip, or process already named,
often useful in work or school contexts.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Tuvimos una llamada, durante la cual resolvimos casi todo. | We had a call, during which we resolved almost everything. | *Durante la cual* for a time-bound event. |
| El viaje, el cual duró tres semanas, nos cambió la perspectiva. | The trip, which lasted three weeks, changed our perspective. | *El cual* referring to *el viaje*. |
| Firmamos el contrato, sin el cual no podíamos empezar a trabajar. | We signed the contract, without which we couldn't start working. | *Sin el cual* for a precondition. |
| La capacitación, gracias a la cual aprendimos el sistema nuevo, fue muy útil. | The training, thanks to which we learned the new system, was very useful. | *Gracias a la cual* in a workplace context. |
