# Possessive Relative Pronoun Cuyo/Cuya and Indefinite Relatives (Quienquiera, Cualquiera) — Conversational Sentences

## Describing a person by something that belongs to them

*Cuyo/cuya* connecting a person to something they own, agreeing with the thing
owned.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Hablé con el vecino cuyo perro se perdió la semana pasada. | I talked to the neighbor whose dog got lost last week. | *Cuyo* agrees with *perro* (masc.). |
| Es la cliente cuya tarjeta fue rechazada esta mañana. | She's the client whose card was declined this morning. | *Cuya* agrees with *tarjeta* (fem.). |
| Conozco al chico cuyos padres son profesores. | I know the guy whose parents are teachers. | *Cuyos* agrees with *padres* (masc. pl.). |
| Es la actriz cuyas películas siempre me hacen llorar. | She's the actress whose movies always make me cry. | *Cuyas* agrees with *películas* (fem. pl.). |

## Talking about businesses or groups by what belongs to them

Same pattern, applied to companies, teams, or institutions.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Es la tienda cuyos precios subieron otra vez. | It's the store whose prices went up again. | Common complaint-register phrase. |
| Trabajamos con una agencia cuyo equipo responde muy rápido. | We work with an agency whose team responds very quickly. | Workplace/business context. |
| Es el hospital cuya sala de emergencias siempre está llena. | It's the hospital whose emergency room is always full. | Everyday complaint about a place. |
| Es el banco cuyas comisiones son las más bajas. | It's the bank whose fees are the lowest. | Comparison context. |

## Saying it doesn't matter who or which one

*Quienquiera* and *cualquiera* for emphasizing "no matter who/which," often
followed by *que* + subjunctive.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Quienquiera que haya hecho esto, tiene que arreglarlo. | Whoever did this has to fix it. | *Quienquiera que* + subjunctive. |
| Cualquiera que gane, va a ser una final emocionante. | Whoever/whichever wins, it's going to be an exciting final. | Common in sports talk. |
| Avísame si llama cualquiera preguntando por mí. | Let me know if anyone calls asking for me. | *Cualquiera* as "anyone." |
| Puedes tomar cualquier autobús que pase por aquí. | You can take any bus that comes through here. | *Cualquier* shortened before the noun. |
| Quienesquiera que sean, no los conozco. | Whoever they are, I don't know them. | Plural *quienesquiera* for more than one person. |
