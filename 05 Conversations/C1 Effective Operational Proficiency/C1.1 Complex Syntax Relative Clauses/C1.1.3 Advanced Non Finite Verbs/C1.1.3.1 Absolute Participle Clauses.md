# Absolute Participle Clauses (Terminada la reunión, nos fuimos) — Conversational Sentences

## Marking the end of a meeting or event

Common in workplace narration and formal recaps of what happened.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Terminada la reunión, cada quien volvió a su oficina. | Once the meeting ended, everyone went back to their office. | *Terminada* agrees with *la reunión* (fem.). |
| Concluido el evento, los organizadores empezaron a limpiar. | Once the event concluded, the organizers started cleaning up. | *Concluido* agrees with *el evento* (masc.). |
| Finalizada la temporada, el equipo se tomó un descanso. | Once the season ended, the team took a break. | *Finalizada* agrees with *la temporada* (fem.). |

## Talking about signed or completed paperwork

Common in business and legal-adjacent conversation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Firmado el contrato, ambas partes celebraron el acuerdo. | Once the contract was signed, both parties celebrated the deal. | *Firmado* agrees with *el contrato* (masc.). |
| Aprobado el presupuesto, el equipo pudo empezar a comprar equipo. | Once the budget was approved, the team could start buying equipment. | *Aprobado* agrees with *el presupuesto* (masc.). |
| Entregados los documentos, solo faltaba esperar la respuesta. | Once the documents were submitted, all that was left was to wait for a response. | *Entregados* agrees with *los documentos* (masc. pl.). |

## Describing a sequence of resolved problems or completed tasks

Useful for narrating a process step by step.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Resuelto el malentendido, volvieron a ser amigos. | Once the misunderstanding was resolved, they became friends again. | *Resuelto* agrees with *el malentendido* (masc.). |
| Terminadas las reparaciones, el carro quedó como nuevo. | Once the repairs were finished, the car was like new. | *Terminadas* agrees with *las reparaciones* (fem. pl.). |
| Hecha la tarea, los niños salieron a jugar. | Once the homework was done, the kids went out to play. | *Hecha* agrees with *la tarea* (fem.). |

## Transitioning to a new topic or point

*Dicho esto* and similar formal transitions.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Dicho esto, pasemos al siguiente punto del orden del día. | That said, let's move to the next item on the agenda. | Common formal-meeting transition phrase. |
| Aclarado ese punto, podemos continuar con la presentación. | Once that point is clarified, we can continue with the presentation. | *Aclarado* agrees with the implied neuter idea. |
| Confirmada la fecha, empezamos a enviar las invitaciones. | Once the date was confirmed, we started sending out invitations. | *Confirmada* agrees with *la fecha* (fem.). |
