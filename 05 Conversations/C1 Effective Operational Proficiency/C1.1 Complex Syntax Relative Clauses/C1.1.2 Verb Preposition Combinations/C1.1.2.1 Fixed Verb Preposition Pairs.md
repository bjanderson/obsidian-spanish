# Fixed Verb-Preposition Pairs altering Meaning (Soñar con, Incurrir en, Depender de) — Conversational Sentences

## Talking about dreams and goals

*Soñar con* + infinitive or noun, a common way to talk about ambitions.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Siempre soñé con tener mi propio restaurante. | I always dreamed of having my own restaurant. | *Soñar con* + noun. |
| Sueño con hablar español perfectamente algún día. | I dream of speaking Spanish perfectly someday. | *Soñar con* + infinitive. |
| ¿Con qué sueñas tú? | What do you dream of? | Question form, preposition stays with *soñar*. |
| Mi abuela siempre soñó con viajar a Europa. | My grandmother always dreamed of traveling to Europe. | Past tense, same fixed pair. |

## Saying something depends on something else

*Depender de*, extremely common in everyday planning talk.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Eso depende de cuánto tiempo tengamos. | That depends on how much time we have. | *Depender de* + clause. |
| Todo depende del clima ese día. | Everything depends on the weather that day. | *De* + *el* contracts to *del*. |
| No sé si podré ir; depende de mi horario. | I don't know if I'll be able to go; it depends on my schedule. | Casual, everyday register. |
| La decisión depende de ustedes. | The decision depends on you all. | *Ustedes* as neutral plural "you." |

## Relying on someone or something

*Contar con*, used constantly in both practical and emotional contexts.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Sabes que puedes contar conmigo. | You know you can count on me. | *Con* + *mí* → *conmigo*. |
| Contamos con el apoyo de toda la familia. | We're counting on the support of the whole family. | Present tense, everyday use. |
| ¿Puedo contar contigo para el sábado? | Can I count on you for Saturday? | *Con* + *ti* → *contigo*. |
| Este hotel cuenta con piscina y gimnasio. | This hotel has a pool and a gym. | *Contar con* also means "to have/include." |

## Complaining or expressing frustration

*Quejarse de* and *cansarse de*, common in venting-style conversation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Mi jefe siempre se queja del tráfico. | My boss always complains about traffic. | *Quejarse de* + *el* → *del*. |
| Me cansé de esperar el autobús todos los días. | I got tired of waiting for the bus every day. | *Cansarse de* + infinitive. |
| No te quejes tanto de la comida. | Don't complain so much about the food. | Negative command form. |
| Se cansaron de las mismas excusas. | They got tired of the same excuses. | *Cansarse de* + noun. |
