# Prepositional Verbs triggering Subjunctive or Infinitive Clauses — Conversational Sentences

## Giving instructions with the same subject (infinitive)

Everyday commands and plans where one person does both actions.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Antes de salir, apaga las luces. | Before leaving, turn off the lights. | Command form, same subject implied. |
| Lávate las manos antes de comer. | Wash your hands before eating. | *Antes de* + infinitive, everyday reminder. |
| No puedo dormir sin tomar un té primero. | I can't sleep without having tea first. | *Sin* + infinitive, same subject. |
| Llámame después de llegar a casa. | Call me after you get home. | *Después de* + infinitive. |

## Making plans that involve someone else (subjunctive)

Common when the outcome depends on a different person's action.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Te dejo la llave para que entres cuando quieras. | I'll leave you the key so you can come in whenever you want. | *Para que* + present subjunctive. |
| Habla más despacio para que todos te entiendan. | Speak more slowly so everyone can understand you. | *Para que* + present subjunctive, group context. |
| Salimos temprano antes de que empiece el tráfico. | We're leaving early before traffic starts. | *Antes de que* + present subjunctive. |
| Guardé un pedazo de pastel para que lo probaras. | I saved a piece of cake for you to try. | *Para que* + imperfect subjunctive, past tense. |

## Doing something without another person noticing or helping

*Sin que* + subjunctive, common in storytelling and complaints.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Los niños salieron sin que nadie los viera. | The kids left without anyone seeing them. | *Sin que* + imperfect subjunctive. |
| No puedo terminar esto sin que me ayudes. | I can't finish this without you helping me. | *Sin que* + present subjunctive. |
| Cambiaron el plan sin que yo lo supiera. | They changed the plan without me knowing. | *Sin que* + imperfect subjunctive. |
| Es difícil viajar sin que surjan imprevistos. | It's hard to travel without unexpected things coming up. | *Sin que* + present subjunctive, impersonal subject. |

## Talking about conditions for something to happen

*Con tal de que* and *a menos que*, common in negotiating or setting terms.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Te presto el carro con tal de que lo devuelvas lleno. | I'll lend you the car provided you return it full. | *Con tal de que* + present subjunctive. |
| Iré a la fiesta con tal de que tú también vayas. | I'll go to the party as long as you go too. | *Con tal de que*, conversational tone. |
| No saldremos a menos que deje de llover. | We won't go out unless it stops raining. | *A menos que* + present subjunctive. |
| Firmaré el contrato con tal de que me den más tiempo. | I'll sign the contract provided they give me more time. | Formal/business context. |
