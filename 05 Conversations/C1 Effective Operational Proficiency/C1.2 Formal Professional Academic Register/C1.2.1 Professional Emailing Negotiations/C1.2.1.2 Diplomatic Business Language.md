# Diplomatic Business Language — Conversational Sentences

## Softening a refusal

Turning down a request or proposal without sounding blunt, using a conditional buffer before the "no."

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Lamentablemente, no nos sería posible reducir el precio en esta etapa del proyecto. | Unfortunately, it wouldn't be possible for us to lower the price at this stage of the project. | The conditional *sería* is what keeps this from sounding like a flat refusal. |
| En este momento no estamos en condiciones de asumir un plazo tan corto. | At this time we're not in a position to take on such a short deadline. | Face-saving refusal formula; avoids a direct *no podemos*. |
| Preferiríamos mantener los términos originales del contrato, al menos por ahora. | We would prefer to keep the original terms of the contract, at least for now. | *Preferiríamos* redirects instead of flatly rejecting. |
| No podríamos garantizar esa fecha de entrega sin comprometer la calidad del producto. | We couldn't guarantee that delivery date without compromising the product's quality. | Conditional *podríamos* + a justification softens the refusal further. |
| Nos tememos que, con los recursos actuales, no sería viable atender esa solicitud. | I'm afraid that, with our current resources, it wouldn't be feasible to handle that request. | *Nos tememos que* signals regret before the refusal lands. |

## Requesting flexibility

Asking for more time, a change of terms, or a concession without sounding demanding.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Nos gustaría solicitar un poco más de flexibilidad en cuanto a la fecha de entrega. | We would like to request a bit more flexibility regarding the delivery date. | *Nos gustaría solicitar* is the standard formula for a formal, softened request. |
| ¿Sería posible ajustar el horario de la reunión para la próxima semana? | Would it be possible to adjust the meeting time to next week? | Question form with the conditional *sería* reads as a genuine ask, not a demand. |
| Quisiéramos proponer un pequeño ajuste en las condiciones de pago. | We would like to propose a small adjustment to the payment terms. | *Quisiéramos* (imperfect subjunctive as a courtesy form) softens the proposal. |
| Agradeceríamos que se nos concediera un plazo adicional para revisar la documentación. | We would appreciate being granted an additional period to review the documentation. | *Agradeceríamos que + subjunctive* is a very formal, indirect request pattern. |
| Nos sería de gran ayuda contar con unos días más antes de confirmar la propuesta. | It would be a great help to have a few more days before confirming the proposal. | Frames the request around benefit rather than obligation. |

## Raising a concern diplomatically

Flagging a problem without assigning blame or sounding alarmist.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Nos preocupa un poco que el presupuesto actual no alcance para cubrir los imprevistos. | We're a bit concerned that the current budget won't be enough to cover unforeseen costs. | *Nos preocupa un poco que + subjunctive* softens the concern. |
| Notamos que el cronograma es bastante ajustado; nos gustaría conversar sobre posibles riesgos. | We've noticed the schedule is quite tight; we'd like to talk about possible risks. | Neutral observation before requesting a conversation, not a complaint. |
| Convendría revisar los indicadores antes de que el proyecto avance a la siguiente fase. | It would be worthwhile to review the indicators before the project moves to the next phase. | *Convendría* is a milder cousin of *sería conveniente*. |
| Nos inquieta que la comunicación entre los equipos no haya sido tan fluida últimamente. | We're a little uneasy that communication between the teams hasn't been as smooth lately. | *Nos inquieta que* raises a soft concern about process, not people. |
| Sería prudente confirmar los detalles técnicos antes de firmar el acuerdo final. | It would be prudent to confirm the technical details before signing the final agreement. | *Sería prudente* frames caution as wisdom rather than distrust. |

## Countering a proposal

Pushing back on an offer or plan while keeping the exchange collaborative.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Si bien entendemos su propuesta, consideramos que el alcance del proyecto justifica un presupuesto mayor. | While we understand your proposal, we believe the project's scope justifies a larger budget. | Concessive opener *si bien* before the counter-argument. |
| Valoramos su oferta, aunque nos gustaría plantear una alternativa que beneficie a ambas partes. | We appreciate your offer, though we'd like to propose an alternative that benefits both sides. | Frames the counter-proposal as mutually beneficial, not adversarial. |
| Nos permitimos discrepar respetuosamente con el plazo sugerido para la entrega final. | We would respectfully disagree with the suggested deadline for the final delivery. | Set formula for formal, polite disagreement. |
| Entendemos las razones detrás de su propuesta; sin embargo, creemos que otra opción podría funcionar mejor. | We understand the reasoning behind your proposal; however, we believe another option might work better. | Validate-then-counter pattern common in negotiation emails. |
| Coincidimos en parte con su planteamiento, aunque nos gustaría ajustar algunos puntos antes de avanzar. | We partly agree with your approach, though we'd like to adjust a few points before moving forward. | Partial agreement softens the counter that follows. |

## Cushioning bad news

Delivering unwelcome updates — delays, price changes, rejected requests — without sounding cold or abrupt.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Lamentamos informarle que el envío se retrasará debido a problemas con el proveedor. | We regret to inform you that the shipment will be delayed due to issues with the supplier. | *Lamentamos informarle que* is the standard formal opener for bad news. |
| Nos vemos en la necesidad de ajustar los precios a partir del próximo trimestre. | We find ourselves needing to adjust prices starting next quarter. | Distances the writer from being the direct cause of the change. |
| Le informamos, con cierta pena, que no podremos participar en la próxima licitación. | We inform you, with some regret, that we won't be able to participate in the next bid. | *Con cierta pena* adds a personal, apologetic touch to formal bad news. |
| Debido a circunstancias fuera de nuestro control, el proyecto sufrirá una breve demora. | Due to circumstances beyond our control, the project will experience a brief delay. | Shifts responsibility away from either party, keeping tone neutral. |
| Entendemos que esta noticia no es la que esperaba, y quedamos a su disposición para conversarlo. | We understand this news isn't what you were expecting, and we remain available to discuss it. | Closes bad news with an offer of continued dialogue. |
