# Domain-Specific Vocabulary (Finance, Law, Politics, Science) — Conversational Sentences

## Finanzas (Finance)

Sentences you'd hear discussing the economy, a company's finances, or a
news report on money matters.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Con esta inflación, el presupuesto familiar ya no alcanza para nada. | With this inflation, the household budget doesn't stretch far enough for anything anymore. | Casual complaint register using *inflación* and *presupuesto* together. |
| Si sigue subiendo la tasa de interés, nadie va a poder pagar su hipoteca. | If the interest rate keeps rising, no one is going to be able to pay their mortgage. | *si* + present, common conversational hypothetical about finance news. |
| La empresa tuvo que vender parte de sus activos para cubrir la deuda. | The company had to sell part of its assets to cover the debt. | Business-discussion sentence pairing *activos* and *deuda*. |
| El nuevo ministro prometió reducir el déficit en dos años. | The new minister promised to reduce the deficit within two years. | Reported-speech style, common in news commentary. |
| Mi banco me subió la tasa de interés sin avisarme. | My bank raised my interest rate without warning me. | Everyday personal-finance complaint; no redundant subject pronoun on *me subió*. |

## Derecho (Law)

Sentences from a legal discussion, a news report on a court case, or someone
recounting a legal situation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Al final, el tribunal falló a favor del trabajador. | In the end, the court ruled in favor of the worker. | *al* contraction (*a + el*); *fallar a favor de* collocation. |
| Le presentaron una demanda por incumplimiento de contrato. | They filed a lawsuit against him for breach of contract. | Indirect object *le*, common way to phrase "against someone" conversationally. |
| No sabía que esa empresa estaba bajo la jurisdicción de otro país. | I didn't know that company was under another country's jurisdiction. | Natural surprise-reaction sentence. |
| Citaron a los testigos para la próxima audiencia. | They summoned the witnesses for the next hearing. | Personal *a* before *los testigos*; impersonal "they" via 3rd person plural. |
| El abogado dijo que la sentencia podría tardar meses. | The lawyer said the ruling could take months. | Reported speech, *podría* for polite/hedged prediction. |
| Todavía no ha cumplido la sentencia completa. | He still hasn't served the full sentence. | Present perfect, common when catching someone up on a case. |

## Política (Politics)

Sentences from election-season conversation, a political discussion, or
news commentary someone might repeat casually.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| El partido perdió varios escaños en las últimas elecciones. | The party lost several seats in the last election. | Common way to summarize election results in conversation. |
| No sé si el electorado va a confiar en él otra vez. | I don't know if the electorate is going to trust him again. | *el electorado* stays singular with *va*, not *van*. |
| Aprobaron el proyecto de ley después de meses de debate. | They passed the bill after months of debate. | *aprobar* + *proyecto de ley*, then it becomes *la ley*. |
| Dicen que va a lanzar su candidatura el próximo mes. | They say he's going to launch his candidacy next month. | *dicen que* — very common way to relay political rumors. |
| Todavía falta el escrutinio final para saber quién ganó. | The final vote count is still needed to know who won. | Election-night phrasing you'd hear on the news or among friends. |
| Durante su mandato no cumplió casi ninguna de sus promesas. | During his term, he barely kept any of his promises. | Common critical remark about a politician's tenure. |

## Ciencia (Science)

Sentences from discussing a study, a scientific finding, or a health-related
news story.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Los científicos plantearon una hipótesis nueva sobre el virus. | The scientists proposed a new hypothesis about the virus. | Neutral reporting style for a scientific claim. |
| Están haciendo un ensayo clínico con voluntarios de varios países. | They're running a clinical trial with volunteers from several countries. | *estar* + gerund for an ongoing process, common in health news. |
| No controlaron bien esa variable, así que los resultados son dudosos. | They didn't control that variable well, so the results are questionable. | Casual critique of a study, the kind you'd hear discussing an article. |
| El hallazgo sorprendió a todo el equipo de investigación. | The finding surprised the whole research team. | Personal *a* before *todo el equipo* (a group treated as a specific object). |
| La muestra era demasiado pequeña para sacar conclusiones firmes. | The sample was too small to draw solid conclusions. | Common hedge when discussing a study's limitations. |
| Publicaron el estudio en una revista revisada por pares. | They published the study in a peer-reviewed journal. | Standard way to reference credibility of a scientific source. |
