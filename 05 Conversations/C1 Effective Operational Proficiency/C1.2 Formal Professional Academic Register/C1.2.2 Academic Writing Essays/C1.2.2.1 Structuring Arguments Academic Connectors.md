# Structuring Arguments and Using Academic Connectors — Conversational Sentences

## Adding a Point

Building on something already said — common when a speaker is stacking evidence
during a presentation or debate.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Quisiera añadir que los datos de este año confirman la misma tendencia. | I'd like to add that this year's data confirms the same trend. | Common way to open a supporting point in a presentation. |
| Asimismo, deberíamos considerar el impacto en las comunidades rurales. | Likewise, we should consider the impact on rural communities. | *asimismo* signals a parallel point, not a contrast. |
| Por otro lado, hay que tener en cuenta la opinión de los expertos en la materia. | On the other hand, we have to take the opinion of experts in the field into account. | Here *por otro lado* adds a further angle, not necessarily a contrast. |
| En este sentido, la propuesta del comité merece una revisión más profunda. | Along these lines, the committee's proposal deserves a deeper review. | Ties the current point back to what the speaker just argued. |
| Además, ningún estudio serio ha refutado esta hipótesis hasta la fecha. | Furthermore, no serious study has refuted this hypothesis to date. | *además* is the most common all-purpose "furthermore" in spoken argument. |

## Contrasting an Idea

Setting one claim against another — the connector a speaker reaches for right
before pushing back on a point.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Entiendo su punto; no obstante, los números cuentan una historia distinta. | I understand your point; nonetheless, the numbers tell a different story. | *no obstante* softens the pushback while still disagreeing. |
| El plan suena atractivo; sin embargo, nadie ha explicado cómo se va a financiar. | The plan sounds appealing; however, no one has explained how it will be funded. | *sin embargo* is the safest default contrast connector in any formal register. |
| Aun así, creo que vale la pena intentarlo antes de descartar la idea. | Even so, I think it's worth trying before dismissing the idea. | *aun así* concedes the previous point but holds the speaker's position. |
| Dicho esto, no podemos ignorar los riesgos que señaló el ponente anterior. | That said, we can't ignore the risks the previous speaker pointed out. | *dicho esto* pivots after briefly conceding something. |
| Por el contrario, la evidencia histórica apunta en la dirección opuesta. | On the contrary, the historical evidence points in the opposite direction. | Stronger contrast than *sin embargo* — flatly opposes the prior claim. |

## Signaling a Consequence

Linking a cause to its effect — useful when walking an audience through why
one fact leads logically to the next.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| El presupuesto se redujo un diez por ciento; por consiguiente, tuvimos que priorizar proyectos. | The budget was cut by ten percent; consequently, we had to prioritize projects. | *por consiguiente* is the standard spoken-formal "therefore." |
| A tenor de lo que acaba de exponer el profesor, deberíamos replantear la metodología. | In light of what the professor just presented, we should rethink the methodology. | *a tenor de* references something just said, common in academic discussion. |
| La demanda sigue cayendo, de ahí que muchas empresas estén recortando personal. | Demand keeps falling, which is why many companies are cutting staff. | *de ahí que* takes the subjunctive: *estén recortando*, not *están*. |
| Por lo tanto, propongo que pospongamos la votación hasta la próxima sesión. | Therefore, I propose that we postpone the vote until the next session. | *por lo tanto* works interchangeably with *por consiguiente* in speech. |
| El informe llegó tarde; en consecuencia, el comité no pudo revisarlo a tiempo. | The report arrived late; as a result, the committee couldn't review it in time. | *en consecuencia* is a close synonym worth recognizing alongside *por consiguiente*. |

## Emphasizing a Key Point

Flagging a claim as the one the audience should pay closest attention to.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Cabe destacar que ningún otro país de la región ha logrado resultados similares. | It's worth noting that no other country in the region has achieved similar results. | Common way to flag the strongest piece of evidence in an argument. |
| Cabe señalar que esta cifra no incluye a los trabajadores informales. | It's worth pointing out that this figure doesn't include informal workers. | Frequently used to flag a caveat or limitation, not just a strength. |
| Vale la pena mencionar que el estudio se llevó a cabo durante dos años. | It's worth mentioning that the study was carried out over two years. | Slightly less formal than *cabe destacar/señalar*, but still appropriate for debate. |
| Hay que subrayar que la decisión afecta a miles de familias directamente. | It has to be stressed that the decision directly affects thousands of families. | *hay que subrayar* is a common spoken variant for emphasis. |
| Lo más importante es que este acuerdo sienta un precedente para futuras negociaciones. | The most important thing is that this agreement sets a precedent for future negotiations. | A plain-language way to emphasize without a fixed connector phrase. |

## Concluding an Argument

Signaling that the speaker is wrapping up — usually right before the final,
strongest claim.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| En suma, los tres argumentos apuntan hacia la misma conclusión. | In short, the three arguments point toward the same conclusion. | *en suma* summarizes points already made, not new information. |
| En definitiva, la propuesta necesita más financiamiento antes de aprobarse. | Ultimately, the proposal needs more funding before it can be approved. | Common way to state a practical bottom line at the end of a discussion. |
| En última instancia, la decisión depende de la voluntad política, no de los recursos. | In the final analysis, the decision depends on political will, not resources. | Reserved for the strongest or final claim in an argument. |
| Para concluir, quisiera agradecer a todos los que participaron en el debate de hoy. | To conclude, I'd like to thank everyone who took part in today's debate. | Standard closing phrase for a formal presentation or talk. |
| En pocas palabras, el proyecto es viable, pero requiere más tiempo del previsto. | In a few words, the project is viable, but it requires more time than anticipated. | *en pocas palabras* is a natural spoken alternative to *en suma*. |
