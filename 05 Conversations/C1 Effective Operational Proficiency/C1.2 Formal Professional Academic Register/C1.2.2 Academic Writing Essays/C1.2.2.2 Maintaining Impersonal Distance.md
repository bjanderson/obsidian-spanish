# Maintaining Impersonal Distance and Objectivity in Texts — Conversational Sentences

## Impersonal *se* for general facts and beliefs

Stating a general fact, belief, or practice without naming who holds it — the
workhorse pattern for opening a claim in a report or presentation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Se sabe que el estrés crónico afecta el sistema inmunológico. | It's known that chronic stress affects the immune system. | *se sabe que* — very common opener for a well-established fact. |
| Se espera que la inflación baje durante el segundo semestre del año. | Inflation is expected to fall during the second half of the year. | Common in economic/financial reporting or presentations. |
| Se recomienda revisar los datos antes de presentar las conclusiones. | It's recommended to review the data before presenting the conclusions. | Impersonal recommendation, common in meetings and reports. |
| En este campo, se necesita más evidencia antes de sacar conclusiones firmes. | In this field, more evidence is needed before drawing firm conclusions. | Impersonal *se* stays singular even though *evidencia* could sound plural in English. |
| Se dice que la nueva normativa entrará en vigor el próximo año. | It's said that the new regulation will take effect next year. | *se dice que* — reporting something without citing a specific source. |

## Passive *se* for actions and events

Shifting the focus onto what happened to something, letting the noun affected
by the action drive verb agreement — common when describing a process, an
event, or a procedure.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Los formularios se entregan antes del quince de cada mes. | The forms are submitted before the fifteenth of each month. | Plural *formularios* controls plural *entregan*. |
| El presupuesto se revisará en la próxima reunión del comité. | The budget will be reviewed at the committee's next meeting. | Future tense passive *se*; singular *presupuesto*. |
| Se implementaron varios cambios tras la auditoría interna. | Several changes were implemented after the internal audit. | Plural *cambios* controls plural *implementaron*. |
| El contrato se firmó sin modificaciones adicionales. | The contract was signed without additional changes. | Singular *contrato* keeps the verb singular. |
| Las solicitudes se procesan en el orden en que se reciben. | Applications are processed in the order they're received. | Two passive *se* constructions in one sentence — common in procedural writing. |

## *Ser*-passive for formal reports and named agents

Reaching for *ser* + past participle specifically when the agent performing
the action needs to be named — a common move in formal reports, minutes, and
historical or legal summaries.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| El presupuesto fue aprobado por la junta directiva ayer. | The budget was approved by the board of directors yesterday. | *por* introduces the agent explicitly. |
| La propuesta fue rechazada por la mayoría de los miembros. | The proposal was rejected by most of the members. | Common in meeting minutes and summaries. |
| El acuerdo fue firmado por ambas partes en presencia de un notario. | The agreement was signed by both parties in the presence of a notary. | Legal/formal-document register. |
| Los cambios fueron implementados por el nuevo equipo directivo. | The changes were implemented by the new management team. | Contrast with the passive-*se* version above — here the agent is stated. |
| El estudio fue financiado por una fundación privada. | The study was funded by a private foundation. | Standard phrasing for acknowledging funding sources in a report. |

## Avoiding first person in favor of impersonal framing

Replacing a personal opinion opener with impersonal phrasing that attributes
the claim to evidence, consensus, or general practice instead of the speaker.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Cabe señalar que los resultados varían según la región. | It's worth noting that the results vary by region. | Standard substitute for "yo noto que" in a presentation or report. |
| Se puede argumentar que el plazo actual es insuficiente. | It could be argued that the current deadline is insufficient. | Softens a critical claim without attributing it to "I." |
| Existe consenso en que la medida tuvo un efecto positivo. | There's consensus that the measure had a positive effect. | Attributes the claim to a group rather than the speaker. |
| Según los datos disponibles, la demanda ha crecido de forma constante. | According to the available data, demand has grown steadily. | Anchors the claim to the data instead of a personal opinion. |
| Vale la pena mencionar que dos de los participantes se retiraron del estudio. | It's worth mentioning that two of the participants withdrew from the study. | Neutral way to flag a limitation without personal commentary. |
