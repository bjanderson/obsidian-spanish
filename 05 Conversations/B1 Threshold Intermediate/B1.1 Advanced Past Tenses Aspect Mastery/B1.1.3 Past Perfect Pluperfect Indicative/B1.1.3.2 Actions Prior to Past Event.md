# Actions Prior to Past Event — Conversational Sentences

## Explaining What Happened Before You Arrived

Common storytelling sentences about what was already done by the time you showed up.

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Cuando llegué, ya habían empezado a comer.                 | When I arrived, they had already started eating.     | *cuando*, *ya*             |
| Para cuando llegamos, ya se había ido todo el mundo.       | By the time we got there, everyone had already left.  | *para cuando*              |
| Cuando desperté, ya había amanecido.                       | When I woke up, it had already gotten light out.       | *cuando*                   |
| Llegué tarde, pero ya habían terminado la reunión.         | I arrived late, but they had already finished the meeting. | *ya*                    |

## Talking About What Someone Hadn't Done Yet

Using *todavía no* + past perfect for something still pending at a past moment.

| Spanish                                       | English                                    | Notes                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Todavía no había llegado cuando empezó la reunión.  | He still hadn't arrived when the meeting started.  | *todavía no*, *cuando*     |
| No habíamos comido nada antes de salir.             | We hadn't eaten anything before leaving.            | negation, *antes de*        |
| Todavía no había decidido qué hacer cuando me llamaste. | I still hadn't decided what to do when you called me. | *todavía no*             |
| Ella no había terminado la carrera cuando se casó.  | She hadn't finished her degree when she got married. | negation, *cuando*           |

## Describing a Sequence of Two Past Events

Practicing the "earlier event / later reference point" pattern directly.

| Spanish                                                | English                                          | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Antes de mudarnos, ya habíamos visitado la ciudad varias veces. | Before we moved, we had already visited the city several times. | *antes de*         |
| Cuando el médico llegó, el paciente ya había mejorado.        | By the time the doctor arrived, the patient had already improved. | *cuando*             |
| Ya habían firmado el contrato cuando surgió el problema.      | They had already signed the contract when the problem came up. | *ya*, *cuando*          |
| Cuando terminó la película, ya me había quedado dormido.      | By the time the movie ended, I had already fallen asleep.  | *cuando*, reflexive       |

## Reacting to Learning Something Had Already Happened

Natural conversational surprise or realization.

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| No sabía que ya habías hablado con ella.        | I didn't know you had already talked to her.  | past perfect in a clause   |
| Me di cuenta de que ya había pasado la hora.    | I realized the time had already passed.        | reflexive + past perfect   |
| ¡No puedo creer que ya se habían ido!           | I can't believe they had already left!          | exclamation                |
| Nunca me imaginé que ya lo habías planeado todo. | I never imagined you had already planned it all. | past perfect, object pronoun |

## Everyday Small Talk With This Pattern

Casual, common examples that come up in daily conversation.

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ya había cerrado la tienda cuando llegamos.     | The store had already closed by the time we got there. | *ya*, *cuando*      |
| Todavía no había pagado la renta cuando me acordé. | I still hadn't paid the rent when I remembered. | *todavía no*             |
| Ya habíamos comprado los boletos antes de que subiera el precio. | We had already bought the tickets before the price went up. | *antes de que*   |
| Cuando por fin llamé, ya había resuelto el problema solo. | By the time I finally called, he had already solved the problem on his own. | *cuando*, *ya* |
