# Auxiliary Haber Imperfect — Conversational Sentences

## Explaining What Had Already Happened

Common ways to say something was already done before another past moment.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ya había comido cuando llegaron.         | I had already eaten when they arrived.       | *había*                    |
| Cuando llegamos, la película ya había empezado. | When we arrived, the movie had already started. | *había*             |
| Ella había terminado la tarea antes de cenar. | She had finished her homework before dinner. | *había*                |
| Ya habíamos hablado de eso antes.        | We had already talked about that before.       | *habíamos*                  |

## Asking About Prior Experience

Questions checking what someone had done before a certain point.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Habías estado aquí antes?               | Had you been here before?                    | *habías*, question           |
| ¿Ya habían comido cuando llegaste?       | Had they already eaten when you arrived?       | *habían*, question           |
| ¿Nunca habías probado esto?              | Had you never tried this?                     | *habías*, *nunca*             |
| ¿Qué habías hecho antes de mudarte aquí? | What had you done before moving here?           | *habías*, question            |

## Narrating a Story With a Prior Event

Using the past perfect to give backstory within a larger narrative.

| Spanish                                             | English                                        | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Cuando por fin llegó, ya nos habíamos ido.               | By the time he finally arrived, we had already left.  | *habíamos*, narrative |
| No sabía que ya habías renunciado a tu trabajo.          | I didn't know you had already quit your job.          | *habías*              |
| Me di cuenta de que nunca había visto esa película.      | I realized I had never seen that movie.               | *había*               |
| Todo estaba en orden porque ya habían limpiado la casa.  | Everything was in order because they had already cleaned the house. | *habían*    |

## Talking About Regret or Surprise Involving the Past Perfect

Emotional reactions framed around something that had (or hadn't) happened.

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Si hubiera sabido que ya habías llegado...       | If I had known you had already arrived...      | *habías*, complex sentence |
| Me sorprendió que ya hubieran salido.            | I was surprised they had already left.          | subjunctive contrast   |
| Nunca había sentido tanto miedo.                 | I had never felt so afraid.                     | *había*, *nunca*        |
| Todo cambió después de que había hablado con ella. | Everything changed after I had talked to her.  | *había*, *después de que* |

## Everyday Small Talk Using the Past Perfect

Natural, casual uses that come up in everyday conversation.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| No me había dado cuenta de la hora.      | I hadn't realized the time.                    | reflexive + *había*          |
| Se me había olvidado por completo.       | I had completely forgotten about it.            | reflexive + *había*          |
| Ya habíamos visto esa serie dos veces.   | We had already seen that series twice.          | *habíamos*                   |
| Nunca había estado tan cansado.          | I had never been so tired.                       | *había*, *nunca*             |
