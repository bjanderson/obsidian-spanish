# Life Experiences and Open Timeframes — Conversational Sentences

## Asking About Someone's Life Experiences

The classic "Have you ever...?" pattern for small talk.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Alguna vez has viajado solo?            | Have you ever traveled alone?               | *alguna vez has*, question  |
| ¿Has probado la comida peruana?          | Have you tried Peruvian food?                | present perfect, question   |
| ¿Alguna vez has vivido en otro país?     | Have you ever lived in another country?       | *alguna vez has*             |
| ¿Has estado en un concierto en vivo?     | Have you been to a live concert?              | present perfect, question   |

## Sharing Things You've Never Done

Using *nunca he* to talk about experiences you're still missing.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Nunca he manejado una motocicleta.       | I've never driven a motorcycle.               | *nunca he*                   |
| Nunca hemos visitado Europa juntos.      | We've never visited Europe together.           | *nunca hemos*                |
| Ella nunca ha probado el café.           | She's never tried coffee.                     | *nunca ha*                   |
| Nunca han ido a un partido de fútbol.    | They've never been to a soccer game.           | *nunca han*                  |

## Talking About This Year, Month, or Week So Far

Using the present perfect for periods that haven't ended yet.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Este mes he leído tres libros.           | I've read three books this month.             | open timeframe               |
| Esta semana no hemos salido para nada.   | We haven't gone out at all this week.          | open timeframe, negation      |
| Este año han viajado mucho.              | They've traveled a lot this year.               | open timeframe               |
| ¿Cuántas veces has ido al gimnasio esta semana? | How many times have you gone to the gym this week? | open timeframe, question |

## Contrasting Open and Closed Timeframes

Pairing present perfect with preterite in the same conversation.

| Spanish                                            | English                                          | Notes             |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| Este año he ido a la playa tres veces; el año pasado fui solo una vez. | This year I've gone to the beach three times; last year I went just once. | present perfect + preterite |
| Todavía no he terminado, pero ayer terminé la mitad.       | I still haven't finished, but yesterday I finished half.  | present perfect + preterite |
| Nunca he estado tan cansado como ayer.                     | I've never been as tired as I was yesterday.             | present perfect + preterite |
| Ya he hablado con ella hoy, pero también la llamé anoche.  | I've already talked to her today, but I also called her last night. | present perfect + preterite |

## Reacting to Someone's Experience

Natural conversational responses when someone shares an experience.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¡No puedo creer que nunca hayas comido tacos! | I can't believe you've never eaten tacos! | *nunca hayas*, subjunctive |
| Qué envidia, yo nunca he estado ahí.     | I'm jealous, I've never been there.            | *nunca he*                   |
| Yo tampoco lo he probado todavía.        | I haven't tried it yet either.                 | *tampoco*, *todavía*         |
| Algún día también lo voy a hacer.        | Someday I'll do it too.                         | future intention             |
