# Regular and Irregular Past Participles — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Regular Completed Actions

Everyday present perfect sentences with regular -ar/-er/-ir participles.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| He estudiado toda la tarde.              | I've studied all afternoon.                  | regular -ar                 |
| Hemos comido demasiado.                  | We've eaten too much.                        | regular -er                 |
| ¿Has salido con tus amigos últimamente?  | Have you gone out with your friends lately?   | regular -ir, question        |
| Ella ha trabajado mucho este mes.        | She's worked a lot this month.                 | regular -ar                 |

## Using Common Irregular Participles

Frequent conversational uses of *dicho, hecho, visto, puesto*.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Ya le has dicho la verdad?              | Have you already told him the truth?          | *decir* → *dicho*            |
| He hecho todo lo posible.                | I've done everything possible.                 | *hacer* → *hecho*            |
| ¿Has visto las noticias hoy?             | Have you seen the news today?                   | *ver* → *visto*              |
| Ya he puesto la mesa.                    | I've already set the table.                      | *poner* → *puesto*           |

## Reporting Things Written, Opened, or Broken

Using *escrito, abierto, roto* in everyday statements.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Le he escrito tres correos y no responde. | I've written him three emails and he hasn't replied. | *escribir* → *escrito*  |
| ¿Quién ha abierto la puerta?             | Who's opened the door?                        | *abrir* → *abierto*, question |
| Se me ha roto el celular otra vez.       | My phone has broken again.                     | *romper* → *roto*, reflexive |
| Han vuelto de su viaje esta semana.      | They've come back from their trip this week.    | *volver* → *vuelto*         |

## Compound Verbs That Inherit Irregular Roots

Sentences using *descubrir, devolver, describir* the way they naturally come up.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Han descubierto un nuevo restaurante.    | They've discovered a new restaurant.           | *descubrir* → *descubierto* |
| Ya le he devuelto el dinero.             | I've already paid him back.                     | *devolver* → *devuelto*     |
| Nos han descrito el problema con detalle. | They've described the problem to us in detail. | *describir* → *descrito*   |
| ¿Has devuelto los libros a la biblioteca? | Have you returned the books to the library?    | *devolver* → *devuelto*     |

## Everyday Check-Ins Using the Present Perfect

Natural back-and-forth exchanges built around these participles.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Has leído el correo que te mandé?       | Have you read the email I sent you?           | *leer* → *leído*, accent   |
| Todavía no he oído nada al respecto.     | I still haven't heard anything about it.        | *oír* → *oído*, accent      |
| ¿Ya han hecho la reservación?            | Have you already made the reservation?           | *hacer* → *hecho*           |
| No hemos visto esa película todavía.     | We haven't seen that movie yet.                  | *ver* → *visto*             |
