# Llevar + Gerund for Ongoing Duration — Conversational Sentences

## Stating how long an action has been going on

*Llevar* (conjugated) + time + gerund, the everyday way to say "I've been doing X for Y."

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Llevo cinco años trabajando en esta empresa. | I've been working at this company for five years. | *yo* form |
| Mi hermana lleva un mes buscando apartamento. | My sister has been looking for an apartment for a month. | third person singular |
| Llevamos dos horas viendo esta película. | We've been watching this movie for two hours. | *nosotros* form |
| Mis vecinos llevan un año remodelando la casa. | My neighbors have been remodeling the house for a year. | third person plural |
| Llevo un rato pensando en tu pregunta. | I've been thinking about your question for a while. | *un rato* + gerund |

## Asking how long something has been happening

*¿Cuánto tiempo* + *llevar* + gerund? — the natural way to ask "how long have you been...?"

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas estudiando español? | How long have you been studying Spanish? | *tú* form question |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo lleva usted viviendo en Bolivia? | How long have you been living in Bolivia? | formal *usted* |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo llevan esperando el autobús? | How long have you all been waiting for the bus? | *ustedes* form |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo lleva funcionando este negocio? | How long has this business been running? | impersonal subject |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas sin ver a tu familia? | How long has it been since you saw your family? | *sin* + infinitive variant |

## Talking about ongoing negative states

*Llevar* + time + *sin* + infinitive, for something that hasn't happened in a stretch of time.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Llevo tres días sin dormir bien. | I haven't slept well in three days. | *sin* + infinitive |
| Llevamos meses sin salir de vacaciones. | We haven't gone on vacation in months. | *nosotros* form |
| Mi hermano lleva semanas sin llamar. | My brother hasn't called in weeks. | third person singular |
| ¿Llevas mucho tiempo sin comer? | Have you gone a long time without eating? | question form |
| Llevan años sin hablarse. | They haven't spoken to each other in years. | reciprocal, third person plural |

## Emphasizing that something is still going on

Adding *todavía* to stress the action hasn't stopped.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Todavía llevo esperando la respuesta. | I'm still waiting for the answer. | *todavía* + *llevar* + gerund |
| Mi abuelo todavía lleva manejando a los ochenta años. | My grandfather is still driving at eighty. | ongoing, no explicit duration |
| ¿Todavía llevas trabajando en el mismo lugar? | Are you still working at the same place? | question form |
| Todavía llevamos buscando una solución. | We're still looking for a solution. | *nosotros* form |
| Ella todavía lleva recuperándose de la operación. | She's still recovering from the surgery. | reflexive gerund |
