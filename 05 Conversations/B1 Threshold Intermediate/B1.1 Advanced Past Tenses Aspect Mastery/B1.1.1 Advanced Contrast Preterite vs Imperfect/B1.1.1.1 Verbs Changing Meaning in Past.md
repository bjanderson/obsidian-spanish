# Verbs Changing Meaning in Past — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About How You Met Someone

Using *conocer* in the preterite to mark the moment two people met.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Dónde conociste a tu esposo?            | Where did you meet your husband?           | *conocer*, preterite       |
| Nos conocimos en una boda.               | We met at a wedding.                        | *conocerse*, reciprocal     |
| Conocí a mi mejor amiga en la universidad. | I met my best friend in college.          | *conocer*, preterite       |
| Ya nos conocíamos de antes.              | We already knew each other from before.     | *conocerse*, imperfect      |

## Explaining When You Found Out Something

Using *saber* in the preterite for the moment information arrived.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Supe la noticia por mi hermana.          | I found out the news from my sister.        | *saber*, preterite          |
| No sabía que te habías mudado.           | I didn't know you had moved.                | *saber*, imperfect          |
| ¿Cuándo supiste que ibas a tener el bebé? | When did you find out you were having the baby? | *saber*, preterite       |
| Sabía que algo estaba mal.               | I knew something was wrong.                  | *saber*, imperfect          |

## Talking About Trying (and Refusing)

Using *querer* in the preterite for attempts or refusals.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Quise arreglarlo, pero no funcionó.      | I tried to fix it, but it didn't work.       | *querer*, preterite affirmative |
| No quiso hablar conmigo después de eso.  | She refused to talk to me after that.        | *querer*, preterite negative |
| Siempre quería viajar más.               | I always wanted to travel more.               | *querer*, imperfect          |
| Quisimos ayudar, pero ya era tarde.      | We tried to help, but it was already too late. | *querer*, preterite affirmative |

## Talking About Managing (or Failing) to Do Something

Using *poder* in the preterite for one-time success or failure.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Al final, pude conseguir las entradas.   | In the end, I managed to get the tickets.    | *poder*, preterite          |
| No pudimos llegar a tiempo por el tráfico. | We couldn't make it on time because of traffic. | *poder*, preterite negative |
| De niño, podía correr muy rápido.        | As a kid, I could run very fast.              | *poder*, imperfect          |
| ¿Pudiste hablar con el jefe?             | Were you able to talk to the boss?             | *poder*, preterite          |

## Mixing All Four in a Short Story

Natural narration that combines these verbs the way a real story would.

| Spanish                                          | English                                       | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| No sabía nada de esto hasta que lo conocí a él.        | I didn't know anything about this until I met him.  | mixed forms                |
| Quiso explicarme todo, pero no pudo encontrar las palabras. | He tried to explain everything, but couldn't find the words. | mixed forms       |
| Cuando supe la verdad, ya conocía a toda su familia.   | By the time I found out the truth, I already knew his whole family. | mixed forms       |
| No quisimos irnos, pero al final pudimos quedarnos un día más. | We didn't want to leave, but in the end we managed to stay one more day. | mixed forms |
