# Multilayered Narrative Framing — Conversational Sentences

## Setting the Scene Before the Main Event

Stacking imperfect background before a preterite payoff, the way real stories open.

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Era muy tarde y todos estaban dormidos cuando llegó.       | It was very late and everyone was asleep when he arrived. | stacked imperfect + preterite |
| Llovía mucho y hacía frío cuando salimos de casa.          | It was raining hard and cold when we left the house. | stacked imperfect + preterite |
| Estábamos todos en la sala viendo la tele cuando se fue la luz. | We were all in the living room watching TV when the power went out. | imperfect + preterite |
| El restaurante estaba lleno y no había mesas cuando llegamos. | The restaurant was full and there were no tables when we arrived. | stacked imperfect + preterite |

## Describing What Was Happening When Something Interrupted

Using the imperfect continuous for the interrupted action.

| Spanish                                    | English                                    | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Estaba manejando cuando me llamaste.            | I was driving when you called me.                  | imperfect continuous       |
| Estábamos cenando cuando empezó el temblor.     | We were having dinner when the earthquake started.  | imperfect continuous       |
| ¿Qué estabas haciendo cuando pasó eso?          | What were you doing when that happened?             | imperfect continuous, question |
| Estaba durmiendo cuando sonó la alarma.         | I was sleeping when the alarm went off.              | imperfect continuous       |

## Describing a Finished Stretch of Activity

Using the preterite continuous for a completed span of time.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Estuve esperando media hora en la fila.  | I was waiting in line for half an hour.    | preterite continuous       |
| Estuvimos hablando hasta muy tarde.      | We were talking until very late.            | preterite continuous       |
| Estuvo lloviendo toda la noche.          | It was raining all night.                    | preterite continuous       |
| Estuve buscando las llaves por todos lados. | I was looking for my keys everywhere.      | preterite continuous       |

## Reflecting on What Should Have Happened

Using *deber* in the past to comment on a story with hindsight.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Debí haber llamado antes de venir.       | I should have called before coming.         | *deber* + *haber*, regret   |
| Debimos haber salido más temprano.       | We should have left earlier.                 | *deber* + *haber*, regret   |
| Le dije que debía descansar más.         | I told her she should rest more.             | *deber*, imperfect advice    |
| Debiste haberme dicho la verdad desde el principio. | You should have told me the truth from the start. | *deber* + *haber*, regret |

## Putting It All Together in a Short Story

Realistic narration mixing background, interruption, and reflection.

| Spanish                                                            | English                                                       | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Era sábado, hacía mucho calor, y estábamos en la playa cuando empezó la tormenta. | It was Saturday, it was very hot, and we were at the beach when the storm started. | full layered scene |
| Estaba durmiendo cuando escuché un ruido extraño en la cocina.           | I was sleeping when I heard a strange noise in the kitchen.            | imperfect continuous + preterite |
| Estuvimos buscando refugio por veinte minutos hasta que por fin encontramos un lugar. | We spent twenty minutes looking for shelter until we finally found a place. | preterite continuous + preterite |
| Debimos haber revisado el clima antes de salir.                          | We should have checked the weather before leaving.                     | *deber* + *haber*    |
