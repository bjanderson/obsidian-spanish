# Irregular Tú Commands — Conversational Sentences

## Calling Someone Over with Ven

Everyday uses of the *venir* command.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ven aquí, quiero mostrarte algo.         | Come here, I want to show you something.       | *venir* → *ven*               |
| Ven conmigo a la cocina.                 | Come with me to the kitchen.                    | *venir* → *ven*                 |
| Ven rápido, es urgente.                  | Come quickly, it's urgent.                       | *venir* → *ven*                   |
| Ven a cenar cuando quieras.              | Come have dinner whenever you want.               | *venir* → *ven*                     |

## Asking Someone to Say Something with Di

Everyday uses of the *decir* command.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Di la verdad, no pasa nada.              | Tell the truth, nothing bad will happen.         | *decir* → *di*                 |
| Dime qué piensas de verdad.              | Tell me what you really think.                    | *decir* → *di*                   |
| Di algo, por favor.                      | Say something, please.                             | *decir* → *di*                     |
| Dile que lo siento mucho.                | Tell him/her that I'm very sorry.                    | *decir* → *di*                       |

## Giving Instructions with Haz, Pon, and Ten

Everyday household and task-related commands.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Haz lo que puedas por hoy.               | Do what you can for today.                       | *hacer* → *haz*                |
| Pon atención, es importante.             | Pay attention, it's important.                     | *poner* → *pon*                  |
| Ten cuidado en la calle.                 | Be careful in the street.                          | *tener* → *ten*                    |
| Haz tu mejor esfuerzo.                   | Do your best effort.                                | *hacer* → *haz*                       |

## Telling Someone to Go Somewhere with Ve

Everyday uses of the *ir* command.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ve a comprar pan, por favor.             | Go buy bread, please.                            | *ir* → *ve*                    |
| Ve a descansar, te lo mereces.           | Go rest, you deserve it.                          | *ir* → *ve*                       |
| Ve con cuidado, está resbaloso.          | Go carefully, it's slippery.                       | *ir* → *ve*                         |
| Ve a preguntarle directamente.           | Go ask him/her directly.                            | *ir* → *ve*                           |

## Everyday Advice Using Sal and Sé

Common phrases mixing more than one irregular command.

| Spanish                                          | English                                    | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Sal de la casa y respira un poco.                        | Get out of the house and get some fresh air.          | *salir* → *sal*             |
| Sé honesto conmigo, siempre.                             | Be honest with me, always.                             | *ser* → *sé*                   |
| Sal a caminar y sé positivo.                             | Go for a walk and be positive.                          | *salir* + *ser*                  |
| Ten fe y sé paciente, todo va a salir bien.               | Have faith and be patient, everything will work out.     | *tener* + *ser*                    |
