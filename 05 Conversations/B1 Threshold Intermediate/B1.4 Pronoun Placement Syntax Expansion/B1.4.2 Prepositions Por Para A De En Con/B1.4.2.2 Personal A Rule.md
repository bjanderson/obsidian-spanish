# Personal A Rule — Conversational Sentences

## Everyday Sentences Seeing or Meeting People

Common statements about running into or seeing someone.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Vi a tu hermano en el centro comercial.  | I saw your brother at the mall.                 | specific person                |
| Conocí a tu amiga en la fiesta.          | I met your friend at the party.                  | specific person                   |
| Ayer visité a mi tía en el hospital.     | Yesterday I visited my aunt in the hospital.       | specific person                     |
| Extraño mucho a mis papás.               | I really miss my parents.                          | specific person                       |

## Caring For People and Pets

Everyday statements about taking care of someone or a pet.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Cuido a mis sobrinos los sábados.        | I take care of my nephews/nieces on Saturdays.  | specific people                |
| Paseo a mi perro todas las mañanas.      | I walk my dog every morning.                       | pet                                |
| Alimento a mis gatos antes de dormir.    | I feed my cats before going to sleep.               | pets                                  |
| Recojo a mi hijo de la escuela.          | I pick up my son from school.                        | specific person                         |

## Things vs. People in the Same Conversation

Pairs showing the contrast between objects and specific people.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Compré una computadora nueva.            | I bought a new computer.                        | thing, no personal *a*         |
| Saludé a mi vecina esta mañana.          | I greeted my neighbor this morning.               | specific person, personal *a*     |
| Perdí mi cartera en el autobús.          | I lost my wallet on the bus.                       | thing, no personal *a*              |
| Encontré a mi amigo perdido después de años. | I found my long-lost friend after years.       | specific person, personal *a*         |

## Looking For Someone Specific vs. Anyone

Everyday contrast between a specific person and a non-specific one.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Busco un plomero de confianza.           | I'm looking for a trustworthy plumber. (any one)  | non-specific, no personal *a*    |
| Busco al plomero que vino la semana pasada. | I'm looking for the plumber who came last week. | specific, personal *a*              |
| Necesito una niñera para el sábado.      | I need a babysitter for Saturday. (any one)       | non-specific, no personal *a*         |
| Necesito a la niñera que siempre viene.  | I need the babysitter who always comes.            | specific, personal *a*                  |

## Everyday Uses of Alguien and Nadie

Common sentences using these indefinite pronouns with the personal a.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Viste a alguien conocido en la fiesta?  | Did you see anyone you knew at the party?          | *alguien*                     |
| No vi a nadie en la calle.               | I didn't see anyone on the street.                   | *nadie*                          |
| ¿Conoces a alguien que hable francés?    | Do you know anyone who speaks French?                 | *alguien*                          |
| No conozco a nadie aquí todavía.         | I don't know anyone here yet.                           | *nadie*                              |
