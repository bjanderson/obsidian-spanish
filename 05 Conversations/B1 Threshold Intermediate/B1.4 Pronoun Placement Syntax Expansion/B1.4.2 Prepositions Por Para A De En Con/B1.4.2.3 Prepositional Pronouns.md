# Prepositional Pronouns — Conversational Sentences

## Everyday Sentences with Para and Mí/Ti

Common everyday statements about who something is for.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Esto es para mí, ¿verdad?                | This is for me, right?                          | *para* + *mí*                  |
| Guardé un pedazo de pastel para ti.      | I saved a piece of cake for you.                  | *para* + *ti*                    |
| Este mensaje no era para mí.             | This message wasn't for me.                        | *para* + *mí*                      |
| ¿Tienes algo especial para ti este año?  | Do you have something special for yourself this year? | *para* + *ti*                    |

## Talking About or With Someone

Everyday sentences using "de" and "con" with prepositional pronouns.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Hablamos mucho de ti la semana pasada.   | We talked a lot about you last week.              | *de* + *ti*                    |
| Nunca hablo mal de él.                   | I never speak badly of him.                        | *de* + *él*                       |
| Prefiero ir con ella que con nadie más.  | I'd rather go with her than with anyone else.        | *con* + *ella*                      |
| Quiero contarte algo sobre nosotros.     | I want to tell you something about us.               | *sobre* + *nosotros*                  |

## Everyday Invitations with Conmigo and Contigo

Common invitations and plans using these fused forms.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Vienes conmigo al cine esta noche?      | Are you coming with me to the movies tonight?    | *conmigo*                      |
| Claro que voy contigo, cuenta conmigo.   | Of course I'll go with you, count on me.           | *contigo* + *conmigo*             |
| Nadie quiere quedarse conmigo hoy.       | Nobody wants to stay with me today.                | *conmigo*, idiomatic                 |
| Me encantaría vivir contigo algún día.   | I would love to live with you someday.              | *contigo*                              |

## Using Sin and A Favor with Pronouns

Everyday sentences without or in favor of someone.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| No puedo vivir sin ti.                   | I can't live without you.                       | *sin* + *ti*                    |
| Todos están a favor de nosotros.         | Everyone is in favor of us.                       | *a favor de* + *nosotros*          |
| Sin ellos, esto no sería posible.        | Without them, this wouldn't be possible.            | *sin* + *ellos*                       |
| Confío mucho en ella.                    | I trust her a lot.                                | *en* + *ella*                           |

## The Entre Exception in Everyday Speech

Common everyday sentences using entre with subject pronouns.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Que quede entre tú y yo.                 | Let it stay between you and me.                  | *entre*, subject pronouns      |
| Entre tú y yo, no confío mucho en él.    | Between you and me, I don't trust him much.        | *entre*, subject pronouns          |
| Hay mucha confianza entre tú y yo.       | There's a lot of trust between you and me.          | *entre*, subject pronouns            |
| Este plan es solo entre tú y yo.         | This plan is just between you and me.                | *entre*, subject pronouns              |
