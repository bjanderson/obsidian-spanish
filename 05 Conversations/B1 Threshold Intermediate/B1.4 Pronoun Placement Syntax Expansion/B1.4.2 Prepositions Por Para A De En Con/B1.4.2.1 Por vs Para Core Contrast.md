# Por vs. Para Core Contrast — Conversational Sentences

## Everyday Reasons and Causes with Por

Common everyday explanations for why something happened.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Llegué tarde por el tráfico.             | I arrived late because of traffic.              | *por*, cause                  |
| No fui a la fiesta por el mal clima.     | I didn't go to the party because of the bad weather. | *por*, cause                    |
| Me disculpé por el malentendido.         | I apologized for the misunderstanding.            | *por*, cause                       |
| Estoy cansado por el viaje.              | I'm tired because of the trip.                     | *por*, cause                         |

## Everyday Goals and Purposes with Para

Common everyday statements about what something is for.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Estudio para aprender un nuevo idioma.   | I study to learn a new language.                | *para*, purpose                |
| Trabajo duro para darle una buena vida a mi familia. | I work hard to give my family a good life.  | *para*, purpose                |
| Compré esto para mi hermana.             | I bought this for my sister.                       | *para*, recipient                  |
| Vine para ayudarte con la mudanza.       | I came to help you with the move.                   | *para*, purpose                      |

## Talking About Time: Duration vs. Deadline

Everyday sentences showing por for duration and para for deadlines.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Estudié por cuatro horas anoche.         | I studied for four hours last night.             | *por*, duration                |
| Necesito terminarlo para mañana.         | I need to finish it by tomorrow.                   | *para*, deadline                  |
| Viví en México por dos años.             | I lived in Mexico for two years.                    | *por*, duration                     |
| El proyecto es para el viernes.          | The project is due Friday.                           | *para*, deadline                      |

## Everyday Exchanges, Trades, and Purchases

Common sentences about paying, trading, or buying something.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Pagué bastante por estos zapatos.        | I paid a lot for these shoes.                     | *por*, exchange                |
| Cambié mi libro por otro más interesante. | I traded my book for a more interesting one.      | *por*, exchange                  |
| ¿Cuánto pagaste por el boleto?           | How much did you pay for the ticket?                | *por*, exchange                    |
| Este regalo es para mi mejor amigo.      | This gift is for my best friend.                     | *para*, recipient                    |

## Movement Through vs. Destination

Everyday sentences about moving through a place versus heading somewhere.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Caminamos por el parque toda la tarde.   | We walked through the park all afternoon.       | *por*, movement through        |
| Salimos para la playa mañana temprano.   | We're leaving for the beach early tomorrow.       | *para*, destination               |
| Pasamos por el centro antes de llegar.   | We passed through downtown before arriving.         | *por*, movement through            |
| Ese autobús va para el aeropuerto.       | That bus is headed for the airport.                  | *para*, destination                  |
