# Converting Le/Les to Se — Conversational Sentences

## Giving Things to a Specific Person

Common everyday sentences giving something to one person.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Se lo di a mi hermano.                   | I gave it to my brother.                        | *le* → *se*, clarified         |
| Se la regalé a mi novia.                 | I gave it to my girlfriend as a gift.              | *le* → *se*, clarified            |
| Se lo mandé a mi papá por correo.        | I sent it to my dad by mail.                       | *le* → *se*, clarified              |
| ¿Se lo compraste a tu mamá?              | Did you buy it for your mom?                        | *le* → *se*, clarified                |

## Explaining or Telling Something to a Group

Common everyday sentences involving telling or explaining to multiple people.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Se lo expliqué a los estudiantes.        | I explained it to the students.                  | *les* → *se*, clarified       |
| Se los mostramos a nuestros clientes.    | We showed them to our clients.                     | *les* → *se*, clarified, *nosotros* |
| Se las contamos a nuestros amigos.       | We told them to our friends.                        | *les* → *se*, clarified, *nosotros*   |
| ¿Se lo dijiste a tus papás?              | Did you tell it to your parents?                     | *les* → *se*, clarified                 |

## Ambiguous Se Clarified with A

Everyday sentences showing how context or an "a" phrase resolves who "se" means.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Se lo di a ella, no a él.                | I gave it to her, not to him.                   | clarifying *a ella*             |
| Se lo entregué a usted, no a ellos.      | I delivered it to you, not to them.               | clarifying *a usted*               |
| Se los presté a mis vecinos.             | I lent them to my neighbors.                       | clarifying *a mis vecinos*           |
| Se la envié a la oficina de mi jefe.     | I sent it to my boss's office.                      | clarifying with a place                |

## Everyday Requests and Confirmations

Common back-and-forth exchanges using the se + direct object pattern.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Se lo puedes pasar cuando termines?     | Can you pass it to him/her when you're done?      | *le* → *se*                    |
| Ya se lo confirmé por teléfono.          | I already confirmed it to him/her by phone.        | *le* → *se*                      |
| Se lo recordé varias veces.              | I reminded him/her of it several times.             | *le* → *se*                        |
| Se lo advertí, pero no me hizo caso.     | I warned him/her, but he/she didn't listen to me.    | *le* → *se*                          |

## Business and Service Contexts

Common sentences about delivering or providing something to customers.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Se lo enviamos mañana sin costo extra.   | We'll send it to you tomorrow at no extra cost.    | *le* → *se*, business context |
| Se los entregamos en la puerta.          | We deliver them to you at the door.                | *les* → *se*, business context   |
| Se lo garantizamos por un año.           | We guarantee it to you for a year.                   | *le* → *se*, business context       |
| Se la cambiamos sin ningún problema.     | We'll exchange it for you with no problem.            | *le* → *se*, business context         |
