# Order of Pronouns — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Gifts and Favors

Common everyday sentences about giving or lending something.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Me lo regaló mi abuela.                  | My grandmother gave it to me as a gift.          | indirect *me* + direct *lo*    |
| Te lo presto con gusto.                  | I'll gladly lend it to you.                       | indirect *te* + direct *lo*      |
| Nos las mandaron por correo.             | They sent them to us by mail.                      | indirect *nos* + direct *las*      |
| ¿Me lo puedes dar mañana?                | Can you give it to me tomorrow?                     | indirect *me* + direct *lo*          |

## Asking Someone to Explain or Show Something

Everyday requests involving explanations or demonstrations.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Te lo explico paso a paso.               | I'll explain it to you step by step.              | indirect *te* + direct *lo*     |
| ¿Me lo puedes mostrar otra vez?          | Can you show it to me again?                        | indirect *me* + direct *lo*       |
| Nos lo enseñaron en la escuela.          | They taught it to us in school.                      | indirect *nos* + direct *lo*        |
| Te la muestro en un momento.             | I'll show it to you in a moment.                      | indirect *te* + direct *la*           |

## Everyday Deliveries and Bringing Things

Common sentences about bringing or delivering something.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Nos lo trajeron esta mañana.             | They brought it to us this morning.               | indirect *nos* + direct *lo*    |
| Te los mando en cuanto pueda.            | I'll send them to you as soon as I can.             | indirect *te* + direct *los*      |
| Me la entregaron ayer.                   | They delivered it to me yesterday.                  | indirect *me* + direct *la*         |
| ¿Nos las traes cuando vengas?            | Will you bring them to us when you come?             | indirect *nos* + direct *las*         |

## Talking About Money and Loans

Everyday sentences about lending or paying back.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Te lo pago la próxima semana.            | I'll pay it back to you next week.                | indirect *te* + direct *lo*     |
| ¿Me lo puedes prestar hasta el viernes?  | Can you lend it to me until Friday?                | indirect *me* + direct *lo*       |
| Nos lo cobraron de más.                  | They overcharged us for it.                         | indirect *nos* + direct *lo*        |
| Te la debo, te la pago pronto.           | I owe it to you, I'll pay you back soon.             | indirect *te* + direct *la*           |

## Confirming and Clarifying in Conversation

Everyday back-and-forth confirming who told/gave what to whom.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Quién te lo dijo?                       | Who told it to you?                             | indirect *te* + direct *lo*    |
| Me lo confirmaron por mensaje.           | They confirmed it to me by message.                | indirect *me* + direct *lo*      |
| Nos lo repitieron varias veces.          | They repeated it to us several times.               | indirect *nos* + direct *lo*       |
| Te lo aseguro, es verdad.                | I assure you, it's true.                             | indirect *te* + direct *lo*, idiomatic |
