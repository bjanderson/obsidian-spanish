# Additive and Contrastive Connectors — Conversational Sentences

## Adding Extra Information with Además

Common everyday ways to add on to what you just said.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Me gusta esta ciudad. Además, la comida es increíble. | I like this city. Plus, the food is incredible. | *además*                    |
| No tengo tiempo hoy. Además, estoy muy cansado. | I don't have time today. Besides, I'm very tired. | *además*                       |
| Habla inglés muy bien. Además, sabe francés. | He/she speaks English really well. Plus, he/she knows French. | *además*                         |
| Es puntual. Además, siempre es amable.   | He/she is punctual. Also, he/she is always kind.   | *además*                           |

## Introducing a Contrast with Sin Embargo

Common everyday ways to soften a statement with a "however."

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Quería quedarme en casa. Sin embargo, salí con mis amigos. | I wanted to stay home. However, I went out with my friends. | *sin embargo*   |
| El examen fue difícil. Sin embargo, aprobé.  | The exam was hard. However, I passed.              | *sin embargo*                      |
| No conocía a nadie. Sin embargo, hice amigos rápido. | I didn't know anyone. However, I made friends quickly. | *sin embargo*                       |
| Llovía mucho. Sin embargo, decidimos ir de todas formas. | It was raining a lot. However, we decided to go anyway. | *sin embargo*                         |

## The More Formal No Obstante

Everyday statements using the slightly more formal alternative to sin embargo.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| El proyecto tenía muchos problemas. No obstante, lo terminamos a tiempo. | The project had many problems. Nonetheless, we finished it on time. | *no obstante*, formal |
| No tenía experiencia. No obstante, consiguió el trabajo. | He/she had no experience. Nonetheless, he/she got the job. | *no obstante*, formal   |
| La situación era complicada. No obstante, encontramos una solución. | The situation was complicated. Nonetheless, we found a solution. | *no obstante*, formal     |
| El clima no ayudaba. No obstante, disfrutamos el viaje. | The weather wasn't helping. Nonetheless, we enjoyed the trip. | *no obstante*, formal       |

## Combining Ideas with Aunque

Common everyday sentences joining two ideas within one sentence.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Aunque estaba lloviendo, salimos a correr. | Although it was raining, we went out for a run. | *aunque*, sentence-internal     |
| Fuimos a la playa aunque hacía frío.     | We went to the beach even though it was cold. | *aunque*, sentence-internal       |
| Aunque no tengo mucho dinero, soy feliz. | Although I don't have much money, I'm happy. | *aunque*, sentence-internal         |
| Terminé el proyecto aunque estaba muy cansado. | I finished the project even though I was very tired. | *aunque*, sentence-internal           |

## Everyday Conversations Mixing Multiple Connectors

Realistic sentences combining more than one connector.

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Aunque estaba nervioso, di la presentación. Además, salió muy bien. | Although I was nervous, I gave the presentation. Plus, it went really well. | *aunque* + *además*   |
| No tenía ganas de ir. Sin embargo, fui, y además me divertí mucho. | I didn't feel like going. However, I went, and I also had a lot of fun. | *sin embargo* + *además* |
