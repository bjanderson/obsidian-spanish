# Causal and Consequential Connectors — Conversational Sentences

## Explaining Reasons with Ya Que

Common everyday explanations that lead with the reason.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ya que estás libre, vamos a comer algo.  | Since you're free, let's go get something to eat. | *ya que*, cause             |
| Ya que lo mencionas, sí tengo una pregunta. | Since you mention it, yes, I do have a question. | *ya que*, cause                |
| Ya que no llovió, pudimos salir a caminar. | Since it didn't rain, we were able to go for a walk. | *ya que*, cause                  |
| Ya que insistes, acepto la invitación.   | Since you insist, I accept the invitation.        | *ya que*, cause                    |

## Everyday Cause-and-Effect with Por Eso

Common everyday statements giving the reason first, then the result.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| No dormí bien. Por eso, estoy de mal humor. | I didn't sleep well. That's why I'm in a bad mood. | *por eso*, consequence     |
| Se me hizo tarde. Por eso, tomé un taxi. | I ran late. That's why I took a taxi.               | *por eso*, consequence        |
| Perdí mi teléfono. Por eso, no contesté tus mensajes. | I lost my phone. That's why I didn't answer your messages. | *por eso*, consequence          |
| Me encanta cocinar. Por eso, decidí estudiar gastronomía. | I love cooking. That's why I decided to study culinary arts. | *por eso*, consequence            |

## Formal Consequences with Por Lo Tanto

Everyday-but-more-formal statements, common in explanations at work or school.

| Spanish                                        | English                                  | Notes                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Las ventas bajaron este mes. Por lo tanto, revisaremos la estrategia. | Sales dropped this month. Therefore, we'll review the strategy. | *por lo tanto*, formal       |
| No cumplió con los requisitos. Por lo tanto, no fue aceptado. | He/she didn't meet the requirements. Therefore, he/she wasn't accepted. | *por lo tanto*, formal   |
| El clima empeoró. Por lo tanto, cancelamos el viaje. | The weather got worse. Therefore, we canceled the trip.  | *por lo tanto*, formal        |
| Faltaba información. Por lo tanto, pospusimos la decisión. | Information was missing. Therefore, we postponed the decision. | *por lo tanto*, formal           |

## Combining Cause and Consequence in One Story

Realistic short exchanges linking a cause to its result.

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ya que llegamos tarde, nos perdimos el inicio. Por eso, no entendimos bien la trama. | Since we arrived late, we missed the beginning. That's why we didn't understand the plot well. | *ya que* + *por eso*  |
| Ya que no había boletos, cambiamos de planes. Por lo tanto, fuimos a otro lugar. | Since there were no tickets, we changed plans. Therefore, we went somewhere else. | *ya que* + *por lo tanto* |

## Everyday Ya Que vs. Porque

Pairs showing the subtle difference between these two "because" words.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Por qué llegaste tarde?                 | Why did you arrive late?                        | direct question                |
| Llegué tarde porque perdí el autobús.    | I arrived late because I missed the bus.          | *porque*, answers "why"          |
| Ya que perdiste el autobús, toma un taxi la próxima vez. | Since you missed the bus, take a taxi next time. | *ya que*, known/inferred reason    |
