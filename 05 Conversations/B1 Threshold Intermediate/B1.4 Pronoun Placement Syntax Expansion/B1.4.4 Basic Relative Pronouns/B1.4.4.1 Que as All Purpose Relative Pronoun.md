# Que as All-Purpose Relative Pronoun — Conversational Sentences

## Describing People with Que

Common everyday sentences identifying someone by what they do.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| El chico que conocí en la fiesta es tu primo. | The guy I met at the party is your cousin.   | *que*, person                |
| La señora que vende tamales pasa todos los días. | The lady who sells tamales comes by every day. | *que*, person                  |
| El amigo que me ayudó se llama Diego.    | The friend who helped me is named Diego.          | *que*, person                    |
| Los vecinos que se mudaron eran muy amables. | The neighbors who moved were very kind.         | *que*, plural people                |

## Describing Things with Que

Common everyday sentences identifying an item or place.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| El restaurante que me recomendaste es excelente. | The restaurant you recommended to me is excellent. | *que*, thing            |
| La canción que escuchamos anoche me encantó. | The song that we listened to last night, I loved. | *que*, thing                 |
| El trabajo que tengo ahora es mejor que el anterior. | The job I have now is better than the previous one. | *que*, thing                   |
| El libro que me prestaste está muy bueno. | The book you lent me is really good.               | *que*, thing                     |

## Everyday Descriptions in Casual Conversation

Common back-and-forth using que to clarify who or what someone means.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Te acuerdas del lugar que visitamos el año pasado? | Do you remember the place we visited last year? | *que*, place    |
| Ese es el perro que siempre ladra en las noches. | That's the dog that always barks at night.        | *que*, animal                |
| La receta que usé viene de mi abuela.    | The recipe I used comes from my grandmother.       | *que*, thing                    |
| El día que llegaste llovía muchísimo.    | The day you arrived, it was raining a lot.          | *que*, time expression             |

## Identifying Specific People or Things in a Group

Everyday sentences narrowing down which one you mean.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| El que llegó tarde fue Marco, no yo.     | The one who arrived late was Marco, not me.        | *que*, identifying person       |
| La camisa que está en la mesa es mía.    | The shirt that's on the table is mine.               | *que*, identifying thing           |
| Los que quieran venir, avísenme.         | Whoever wants to come, let me know.                  | *que*, identifying group              |
| Elegí el que era más barato.             | I chose the one that was cheaper.                     | *que*, identifying thing                |

## Never Dropping Que in Everyday Speech

Pairs showing the English drop vs. the required Spanish "que."

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| La persona que conocí ayer es simpática. | The person I met yesterday is nice.               | English can drop "that/who," Spanish can't |
| El correo que envié no llegó.            | The email I sent didn't arrive.                     | English can drop "that," Spanish can't       |
| La ciudad que visitamos es preciosa.     | The city we visited is beautiful.                     | English can drop "that," Spanish can't         |
