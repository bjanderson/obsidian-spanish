# Quien/Quienes for People after Prepositions — Conversational Sentences

## Talking about Who You Work or Study With

Everyday sentences identifying a coworker, classmate, or partner in an activity.

| Spanish                                          | English                                       | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| La chica con quien estudio español es de Chile.   | The girl I study Spanish with is from Chile.       | *con quien*, singular            |
| El compañero con quien comparto oficina llega tarde. | The coworker I share an office with arrives late. | *con quien*, singular              |
| Los colegas con quienes trabajo son muy amables.  | The colleagues I work with are very kind.          | *con quienes*, plural                |
| La vecina con quien salgo a caminar vive al lado. | The neighbor I go walking with lives next door.    | *con quien*, singular                  |

## Talking about Who You Did Something For

Sentences about gifts, favors, or effort made on someone's behalf.

| Spanish                                             | English                                        | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| El amigo para quien organicé la fiesta cumple treinta años. | The friend I organized the party for is turning thirty. | *para quien*, singular    |
| La compañera para quien traduje el documento me lo agradeció. | The colleague I translated the document for thanked me. | *para quien*, singular      |
| Los clientes para quienes preparamos la propuesta la aprobaron. | The clients we prepared the proposal for approved it.   | *para quienes*, plural        |
| La profesora para quien hice el favor me devolvió el favor. | The teacher I did the favor for returned the favor.      | *para quien*, singular          |

## Mentioning Someone You Saw or Talked About

Sentences using the personal *a* before *quien* for a direct object, or *de quien(es)* for someone mentioned in conversation.

| Spanish                                       | English                                    | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| La persona a quien vi en el metro trabaja conmigo. | The person I saw on the subway works with me. | *a quien*, personal *a*             |
| El chico de quien te hablé llegó ayer.        | The guy I told you about arrived yesterday.  | *de quien*, singular                  |
| Las amigas de quienes hablamos ya se fueron.  | The friends we talked about already left.     | *de quienes*, plural                    |
| El vecino a quien saludé esta mañana es nuevo. | The neighbor I greeted this morning is new.  | *a quien*, personal *a*                   |

## Adding Extra Information about Someone

Non-restrictive clauses, set off by commas, giving extra detail about a person already identified.

| Spanish                                         | English                                    | Notes                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Mi jefa, quien acaba de volver de viaje, tiene una reunión hoy. | My boss, who just got back from a trip, has a meeting today. | *quien*, non-restrictive            |
| Mi primo, quien vive en Medellín, viene a visitarnos. | My cousin, who lives in Medellín, is coming to visit us. | *quien*, non-restrictive              |
| Su abuela, quien tiene noventa años, todavía cocina todos los días. | Her grandmother, who is ninety years old, still cooks every day. | *quien*, non-restrictive                |
| Nuestro vecino, quien es médico, nos ayudó anoche. | Our neighbor, who is a doctor, helped us last night. | *quien*, non-restrictive                  |

## Groups of People with Quienes

Sentences about a plural group linked by a preposition.

| Spanish                                        | English                                   | Notes                  |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Los amigos con quienes salgo los sábados son de la universidad. | The friends I go out with on Saturdays are from college. | *con quienes*, plural           |
| Las primas con quienes crecí viven ahora en Lima. | The cousins I grew up with now live in Lima.     | *con quienes*, plural             |
| Los vecinos de quienes te hablé se mudaron el mes pasado. | The neighbors I told you about moved last month. | *de quienes*, plural                 |
