# Donde, Como, Cuando as Relative Adverbs — Conversational Sentences

## Talking about Places with Donde

Everyday sentences pointing back to a specific place.

| Spanish                                            | English                                       | Notes            |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| El restaurante donde comimos ayer es buenísimo.     | The restaurant where we ate yesterday is really good. | *donde*, place            |
| Este es el barrio donde crecí.                      | This is the neighborhood where I grew up.           | *donde*, place              |
| La tienda donde compré la camisa está cerrada hoy.  | The store where I bought the shirt is closed today.  | *donde*, place                |
| No recuerdo el hotel donde nos quedamos esa vez.    | I don't remember the hotel where we stayed that time. | *donde*, place                  |

## Talking about Times with Cuando

Everyday sentences pointing back to a moment or period.

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes           |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| Recuerdo la primera vez cuando te vi.              | I remember the first time when I saw you.        | *cuando*, time            |
| Fue en esa época cuando empecé a estudiar español. | It was during that time when I started studying Spanish. | *cuando*, time              |
| Ese verano cuando viajamos juntos fue increíble.   | That summer when we traveled together was amazing. | *cuando*, time                |
| Todavía pienso en el día cuando me gradué.         | I still think about the day when I graduated.     | *cuando*, time                  |

## Describing a Way of Doing Something with Como

Everyday sentences pointing back to a manner or method.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Me encanta como explicas las cosas.                    | I love how you explain things.                      | *como*, manner        |
| Nadie entiende como logró terminar tan rápido.         | Nobody understands how he managed to finish so fast. | *como*, manner          |
| Admiro como manejas el estrés en el trabajo.           | I admire how you handle stress at work.              | *como*, manner            |
| No me gusta como quedó pintada la pared.               | I don't like how the wall turned out painted.        | *como*, manner              |

## Casual Back-and-Forth Using All Three

Everyday conversational exchanges that mix place, time, and manner in context.

| Spanish                                             | English                                       | Notes            |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| ¿Te acuerdas del lugar donde nos conocimos?              | Do you remember the place where we met?              | *donde*, place            |
| Fue justo cuando llegaste que empezó a llover.           | It was right when you arrived that it started to rain. | *cuando*, time              |
| Así como lo hiciste tú, quedó perfecto.                  | The way you did it, it turned out perfect.            | *como*, manner                |
| Ese es el pueblo donde vive mi abuela.                   | That's the town where my grandmother lives.            | *donde*, place                  |

## Extra Detail with Non-Restrictive Clauses

Sentences using these adverbs with commas to add extra information, not to identify.

| Spanish                                                    | English                                                | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Mi pueblo, donde todos se conocen, es muy pequeño.           | My town, where everyone knows each other, is very small.      | *donde*, non-restrictive           |
| El próximo mes, cuando termine el proyecto, voy a descansar. | Next month, when I finish the project, I'm going to rest.      | *cuando*, non-restrictive             |
| Lo resolvió a su manera, como siempre hace las cosas.        | He solved it his way, as he always does things.                | *como*, non-restrictive                 |
