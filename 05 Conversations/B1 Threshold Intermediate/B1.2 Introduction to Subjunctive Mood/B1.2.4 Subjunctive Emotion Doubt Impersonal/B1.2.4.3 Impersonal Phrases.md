# Impersonal Phrases — Conversational Sentences

## Giving Advice with Es Importante Que

Everyday advice using this common impersonal expression.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Es importante que descanses bien.        | It's important that you rest well.              | *es importante que*           |
| Es importante que hables con ella.       | It's important that you talk to her.             | *es importante que*             |
| Es importante que todos estén de acuerdo. | It's important that everyone agree.             | *es importante que*, plural        |
| Es importante que sepas la verdad.       | It's important that you know the truth.          | *es importante que*                 |

## Stating Requirements with Es Necesario Que

Common statements of necessity, especially at work or school.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Es necesario que entreguemos el proyecto hoy. | It's necessary that we submit the project today. | *es necesario que*, *nosotros*   |
| Es necesario que traigas tu identificación. | It's necessary that you bring your ID.            | *es necesario que*                  |
| Es necesario que todos firmen el documento. | It's necessary that everyone sign the document.    | *es necesario que*, plural            |
| Es necesario que lleguemos antes de las ocho. | It's necessary that we arrive before eight.        | *es necesario que*, *nosotros*          |

## Other Common Judgment Expressions

Everyday value judgments beyond importance and necessity.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Es bueno que lo intentes.                | It's good that you're trying.                   | *es bueno que*                |
| Es raro que no haya respondido.          | It's strange that he/she hasn't responded.       | *es raro que*                   |
| Es mejor que esperemos aquí.             | It's better that we wait here.                   | *es mejor que*, *nosotros*        |
| Es una lástima que no puedas venir.      | It's a shame that you can't come.                | *es una lástima que*               |

## Facts vs. Judgments in Everyday Speech

Pairs contrasting fact-based (indicative) and judgment-based (subjunctive) impersonal phrases.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Es verdad que llueve mucho aquí.         | It's true that it rains a lot here.              | fact → indicative                |
| Es raro que llueva tanto en esta época.  | It's strange that it rains this much this time of year. | judgment → subjunctive         |
| Es cierto que el examen es difícil.      | It's certain that the exam is hard.               | fact → indicative                  |
| Es importante que estudies para el examen difícil. | It's important that you study for the hard exam. | judgment → subjunctive             |

## Everyday General Statements Without a Subject

Common uses of the infinitive when there's no specific person in mind.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Es importante dormir bien.               | It's important to sleep well.                   | infinitive, general statement    |
| Es necesario practicar todos los días.   | It's necessary to practice every day.            | infinitive, general statement      |
| Es bueno comer verduras.                 | It's good to eat vegetables.                     | infinitive, general statement        |
| Es mejor no discutir por tonterías.      | It's better not to argue over silly things.        | infinitive, general statement          |
