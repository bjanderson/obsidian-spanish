# Expressions of Emotion — Conversational Sentences

## Expressing Happiness with Me Alegra Que

Everyday ways to say something makes you happy.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Me alegra que hayas venido.              | I'm glad you came.                            | *me alegra que*             |
| Me alegra que todo salga bien.           | I'm glad everything is turning out well.        | *me alegra que*               |
| Nos alegra que estén todos aquí.         | We're glad everyone's here.                     | *nos alegra que*, *nosotros*     |
| Le alegra que su hijo saque buenas notas. | It makes him/her happy that his/her son gets good grades. | *le alegra que*, third person |

## Expressing Fear with Tengo Miedo De Que

Everyday worries and fears about outcomes.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Tengo miedo de que se enoje conmigo.     | I'm afraid he/she will get mad at me.           | *tener miedo de que*           |
| Tenemos miedo de que cancelen el vuelo.  | We're afraid they'll cancel the flight.          | *tener miedo de que*, *nosotros*  |
| Tiene miedo de que la despidan.          | She's afraid they'll fire her.                   | *tener miedo de que*, third person |
| Tengo miedo de que no alcance el tiempo. | I'm afraid there won't be enough time.            | *tener miedo de que*, idiomatic     |

## Expressing Surprise and Annoyance

Reactions of surprise or irritation, very common in casual speech.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Me sorprende que no hayas contestado.    | It surprises me that you didn't answer.         | *me sorprende que*             |
| Me molesta que lleguen tarde siempre.    | It bothers me that they always arrive late.       | *me molesta que*, plural           |
| Le sorprende que ya termines.            | It surprises him/her that you're already finishing. | *le sorprende que*             |
| Nos molesta que no avisen antes.         | It bothers us that they don't give notice beforehand. | *nos molesta que*, *nosotros*      |

## Expressing Sadness and Sympathy

Common expressions for sadness or feeling sorry for someone.

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Siento mucho que te sientas mal.           | I'm very sorry you're feeling bad.                | *sentir que*                |
| Me pone triste que no puedas venir.        | It makes me sad that you can't come.               | *me pone triste que*           |
| Nos entristece que se mude tan lejos.      | It saddens us that he/she is moving so far away.    | *nos entristece que*, *nosotros*    |
| Le da pena que nadie se acuerde.           | It saddens him/her that nobody remembers.            | *le da pena que*, idiomatic          |

## Certain Facts That Still Trigger the Subjunctive

Pairs showing that certainty doesn't remove the subjunctive when emotion is the trigger.

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Es viernes.                                    | It's Friday.                                   | plain statement, indicative  |
| Me alegra que hoy sea viernes.                 | I'm glad today is Friday.                       | certain fact, still subjunctive |
| Ganaste el partido.                            | You won the game.                               | plain statement, indicative  |
| Me alegra mucho que hayas ganado el partido.   | I'm very glad you won the game.                  | certain fact, still subjunctive |
