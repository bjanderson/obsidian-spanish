# Requests and Recommendations — Conversational Sentences

## Making Direct Requests with Pedir Que

Common everyday requests using *pedir que*.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Te pido que me hagas un favor.           | I'm asking you to do me a favor.               | *pedir que*                 |
| Le pido que hable más despacio.          | I'm asking him/her to speak more slowly.         | *pedir que*, *usted*          |
| Nos piden que lleguemos temprano.        | They're asking us to arrive early.               | *pedir que*, *nosotros*         |
| Les pido que tengan paciencia.           | I'm asking you all to be patient.                | *pedir que*, plural             |

## Giving Recommendations with Recomendar Que

Everyday advice and recommendations.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Te recomiendo que veas esa película.     | I recommend that you watch that movie.          | *recomendar que*              |
| Le recomiendo que descanse un poco.      | I recommend that he/she rest a little.           | *recomendar que*, *usted*         |
| Mi médico me recomienda que camine más. | My doctor recommends that I walk more.           | *recomendar que*, health context   |
| Nos recomiendan que reservemos con tiempo. | They recommend we make a reservation ahead of time. | *recomendar que*, *nosotros*   |

## Offering Suggestions with Sugerir Que

Lighter, softer suggestions than a recommendation.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Te sugiero que lo pienses bien.          | I suggest you think it over.                    | *sugerir que*                |
| Le sugiero que hable con su jefe.        | I suggest he/she talk to his/her boss.           | *sugerir que*, *usted*          |
| Les sugiero que traigan agua.            | I suggest you all bring water.                   | *sugerir que*, plural             |
| Nos sugieren que evitemos esa calle.     | They suggest we avoid that street.               | *sugerir que*, *nosotros*         |

## Pedir vs. Preguntar in Context

Pairs showing the difference between requesting an action and asking a question.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Te pregunto si vienes a la fiesta.       | I'm asking you if you're coming to the party.     | *preguntar*, question about fact   |
| Te pido que vengas a la fiesta.          | I'm asking you to come to the party.              | *pedir que*, requesting action     |
| Le pregunto qué hora es.                 | I'm asking him/her what time it is.               | *preguntar*, information            |
| Le pido que me diga la hora.             | I'm asking him/her to tell me the time.            | *pedir que*, requesting action       |

## Combining Requests and Recommendations in Real Speech

Realistic sentences mixing more than one trigger verb.

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Te pido que confíes en mí y te recomiendo que lo intentes.    | I'm asking you to trust me and I recommend you try it.  | *pedir que* + *recomendar que* |
| Te sugiero que descanses y te pido que no trabajes tanto.     | I suggest you rest and I'm asking you not to work so much. | *sugerir que* + *pedir que*     |
| Mi jefe me pide que llegue temprano y me sugiere que organice mejor mi tiempo. | My boss asks me to arrive early and suggests I organize my time better. | *pedir que* + *sugerir que*    |
