# Verbs of Desire and Will — Conversational Sentences

## Asking What Someone Wants From You

The classic "what do you want me to do" pattern — extremely common in daily
Spanish.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Qué quieres que haga?                   | What do you want me to do?                    | *querer que*                |
| ¿Quieres que te ayude con eso?           | Do you want me to help you with that?          | *querer que*                |
| ¿Quieres que vaya contigo?               | Do you want me to go with you?                  | *querer que*, *ir*            |
| ¿Quieres que te llame más tarde?         | Do you want me to call you later?                | *querer que*                |

## Telling Someone What You Want Them to Do

Everyday requests and instructions using *querer que*.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Quiero que me digas la verdad.           | I want you to tell me the truth.               | *querer que*                |
| Quiero que descanses este fin de semana. | I want you to rest this weekend.                | *querer que*                |
| Mi mamá quiere que la llame todos los días. | My mom wants me to call her every day.       | *querer que*, third person    |
| No quiero que te preocupes por mí.       | I don't want you to worry about me.             | *querer que*, negation          |

## Expressing Hope with Esperar Que

Everyday hopes for other people's outcomes.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que te vaya bien en la entrevista. | I hope it goes well for you in the interview. | *esperar que*, idiomatic     |
| Espero que descanses bien esta noche.    | I hope you rest well tonight.                  | *esperar que*                |
| Esperamos que todos lleguen sin problema. | We hope everyone arrives without trouble.       | *esperar que*, *nosotros*      |
| Espero que no sea nada grave.            | I hope it's nothing serious.                     | *esperar que*, health context     |

## Wishing Someone Well with Desear Que

Polite, warm wishes — common at celebrations and goodbyes.

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Te deseo que tengas mucho éxito.           | I wish you a lot of success.                     | *desear que*                |
| Deseamos que se mejore pronto.             | We wish you a speedy recovery.                    | *desear que*, formal (usted)   |
| Les deseo que pasen unas felices fiestas.  | I wish you all a happy holiday season.             | *desear que*, plural           |
| Deseo que todo salga como lo planeaste.    | I hope everything turns out how you planned it.    | *desear que*, idiomatic          |

## Same Subject vs. Different Subject in Practice

Pairs showing when the sentence switches from infinitive to subjunctive.

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Quiero salir temprano hoy.                 | I want to leave early today.                      | same subject → infinitive      |
| Quiero que salgas temprano hoy.            | I want you to leave early today.                   | different subject → subjunctive |
| Espero terminar el proyecto a tiempo.      | I hope to finish the project on time.               | same subject → infinitive      |
| Espero que terminemos el proyecto a tiempo. | I hope we finish the project on time.              | different subject → subjunctive |
