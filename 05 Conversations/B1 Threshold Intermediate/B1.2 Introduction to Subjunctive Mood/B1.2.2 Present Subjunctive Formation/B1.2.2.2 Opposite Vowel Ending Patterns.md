# Opposite Vowel Ending Patterns — Conversational Sentences

## Wishing Someone Would Do an -AR Verb

Everyday sentences using the *-e-* subjunctive ending on -AR verbs.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que hables con tu jefe.           | I hope you talk to your boss.                 | -AR, *-e-* ending           |
| Quiero que estudies más para el examen.  | I want you to study more for the exam.          | -AR, *-e-* ending           |
| Ojalá que llegues a tiempo.              | I hope you arrive on time.                     | -AR, *-e-* ending           |
| Dudo que trabaje los fines de semana.    | I doubt he works on weekends.                   | -AR, *-e-* ending           |

## Wishing Someone Would Do an -ER/-IR Verb

Everyday sentences using the *-a-* subjunctive ending on -ER/-IR verbs.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que comas algo antes de salir.    | I hope you eat something before leaving.       | -ER, *-a-* ending           |
| Quiero que leas este libro.              | I want you to read this book.                   | -ER, *-a-* ending           |
| Dudo que vivan tan lejos.                | I doubt they live that far away.                 | -IR, *-a-* ending           |
| Espero que escribas pronto.              | I hope you write soon.                           | -IR, *-a-* ending           |

## Talking About What "We" Should Do

Practicing the *nosotros* form, including the -IR stem-change exception.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Espero que comamos juntos pronto.           | I hope we eat together soon.                 | -ER, *nosotros*, no change       |
| Quiero que pensemos bien antes de decidir.  | I want us to think carefully before deciding. | -AR stem-changer, no *nosotros* change |
| Ojalá que pidamos la cuenta ya.             | I hope we ask for the check already.           | -IR stem-changer, *nosotros* does change |
| Espero que durmamos bien esta noche.        | I hope we sleep well tonight.                  | -IR stem-changer, *nosotros* does change |

## Using Spelling-Change Verbs Naturally

Everyday sentences with -car, -gar, -zar verbs and their spelling patch.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que saques buenas notas.          | I hope you get good grades.                    | *sacar* → *saques*           |
| Quiero que juguemos otro partido.        | I want us to play another game.                 | *jugar* → *juguemos*         |
| Dudo que comiences hoy mismo.            | I doubt you'll start today.                     | *comenzar* → *comiences*      |
| Ojalá que lleguemos antes que ellos.     | I hope we arrive before them.                    | *llegar* → *lleguemos*        |

## Combining Endings in a Longer Sentence

Practicing multiple subjunctive verbs together, mixing -AR and -ER/-IR endings.

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que hables con ella y que ella te escuche.            | I hope you talk to her and that she listens to you.  | -AR + -ER endings          |
| Quiero que comamos temprano y que después caminemos un poco. | I want us to eat early and then walk a little after. | -ER + -AR endings          |
| Ojalá que estudiemos juntos y que aprendamos rápido.         | I hope we study together and learn quickly.          | -AR + -ER endings           |
| Dudo que vivan aquí y que trabajen tan lejos.                | I doubt they live here and work that far away.        | -IR + -AR endings           |
