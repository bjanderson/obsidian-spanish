# Irregular Subjunctive Stems DISHES — Conversational Sentences

## Using Dar in Everyday Requests

Common conversational uses of *dar* in the subjunctive.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que me des una oportunidad.       | I hope you give me a chance.                   | *dar*                       |
| Quiero que le des las gracias de mi parte. | I want you to thank her on my behalf.        | *dar*                       |
| Ojalá que nos den buenas noticias.       | I hope they give us good news.                  | *dar*                       |
| Dudo que te dé tiempo de terminar hoy.   | I doubt you'll have time to finish today.        | *dar*, idiomatic             |

## Using Ir to Talk About Going Somewhere

Common everyday sentences with *ir* in the subjunctive.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Quiero que vayas conmigo al doctor.      | I want you to go with me to the doctor.         | *ir*                        |
| Espero que vayamos juntos este año.      | I hope we go together this year.                 | *ir*, *nosotros*             |
| Prefiero que no vayan solos.             | I'd rather they not go alone.                     | *ir*, negation                |
| Ojalá que vaya todo bien en tu viaje.    | I hope everything goes well on your trip.          | *ir*, idiomatic                |

## Using Ser and Estar in the Subjunctive

Practicing both "to be" verbs side by side, keeping their uses distinct.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que sea una buena decisión.       | I hope it's a good decision.                   | *ser*, evaluation             |
| Quiero que estés tranquilo.              | I want you to be calm.                          | *estar*, state                |
| Dudo que sean tan diferentes.            | I doubt they're that different.                  | *ser*, comparison              |
| Ojalá que estemos listos a tiempo.       | I hope we're ready on time.                       | *estar*, *nosotros*            |

## Using Haber and Saber

Everyday phrases with these two frequently confused DISHES verbs.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que no haya tráfico hoy.          | I hope there's no traffic today.                | *haber*, impersonal            |
| Ojalá que haya suficiente comida.        | I hope there's enough food.                      | *haber*, impersonal            |
| Quiero que sepas cuánto te aprecio.      | I want you to know how much I appreciate you.      | *saber*                         |
| Dudo que sepan la verdad todavía.        | I doubt they know the truth yet.                  | *saber*, negation                |

## Mixing DISHES Verbs in Natural Conversation

Realistic sentences combining more than one DISHES verb.

| Spanish                                            | English                                       | Notes                |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que estés bien y que todo vaya según lo planeado.   | I hope you're well and everything goes as planned.  | *estar* + *ir*              |
| Ojalá que sepas que siempre puedes contar conmigo.         | I hope you know you can always count on me.          | *saber*                     |
| Quiero que seas feliz y que no haya más problemas.         | I want you to be happy and for there to be no more problems. | *ser* + *haber*        |
| Dudo que te dé pena pedir ayuda.                           | I doubt you'll feel embarrassed asking for help.       | *dar*, idiomatic              |
