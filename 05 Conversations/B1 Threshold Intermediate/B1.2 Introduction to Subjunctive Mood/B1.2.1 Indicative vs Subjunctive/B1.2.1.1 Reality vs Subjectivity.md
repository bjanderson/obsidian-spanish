# Reality vs Subjectivity — Conversational Sentences

## Stating Plain Facts (Indicative)

Everyday statements about things that are simply true.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Sé que ella vive en Bogotá.              | I know she lives in Bogotá.                  | indicative, certainty       |
| Es verdad que él tiene razón.            | It's true that he's right.                    | indicative, fact             |
| Estoy seguro de que va a llover.         | I'm sure it's going to rain.                   | indicative, certainty        |
| Todos saben que el examen es mañana.     | Everyone knows the exam is tomorrow.           | indicative, fact              |

## Expressing Hope or Wishes (Subjunctive)

Common everyday phrases that trigger the subjunctive through desire.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que todo salga bien.              | I hope everything turns out well.             | subjunctive, hope            |
| Ojalá que llueva pronto.                 | I hope it rains soon.                          | subjunctive, *ojalá*          |
| Quiero que vengas a la fiesta.           | I want you to come to the party.                | subjunctive, desire            |
| Deseo que tengas un buen viaje.          | I wish you a good trip.                          | subjunctive, desire            |

## Expressing Doubt (Subjunctive)

Everyday phrases that shift from certainty into doubt.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Dudo que llegue a tiempo.                | I doubt he'll arrive on time.                  | subjunctive, doubt           |
| No creo que sea buena idea.              | I don't think it's a good idea.                | subjunctive, doubt           |
| No es cierto que ella lo sepa.           | It's not certain that she knows it.             | subjunctive, doubt           |
| Puede que tengas razón.                  | You might be right.                             | subjunctive, possibility      |

## Using Antes de Que — Subject Changes

Practicing the *antes de que* + subjunctive trigger with a change in subject.

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Quiero terminar antes de que llegues.           | I want to finish before you arrive.           | *antes de que*, subject change   |
| Avísame antes de que te vayas.                  | Let me know before you leave.                  | *antes de que*, subject change   |
| Debemos salir antes de que empiece a llover.    | We should leave before it starts raining.       | *antes de que*, subject change   |
| Ella se fue antes de que yo pudiera despedirme. | She left before I could say goodbye.            | *antes de que*, subject change    |

## Using Antes de + Infinitive — Same Subject

Practicing the simpler structure when there's no subject change.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Siempre reviso mi correo antes de salir. | I always check my email before leaving.        | *antes de* + infinitive     |
| Quiero descansar antes de empezar.       | I want to rest before starting.                | *antes de* + infinitive     |
| Piénsalo bien antes de decidir.          | Think it over well before deciding.             | *antes de* + infinitive     |
| Nos lavamos las manos antes de comer.    | We wash our hands before eating.                | *antes de* + infinitive     |
