# Main Clause Que Subordinate Structure — Conversational Sentences

## Wanting Someone Else to Do Something

The classic *querer que* + subjunctive pattern in everyday requests.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Quiero que me ayudes con esto.           | I want you to help me with this.             | *querer que*, different subjects |
| Mi mamá quiere que yo estudie más.       | My mom wants me to study more.                | *querer que*                |
| Queremos que ustedes vengan a cenar.     | We want you all to come to dinner.             | *querer que*                |
| No quiero que te preocupes.              | I don't want you to worry.                     | *querer que*, negation       |

## Same Subject — Skipping Que Entirely

Contrasting sentences where the subject stays the same, so no subjunctive is needed.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Quiero ayudarte con esto.                | I want to help you with this.                  | same subject, infinitive     |
| Quiero estudiar más este semestre.       | I want to study more this semester.             | same subject, infinitive     |
| Queremos venir a cenar el sábado.        | We want to come to dinner Saturday.              | same subject, infinitive     |
| No quiero preocuparme por eso.           | I don't want to worry about that.                | same subject, infinitive     |

## Letting Someone Know When Something Happens

Using *cuando* + subjunctive for future, not-yet-happened events.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Avísame cuando termines.                 | Let me know when you finish.                   | *cuando* trigger             |
| Dime cuando estés listo.                 | Tell me when you're ready.                      | *cuando* trigger             |
| Llámame cuando tengas tiempo.            | Call me when you have time.                     | *cuando* trigger             |
| Escríbeme cuando llegues al aeropuerto.  | Text me when you arrive at the airport.          | *cuando* trigger             |

## Building Sentences With Different Subjects

Practicing the subject-swap in the subordinate clause.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Espero que puedas venir.                 | I hope you can come.                          | different subjects           |
| Espero que ellos entiendan la situación. | I hope they understand the situation.           | different subjects           |
| Mi jefe quiere que terminemos hoy.       | My boss wants us to finish today.             | different subjects           |
| Prefiero que ella decida.                | I'd rather she decide.                          | different subjects           |

## Catching the Structure in Casual Speech

Natural sentences where the main-clause-que-subordinate pattern shows up without you noticing.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ojalá que todo salga bien.               | I hope everything turns out well.              | *ojalá que*                  |
| Espero que no llueva mañana.             | I hope it doesn't rain tomorrow.                | *espero que*                 |
| Avísame cuando puedas.                   | Let me know whenever you can.                    | *cuando* trigger              |
| Quiero que sepas que te apoyo.           | I want you to know that I support you.          | *querer que*                  |
