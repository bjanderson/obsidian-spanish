# Completed Actions vs Background States — Conversational Sentences

## Narrating a Sequence of Events (Preterite Chain)

When one finished action follows another, both stay in the preterite.

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                      |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Me desperté, me duché y salí de casa.          | I woke up, showered, and left the house.    | three sequential events           |
| Llegamos, pedimos comida y comimos rápido.     | We arrived, ordered food, and ate quickly.  | sequential events                 |
| Ella abrió la puerta y vio a su amigo.         | She opened the door and saw her friend.     | sequential events, personal *a*   |
| Pagamos la cuenta y nos fuimos.                | We paid the bill and left.                  | sequential events                 |

## Setting a Scene Before an Event (Imperfect + Preterite)

Description sets up the background, then the preterite delivers the event.

| Spanish                                       | English                                  | Notes                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Hacía frío cuando salimos esa mañana.               | It was cold when we went out that morning.      | imperfect scene + preterite event |
| El restaurante estaba lleno, así que esperamos.     | The restaurant was full, so we waited.          | imperfect cause + preterite result |
| Todos dormían cuando sonó el teléfono.              | Everyone was sleeping when the phone rang.       | imperfect scene + preterite event |
| La casa estaba oscura, pero encontramos la luz.     | The house was dark, but we found the light.     | imperfect scene + preterite event |

## Describing a Habit That's Now Over

Repeated past actions, now finished, are natural imperfect territory.

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                     |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| De joven, viajaba mucho por trabajo.           | As a young person, I used to travel a lot for work. | habitual                    |
| Mi abuela cocinaba todos los domingos.         | My grandmother used to cook every Sunday.   | habitual                          |
| Antes salíamos todos los fines de semana.      | We used to go out every weekend before.     | habitual                          |
| Él siempre llegaba tarde a las reuniones.      | He always used to arrive late to meetings.  | habitual                          |

## Explaining a Reason with Background Information

Imperfect explains "why," preterite states "what happened."

| Spanish                                          | English                                  | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| No fuimos a la playa porque llovía mucho.              | We didn't go to the beach because it was raining a lot. | imperfect reason         |
| Se durmió temprano porque estaba muy cansado.          | He fell asleep early because he was very tired.  | imperfect reason                 |
| Cancelaron el vuelo porque había una tormenta.         | They canceled the flight because there was a storm. | imperfect reason              |
| Compré un abrigo porque hacía mucho frío.              | I bought a coat because it was very cold.        | imperfect reason                 |

## Describing a Finished Stretch of Time (Still Preterite)

A specific, completed duration takes the preterite, even if it lasted a long time.

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Vivimos en esa casa por cinco años.                   | We lived in that house for five years.      | one finished stretch              |
| Trabajé en esa empresa durante dos años.              | I worked at that company for two years.     | one finished stretch              |
| Estudiaron juntos toda la tarde.                      | They studied together all afternoon.        | one finished stretch              |
| Esperé más de una hora en el consultorio.             | I waited more than an hour at the doctor's office. | one finished stretch      |
