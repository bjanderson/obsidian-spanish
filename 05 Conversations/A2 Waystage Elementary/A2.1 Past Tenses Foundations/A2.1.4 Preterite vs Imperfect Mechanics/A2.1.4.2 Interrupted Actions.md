# Interrupted Actions — Conversational Sentences

## A Sudden Event Interrupting an Ongoing Action

The classic pattern: imperfect for the ongoing action, preterite for the sudden interruption.

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Estaba durmiendo cuando sonó el teléfono.             | I was sleeping when the phone rang.            | imperfect + *cuando* + preterite  |
| Estábamos cenando cuando se fue la luz.               | We were having dinner when the power went out. | imperfect + *cuando* + preterite  |
| Manejaba a casa cuando vi el accidente.               | I was driving home when I saw the accident.    | imperfect + *cuando* + preterite  |
| Ella se duchaba cuando alguien tocó la puerta.        | She was showering when someone knocked on the door. | imperfect + *cuando* + preterite |

## Starting with the Interruption Instead

The order can flip without changing which verb gets which tense.

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Cuando llegué a la fiesta, todos bailaban.            | When I arrived at the party, everyone was dancing. | preterite first, imperfect second |
| Cuando sonó la alarma, todavía dormíamos.             | When the alarm went off, we were still sleeping. | preterite first, imperfect second |
| Cuando empezó a llover, caminábamos por el parque.    | When it started raining, we were walking through the park. | preterite first, imperfect second |
| Cuando te llamé, ¿qué hacías?                         | When I called you, what were you doing?        | preterite first, imperfect second |

## Two Parallel Ongoing Actions (Mientras + Imperfect)

*Mientras* often pairs two things happening at once, both imperfect.

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Mientras cocinaba, escuchaba música.                  | While I was cooking, I was listening to music. | two parallel imperfects           |
| Mientras ella estudiaba, yo veía la tele.             | While she was studying, I was watching TV.     | two parallel imperfects           |
| Mientras esperábamos, hablábamos de trabajo.          | While we waited, we talked about work.         | two parallel imperfects           |
| Mientras manejaba, pensaba en el examen.              | While I was driving, I was thinking about the exam. | two parallel imperfects       |

## An Interruption During a Mientras Clause

When the second half of *mientras* is a one-time event, it switches to preterite.

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Mientras cocinaba, sonó el teléfono.                  | While I was cooking, the phone rang.           | imperfect + *mientras* + preterite |
| Mientras manejaba, su celular sonó.                   | While she was driving, her phone rang.         | imperfect + *mientras* + preterite |
| Mientras trabajábamos, llegó el jefe sin avisar.      | While we were working, the boss arrived without warning. | imperfect + *mientras* + preterite |
| Mientras dormía, alguien entró a la casa.             | While I was sleeping, someone entered the house. | imperfect + *mientras* + preterite |

## Everyday Interruptions People Actually Describe

Natural, common ways this pattern comes up in daily conversation.

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Estaba en la ducha cuando escuché el timbre.          | I was in the shower when I heard the doorbell. | imperfect + *cuando* + preterite  |
| Veíamos una película cuando se apagó la luz.          | We were watching a movie when the power went out. | imperfect + *cuando* + preterite |
| Trabajaba en el jardín cuando empezó a llover fuerte. | I was working in the garden when it started raining hard. | imperfect + *cuando* + preterite |
| Caminaba al trabajo cuando me llamaste.               | I was walking to work when you called me.      | imperfect + *cuando* + preterite  |
