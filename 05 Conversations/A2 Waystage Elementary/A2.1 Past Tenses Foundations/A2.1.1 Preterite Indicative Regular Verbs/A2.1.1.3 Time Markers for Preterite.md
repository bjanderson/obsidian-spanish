# Time Markers for Preterite — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Yesterday

*Ayer* is the single most common preterite trigger in everyday speech.

| Spanish                              | English                          | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ayer trabajé hasta muy tarde.             | Yesterday I worked until very late.    | *ayer* + preterite        |
| ¿Qué hiciste ayer por la tarde?           | What did you do yesterday afternoon?   | *ayer* + preterite        |
| Ayer mi hermana cumplió veinte años.      | Yesterday my sister turned twenty.     | *ayer* + preterite        |
| Ayer no comimos nada en todo el día.      | Yesterday we didn't eat anything all day. | *ayer* + preterite     |

## Talking About Last Night

*Anoche* comes up constantly in casual catch-up conversations.

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                 |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Anoche salimos a cenar con unos amigos.      | Last night we went out to dinner with friends. | *anoche* + preterite      |
| ¿Escuchaste ese ruido anoche?                | Did you hear that noise last night?         | *anoche* + preterite      |
| Anoche llovió muchísimo.                     | Last night it rained a lot.                 | *anoche* + preterite      |
| No dormí bien anoche por el calor.           | I didn't sleep well last night because of the heat. | *anoche* + preterite  |

## Talking About Last Week, Month, or Year

*Pasado*/*pasada* marks a longer stretch of closed time.

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| La semana pasada empecé un curso nuevo.              | Last week I started a new course.            | *la semana pasada*           |
| El mes pasado mis padres visitaron la ciudad.        | Last month my parents visited the city.      | *el mes pasado*              |
| El año pasado cambié de trabajo.                     | Last year I changed jobs.                    | *el año pasado*              |
| ¿Terminaste ese proyecto el fin de semana pasado?    | Did you finish that project last weekend?    | *el fin de semana pasado*    |

## Measuring Time Backward with Hace

*Hace* + a length of time answers "how long ago."

| Spanish                                     | English                              | Notes                  |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Hace una hora terminé la reunión.                  | An hour ago I finished the meeting.        | *hace* + time                |
| ¿Hace cuánto tiempo llegaste a la ciudad?          | How long ago did you arrive in the city?   | *hace cuánto tiempo*         |
| Hace dos semanas compramos un carro nuevo.         | Two weeks ago we bought a new car.         | *hace* + time                |
| Hace un rato me llamó mi mamá.                     | A little while ago my mom called me.       | *hace un rato*               |

## Marking a Sudden Event

*De repente* signals an abrupt shift, common in storytelling.

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                  |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| De repente se cortó la luz.                        | Suddenly the power went out.               | *de repente*                 |
| Estábamos caminando y de repente empezó a llover.  | We were walking and suddenly it started to rain. | *de repente* + preterite |
| De repente escuché un grito en la calle.           | Suddenly I heard a scream in the street.   | *de repente*                 |
| De repente todos se quedaron callados.             | Suddenly everyone went quiet.              | *de repente*                 |
