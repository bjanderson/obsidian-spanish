# ER IR Preterite Patterns — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About What You Ate or Drank

*Comer* and *beber* are two of the most common *-ER* verbs in daily talk.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Comí demasiado en la cena.          | I ate too much at dinner.            | *comer*, *yo*             |
| Bebí un café antes de salir.        | I drank a coffee before leaving.     | *beber*, *yo*             |
| ¿Comiste algo saludable hoy?        | Did you eat something healthy today? | *comer*, *tú*             |
| No bebimos nada de alcohol.         | We didn't drink any alcohol.         | *beber*, *nosotros*       |

## Describing a Move or Change of Residence

*Vivir* and *salir* come up constantly when telling people where you've been.

| Spanish                              | English                             | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Viví en Bogotá por tres años.            | I lived in Bogotá for three years.       | *vivir*, *yo*             |
| ¿Saliste temprano del trabajo ayer?      | Did you leave work early yesterday?      | *salir*, *tú*             |
| Mi hermano vivió con nosotros un tiempo. | My brother lived with us for a while.    | *vivir*, *él*             |
| Salimos de la ciudad el viernes pasado.  | We left the city last Friday.            | *salir*, *nosotros*       |

## Writing and Communication

*Escribir* and *recibir* — two *-IR* verbs that show up whenever people talk about messages, letters, or news.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes               |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Le escribí un mensaje a mi mamá.            | I wrote a message to my mom.              | *escribir*, *yo*         |
| ¿Recibiste mi correo electrónico?           | Did you receive my email?                 | *recibir*, *tú*          |
| Ella escribió su tesis en dos meses.        | She wrote her thesis in two months.       | *escribir*, *ella*       |
| Recibimos la invitación esta semana.        | We received the invitation this week.     | *recibir*, *nosotros*    |

## Talking About Learning and Understanding

*Aprender* and *entender* often pair with school, language learning, or figuring something out.

| Spanish                                    | English                                 | Notes                  |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Aprendí a cocinar viendo videos.                  | I learned to cook by watching videos.        | *aprender*, *yo*            |
| ¿Entendiste la explicación del profesor?          | Did you understand the teacher's explanation? | *entender*, *tú*            |
| Mis compañeros aprendieron rápido.                | My coworkers learned fast.                   | *aprender*, *ellos*         |
| Al final entendimos todo el problema.             | In the end we understood the whole problem.  | *entender*, *nosotros*      |

## Everyday Physical Actions in the Past

Common *-ER*/*-IR* action verbs describing what happened during the day.

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Corrí cinco kilómetros esta mañana.         | I ran five kilometers this morning.       | *correr*, *yo*            |
| ¿Abriste la ventana? Hace mucho calor.      | Did you open the window? It's very hot.   | *abrir*, *tú*             |
| El niño rompió su juguete favorito.         | The boy broke his favorite toy.           | *romper*, *él*            |
| Subimos al techo para ver la vista.         | We went up to the roof to see the view.   | *subir*, *nosotros*       |
