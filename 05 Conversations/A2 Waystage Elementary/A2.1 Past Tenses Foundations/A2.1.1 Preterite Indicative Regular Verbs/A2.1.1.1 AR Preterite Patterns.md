# AR Preterite Patterns — Conversational Sentences

## Recounting a Single Completed Action

The most basic use: saying you did one specific thing.

| Spanish                          | English                       | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Llamé a mi hermana esta mañana.    | I called my sister this morning. | *llamar*, *yo*              |
| Ayudé a mi vecino con las bolsas.  | I helped my neighbor with the bags. | personal *a* before *mi vecino* |
| Compré leche en el mercado.        | I bought milk at the market.     | *comprar*, *yo*             |
| Terminé la tarea antes de cenar.   | I finished the homework before dinner. | *terminar*, *yo*      |

## Asking Someone What They Did

Yes/no and open questions using the *tú* preterite.

| Spanish                             | English                          | Notes                 |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| ¿Hablaste con el profesor?             | Did you talk to the teacher?         | *hablar*, *tú*            |
| ¿A qué hora llegaste a casa?           | What time did you get home?          | *llegar*, *tú*            |
| ¿Bailaste mucho en la boda?            | Did you dance a lot at the wedding?  | *bailar*, *tú*            |
| ¿Ya pagaste la cuenta?                 | Did you already pay the bill?        | *pagar*, *tú*             |

## Talking About Someone Else's Completed Action

Third-person singular narration, common in stories and gossip.

| Spanish                                | English                            | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Mi jefa aprobó el proyecto ayer.           | My boss approved the project yesterday. | *aprobar*, *ella*        |
| El niño lloró toda la noche.               | The boy cried all night.               | *llorar*, *él*           |
| Ella visitó a sus abuelos el domingo.      | She visited her grandparents on Sunday. | personal *a* before *sus abuelos* |
| Mi amigo manejó desde Bogotá.              | My friend drove from Bogotá.           | *manejar*, *él*          |

## Describing What a Group Did Together

*Nosotros* forms — remember these look identical to the present tense.

| Spanish                                  | English                          | Notes                     |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Anoche cenamos en un restaurante nuevo.       | Last night we had dinner at a new restaurant. | *cenar*, *nosotros*, time marker clarifies past |
| Practicamos español por dos horas.            | We practiced Spanish for two hours.   | *practicar*, *nosotros*       |
| Organizamos la fiesta sorpresa juntos.        | We organized the surprise party together. | *organizar*, *nosotros*   |
| El sábado limpiamos toda la casa.             | On Saturday we cleaned the whole house. | *limpiar*, *nosotros*       |

## Talking About What Other People Did

Third-person plural, common when telling a story about friends or family.

| Spanish                               | English                          | Notes                  |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Mis padres celebraron su aniversario.      | My parents celebrated their anniversary. | *celebrar*, *ellos*      |
| Mis compañeros estudiaron toda la tarde.   | My coworkers studied all afternoon.  | *estudiar*, *ellos*      |
| Los turistas caminaron por el centro.      | The tourists walked through downtown. | *caminar*, *ellos*       |
| Ustedes ganaron el partido, ¿verdad?       | You (all) won the game, right?       | *ganar*, *ustedes*       |
