# Irregular Imperfect Verbs — Conversational Sentences

## Describing What Someone Used to Be (Ser)

*Era* is the go-to verb for describing identity, personality, or state in the past.

| Spanish                              | English                             | Notes               |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| De joven, era muy tímido.                | As a young person, I was very shy.     | *ser*, *yo*                |
| ¿Eras buen estudiante en la escuela?     | Were you a good student in school?     | *ser*, *tú*                |
| Mi abuelo era un hombre muy paciente.    | My grandfather was a very patient man. | *ser*, *él*                |
| Éramos buenos amigos en ese entonces.    | We were good friends back then.        | *ser*, *nosotros*          |

## Talking About Where You Used to Go (Ir)

*Iba* describes a repeated destination or habitual trip in the past.

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                 |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Iba al gimnasio todos los días.               | I used to go to the gym every day.         | *ir*, *yo*                   |
| ¿Ibas a esa escuela desde pequeño?            | Did you go to that school since you were little? | *ir*, *tú*             |
| Mis abuelos iban a misa los domingos.         | My grandparents used to go to mass on Sundays. | *ir*, *ellos*             |
| Íbamos de vacaciones a la playa cada año.     | We used to go on vacation to the beach every year. | *ir*, *nosotros*        |

## Talking About What You Used to Watch (Ver)

*Veía* is common for describing shows, movies, or people you used to see regularly.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                  |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Veía esa serie todas las noches.          | I used to watch that show every night. | *ver*, *yo*                  |
| ¿Veías dibujos animados de niño?          | Did you use to watch cartoons as a kid? | *ver*, *tú*                 |
| Ella veía a su familia cada fin de semana. | She used to see her family every weekend. | *ver*, *ella*, personal *a*  |
| Veíamos las noticias juntos antes de cenar. | We used to watch the news together before dinner. | *ver*, *nosotros*         |

## Combining All Three in a Description

Storytelling naturally mixes *era*, *iba*, and *veía* together.

| Spanish                                            | English                                     | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Era muy joven cuando iba a esa escuela.                  | I was very young when I used to go to that school. | *ser* + *ir*                    |
| Mi tía era doctora y veía muchos pacientes.              | My aunt was a doctor and used to see many patients. | *ser* + *ver*                   |
| De niños, íbamos al parque y veíamos a los otros niños.  | As kids, we used to go to the park and see the other kids. | *ir* + *ver*, personal *a*  |
| El barrio era tranquilo, y todos se conocían.            | The neighborhood was quiet, and everyone knew each other. | *ser* + *conocerse*         |
