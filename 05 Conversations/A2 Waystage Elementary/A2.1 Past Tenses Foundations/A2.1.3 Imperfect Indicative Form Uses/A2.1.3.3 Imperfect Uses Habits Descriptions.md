# Imperfect Uses Habits Descriptions — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Old Habits and Routines

The imperfect is the natural tense for "used to" statements about repeated past behavior.

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Antes fumaba, pero ya lo dejé.                 | I used to smoke, but I already quit.       | habit                           |
| ¿Comías carne antes de ser vegetariano?        | Did you use to eat meat before being vegetarian? | habit                     |
| Salíamos los viernes por la noche.             | We used to go out on Friday nights.        | habit                            |
| Mis padres siempre discutían por cosas pequeñas. | My parents always used to argue over small things. | habit                    |

## Describing People and Places

The imperfect sets the scene when describing what someone or something was like.

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Mi primer jefe era muy exigente.               | My first boss was very demanding.          | description                     |
| ¿Cómo era tu casa cuando eras niño?            | What was your house like when you were a kid? | description                 |
| El pueblo era pequeño pero muy bonito.         | The town was small but very pretty.        | description                     |
| Estábamos muy nerviosos antes del examen.      | We were very nervous before the exam.      | description (emotional state)   |

## Giving Someone's Age in the Past

Spanish always uses the imperfect of *tener* for past ages.

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Tenía doce años cuando me mudé a esta ciudad.  | I was twelve when I moved to this city.    | age                              |
| ¿Cuántos años tenías cuando te casaste?        | How old were you when you got married?     | age                              |
| Mi abuelo tenía ochenta años cuando murió.     | My grandfather was eighty when he died.    | age                              |
| Todavía tenía diez años en esa foto.           | I was still ten years old in that photo.   | age                              |

## Telling Time in the Past

Time in the past always takes the imperfect of *ser*, even for a specific moment.

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Eran las siete cuando sonó el despertador.     | It was seven when the alarm went off.      | time                             |
| Era medianoche cuando terminamos la película.  | It was midnight when we finished the movie. | time                            |
| ¿Qué hora era cuando llegaste a casa?          | What time was it when you got home?        | time                             |
| Eran casi las nueve cuando empezó la reunión.  | It was almost nine when the meeting started. | time                           |

## Mixing Uses in One Story

A short narration often blends habit, description, age, and time together.

| Spanish                                              | English                                     | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Tenía seis años y siempre jugaba en ese parque.             | I was six and always used to play in that park.    | age + habit                     |
| Era invierno, hacía frío, y la calle estaba vacía.          | It was winter, it was cold, and the street was empty. | description chain           |
| Eran las cinco y el cielo ya estaba oscuro.                 | It was five and the sky was already dark.           | time + description             |
| Vivíamos en un pueblo pequeño donde todos se conocían.      | We used to live in a small town where everyone knew each other. | habit + description   |
