# Regular aba and ia Endings — Conversational Sentences

## Describing Childhood Habits

The imperfect is the default tense for talking about what you used to do as a kid.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                  |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| De niño, jugaba fútbol todos los días.    | As a kid, I used to play soccer every day. | *jugar*, *yo*             |
| ¿Hablabas otro idioma en casa?            | Did you use to speak another language at home? | *hablar*, *tú*        |
| Mi abuela cocinaba para toda la familia.  | My grandma used to cook for the whole family. | *cocinar*, *ella*      |
| Caminábamos a la escuela.                 | We used to walk to school.             | *caminar*, *nosotros*        |

## Describing Repeated Past Routines

The imperfect naturally describes a habit or routine without a clear endpoint.

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                   |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Trabajaba de noche antes de este empleo.       | I used to work nights before this job.     | *trabajar*, *yo*                |
| ¿Comías en ese restaurante seguido?            | Did you use to eat at that restaurant often? | *comer*, *tú*                 |
| Mis vecinos siempre escuchaban música alta.    | My neighbors always used to play loud music. | *escuchar*, *ellos*           |
| Vivíamos en un apartamento pequeño.            | We used to live in a small apartment.      | *vivir*, *nosotros*             |

## Describing What Was Happening at a Moment

The imperfect can also work like "was/were -ing," setting a background scene.

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                  |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Yo estudiaba cuando llamaste.                 | I was studying when you called.            | *estudiar*, *yo*                |
| Llovía mucho esa tarde.                       | It was raining a lot that afternoon.       | *llover*, weather use            |
| Ella leía un libro en el parque.              | She was reading a book at the park.        | *leer*, *ella*                   |
| Los niños jugaban afuera cuando empezó.       | The kids were playing outside when it started. | *jugar*, *ellos*             |

## Talking About Past Feelings or States

The imperfect describes how someone felt or what something was like over a stretch of time.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                  |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Yo admiraba mucho a mi maestra.           | I really admired my teacher.           | *admirar*, *yo*              |
| ¿Extrañabas tu país al principio?         | Did you miss your country at first?    | *extrañar*, *tú*             |
| Él siempre parecía muy tranquilo.         | He always seemed very calm.            | *parecer*, *él*              |
| Creíamos en esa idea.                     | We used to believe in that idea.       | *creer*, *nosotros*          |

## Describing People and Places in the Past

Common in storytelling, when setting a scene from memory.

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Mi abuelo era alto y hablaba despacio.         | My grandfather was tall and used to speak slowly. | *hablar*, *él*             |
| La casa donde vivíamos tenía un jardín grande. | The house where we used to live had a big garden. | *vivir*, *nosotros*        |
| El pueblo parecía muy tranquilo entonces.      | The town seemed very calm back then.       | *parecer*, impersonal-ish       |
| Mis padres se conocían desde la universidad.   | My parents used to know each other since college. | *conocerse*, *ellos*      |
