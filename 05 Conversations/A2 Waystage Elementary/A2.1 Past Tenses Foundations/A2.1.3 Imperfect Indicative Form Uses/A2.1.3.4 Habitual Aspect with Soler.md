# Habitual Aspect with Soler — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Current Habits (Present Soler)

*Soler* in the present is a compact, natural way to say what someone usually does.

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Suelo levantarme temprano entre semana.          | I usually get up early on weekdays.    | present *soler*, *yo*            |
| ¿Sueles almorzar en la oficina?                  | Do you usually have lunch at the office? | present *soler*, *tú*          |
| Mi jefe suele llegar antes que todos.            | My boss usually arrives before everyone. | present *soler*, *él*          |
| Solemos ver una película los viernes.            | We usually watch a movie on Fridays.   | present *soler*, *nosotros*      |

## Talking About Past Routines (Imperfect Soler)

*Solía* is a natural alternative to the plain imperfect for describing a discontinued habit.

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Solía correr todas las mañanas.                  | I used to run every morning.           | imperfect *soler*, *yo*          |
| ¿Solías estudiar con música de fondo?            | Did you use to study with background music? | imperfect *soler*, *tú*     |
| Mi abuela solía contar historias increíbles.     | My grandmother used to tell amazing stories. | imperfect *soler*, *ella*   |
| Solíamos ir de camping cada verano.              | We used to go camping every summer.    | imperfect *soler*, *nosotros*    |

## Contrasting What Used to Happen with What Happens Now

*Soler* naturally sets up a "then vs. now" comparison.

| Spanish                                          | English                                  | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Antes solía fumar, pero ya no.                          | I used to smoke, but not anymore.               | imperfect *soler* + contrast      |
| Solía vivir en el centro, ahora vivo en las afueras.    | I used to live downtown; now I live in the suburbs. | imperfect *soler* + contrast  |
| Solíamos pelear mucho, pero ahora nos llevamos bien.    | We used to fight a lot, but now we get along well. | imperfect *soler* + contrast   |
| Ya no suele salir tanto como antes.                     | He/she doesn't usually go out as much as before. | present *soler*, negative        |

## Asking About Other People's Habits

Common when catching up or getting to know someone's routine.

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| ¿Qué sueles hacer los fines de semana?                | What do you usually do on weekends?           | present *soler*, *tú*             |
| ¿Solían pasar las vacaciones juntos?                   | Did you (all) use to spend vacations together? | imperfect *soler*, *ustedes*    |
| ¿Suele venir a esta cafetería?                         | Does he/she usually come to this café?        | present *soler*, formal          |
| ¿A qué hora solía terminar el trabajo antes?           | What time did he/she used to finish work before? | imperfect *soler*, formal      |
