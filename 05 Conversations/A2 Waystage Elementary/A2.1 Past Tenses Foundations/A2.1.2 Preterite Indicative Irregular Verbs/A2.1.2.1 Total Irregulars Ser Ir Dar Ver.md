# Total Irregulars Ser Ir Dar Ver — Conversational Sentences

## Saying Where You Went

*Ir* + *a* + destination is one of the most common past-tense patterns in daily talk.

| Spanish                              | English                             | Notes               |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Fui al gimnasio antes del trabajo.    | I went to the gym before work.        | *ir*, *yo*, *al* + *del* contractions |
| ¿Fuiste a la fiesta de Carla?         | Did you go to Carla's party?          | *ir*, *tú*                |
| Fuimos al cine el sábado.             | We went to the movies on Saturday.    | *ir*, *nosotros*, *al*    |
| Mis amigos fueron a la playa sin mí.  | My friends went to the beach without me. | *ir*, *ellos*           |

## Describing What Someone Was

*Ser* in the preterite describes a role, identity, or state that's now over.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Fui estudiante en esa universidad.       | I was a student at that university.      | *ser*, *yo*               |
| Ella fue presidenta de la empresa.       | She was president of the company.        | *ser*, *ella*             |
| ¿Fuiste tú quien llamó anoche?           | Were you the one who called last night?  | *ser*, *tú*               |
| Fuimos vecinos por muchos años.          | We were neighbors for many years.        | *ser*, *nosotros*         |

## Giving Something to Someone

*Dar* almost always shows up with an indirect object pronoun.

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| Le di las gracias a mi profesor.             | I gave thanks to my teacher.               | *dar*, *yo*, personal *a*      |
| ¿Le diste el mensaje a tu jefe?              | Did you give the message to your boss?     | *dar*, *tú*, personal *a*      |
| Nos dieron una sorpresa muy linda.           | They gave us a very nice surprise.         | *dar*, *ellos*                 |
| Le dimos un regalo a la abuela.              | We gave a gift to grandma.                 | *dar*, *nosotros*, personal *a*|

## Talking About What You Saw

*Ver* is common when describing entertainment, events, or chance encounters.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                 |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Vi a mi ex en el supermercado.            | I saw my ex at the supermarket.            | *ver*, *yo*, personal *a*    |
| ¿Viste las noticias esta mañana?          | Did you see the news this morning?         | *ver*, *tú*                  |
| Ella vio un accidente en la calle.        | She saw an accident on the street.         | *ver*, *ella*                |
| Vimos a nuestros primos en la boda.       | We saw our cousins at the wedding.         | *ver*, *nosotros*, personal *a* |

## Telling a Quick Story with Both Verbs

*Ir* and *ver* often appear together when narrating a simple sequence of events.

| Spanish                                        | English                                  | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Fui al centro y vi a un viejo amigo.            | I went downtown and saw an old friend.      | *ir* + *ver*, personal *a*      |
| Fuimos al parque y vimos un concierto gratis.   | We went to the park and saw a free concert. | *ir* + *ver*, *nosotros*        |
| ¿Fuiste al médico? ¿Qué te dijo?                | Did you go to the doctor? What did he tell you? | *ir*, follow-up question    |
| Fueron a la tienda y les dieron un descuento.   | They went to the store and got a discount.  | *ir* + *dar* (passive-ish use) |
