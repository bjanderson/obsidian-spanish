# Orthographic Preterite Changes — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Paying for Something (Pagar)

*Pagué* comes up constantly in everyday transactions.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Pagué la renta ayer.                      | I paid the rent yesterday.                 | *pagar*, *yo*              |
| Ya pagué la cuenta del restaurante.       | I already paid the restaurant bill.        | *pagar*, *yo*              |
| Pagué demasiado por este boleto.          | I paid too much for this ticket.           | *pagar*, *yo*              |
| Le pagué al plomero en efectivo.          | I paid the plumber in cash.                | *pagar*, *yo*, personal *a* |

## Talking About Looking for Something (Buscar)

*Busqué* is common when describing a search, whether for an object, a job, or a person.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Busqué mi teléfono por todos lados.       | I looked for my phone everywhere.          | *buscar*, *yo*              |
| Busqué trabajo durante meses.             | I looked for work for months.              | *buscar*, *yo*              |
| Te busqué en la fiesta pero no te vi.     | I looked for you at the party but didn't see you. | *buscar*, *yo*        |
| Busqué información antes de decidir.      | I looked for information before deciding.  | *buscar*, *yo*              |

## Talking About Starting Something (Empezar)

*Empecé* signals the beginning of an activity or habit.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Empecé un nuevo trabajo este mes.         | I started a new job this month.            | *empezar*, *yo*             |
| Empecé a correr todos los días.           | I started running every day.               | *empezar*, *yo*             |
| Empecé la dieta el lunes pasado.          | I started the diet last Monday.            | *empezar*, *yo*             |
| Empecé tarde porque perdí el autobús.     | I started late because I missed the bus.   | *empezar*, *yo*             |

## Talking About Arriving Somewhere (Llegar)

*Llegué* is one of the most frequent -gar preterite forms in casual speech.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Llegué tarde a la reunión.                | I arrived late to the meeting.             | *llegar*, *yo*              |
| Llegué justo a tiempo para el vuelo.      | I arrived just in time for the flight.     | *llegar*, *yo*              |
| Llegué a casa muy cansado.                | I arrived home very tired.                 | *llegar*, *yo*              |
| Llegué antes que todos los demás.         | I arrived before everyone else.            | *llegar*, *yo*              |

## Talking About Everyday Actions with Other -car/-gar/-zar Verbs

More common verbs from this group that show up constantly in casual talk.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Toqué el timbre pero nadie abrió.         | I rang the doorbell but nobody answered.   | *tocar*, *yo*               |
| Jugué videojuegos toda la tarde.          | I played video games all afternoon.        | *jugar*, *yo*               |
| Crucé la frontera sin problema.           | I crossed the border without a problem.    | *cruzar*, *yo*              |
| Saqué buenas notas este semestre.         | I got good grades this semester.           | *sacar*, *yo*               |
