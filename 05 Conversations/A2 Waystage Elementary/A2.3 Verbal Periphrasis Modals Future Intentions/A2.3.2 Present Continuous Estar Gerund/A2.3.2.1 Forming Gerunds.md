# Forming the Gerund (-ando, -iendo) — Conversational Sentences

## Describing What You're Doing Right Now

First-person present progressive, common when someone asks what you're up to.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Estoy trabajando desde casa hoy. | I'm working from home today. | *-ar* verb, *yo* form |
| Estoy comiendo, te llamo en un rato. | I'm eating, I'll call you in a bit. | casual phone excuse |
| Estoy escribiendo un mensaje. | I'm writing a message. | *-ir* verb, *yo* form |
| No puedo hablar, estoy manejando. | I can't talk, I'm driving. | common excuse phrase |

## Asking What Someone Is Doing

Questions directed at a friend or family member.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Qué estás haciendo? | What are you doing? | most common progressive question |
| ¿Estás escuchando música? | Are you listening to music? | yes/no question |
| ¿Con quién estás hablando? | Who are you talking to? | question word + progressive |
| ¿Todavía estás durmiendo? | Are you still sleeping? | *todavía* + progressive |

## Talking About Someone Else in the Moment

Third-person singular and plural, describing an ongoing action happening now.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Mi hermana está estudiando para un examen. | My sister is studying for an exam. | *ella* form |
| Los niños están jugando en el parque. | The kids are playing in the park. | *ellos* form |
| Mi jefe está esperando tu respuesta. | My boss is waiting for your answer. | *él* form |
| Están limpiando la oficina ahora mismo. | They're cleaning the office right now. | *ahora mismo* for emphasis |

## Group Actions Happening Now

*Nosotros* form, describing shared ongoing actions.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Estamos viendo una película. | We're watching a movie. | *-er* verb, *nosotros* form |
| Estamos tratando de resolver el problema. | We're trying to solve the problem. | *estar + tratando de + infinitive* |
| ¿Estamos perdiendo el tiempo? | Are we wasting time? | rhetorical/casual question |
| Estamos esperando el autobús. | We're waiting for the bus. | *-ar* verb, *nosotros* form |
