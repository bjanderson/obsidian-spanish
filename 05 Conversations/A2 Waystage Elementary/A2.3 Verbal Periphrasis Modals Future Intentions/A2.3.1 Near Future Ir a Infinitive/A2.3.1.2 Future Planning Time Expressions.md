# Time Expressions for Future Planning — Conversational Sentences

## Making Plans for Today or Tonight

Same-day plans, common in casual daily conversation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Esta noche voy a quedarme en casa. | Tonight I'm going to stay home. | time expression at the start |
| ¿Vas a comer algo más tarde? | Are you going to eat something later? | *más tarde* at the end |
| Voy a terminar esto en un rato. | I'm going to finish this in a little while. | near-immediate future |
| Hoy vamos a tener una reunión. | Today we're going to have a meeting. | *nosotros* form |

## Making Plans for Tomorrow

Next-day plans, one of the most common near-future contexts.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Mañana voy a ir al médico. | Tomorrow I'm going to go to the doctor. | time expression first |
| ¿Qué vas a hacer mañana? | What are you going to do tomorrow? | open-ended question |
| Vamos a salir temprano mañana. | We're going to leave early tomorrow. | *nosotros* form |
| Pasado mañana va a llegar mi tía. | The day after tomorrow my aunt is going to arrive. | less common but useful marker |

## Making Plans for This Week or Weekend

Plans within the current week, often for social or errand-type activities.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Este fin de semana vamos a ir a la playa. | This weekend we're going to go to the beach. | time expression at the start |
| ¿Vas a trabajar esta semana? | Are you going to work this week? | yes/no question |
| Voy a limpiar mi cuarto este fin de semana. | I'm going to clean my room this weekend. | time expression at the end |
| Vamos a vernos esta semana, ¿verdad? | We're going to see each other this week, right? | tag question, casual confirmation |

## Making Plans Further Out

Next month or beyond — plans that are decided but not immediate.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| El próximo mes voy a cambiar de casa. | Next month I'm going to move to a new place. | time expression first |
| Mis padres van a viajar la próxima semana. | My parents are going to travel next week. | *ellos* form |
| ¿Van a abrir la nueva tienda el próximo mes? | Are they going to open the new store next month? | impersonal *they* |
| Vamos a celebrar tu cumpleaños la próxima semana. | We're going to celebrate your birthday next week. | *nosotros* form |
