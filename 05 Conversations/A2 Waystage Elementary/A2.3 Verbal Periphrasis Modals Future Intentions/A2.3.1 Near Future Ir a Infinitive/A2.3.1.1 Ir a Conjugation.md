# Conjugating Ir + a + Base Verb — Conversational Sentences

## Stating Personal Plans

Talking about what you (or someone) plan to do, in first or second person.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Voy a dormir temprano hoy. | I'm going to sleep early today. | *yo* form, dropped subject pronoun |
| ¿Vas a salir esta noche? | Are you going to go out tonight? | casual question to a friend |
| Voy a llamarte más tarde. | I'm going to call you later. | attached object pronoun *-te* |
| ¿Vas a venir a la fiesta? | Are you going to come to the party? | invitation-style question |

## Talking About Someone Else's Plans

Third-person singular, common in gossip or making plans on someone's behalf.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Mi jefe va a llegar tarde. | My boss is going to arrive late. | *él* form |
| Ana va a cambiar de trabajo. | Ana is going to change jobs. | named subject |
| Creo que va a llover. | I think it's going to rain. | impersonal *it*, weather use |
| Él va a explicarnos todo mañana. | He's going to explain everything to us tomorrow. | attached object pronoun *-nos* |

## Group Plans with Nosotros

Making plans that include the speaker and others.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Vamos a cenar juntos el viernes. | We're going to have dinner together on Friday. | casual group plan |
| Vamos a ver, todavía no sé. | We'll see, I still don't know. | fixed conversational phrase |
| ¿Vamos a pedir pizza esta noche? | Are we going to order pizza tonight? | suggestion phrased as question |
| No vamos a llegar a tiempo. | We're not going to arrive on time. | negation before *ir* |

## Asking About Others' Plans

Questions directed at a group or a formal *usted*.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Qué van a hacer ustedes el fin de semana? | What are you all going to do this weekend? | *ustedes* form |
| ¿Va a necesitar algo más, señor? | Are you going to need anything else, sir? | formal *usted*, service context |
| ¿Cuándo van a mudarse tus papás? | When are your parents going to move? | *ellos* with question word |
| ¿Quién va a pagar la cuenta? | Who is going to pay the bill? | subject is the question word |
