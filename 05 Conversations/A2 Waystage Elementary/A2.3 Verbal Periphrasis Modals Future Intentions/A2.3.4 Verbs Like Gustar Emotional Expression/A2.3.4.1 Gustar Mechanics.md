# Indirect Pronoun + Gustar Mechanics — Conversational Sentences

## Asking About Preferences

Common small-talk questions using *gustar*.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Te gusta la comida picante? | Do you like spicy food? | singular noun, casual question |
| ¿Qué tipo de música te gusta? | What kind of music do you like? | open-ended preference question |
| ¿Les gusta este restaurante? | Do you all like this restaurant? | *les* form, group question |
| ¿Le gusta el café o el té? | Do you like coffee or tea? | formal *usted* question |

## Sharing Your Own Preferences

First-person statements about likes and dislikes.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Me gusta mucho esta canción. | I really like this song. | intensifier *mucho* |
| No me gustan los lugares ruidosos. | I don't like noisy places. | plural noun, negation |
| Me gusta salir los fines de semana. | I like going out on weekends. | infinitive, always singular *gusta* |
| A mí me gusta más el chocolate que la vainilla. | I like chocolate more than vanilla. | emphatic *a mí* + comparison |

## Talking About Other People's Preferences

Third-person statements, common when describing family or friends.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| A mi hermano le gusta jugar videojuegos. | My brother likes playing video games. | clarifying phrase + *le* |
| A mis amigos les gustan las películas de terror. | My friends like horror movies. | clarifying phrase + *les*, plural |
| A ella no le gusta madrugar. | She doesn't like waking up early. | negation before *le* |
| A mis papás les gusta cocinar juntos. | My parents like cooking together. | infinitive, singular *gusta* |

## Confirming or Reacting to Preferences

Short responses and reactions in everyday conversation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| A mí también me gusta eso. | I like that too. | agreement phrase |
| A mí no me gusta para nada. | I don't like it at all. | strong disagreement |
| ¿En serio te gusta ese programa? | Do you seriously like that show? | surprised reaction |
| Sí, me gusta mucho. | Yes, I like it a lot. | short affirmative answer |
