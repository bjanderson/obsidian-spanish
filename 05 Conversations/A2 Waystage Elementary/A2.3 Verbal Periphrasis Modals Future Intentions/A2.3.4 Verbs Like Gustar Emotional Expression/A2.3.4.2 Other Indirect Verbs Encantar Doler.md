# Other Indirect Verbs (encantar, doler, importar, faltar) — Conversational Sentences

## Expressing Strong Enthusiasm with Encantar

Everyday ways to say you love something.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Me encanta este restaurante. | I love this restaurant. | singular, *yo* |
| ¿Te encantan las series de misterio? | Do you love mystery shows? | plural, question |
| A mi hermana le encanta cocinar. | My sister loves cooking. | infinitive, singular *encanta* |
| Nos encanta pasar tiempo juntos. | We love spending time together. | infinitive, *nosotros* |

## Talking About Pain with Doler

Common ways to describe physical discomfort.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Me duele mucho la cabeza hoy. | My head really hurts today. | singular, intensifier *mucho* |
| ¿Te duele algo? | Does something hurt? | general check-in question |
| Le duelen las rodillas cuando corre. | His/her knees hurt when he/she runs. | plural, *le* |
| Nos duelen los pies de tanto caminar. | Our feet hurt from walking so much. | plural, *nosotros* |

## Saying What Matters with Importar

Everyday expressions about caring or not caring.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| No me importa lo que piensen los demás. | I don't care what other people think. | negation, singular |
| Nos importa mucho tu opinión. | Your opinion matters a lot to us. | affirmative, *nosotros* |
| ¿Te importa si abro la ventana? | Do you mind if I open the window? | idiomatic permission-asking |
| A él no le importan las apariencias. | Appearances don't matter to him. | plural, negation |

## Talking About What's Missing with Faltar

Common ways to say something is missing or still needed.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Me falta un poco de dinero para el boleto. | I'm a little short on money for the ticket. | singular |
| Nos faltan dos personas para el equipo. | We're missing two people for the team. | plural |
| ¿Te falta algo? | Are you missing anything? | check-in question |
| Todavía le falta terminar el informe. | He/she still needs to finish the report. | *faltar* + infinitive |
