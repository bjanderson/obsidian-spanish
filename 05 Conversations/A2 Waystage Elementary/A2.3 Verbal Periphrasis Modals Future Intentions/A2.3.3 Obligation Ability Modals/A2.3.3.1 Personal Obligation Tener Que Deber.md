# Personal Obligation (Tener que, Deber) — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Work and Schedule Obligations

*Tener que*, common when explaining a real commitment.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Tengo que trabajar hasta tarde hoy. | I have to work late today. | *yo* form |
| ¿Tienes que ir a la oficina mañana? | Do you have to go to the office tomorrow? | *tú* form question |
| Tenemos que terminar esto antes del viernes. | We have to finish this before Friday. | *nosotros* form |
| Mi jefe tiene que aprobar el proyecto primero. | My boss has to approve the project first. | *él* form |

## Making Excuses

*Tener que* used as a polite excuse to end a conversation or decline an invitation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Lo siento, tengo que irme. | Sorry, I have to go. | common excuse phrase |
| No puedo, tengo que cuidar a mi hermanito. | I can't, I have to take care of my little brother. | personal *a* before *mi hermanito* |
| Tengo que colgar, te llamo luego. | I have to hang up, I'll call you later. | phone-call excuse |
| Tenemos que salir ya, se nos hace tarde. | We have to leave now, we're running late. | idiomatic urgency phrase |

## Giving Advice with Deber

*Deber*, common when suggesting what someone should do.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Deberías hablar con un médico. | You should talk to a doctor. | conditional-flavored advice |
| Debemos pensarlo con calma. | We should think it over calmly. | *nosotros* form |
| Creo que debes disculparte con ella. | I think you should apologize to her. | opinion + advice |
| No deben preocuparse tanto. | They shouldn't worry so much. | negative advice |

## Contrasting Obligation and Advice

Sentences that show the difference between *tener que* (must) and *deber* (should).

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Tengo que ir al banco, pero debería ir al gimnasio también. | I have to go to the bank, but I should go to the gym too. | contrasts both forms |
| No tienes que venir, pero deberías. | You don't have to come, but you should. | shows the weight difference |
| Tenemos que pagar la renta, y también deberíamos ahorrar más. | We have to pay rent, and we should also save more. | both forms in one sentence |
| Ella tiene que estudiar, pero debería descansar un poco primero. | She has to study, but she should rest a little first. | strong obligation vs. soft advice |
