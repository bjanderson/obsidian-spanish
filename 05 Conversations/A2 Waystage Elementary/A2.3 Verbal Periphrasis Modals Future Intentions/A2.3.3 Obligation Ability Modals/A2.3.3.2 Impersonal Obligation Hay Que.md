# Impersonal Obligation (Hay que) — Conversational Sentences

## Giving General Advice

*Hay que* used to state advice that applies to anyone, not a specific person.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Hay que tomar mucha agua todos los días. | You have to drink a lot of water every day. | general health advice |
| Para aprender un idioma, hay que practicar constantemente. | To learn a language, you have to practice constantly. | general learning advice |
| Hay que dormir bien para rendir en el trabajo. | You need to sleep well to perform at work. | general life advice |
| Hay que comer más verduras. | You should eat more vegetables. | general recommendation |

## Stating Rules or Norms

*Hay que* used for shared rules or expectations in a place or situation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Aquí hay que quitarse los zapatos. | Here you have to take off your shoes. | house rule |
| Para entrar, hay que mostrar una identificación. | To get in, you have to show an ID. | venue rule |
| En esta empresa hay que llegar puntual. | At this company you have to arrive on time. | workplace norm |
| Hay que pedir permiso antes de salir. | You have to ask permission before leaving. | general expectation |

## Reacting to a Situation

*Hay que* used in reaction to something that just happened or needs addressing.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Hay que arreglar esto lo antes posible. | This needs to be fixed as soon as possible. | urgency |
| Hay que avisarle a todo el mundo. | Everyone needs to be told. | general instruction |
| No hay que exagerar, no es tan grave. | There's no need to exaggerate, it's not that serious. | calming remark |
| Hay que tener paciencia con estas cosas. | You have to be patient with these things. | reassurance |

## Contrasting Hay Que with Tener Que

Sentences that show the difference between a general rule and a personal obligation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Hay que estudiar mucho, pero yo también tengo que trabajar. | You have to study a lot, but I also have to work. | general vs. personal |
| No hay que gritar, pero tú tienes que calmarte. | There's no need to yell, but you have to calm down. | general vs. specific |
| Hay que respetar las reglas, y todos tenemos que seguirlas. | The rules have to be respected, and we all have to follow them. | both forms together |
| Hay que pensarlo bien antes de decidir algo tan importante. | You have to think it through well before deciding something so important. | general caution |
