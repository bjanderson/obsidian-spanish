# Future for Speculation and Probability — Conversational Sentences

## Reacting to an Unexpected Knock or Call

The most classic use of this pattern — wondering who's showing up unannounced.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Quién será?                         | Who could that be?                     | *ser*, someone at the door |
| ¿Quién llamará a esta hora?          | Who could be calling at this hour?     | *llamar*, phone rings     |
| Será el cartero.                     | It's probably the mail carrier.        | *ser*, answering own guess |
| No sé quién será, pero voy a ver.    | I don't know who it could be, but I'll go check. | *ser*, uncertainty  |

## Guessing Where Someone Is

Speculating about a person's location when you can't reach them.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Estará en el trabajo todavía.        | She's probably still at work.          | *estar*, location        |
| ¿Dónde estará mi hermano?            | Where could my brother be?             | *estar*, question        |
| Estarán en el tráfico, como siempre. | They're probably stuck in traffic, as usual. | *estar*, plural   |
| No contesta — estará ocupado.        | He's not answering — he's probably busy. | *estar*, explaining silence |

## Guessing Someone's Age or Identity

Everyday small talk where you're estimating rather than stating a fact.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Tendrá unos treinta años.                | He's probably around thirty.            | *tener*, age             |
| ¿Será la nueva vecina?                   | Could that be the new neighbor?         | *ser*, identity          |
| Será estudiante, por la mochila.         | She's probably a student, judging by the backpack. | *ser*, inference |
| Serán hermanos — se parecen mucho.       | They're probably siblings — they look a lot alike. | *ser*, inference   |

## Explaining Away an Unusual Situation

Using speculation to make sense of something odd or unexplained.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Se habrá olvidado de la cita.               | He probably forgot about the appointment.  | reflexive, *haber*       |
| No tendrá ganas de salir hoy.               | She probably doesn't feel like going out today. | *tener*, negation   |
| Se le habrá dañado el carro otra vez.       | Her car probably broke down again.         | *dañar*, indirect object  |
| Estará resfriada, por eso no vino.          | She's probably got a cold, that's why she didn't come. | *estar*, reasoning |

## Doubting or Questioning a Claim

Using the future tense to express skepticism about what someone said.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Será verdad lo que cuenta?              | Could what he's saying be true?         | *ser*, doubt             |
| No lo creo, pero será cierto.            | I don't believe it, but it's probably true. | *ser*, conceding      |
| ¿Sabrá lo que está haciendo?             | Does he even know what he's doing?      | *saber*, rhetorical      |
| Querrá decir otra cosa.                  | He probably means something else.       | *querer*, clarifying      |
