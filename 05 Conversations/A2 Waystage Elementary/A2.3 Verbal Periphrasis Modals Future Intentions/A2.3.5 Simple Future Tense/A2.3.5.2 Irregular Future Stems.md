# Irregular Future Stems — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Obligation With Tener Que

Using *tendré/tendrás/tendrá que* + infinitive to say what someone will have to do.

| Spanish                              | English                           | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Tendré que estudiar más este semestre.   | I'll have to study more this semester.  | yo, *tener*             |
| Tendrás que llamarlo tú mismo.           | You'll have to call him yourself.       | tú, *tener*             |
| Tendrá que esperar unos minutos.         | You'll have to wait a few minutes.      | usted, *tener*, polite  |
| Tendremos que salir más temprano.        | We'll have to leave earlier.            | nosotros, *tener*       |

## Asking or Offering to Help

Common questions and offers using *poder* in the future.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| ¿Podrás ayudarme con esto?           | Will you be able to help me with this? | tú, *poder*          |
| No podré ir a la reunión mañana.     | I won't be able to go to the meeting tomorrow. | yo, *poder*, negation |
| Ella podrá terminar el proyecto sola. | She'll be able to finish the project alone. | ella, *poder*    |
| ¿Podremos reservar una mesa para dos? | Will we be able to book a table for two? | nosotros, *poder*    |

## Making Arrangements — Coming and Going

Everyday plans about who's coming, leaving, or arriving.

| Spanish                              | English                          | Notes             |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Vendrán a cenar el viernes.              | They'll come for dinner Friday.         | ellos, *venir*       |
| Saldremos del aeropuerto a las nueve.    | We'll leave the airport at nine.        | nosotros, *salir*    |
| ¿A qué hora vendrás mañana?              | What time will you come tomorrow?       | tú, *venir*          |
| Mi hermano pondrá las maletas en el carro. | My brother will put the bags in the car. | él, *poner*         |

## Speculating What Someone Will Say or Do

Using *decir* and *hacer* in the future for everyday remarks about plans or reactions.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| ¿Qué dirá tu mamá cuando lo sepa?    | What will your mom say when she finds out? | ella, *decir*     |
| Haré todo lo posible por llegar a tiempo. | I'll do everything possible to arrive on time. | yo, *hacer*   |
| ¿Qué harás si no te contesta?        | What will you do if he doesn't answer you? | tú, *hacer*       |
| Él dirá que no tiene tiempo, como siempre. | He'll say he doesn't have time, like always. | él, *decir*   |

## Uncertainty About Facts or Knowledge

Using *saber* and *querer* in the future when someone isn't sure yet.

| Spanish                              | English                          | Notes             |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| No sabré los resultados hasta el lunes.  | I won't know the results until Monday.  | yo, *saber*, negation |
| ¿Querrás algo de comer más tarde?        | Will you want something to eat later?   | tú, *querer*          |
| Ellos sabrán qué hacer llegado el momento. | They'll know what to do when the time comes. | ellos, *saber*   |
| Con el tiempo, querrás volver aquí.      | With time, you'll want to come back here. | tú, *querer*         |
