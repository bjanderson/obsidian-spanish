# Regular Future Endings (-é, -ás, -á, -emos, -án) — Conversational Sentences

## Making a Promise or Commitment

Using the future tense to promise you'll do something.

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Te llamaré esta noche.             | I'll call you tonight.             | yo, -ar verb           |
| Te escribiré mañana, lo prometo.   | I'll write to you tomorrow, I promise. | yo, -ir verb        |
| No te fallaré.                     | I won't let you down.              | yo, negation           |
| Terminaré el trabajo hoy mismo.    | I'll finish the work today.        | yo, -ar verb           |

## Asking What Someone Will Do

Everyday questions about someone's plans, without using *ir a*.

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| ¿A qué hora llegarás?              | What time will you arrive?         | tú, -ar verb            |
| ¿Comerás algo antes de salir?      | Will you eat something before leaving? | tú, -er verb        |
| ¿Dónde vivirán ustedes?            | Where will you (all) live?         | ustedes, -ir verb       |
| ¿Cuándo abrirá la tienda?          | When will the store open?          | usted/ella, -ir verb    |

## Predicting or Announcing Future Events

Statements about things expected to happen, common in news and everyday plans.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                 |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| El próximo año viviremos en otra ciudad.  | Next year we'll live in another city.    | nosotros, no accent      |
| Mis papás venderán la casa pronto.        | My parents will sell the house soon.     | ellos, -er verb          |
| La reunión empezará a las diez.           | The meeting will start at ten.           | ella (impersonal), -ar   |
| Mañana lloverá todo el día.               | Tomorrow it will rain all day.           | impersonal, -er verb     |

## Making Plans With a Group

Group commitments and shared future actions.

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes             |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Estudiaremos juntos para el examen.          | We'll study together for the exam.       | nosotros, -ar verb   |
| Compartiremos el costo del taxi.             | We'll split the cost of the taxi.        | nosotros, -ir verb   |
| Nos veremos el viernes, ¿verdad?             | We'll see each other Friday, right?      | nosotros, -er verb   |
| Ellos nos ayudarán con la mudanza.           | They'll help us with the move.           | ellos, -ar verb      |

## Reassuring Someone About the Future

Softening bad news or calming someone down with a future-tense promise.

| Spanish                              | English                             | Notes             |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Todo mejorará, ya verás.                | Everything will get better, you'll see.  | impersonal, -ar verb |
| No te preocupes, encontraremos una solución. | Don't worry, we'll find a solution.  | nosotros, -ar verb   |
| Ella entenderá con el tiempo.           | She'll understand with time.             | ella, -er verb       |
| Al final todo se resolverá.             | In the end everything will work out.     | impersonal, reflexive -er |
