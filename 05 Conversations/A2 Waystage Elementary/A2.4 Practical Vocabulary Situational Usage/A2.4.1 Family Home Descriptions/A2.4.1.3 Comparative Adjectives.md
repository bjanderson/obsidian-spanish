# Comparative Adjectives — Conversational Sentences

## Comparing People

Everyday comparisons between family members, friends, or coworkers.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Mi hermana es más alta que yo.           | My sister is taller than me.                | *más...que*                |
| Él es menos paciente que su hermano.     | He's less patient than his brother.          | *menos...que*              |
| Somos casi de la misma edad, ella es tan alta como yo. | We're almost the same age, she's as tall as me. | *tan...como*     |
| Mi jefe es mayor que mi papá.            | My boss is older than my dad.                | irregular, *mayor*          |

## Comparing Places and Things

Talking about size, price, quality when comparing objects or locations.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Este apartamento es más caro que el otro. | This apartment is more expensive than the other. | *más...que*         |
| La ciudad es menos ruidosa que antes.    | The city is less noisy than before.          | *menos...que*              |
| Este café es tan bueno como el de mi casa. | This coffee is as good as the one at my house. | *tan...como*         |
| Aquel restaurante es mejor que este.     | That restaurant is better than this one.     | irregular, *mejor*          |

## Comparing Abilities and Habits

Comparing how well or how much people do something.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Ella cocina mejor que yo.                | She cooks better than me.                   | irregular, *mejor*          |
| Trabajamos tanto como ustedes.           | We work as much as you all.                 | *tanto como*, verbs         |
| Él corre más rápido que su hermano.      | He runs faster than his brother.             | *más...que*                |
| Estudio menos que antes.                 | I study less than before.                    | *menos que*                |

## Reacting With a Comparison

Natural conversational reactions that use comparisons.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| ¡Qué casa tan bonita! Es más grande que la mía. | What a beautiful house! It's bigger than mine. | *más...que*    |
| No sabía que eras mayor que tu esposo.   | I didn't know you were older than your husband. | irregular, *mayor*     |
| Este plan es peor que el anterior.       | This plan is worse than the previous one.    | irregular, *peor*           |
| Tu español ya es tan bueno como el mío.  | Your Spanish is already as good as mine.     | *tan...como*                |

## Asking Someone to Compare

Questions that prompt a comparison in response.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| ¿Quién es mayor, tú o tu hermano?        | Who's older, you or your brother?            | question, *mayor*           |
| ¿Este trabajo es más difícil que el anterior? | Is this job harder than the last one?    | question, *más...que*      |
| ¿Es tan caro como dicen?                 | Is it as expensive as they say?              | question, *tan...como*      |
| ¿Cuál de los dos es mejor?               | Which of the two is better?                  | question, *mejor*           |
