# Extended Family Vocabulary — Conversational Sentences

## Introducing Extended Family

Common ways to introduce or mention extended family members in conversation.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Te presento a mi tía Rosa.               | This is my aunt Rosa.                   | *presentar a*, personal *a* |
| Mi abuelo vive con nosotros.             | My grandfather lives with us.           | *abuelo*                 |
| Este es mi primo, el hijo de mi tío.     | This is my cousin, my uncle's son.       | *primo*, *de*             |
| Mi cuñada es de Argentina.               | My sister-in-law is from Argentina.      | *cuñada*                  |

## Talking About Family Gatherings

Everyday sentences about family events and who's coming.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Van a venir mis suegros el sábado.          | My in-laws are coming over Saturday.       | *suegros*, plural         |
| Toda la familia se reúne en Navidad.        | The whole family gets together at Christmas. | *se reúne*             |
| Mis sobrinos siempre hacen ruido en las fiestas. | My nieces/nephews always make noise at parties. | *sobrinos*        |
| Invitamos a mis primos y a mis tíos.        | We invited my cousins and my aunts/uncles.  | personal *a*              |

## Describing Family Relationships

Explaining how people are related to each other.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ella es la hermana de mi mamá, o sea mi tía. | She's my mom's sister, so my aunt.        | *o sea*                   |
| Mi hermanastro es hijo del segundo esposo de mi mamá. | My stepbrother is my mom's second husband's son. | *hermanastro*  |
| Mis bisabuelos ya no viven, pero mi abuela habla mucho de ellos. | My great-grandparents are no longer alive, but my grandmother talks about them a lot. | *bisabuelos* |
| No tengo hermanos, solo primos.             | I don't have siblings, only cousins.        | negation                  |

## Asking About Someone's Family

Everyday questions to learn more about someone's relatives.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Tienes muchos primos?                   | Do you have a lot of cousins?           | *tener*, question         |
| ¿Vive tu abuela cerca de aquí?           | Does your grandmother live near here?    | *vivir*, question         |
| ¿Cómo se llama tu cuñado?                | What's your brother-in-law's name?       | *se llama*                |
| ¿Te llevas bien con tu madrastra?        | Do you get along with your stepmother?   | *llevarse bien*           |

## Talking About Growing Up With Step-Family

Situations that come up when discussing blended families.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Crecí con mi padrastro desde los seis años. | I grew up with my stepfather since I was six. | *padrastro*           |
| Mi hijastra vive con su papá los fines de semana. | My stepdaughter lives with her dad on weekends. | *hijastra*        |
| Mis medios hermanos son mucho más chicos que yo. | My half-siblings are much younger than me. | *medios hermanos*     |
| Nos llevamos como hermanos, aunque somos primos. | We're as close as siblings, even though we're cousins. | *como hermanos*   |
