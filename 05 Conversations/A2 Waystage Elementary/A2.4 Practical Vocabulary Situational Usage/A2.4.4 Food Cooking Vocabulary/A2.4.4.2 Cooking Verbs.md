# Common Cooking Verbs — Conversational Sentences

## Talking about what you're about to cook

Using *ir a* + infinitive to describe the next step in a recipe or meal.

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                  |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Voy a hervir el agua para el arroz.          | I'm going to boil the water for the rice.    | *hervir*, *ir a*              |
| Vamos a freír las papas.                     | We're going to fry the potatoes.             | *freír*, *nosotros*           |
| ¿Vas a hornear un pastel?                    | Are you going to bake a cake?                | *hornear*, question            |
| Voy a picar el ajo primero.                  | I'm going to chop the garlic first.          | *picar*                       |
| Van a asar la carne en el patio.             | They're going to grill the meat on the patio. | *asar*, *ellos*               |

## Giving instructions with informal commands

Telling someone what to do in the kitchen using *tú* commands.

| Spanish                              | English                        | Notes                 |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Pica la cebolla bien fina.              | Chop the onion very fine.            | informal command, *picar*     |
| Añade un poco de sal.                   | Add a little salt.                   | informal command, *añadir*    |
| No frías el pescado todavía.            | Don't fry the fish yet.               | negative command               |
| Revuelve la sopa antes de servirla.     | Stir the soup before serving it.      | informal command, *revolver*  |
| Calienta el aceite a fuego medio.       | Heat the oil on medium heat.          | informal command, *calentar*  |

## Describing a recipe step with the impersonal *se*

The normal way recipes describe a process without naming a specific person.

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Se pica la cebolla y se agrega al sartén.        | The onion is chopped and added to the pan.   | impersonal *se*, two verbs |
| Se hornea el pan por cuarenta minutos.           | The bread is baked for forty minutes.        | impersonal *se*            |
| Primero se hierve el agua, luego se agrega la sal. | First the water is boiled, then the salt is added. | sequence, impersonal *se* |
| Se mezclan los ingredientes secos aparte.        | The dry ingredients are mixed separately.     | plural impersonal *se*     |
| Se escurre la pasta antes de servir.             | The pasta is drained before serving.          | impersonal *se*            |

## Talking about what someone usually cooks

Present tense to describe habits or typical preparation methods.

| Spanish                                    | English                            | Notes             |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Mi mamá siempre hornea el pollo, no lo fríe.  | My mom always bakes the chicken, not fries it. | *hornear* vs *freír*    |
| Cocino con aceite de oliva casi siempre.      | I cook with olive oil almost always.     | *cocinar*                 |
| Ellos asan carne todos los domingos.          | They grill meat every Sunday.            | *asar*, habitual           |
| ¿Cómo preparas los frijoles?                  | How do you prepare the beans?             | question, *preparar*      |
| Prefiero hervir las verduras en vez de freírlas. | I prefer to boil the vegetables instead of frying them. | *preferir* + infinitive |

## Asking for help or checking on the food

Everyday kitchen questions between people cooking together.

| Spanish                               | English                             | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| ¿Ya está hirviendo el agua?               | Is the water boiling yet?                 | present progressive        |
| ¿Me ayudas a pelar las zanahorias?         | Can you help me peel the carrots?          | *ayudar a* + infinitive    |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo hay que hornearlo?          | How long does it need to bake?             | *hay que*                  |
| ¿Ya se enfrió lo suficiente para comer?    | Has it cooled off enough to eat yet?       | *enfriarse*, *ya*          |
| ¿Le pongo más sal o ya está bien?          | Should I add more salt, or is it good already? | indirect question       |
