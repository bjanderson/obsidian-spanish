# Fruits, Vegetables, Meats, and Grocery-Shopping Vocabulary — Conversational Sentences

## Asking if a store has an item

Using *tener* to ask whether a store or market carries something.

| Spanish                              | English                          | Notes                  |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ¿Tienen aguacates maduros?            | Do you have ripe avocados?          | *tener*, *maduro*             |
| ¿Tiene papas hoy?                     | Do you have potatoes today?          | formal *usted*                |
| ¿Tienen carne de res fresca?          | Do you have fresh beef?              | *fresco/a*                    |
| ¿No tienen limones?                   | You don't have limes?                | negative question              |
| ¿Tienen algo de pescado?              | Do you have any fish?                | *algo de*                     |

## Making a shopping list or plan

Talking about what you need to buy, using *necesitar* and *ir a* + infinitive.

| Spanish                                          | English                                   | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Necesito comprar cebolla y ajo.                   | I need to buy onion and garlic.               | *necesitar*                     |
| Voy a hacer la lista de compras.                  | I'm going to make the shopping list.          | *ir a* + infinitive             |
| Vamos a pasar por el supermercado.                | We're going to stop by the supermarket.        | *pasar por*                     |
| Tengo que comprar huevos antes del viernes.       | I have to buy eggs before Friday.              | *tener que*, *del*             |
| ¿Qué necesitamos para la receta?                  | What do we need for the recipe?                | *necesitar*                     |

## Describing food at the moment

Using *estar* for a temporary state — freshness, ripeness, temperature.

| Spanish                             | English                              | Notes               |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| El pan está fresco todavía.           | The bread is still fresh.                 | *estar*, *todavía*         |
| Estas fresas están muy dulces.        | These strawberries are very sweet.        | *estar*, adjective agreement |
| El plátano no está maduro.            | The banana isn't ripe.                    | *estar*, negation           |
| La leche ya no está fría.             | The milk isn't cold anymore.              | *ya no*                    |
| Los mariscos están congelados.        | The seafood is frozen.                    | *estar*, *congelado*       |

## Talking about where things are in the store

Locating items in the market or supermarket using *estar* and prepositions.

| Spanish                                           | English                                | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Las verduras están al fondo del supermercado.       | The vegetables are at the back of the supermarket. | *al*, *del*         |
| El pasillo de carnes está a la derecha.             | The meat aisle is on the right.            | *estar*, location             |
| Las frutas están cerca de la entrada.               | The fruits are near the entrance.          | *cerca de*                    |
| La caja está al lado de la salida.                  | The checkout is next to the exit.          | *al lado de*                  |
| ¿Dónde está el pan?                                 | Where's the bread?                         | *estar*, question              |

## Everyday shopping small talk

Common phrases exchanged with a cashier or vendor while shopping.

| Spanish                                    | English                             | Notes             |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| ¿Algo más?                                    | Anything else?                           | vendor phrase              |
| Eso es todo, gracias.                         | That's everything, thanks.               | customer phrase            |
| ¿Cuánto cuestan las manzanas?                 | How much do the apples cost?             | *cuánto* + plural           |
| Deme medio kilo de tomates, por favor.        | Give me half a kilo of tomatoes, please. | *deme* (command)           |
| ¿Aceptan tarjeta o solo efectivo?             | Do you take card or only cash?           | *aceptar*, payment vocab   |
