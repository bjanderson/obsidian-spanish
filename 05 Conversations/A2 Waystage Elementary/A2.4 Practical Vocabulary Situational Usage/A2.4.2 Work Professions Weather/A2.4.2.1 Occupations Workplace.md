# Occupations and Workplace Contexts — Conversational Sentences

## Asking and Saying What Someone Does

The most common small-talk question about work.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿A qué te dedicas?                       | What do you do (for work)?                | common phrase             |
| Soy programadora, trabajo desde casa.    | I'm a programmer, I work from home.        | *ser*, no article          |
| Mi esposo es abogado.                    | My husband is a lawyer.                    | *ser*, no article          |
| Trabajo de mesero los fines de semana.   | I work as a waiter on weekends.            | *trabajar de*              |

## Talking About the Workplace

Everyday sentences about the office, coworkers, and daily work life.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Mi oficina queda cerca de mi casa.       | My office is close to my house.            | *quedar*, location         |
| Tengo una reunión con el jefe a las nueve. | I have a meeting with the boss at nine. | *jefe*                     |
| Mi compañera de trabajo es muy simpática. | My coworker is very nice.                 | *compañera de trabajo*     |
| ¿Cuál es tu horario esta semana?         | What's your schedule this week?            | *horario*, question        |

## Talking About Salary and Hiring

Common but slightly more personal work conversations.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Van a contratar a dos personas más.      | They're going to hire two more people.     | *contratar a*              |
| El sueldo aquí no está mal.              | The salary here isn't bad.                 | *sueldo*                   |
| Renunció porque encontró algo mejor.     | She quit because she found something better. | *renunciar*, past       |
| Todavía estoy buscando empleo.           | I'm still looking for a job.               | *empleo*, formal            |

## Reacting to News About Someone's Job

Natural conversational responses to work-related updates.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¡Qué bien que te contrataron!            | That's great that they hired you!           | *contratar*, past           |
| No puedo creer que renunciaste sin avisar. | I can't believe you quit without notice.  | *renunciar*                |
| Me alegra que tengas un mejor horario ahora. | I'm glad you have a better schedule now. | *horario*               |
| Qué mal que tu jefe sea tan exigente.    | That's rough that your boss is so demanding. | *jefe*                    |

## Describing a Typical Workday

Everyday sentences about routines at work.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Empiezo a trabajar a las ocho.           | I start work at eight.                     | *empezar a*                |
| Tenemos una reunión de equipo cada lunes. | We have a team meeting every Monday.      | *reunión*                  |
| Como con mis compañeros de trabajo al mediodía. | I eat lunch with my coworkers at noon. | *compañeros de trabajo*   |
| Salgo de la oficina a las seis.          | I leave the office at six.                 | *salir de*                 |
