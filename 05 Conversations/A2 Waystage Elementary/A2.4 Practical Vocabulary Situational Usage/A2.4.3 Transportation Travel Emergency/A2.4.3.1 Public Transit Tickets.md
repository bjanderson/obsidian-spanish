# Modes of Transit and Purchasing Tickets — Conversational Sentences

## Asking About Bus, Train, or Metro Schedules

Common questions when trying to catch public transit.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿A qué hora sale el próximo tren?        | What time does the next train leave?       | *tren*, question           |
| ¿Este autobús va al centro?              | Does this bus go downtown?                 | *autobús*, question        |
| ¿Cada cuánto pasa el metro?              | How often does the subway come?             | *metro*, question          |
| ¿En qué andén sale el tren a Guadalajara? | What platform does the train to Guadalajara leave from? | *andén*         |

## Buying a Ticket

Everyday phrases at a ticket window or machine.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Quiero un boleto de ida y vuelta, por favor. | I'd like a round-trip ticket, please.  | *boleto*, *por favor*      |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta el pasaje a la playa?     | How much is the fare to the beach?          | *pasaje*, question          |
| ¿Dónde compro el boleto?                 | Where do I buy the ticket?                  | *boleto*, question          |
| Deme dos boletos solo de ida, por favor. | Give me two one-way tickets, please.        | formal command             |

## Choosing How to Get Somewhere

Talking about which mode of transit to use.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Prefiero tomar el metro, es más rápido.  | I prefer to take the subway, it's faster.  | *tomar*                    |
| Vamos a pedir un taxi al aeropuerto.     | We're going to call a taxi to the airport.  | *pedir un taxi*             |
| Es mejor ir a pie, no está lejos.        | It's better to go on foot, it's not far.    | *a pie*                     |
| Ella siempre viaja en avión por trabajo. | She always travels by plane for work.       | *viajar en*                 |

## Talking About Getting to the Station

Common logistics sentences before a trip.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Tenemos que estar en la estación a las siete. | We have to be at the station by seven. | *estación*              |
| No encuentro la parada del autobús.      | I can't find the bus stop.                  | *parada*                    |
| El taxi nos deja justo enfrente.         | The taxi drops us off right in front.       | *dejar*                     |
| Perdimos el tren por cinco minutos.      | We missed the train by five minutes.        | *perder*, past               |

## Asking for Help With Directions or Transit

Practical questions when you're unsure how to get somewhere.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Cómo llego a la estación de tren?       | How do I get to the train station?          | question                   |
| ¿Necesito hacer transbordo?              | Do I need to transfer?                       | *transbordo*                |
| ¿Este boleto sirve para el metro también? | Does this ticket work for the subway too?  | *servir para*                |
| ¿Sabe si hay taxis por aquí?             | Do you know if there are taxis around here?  | *saber*, question             |
