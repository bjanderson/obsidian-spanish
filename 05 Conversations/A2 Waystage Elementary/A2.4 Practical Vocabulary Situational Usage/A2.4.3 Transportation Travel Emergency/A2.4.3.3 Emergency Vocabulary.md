# Emergency Vocabulary — Conversational Sentences

## Calling for Immediate Help

Urgent phrases for when something is happening right now.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¡Auxilio! ¡Alguien me está siguiendo!    | Help! Someone is following me!             | *auxilio*, urgent          |
| ¡Llame a la policía, por favor!          | Please call the police!                     | *llamar a*                  |
| ¡Llame a una ambulancia, es urgente!     | Call an ambulance, it's urgent!             | *llamar a*                  |
| ¡Necesito ayuda ahora mismo!             | I need help right now!                       | *necesitar*                 |

## Reporting a Lost or Stolen Item

Common phrases when you've lost something or been robbed.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Perdí mi pasaporte y no sé dónde buscarlo. | I lost my passport and don't know where to look for it. | *perder*         |
| Me robaron el celular en el metro.       | Someone stole my phone on the subway.        | *robar*, impersonal        |
| Dejé mi maleta en el taxi.               | I left my suitcase in the taxi.               | *dejar*, past                |
| ¿Dónde queda la comisaría más cercana?   | Where's the nearest police station?           | question                    |

## Describing a Medical Situation

Phrases for explaining what's wrong to a doctor or pharmacist.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Me siento muy mal desde esta mañana.     | I've felt really sick since this morning.  | *sentirse*                  |
| Soy alérgico a los mariscos.             | I'm allergic to shellfish.                   | *soy alérgico a*             |
| Necesito ver a un médico lo antes posible. | I need to see a doctor as soon as possible. | *necesitar*, personal *a*  |
| Tuve un accidente y me duele la pierna.  | I had an accident and my leg hurts.          | *tener*, *doler*             |

## Asking Where to Get Help

Practical questions when you don't know where to go.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Dónde está el hospital más cercano?     | Where's the nearest hospital?               | question                    |
| ¿Hay una farmacia cerca de aquí?         | Is there a pharmacy near here?                | *hay*                       |
| ¿Alguien habla inglés? Es una emergencia. | Does anyone speak English? It's an emergency. | *alguien*, *emergencia*  |
| ¿Puede llevarme al hospital, por favor?  | Can you take me to the hospital, please?      | polite request                |

## Reassuring or Checking on Someone

Sentences for when you're the one helping someone else.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Estás bien? ¿Necesitas ayuda?           | Are you okay? Do you need help?              | question                    |
| Tranquilo, ya llamé a la ambulancia.     | Calm down, I already called the ambulance.   | *tranquilo*, past           |
| Quédate aquí, voy a buscar ayuda.        | Stay here, I'm going to get help.             | command, *ir a*              |
| No te preocupes, todo va a estar bien.   | Don't worry, everything's going to be fine.   | command, *ir a*              |
