# Hotel Reservations and Travel Logistics — Conversational Sentences

## Making a Reservation

Common phrases when booking a room, in person or by phone.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Quisiera reservar una habitación doble.  | I'd like to book a double room.            | *quisiera*, polite         |
| ¿Tienen habitaciones disponibles para el fin de semana? | Do you have rooms available for the weekend? | *tener*, question |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta la habitación por noche?  | How much does the room cost per night?      | *por noche*                 |
| Necesito una habitación para tres noches. | I need a room for three nights.            | *necesitar*                 |

## Checking In

Phrases you'd use at the front desk when arriving.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Tengo una reservación a nombre de López. | I have a reservation under the name López. | *a nombre de*             |
| ¿A qué hora puedo hacer el check-in?     | What time can I check in?                   | question                   |
| ¿Me puede dar otra llave, por favor?     | Could you give me another key, please?      | polite request              |
| ¿El desayuno está incluido?              | Is breakfast included?                       | *incluido*, agreement       |

## Asking About Hotel Services

Everyday requests and questions during a stay.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Tienen wifi gratis?                     | Do you have free wifi?                       | question                   |
| ¿A qué hora sirven el desayuno?          | What time do they serve breakfast?           | *servir*                    |
| ¿Podría darme más toallas?               | Could you give me more towels?               | polite request               |
| ¿Hay servicio de lavandería?             | Is there laundry service?                     | *hay*                       |

## Checking Out and Handling Logistics

Common sentences around leaving the hotel or adjusting plans.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿A qué hora es la hora de salida?        | What time is checkout?                       | *hora de salida*             |
| Necesito confirmar mi reservación.       | I need to confirm my reservation.            | *confirmar*                   |
| Quiero cancelar mi reservación para mañana. | I want to cancel my reservation for tomorrow. | *cancelar*                |
| Dejé mi maleta en la habitación por error. | I left my suitcase in the room by mistake.  | *dejar*, past                |

## Handling a Problem During the Stay

Practical sentences for when something isn't quite right.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Perdí la llave de mi habitación.         | I lost my room key.                          | *perder*, past               |
| El aire acondicionado no funciona.       | The air conditioning doesn't work.            | *funcionar*                   |
| ¿Puede cambiarme de habitación?          | Can you move me to a different room?          | polite request                 |
| No hay agua caliente esta mañana.        | There's no hot water this morning.            | *hay*, negation                |
