# Direct Objects Identification — Conversational Sentences

## Confirming You Know Someone

*Lo*/*la* come up constantly when talking about people you do or don't know.

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ¿Conoces a mi hermano? — Sí, lo conozco.  | Do you know my brother? — Yes, I know him. | *lo*, masculine person       |
| ¿Conoces a esa chica? — No, no la conozco. | Do you know that girl? — No, I don't know her. | *la*, feminine person       |
| ¿Conocen a mis papás? — Sí, los conocemos. | Do you (all) know my parents? — Yes, we know them. | *los*, mixed group           |
| ¿La conoces bien?                          | Do you know her well?               | *la*, feminine person           |

## Talking About Objects You Have or Need

*Lo/la/los/las* stand in for things already mentioned.

| Spanish                                     | English                              | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ¿Tienes mi número? — Sí, lo tengo.               | Do you have my number? — Yes, I have it.    | *lo*, masculine thing            |
| ¿Necesitas la contraseña? — Sí, la necesito.     | Do you need the password? — Yes, I need it. | *la*, feminine thing             |
| ¿Trajiste los documentos? — Sí, los traje.       | Did you bring the documents? — Yes, I brought them. | *los*, masculine plural  |
| ¿Compraste las entradas? — Sí, ya las compré.    | Did you buy the tickets? — Yes, I already bought them. | *las*, feminine plural |

## Talking About Watching or Seeing Something

*Ver* pairs naturally with direct object pronouns in everyday talk.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ¿Viste la serie nueva? — Sí, la vi ayer.  | Did you see the new show? — Yes, I saw it yesterday. | *la*, feminine thing     |
| ¿Ya viste esa película? — No, no la he visto. | Have you already seen that movie? — No, I haven't seen it. | *la*, feminine thing |
| Los vi en el centro esta mañana.          | I saw them downtown this morning.           | *los*, people                    |
| ¿Me viste en la foto?                      | Did you see me in the photo?                | *me*, direct object               |

## Referring Back to an Idea (Neuter Lo)

*Lo* can point at a whole idea instead of a specific noun.

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                     |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| ¿Sabes por qué se fue? — No lo sé.             | Do you know why he left? — I don't know.    | neuter *lo*, refers to a fact      |
| No lo puedo creer.                             | I can't believe it.                         | neuter *lo*                        |
| Ella ya lo sabía todo.                         | She already knew it all.                    | neuter *lo*                        |
| Lo siento mucho.                               | I'm very sorry (about it/that).             | neuter *lo*, fixed expression      |

## Everyday Requests and Confirmations

Direct object pronouns show up naturally in quick daily exchanges.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| ¿Me esperas un momento?                   | Can you wait for me a moment?               | *me*, direct object                |
| Te veo en la entrada.                     | I'll see you at the entrance.               | *te*, direct object                |
| Nos llamó anoche para avisar.             | He/she called us last night to let us know. | *nos*, direct object                |
| ¿Los invitamos a la fiesta?               | Should we invite them to the party?         | *los*, people                       |
