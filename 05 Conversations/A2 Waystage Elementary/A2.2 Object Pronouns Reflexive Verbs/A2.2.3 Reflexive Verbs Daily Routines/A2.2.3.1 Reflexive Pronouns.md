# Reflexive Pronouns — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Your Own Morning Routine

*Yo* forms with reflexive pronouns come up constantly when describing your own habits.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Me levanto muy temprano entre semana.     | I get up very early on weekdays.            | *levantarse*, *yo*               |
| Me lavo los dientes después de desayunar. | I brush my teeth after breakfast.           | *lavarse*, *yo*                   |
| Me visto rápido cuando llego tarde.       | I get dressed quickly when I'm running late. | *vestirse*, *yo*                 |
| Me preparo un café antes de salir.        | I make myself a coffee before leaving.      | *prepararse*, *yo*                |

## Asking Someone About Their Routine

*Tú* forms with reflexive pronouns are common in casual questions.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ¿A qué hora te despiertas normalmente?    | What time do you usually wake up?           | *despertarse*, *tú*               |
| ¿Te bañas por la mañana o por la noche?   | Do you bathe in the morning or at night?    | *bañarse*, *tú*                   |
| ¿Te acuestas tarde los fines de semana?   | Do you go to bed late on weekends?          | *acostarse*, *tú*                 |
| ¿Cómo te sientes hoy?                     | How do you feel today?                      | *sentirse*, *tú*                  |

## Describing What Someone Else Does

*Él/ella* forms describing a third person's habits.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Mi hermana se maquilla antes de salir.    | My sister puts on makeup before going out.  | *maquillarse*, *ella*             |
| Mi papá se afeita todas las mañanas.      | My dad shaves every morning.                | *afeitarse*, *él*                 |
| Ella se peina frente al espejo.           | She combs her hair in front of the mirror.  | *peinarse*, *ella*                |
| Mi abuelo se sienta en el mismo lugar siempre. | My grandpa always sits in the same spot. | *sentarse*, *él*                 |

## Talking About a Group's Shared Routine

*Nosotros* forms for things you and others do together.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Nos preparamos juntos antes de la fiesta. | We get ready together before the party.     | *prepararse*, *nosotros*          |
| Nos sentamos siempre en la misma mesa.    | We always sit at the same table.            | *sentarse*, *nosotros*            |
| Nos vestimos rápido para no llegar tarde. | We get dressed quickly so we're not late.   | *vestirse*, *nosotros*            |
| Nos acostamos tarde cuando hay partido.   | We go to bed late when there's a game.      | *acostarse*, *nosotros*           |

## Talking About Other People's Habits

*Ellos/ustedes* forms sharing the same *se* as *él/ella*.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Mis vecinos se levantan antes del amanecer. | My neighbors get up before dawn.          | *levantarse*, *ellos*             |
| Los niños se lavan las manos antes de cenar. | The kids wash their hands before dinner.  | *lavarse*, *ellos*                |
| ¿Ustedes se preparan solos o juntos?      | Do you (all) get ready alone or together?   | *prepararse*, *ustedes*           |
| Mis abuelos se acuestan muy temprano.     | My grandparents go to bed very early.       | *acostarse*, *ellos*              |
