# Non Reflexive vs Reflexive Meaning Shifts — Conversational Sentences

## Sleeping vs. Falling Asleep (Dormir/Dormirse)

The classic pair for showing state versus the moment of transition.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Duermo muy bien cuando hace fresco.       | I sleep very well when it's cool out.       | *dormir*, state                  |
| Me dormí leyendo anoche.                  | I fell asleep reading last night.           | *dormirse*, transition            |
| ¿Duermes ocho horas normalmente?          | Do you usually sleep eight hours?           | *dormir*, state                   |
| Se durmió a mitad de la reunión.          | He/she fell asleep in the middle of the meeting. | *dormirse*, transition       |

## Going vs. Leaving (Ir/Irse)

*Ir* needs a destination; *irse* just means departing.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Voy al gimnasio después del trabajo.      | I'm going to the gym after work.            | *ir*, destination                 |
| Me voy, tengo que llegar a tiempo.        | I'm leaving, I have to get there on time.   | *irse*, departure                 |
| ¿Vas a la fiesta este sábado?             | Are you going to the party this Saturday?   | *ir*, destination                 |
| Se fue sin decir nada.                    | He/she left without saying anything.        | *irse*, departure                 |

## Putting On vs. Becoming (Ponerse)

One reflexive verb, two common meanings decided by context.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Me puse los zapatos y salí corriendo.     | I put on my shoes and ran out.              | *ponerse*, "to put on"            |
| Se puso rojo de vergüenza.                | He turned red from embarrassment.           | *ponerse*, "to become"            |
| ¿Te pusiste el suéter que te regalé?      | Did you put on the sweater I gave you?      | *ponerse*, "to put on"            |
| Nos pusimos felices con la noticia.       | We became happy with the news.              | *ponerse*, "to become"            |

## Eating vs. Eating Something Up (Comer/Comerse)

*Comerse* adds the sense of finishing something completely.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Como fruta todos los días.                | I eat fruit every day.                      | *comer*, general                  |
| Me comí todo el pastel yo solo.           | I ate the whole cake by myself.             | *comerse*, complete consumption   |
| ¿Comiste algo antes de salir?             | Did you eat something before leaving?       | *comer*, general                  |
| Se comió toda la pizza en cinco minutos.  | He/she ate the whole pizza in five minutes. | *comerse*, complete consumption   |

## Finding vs. Running Into Someone (Encontrar/Encontrarse)

*Encontrarse con* means meeting someone by chance or by plan.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Encontré mis llaves debajo del sofá.      | I found my keys under the couch.            | *encontrar*, finding an object    |
| Me encontré con un viejo amigo hoy.       | I ran into an old friend today.             | *encontrarse con*, meeting a person |
| ¿Encontraste lo que buscabas?             | Did you find what you were looking for?     | *encontrar*, finding an object    |
| Nos encontramos en el café a las cinco.   | We're meeting up at the café at five.       | *encontrarse*, planned meeting     |
