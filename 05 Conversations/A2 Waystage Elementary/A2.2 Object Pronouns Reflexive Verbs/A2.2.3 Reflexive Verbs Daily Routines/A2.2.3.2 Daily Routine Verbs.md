# Daily Routine Verbs — Conversational Sentences

## Describing the Start of Your Day

The classic morning-routine sequence, told in first person.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Me despierto antes de que suene la alarma. | I wake up before the alarm goes off.       | *despertarse*, *yo*               |
| Me levanto y voy directo a la cocina.     | I get up and go straight to the kitchen.    | *levantarse*, *yo*                |
| Me ducho en cinco minutos si tengo prisa. | I shower in five minutes if I'm in a hurry. | *ducharse*, *yo*                  |
| Me visto mientras escucho las noticias.   | I get dressed while I listen to the news.   | *vestirse*, *yo*                  |

## Asking About Someone's Routine

*Tú* forms with these verbs in casual conversation.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ¿A qué hora te levantas los sábados?      | What time do you get up on Saturdays?       | *levantarse*, *tú*                |
| ¿Te bañas o te duchas normalmente?        | Do you usually take a bath or shower?       | *bañarse*/*ducharse*, *tú*        |
| ¿Te afeitas todos los días?               | Do you shave every day?                     | *afeitarse*, *tú*                 |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo te tomas para arreglarte?  | How long does it take you to get ready?     | *arreglarse*, *tú*                |

## Describing a Family Member's Routine

Common when talking about siblings, parents, or kids.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Mi hermana se maquilla frente al espejo.  | My sister does her makeup in front of the mirror. | *maquillarse*, *ella*        |
| Mi hijo se cepilla los dientes solo ahora. | My son brushes his own teeth now.           | *cepillarse*, *él*                |
| Mi esposo se afeita con navaja, no con máquina. | My husband shaves with a razor, not an electric shaver. | *afeitarse*, *él*         |
| Mi abuela se peina el pelo todas las noches. | My grandma combs her hair every night.     | *peinarse*, *ella*                |

## Describing a Shared Household Routine

*Nosotros* forms for a family's or roommates' shared schedule.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Nos levantamos temprano entre semana.     | We get up early on weekdays.                | *levantarse*, *nosotros*          |
| Nos preparamos juntos para el trabajo.    | We get ready together for work.             | *prepararse*, *nosotros*          |
| Nos ponemos el uniforme antes de salir.   | We put on our uniforms before leaving.      | *ponerse*, *nosotros*             |
| Nos acostamos casi a la misma hora.       | We go to bed at almost the same time.       | *acostarse*, *nosotros*           |

## Talking About What You Put On or Take Off

*Ponerse* and *quitarse* as opposites, common when discussing clothing and weather.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Me pongo un abrigo cuando hace frío.      | I put on a coat when it's cold.             | *ponerse*, *yo*                   |
| ¿Te quitas los zapatos al entrar a casa?  | Do you take off your shoes when you enter the house? | *quitarse*, *tú*             |
| Se pone nervioso antes de los exámenes.   | He gets nervous before exams.               | *ponerse*, idiomatic use          |
| Nos quitamos la chaqueta porque hacía calor. | We took off our jackets because it was hot. | *quitarse*, *nosotros*         |
