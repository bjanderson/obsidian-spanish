# Estar Conjugation Present Indicative — Conversational Sentences

## Asking and Answering How Are You

The single most common exchange that uses *estar*.

| Spanish                | English                | Notes                    |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ¿Cómo estás?                 | How are you?                 | *tú* form, informal            |
| Estoy bien, gracias.         | I'm fine, thanks.            | *yo* form, standard reply      |
| ¿Cómo está usted?            | How are you?                 | *usted*, formal                |
| Estoy un poco cansado.       | I'm a little tired.          | *yo* form with modifier        |

## Saying Where Someone Is

Using *estar* to give a location.

| Spanish                     | English                     | Notes               |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Estoy en el trabajo.              | I'm at work.                     | *yo* form                  |
| ¿Dónde estás?                     | Where are you?                   | common text-message opener |
| Mi hermano está en la escuela.    | My brother is at school.         | *él* form                  |
| Estamos en casa de mis padres.    | We're at my parents' house.      | *nosotros* form            |

## Describing How Someone Feels Right Now

Temporary emotional or physical states.

| Spanish                   | English                    | Notes                |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Estoy muy contento hoy.         | I'm very happy today.            | *yo* form                   |
| Ella está preocupada.           | She's worried.                   | *ella* form                 |
| Estamos nerviosos por el examen. | We're nervous about the exam.   | *nosotros* form             |
| ¿Por qué estás triste?          | Why are you sad?                 | question form               |

## Talking About a Group's Situation

Using *estar* with plural subjects.

| Spanish                     | English                     | Notes             |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Están listos para salir.          | They're ready to go.             | *ellos* form              |
| ¿Ustedes están bien?              | Are you (all) okay?              | *ustedes*, formal plural  |
| Mis amigos están de vacaciones.   | My friends are on vacation.      | *ellos* form              |
| Todos estamos muy ocupados.       | We're all very busy.             | *nosotros* form           |
