# Yo-GO Irregular Verbs — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Daily Activities with Hacer

Common *yo* form uses of *hacer*.

| Spanish                     | English                      | Notes             |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Hago ejercicio por la mañana.       | I exercise in the morning.             | *hacer*, *yo*               |
| No hago mucho los domingos.        | I don't do much on Sundays.            | *hacer*, negative           |
| ¿Qué haces los fines de semana?    | What do you do on weekends?            | *hacer*, *tú*, regular      |
| Hacemos la cena juntos.            | We make dinner together.               | *hacer*, *nosotros*, regular |

## Talking About Where Things Go with Poner

Common *yo* form uses of *poner*.

| Spanish                     | English                       | Notes             |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Pongo mis llaves aquí.              | I put my keys here.                    | *poner*, *yo*               |
| Siempre pongo la alarma a las seis. | I always set the alarm for six.        | *poner*, *yo*               |
| ¿Dónde pones tus cosas?             | Where do you put your things?          | *poner*, *tú*, regular      |
| Ponemos la mesa antes de comer.     | We set the table before eating.        | *poner*, *nosotros*, regular |

## Talking About Leaving with Salir

Common *yo* form uses of *salir*.

| Spanish                        | English                         | Notes             |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Salgo del trabajo a las cinco.          | I leave work at five.                     | *salir*, *yo*               |
| ¿A qué hora sales de casa?              | What time do you leave home?              | *salir*, *tú*, regular      |
| Salgo con mis amigos los viernes.       | I go out with my friends on Fridays.      | *salir*, *yo*               |
| Salimos temprano hoy.                   | We're leaving early today.                | *salir*, *nosotros*, regular |

## Bringing Things and Sharing Knowledge

*Traer* and *saber* in everyday talk.

| Spanish                       | English                       | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Traigo mi computadora al trabajo.     | I bring my computer to work.           | *traer*, *yo*               |
| ¿Traes algo para la fiesta?           | Are you bringing something for the party? | *traer*, *tú*, regular  |
| No sé la respuesta.                   | I don't know the answer.               | *saber*, *yo*               |
| Sé cocinar muy bien.                  | I know how to cook very well.          | *saber*, *yo*               |

## Combining Yo-GO Verbs Naturally

Everyday sentences mixing several of these verbs.

| Spanish                              | English                                | Notes                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Hago la maleta y salgo temprano.              | I pack my bag and leave early.                  | *hacer* + *salir*                |
| Pongo la tele cuando llego a casa.            | I turn on the TV when I get home.               | *poner*                          |
| Traigo comida cuando salgo con amigos.        | I bring food when I go out with friends.        | *traer* + *salir*                |
| No sé qué hago este fin de semana.            | I don't know what I'm doing this weekend.       | *saber* + *hacer*                |
