# Stem Changers e-ie, o-ue, e-i — Conversational Sentences

## Saying What You Want with Querer

Everyday uses of *e → ie* verbs to express wants.

| Spanish                      | English                     | Notes             |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Quiero un vaso de agua.             | I want a glass of water.             | *querer*, *yo*             |
| ¿Qué quieres hacer hoy?             | What do you want to do today?        | *querer*, *tú*             |
| No entiendo esta palabra.           | I don't understand this word.        | *entender*, *yo*           |
| Pensamos ir al cine.                | We're thinking of going to the movies. | *pensar*, *nosotros*, no change |

## Asking for Help or Permission with Poder

*O → ue* verbs in requests.

| Spanish                       | English                       | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| ¿Puedes ayudarme un momento?          | Can you help me for a moment?          | *poder*, *tú*              |
| No puedo salir esta noche.            | I can't go out tonight.                | *poder*, *yo*              |
| ¿Pueden esperar cinco minutos?        | Can you (all) wait five minutes?       | *poder*, *ustedes*         |
| Mi hermano vuelve el lunes.           | My brother comes back Monday.          | *volver*, *él*             |

## Requesting Things with Pedir

*E → i* verbs when asking for something.

| Spanish                     | English                      | Notes            |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Pido la cuenta, por favor.          | I'm asking for the check, please.      | *pedir*, *yo*              |
| ¿Qué pides normalmente aquí?        | What do you usually order here?        | *pedir*, *tú*              |
| Ella siempre pide ayuda.            | She always asks for help.              | *pedir*, *ella*            |
| Repetimos la pregunta.              | We repeat the question.                | *repetir*, *nosotros*, no change |

## Talking About Sleep and Daily Habits

Mixing stem-changers into everyday routine talk.

| Spanish                    | English                     | Notes            |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Duermo ocho horas cada noche.      | I sleep eight hours every night.       | *dormir*, *yo*             |
| ¿A qué hora empiezas a trabajar?   | What time do you start work?           | *empezar*, *tú*            |
| Mis papás encuentran tiempo libre. | My parents find free time.             | *encontrar*, *ellos*       |
| Seguimos las instrucciones.        | We follow the instructions.            | *seguir*, *nosotros*, no change |

## Mixing Stem-Changers in Everyday Talk

Combining several patterns naturally.

| Spanish                            | English                             | Notes                  |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Quiero dormir más.                          | I want to sleep more.                        | *querer* + *dormir*            |
| ¿Puedes pedir la comida por mí?             | Can you order the food for me?               | *poder* + *pedir*              |
| Pienso que puedes lograrlo.                 | I think you can do it.                       | *pensar* + *poder*             |
| Ella quiere volver temprano.                | She wants to come back early.                | *querer* + *volver*            |
