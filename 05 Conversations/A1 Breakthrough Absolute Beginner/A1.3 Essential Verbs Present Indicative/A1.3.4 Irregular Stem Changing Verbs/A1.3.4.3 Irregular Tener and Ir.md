# Irregular Tener and Ir — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About What You Have

Everyday uses of *tener*.

| Spanish                     | English                       | Notes             |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Tengo dos perros.                   | I have two dogs.                       | *tener*, *yo*               |
| ¿Tienes hermanos?                   | Do you have siblings?                  | *tener*, *tú*               |
| Ella tiene veinte años.             | She's twenty years old.                | *tener*, idiomatic age      |
| Tenemos una reunión a las tres.     | We have a meeting at three.            | *tener*, *nosotros*         |

## Expressing Obligation with Tener Que

*Tener que* + infinitive for things you have to do.

| Spanish                        | English                          | Notes             |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Tengo que terminar este proyecto.       | I have to finish this project.              | *tener que* + infinitive   |
| ¿Tienes que trabajar el sábado?         | Do you have to work on Saturday?            | *tener que* + infinitive   |
| Tenemos que llegar temprano.            | We have to arrive early.                    | *tener que* + infinitive   |
| Mis padres tienen que viajar por trabajo. | My parents have to travel for work.       | *tener que* + infinitive   |

## Talking About Where You're Going

Everyday uses of *ir*.

| Spanish                    | English                     | Notes             |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Voy al gimnasio los lunes.         | I go to the gym on Mondays.            | *ir*, *al* contraction      |
| ¿Adónde vas?                       | Where are you going?                   | *ir*, *tú*                  |
| Ella va a la oficina en bus.       | She goes to the office by bus.         | *ir*, *ella*                |
| Vamos al cine esta noche.          | We're going to the movies tonight.     | *ir*, *nosotros*, *al*      |

## Talking About Near-Future Plans with Ir A

*Ir a* + infinitive for things you're about to do.

| Spanish                            | English                              | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Voy a llamar a mi mamá.                     | I'm going to call my mom.                         | *ir a* + infinitive, personal *a* |
| ¿Vas a estudiar esta noche?                 | Are you going to study tonight?                   | *ir a* + infinitive        |
| Vamos a cenar en ese restaurante nuevo.     | We're going to have dinner at that new restaurant. | *ir a* + infinitive        |
| Mis amigos van a mudarse el próximo mes.    | My friends are going to move next month.          | *ir a* + infinitive        |

## Combining Tener and Ir Naturally

Everyday sentences that mix obligation and near-future plans.

| Spanish                              | English                                  | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Tengo que ir al banco.                        | I have to go to the bank.                          | *tener que* + *ir a*             |
| ¿Tienes que ir a la reunión mañana?           | Do you have to go to the meeting tomorrow?         | *tener que* + *ir a*             |
| Vamos a ir al parque si tenemos tiempo.       | We're going to go to the park if we have time.     | *ir a* + *tener*                 |
| No tengo tiempo, pero voy a intentarlo.       | I don't have time, but I'm going to try.           | *tener* + *ir a*                 |
