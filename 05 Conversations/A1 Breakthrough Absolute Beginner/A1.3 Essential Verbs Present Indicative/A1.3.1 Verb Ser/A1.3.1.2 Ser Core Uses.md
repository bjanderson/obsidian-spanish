# Ser Core Uses — Conversational Sentences

## Stating Origin with Ser de

The most common way to say where someone is from.

| Spanish                     | English                   | Notes                |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿De dónde eres?                 | Where are you from?           | very common question     |
| Soy de Guatemala.               | I'm from Guatemala.           | reply                    |
| Mi papá es de Ecuador.          | My dad is from Ecuador.       | third person              |
| Somos de la misma ciudad.       | We're from the same city.     | nosotros                 |

## Talking About Profession with Ser

Stating what someone does for a living, without an article.

| Spanish                     | English                     | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Soy contador.                    | I'm an accountant.               | no article                     |
| ¿A qué te dedicas?               | What do you do for work?         | common opener                  |
| Mi mamá es abogada.               | My mom is a lawyer.              | profession, feminine ending    |
| Es una excelente doctora.         | She's an excellent doctor.       | adjective added, article returns |

## Describing Nationality

Nationality words agree in gender and number with the subject.

| Spanish                       | English                    | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Es venezolano.                      | He's Venezuelan.                | masculine singular            |
| Es venezolana.                      | She's Venezuelan.               | feminine singular             |
| Son argentinos.                     | They're Argentine.              | masculine plural              |
| ¿Eres mexicana?                     | Are you Mexican?                | question, feminine            |

## Describing Someone's Traits

Physical and personality traits that describe what someone is generally like.

| Spanish                    | English                    | Notes                  |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Mi hermano es muy alto.          | My brother is very tall.         | physical trait              |
| Ella es súper simpática.         | She's super nice.                | personality trait           |
| Somos bastante tímidos.          | We're pretty shy.                | plural personality trait    |
| ¿Cómo es tu jefe?                | What's your boss like?           | asking for description      |

## Ser With Family and Relationships

Describing who someone is in relation to the speaker.

| Spanish                    | English                     | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Es mi mejor amigo.                | He's my best friend.              | relationship                   |
| Son mis vecinos.                  | They're my neighbors.             | plural relationship            |
| Somos primos.                     | We're cousins.                    | reciprocal relationship        |
| Es la esposa de Carlos.           | She's Carlos's wife.              | possession with *de*           |
