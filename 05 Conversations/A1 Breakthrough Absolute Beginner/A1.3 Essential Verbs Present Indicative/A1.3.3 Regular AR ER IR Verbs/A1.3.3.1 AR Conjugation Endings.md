# AR Conjugation Endings — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About What You Do

Common *yo* form statements about daily activities.

| Spanish                     | English                     | Notes             |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Trabajo en una oficina.           | I work in an office.             | *trabajar*, *yo*         |
| Estudio por la mañana.            | I study in the morning.          | *estudiar*, *yo*         |
| Escucho música todos los días.    | I listen to music every day.     | *escuchar*, *yo*         |
| Cocino los fines de semana.       | I cook on weekends.              | *cocinar*, *yo*          |

## Asking a Friend About Their Habits

*Tú* form questions between friends.

| Spanish                    | English                     | Notes            |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Dónde trabajas?                  | Where do you work?               | *trabajar*, *tú*         |
| ¿Hablas otro idioma?              | Do you speak another language?   | *hablar*, *tú*           |
| ¿Cuánto ganas al mes?             | How much do you earn a month?    | *ganar*, *tú*, *al* contraction |
| ¿Manejas al trabajo?              | Do you drive to work?            | *manejar*, *tú*          |

## Describing What Someone Else Does

*Él/ella* form to describe a third person.

| Spanish                     | English                    | Notes             |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Mi hermana canta muy bien.         | My sister sings very well.        | *cantar*, *ella*         |
| Mi papá maneja un camión.          | My dad drives a truck.            | *manejar*, *él*          |
| Ella practica yoga.                | She practices yoga.               | *practicar*, *ella*      |
| Mi jefe llega temprano.            | My boss arrives early.            | *llegar*, *él*           |

## Talking About What We Do Together

*Nosotros* form for shared activities.

| Spanish                       | English                        | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Caminamos por el parque.              | We walk through the park.              | *caminar*, *nosotros*    |
| Cenamos juntos los domingos.          | We have dinner together on Sundays.    | *cenar*, *nosotros*      |
| Compramos comida en el mercado.       | We buy food at the market.             | *comprar*, *nosotros*    |
| Practicamos español en casa.          | We practice Spanish at home.           | *practicar*, *nosotros*  |

## Talking About What Other People Do

*Ellos/ustedes* form for groups.

| Spanish                       | English                       | Notes            |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Mis vecinos viajan mucho.             | My neighbors travel a lot.           | *viajar*, *ellos*        |
| Mis padres bailan salsa los sábados.  | My parents dance salsa on Saturdays. | *bailar*, *ellos*        |
| Mis amigos estudian en la universidad. | My friends study at the university.  | *estudiar*, *ellos*      |
| ¿Ustedes toman café?                  | Do you (all) drink coffee?           | *tomar*, *ustedes*       |
