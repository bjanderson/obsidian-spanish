# IR Conjugation Endings — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Where You Live

Common *yo* form statements with *vivir*.

| Spanish                    | English                     | Notes            |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Vivo cerca del centro.            | I live near downtown.            | *vivir*, *yo*, *del*     |
| Vivo con mis padres.              | I live with my parents.          | *vivir*, *yo*             |
| Escribo un blog de viajes.        | I write a travel blog.           | *escribir*, *yo*          |
| Abro la tienda a las ocho.        | I open the store at eight.       | *abrir*, *yo*             |

## Asking a Friend About Habits

*Tú* form questions.

| Spanish                    | English                     | Notes            |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| ¿Dónde vives?                     | Where do you live?               | *vivir*, *tú*             |
| ¿Escribes mucho?                  | Do you write a lot?              | *escribir*, *tú*          |
| ¿A qué hora abres el negocio?     | What time do you open the business? | *abrir*, *tú*          |
| ¿Asistes a la clase de yoga?      | Do you attend the yoga class?    | *asistir*, *tú*           |

## Describing What Someone Else Does

*Él/ella* form.

| Spanish                     | English                     | Notes             |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Mi tía vive en Panamá.              | My aunt lives in Panama.             | *vivir*, *ella*           |
| Ella escribe novelas.               | She writes novels.                   | *escribir*, *ella*        |
| Él abre el correo cada mañana.      | He opens his mail every morning.     | *abrir*, *él*              |
| Ella recibe a sus amigos los viernes. | She receives her friends on Fridays. | *recibir*, personal *a*  |

## Talking About What We Do Together

*Nosotros* form.

| Spanish                        | English                        | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Compartimos un apartamento.            | We share an apartment.                 | *compartir*, *nosotros*  |
| Vivimos cerca del trabajo.             | We live near work.                     | *vivir*, *del*           |
| Escribimos cartas a la familia.        | We write letters to the family.        | *escribir*, *nosotros*   |
| Subimos al segundo piso.               | We go up to the second floor.          | *subir*, *nosotros*      |

## Talking About What Other People Do

*Ellos/ustedes* form.

| Spanish                        | English                        | Notes            |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Mis vecinos viven en el quinto piso.   | My neighbors live on the fifth floor.  | *vivir*, *ellos*          |
| Mis colegas reciben muchos correos.    | My coworkers receive a lot of emails.  | *recibir*, *ellos*        |
| ¿Ustedes asisten a la boda?            | Are you (all) attending the wedding?   | *asistir*, *ustedes*      |
| Ellos abren temprano los lunes.        | They open early on Mondays.            | *abrir*, *ellos*          |
