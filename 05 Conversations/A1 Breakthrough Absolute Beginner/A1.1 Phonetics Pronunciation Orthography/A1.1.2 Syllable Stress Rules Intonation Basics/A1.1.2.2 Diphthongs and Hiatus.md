# Diphthongs and Hiatus — Conversational Sentences

Natural sentences a Spanish speaker would actually say, using this lesson's point,
grouped by the pattern each sentence follows.

## Common diphthongs in everyday words

Weak+strong or strong+weak vowel pairs that glide into one syllable.

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Estoy bien, gracias.                 | I'm well, thanks.                 | *bien* → diphthong (*ie*)        |
| Tengo una reunión a las tres.        | I have a meeting at three.        | *reunión* → diphthong (*eu*)     |
| Necesitamos más cuidado aquí.        | We need more care here.           | *cuidado* → diphthong (*ui*)     |
| El cielo está muy nublado hoy.       | The sky is very cloudy today.     | *cielo*, *nublado* → diphthongs (*ie*, *ua*) |
| Quiero un carro nuevo.               | I want a new car.                 | *quiero*, *nuevo* → diphthongs (*ie*, *ue*) |

## Common hiatuses in everyday words

Strong+strong vowel pairs that stay in separate syllables.

| Spanish                             | English                       | Notes                            |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Ese hotel es muy feo.                    | That hotel is very ugly.          | *feo* → hiato (*eo*)                |
| Leo el periódico cada mañana.            | I read the newspaper every morning. | *leo* → hiato (*eo*)              |
| Hay mucho caos en la ciudad.             | There is a lot of chaos in the city. | *caos* → hiato (*ao*)            |
| Peleamos por cosas pequeñas a veces.     | We fight over small things sometimes. | *peleamos* → hiato (*ea*)        |

## Accent-forced hiatus (breaking what looks like a diphthong)

An accent on the weak vowel splits the pair into two syllables on purpose.

| Spanish                              | English                       | Notes                              |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| ¿Qué día es hoy?                          | What day is it today?              | *día* → hiato forced by the accent on *í* |
| Vivo en un país pequeño.                  | I live in a small country.         | *país* → hiato forced by the accent on *í* |
| Me gusta comer maíz.                      | I like to eat corn.                | *maíz* → hiato forced by the accent on *í* |
| Ella tiene mucha alegría.                 | She has a lot of joy.              | *alegría* → hiato forced by the accent on *í* |

## Diphthongs and hiatuses in the same sentence

Real speech mixes both patterns often — practice hearing the shift.

| Spanish                                       | English                                  | Notes                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Hoy el cielo está feo y hay caos en la calle.       | Today the sky looks bad and there's chaos in the street. | *cielo* (diptongo), *feo*/*caos* (hiato) |
| Quiero saber qué día es y si hace frío.             | I want to know what day it is and if it's cold. | *quiero* (diptongo), *día*/*frío* (hiato) |
| Tenemos cuidado porque hay mucho caos hoy.          | We're being careful because there's a lot of chaos today. | *cuidado* (diptongo), *caos* (hiato) |
