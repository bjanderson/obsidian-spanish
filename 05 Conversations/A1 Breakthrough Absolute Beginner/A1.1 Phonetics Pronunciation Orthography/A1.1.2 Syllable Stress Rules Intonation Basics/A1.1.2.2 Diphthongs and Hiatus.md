# Diphthongs and Hiatus — Conversational Sentences

## Common diphthongs in everyday words

Weak+strong or strong+weak vowel pairs that glide into one syllable.

| Spanish                        | English                       | Notes                                        |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Estoy bien, gracias.           | I'm well, thanks.             | _bien_ → diphthong (_ie_)                    |
| Tengo una reunión a las tres.  | I have a meeting at three.    | _reunión_ → diphthong (_eu_)                 |
| Necesitamos más cuidado aquí.  | We need more care here.       | _cuidado_ → diphthong (_ui_)                 |
| El cielo está muy nublado hoy. | The sky is very cloudy today. | _cielo_, _nublado_ → diphthongs (_ie_, _ua_) |
| Quiero un carro nuevo.         | I want a new car.             | _quiero_, _nuevo_ → diphthongs (_ie_, _ue_)  |

## Common hiatuses in everyday words

Strong+strong vowel pairs that stay in separate syllables.

| Spanish                              | English                               | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Ese hotel es muy feo.                | That hotel is very ugly.              | _feo_ → hiato (_eo_)      |
| Leo el periódico cada mañana.        | I read the newspaper every morning.   | _leo_ → hiato (_eo_)      |
| Hay mucho caos en la ciudad.         | There is a lot of chaos in the city.  | _caos_ → hiato (_ao_)     |
| Peleamos por cosas pequeñas a veces. | We fight over small things sometimes. | _peleamos_ → hiato (_ea_) |

## Accent-forced hiatus (breaking what looks like a diphthong)

An accent on the weak vowel splits the pair into two syllables on purpose.

| Spanish                   | English                    | Notes                                         |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| ¿Qué día es hoy?          | What day is it today?      | _día_ → hiato forced by the accent on _í_     |
| Vivo en un país pequeño.  | I live in a small country. | _país_ → hiato forced by the accent on _í_    |
| Me gusta comer maíz.      | I like to eat corn.        | _maíz_ → hiato forced by the accent on _í_    |
| Ella tiene mucha alegría. | She has a lot of joy.      | _alegría_ → hiato forced by the accent on _í_ |

## Diphthongs and hiatuses in the same sentence

Real speech mixes both patterns often — practice hearing the shift.

| Spanish                                       | English                                                   | Notes                                     |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Hoy el cielo está feo y hay caos en la calle. | Today the sky looks bad and there's chaos in the street.  | _cielo_ (diptongo), _feo_/_caos_ (hiato)  |
| Quiero saber qué día es y si hace frío.       | I want to know what day it is and if it's cold.           | _quiero_ (diptongo), _día_/_frío_ (hiato) |
| Tenemos cuidado porque hay mucho caos hoy.    | We're being careful because there's a lot of chaos today. | _cuidado_ (diptongo), _caos_ (hiato)      |
