# Indefinite Articles — Conversational Sentences

## Introducing something for the first time

_Un/una_ introduces a new, unspecified thing into the conversation.

| Spanish                        | English                     | Notes               |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Tengo un perro nuevo.          | I have a new dog.           | _un_ (masc. sing.). |
| Compré una camisa ayer.        | I bought a shirt yesterday. | _una_ (fem. sing.). |
| Vi un carro increíble.         | I saw an amazing car.       | _un_ (masc. sing.). |
| Necesito una respuesta pronto. | I need an answer soon.      | _una_ (fem. sing.). |

## Talking about "some" of something plural

_Unos/unas_ covers an unspecified small quantity.

| Spanish                      | English                          | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Hay unos mensajes para ti.   | There are some messages for you. | _unos_ (masc. plural). |
| Tengo unas dudas sobre esto. | I have some doubts about this.   | _unas_ (fem. plural).  |
| Compramos unos regalos.      | We bought some gifts.            | _unos_ (masc. plural). |
| Vinieron unas amigas mías.   | Some friends of mine came over.  | _unas_ (fem. plural).  |

## Estimating a quantity with unos/unas

_Unos/unas_ also means "about" or "approximately" before a number.

| Spanish                      | English                            | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Llego en unos cinco minutos. | I'll arrive in about five minutes. | Approximation with a number. |
| Cuesta unos veinte dólares.  | It costs about twenty dollars.     | Approximation with a number. |
| Había unas cien personas.    | There were about a hundred people. | Approximation with a number. |
