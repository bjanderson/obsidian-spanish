# Definite Articles — Conversational Sentences

## Pointing out a specific thing

The most basic use of _el/la/los/las_ — identifying a particular object.

| Spanish                       | English                    | Notes                                  |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| El libro está en la mesa.     | The book is on the table.  | _el_ (masc. sing.), _la_ (fem. sing.). |
| Los niños están en el parque. | The kids are at the park.  | _los_ (masc. plural).                  |
| Las llaves están aquí.        | The keys are here.         | _las_ (fem. plural).                   |
| ¿Viste la película nueva?     | Did you see the new movie? | _la_ (fem. sing.).                     |

## Talking about a general category

Definite articles show up with things you like or dislike, even when
English would drop "the."

| Spanish                     | English                | Notes                                      |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Me gusta la música en vivo. | I like live music.     | General category, article required.        |
| No me gusta el frío.        | I don't like the cold. | General category, article required.        |
| Odio las mentiras.          | I hate lies.           | General category, article required.        |
| El café me encanta.         | I love coffee.         | Subject placed first, still takes article. |

## Saying what day something happens

Days of the week take the article for recurring or specific events, but
not right after _ser_.

| Spanish                        | English                        | Notes                             |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Nos vemos el jueves.           | See you on Thursday.           | _el jueves_ — specific day.       |
| Trabajo los lunes y miércoles. | I work Mondays and Wednesdays. | _los_ — recurring days.           |
| Hoy es viernes.                | Today is Friday.               | No article after _ser_.           |
| La reunión es el martes.       | The meeting is on Tuesday.     | _el martes_ — specific event day. |
