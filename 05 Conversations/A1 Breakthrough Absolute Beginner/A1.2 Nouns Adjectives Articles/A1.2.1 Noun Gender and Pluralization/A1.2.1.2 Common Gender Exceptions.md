# Common Gender Exceptions — Conversational Sentences

## Talking about a problem or plan (-ma words)

_-ma_ words like _problema, programa,_ and _sistema_ stay masculine despite
ending in _-a_, and they come up constantly in daily conversation.

| Spanish                      | English                        | Notes                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Tenemos un problema.         | We have a problem.             | _el problema_ — masculine despite _-a_. |
| ¿Cuál es el programa de hoy? | What's today's schedule?       | _el programa_ — masculine.              |
| El sistema no funciona bien. | The system isn't working well. | _el sistema_ — masculine.               |
| Fue un buen día.             | It was a good day.             | _el día_ — masculine.                   |

## Asking about someone's hand or a photo (-o words that are feminine)

_Mano, foto,_ and _moto_ stay feminine even though they end in _-o_.

| Spanish                       | English                  | Notes                                            |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Dame la mano.                 | Give me your hand.       | _la mano_ — feminine despite _-o_.               |
| Me encanta esa foto.          | I love that photo.       | _la foto_ — feminine.                            |
| ¿Es tu moto?                  | Is that your motorcycle? | _la moto_ — feminine.                            |
| Se lastimó la mano izquierda. | She hurt her left hand.  | _la mano izquierda_ — feminine adjective agrees. |

## Describing water or something starting with stressed a-/ha-

_Agua_ and similar words take _el_ in the singular for pronunciation but
stay grammatically feminine.

| Spanish                                 | English                                | Notes                                     |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| El agua está fría.                      | The water is cold.                     | _el agua_, but _fría_ stays feminine.     |
| Necesito un poco de agua fresca.        | I need a little fresh water.           | _agua fresca_ — feminine adjective.       |
| Las aguas del lago son tranquilas.      | The lake's waters are calm.            | Plural reverts to _las_.                  |
| Cuidado con el hacha, está muy afilada. | Careful with the axe, it's very sharp. | _el hacha_, feminine adjective _afilada_. |
