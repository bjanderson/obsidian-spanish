# Standard Gender Endings — Conversational Sentences

## Asking where a common -o/-a object is

Everyday objects with the standard endings, paired with their article.

| Spanish                    | English                      | Notes                          |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ¿Dónde está el libro?      | Where is the book?           | _el libro_, _-o_ → masculine.  |
| La mesa está en la cocina. | The table is in the kitchen. | _la mesa_, _-a_ → feminine.    |
| ¿Tienes el carro hoy?      | Do you have the car today?   | _el carro_, _-o_ → masculine.  |
| Deja la mochila aquí.      | Leave the backpack here.     | _la mochila_, _-a_ → feminine. |

## Talking about places using -dad/-ción words

Common feminine nouns from the _-dad_/_-ción_ family come up constantly in
everyday talk about places and information.

| Spanish                            | English                     | Notes                                 |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Esta ciudad es enorme.             | This city is huge.          | _la ciudad_, _-dad_ → feminine.       |
| Necesito más información.          | I need more information.    | _la información_, _-ción_ → feminine. |
| La libertad es importante para mí. | Freedom is important to me. | _la libertad_, _-tad_ → feminine.     |
| ¿Cuál es la dirección?             | What's the address?         | _la dirección_, _-ción_ → feminine.   |

## Describing a color using an -or word

_-or_-ending nouns like _color_ and _amor_ show up in casual descriptions.

| Spanish                     | English                     | Notes                                    |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Me encanta ese color.       | I love that color.          | _el color_, _-or_ → masculine.           |
| Es un amor de persona.      | She's a sweetheart.         | _el amor_, _-or_ → masculine, idiomatic. |
| ¿Cuál es tu color favorito? | What's your favorite color? | _el color_ again in a common question.   |

## Learning a new word together with its article

The habit of pairing a new noun with _el/la_ shows up naturally when
someone explains or corrects vocabulary.

| Spanish                                  | English                                | Notes                                       |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Se dice "el problema", no "la problema". | It's "el problema," not "la problema." | Corrective, teaching context.               |
| ¿Es "el mapa" o "la mapa"?               | Is it "el mapa" or "la mapa"?          | Common learner question.                    |
| Aprendí la palabra con su artículo.      | I learned the word with its article.   | Reinforces the study habit from the lesson. |
