# Pluralization Rules — Conversational Sentences

## Talking about more than one everyday object (vowel-ending nouns)

Vowel-ending nouns just add _-s_, and this pattern is everywhere in daily
speech.

| Spanish                           | English                                    | Notes               |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| Las casas del barrio son bonitas. | The houses in the neighborhood are pretty. | _casa_ → _casas_.   |
| Compré unos libros nuevos.        | I bought some new books.                   | _libro_ → _libros_. |
| Los coches están afuera.          | The cars are outside.                      | _coche_ → _coches_. |

## Talking about consonant-ending nouns

Consonant-ending nouns need _-es_, which shows up constantly with words
like _ciudad, papel,_ and _reloj_.

| Spanish                    | English                    | Notes                  |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Visitamos varias ciudades. | We visited several cities. | _ciudad_ → _ciudades_. |
| ¿Dónde están los papeles?  | Where are the papers?      | _papel_ → _papeles_.   |
| Tengo dos relojes.         | I have two watches.        | _reloj_ → _relojes_.   |

## Talking about -z nouns that switch to -c

_Lápiz_ and _vez_ are common words that shift _z_ to _c_ before _-es_.

| Spanish                             | English                      | Notes                        |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Necesito más lápices.               | I need more pencils.         | _lápiz_ → _lápices_.         |
| Lo he hecho muchas veces.           | I've done it many times.     | _vez_ → _veces_.             |
| Esta es la última vez, ¿de acuerdo? | This is the last time, okay? | Singular _vez_ for contrast. |

## Mentioning people whose plural shifts the accent

Words like _joven_ and _nación_ shift their written accent in the plural
to keep the original stress.

| Spanish                              | English                                       | Notes                                  |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Hay muchos jóvenes en la fiesta.     | There are a lot of young people at the party. | _joven_ → _jóvenes_, accent added.     |
| Varias naciones firmaron el acuerdo. | Several nations signed the agreement.         | _nación_ → _naciones_, accent dropped. |
