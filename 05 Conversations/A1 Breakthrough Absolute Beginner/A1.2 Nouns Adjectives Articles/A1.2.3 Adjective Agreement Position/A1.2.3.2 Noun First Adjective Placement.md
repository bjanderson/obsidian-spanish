# Noun-First Adjective Placement — Conversational Sentences

## Describing an object with a single adjective

The everyday default — noun first, then the describing word right after.

| Spanish                     | English                     | Notes                             |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Quiero la camisa azul.      | I want the blue shirt.      | Noun _camisa_, then _azul_.       |
| Vive en una casa grande.    | She lives in a big house.   | Noun _casa_, then _grande_.       |
| Leí un libro interesante.   | I read an interesting book. | Noun _libro_, then _interesante_. |
| Compré unos zapatos negros. | I bought some black shoes.  | Noun _zapatos_, then _negros_.    |

## Describing a person's nationality or trait

Nationality and personality adjectives follow the same noun-first pattern.

| Spanish                          | English                      | Notes                            |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Conocí a una chica mexicana.     | I met a Mexican girl.        | Noun _chica_, then _mexicana_.   |
| Es un hombre muy amable.         | He's a very kind man.        | Noun _hombre_, then _amable_.    |
| Trabajo con un equipo talentoso. | I work with a talented team. | Noun _equipo_, then _talentoso_. |

## Stacking two adjectives after the noun

Multiple descriptions often stack after the noun, joined with _y_.

| Spanish                                      | English                                              | Notes                                  |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Tienen una casa grande y bonita.             | They have a big, pretty house.                       | Two adjectives stacked with _y_.       |
| Es una persona inteligente y trabajadora.    | She's an intelligent, hardworking person.            | Two adjectives stacked with _y_.       |
| Buscamos un apartamento pequeño pero cómodo. | We're looking for a small but comfortable apartment. | Two adjectives contrasted with _pero_. |
