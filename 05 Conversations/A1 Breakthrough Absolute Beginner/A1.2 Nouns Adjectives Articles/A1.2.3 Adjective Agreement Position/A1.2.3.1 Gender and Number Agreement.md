# Gender and Number Agreement — Conversational Sentences

## Describing something with a full four-form adjective

_-o_-ending adjectives like _rojo_ and _bonito_ shift for both gender and
number.

| Spanish                         | English                         | Notes                   |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Me encanta tu carro rojo.       | I love your red car.            | Masc. sing. _rojo_.     |
| Tiene una casa bonita.          | She has a pretty house.         | Fem. sing. _bonita_.    |
| Compramos unos muebles baratos. | We bought some cheap furniture. | Masc. plural _baratos_. |
| Son unas fotos hermosas.        | They're beautiful photos.       | Fem. plural _hermosas_. |

## Describing something with a number-only adjective

Consonant- or _-e_-ending adjectives like _azul, feliz,_ and _inteligente_
only shift for number.

| Spanish                           | English                            | Notes                                |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Tiene los ojos azules.            | She has blue eyes.                 | _azul_ → _azules_, no gender change. |
| Es una persona muy feliz.         | She's a very happy person.         | _feliz_ stays the same for gender.   |
| Son estudiantes muy inteligentes. | They're very intelligent students. | _inteligente_ → _inteligentes_.      |
| Vive en un apartamento grande.    | He lives in a big apartment.       | _grande_ stays the same for gender.  |

## Checking or correcting agreement out loud

Agreement mistakes are common enough that native speakers often correct
them casually.

| Spanish                                                   | English                                                 | Notes                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Se dice "la casa roja", no "la casa rojo".                | It's "la casa roja," not "la casa rojo."                | Corrective, teaching context.               |
| ¿Es "azul" o "azula"? Es "azul" siempre.                  | Is it "azul" or "azula"? It's always "azul."            | Reinforces that _azul_ doesn't change.      |
| Las paredes quedaron muy bien, ¿verdad? Sí, están lindas. | The walls turned out great, right? Yes, they look nice. | Plural feminine agreement in natural reply. |
