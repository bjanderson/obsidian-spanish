# Invariable Adjectives — Conversational Sentences

## Describing something orange, pink, or violet

These color words never change, no matter the noun's gender or number.

| Spanish                                   | English                                          | Notes                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Me gusta tu camisa naranja.               | I like your orange shirt.                        | _naranja_ stays the same.               |
| Compré unas flores rosa.                  | I bought some pink flowers.                      | _rosa_ stays the same, even plural.     |
| Tiene una bufanda violeta.                | She has a violet scarf.                          | _violeta_ stays the same.               |
| Son unos zapatos naranja bien llamativos. | Those are some really eye-catching orange shoes. | _naranja_ stays the same in the plural. |

## Describing something brown with café

_Café_ is the common, safe choice for brown across neutral Latin American
Spanish, and it never changes either.

| Spanish                             | English                           | Notes                               |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Los zapatos café son mis favoritos. | The brown shoes are my favorites. | _café_ stays the same, even plural. |
| Tiene el pelo café oscuro.          | She has dark brown hair.          | _café_ plus invariable _oscuro_.    |
| Busco una mesa color café.          | I'm looking for a brown table.    | _color_ construction — always safe. |

## Adding light or dark to a color

_Claro_ and _oscuro_ stay fixed after any color, regardless of the noun.

| Spanish                              | English                               | Notes                                               |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Prefiero el azul claro.              | I prefer light blue.                  | _claro_ stays invariable.                           |
| Las paredes son verde oscuro.        | The walls are dark green.             | _oscuro_ stays invariable, even with a plural noun. |
| Quiero pintar la puerta verde claro. | I want to paint the door light green. | _claro_ stays invariable.                           |
