# Subject Pronoun Omission — Conversational Sentences

## Everyday Statements with the Pronoun Dropped

The normal, unmarked way to talk about yourself or someone already established.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                         |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Vivo en un apartamento pequeño.        | I live in a small apartment.          | no *yo* needed                       |
| Trabaja en el centro.                  | He/She works downtown.                | no *él/ella* needed, context clear   |
| Salimos a las siete de la mañana.      | We leave at seven in the morning.     | no *nosotros* needed                 |
| Estudian en la universidad pública.    | They study at the public university.  | no *ellos* needed                    |

## Everyday Questions with the Pronoun Dropped

Casual, natural-sounding questions between people who already know the context.

| Spanish                     | English                        | Notes                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| ¿Trabajas los fines de semana?     | Do you work on weekends?            | no *tú* needed                   |
| ¿Vienen a la cena mañana?          | Are you all coming to dinner tomorrow? | no *ustedes* needed          |
| ¿Ya comiste?                       | Did you already eat?                | no *tú* needed                   |
| ¿Sabe qué hora es?                 | Do you know what time it is?        | no *usted* needed, formal        |

## Keeping the Pronoun for Contrast

Sentences that compare two people, where dropping the pronoun would lose the
contrast.

| Spanish                                   | English                                | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Yo prefiero café, ella prefiere té.               | I prefer coffee, she prefers tea.            | contrast between *yo* and *ella*       |
| Tú estudias medicina, él estudia derecho.         | You study medicine, he studies law.          | contrast between *tú* and *él*         |
| Nosotros llegamos temprano, ellos llegan tarde.   | We arrive early, they arrive late.           | contrast between two groups            |
| Usted decide, yo solo opino.                      | You decide, I just give my opinion.          | contrast, formal vs. informal roles    |

## Keeping the Pronoun for Emphasis

Sentences that single someone out or push back on an assumption.

| Spanish                          | English                            | Notes                        |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Yo sí puedo ayudarte con eso.          | I can definitely help you with that.     | *yo* for emphasis                    |
| ¿Tú hiciste esto?                      | Did you do this?                          | *tú* for pointed emphasis            |
| Ella fue la que llamó, no yo.          | She's the one who called, not me.        | *ella* and *yo* both emphasized      |
| Nosotros nunca dijimos eso.            | We never said that.                       | *nosotros* pushing back              |

## Keeping the Pronoun for Clarity with Usted/Él/Ella

Cases where the shared verb form of *él/ella/usted* would otherwise be ambiguous.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                             |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| ¿Usted trabaja aquí o solo visita?          | Do you work here or are you just visiting?  | *usted* clarifies who's addressed         |
| Él llega hoy, pero ella llega el lunes.     | He arrives today, but she arrives Monday.   | both kept to avoid confusion              |
| ¿Ella es tu jefa?                           | Is she your boss?                            | *ella* needed — no other context clue     |
| Usted puede pasar primero.                  | You may go first.                            | *usted* kept in a polite offer            |
