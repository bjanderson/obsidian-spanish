# Subject Pronouns Overview — Conversational Sentences

## Introducing Yourself and Others with Yo/Él/Ella

Basic self-introductions and pointing out who someone is.

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                      |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Yo soy de Cochabamba.                | I'm from Cochabamba.               | *yo*                             |
| Él es mi hermano mayor.              | He's my older brother.             | *él*                             |
| Ella trabaja en el hospital.         | She works at the hospital.         | *ella*                           |
| Yo estudio ingeniería.               | I study engineering.               | *yo*                             |

## Talking to Friends with Tú

Casual questions and comments you'd use with someone your own age.

| Spanish                       | English                        | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| ¿Tú vas a la fiesta el sábado?      | Are you going to the party Saturday? | *tú*, informal                  |
| Tú siempre llegas temprano.         | You always arrive early.             | *tú*, informal                  |
| ¿Tú conoces a mi prima?             | Do you know my cousin?               | *tú*, informal                  |
| Tú y yo somos buenos amigos.        | You and I are good friends.          | *tú*, paired with *yo*          |

## Speaking Formally with Usted

Polite exchanges with someone you don't know well or who deserves respect.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| ¿Usted es el doctor Ramírez?           | Are you Dr. Ramírez?                  | *usted*, formal                    |
| Usted puede sentarse aquí, señora.      | You may sit here, ma'am.               | *usted*, formal                    |
| ¿De dónde es usted?                    | Where are you from?                    | *usted*, formal                    |
| Muchas gracias por su ayuda, usted es muy amable. | Thank you for your help, you're very kind. | *usted*, formal, polite register |

## Talking About Groups with Nosotros/Nosotras

Statements about "we," matching the group's gender.

| Spanish                             | English                        | Notes                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Nosotros vamos al cine esta noche.         | We're going to the movies tonight.  | *nosotros*, mixed or male group           |
| Nosotras somos compañeras de trabajo.      | We're coworkers.                     | *nosotras*, all-female group              |
| Nosotros no comemos carne.                 | We don't eat meat.                   | *nosotros*                                |
| Nosotras vivimos juntas en un apartamento. | We live together in an apartment.    | *nosotras*, all-female group              |

## Addressing a Group with Ustedes

Everyday sentences aimed at more than one person.

| Spanish                              | English                             | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| ¿Ustedes ya cenaron?                        | Have you all already had dinner?           | *ustedes*, only plural "you" in LatAm |
| Ustedes pueden esperar aquí.                | You all can wait here.                     | *ustedes*                             |
| ¿Ustedes vienen en carro o en bus?          | Are you all coming by car or by bus?       | *ustedes*                             |
| Los invito a ustedes a mi cumpleaños.       | I invite you all to my birthday.           | *ustedes*                             |
