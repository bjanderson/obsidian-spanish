# Simple Negation with No — Conversational Sentences

## Everyday Denials with No

Plain statements turning a fact negative.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                  |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| No tengo tiempo hoy.                   | I don't have time today.              | *no* + verb                    |
| No me gusta el café frío.              | I don't like cold coffee.             | *no* + verb                    |
| Mi hermana no fuma.                    | My sister doesn't smoke.              | subject + *no* + verb          |
| No hablamos mucho últimamente.         | We haven't talked much lately.        | *no* + verb                    |

## Negative Questions

Questions phrased with *no* to check an assumption or express surprise.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| ¿No tienes frío con esa chaqueta?      | Aren't you cold in that jacket?       | negative question                    |
| ¿No conoces a mi vecino?               | Don't you know my neighbor?           | negative question                    |
| ¿No vas a comer nada?                  | Aren't you going to eat anything?     | negative question                    |
| ¿No es tarde para llamar?              | Isn't it late to call?                | negative question                    |

## Correcting or Clarifying with No

Pushing back on something someone said or assumed.

| Spanish                                | English                                | Notes                       |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| No, no vivo aquí, solo estoy de visita.        | No, I don't live here, I'm just visiting.     | doubled *no* in a correction         |
| No es mi culpa.                                | It's not my fault.                            | *no* + verb                          |
| Ese no es mi estilo.                           | That's not my style.                          | *no* + verb                          |
| No creo que sea buena idea.                    | I don't think that's a good idea.             | *no* + verb                          |

## Everyday Refusals and Limits

Turning down an offer or setting a boundary.

| Spanish                          | English                            | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| No puedo salir esta noche.             | I can't go out tonight.                   | *no* + verb                      |
| No quiero hablar de eso ahora.         | I don't want to talk about that now.      | *no* + verb                      |
| No trabajo los fines de semana.        | I don't work on weekends.                 | *no* + verb                      |
| No debes preocuparte tanto.            | You shouldn't worry so much.              | *no* + verb                      |

## Negative Answers in Conversation

Short exchanges where the doubled *no* answer comes up naturally.

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| —¿Vienes a la reunión? —No, no puedo hoy.             | "Are you coming to the meeting?" "No, I can't today." | doubled *no*                  |
| —¿Te gustó la comida? —No, no mucho.                  | "Did you like the food?" "No, not much."       | doubled *no*                  |
| —¿Ya terminaste? —No, no todavía.                     | "Did you finish already?" "No, not yet."       | doubled *no*                  |
| —¿Sabes dónde está Marcos? —No, no lo he visto.       | "Do you know where Marcos is?" "No, I haven't seen him." | doubled *no*             |
