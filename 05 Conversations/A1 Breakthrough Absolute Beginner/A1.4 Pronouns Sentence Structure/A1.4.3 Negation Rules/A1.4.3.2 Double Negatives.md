# Double Negatives — Conversational Sentences

## Saying "Nothing" with Nada

Everyday complaints and statements involving *nada*.

| Spanish                            | English                              | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| No hay nada en el refrigerador.           | There's nothing in the fridge.               | *no* + verb + *nada*                 |
| No entendí nada de la clase.              | I didn't understand anything in class.       | *no* + verb + *nada*                 |
| Nada me sorprende ya.                     | Nothing surprises me anymore.                | *nada* first, no *no*                |
| No tengo nada que hacer hoy.              | I have nothing to do today.                  | *no* + verb + *nada*                 |

## Saying "Nobody" with Nadie

Talking about the absence of people.

| Spanish                          | English                              | Notes                      |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| No hay nadie en casa.                  | There's nobody home.                        | *no* + verb + *nadie*               |
| Nadie contestó el teléfono.            | Nobody answered the phone.                   | *nadie* first, no *no*              |
| No conozco a nadie en esta ciudad.     | I don't know anybody in this city.           | *no* + verb + *nadie*, personal *a* |
| Nadie me avisó del cambio.             | Nobody told me about the change.             | *nadie* first, no *no*              |

## Saying "Never" with Nunca

Habits and things that just don't happen.

| Spanish                          | English                              | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| No llego tarde nunca.                  | I never arrive late.                        | *no* + verb + *nunca*                |
| Nunca he probado la comida tailandesa. | I've never tried Thai food.                  | *nunca* first, no *no*               |
| No como comida rápida nunca.           | I never eat fast food.                       | *no* + verb + *nunca*                |
| Nunca falta a su trabajo.              | He/She never misses work.                    | *nunca* first, no *no*               |

## Agreeing or Disagreeing with Tampoco

Responding to a negative statement someone else made.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| —No me gusta el picante. —A mí tampoco.         | "I don't like spicy food." "Me neither."     | *tampoco*, agreeing negative response    |
| No fui a la fiesta, y mi hermano tampoco.       | I didn't go to the party, and neither did my brother. | *tampoco*, second clause          |
| Ella no sabe cocinar, y yo tampoco.             | She doesn't know how to cook, and neither do I. | *tampoco*                            |
| No he viajado mucho, y tampoco he salido del país. | I haven't traveled much, and I haven't left the country either. | *tampoco*                     |

## Ruling Something Out with Ningún/Ninguna

Saying there's not a single one of something.

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                              |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| No tengo ningún problema con el plan.         | I don't have any problem with the plan.      | *ningún*, agrees with *problema*             |
| No encontré ninguna solución todavía.         | I haven't found any solution yet.            | *ninguna*, agrees with *solución*            |
| No hay ninguna razón para preocuparse.        | There's no reason to worry.                  | *ninguna*, agrees with *razón*               |
| No tomé ninguna decisión sin consultarte.     | I didn't make any decision without asking you. | *ninguna*, agrees with *decisión*          |
