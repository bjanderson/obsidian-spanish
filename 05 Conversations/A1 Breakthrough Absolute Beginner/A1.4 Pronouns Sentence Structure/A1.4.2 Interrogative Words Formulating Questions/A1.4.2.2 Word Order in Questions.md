# Word Order in Questions — Conversational Sentences

## Questions with a Question Word and a Named Subject

Question word, verb, subject — the required order once a question word starts
the sentence.

| Spanish                               | English                                | Notes                            |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| ¿Dónde trabaja tu papá?                        | Where does your dad work?                     | question word + verb + subject                |
| ¿Cuándo llega el avión de Lucía?               | When does Lucía's plane arrive?               | question word + verb + subject                |
| ¿Por qué llora ese niño?                       | Why is that boy crying?                       | question word + verb + subject                |
| ¿A qué hora cierra la tienda de la esquina?    | What time does the corner store close?        | question word + verb + subject                |

## Yes/No Questions Without Inversion

Just adding question marks and rising intonation, subject dropped as usual.

| Spanish                          | English                            | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| ¿Vienes a la fiesta el viernes?         | Are you coming to the party Friday?       | no question word, subject dropped    |
| ¿Ya terminaste la tarea?                | Did you already finish your homework?     | no question word, subject dropped    |
| ¿Quieren pizza o hamburguesas?          | Do you all want pizza or hamburgers?      | no question word, subject dropped    |
| ¿Puedo pasar?                           | Can I come in?                             | no question word, subject dropped    |

## Yes/No Questions with the Subject After the Verb

Same yes/no questions, but with the subject stated and inverted.

| Spanish                        | English                           | Notes                       |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| ¿Habla usted alemán?                  | Do you speak German?                      | subject after verb, formal          |
| ¿Tiene tu hermana un carro nuevo?     | Does your sister have a new car?          | subject after verb                  |
| ¿Saben ustedes la respuesta?          | Do you all know the answer?               | subject after verb, plural          |
| ¿Está lista la comida?                | Is the food ready?                        | subject after verb                  |

## Questions with a Question Phrase and No Stated Subject

Question word right up front, subject dropped because it's clear from context.

| Spanish                          | English                             | Notes                            |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| ¿Dónde vives ahora?                     | Where do you live now?                     | question word + verb, subject dropped          |
| ¿Cómo llegaste tan rápido?              | How did you get here so fast?              | question word + verb, subject dropped          |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas estudiando?       | How long have you been studying?           | question word + verb, subject dropped          |
| ¿Qué piensas hacer el fin de semana?    | What are you planning to do this weekend?  | question word + verb, subject dropped          |

## Follow-Up Questions in a Conversation

The kind of quick back-and-forth question order that comes up mid-conversation.

| Spanish                             | English                              | Notes                             |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| —Me mudé la semana pasada. —¿A dónde te mudaste?  | "I moved last week." "Where did you move to?" | question word + verb, subject dropped     |
| —Llamó mi tío. —¿Qué quería tu tío?          | "My uncle called." "What did your uncle want?" | question word + verb + subject           |
| —Se fueron temprano. —¿Por qué se fueron tan temprano? | "They left early." "Why did they leave so early?" | question phrase + verb + implied subject |
| —Compré un carro nuevo. —¿Cuánto te costó?   | "I bought a new car." "How much did it cost you?" | question word + verb, subject dropped     |
