# Self Introductions — Conversational Sentences

## Giving Your Name

The most basic building block of any introduction.

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Hola, me llamo Valeria.                | Hi, my name is Valeria.                | *me llamo*, casual                   |
| Buenos días, soy el señor Ríos.        | Good morning, I'm Mr. Ríos.            | *soy*, formal introduction           |
| Me llamo Tomás, para servirle.         | My name is Tomás, at your service.     | polite/formal phrase                 |
| Hola a todos, soy Camila.              | Hi everyone, I'm Camila.               | introducing yourself to a group      |

## Asking Someone's Name

Both registers of "what's your name."

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| ¿Cómo te llamas?                       | What's your name?                     | informal                             |
| ¿Cómo se llama usted?                  | What's your name?                     | formal                               |
| Disculpe, ¿cómo se llama?              | Excuse me, what's your name?          | formal, polite opener                |
| Oye, ¿cómo te llamas? Soy Iván.        | Hey, what's your name? I'm Iván.      | casual, offering your own name too   |

## Responding to an Introduction

The standard replies you'll use constantly once names are exchanged.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                              |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Mucho gusto, Valeria.                       | Nice to meet you, Valeria.                  | standard response                            |
| Encantada de conocerte.                     | Delighted to meet you.                       | female speaker                               |
| Encantado, igualmente.                      | Delighted, likewise.                         | male speaker, returning the greeting          |
| El gusto es mío.                            | The pleasure is mine.                        | polite reply to *mucho gusto*                |

## Sharing Origin and Occupation

Rounding out an introduction with where you're from and what you do.

| Spanish                                | English                                 | Notes                            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Soy de Ecuador, pero vivo en Lima.               | I'm from Ecuador, but I live in Lima.            | *soy de*, origin                         |
| Trabajo como enfermera en un hospital.           | I work as a nurse at a hospital.                  | *trabajo como*, occupation                |
| Soy estudiante de ingeniería.                    | I'm an engineering student.                       | *soy* + occupation/role                   |
| Trabajo en una empresa de tecnología.            | I work at a technology company.                   | *trabajo en*, workplace                   |

## Full Introductions

Stringing name, origin, and occupation together the way people actually do it.

| Spanish                                                    | English                                                   | Notes                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Hola, me llamo Sofía, soy de Perú y trabajo como diseñadora.           | Hi, my name is Sofía, I'm from Peru, and I work as a designer.      | name + origin + occupation                 |
| Buenos días, soy el doctor Vega, mucho gusto.                         | Good morning, I'm Dr. Vega, nice to meet you.                       | formal, name + title                       |
| Me llamo Iker, soy de Bolivia, y estudio derecho.                     | My name is Iker, I'm from Bolivia, and I study law.                 | name + origin + activity                   |
| Encantada, soy Renata, trabajo en el centro y vivo cerca de aquí.     | Delighted, I'm Renata, I work downtown and I live near here.        | full casual introduction                   |
