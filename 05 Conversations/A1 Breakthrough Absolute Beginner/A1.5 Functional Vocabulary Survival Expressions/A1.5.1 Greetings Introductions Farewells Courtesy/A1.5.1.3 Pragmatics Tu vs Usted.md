# Pragmatics of Tú vs. Usted — Conversational Sentences

## Formal Register with Strangers

Interactions with someone you don't know — the safe default.

| Spanish                                | English                                | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Disculpe, ¿sabe usted dónde queda el correo?     | Excuse me, do you know where the post office is? | *usted*, asking a stranger            |
| Buenas tardes, ¿me puede atender, por favor?     | Good afternoon, can you help me, please?         | *usted*, service context               |
| Perdone, ¿es usted el dueño de la tienda?        | Excuse me, are you the owner of the store?       | *usted*, addressing an unknown adult   |
| Con permiso, ¿podría usted repetir eso?          | Excuse me, could you repeat that?                | *usted*, polite request                |

## Formal Register with Authority Figures

The professional and respect-driven end of the formal spectrum.

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Buenos días, jefe, ¿tiene un minuto?                 | Good morning, boss, do you have a minute?   | *usted*, workplace authority              |
| Doctora, ¿qué me recomienda usted?                   | Doctor, what do you recommend?               | *usted*, professional context             |
| Señor director, quisiera hablar con usted.           | Mr. Director, I'd like to speak with you.    | *usted*, formal request                   |
| Gracias por su tiempo, profesor.                     | Thank you for your time, professor.          | *usted* implied via *su*                  |

## Informal Register with Peers and Friends

Everyday *tú* with people your own age or already close to you.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                           |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Oye, ¿vienes al partido el sábado?          | Hey, are you coming to the game Saturday?   | *tú*, casual peer                        |
| ¿Tú qué opinas de la película?              | What do you think of the movie?              | *tú*, friend                              |
| Ayúdame con esto, ¿sí?                       | Help me with this, okay?                     | *tú* command form, friend                 |
| ¿Ya llegaste a casa? Avísame.                | Did you get home yet? Let me know.           | *tú*, close friend/family                 |

## Proposing to Switch Registers

The moment a relationship shifts from *usted* to *tú*.

| Spanish                                          | English                                       | Notes                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Ya nos conocemos bien, ¿nos podemos tutear?              | We know each other well now, can we use tú?           | proposing the switch                         |
| No hace falta que me hable de usted, tutéame.            | You don't need to use usted with me, use tú.           | inviting informality                          |
| Con confianza, puedes tutearme.                          | Feel free to use tú with me.                            | inviting informality                          |
| Prefiero que me hables de tú, no de usted.               | I'd prefer you use tú with me, not usted.               | explicit preference                           |

## Navigating Mixed or Uncertain Situations

Moments where the right register isn't obvious, and how speakers handle it.

| Spanish                                          | English                                            | Notes                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| No sé si tutearlo o hablarle de usted.                   | I don't know whether to use tú or usted with him/her.        | expressing the uncertainty itself                |
| Por respeto, mejor le hablo de usted.                    | Out of respect, I'd better use usted with them.              | erring toward the safer, more formal choice      |
| Aunque es joven, en el trabajo le hablo de usted.        | Even though they're young, at work I use usted with them.    | authority context overriding age                 |
| Con mis compañeros de clase siempre uso tú.              | With my classmates I always use tú.                           | peer context, default informal                   |
