# Time of Day Greetings and Farewells — Conversational Sentences

## Morning Greetings

Everyday ways to open a conversation before midday.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| ¡Buenos días! ¿Cómo dormiste?          | Good morning! How did you sleep?      | casual, informal                     |
| Buenos días, ¿ya desayunaste?          | Good morning, did you already have breakfast? | casual                        |
| Buenos días a todos.                   | Good morning, everyone.                | greeting a group                     |
| Buenos días, doctora, ¿cómo está?      | Good morning, doctor, how are you?     | formal                               |

## Afternoon Greetings

Openers for the afternoon stretch of the day.

| Spanish                             | English                              | Notes                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Buenas tardes, ¿tiene un momento?           | Good afternoon, do you have a moment?       | formal                               |
| Buenas tardes, ¿qué tal el día?             | Good afternoon, how's the day going?        | casual                               |
| Buenas tardes, bienvenidos.                 | Good afternoon, welcome.                     | greeting a group, formal-leaning     |
| Hola, buenas tardes, ¿cómo va todo?         | Hi, good afternoon, how's everything going? | combining hola with the greeting     |

## Night Greetings and Farewells

*Buenas noches* doing double duty as both hello and goodbye.

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Buenas noches, pasen, siéntense.       | Good evening, come in, have a seat.   | greeting, arriving somewhere at night  |
| Buenas noches, que descanses.          | Good night, sleep well.                | farewell, informal                     |
| Buenas noches, que tenga buena noche.  | Good night, have a good night.         | farewell, formal                       |
| Ya es tarde, buenas noches a todos.    | It's late already, good night everyone.| farewell to a group                    |

## Casual Farewells

Everyday ways to say goodbye that aren't the more final *adiós*.

| Spanish                            | English                              | Notes                           |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Nos vemos el lunes.                         | See you Monday.                              | *nos vemos*, casual                     |
| Hasta luego, cuídate.                       | See you later, take care.                    | *hasta luego*, casual                   |
| Hasta mañana, descansa.                     | See you tomorrow, get some rest.             | *hasta mañana*, casual                  |
| Bueno, me voy. ¡Hasta pronto!               | Well, I'm heading out. See you soon!         | *hasta pronto*, ending a visit          |

## Mixing Greetings with Small Talk

Real conversational openers that combine a greeting with a quick question.

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                                |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Hola, buenos días, ¿qué tal la semana?                   | Hi, good morning, how's the week going?              | greeting + small talk, casual                |
| Buenas tardes, ¿cómo le va?                              | Good afternoon, how's it going for you?              | formal small talk                             |
| Buenas noches, ¿ya cenaste?                              | Good evening, did you already have dinner?           | casual, night context                         |
| Adiós, fue un gusto verte, saludos a tu familia.         | Goodbye, it was great to see you, say hi to your family. | farewell + closing pleasantry           |
