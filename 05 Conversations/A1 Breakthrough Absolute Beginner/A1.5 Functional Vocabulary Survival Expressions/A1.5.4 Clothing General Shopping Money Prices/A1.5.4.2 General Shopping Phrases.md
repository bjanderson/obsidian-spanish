# General Shopping Phrases — Conversational Sentences

## Opening the interaction with a salesperson

The greeting exchange when you walk into a store, and the standard polite
response.

| Spanish                                          | English                                       | Notes                              |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Buenas tardes, ¿le puedo ayudar en algo?            | Good afternoon, can I help you with something?      | salesperson's opening line             |
| Solo estoy mirando, gracias.                        | I'm just looking, thanks.                           | standard polite response               |
| Estoy buscando un regalo para mi mamá.              | I'm looking for a gift for my mom.                  | _buscar_ + purpose                     |
| ¿Tienen algo en oferta esta semana?                 | Do you have anything on sale this week?             | common opening question                |

## Asking to try something on

Requests to use the fitting room and check the fit.

| Spanish                                             | English                                       | Notes                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| ¿Puedo probarme esta camisa?                             | Can I try on this shirt?                            | _probarme_ + noun                      |
| ¿Dónde está el probador, por favor?                      | Where is the fitting room, please?                  | —                                        |
| Me queda un poco ajustado. ¿Tiene una talla más grande?  | It fits me a little tight. Do you have a bigger size? | describing fit                        |
| Este pantalón me queda perfecto.                          | These pants fit me perfectly.                       | positive fit feedback                  |

## Asking about alternatives

Follow-up questions when the first option isn't quite right.

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                              |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| ¿Tiene esto en otro color?                          | Do you have this in another color?                  | common follow-up                       |
| ¿Hay una talla más pequeña?                         | Is there a smaller size?                            | uses _hay_ for availability            |
| ¿Me puede mostrar algo similar?                     | Can you show me something similar?                  | polite request for options             |
| Prefiero algo menos formal.                          | I prefer something less formal.                     | expressing a preference                |

## Closing the sale

Wrapping up the interaction, whether buying or not.

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                              |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Está perfecto — me lo llevo.                | It's perfect — I'll take it.               | decision to buy                        |
| Voy a pensarlo, gracias.                     | I'll think about it, thanks.               | polite way to walk away                |
| ¿Aceptan tarjeta de crédito?                 | Do you accept credit cards?                | closing/payment question               |
| Muchas gracias por su ayuda.                 | Thank you very much for your help.         | polite closing                         |
