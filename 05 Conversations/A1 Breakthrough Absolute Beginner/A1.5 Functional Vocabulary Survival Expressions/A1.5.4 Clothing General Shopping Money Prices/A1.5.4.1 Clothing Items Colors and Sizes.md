# Clothing Items, Colors, and Sizes — Conversational Sentences

## Describing what you're wearing or need

Everyday statements about clothing, using color adjectives that agree with
the noun.

| Spanish                                          | English                                    | Notes                              |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Hoy llevo una camisa blanca y pantalones negros.    | Today I'm wearing a white shirt and black pants. | _llevar_ = "to wear"                   |
| Necesito unos zapatos cómodos para caminar.         | I need some comfortable shoes for walking.    | adjective after the noun               |
| Mi abrigo favorito es azul oscuro.                  | My favorite coat is dark blue.                 | _oscuro/claro_ modify the color        |
| Ella siempre usa vestidos de colores brillantes.    | She always wears bright-colored dresses.       | _colores brillantes_                   |
| Este suéter me queda un poco grande.                | This sweater fits me a little big.             | _quedar_ for fit                       |

## Asking about someone else's clothing or size

Questions a friend, salesperson, or family member might ask about clothes.

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes                              |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| ¿De qué color es tu chaqueta nueva?            | What color is your new jacket?              | asking about a specific item           |
| ¿Qué talla usas normalmente?                   | What size do you usually wear?              | _talla_ for clothing                   |
| ¿Qué número calzas?                            | What shoe size do you wear?                 | _número_ + _calzar_ for shoes          |
| ¿Tienes una falda que combine con esta blusa?  | Do you have a skirt that matches this top?  | _combinar_ = "to match"                |
| ¿Estos pantalones te quedan bien?              | Do these pants fit you well?                | _quedar bien/mal_                      |

## Comparing colors and sizes

Sentences that compare two clothing items directly.

| Spanish                                              | English                                        | Notes                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Prefiero el vestido rojo al vestido verde.                | I prefer the red dress to the green dress.          | _preferir X a Y_                       |
| Esta talla es más grande que la otra.                     | This size is bigger than the other one.             | comparative with _más...que_           |
| Los zapatos cafés son más cómodos que los negros.         | The brown shoes are more comfortable than the black ones. | _café_ stays invariable          |
| Mi chaqueta es del mismo color que la tuya.                | My jacket is the same color as yours.               | _del mismo color que_                  |

## Shopping-adjacent needs

Short, practical sentences for finding the right item.

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Busco una camisa de manga larga en talla mediana.     | I'm looking for a long-sleeve shirt in medium.    | _de manga larga/corta_                 |
| ¿Tienen este suéter en otro color?                    | Do you have this sweater in another color?        | common store question                  |
| Necesito unas botas para el invierno.                 | I need some boots for the winter.                 | seasonal clothing vocabulary           |
| Este color no me gusta; prefiero algo más claro.       | I don't like this color; I prefer something lighter. | _claro/oscuro_ as intensity modifiers |
