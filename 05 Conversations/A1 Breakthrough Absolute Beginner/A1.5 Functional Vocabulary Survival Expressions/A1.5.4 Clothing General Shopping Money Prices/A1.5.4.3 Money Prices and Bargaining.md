# Money, Prices, and Bargaining — Conversational Sentences

## Asking a price

The most basic exchange: asking how much something costs and getting an
answer with a number.

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                              |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta este vestido?                  | How much does this dress cost?              | basic price question                   |
| ¿Cuánto vale esta chaqueta?                    | How much is this jacket worth?              | _vale_ as an alternative to _cuesta_   |
| Cuesta cuarenta y cinco bolivianos.             | It costs forty-five bolivianos.             | needs numbers vocabulary               |
| ¿Y los zapatos, cuánto valen?                   | And the shoes, how much are they?           | plural _valen_ for a plural item       |

## Reacting to a price

Short, natural reactions once you hear how much something costs.

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes                              |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| ¡Uy, es muy caro!                                    | Whoa, that's very expensive!                  | common exclamation                     |
| Qué barato, me lo llevo.                             | That's so cheap, I'll take it.                | positive reaction + decision           |
| Es un poco caro para mí.                             | It's a bit expensive for me.                  | softer, polite complaint               |
| Está dentro de mi presupuesto.                        | It's within my budget.                        | _presupuesto_ = "budget"               |

## Bargaining at a market

Phrases for negotiating a price at an open-air market, common practice in
many Latin American countries.

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| ¿Me puede hacer un descuento?                              | Can you give me a discount?                          | polite bargaining opener               |
| ¿Cuál es su mejor precio?                                  | What's your best price?                              | softer alternative opener              |
| Le doy treinta bolivianos por los dos.                     | I'll give you thirty bolivianos for both.            | making a counteroffer                  |
| Es lo último que le puedo dar.                             | That's the last price I can give you.                | seller closing the negotiation          |

## Paying

Wrapping up the transaction once a price is settled.

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                              |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| ¿Aceptan tarjeta o solo efectivo?           | Do you accept card or only cash?           | common payment question                |
| Aquí tiene, gracias.                         | Here you go, thanks.                       | handing over payment                   |
| ¿Me puede dar el cambio, por favor?          | Can you give me the change, please?        | _el cambio_ = "the change"             |
| Gracias por el descuento.                    | Thanks for the discount.                   | polite closing after bargaining         |
