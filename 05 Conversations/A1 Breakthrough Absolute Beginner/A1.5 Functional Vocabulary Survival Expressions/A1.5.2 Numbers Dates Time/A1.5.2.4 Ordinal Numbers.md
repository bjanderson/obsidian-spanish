# Ordinal Numbers — Conversational Sentences

## Floors and Locations

Ordinals used constantly to describe where a place is in a building.

| Spanish                                    | English                                  | Notes                              |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Vivo en el segundo piso.                   | I live on the second floor.               | masculine agreement with _piso_      |
| La oficina está en el tercer piso.         | The office is on the third floor.          | _tercer_ before masc. singular noun  |
| Es la primera puerta a la derecha.         | It's the first door on the right.         | feminine _primera_ with _puerta_     |
| Nuestra mesa es la cuarta junto a la ventana. | Our table is the fourth one by the window. | feminine _cuarta_                    |
| El baño está en el primer pasillo.         | The bathroom is in the first hallway.      | _primer_ before masc. singular noun  |

## Order and Place in a Competition

Talking about who finished where in a race, contest, or ranking.

| Spanish                                    | English                                     | Notes                             |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Llegó en primer lugar en la carrera.       | She finished in first place in the race.       | _primer_ before masc. singular noun |
| Mi equipo quedó en segundo lugar.          | My team ended up in second place.              | masculine agreement with _lugar_    |
| Vi al primer corredor cruzar la meta.      | I saw the first runner cross the finish line.  | personal _a_ + _al_ = _a + el_      |
| Terminamos en tercer lugar del torneo.     | We finished in third place in the tournament.  | _del_ = _de + el_                   |
| Es la segunda vez que gana el campeonato.  | It's the second time he wins the championship. | feminine _segunda_ with _vez_       |

## "First Time" Experiences

The common everyday pattern _es mi primera vez_ ("it's my first time").

| Spanish                                      | English                                    | Notes                              |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Es mi primera vez en Bolivia.                 | It's my first time in Bolivia.                | feminine _primera_ with _vez_        |
| Es la segunda vez que como pupusas.           | It's the second time I've eaten pupusas.      | feminine _segunda_ with _vez_        |
| ¿Es tu primera vez en el hospital?            | Is this your first time at the hospital?      | question form                        |
| Fue la tercera vez que llamó al médico.       | It was the third time he called the doctor.   | _al_ = _a + el_, personal _a_        |
| No es la primera vez que pasa esto.           | It's not the first time this has happened.    | negative construction                |

## Sequence in Reading and Learning

Ordinals used to talk about chapters, lessons, and steps in order.

| Spanish                                         | English                                    | Notes                              |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Es el cuarto capítulo del libro.                  | It's the fourth chapter of the book.          | _del_ = _de + el_                    |
| La primera pregunta del examen fue difícil.       | The first question on the exam was difficult. | feminine _primera_, _del_ = _de + el_ |
| Estamos en la quinta lección del curso.           | We're on the fifth lesson of the course.      | feminine _quinta_, _del_ = _de + el_ |
| El profesor explicó el primer ejercicio otra vez. | The teacher explained the first exercise again. | _primer_ before masc. singular noun |
| Ya terminé el sexto capítulo.                     | I already finished the sixth chapter.         | masculine agreement with _capítulo_ |

## Anniversaries and Milestones

Ordinals marking birthdays, anniversaries, and days on the job.

| Spanish                                    | English                                       | Notes                             |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Hoy es mi quinto día en el nuevo trabajo.  | Today is my fifth day at the new job.            | masculine agreement with _día_      |
| Celebramos nuestro primer aniversario.     | We celebrated our first anniversary.             | _primer_ before masc. singular noun |
| Es su décimo cumpleaños.                   | It's his tenth birthday.                         | masculine agreement with _cumpleaños_ |
| Este es mi tercer año en la universidad.   | This is my third year at the university.         | _tercer_ before masc. singular noun |
| Fue su segunda entrevista de trabajo.      | It was her second job interview.                 | feminine _segunda_                  |
