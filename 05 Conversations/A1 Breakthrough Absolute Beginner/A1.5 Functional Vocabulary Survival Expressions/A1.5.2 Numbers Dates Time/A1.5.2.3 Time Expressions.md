# Time Expressions — Conversational Sentences

## Asking and Telling the Time

The most basic time exchange.

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| ¿Qué hora es? —Son las cuatro.              | "What time is it?" "It's four o'clock."      | basic exchange                        |
| Es la una en punto.                         | It's one o'clock sharp.                       | *es la una* + *en punto*              |
| Son las nueve y media de la noche.          | It's 9:30 p.m.                                | *y media* + *de la noche*             |
| ¿Tienes hora? Se me paró el reloj.          | Do you have the time? My watch stopped.       | casual way to ask the time            |

## Scheduling Around Specific Times

Everyday sentences about when things start or happen.

| Spanish                                    | English                                  | Notes                            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| La reunión empieza a las diez.                   | The meeting starts at ten.                          | *a las* + hour                             |
| Llego a la una y cuarto.                         | I'll arrive at 1:15.                                | *a la una* + *y cuarto*                    |
| El vuelo sale a las seis menos diez.             | The flight leaves at 5:50.                           | *menos* pattern                             |
| Abren a las nueve de la mañana.                  | They open at 9 a.m.                                  | *a las* + *de la mañana*                   |

## Talking About Punctuality

Sentences about being early, late, or right on time.

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                            |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Llegaste diez minutos tarde.                  | You arrived ten minutes late.                | using minutes as a duration                 |
| Llegamos justo a las ocho en punto.           | We arrived right at eight o'clock sharp.     | *en punto*                                  |
| Falta media hora para la cita.                | There's half an hour left until the appointment. | *media hora*                          |
| Siempre llega cinco minutos antes.            | He/She always arrives five minutes early.    | describing a habit                          |

## Everyday Routines Tied to Time

Time expressions embedded in describing a daily schedule.

| Spanish                                      | English                                   | Notes                              |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Me levanto a las seis y media todos los días.      | I get up at 6:30 every day.                        | *a las* + *y media*                          |
| Almuerzo a la una de la tarde.                     | I have lunch at 1 p.m.                              | *a la una* + *de la tarde*                   |
| Ceno a las ocho y salgo a las nueve.               | I have dinner at eight and go out at nine.         | two clock times in one sentence               |
| Trabajo de nueve de la mañana a cinco de la tarde. | I work from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.                       | a.m./p.m. range                               |

## Quick Time Check-Ins

Short, natural exchanges people actually have about time.

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                            |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| —¿Qué hora tienes? —Son las siete menos cuarto.    | "What time do you have?" "It's 6:45."          | *menos cuarto*                             |
| Ya es tarde, son casi las once.                    | It's already late, it's almost eleven.         | *casi* + time                               |
| Todavía es temprano, apenas son las tres.          | It's still early, it's only three.             | *apenas* + time                              |
| Nos vemos a eso de las cinco.                      | See you around five.                            | *a eso de*, approximate time                 |
