# Calendar Vocabulary — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About Days of the Week

Everyday plans tied to specific days.

| Spanish                          | English                            | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Hoy es miércoles.                      | Today is Wednesday.                        | lowercase day name                    |
| El examen es el viernes.               | The exam is on Friday.                     | *el* + specific day                   |
| Los martes tengo clase de yoga.        | On Tuesdays I have yoga class.             | *los* + day, habitual                 |
| Nos vemos el domingo, ¿va?             | See you Sunday, sound good?                | *el* + specific day                   |

## Talking About Months

Sentences about events tied to a particular month.

| Spanish                            | English                                | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Mi cumpleaños es en abril.                  | My birthday is in April.                        | lowercase month                        |
| Las vacaciones empiezan en diciembre.       | Vacation starts in December.                     | lowercase month                        |
| Nací en el mes de septiembre.               | I was born in the month of September.            | lowercase month                        |
| En febrero hace mucho frío aquí.            | In February it's very cold here.                 | lowercase month                        |

## Giving Full Dates

The complete date formula in real sentences.

| Spanish                                       | English                                     | Notes                          |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Mi cita es el 8 de junio.                            | My appointment is June 8th.                          | date formula                            |
| Nací el 22 de noviembre de 1995.                     | I was born on November 22nd, 1995.                    | full date formula                       |
| La boda es el primero de mayo.                       | The wedding is on May 1st.                            | *el primero* for the 1st                |
| El contrato empieza el 15 de enero de 2027.          | The contract starts on January 15th, 2027.            | full date formula                       |

## Asking About the Day or Date

Common questions when scheduling or confirming plans.

| Spanish                                     | English                                  | Notes                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| ¿Qué día es hoy?                                    | What day is it today?                              | asking the day of the week                    |
| ¿Cuál es la fecha límite?                           | What's the deadline date?                          | *cuál es la fecha*, specific use              |
| ¿Qué día cae tu cumpleaños este año?               | What day does your birthday fall on this year?     | combining day + date concepts                  |
| ¿Para cuándo es la entrega?                        | When is the deadline for the delivery?              | related scheduling question                    |

## Scheduling and Recurring Events

Real sentences about routines and repeating plans by day or date.

| Spanish                                        | English                                    | Notes                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Tenemos reunión todos los lunes.                        | We have a meeting every Monday.                      | *todos los* + day                        |
| El pago vence el 5 de cada mes.                         | The payment is due the 5th of every month.           | recurring monthly date                    |
| La clase es los martes y jueves.                        | The class is on Tuesdays and Thursdays.               | multiple days combined                     |
| El evento se repite cada 3 de octubre.                  | The event happens every October 3rd.                  | recurring yearly date                      |
