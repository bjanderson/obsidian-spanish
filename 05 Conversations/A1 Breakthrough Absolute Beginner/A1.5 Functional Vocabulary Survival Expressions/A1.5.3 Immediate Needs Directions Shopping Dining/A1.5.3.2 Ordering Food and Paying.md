# Ordering Food and Asking for the Check — Conversational Sentences

## Opening the Order

The waiter's questions and how a customer typically responds.

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Buenas noches, ¿ya saben qué van a pedir?          | Good evening, do you know what you'd like to order? | server opening, formal            |
| ¿Qué le gustaría de tomar?                        | What would you like to drink?                       | server asking, formal                  |
| Quisiera empezar con una sopa.                    | I'd like to start with a soup.                       | *quisiera*, polite ordering            |
| Para mí, el especial del día, por favor.          | For me, the special of the day, please.              | *para mí*, group order                 |

## Asking for Recommendations

Getting the server's input before deciding.

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| ¿Qué me recomienda de plato fuerte?               | What do you recommend for a main course?            | *usted* form, service context           |
| ¿Cuál es la especialidad de la casa?              | What's the house specialty?                          | asking about the menu                   |
| ¿Este plato es picante?                           | Is this dish spicy?                                  | clarifying before ordering              |
| ¿Tiene algo sin gluten?                           | Do you have something gluten-free?                   | dietary question                        |

## Mid-Meal Requests

Polite requests that come up during the meal itself.

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| ¿Me trae más pan, por favor?                      | Could you bring me more bread, please?              | *me trae*, mid-meal request             |
| ¿Nos puede traer otra ronda de bebidas?           | Could you bring us another round of drinks?         | group request                           |
| Disculpe, esto no es lo que pedí.                 | Excuse me, this isn't what I ordered.                | polite correction                        |
| Está delicioso, gracias.                          | It's delicious, thank you.                            | complimenting the food                   |

## Closing the Meal

Asking for and settling the bill.

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| La cuenta, por favor.                             | The check, please.                                   | standard closing phrase                 |
| ¿Me trae la cuenta cuando pueda?                  | Could you bring the check whenever you can?         | more relaxed phrasing                    |
| ¿Está incluida la propina?                        | Is the tip included?                                 | clarifying tipping                       |
| ¿Aceptan tarjeta de crédito?                      | Do you accept credit cards?                          | payment method question                  |

## Splitting the Bill and Wrapping Up

Common phrases for group dining and leaving.

| Spanish                                       | English                                      | Notes                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| ¿Podemos dividir la cuenta?                                | Can we split the check?                                  | group payment question                         |
| Yo invito esta vez.                                        | This one's on me.                                         | offering to pay                                |
| Todo estuvo muy rico, gracias por la recomendación.        | Everything was delicious, thanks for the recommendation. | closing pleasantry                              |
| Nos vemos pronto, gracias por todo.                        | See you soon, thanks for everything.                      | leaving the restaurant                          |
