# Simple Directional Vocabulary — Conversational Sentences

## Asking How to Get Somewhere

Common openers when you're lost or need a route.

| Spanish                                    | English                                    | Notes                            |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Disculpe, ¿cómo llego al centro?                  | Excuse me, how do I get downtown?                 | *cómo llego a*, formal                        |
| ¿Me puede decir cómo llegar a la plaza?           | Can you tell me how to get to the square?         | polite, formal request                        |
| ¿Dónde queda la parada de taxis?                  | Where's the taxi stand located?                    | *dónde queda*                                 |
| Perdón, estoy perdido, ¿me ayuda?                 | Sorry, I'm lost, can you help me?                  | casual request for help                        |

## Giving Turn-by-Turn Directions

Real instructions strung together the way a local would give them.

| Spanish                                              | English                                          | Notes                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Siga todo derecho hasta el semáforo.                        | Go straight ahead until the traffic light.               | *siga* + *derecho*                            |
| Gire a la derecha en la segunda calle.                      | Turn right on the second street.                          | *gire* + *a la derecha*                       |
| Doble a la izquierda después del puente.                    | Turn left after the bridge.                                | *doble* + *a la izquierda*                    |
| Siga derecho dos cuadras y luego gire a la derecha.         | Go straight two blocks and then turn right.                | combined multi-step instruction               |

## Describing Where Something Is

Using landmarks and relative position to explain a location.

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Está al lado del banco.                                  | It's next to the bank.                               | *al lado del*                                  |
| Queda enfrente de la plaza principal.                    | It's across from the main square.                     | *enfrente de*                                  |
| Está cerca del mercado, en la esquina.                   | It's near the market, on the corner.                  | *cerca del* + *en la esquina*                  |
| Queda lejos de aquí, mejor toma un taxi.                 | It's far from here, better take a taxi.                | *lejos de*                                      |

## Estimating Distance

Practical distance phrases for everyday navigation.

| Spanish                                    | English                                    | Notes                              |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Está a cinco cuadras de aquí.                     | It's five blocks from here.                        | number + *cuadras* + *de*                     |
| Queda a diez minutos caminando.                   | It's ten minutes on foot.                          | walking distance                               |
| Está muy cerca, casi enfrente.                    | It's very close, almost right across.              | *muy cerca* + *casi*                           |
| Es un poco lejos, mejor toma el bus.               | It's a bit far, better take the bus.                | *un poco lejos*                                |

## Confirming You Understood the Directions

Follow-up checks that come up naturally after receiving directions.

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Entonces, ¿todo derecho y luego a la izquierda?          | So, straight ahead and then left?                    | confirming the instructions                    |
| ¿Está muy lejos de aquí caminando?                       | Is it very far from here on foot?                    | follow-up distance question                    |
| Perfecto, muchas gracias por la ayuda.                   | Perfect, thank you very much for the help.            | closing the exchange                            |
| ¿Hay alguna señal o algo para reconocer el lugar?        | Is there a sign or something to recognize the place?  | asking for a landmark                           |
