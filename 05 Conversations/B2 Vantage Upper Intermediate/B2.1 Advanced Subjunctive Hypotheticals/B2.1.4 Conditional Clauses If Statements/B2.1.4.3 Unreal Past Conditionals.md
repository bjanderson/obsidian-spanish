# Unreal Past Conditionals (Si + Pluperfect Subjunctive) — Conversational Sentences

## Reflecting on Missed Opportunities

Everyday sentences imagining a different outcome for something already over.

| Spanish                                                | English                                                | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Si hubiera sabido la verdad, te lo habría dicho.             | If I had known the truth, I would have told you.               | pluperfect subjunctive → conditional perfect |
| Si hubiéramos salido antes, no habríamos perdido el vuelo.   | If we had left earlier, we wouldn't have missed the flight.      | pluperfect subjunctive → conditional perfect   |
| Si hubiera aceptado esa oferta, mi vida sería distinta.      | If I had accepted that offer, my life would be different.         | pluperfect subjunctive → conditional perfect     |
