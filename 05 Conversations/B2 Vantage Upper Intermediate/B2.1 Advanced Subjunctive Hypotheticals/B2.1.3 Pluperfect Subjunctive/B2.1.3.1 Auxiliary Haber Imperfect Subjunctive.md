# Auxiliary Haber in Imperfect Subjunctive + Past Participle — Conversational Sentences

## Hoping Something Had Already Happened

Everyday sentences hoping an earlier action was already complete by some past point.

| Spanish                                            | English                                             | Notes                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Esperaba que hubieras llegado antes de la reunión.   | I was hoping you had arrived before the meeting.        | *hubieras* + *llegado*                  |
| Esperaba que ya hubieran empacado las maletas.       | I was hoping they had already packed the bags.            | *hubieran* + *empacado*                   |
| Ojalá hubiéramos salido más temprano.                | I wish we had left earlier.                                  | *hubiéramos* + *salido*                     |

## Doubting Something Had Already Happened

Everyday sentences doubting whether an earlier action was really complete.

| Spanish                                            | English                                             | Notes                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Dudaba que hubieran terminado antes del plazo.       | I doubted they had finished before the deadline.          | *hubieran* + *terminado*                  |
| No creía que él hubiera leído el contrato completo.  | I didn't believe he had read the whole contract.            | *hubiera* + *leído*                         |
| Dudábamos que hubieras hecho eso tú solo.            | We doubted you had done that all by yourself.                | *hubieras* + *hecho*, irregular               |

## Reacting to Something That Had Already Happened

Everyday sentences reacting emotionally to a past-before-past event.

| Spanish                                            | English                                             | Notes                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Me alegró que ya hubiéramos resuelto el problema.    | I was glad we had already solved the problem.               | *hubiéramos* + *resuelto*                    |
| Sentí mucho que no hubieras visto la exposición.     | I felt bad that you hadn't seen the exhibit.                  | *hubieras* + *visto*                           |
| Fue una sorpresa que hubieran cambiado los planes.   | It was a surprise that they had changed the plans.              | *hubieran* + *cambiado*                          |

## Describing a Two-Step Past

Sentences showing the earlier action happening before another past moment.

| Spanish                                            | English                                             | Notes                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Para cuando llegamos, esperaba que ya hubieran comido. | By the time we arrived, I was hoping they had already eaten. | pluperfect subjunctive, two-step past |
| Antes de la boda, dudaba que hubieran hecho las paces.  | Before the wedding, I doubted they had made peace.          | pluperfect subjunctive, two-step past   |

## Irregular Participles with Hubiera

Sentences highlighting common irregular past participles in this construction.

| Spanish                                            | English                                             | Notes                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Ojalá hubiera dicho la verdad desde el principio.     | I wish I had told the truth from the beginning.             | *dicho*, irregular participle           |
| No creía que hubieran puesto tanto esfuerzo.          | I didn't think they had put in so much effort.                | *puesto*, irregular participle            |
| Dudaba que hubiera escrito esa carta él mismo.        | I doubted he had written that letter himself.                   | *escrito*, irregular participle             |
