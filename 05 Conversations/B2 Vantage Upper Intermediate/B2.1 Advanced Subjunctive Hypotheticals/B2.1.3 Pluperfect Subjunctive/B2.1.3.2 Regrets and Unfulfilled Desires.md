# Expressing Unfulfilled Past Desires and Regrets — Conversational Sentences

## Regretting Missed Opportunities

Everyday sentences wishing a missed chance had gone differently.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Ojalá hubiera estudiado más para el examen.          | I wish I had studied more for the exam.                  | *ojalá* + pluperfect subjunctive       |
| Ojalá hubiéramos aceptado esa oferta de trabajo.     | I wish we had accepted that job offer.                     | *ojalá* + pluperfect subjunctive         |
| Ojalá hubiera aprendido a tocar guitarra de joven.   | I wish I had learned to play guitar when I was young.        | *ojalá* + pluperfect subjunctive           |

## Wishing Someone Had Been There

Everyday sentences wishing another person had shared an experience.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Ojalá hubieras venido a la fiesta anoche.            | I wish you had come to the party last night.               | *ojalá* + pluperfect subjunctive       |
| Cómo me gustaría que hubieras conocido a mi abuela.  | How I wish you had met my grandmother.                       | regret expression + pluperfect subjunctive |
| Ojalá hubieran estado con nosotros ese día.          | I wish they had been with us that day.                         | *ojalá* + pluperfect subjunctive           |

## Wishing Something Bad Hadn't Happened

Everyday sentences wishing an unfortunate event hadn't occurred.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Ojalá no hubiéramos perdido el vuelo.                | I wish we hadn't missed the flight.                         | *ojalá* + pluperfect subjunctive         |

## Wishing You'd Known Something Sooner

Everyday sentences about information that arrived too late.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Si tan solo hubiera sabido la verdad antes.          | If only I had known the truth sooner.                       | regret expression + pluperfect subjunctive |
| Ojalá me hubieran avisado con tiempo.                | I wish they had let me know in time.                           | *ojalá* + pluperfect subjunctive           |
| Ojalá hubiera preguntado antes de decidir.           | I wish I had asked before deciding.                              | *ojalá* + pluperfect subjunctive             |

## Comparing Still-Possible vs. Already-Too-Late Wishes

Pairs contrasting a wish that could still happen with one that's already too late.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Ojalá tuviera más tiempo. → Ojalá hubiera tenido más tiempo. | I wish I had more time. → I wish I had had more time. | still possible vs. too late |
| Ojalá pudiera ir. → Ojalá hubiera podido ir.         | I wish I could go. → I wish I could have gone.               | still possible vs. too late      |
