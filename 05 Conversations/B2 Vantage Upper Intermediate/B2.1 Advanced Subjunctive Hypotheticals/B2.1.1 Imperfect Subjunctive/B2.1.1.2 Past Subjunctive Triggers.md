# Past Subjunctive Triggers — Conversational Sentences

## Wishing for Something with Ojalá

Everyday sentences wishing for something unlikely or contrary to reality right now.

| Spanish                                            | English                                       | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Ojalá tuviera más tiempo libre.                       | I wish I had more free time.                          | *ojalá* + imperfect subjunctive |
| Ojalá pudiéramos vernos más seguido.                  | I wish we could see each other more often.             | *ojalá* + imperfect subjunctive   |
| Ojalá no lloviera tanto esta semana.                  | I wish it weren't raining so much this week.            | *ojalá* + imperfect subjunctive     |
| Ojalá estuvieras aquí para verlo.                     | I wish you were here to see it.                          | *ojalá* + imperfect subjunctive       |

## Reporting Past Wants and Requests

Everyday sentences about what someone wanted or asked for in the past.

| Spanish                                            | English                                       | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Mi mamá quería que llamara todos los domingos.        | My mom wanted me to call every Sunday.                 | *querer* trigger              |
| El profesor pidió que entregáramos la tarea a tiempo. | The teacher asked us to turn in the homework on time.   | *pedir* trigger                 |
| Mi jefe esperaba que resolviera el problema solo.     | My boss was hoping I would solve the problem alone.      | *esperar* trigger                 |
| Nos recomendaron que llegáramos temprano.             | They recommended that we arrive early.                    | *recomendar* trigger                |

## Describing Past Doubt or Uncertainty

Everyday sentences expressing that something wasn't believed or was uncertain in the past.

| Spanish                                            | English                                       | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Dudaba que fuera tan tarde ya.                        | I doubted it was already that late.                    | *dudar* trigger                  |
| No creíamos que hubiera tanta gente en la fiesta.     | We didn't believe there would be so many people at the party. | *no creer* trigger        |
| Era poco probable que llegáramos a tiempo.            | It was unlikely we would arrive on time.                 | impersonal expression                |

## Describing One Action Depending on Another (Past)

Everyday sentences where one past action had to happen before another.

| Spanish                                            | English                                       | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Le dije que me avisara en cuanto llegara.             | I told him to let me know as soon as he arrived.        | time-clause trigger, past narration     |
| Prometió llamarme una vez que terminara el trabajo.   | He promised to call me once he finished work.             | *una vez que* trigger                     |
| Esperé hasta que ella saliera de la reunión.          | I waited until she came out of the meeting.                | *hasta que* trigger                         |

## Using Antes de Que in Everyday Habits

Sentences showing that *antes de que* always takes the subjunctive, even for routines.

| Spanish                                            | English                                       | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Siempre cerraba la tienda antes de que oscureciera.   | I always closed the store before it got dark.           | *antes de que*, habitual, still subjunctive |
| Revisaba el correo antes de que empezara la reunión.  | I checked my email before the meeting started.            | *antes de que*, still subjunctive             |
| Siempre nos despedíamos antes de que saliera el tren. | We always said goodbye before the train left.              | *antes de que*, still subjunctive               |
