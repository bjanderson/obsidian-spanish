# Imperfect Subjunctive Conjugation — Conversational Sentences

## Talking about Past Wishes and Wants

Everyday sentences about what someone wanted or wished for in the past.

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Quería que me llamaras anoche.                       | I wanted you to call me last night.                  | *querer* + *llamaras*             |
| Mis papás querían que estudiáramos medicina.         | My parents wanted us to study medicine.               | *querer* + *estudiáramos*           |
| Esperaba que vinieras a la boda.                     | I was hoping you would come to the wedding.            | *esperar* + *vinieras*                |
| Ojalá tuviéramos más tiempo juntos.                  | I wish we had more time together.                       | *ojalá* + *tuviéramos*                  |

## Expressing Past Doubt or Disbelief

Everyday sentences about not believing something was true or possible.

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Dudaba que dijeras eso en serio.                     | I doubted you meant that seriously.                    | *dudar* + *dijeras*                |
| No creía que pudieran terminar tan rápido.           | I didn't believe they could finish so fast.             | *no creer* + *pudieran*              |
| Era imposible que llegáramos a tiempo.               | It was impossible for us to arrive on time.             | *ser imposible* + *llegáramos*         |
| Nadie pensaba que fuera tan difícil.                 | Nobody thought it would be so hard.                      | *pensar* + *fuera*                       |

## Giving Advice or Instructions in the Past

Everyday sentences about what someone asked or told another person to do.

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Me pidió que hiciera la tarea antes de salir.        | She asked me to do the homework before going out.       | *pedir* + *hiciera*                  |
| El doctor le recomendó que descansara más.           | The doctor recommended that he rest more.                | *recomendar* + *descansara*           |
| Nos dijeron que lleváramos paraguas.                 | They told us to bring umbrellas.                          | *decir* + *lleváramos*                  |
| Le sugerí que hablara con el jefe.                   | I suggested that she talk to the boss.                     | *sugerir* + *hablara*                     |

## Talking about Hypothetical Situations with Si

Sentences pairing the imperfect subjunctive with *si* to describe unreal or unlikely situations.

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Si tuviera más dinero, viajaría todo el año.         | If I had more money, I would travel all year.           | *si* + *tuviera*                   |
| Si supieras la verdad, me entenderías.               | If you knew the truth, you would understand me.          | *si* + *supieras*                    |
| Si viviéramos más cerca, nos veríamos más seguido.   | If we lived closer, we would see each other more often.  | *si* + *viviéramos*                    |
| Si pudieras cambiar algo, ¿qué cambiarías?           | If you could change something, what would you change?     | *si* + *pudieras*                        |

## Casual Everyday Exchanges

Common back-and-forth conversational uses.

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Actuaste como si no me conocieras.                   | You acted as if you didn't know me.                     | *como si* + *conocieras*                 |
| Ella habla como si fuera experta en el tema.         | She talks as if she were an expert on the subject.       | *como si* + *fuera*                        |
| Me trató como si yo tuviera la culpa.                | He treated me as if I were at fault.                       | *como si* + *tuviera*                        |
