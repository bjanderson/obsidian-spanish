# Subjunctive Evaluation of Completed Past Actions — Conversational Sentences

## Checking In on Something That Should Be Done

Everyday sentences hoping a recent task or trip already wrapped up well.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Espero que hayas llegado bien a casa.                | I hope you got home okay.                              | hope, finished action             |
| Espero que hayan tenido un buen viaje.               | I hope you all had a good trip.                          | hope, finished action               |
| Ojalá hayas encontrado lo que buscabas.              | I hope you found what you were looking for.                | hope, finished action                 |

## Reacting to Recent News with Joy or Relief

Everyday sentences responding warmly to something that just happened.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Me alegra que hayas conseguido el trabajo.           | I'm glad you got the job.                               | emotional reaction, joy            |
| Qué alivio que hayan encontrado las llaves.          | What a relief that they found the keys.                   | emotional reaction, relief           |
| Me da gusto que hayamos terminado a tiempo.          | I'm happy that we finished on time.                          | emotional reaction, joy                |

## Reacting to Recent News with Regret or Sympathy

Everyday sentences expressing that something recent was unfortunate.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Siento que hayas tenido tantos problemas hoy.        | I'm sorry you've had so many problems today.              | emotional reaction, sympathy         |
| Es una lástima que no hayan podido venir a la cena.  | It's a shame you weren't able to come to dinner.            | value judgment, regret                 |
| Qué pena que se te haya olvidado la cita.            | What a shame that you forgot the appointment.                | value judgment, regret                   |

## Doubting Whether Something Already Happened

Everyday sentences questioning whether a recent action really took place.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Dudo que ya hayan terminado el proyecto.             | I doubt they've finished the project yet.                | doubt, finished action              |
| No creo que él haya leído todo el correo.            | I don't think he read the entire email.                    | doubt, finished action                |
| Dudo que hayamos hecho suficiente esta semana.       | I doubt we've done enough this week.                          | doubt, finished action                  |

## Making a Value Judgment about Recent Events

Everyday sentences evaluating a recent action as good, strange, or notable.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Qué bueno que hayas decidido quedarte.               | It's great that you decided to stay.                     | value judgment, positive            |
| Qué extraño que no haya llamado todavía.             | It's strange that he hasn't called yet.                    | value judgment, curious               |
| Es normal que te hayas sentido nervioso al principio.| It's normal that you felt nervous at first.                  | value judgment, neutral                 |
