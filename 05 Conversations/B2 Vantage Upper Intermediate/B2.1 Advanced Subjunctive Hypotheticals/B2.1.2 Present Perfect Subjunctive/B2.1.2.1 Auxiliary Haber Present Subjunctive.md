# Auxiliary Haber in Present Subjunctive + Past Participle — Conversational Sentences

## Hoping Something Recent Happened

Everyday sentences hoping a recent action has already taken place.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Espero que hayas dormido bien.                       | I hope you slept well.                                | *hayas* + *dormido*                 |
| Espero que hayan llegado sin problemas.              | I hope they arrived without any problems.               | *hayan* + *llegado*                   |
| Ojalá hayas descansado este fin de semana.           | I hope you rested this weekend.                            | *hayas* + *descansado*                  |

## Expressing Doubt about a Recent Action

Everyday sentences doubting whether something recent already happened.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Dudo que hayan terminado el proyecto ya.             | I doubt they've finished the project already.           | *hayan* + *terminado*                 |
| No creo que ella haya visto el mensaje todavía.      | I don't think she has seen the message yet.               | *haya* + *visto*, irregular             |
| Dudo que él haya llegado tan rápido.                 | I doubt he arrived that quickly.                            | *haya* + *llegado*                        |

## Reacting Emotionally to Something Recent

Everyday sentences reacting with feeling to a recently completed action.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Me alegra que hayas conseguido el trabajo.           | I'm glad you got the job.                              | *hayas* + *conseguido*               |
| Qué bueno que hayan resuelto el problema.            | It's great that they've solved the problem.              | *hayan* + *resuelto*                   |
| Siento que hayas tenido un mal día.                  | I'm sorry you've had a bad day.                            | *hayas* + *tenido*                       |

## Saying Something Is Possible or Uncertain

Everyday sentences using impersonal expressions with a recent completed action.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Es posible que Marta ya haya salido.                 | It's possible Marta has already left.                    | *haya* + *salido*                    |
| Puede ser que hayan cambiado los planes.             | It could be that they've changed the plans.                | *hayan* + *cambiado*                    |
| Es probable que haya hecho frío toda la semana.      | It's likely it's been cold all week.                          | *haya* + *hecho*, irregular              |

## Irregular Participles in Everyday Speech

Sentences highlighting common irregular past participles in this construction.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Espero que hayas dicho la verdad.                    | I hope you told the truth.                              | *dicho*, irregular participle          |
| Dudo que haya escrito esa carta él solo.             | I doubt he wrote that letter by himself.                  | *escrito*, irregular participle          |
| Ojalá hayan puesto la mesa antes de que lleguen.     | I hope they've set the table before they arrive.            | *puesto*, irregular participle             |
