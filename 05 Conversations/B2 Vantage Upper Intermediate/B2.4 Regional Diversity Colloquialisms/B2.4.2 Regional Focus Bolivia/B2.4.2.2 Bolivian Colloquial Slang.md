# Bolivian-Specific Colloquial Vocabulary — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About a Hangover

Used the morning after drinking too much.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Tengo un ch'aki tremendo hoy. | I have a terrible hangover today. | Very common casual complaint |
| No puedo ni hablar, tengo ch'aki. | I can barely talk, I have a hangover. | Exaggerated for emphasis |
| ¿Otra vez con ch'aki? Deberías tomar menos. | Hungover again? You should drink less. | Teasing a friend |

## Agreeing or Wrapping Up with "Ya Pues"

Used to agree, close a topic, or add mild emphasis.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Nos vemos a las ocho? — Ya pues. | Shall we meet at eight? — Alright then. | Simple agreement |
| Ya pues, hagámoslo a tu manera. | Fine then, let's do it your way. | Conceding a point |
| Bueno, ya pues, no hay más que discutir. | Well, that settles it, nothing more to discuss. | Closing a conversation |

## Talking About Regional Identity

Used to describe where someone in Bolivia is from.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Ella es choca, nació en Santa Cruz. | She's from the lowlands, born in Santa Cruz. | Regional identity, not an insult |
| Mi papá es colla, de La Paz. | My dad is from the highlands, from La Paz. | Counterpart term to choco/a |
| Acá hay mucha mezcla entre collas y chocos. | There's a lot of mixing here between highlanders and lowlanders. | Describing cultural blending |

## Describing Everyday Frustrations and Reactions

Used for common casual complaints or reactions in daily Bolivian speech.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Ya pues, no seas necio y avisa cuando llegues. | Come on, don't be stubborn, let me know when you arrive. | Mild scolding tone |
| Con este ch'aki no puedo ni desayunar. | With this hangover I can't even eat breakfast. | Combining with daily routine |
| Los chocos tienen fama de ser muy alegres. | People from the lowlands have a reputation for being very cheerful. | Cultural generalization, said casually |
