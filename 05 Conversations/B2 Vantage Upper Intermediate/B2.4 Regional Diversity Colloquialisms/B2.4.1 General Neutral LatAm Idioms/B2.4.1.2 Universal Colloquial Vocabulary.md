# Universal Colloquial Vocabulary across Latin America — Conversational Sentences

## Reacting with the Widely-Understood Option

Used when you want to be understood no matter which country you're in.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¡Qué chévere! No sabía que ibas a venir. | How cool! I didn't know you were coming. | Safe choice almost anywhere in Latin America |
| Tu casa está bien chévere. | Your house is really cool. | Modifying a noun with *bien* |
| Me parece chévere la idea. | I think the idea is cool. | Standalone opinion |

## Complimenting Something, Mexico-Style

Used casually with Mexican friends or when describing something from Mexico.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Ese concierto estuvo bien chido. | That concert was really cool. | Common with friends, informal |
| Tu chamarra está chida. | Your jacket is cool. | *Chamarra* is itself a Mexican-Spanish word for jacket |
| Qué chido que ganaste el partido. | How cool that you won the game. | Reacting to good news |

## Complimenting Something, Argentina-Style

Used casually in Argentina or with Argentine friends.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| La película estuvo copada. | The movie was great. | Common informal praise |
| Che, ese lugar es bárbaro. | Hey, that place is awesome. | *Che* is a common Argentine filler word for addressing someone |
| Fue una fiesta copada. | It was a cool party. | Modifying a noun |

## Talking About Feeling Good, Costa Rica-Style

Used broadly, not just for "cool" — closer to "all good" or a greeting.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Cómo estás? — Pura vida, gracias. | How are you? — All good, thanks. | Common response to a greeting |
| Todo salió bien, pura vida. | Everything turned out fine, all good. | Wrapping up a story positively |
| Pura vida, no te preocupes. | It's all good, don't worry. | Reassuring someone |

## Reacting to Colombian and Peruvian Slang

Used with Colombian or Peruvian friends, or when describing something from those countries.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¡Qué nota tu nuevo carro! | How cool, your new car! | Colombia-specific |
| Ese plan suena bien bacán. | That plan sounds really cool. | Peru-specific |
| Estuvo bien bacán la excursión. | The trip was really cool. | Peru-specific |
