# Structure: Se + Indirect Pronoun + Verb + Subject — Conversational Sentences

## Forgetting Things

Everyday sentences about forgetting something, framed as an accident.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Se me olvidó traer el paraguas.                      | I forgot to bring the umbrella.                            | *se me* + *olvidó*                  |
| Se le olvidó llamar a su mamá.                       | He forgot to call his mom.                                   | *se le* + *olvidó*                    |
| Se nos olvidó comprar leche.                         | We forgot to buy milk.                                         | *se nos* + *olvidó*                     |

## Dropping or Breaking Things

Everyday sentences about accidentally dropping or breaking something.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Se me cayó el teléfono en la calle.                  | I dropped my phone on the street.                          | *se me* + *cayó*                    |
| Se le rompió el vaso lavando los platos.             | He broke the glass washing dishes.                           | *se le* + *rompió*                    |
| Se nos cayeron las bolsas de compras.                | We dropped the shopping bags.                                  | *se nos* + *cayeron*                    |

## Losing Things

Everyday sentences about losing something without meaning to.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Se me perdió el paraguas en el bus.                  | I lost my umbrella on the bus.                             | *se me* + *perdió*                  |
| A ella se le perdió el anillo en la playa.           | She lost her ring at the beach.                              | *se le* + *perdió*, clarified with *a ella* |
| Se nos perdieron las llaves del carro.               | We lost the car keys.                                          | *se nos* + *perdieron*                  |

## Running Out of Things

Everyday sentences about something running out unexpectedly.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Se nos acabó la gasolina en la carretera.            | We ran out of gas on the highway.                          | *se nos* + *acabó*                    |
| Se le acabaron las ideas a mitad del proyecto.       | He ran out of ideas halfway through the project.               | *se le* + *acabaron*, clarified          |
| Se me acabó la paciencia esta tarde.                 | I ran out of patience this afternoon.                          | *se me* + *acabó*                       |

## Clarifying Who with A + Person

Everyday sentences using *a + name* to clarify an ambiguous *le/les*.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| A Javier se le rompieron los audífonos.              | Javier's headphones broke.                                | *a Javier* + *se le* clarifies who       |
| A mis papás se les olvidó la cita.                   | My parents forgot the appointment.                          | *a mis papás* + *se les* clarifies who     |
