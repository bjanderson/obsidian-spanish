# Form and Agreement of Canonical Passive Voice — Conversational Sentences

## Talking about News and Announcements

Sentences describing events reported formally, as in news or official statements.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El nuevo hospital fue inaugurado por el gobernador.  | The new hospital was inaugurated by the governor.        | *fue* + *inaugurado* (masc. sg.)   |
| La ley fue aprobada por el congreso ayer.            | The law was approved by congress yesterday.                | *fue* + *aprobada* (fem. sg.)        |
| Los resultados serán anunciados mañana.              | The results will be announced tomorrow.                     | future tense passive                   |

## Talking about Art and Creative Work

Sentences crediting who made a book, painting, building, or similar work.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Esa novela fue escrita por un autor colombiano.      | That novel was written by a Colombian author.              | *fue* + *escrita* (fem. sg.)          |
| El mural fue pintado por artistas locales.           | The mural was painted by local artists.                       | *fue* + *pintado* (masc. sg.)           |
| El edificio fue diseñado por una arquitecta famosa.  | The building was designed by a famous architect.                | *fue* + *diseñado* (masc. sg.)            |

## Describing Institutional Processes

Sentences describing how something is handled by an organization or process.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El premio es otorgado cada año por la universidad.   | The prize is awarded every year by the university.         | present tense passive                 |
| Las solicitudes son revisadas por un comité.         | The applications are reviewed by a committee.                | present tense passive                   |
| Los productos son inspeccionados antes de enviarlos. | The products are inspected before being sent.                  | present tense passive, no *por* phrase    |

## Describing Historical Events

Sentences narrating past events in a formal, written style.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El tratado fue firmado en 1848.                      | The treaty was signed in 1848.                            | *fue* + *firmado*, no *por* phrase     |
| La ciudad fue fundada por colonos españoles.         | The city was founded by Spanish colonists.                   | *fue* + *fundada* (fem. sg.)             |
| El monumento fue construido hace más de cien años.   | The monument was built more than a hundred years ago.          | *fue* + *construido*, no *por* phrase      |

## Comparing Active and Passive Versions

Pairs showing the same event described actively and passively.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El chef preparó la cena. → La cena fue preparada por el chef. | The chef prepared dinner. → Dinner was prepared by the chef. | active vs. passive                |
| El comité decidirá el ganador. → El ganador será decidido por el comité. | The committee will decide the winner. → The winner will be decided by the committee. | active vs. passive |
