# Register Appropriateness (Formal vs. Spoken Passive) — Conversational Sentences

## Everyday Talk about Household Tasks

Casual sentences about chores using the natural spoken alternative.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Se lavó la ropa esta mañana.                         | The laundry got washed this morning.                     | spoken alternative, passive *se*     |
| Se limpió la cocina antes de la cena.                | The kitchen got cleaned before dinner.                     | spoken alternative, passive *se*       |
| Mi hermano arregló el carro ayer.                    | My brother fixed the car yesterday.                          | spoken alternative, active voice         |

## Casual News Recap Between Friends

Casual sentences recapping news the way a friend would say it out loud.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Cancelaron el concierto por la lluvia.               | They canceled the concert because of the rain.             | spoken alternative, unspecified plural  |
| Se resolvió el problema al final.                    | The problem got solved in the end.                          | spoken alternative, passive *se*          |
| Subieron los precios otra vez.                       | Prices went up again.                                          | spoken alternative, unspecified plural      |

## Formal Announcements

Formal-register sentences where the ser passive still sounds natural.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El nuevo horario será anunciado la próxima semana.   | The new schedule will be announced next week.              | *ser* passive, formal announcement    |
| La propuesta fue rechazada por el consejo.           | The proposal was rejected by the council.                    | *ser* passive, agent matters            |
| El acuerdo fue firmado por ambas partes.             | The agreement was signed by both parties.                      | *ser* passive, formal, agent matters       |

## Comparing the Same Idea in Two Registers

Pairs showing the formal *ser* passive next to its everyday spoken equivalent.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El puente fue construido en 1990. → Se construyó el puente en 1990. | The bridge was built in 1990. → They built the bridge in 1990. | formal vs. spoken |
| La carta fue enviada ayer. → Se envió la carta ayer. | The letter was sent yesterday. → The letter got sent yesterday. | formal vs. spoken |

## When the Agent Still Matters in Speech

Sentences showing the ser passive is still natural when who did it is the point.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El gol fue anotado por Messi.                        | The goal was scored by Messi.                             | *ser* passive, agent is the point   |
| El premio fue ganado por una estudiante de aquí.     | The prize was won by a student from here.                   | *ser* passive, agent is the point     |
