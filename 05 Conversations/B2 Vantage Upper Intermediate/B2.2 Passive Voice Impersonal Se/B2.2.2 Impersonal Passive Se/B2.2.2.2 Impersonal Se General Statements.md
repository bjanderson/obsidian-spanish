# Impersonal Se for General Statements — Conversational Sentences

## Talking about a Place or Restaurant

Everyday sentences describing general quality using impersonal se.

| Spanish                                | English                                      | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Se come muy bien en ese restaurante.      | The food is really good at that restaurant.       | impersonal *se*, general statement       |
| Se vive tranquilo en este barrio.         | Life is peaceful in this neighborhood.              | impersonal *se*, general statement         |
| Se duerme bien con este clima.            | You sleep well in this weather.                       | impersonal *se*, general statement           |

## Sharing Rumors or Common Knowledge

Everyday sentences sharing something "they say" or that's commonly known.

| Spanish                                | English                                      | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Se dice que va a subir el precio de la gasolina. | They say gas prices are going to go up.           | impersonal *se*, hearsay                 |
| Se dice que el nuevo jefe es muy exigente.       | They say the new boss is very demanding.             | impersonal *se*, hearsay                   |
| Se sabe que el tráfico empeora los viernes.      | It's known that traffic gets worse on Fridays.         | impersonal *se*, common knowledge            |

## Giving General Advice or Rules

Everyday sentences giving advice that applies to everyone in general.

| Spanish                                | English                                      | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Se necesita paciencia para aprender un idioma. | Learning a language takes patience.                | impersonal *se*, general advice          |

| Se debe llegar temprano a las entrevistas. | You should arrive early to interviews.                   | impersonal *se*, general advice              |

## Comparing Passive Se and Impersonal Se

Pairs showing the difference between agreement-based passive se and singular-only impersonal se.

| Spanish                                | English                                      | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Se venden casas. (passive se, plural verb) | Houses are sold.                                      | passive *se* vs. impersonal *se*         |
| Se vive bien aquí. (impersonal se, always singular) | Life is good here.                                     | passive *se* vs. impersonal *se*           |
