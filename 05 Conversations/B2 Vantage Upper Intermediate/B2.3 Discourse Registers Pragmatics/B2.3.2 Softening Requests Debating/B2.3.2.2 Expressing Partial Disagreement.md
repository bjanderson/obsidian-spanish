# Expressing Partial Agreement — Conversational Sentences

## Disagreeing Diplomatically in a Discussion

Everyday sentences pushing back gently in a conversation or debate.

| Spanish                                                     | English                                                | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Entiendo tu punto, pero yo lo veo diferente.                        | I understand your point, but I see it differently.          | *entiendo tu punto, pero...*              |
| Respeto tu opinión, pero no estoy de acuerdo.                       | I respect your opinion, but I don't agree.                    | respectful disagreement                     |
| Tienes razón en parte, pero falta considerar otras cosas.           | You're partly right, but other things need to be considered.    | *tienes razón en parte, pero...*              |

## Sharing a Personal Opinion Carefully

Everyday sentences framing an opinion as personal rather than absolute.

| Spanish                                                     | English                                                | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Desde mi perspectiva, esa regla no tiene sentido.                   | From my perspective, that rule doesn't make sense.           | *desde mi perspectiva*                    |
| Desde mi punto de vista, deberíamos esperar un poco más.            | From my point of view, we should wait a bit longer.            | *desde mi punto de vista*                   |
| A mi parecer, esa fue la mejor decisión.                            | In my opinion, that was the best decision.                       | opinion-softening phrase                      |

## Leaving Room for the Other Person's Idea

Everyday sentences acknowledging a possibility while still disagreeing.

| Spanish                                                     | English                                                | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Es posible, pero no creo que sea lo mejor.                          | That's possible, but I don't think it's the best option.       | *es posible, pero...*                     |
| Puede ser, pero yo tengo mis dudas.                                 | Maybe, but I have my doubts.                                       | softening a doubt                           |
| No lo había pensado así, pero tiene sentido.                        | I hadn't thought of it that way, but it makes sense.              | acknowledging a new perspective               |

## Presenting Both Sides of an Issue

Everyday sentences weighing two sides before landing on an opinion.

| Spanish                                                     | English                                                | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Por un lado entiendo la regla, por otro lado me parece injusta.     | On one hand I understand the rule, on the other hand it seems unfair to me. | *por un lado... por otro lado...*      |
| Por un lado es caro, pero por otro lado vale la pena.               | On one hand it's expensive, but on the other hand it's worth it.    | *por un lado... por otro lado...*         |

## Softening a Correction

Everyday sentences correcting someone gently instead of bluntly.

| Spanish                                                     | English                                                | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Creo que ahí hay un pequeño error, pero corrígeme si me equivoco.   | I think there's a small mistake there, but correct me if I'm wrong. | softening a correction                    |
| No estoy seguro de que eso sea correcto, pero puedo estar equivocado. | I'm not sure that's correct, but I could be wrong.               | softening a correction                      |
