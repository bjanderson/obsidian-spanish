# Adjusting Pronouns, Time, and Spatial References in Indirect Speech — Conversational Sentences

## Shifting Possessives and Pronouns

Everyday sentences adjusting "my/I" to "his/her" when reporting.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Sara dijo que iba a su casa después del trabajo.     | Sara said she was going to her house after work.          | possessive shift: *mi* → *su*        |
| Pedro dijo que ese libro era suyo.                   | Pedro said that book was his.                                | possessive shift: *mío* → *suyo*       |
| Marta dijo que le gustaba mucho su nuevo trabajo.    | Marta said she really liked her new job.                       | possessive shift: *mi* → *su*            |

## Shifting Time References

Everyday sentences adjusting "today/tomorrow/yesterday" when reporting later.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| María dijo que llegaba ese día por la tarde.         | María said she was arriving that day in the afternoon.       | time shift: *hoy* → *ese día*        |
| Juan dijo que me había visto el día anterior.        | Juan said he had seen me the day before.                       | time shift: *ayer* → *el día anterior* |
| Ana dijo que nos veríamos al día siguiente.          | Ana said we would see each other the next day.                  | time shift: *mañana* → *al día siguiente*  |

## Shifting Place References

Everyday sentences adjusting "here/there" when reporting from elsewhere.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Sara dijo que iba allí, no aquí.                     | Sara said she was going there, not here.                     | place shift: *aquí* → *allí*         |
| Luis dijo que vivía allí desde hacía años.           | Luis said he had lived there for years.                          | place shift: *aquí* → *allí*           |
| Elena dijo que había dejado las llaves ahí.          | Elena said she had left the keys there.                            | place shift: *aquí* → *ahí*              |

## Shifting Demonstratives

Everyday sentences adjusting "this" to "that" in reported speech.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Carlos dijo que esa camisa le gustaba mucho.         | Carlos said he really liked that shirt.                       | demonstrative shift: *esta* → *esa*       |
| Dijo que ese proyecto le había costado mucho trabajo. | He said that project had taken a lot of work.                  | demonstrative shift: *este* → *ese*         |

## Combining Multiple Shifts in One Sentence

Everyday sentences combining tense, pronoun, and reference shifts together.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Sara dijo que iría a su casa al día siguiente.       | Sara said she would go to her house the next day.            | tense + possessive + time shift       |
| Juan dijo que había dejado su mochila allí ese día.  | Juan said he had left his backpack there that day.              | tense + possessive + place + time shift |
