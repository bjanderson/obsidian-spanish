# Tense Shifts in Past Reporting — Conversational Sentences

## Reporting What Someone Said They Felt

Everyday sentences reporting an emotion or state (present → imperfect).

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Mi hermana dijo que estaba muy cansada.              | My sister said she was very tired.                        | present → imperfect                  |
| El profesor dijo que estaba contento con la clase.   | The teacher said he was happy with the class.               | present → imperfect                    |
| Mi jefe dijo que estaba muy ocupado hoy.             | My boss said he was very busy today.                         | present → imperfect                      |

## Reporting a Future Plan

Everyday sentences reporting a plan someone mentioned (future → conditional).

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Ella dijo que llegaría a las ocho.                   | She said she would arrive at eight.                        | future → conditional                 |
| Mi amigo dijo que me llamaría por la tarde.          | My friend said he would call me in the afternoon.            | future → conditional                   |
| Dijeron que terminarían el proyecto la próxima semana. | They said they would finish the project next week.            | future → conditional                     |

## Reporting Something That Already Happened

Everyday sentences reporting a completed past action.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Mi mamá dijo que había cocinado toda la mañana.      | My mom said she had cooked all morning.                     | preterite/present perfect → pluperfect   |
| Él dijo que ya había hablado con el gerente.         | He said he had already talked to the manager.                 | present perfect → pluperfect               |
| Dijo que había terminado el examen temprano.         | She said she had finished the exam early.                       | present perfect → pluperfect                 |

## Reporting a Request or Command

Everyday sentences reporting an instruction someone gave.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Me dijo que cerrara la puerta con cuidado.           | She told me to close the door carefully.                     | imperative → imperfect subjunctive       |
| El doctor me dijo que descansara unos días.          | The doctor told me to rest for a few days.                    | imperative → imperfect subjunctive         |
| Mi papá me dijo que no llegara tarde.                | My dad told me not to arrive late.                              | imperative → imperfect subjunctive           |

## Keeping the Present When Still True

Everyday sentences where the reported fact is still current.

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Me dijo que vive en Cochabamba.                      | She told me she lives in Cochabamba.                       | present kept, still true               |
| Dijo que trabaja desde casa ahora.                   | He said he works from home now.                              | present kept, still true                 |
