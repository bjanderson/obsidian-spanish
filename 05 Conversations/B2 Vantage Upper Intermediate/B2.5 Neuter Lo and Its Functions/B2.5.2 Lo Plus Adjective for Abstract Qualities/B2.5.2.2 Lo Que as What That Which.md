# Lo Que as "What/That Which" — Conversational Sentences

## Starting a Sentence with What You Like or Need

Used when the whole "lo que" clause is the subject of the sentence.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Lo que más me gusta de mi trabajo es la flexibilidad. | What I like most about my job is the flexibility. | lo que as subject |
| Lo que necesitamos es más tiempo. | What we need is more time. | lo que as subject |
| Lo que quiero es un poco de paz y tranquilidad. | What I want is some peace and quiet. | lo que as subject |

## Describing What Someone Does or Says

Used when "lo que" is the object of a verb like hacer or decir.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Siempre haces lo que quieres. | You always do what you want. | lo que as object of haces |
| No creo todo lo que dice la gente. | I don't believe everything people say. | lo que as object of dice |
| Ella hizo exactamente lo que le pedí. | She did exactly what I asked her to. | lo que as object of pedí |

## Expressing Not Understanding Something

Used to admit confusion about a situation or statement.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| No entiendo lo que pasó anoche. | I don't understand what happened last night. | lo que after entiendo |
| No sé lo que está pensando. | I don't know what he/she is thinking. | lo que after sé |
| No me queda claro lo que quieres decir. | It's not clear to me what you mean. | lo que after quedar claro |

## Reflecting on a Loss or Realization

Used for reflective or philosophical statements about experience.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Nunca valoras lo que tienes hasta que lo pierdes. | You never appreciate what you have until you lose it. | lo que as object of valoras |
| Aprendí lo que realmente importa en la vida. | I learned what really matters in life. | lo que as object of aprendí |
| A veces lo que parece un problema termina siendo una oportunidad. | Sometimes what seems like a problem ends up being an opportunity. | lo que as subject |
