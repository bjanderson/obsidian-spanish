# Forming Abstract Nouns with Lo + Adjective — Conversational Sentences

## Talking About the Good Side of Something

Used to highlight the positive aspect of a situation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Lo bueno de trabajar desde casa es que ahorro tiempo. | The good thing about working from home is that I save time. | lo bueno + de + infinitive |
| Lo mejor del viaje fue la comida. | The best part of the trip was the food. | lo mejor (irregular comparative adjective) |
| Lo positivo es que ya terminamos lo más difícil. | The positive thing is that we already finished the hardest part. | lo positivo as subject |

## Talking About the Bad or Hard Side of Something

Used to highlight the negative or challenging aspect of a situation.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Lo malo de vivir aquí es el tráfico. | The bad thing about living here is the traffic. | lo malo + de + infinitive |
| Lo difícil de este trabajo es la presión constante. | The hard part of this job is the constant pressure. | lo difícil + de + noun |
| Lo peor fue tener que despertarnos tan temprano. | The worst part was having to wake up so early. | lo peor (irregular comparative adjective) |

## Introducing a Surprising or Notable Fact

Used to draw attention to something unexpected.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Lo curioso es que nunca lo habíamos notado antes. | The curious thing is that we had never noticed it before. | lo curioso + que clause |
| Lo increíble es que lo logramos sin ayuda. | The incredible thing is that we managed it without help. | lo increíble + que clause |
| Lo extraño fue que nadie contestó el teléfono. | The strange thing was that no one answered the phone. | lo extraño + que clause |

## Using Lo + Adjective as the Whole Subject

Used when the abstract quality itself is the main subject of the sentence.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Lo importante es llegar a tiempo. | The important thing is to arrive on time. | lo importante as subject |
| Lo esencial es mantener la calma. | The essential thing is to stay calm. | lo esencial as subject |
| Lo primero es descansar bien. | The first thing is to rest well. | lo primero as subject |
