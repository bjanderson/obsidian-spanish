# Common Idiomatic Uses of Lo — Conversational Sentences

## Describing Distance or Duration With A Lo Largo De

Used to talk about something spanning a distance or period of time.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Hay varios cafés a lo largo de esta calle. | There are several cafés along this street. | a lo largo de + place |
| A lo largo del año, ahorré bastante dinero. | Throughout the year, I saved quite a bit of money. | a lo largo del (contraction with de + el) |
| A lo largo de su carrera, ha ganado muchos premios. | Throughout his/her career, he/she has won many awards. | a lo largo de + abstract noun |

## Noticing Something Far Away With A Lo Lejos

Used to describe something visible but distant.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Se veían las montañas a lo lejos. | The mountains could be seen in the distance. | a lo lejos |
| Escuché un ruido extraño a lo lejos. | I heard a strange noise in the distance. | a lo lejos |
| A lo lejos, se podía ver la tormenta acercándose. | In the distance, you could see the storm approaching. | a lo lejos, sentence-initial |

## Asking for Something Urgently With Lo Antes Posible

Used to request something be done quickly.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Necesito una respuesta lo antes posible. | I need an answer as soon as possible. | lo antes posible |
| Por favor, llámame lo antes posible. | Please call me as soon as possible. | lo antes posible |
| Vamos a resolver esto lo antes posible. | We're going to resolve this as soon as possible. | lo antes posible |

## Reporting Secondhand Information

Used to share something you've heard but aren't fully certain about, or referring to a known situation without spelling it out.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| A lo que parece, va a llover mañana. | Apparently, it's going to rain tomorrow. | a lo que parece |
| A lo que parece, el vuelo se retrasó. | Apparently, the flight was delayed. | a lo que parece |
| ¿Ya te contaron lo de la fiesta? | Did they already tell you about that whole party thing? | lo de + noun |
| Lo de ayer fue una locura total. | That whole thing yesterday was total madness. | lo de + time expression |
