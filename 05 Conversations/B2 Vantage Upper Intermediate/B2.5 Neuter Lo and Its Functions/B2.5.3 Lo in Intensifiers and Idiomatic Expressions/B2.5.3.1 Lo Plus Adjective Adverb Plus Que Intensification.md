# Lo + Adjective/Adverb + Que for Intensification — Conversational Sentences

## Giving Encouragement Regardless of Degree

Used to encourage someone by saying the degree of a quality doesn't change what they should do.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| No importa lo difícil que sea, puedes lograrlo. | No matter how hard it is, you can do it. | Subjunctive after no importa |
| No importa lo cansado que estés, ya casi terminamos. | No matter how tired you are, we're almost done. | Subjunctive after no importa |
| No importa lo nervioso que te sientas, todo va a salir bien. | No matter how nervous you feel, everything will be fine. | Subjunctive after no importa |

## Emphasizing Degree With Other Concessive Phrases

Used with similar expressions that also trigger the subjunctive.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Por muy ocupado que estés, deberías descansar un poco. | However busy you are, you should rest a little. | Subjunctive after por muy... que |
| Por más caro que sea, vale la pena comprarlo. | However expensive it is, it's worth buying. | Subjunctive after por más... que |

## Expressing Surprise at a Degree (Indicative)

Used to state a fact about how much of a quality something has, without discounting it.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Me sorprende lo rápido que aprendiste español. | I'm surprised how fast you learned Spanish. | Indicative — states a fact, no concessive expression |
| No puedo creer lo grande que está tu hijo. | I can't believe how big your son has gotten. | Indicative — states a fact |
| Me encanta lo tranquilo que es este pueblo. | I love how peaceful this town is. | Indicative — states a fact |

## Talking About How Something Was Done

Used with adverbs instead of adjectives, still following the same formula.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| No importa lo rápido que manejes, no vamos a llegar a tiempo. | No matter how fast you drive, we won't get there on time. | Adverb + subjunctive after no importa |
| Me sorprendió lo bien que salió todo. | I was surprised how well everything turned out. | Adverb + indicative, stating a fact |
