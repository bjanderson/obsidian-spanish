# Lo Replacing Masculine Nouns and Ideas — Conversational Sentences

## Replacing a Specific Masculine Noun

Used when answering a question about a masculine singular thing already mentioned.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Trajiste el paraguas? Sí, lo traje. | Did you bring the umbrella? Yes, I brought it. | Lo = el paraguas |
| ¿Ya viste el mensaje? Lo vi hace un rato. | Did you already see the message? I saw it a while ago. | Lo = el mensaje |
| ¿Dónde dejaste el celular? Lo dejé en la mesa. | Where did you leave your phone? I left it on the table. | Lo = el celular |

## Replacing a Person (Masculine)

Used when the direct object is a specific man or boy already mentioned.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Ya conociste a mi hermano? Lo conocí ayer. | Have you met my brother yet? I met him yesterday. | Lo = mi hermano |
| Busqué a Pedro toda la tarde, pero no lo encontré. | I looked for Pedro all afternoon, but I didn't find him. | Lo = Pedro |
| ¿Viste al profesor hoy? No, no lo vi. | Did you see the teacher today? No, I didn't see him. | Lo = al profesor |

## Replacing a Whole Action or Situation

Used when "lo" stands in for an entire event or accomplishment, not one noun.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Terminaste el informe a tiempo? Sí, lo hice. | Did you finish the report on time? Yes, I did. | Lo = the whole act of finishing on time |
| Ella ganó la competencia. Sí, lo hizo. | She won the competition. Yes, she did. | Lo = the whole idea of winning |
| ¿Arreglaste el problema? Ya lo arreglé. | Did you fix the problem? I already fixed it. | Lo = the whole situation of the problem |

## Combining With Other Sentence Elements

Used to show "lo" working naturally inside longer, more complex sentences.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| No lo puedo creer todavía. | I still can't believe it. | Lo before the conjugated verb puedo |
| Quiero hacerlo antes del viernes. | I want to do it before Friday. | Lo attached to the infinitive hacerlo |
| Nunca lo había visto tan feliz. | I had never seen him that happy. | Lo before the compound verb había visto |
