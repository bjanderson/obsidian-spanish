# Lo Referring Back to Previously Stated Adjectives/Concepts — Conversational Sentences

## Confirming a Quality Someone Mentioned

Used to agree that someone or something has a quality just described.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Tu jefe es exigente? Sí, lo es, bastante. | Is your boss demanding? Yes, he is, quite a bit. | Lo picks up exigente |
| ¿Está lista la comida? Sí, ya lo está. | Is the food ready? Yes, it already is. | Lo picks up lista |
| ¿Son caros esos zapatos? Sí, lo son, pero valen la pena. | Are those shoes expensive? Yes, they are, but they're worth it. | Lo stays the same despite plural adjective |

## Denying a Quality Someone Mentioned

Used to disagree that something has the quality mentioned.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Es difícil el examen? No, no lo es tanto. | Is the exam hard? No, it's not that hard. | Lo picks up difícil |
| ¿Están cansados los niños? No, no lo están todavía. | Are the kids tired? No, they're not yet. | Lo stays the same despite plural adjective |
| ¿Es peligroso ese barrio? No, ya no lo es. | Is that neighborhood dangerous? No, it isn't anymore. | Lo picks up peligroso |

## Talking About Manner With Lo

Used to confirm or deny how something is done.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Cocinas bien? Sí, lo hago, aunque no soy chef. | Do you cook well? Yes, I do, though I'm not a chef. | Lo picks up the adverb bien |
| ¿Manejas con cuidado? Sí, lo hago siempre. | Do you drive carefully? Yes, I always do. | Lo picks up con cuidado |

## Short Factual Answers Without Lo

Used when the question is a bare fact about profession, nationality, or identity.

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| ¿Eres ingeniero? Sí, soy. | Are you an engineer? Yes, I am. | Short factual statement, lo omitted |
| ¿Es peruana tu amiga? Sí, es. | Is your friend Peruvian? Yes, she is. | Short factual statement, lo omitted |
| ¿Es un buen ingeniero? Sí, lo es, de los mejores. | Is he a good engineer? Yes, he is, one of the best. | Lo returns once a quality is added |
