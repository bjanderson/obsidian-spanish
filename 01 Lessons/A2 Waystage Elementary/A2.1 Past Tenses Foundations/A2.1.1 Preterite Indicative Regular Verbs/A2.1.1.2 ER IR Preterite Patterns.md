## Explanation

You already know the preterite endings for *-AR* verbs. Good news: *-ER* and
*-IR* verbs share one single set of preterite endings between them — learn one
pattern, and it covers both groups at once.

Take *comer* (to eat). Drop the *-er* and you're left with the stem *com-*.
Add *-í* for *yo* and you get *comí* ("I ate"). Add *-iste* for *tú* and you get
*comiste* ("you ate"). Now take *vivir* (to live): drop the *-ir*, add the exact
same endings — *viví*, *viviste*, *vivió*... Same pattern, different stem.

The only place *-ER* and *-IR* verbs split apart is the *nosotros* form: *-ER*
verbs use *-imos* (*comimos*), and *-IR* verbs also use *-imos* (*vivimos*) — so
even there, they match. Once you know these six endings, every regular *-ER*
and *-IR* verb in the past falls into place.

As with *-AR* verbs, the *yo* and *él/ella/usted* forms carry a written accent:
*comí*, *comió*. And just like *hablamos*, the *-IR* *nosotros* form (*vivimos*)
is identical in the present and the preterite — only context tells them apart.

## Key Points / Conjugation / Rules

| Subject             | Ending | *comer*  | *vivir*  |
| -------------------- | ------ | -------- | -------- |
| yo                    | -í     | comí     | viví     |
| tú                    | -iste  | comiste  | viviste  |
| él / ella / usted     | -ió    | comió    | vivió    |
| nosotros              | -imos  | comimos  | vivimos  |
| ellos / ustedes       | -ieron | comieron | vivieron |

- Drop the *-er* or *-ir* from the infinitive to get the stem, then add the
  same set of preterite endings to either one.
- The *yo* and *él/ella/usted* endings (*-í*, *-ió*) always carry a written accent.
- The *-IR* *nosotros* form (*-imos*) is identical in the present and the
  preterite — only context tells them apart. The *-ER* *nosotros* form
  (*-imos*) is new in the preterite, so it doesn't cause the same confusion.
- As always, drop the subject pronoun once the ending makes the subject clear.

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                          | Notes                  |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Comí tacos en la calle.                | I ate tacos on the street.          | *comer*, *yo*             |
| ¿Viviste en Bolivia?                   | Did you live in Bolivia?            | *vivir*, *tú*             |
| Ella escribió una carta larga.         | She wrote a long letter.            | *escribir*, *ella*        |
| Aprendimos mucho en esa clase.         | We learned a lot in that class.     | *aprender*, *nosotros*    |
| Mis primos salieron temprano.          | My cousins left early.              | *salir*, *ellos*          |
| Bebí demasiado café esta mañana.       | I drank too much coffee this morning. | *beber*, *yo*            |

## Common Mistakes

- Using the *-AR* ending *-aste* instead of *-iste* for a *-ER*/*-IR* verb —
  *comiste*, not *comaste*.
- Dropping the accent on *comí* or *vivió* — without it, the form is misspelled.
- Forgetting that *-ER* and *-IR* verbs share the same preterite endings — there's
  no need to memorize two separate sets.
