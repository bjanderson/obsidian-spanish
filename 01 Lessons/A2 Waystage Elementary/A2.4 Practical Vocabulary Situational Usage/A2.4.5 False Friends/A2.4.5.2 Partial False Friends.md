## Explanation

Some false friends aren't total mismatches — the Spanish word shares a real but
narrower or shifted meaning with its English lookalike. These are harder to catch
than [[A2.4.5.1 Near-Total False Friends]] because the guessed meaning is often
close enough to sound right, but still wrong in context.

For each pair below, learn exactly where the Spanish meaning diverges from the
English one, and which Spanish word actually covers the English sense.

## Key Points / Conjugation / Rules

- _Actualmente_ means **currently / nowadays**, not "actually." "Actually" is
  _de hecho_ or _en realidad_.
- _Realizar_ means **to carry out / to accomplish** (a plan, a dream), not "to
  realize" (become aware). "To realize" is _darse cuenta de_.
- _Atender_ means **to attend to / to assist** (a customer, a patient), not "to
  attend" an event. "To attend" (an event/class) is _asistir a_.
- _Asistir (a)_ means **to attend** (an event), not "to assist" (help). "To
  assist/help" is _ayudar_.
- _Introducir_ means **to insert / to bring in** (a topic, an object), not "to
  introduce" a person. "To introduce someone" is _presentar_.
- _Discutir_ often means **to argue**, not just a neutral "discuss." For a
  calm, neutral discussion, use _hablar sobre_ or _conversar_.
- _Recordar_ means **to remember**, but also "to remind" someone — not "to
  record." "To record" (audio/video) is _grabar_.

## Examples

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Actualmente vivo en Guadalajara. | I currently live in Guadalajara. | Not "actually" — that's _de hecho_. |
| De hecho, no sabía que te habías mudado. | Actually, I didn't know you had moved. | Correct word for "actually." |
| Por fin realizó su sueño de viajar por Sudamérica. | He finally realized (fulfilled) his dream of traveling through South America. | Not "to realize" as in "become aware." |
| Me di cuenta de que había olvidado las llaves. | I realized I had forgotten the keys. | Correct word for "to realize" (become aware). |
| La recepcionista atendió a tres clientes seguidos. | The receptionist attended to three customers in a row. | Not "to attend" an event. |
| Voy a asistir a la boda de mi primo el sábado. | I'm going to attend my cousin's wedding on Saturday. | Correct word for "to attend" an event. |
| Les presenté a mi amiga a mis padres. | I introduced my friend to my parents. | Not _introducir_, which is for objects/topics. |
| No quiero discutir contigo sobre esto otra vez. | I don't want to argue with you about this again. | _Discutir_ usually implies conflict, not neutral discussion. |
