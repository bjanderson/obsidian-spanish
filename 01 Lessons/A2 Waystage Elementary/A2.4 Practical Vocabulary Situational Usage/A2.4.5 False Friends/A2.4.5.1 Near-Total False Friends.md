## Explanation

A false friend (_falso amigo_) is a Spanish word that looks or sounds like an English
word but has a different meaning. Near-total false friends are the riskiest kind: the
Spanish word shares almost no meaning with the English word it resembles. Learners
who guess the meaning from the English cognate end up saying something completely
wrong.

For each pair below, learn the Spanish word's real meaning first, then treat the
English lookalike as a warning sign, not a translation.

## Key Points / Conjugation / Rules

- _Embarazada_ means **pregnant**, not "embarrassed." "Embarrassed" is
  _avergonzado/a_ or _apenado/a_.
- _Éxito_ means **success**, not "exit." "Exit" is _salida_.
- _Sopa_ means **soup**, not "soap." "Soap" is _jabón_.
- _Carpeta_ means **folder** (for papers), not "carpet." "Carpet" is _alfombra_.
- _Librería_ means **bookstore**, not "library." "Library" is _biblioteca_.
- _Pie_ means **foot**, not "pie" (the dessert). "Pie" (dessert) is _pastel_ or
  _tarta_.
- _Ropa_ means **clothes**, not "rope." "Rope" is _cuerda_.
- _Molestar_ means **to bother** or **to annoy**, not "to molest" in the serious
  English sense. Use it freely for everyday annoyances: _No me molestes_ ("Don't
  bother me").

## Examples

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Mi hermana está embarazada. | My sister is pregnant. | Not "embarrassed" — that mistake is a classic beginner error. |
| El proyecto fue un gran éxito. | The project was a big success. | Not "exit" — _salida_ is the word for that. |
| Voy a preparar sopa de pollo. | I'm going to make chicken soup. | Not "soap" — _jabón_ is what you wash with. |
| Guardé los documentos en una carpeta. | I saved the documents in a folder. | Not "carpet" — _alfombra_ covers the floor. |
| Compré el libro en una librería cerca de mi casa. | I bought the book at a bookstore near my house. | Not "library" — _biblioteca_ is where you borrow books. |
| Me duele el pie derecho. | My right foot hurts. | Not "pie" the dessert — that's _pastel_. |
| Necesito lavar toda esta ropa. | I need to wash all these clothes. | Not "rope" — _cuerda_ is for tying things. |
| Ese ruido me molesta mucho. | That noise bothers me a lot. | Everyday word for annoyance, not the serious English sense of "molest." |
