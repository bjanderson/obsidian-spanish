## Explanation

Spanish has an easy way to talk about the near future: *ir* + *a* + infinitive. This
mirrors the English "going to + verb," so it is intuitive for English speakers.

To build it:

1. Conjugate *ir* (to go) in the present tense to match the subject.
2. Add the word *a*.
3. Add the main verb in its infinitive (unconjugated) form.

Only *ir* changes form. The main verb never changes, no matter the subject.

## Key Points / Conjugation / Rules

**Present tense of _ir_:**

| Subject               | Conjugation |
| ---------------------- | ----------- |
| yo                     | voy         |
| tú                     | vas         |
| usted / él / ella      | va          |
| nosotros / nosotras    | vamos       |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas | van        |

- Formula: **[conjugated _ir_] + a + [infinitive]**
- The infinitive after *a* never changes: *Voy a comer*, *Vamos a comer*, *Van a comer*.
- Because *ir* already sets the subject, the subject pronoun is usually dropped: *Voy a
  trabajar* (not *Yo voy a trabajar*), unless needed for contrast or clarity.
- When *ir a* is followed by a verb starting with a vowel, the two *a* sounds blend in
  speech: *Voy a ir* often sounds like *voyair*. This does not change spelling.

## Examples

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                                  |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Voy a descansar esta tarde.       | I am going to rest this afternoon. | *yo* form, dropped subject pronoun     |
| ¿Vas a llamar a tu hermana?       | Are you going to call your sister? | personal *a* before *tu hermana*       |
| Marta va a estudiar para el examen. | Marta is going to study for the exam. | *él/ella/usted* form with a named subject |
| Vamos a comer en ese restaurante.  | We are going to eat at that restaurant. | *nosotros* form                        |
| Mis padres van a viajar a Perú.   | My parents are going to travel to Peru. | *ellos* form, plural subject           |
| ¿Qué van a hacer ustedes mañana?  | What are you all going to do tomorrow? | question form with interrogative       |

## Common Mistakes

- Conjugating the second verb instead of leaving it in the infinitive: *Voy a comer* is
  correct, not *Voy a como*.
- Forgetting *a* between *ir* and the infinitive: *Voy comer* is missing the required
  *a*.
- Using *ir* for the destination sense ("Voy a la tienda") and confusing it with this
  construction — *ir a* + infinitive always needs a verb after *a*, not a place.
