## Explanation

You already know how to build [[B1.2.2.1 Present Subjunctive Stems|the subjunctive
stem]]. Now for the endings — and here's the trick that makes the present subjunctive
easy to remember: the endings are the **opposite vowel** from what you'd expect. -AR
verbs, which normally use *-a-* endings in the present indicative, switch to *-e-*
endings in the subjunctive. -ER/-IR verbs, which normally use *-e-*/*-i-* endings,
switch to *-a-* endings.

## Key Points / Conjugation / Rules

**-AR verbs take -e- endings:**

| Subject             | Ending | Example (*hablar*, stem *habl-*) |
| --------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------ |
| yo                    | -e       | hable                                 |
| tú                    | -es      | hables                                |
| él/ella/usted          | -e       | hable                                 |
| nosotros/nosotras      | -emos    | hablemos                              |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes    | -en      | hablen                                |

**-ER/-IR verbs take -a- endings:**

| Subject             | Ending | Example (*comer*, stem *com-*) | Example (*vivir*, stem *viv-*) |
| --------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| yo                    | -a       | coma                                 | viva                                 |
| tú                    | -as      | comas                                | vivas                                |
| él/ella/usted          | -a       | coma                                 | viva                                 |
| nosotros/nosotras      | -amos    | comamos                              | vivamos                              |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes    | -an      | coman                                | vivan                                |

- Notice -ER and -IR verbs share the exact same subjunctive endings — the split that
  matters is only -AR vs. everything else.
- **Stem-changing -IR verbs also change in the *nosotros* form** — a difference from
  the present indicative, where *nosotros* is normally unaffected. *Pedir* (e→i): *yo
  pido* → *pidamos* (not *pedamos*). This is a small but important exception.
- -AR and -ER stem-changing verbs (*pensar*, *poder*) do **not** change in *nosotros*
  — they behave like the present indicative there: *pensemos*, *podamos* (no *ie* or
  *ue* in the *nosotros* form).
- Spelling-change verbs (-car, -gar, -zar) adjust the stem's final consonant to keep
  the pronunciation consistent before the *-e* ending: *buscar → busque*, *pagar →
  pague*, *empezar → empiece*. This isn't a separate ending rule — it's the same *-e*
  ending, just with a spelling patch to the stem.

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Espero que hables con ella.                   | I hope you talk to her.                       | -AR, *-e-* ending                |
| Quiero que comamos juntos.                    | I want us to eat together.                     | -ER, *-a-* ending, *nosotros*    |
| Dudo que vivan aquí todavía.                  | I doubt they still live here.                   | -IR, *-a-* ending                |
| Espero que pidamos la cuenta pronto.          | I hope we ask for the check soon.               | stem-changing -IR, *nosotros* changes |
| Quiero que pensemos en otra opción.           | I want us to think of another option.            | stem-changing -AR, *nosotros* unchanged |
| Espero que busques la solución correcta.      | I hope you find the right solution.              | spelling change, *-car* → *-que* |

## Common Mistakes

- Mixing up which group gets which vowel: writing *hablo* → *habla* (keeping the -a-)
  instead of switching to *hable* — remember, -AR flips to *-e-*.
- Forgetting that -ER and -IR share identical subjunctive endings, and second-guessing
  whether *vivir* should look different from *comer* in the endings — it doesn't.
- Applying the *nosotros* stem change to -AR/-ER stem changers: *pensemos* is correct
  (no change), but *piensemos* is wrong.
- Missing the *nosotros* stem change on -IR verbs: *pedamos* is wrong — it must be
  *pidamos*, keeping the *e→i* change.
- Forgetting the spelling patch on -car/-gar/-zar verbs and writing *buscue* or
  *pagie* instead of *busque*, *pague*.
