## Explanation

Asking for and understanding directions relies on a small, fixed set of
phrases that combine into almost any set of instructions you'll actually hear
on the street. The three core ones are _a la derecha_ ("to the right"), _a la
izquierda_ ("to the left"), and _derecho_ or _todo derecho_ ("straight ahead"
— note this is a different word from _derecha_, "right," even though they look
similar).

To ask for directions, _¿Cómo llego a...?_ ("How do I get to...?") or _¿Dónde
queda...?_ ("Where is... located?" — from _quedar_, "to be located") are the
standard openers: _¿Cómo llego a la plaza principal?_ ("How do I get to the
main square?"). Directions are typically given as a string of commands using
_gire_ or _doble_ ("turn," formal _usted_ command) plus a direction: _Gire a la
derecha_ ("Turn right"), _Doble a la izquierda en la esquina_ ("Turn left at
the corner").

Distance and landmarks round out real directions: _cerca de_ ("near"), _lejos
de_ ("far from"), _al lado de_ ("next to" — using the _al_ contraction from
[[A1.2.2.1 Definite Articles]]), _enfrente de_ ("across from/in front of"), and
_en la esquina_ ("on the corner"). A phrase like _Está a dos cuadras de aquí_
("It's two blocks from here") combines a number with _cuadra_ ("city block")
to give a concrete distance.

## Key Points / Conjugation / Rules

| Spanish                | English                          | Notes                                  |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| a la derecha                | to the right                            | —                                              |
| a la izquierda              | to the left                             | —                                              |
| derecho / todo derecho       | straight ahead                          | not the same word as *derecha* ("right")      |
| Gire / Doble + direction    | Turn + direction                        | formal _usted_ command                        |
| ¿Cómo llego a...?           | How do I get to...?                     | asking for a route                             |
| ¿Dónde queda...?            | Where is... located?                    | asking for a location                          |
| cerca de / lejos de          | near / far from                          | —                                              |
| al lado de                   | next to                                  | uses the _al_ contraction                     |
| en la esquina                | on the corner                            | —                                              |
| a + number + cuadras de      | + number + blocks from                   | e.g. *a dos cuadras de aquí*                  |

- _Derecho_ ("straight") and _derecha_ ("right") look alike but mean different
  things — mixing them up changes the direction entirely.
- Direction commands (_gire, doble_) default to the formal _usted_ form, since
  you're typically asking or being told by a stranger.
- Distances combine a number + _cuadras_ ("blocks") + _de_ + reference point.

## Examples

| Spanish                                      | English                                     | Notes                              |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Disculpe, ¿cómo llego a la estación?               | Excuse me, how do I get to the station?             | asking for a route, formal                 |
| Siga todo derecho y luego gire a la izquierda.     | Go straight ahead and then turn left.               | combined instruction                        |
| El banco está al lado de la farmacia.              | The bank is next to the pharmacy.                   | *al lado de*                                |
| Queda a tres cuadras de aquí.                      | It's three blocks from here.                        | distance with *cuadras*                    |
| ¿Dónde queda el museo?                             | Where is the museum located?                        | *dónde queda*                               |
| Está enfrente del parque, en la esquina.           | It's across from the park, on the corner.            | *enfrente de* + *en la esquina*, with *del* |

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing _derecho_ ("straight") with _derecha_ ("right") — _Gire derecho_
  doesn't mean "turn straight"; the intended phrase is likely _Siga derecho_
  ("continue straight") or _Gire a la derecha_ ("turn right").
- Forgetting the _al/del_ contraction before a masculine noun — _al lado de el
  banco_ should be _al lado del banco_.
- Using an informal _tú_ command (_gira_) when the context calls for the formal
  _usted_ form (_gire_) with a stranger giving or receiving directions.
