# famoso / famosa / famosos / famosas

## Meaning

*Famoso* means "famous" — widely known or recognized. It agrees in gender and
number: *famoso*, *famosa*, *famosos*, *famosas*. Often paired with *por* to say
what someone is famous for (*famoso por su música*, "famous for his music").

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form    |
| ------------------- | ------- |
| Masculine singular   | famoso  |
| Feminine singular    | famosa  |
| Masculine plural     | famosos |
| Feminine plural      | famosas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                    | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Es un chef muy famoso.                             | He's a very famous chef.                      | masculine singular           |
| La actriz es famosa por sus películas de terror.   | The actress is famous for her horror movies.  | feminine singular, "famosa por" |
| Estos escritores son famosos en todo el país.      | These writers are famous throughout the country. | masculine plural         |
| Sus recetas no son famosas todavía.                | Her recipes aren't famous yet.                | feminine plural, negation    |
| ¿Es famoso ese cantante?                           | Is that singer famous?                        | question                     |
