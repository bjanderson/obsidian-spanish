# verdadero / verdadera / verdaderos / verdaderas

## Meaning

*Verdadero* means "true" or "real." Placed before the noun it usually
means "genuine/actual" (*un verdadero amigo*, "a true friend"); placed
after the noun it means "factually true" (*una historia verdadera*, "a
true story"). It agrees in gender and number with the noun it describes:
*verdadero*, *verdadera*, *verdaderos*, *verdaderas*. It's a close
near-synonym of [[real|real]] — *real* is the more neutral, all-purpose
word for "real," while *verdadero* leans slightly more toward "genuine" or
"true" versus fake/false.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form         |
| ------------------- | ------------ |
| Masculine singular   | verdadero    |
| Feminine singular    | verdadera    |
