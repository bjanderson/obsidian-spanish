# mi / mis

## Meaning

*Mi* and *mis* are short-form possessive adjectives placed before the noun —
*mi* before a singular noun, *mis* before a plural one. They don't change for
gender: *mi libro* (my book) and *mi casa* (my house) both use *mi*. Don't
confuse this unstressed *mi* with the stressed pronoun [[mí]] used after a
preposition (*para mí*, "for me").

## Full Paradigm

| Number   | Form |
| -------- | ---- |
| Singular | mi   |
| Plural   | mis  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi hermano vive en La Paz.                  | My brother lives in La Paz.              | singular                           |
| ¿Dónde dejé mis llaves?                     | Where did I leave my keys?               | plural, question                    |
| Perdí mi teléfono en el bus.                | I lost my phone on the bus.              | singular                           |
| Mis padres no saben nada de esto.           | My parents don't know anything about this. | plural, negation                 |
| Ese es mi asiento.                          | That's my seat.                          | singular                           |
