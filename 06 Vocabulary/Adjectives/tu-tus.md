# tu / tus

## Meaning

*Tu* and *tus* are short-form possessive adjectives for [[tú]], placed before
the noun — *tu* before a singular noun, *tus* before a plural one. They don't
change for gender: *tu libro* (your book) and *tu casa* (your house) both use
*tu*. Don't confuse this unstressed *tu* with the stressed pronoun [[ti]] used
after a preposition, or with formal [[su-sus|su / sus]].

## Full Paradigm

| Number   | Form |
| -------- | ---- |
| Singular | tu   |
| Plural   | tus  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ¿Cómo se llama tu perro?                    | What's your dog's name?                  | singular, question                  |
| Olvidaste tus llaves en la mesa.            | You forgot your keys on the table.       | plural                             |
| Tu casa es muy bonita.                      | Your house is very pretty.               | singular                           |
| No encuentro tus zapatos.                   | I can't find your shoes.                 | plural, negation                    |
| ¿Es tu hermana la que llegó?                | Is that your sister who arrived?         | singular, question                  |
