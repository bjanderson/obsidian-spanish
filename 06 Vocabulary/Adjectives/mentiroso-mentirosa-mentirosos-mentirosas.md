# mentiroso / mentirosa / mentirosos / mentirosas

## Meaning

*Mentiroso* means "lying" as an adjective, or "liar" as a noun — someone who doesn't
tell the truth. It agrees in gender and number: *mentiroso*, *mentirosa*, *mentirosos*,
*mentirosas*. Works both as an adjective (*un cuento mentiroso*) and as a standalone
noun (*un mentiroso*, "a liar").

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form         |
| ------------------ | ------------ |
| Masculine singular  | mentiroso    |
| Feminine singular   | mentirosa    |
| Masculine plural    | mentirosos   |
| Feminine plural     | mentirosas   |
