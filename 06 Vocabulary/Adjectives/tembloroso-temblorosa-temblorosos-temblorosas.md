# tembloroso / temblorosa / temblorosos / temblorosas

## Meaning

*Tembloroso* means "trembling" or "shaky" — related to the verb *temblar*
("to tremble/shake"), usually describing a voice, hands, or body affected
by cold, fear, or nervousness. It agrees in gender and number with the
noun it describes: *tembloroso*, *temblorosa*, *temblorosos*,
*temblorosas*.
