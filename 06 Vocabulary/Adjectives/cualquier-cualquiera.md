# cualquier / cualquiera

## Meaning

*Cualquier* is the short form used directly before a singular noun of either
gender: *cualquier día* ("any day"), *cualquier persona* ("any person"). Its
full form, *cualquiera*, is used after the noun, or on its own as a pronoun
meaning "anyone" or "whichever one": *Cualquiera puede aprender español*
("Anyone can learn Spanish"). Both are invariable for gender. The plural
*cualesquiera* exists but is rare in everyday speech.

## Full Paradigm

| Position                        | Form        |
| -------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Before a singular noun            | cualquier   |
| Standalone pronoun / after a noun | cualquiera  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Puedes venir cualquier día de la semana.    | You can come any day of the week.        | before a noun                      |
| Cualquiera puede aprender español.          | Anyone can learn Spanish.                | standalone pronoun, subject         |
| ¿Tienes un libro cualquiera que me recomiendes? | Do you have any book you'd recommend?  | after a noun, question              |
| No es un trabajo cualquiera.                | It isn't just any job.                   | after a noun, negation              |
| Elige cualquier opción que prefieras.       | Pick whichever option you prefer.        | before a noun                      |
