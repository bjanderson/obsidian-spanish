# enojado / enojada / enojados / enojadas

## Meaning

*Enojado* means "angry" — the past participle of *enojar* used with *estar*
to describe a state. Paired with *con* to say who someone is angry at
(*enojado con su hermano*). Agrees in gender and number: *enojado*,
*enojada*, *enojados*, *enojadas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form       |
| ------------------- | ---------- |
| Masculine singular   | enojado    |
| Feminine singular    | enojada    |
| Masculine plural     | enojados   |
| Feminine plural      | enojadas   |
