# contento / contenta / contentos / contentas

## Meaning

*Contento* means "content" or "happy" — a state of satisfaction, always with
*estar*. Paired with *con* to say what someone is happy about/with (*contento
con su trabajo*). Agrees in gender and number: *contento*, *contenta*,
*contentos*, *contentas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form        |
| ------------------- | ----------- |
| Masculine singular   | contento    |
| Feminine singular    | contenta    |
| Masculine plural     | contentos   |
| Feminine plural      | contentas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                                       | Notes             |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Estoy muy contento con los resultados.                       | I'm very content with the results.                    | masculine singular       |
| Está contenta con su nuevo trabajo.                          | She's happy with her new job.                         | feminine singular           |
| Los estudiantes están contentos con las notas.                | The students are happy with their grades.             | masculine plural               |
| Las vecinas están contentas con el parque nuevo.              | The neighbors are happy with the new park.            | feminine plural                  |
| ¿No estás contento?                                          | Aren't you happy?                                     | question, negation                 |
