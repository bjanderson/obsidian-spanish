# social

## Meaning

*Social* means "social." It has the same form for masculine and feminine
— it only changes for number: *social*, *sociales*.

## Full Paradigm

| Number     | Form        |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Singular     | social      |
| Plural       | sociales    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Tiene un problema social importante.             | It's a significant social problem.            | masculine singular                 |
| ¿Es esta una red social nueva?                   | Is this a new social network?                 | question                          |
| No asistió al evento social.                     | She didn't attend the social event.           | negation, *al* contraction (verb *asistir a*) |
| Las redes sociales cambiaron la comunicación.    | Social networks changed communication.        | feminine plural                    |
| Los programas sociales ayudan a muchas familias. | Social programs help many families.           | masculine plural                   |
