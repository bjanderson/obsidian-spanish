# alegre

## Meaning

*Alegre* means "cheerful" or "happy" — can describe a personality (*ser
alegre*) or a momentary mood (*estar alegre*). It has the same form for
masculine and feminine, changing only for number: *alegre*, *alegres*.

## Full Paradigm

| Number     | Form    |
| ----------- | ------- |
| Singular     | alegre  |
| Plural       | alegres |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes            |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| Mi abuela es muy alegre.                          | My grandmother is very cheerful.             | singular, ser           |
| Hoy estoy alegre por la buena noticia.            | Today I'm happy because of the good news.    | singular, estar          |
| Los colores de la fiesta son alegres.             | The colors of the party are cheerful.        | plural                     |
| ¿No estás alegre por el viaje?                    | Aren't you happy about the trip?             | question, negation           |
| Las canciones alegres animan a la gente.          | Cheerful songs cheer people up.              | plural, personal *a*           |
