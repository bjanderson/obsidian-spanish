# hermoso / hermosa / hermosos / hermosas

## Meaning

*Hermoso* means "beautiful" — for people, places, and things, with a
slightly more elevated or heartfelt tone than *bonito* (though it's still
extremely common in everyday speech across Latin America, not just written
or formal usage). It agrees in gender and number with the noun it
describes: *hermoso*, *hermosa*, *hermosos*, *hermosas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form     |
| ------------------- | -------- |
| Masculine singular   | hermoso  |
| Feminine singular    | hermosa  |
| Masculine plural     | hermosos |
| Feminine plural      | hermosas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                  | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Qué paisaje tan hermoso.                          | What a beautiful landscape.                    | masculine singular                 |
| Tiene una voz hermosa.                             | She has a beautiful voice.                     | feminine singular                   |
| ¿No son hermosos estos jardines?                   | Aren't these gardens beautiful?                | question, masculine plural          |
| La ciudad no era tan hermosa como esperábamos.     | The city wasn't as beautiful as we expected.   | negation, feminine singular          |
| Vimos playas hermosas en la costa.                 | We saw beautiful beaches on the coast.         | feminine plural                     |
