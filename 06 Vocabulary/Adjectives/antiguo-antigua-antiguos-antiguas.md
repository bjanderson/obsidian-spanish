# antiguo / antigua / antiguos / antiguas

## Meaning

*Antiguo* means "old" or "ancient" — usually about age tied to history
(a building, a civilization, an object). It agrees in gender and number:
*antiguo*, *antigua*, *antiguos*, *antiguas*. Placed before the noun it can
also mean "former" (*su antiguo profesor*, "her former teacher") rather than
literal age.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form      |
| ------------------- | --------- |
| Masculine singular   | antiguo   |
| Feminine singular    | antigua   |
| Masculine plural     | antiguos  |
| Feminine plural      | antiguas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                        | Notes                              |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| El castillo es antiguo.                            | The castle is ancient.                              | masculine singular                        |
| La ciudad tiene una muralla antigua.               | The city has an ancient wall.                       | feminine singular                          |
| ¿Visitaste las ruinas antiguas del imperio?        | Did you visit the ancient ruins of the empire?      | feminine plural, question, *del*          |
| No conservan los documentos antiguos del archivo.  | They don't keep the old documents from the archive. | masculine plural, negation, *del*         |
| Su antiguo profesor la ayudó con el proyecto.      | Her former teacher helped her with the project.     | masculine singular, "former" sense         |
