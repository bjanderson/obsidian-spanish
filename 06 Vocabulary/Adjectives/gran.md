# gran

## Meaning

*Gran* is the apocopated (shortened) form of *grande*, used right before
a singular noun of either gender — *un gran hombre* ("a great man"), *una
gran mujer* ("a great woman"). It's invariable for gender in this position
and usually shifts the meaning toward "great/outstanding" rather than
"physically large." *Grande* returns after the noun, or in the plural.

## Full Paradigm

| Position                      | Form    |
| ------------------------------ | ------- |
| Before singular noun (m or f)   | gran    |
| After noun / plural              | grande / grandes |
