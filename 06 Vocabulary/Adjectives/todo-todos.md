# todo / todos

## Meaning

*Todo* is a quantifier adjective that agrees in gender and number with the noun it
describes: *todo*, *toda*, *todos*, *todas*. With *el/la* + a singular noun, it
means "the whole/all of": *todo el día* ("all day," "the whole day"), *toda la
noche* ("all night"). Without the article, before a bare singular noun, it means
"every": *toda persona tiene derechos* ("every person has rights"). *Todo* also
works as a neuter pronoun on its own, meaning "everything": *Todo está listo*
("Everything is ready").

*Todos* is the masculine plural form, used before a masculine or mixed-gender
plural noun: *todos los estudiantes* ("all the students"). *Todos* is also,
extremely commonly, used on its own as a pronoun meaning "everyone" or
"everybody" — covering a group of people without naming a specific noun.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number   | Form  |
| -------------------- | ------- |
| Masculine singular      | todo    |
| Feminine singular        | toda    |
| Masculine plural           | todos   |
| Feminine plural              | todas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Trabajó toda la tarde sin parar.            | He worked all afternoon without stopping. | feminine singular                 |
| Todo el pueblo participó en la fiesta.      | The whole town took part in the party.   | masculine singular                 |
| Todo está en orden.                         | Everything is in order.                  | neuter pronoun use                 |
| Toda persona merece respeto.                | Every person deserves respect.           | distributive "every," no article    |
| Todos los pasajeros deben abordar ya.       | All passengers must board now.           | masculine plural, before a noun    |
| Todos llegaron temprano a la reunión.       | Everyone arrived early to the meeting.   | pronoun, "everyone"                 |
| Invité a todos mis compañeros.              | I invited all my coworkers.              | masculine plural + personal *a*     |
| Todos los días camino al trabajo.           | Every day I walk to work.                | masculine plural, "every"           |
