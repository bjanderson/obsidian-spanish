# blando / blanda / blandos / blandas

## Meaning

*Blando* means "soft, tender" — mostly of physical texture (contrast with
*duro*); it can also describe a lenient or weak character (*ser blando
con los hijos*, "to be too soft/lenient with one's kids"). It agrees in
gender and number: *blando*, *blanda*, *blandos*, *blandas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form      |
| ------------------- | --------- |
