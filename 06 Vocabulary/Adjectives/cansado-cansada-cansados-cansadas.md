# cansado / cansada / cansados / cansadas

## Meaning

*Cansado* means "tired, worn out" — physically or mentally. It's almost
always used with *estar*, since it describes a temporary state rather than
a fixed trait. It agrees in gender and number: *cansado*, *cansada*,
*cansados*, *cansadas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form      |
| ------------------- | --------- |
| Masculine singular   | cansado   |
| Feminine singular    | cansada   |
| Masculine plural     | cansados  |
| Feminine plural      | cansadas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                               | Notes                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Después de correr, Marco está cansado.         | After running, Marco is tired.         | masculine singular           |
| ¿Estás cansada de esperar?                     | Are you tired of waiting?              | feminine singular, question  |
| Los estudiantes están cansados hoy.            | The students are tired today.          | masculine plural              |
| No estamos cansadas todavía.                   | We aren't tired yet.                   | feminine plural, negation     |
| Se ve cansado después del viaje.               | He looks tired after the trip.         | masculine singular, del       |
