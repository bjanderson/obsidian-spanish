# egoísta

## Meaning

*Egoísta* means "selfish" — putting one's own interests before others'. Adjectives
ending in *-ista* are invariable for gender (same form for masculine and feminine);
it only changes for number: *egoísta*, *egoístas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Number     | Form      |
| ----------- | --------- |
| Singular     | egoísta   |
| Plural       | egoístas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                      | English                                     | Notes                        |
