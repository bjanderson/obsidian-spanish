# lleno / llena / llenos / llenas

## Meaning

*Lleno* means "full." It agrees in gender and number: *lleno*, *llena*,
*llenos*, *llenas*. It's commonly used with *de* to say what something is
full of, as in *lleno de gente* ("full of people").

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form   |
| ------------------- | ------ |
| Masculine singular   | lleno  |
| Feminine singular    | llena  |
| Masculine plural     | llenos |
| Feminine plural      | llenas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                 | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El vaso está lleno de agua.                       | The glass is full of water.                       | masculine singular                 |
| La sala estaba llena de gente.                    | The room was full of people.                      | feminine singular, "lleno de gente" |
| ¿Están llenos los estacionamientos hoy?           | Are the parking lots full today?                  | masculine plural, question         |
| No vimos mesas llenas a esa hora.                 | We didn't see full tables at that hour.           | feminine plural, negation          |
| Llegamos al teatro y ya estaba lleno.             | We arrived at the theater and it was already full. | masculine singular, "al" contraction |

## Related Vocabulary
