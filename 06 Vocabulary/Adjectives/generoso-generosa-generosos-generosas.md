# generoso / generosa / generosos / generosas

## Meaning

*Generoso* means "generous" — willing to give or share freely, of money, time, or
attention. It agrees in gender and number: *generoso*, *generosa*, *generosos*,
*generosas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form         |
| ------------------ | ------------ |
| Masculine singular  | generoso     |
| Feminine singular   | generosa     |
| Masculine plural    | generosos    |
| Feminine plural     | generosas    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                       | Notes                       |

| Mi tío es muy generoso con su tiempo.                 | My uncle is very generous with his time.            | masculine singular                |
| Ella fue generosa al compartir su comida.             | She was generous in sharing her food.               | feminine singular                 |
