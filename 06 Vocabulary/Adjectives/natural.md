# natural

## Meaning

*Natural* means "natural" — occurring in nature, not artificial or forced.
It has the same form for masculine and feminine — it only changes for
number: *natural*, *naturales*.

## Full Paradigm

| Number     | Form       |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| Singular     | natural    |
| Plural       | naturales  |

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El parque tiene un lago natural.                 | The park has a natural lake.                  | masculine singular                 |
| Prefieren productos naturales.                   | They prefer natural products.                 | masculine plural                   |
| ¿Es natural este jugo?                           | Is this juice natural?                        | masculine singular, question       |
| No fue un proceso natural.                       | It wasn't a natural process.                  | negation                          |
| Estudiaron los recursos naturales del país.      | They studied the country's natural resources. | masculine plural, del contraction  |
