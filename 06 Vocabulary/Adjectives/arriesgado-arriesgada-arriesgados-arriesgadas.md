# arriesgado / arriesgada / arriesgados / arriesgadas

## Meaning

*Arriesgado* means "risky, daring" — describes an action or decision that
carries risk, or a person willing to take risks. It's also the past
participle of *arriesgar* ("to risk"). It agrees in gender and number:
*arriesgado*, *arriesgada*, *arriesgados*, *arriesgadas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form          |
| ------------------- | ------------- |
| Masculine singular   | arriesgado    |
| Feminine singular    | arriesgada    |
| Masculine plural     | arriesgados   |
| Feminine plural      | arriesgadas   |
