# conocido / conocida / conocidos / conocidas

## Meaning

*Conocido* means "known" or "well-known" — the past participle of *conocer*
used as an adjective. It also works as a noun meaning "acquaintance." Agrees
in gender and number: *conocido*, *conocida*, *conocidos*, *conocidas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form      |
| ------------------- | --------- |
| Masculine singular   | conocido  |
| Feminine singular    | conocida  |
| Masculine plural     | conocidos |
| Feminine plural      | conocidas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                       | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
