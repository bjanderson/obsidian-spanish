# rico / rica / ricos / ricas

## Meaning

*Rico* means "rich," having a lot of money. With food, it also means
"tasty/delicious" — a very common secondary sense in everyday speech
(*la comida está rica*, "the food is tasty"). It agrees in gender and
number.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form  |
| ------------------- | ----- |
| Masculine singular   | rico  |
| Feminine singular    | rica  |
| Masculine plural     | ricos |
| Feminine plural      | ricas |

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                          | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El empresario es rico.                           | The businessman is rich.                   | masculine singular                 |
| La comida está muy rica.                         | The food is very tasty.                    | feminine singular, "tasty"         |
| ¿Es rica su familia?                             | Is his family rich?                        | feminine singular, question        |
| No son tan ricos como parecen.                   | They aren't as rich as they seem.          | masculine plural, negation         |
| Probamos unos postres ricos.                     | We tried some tasty desserts.              | masculine plural, "tasty"          |
