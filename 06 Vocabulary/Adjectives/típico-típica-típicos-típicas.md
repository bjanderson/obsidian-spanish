# típico / típica / típicos / típicas

## Meaning

*Típico* means "typical" — characteristic of a particular place, group, or
situation, often used for food, dress, or customs. It agrees in gender and
number: *típico*, *típica*, *típicos*, *típicas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number     | Form    |
| -------------------- | ------- |
| Masculine singular    | típico  |
| Feminine singular     | típica  |
| Masculine plural      | típicos |
| Feminine plural       | típicas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El mole es un plato típico de la región.               | Mole is a typical dish of the region.                | masculine singular              |
| Lleva la ropa típica de su pueblo.                     | She wears her town's typical clothing.               | feminine singular               |
| ¿Cuáles son los platillos típicos de aquí?             | What are the typical dishes here?                    | masculine plural, question      |
| No son tan típicas esas costumbres en la capital.      | Those customs aren't so typical in the capital.      | feminine plural, negation       |
| Celebran las fiestas típicas cada año en el pueblo.    | They celebrate the typical festivals every year in the town. | feminine plural         |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[tradicional|tradicional]] — a close relative; something típico is often also tradicional, though típico leans more toward "characteristic" and tradicional toward "long-established."
