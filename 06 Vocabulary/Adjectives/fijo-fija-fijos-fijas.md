# fijo / fija / fijos / fijas

## Meaning

*Fijo* means "fixed" or "set" — not moving or not subject to change (*un
precio fijo*, "a fixed price"; *un empleo fijo*, "a permanent job"). It
agrees in gender and number with the noun it describes: *fijo*, *fija*,
*fijos*, *fijas*. Don't confuse it with the near-synonym
[[firme|firme]], which means "solid" or "unwavering" rather than
"unchanging/in place."

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form  |
| ------------------- | ----- |
| Masculine singular   | fijo  |
| Feminine singular    | fija  |
| Masculine plural     | fijos |
| Feminine plural      | fijas |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[firme|firme]] — a near-synonym; *firme* means "solid" or "unwavering," while *fijo* means "fixed in place" or "unchanging."
