# sabroso / sabrosa / sabrosos / sabrosas

## Meaning

*Sabroso* means "tasty" or "flavorful" — describing food with good flavor.
It agrees in gender and number: *sabroso*, *sabrosa*, *sabrosos*,
*sabrosas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form      |
| ------------------- | --------- |
| Masculine singular   | sabroso   |
| Feminine singular    | sabrosa   |
| Masculine plural     | sabrosos  |
| Feminine plural      | sabrosas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                    | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| El caldo está sabroso.                             | The broth is tasty.                          | masculine singular                 |
| La salsa quedó muy sabrosa.                        | The sauce turned out very tasty.             | feminine singular                  |
| ¿Es sabrosa la comida del mercado?                 | Is the food from the market tasty?           | feminine singular, question, *del* |
| Los tamales no estaban muy sabrosos hoy.           | The tamales weren't very tasty today.        | masculine plural, negation         |
| Compramos unas frutas sabrosas en el mercado.      | We bought some tasty fruits at the market.   | feminine plural                    |
