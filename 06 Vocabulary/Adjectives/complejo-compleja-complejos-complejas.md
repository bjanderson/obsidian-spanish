# complejo / compleja / complejos / complejas

## Meaning

*Complejo* means "complex" — made of many interconnected parts, often in a
more formal or technical register than *complicado*. As a masculine noun,
*el complejo* also means "complex" in the sense of a building complex or a
psychological complex — but this file covers the adjective use. It agrees
in gender and number: *complejo*, *compleja*, *complejos*, *complejas*.
