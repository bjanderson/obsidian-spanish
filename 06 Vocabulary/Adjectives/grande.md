# grande

## Meaning

*Grande* means "big" or "great." It has the same form for masculine and
feminine, only changing for number: *grande*, *grandes*. Before a singular
noun of either gender, *grande* shortens to *gran* and shifts meaning
toward "great" rather than "large" (*un gran hombre*, "a great man," vs.
*un hombre grande*, "a big/tall man").

## Full Paradigm

| Number     | Form      |
| ----------- | --------- |
| Singular     | grande    |
| Plural       | grandes   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                               | Notes                       |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Vivimos en una casa grande.                          | We live in a big house.                        | feminine, after noun, "size" sense  |
| ¿Es grande el problema?                              | Is the problem big?                            | question                          |
| No es tan grande como pensaba.                       | It's not as big as I thought.                  | negation                          |
| Sus manos son grandes.                               | His hands are big.                             | plural                            |
| Los edificios grandes dominan el centro.             | The big buildings dominate downtown.           | masculine plural                  |
