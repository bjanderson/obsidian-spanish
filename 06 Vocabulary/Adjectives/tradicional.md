# tradicional

## Meaning

*Tradicional* means "traditional" — something that follows a long-established
custom or practice. It has the same form for masculine and feminine — it only
changes for number: *tradicional*, *tradicionales*.

## Full Paradigm

| Number     | Form            |
| ----------- | --------------- |
| Singular     | tradicional     |
| Plural       | tradicionales   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                             | English                                          | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| La quinceañera es una celebración tradicional en muchos países. | The quinceañera is a traditional celebration in many countries. | feminine singular          |
| El traje tradicional tiene colores brillantes.          | The traditional outfit has bright colors.              | masculine singular            |
| ¿Todavía preparan las recetas tradicionales de la abuela? | Do they still make grandma's traditional recipes?      | feminine plural, question    |
| No respetan los valores tradicionales de la comunidad.  | They don't respect the community's traditional values. | masculine plural, negation    |
| El mercado vende artesanías tradicionales todos los sábados. | The market sells traditional crafts every Saturday.  | feminine plural               |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[actual|actual]] — a contrasting idea, describing what belongs to the present day rather than to custom.
- [[típico-típica-típicos-típicas|típico / típica / típicos / típicas]] — a close relative; something tradicional is often also típico, though típico leans more toward "characteristic" and tradicional toward "long-established."
