# vacío / vacía / vacíos / vacías

## Meaning

*Vacío* means "empty," in the sense of containing nothing. It agrees in
gender and number: *vacío*, *vacía*, *vacíos*, *vacías*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form    |
| ------------------- | ------- |
| Masculine singular   | vacío   |
| Feminine singular    | vacía   |
| Masculine plural     | vacíos  |
| Feminine plural      | vacías  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| El refrigerador está vacío.                       | The refrigerator is empty.                    | masculine singular                 |
| La botella quedó vacía.                           | The bottle ended up empty.                    | feminine singular                  |
| ¿Por qué están vacíos los pasillos?               | Why are the hallways empty?                   | masculine plural, question         |
| No dejes las cajas vacías en la entrada.          | Don't leave the empty boxes at the entrance.  | feminine plural, negative command  |
| Encontramos vacío el estacionamiento del edificio. | We found the building's parking lot empty.    | masculine singular, "del" contraction |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[libre]] — a related sense of "unoccupied" or "available," as in *un asiento libre* versus *un asiento vacío*.
