# serio / seria / serios / serias

## Meaning

*Serio* means "serious" — describing a personality (*ser serio*) or a
temporary demeanor (*estar serio*). Also used in the phrase *en serio*
("seriously/for real"). Agrees in gender and number: *serio*, *seria*,
*serios*, *serias*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form   |
| ------------------- | ------ |
| Masculine singular   | serio  |
| Feminine singular    | seria  |
| Masculine plural     | serios |
| Feminine plural      | serias |

## Examples

| Spanish                             | English                        | Notes             |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| El director es muy serio.                  | The director is very serious.        | masculine singular       |
| Hoy está seria mi jefa.                    | My boss is serious today.            | feminine singular          |
| Los médicos parecían serios.               | The doctors looked serious.          | masculine plural             |
| Sus advertencias son serias.               | Her warnings are serious.            | feminine plural                |
| ¿Hablas en serio?                          | Are you serious?                     | question, idiom "en serio"     |
