# gracioso / graciosa / graciosos / graciosas

## Meaning

*Gracioso* means "funny" or "witty" — someone or something that makes people
laugh. Different from *divertido* (fun/enjoyable) — *gracioso* is specifically
about being comical. Agrees in gender and number: *gracioso*, *graciosa*,
*graciosos*, *graciosas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form       |
| ------------------- | ---------- |
| Masculine singular   | gracioso   |
| Feminine singular    | graciosa   |
| Masculine plural     | graciosos  |
| Feminine plural      | graciosas  |
