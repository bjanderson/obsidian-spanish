# cierto / cierta / ciertos / ciertas

## Meaning

*Cierto* means "true" or "certain" (*Es cierto que va a llover*, "It's true
that it's going to rain"). Placed before a noun, it shifts to mean "a
certain/some" (*ciertos rumores*, "certain rumors") — a common secondary
sense worth noticing. It agrees in gender and number.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form    |
| ------------------- | ------- |
| Masculine singular   | cierto  |
| Feminine singular    | cierta  |
| Masculine plural     | ciertos |
| Feminine plural      | ciertas |

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                             | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Es cierto que va a llover.                       | It's true that it's going to rain.            | masculine singular, impersonal     |
| Su historia parece cierta.                       | Her story seems true.                         | feminine singular                  |
| ¿Es cierto eso?                                  | Is that true?                                 | masculine singular, question       |
| No es cierta esa noticia.                        | That news isn't true.                         | feminine singular, negation        |
| Hay ciertos rumores sobre la empresa.            | There are certain rumors about the company.   | masculine plural, "certain/some"   |
