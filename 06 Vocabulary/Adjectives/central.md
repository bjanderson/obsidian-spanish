# central

## Meaning

*Central* means "central" — physically in the middle of something (*la
plaza central*, "the central square") or figuratively the main/most
important one (*el punto central del debate*, "the central point of the
debate"). It has the same form for masculine and feminine — it only
changes for number, with a spelling change: *central*, *centrales*.

## Full Paradigm

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | central    |
| Plural   | centrales  |
