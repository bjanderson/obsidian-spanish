# demás

## Meaning

*Demás* is invariable — the same form for masculine, feminine, singular, and
plural. It almost always appears with an article: *los demás* / *las demás*
("the rest," "the others," referring to people or plural things), or *lo
demás* (neuter, "the rest of it," "everything else").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Los demás estudiantes ya se fueron.         | The other students already left.         | masculine plural, with article      |
| Las demás opciones no me convencen.         | The other options don't convince me.     | feminine plural, with article       |
| Lo demás no importa.                        | The rest doesn't matter.                 | neuter, "the rest of it"            |
| Terminé mi parte; ahora falta lo demás.     | I finished my part; now the rest is left. | neuter                             |
| ¿Y los demás, dónde están?                  | And the others, where are they?          | question                           |

## Related Vocabulary
