# duro / dura / duros / duras

## Meaning

*Duro* means "hard" — physically firm or tough, or figuratively difficult
or harsh (*un examen duro*, "a hard exam"). It agrees in gender and
number: *duro*, *dura*, *duros*, *duras*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form   |
| ------------------- | ------ |
| Masculine singular   | duro   |
| Feminine singular    | dura   |
| Masculine plural     | duros  |
| Feminine plural      | duras  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                     |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| El pan está duro.                             | The bread is hard/stale.                | masculine singular            |
| La cama es dura.                              | The bed is hard.                        | feminine singular              |
| Los exámenes fueron duros este semestre.      | The exams were hard this semester.      | masculine plural                 |
| Sus manos están duras del trabajo.            | His hands are hard from work.           | feminine plural, del contraction |
| No es tan duro como crees.                    | It isn't as hard as you think.          | negation                         |
