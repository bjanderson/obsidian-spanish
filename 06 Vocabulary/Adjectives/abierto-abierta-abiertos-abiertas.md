# abierto / abierta / abiertos / abiertas

## Meaning

*Abierto* means "open." It's also the irregular past participle of the verb
*abrir* ("to open"), used here as a standalone adjective describing a
state. It agrees in gender and number: *abierto*, *abierta*, *abiertos*,
*abiertas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form     |
| ------------------- | -------- |
| Masculine singular   | abierto  |
| Feminine singular    | abierta  |
| Masculine plural     | abiertos |
| Feminine plural      | abiertas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                    | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| La tienda está abierta hasta las nueve.                | The store is open until nine.                     | feminine singular                  |
| El museo está abierto los domingos.                    | The museum is open on Sundays.                    | masculine singular                 |
| ¿Están abiertos los bancos hoy?                        | Are the banks open today?                         | masculine plural, question         |
| No encontramos farmacias abiertas cerca del hospital.  | We didn't find open pharmacies near the hospital. | feminine plural, negation, "del" contraction |
| Dejaron la puerta abierta toda la noche.               | They left the door open all night.                | feminine singular                  |
