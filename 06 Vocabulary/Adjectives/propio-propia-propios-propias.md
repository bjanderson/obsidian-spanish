# propio / propia / propios / propias

## Meaning

*Propio* means "own," emphasizing that something belongs to the person
just mentioned. It agrees in gender and number: *propio*, *propia*,
*propios*, *propias*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form     |
| ------------------- | -------- |
| Masculine singular   | propio   |
| Feminine singular    | propia   |
| Masculine plural     | propios  |
| Feminine plural      | propias  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                 | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Tiene su propio negocio.                            | He has his own business.                           | masculine singular                 |
| ¿Escribiste esto con tus propias palabras?          | Did you write this in your own words?              | feminine plural, question           |
| No tiene casa propia todavía.                       | She doesn't have her own house yet.                | feminine singular, negation         |
| Los estudiantes trajeron sus propios libros.        | The students brought their own books.              | masculine plural                   |
| Cada país tiene su propia cultura.                  | Each country has its own culture.                  | feminine singular                  |
