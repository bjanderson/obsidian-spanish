# pobre

## Meaning

*Pobre* means "poor." After a noun it means "lacking money" (*una familia
pobre*); before a noun it shifts to "unfortunate/pitiable" (*ese pobre
hombre*, "that poor man"). It has the same form for masculine and
feminine — it only changes for number: *pobre*, *pobres*.

## Full Paradigm

| Number     | Form     |
| ----------- | -------- |
| Singular     | pobre    |
| Plural       | pobres   |

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| La familia es pobre.                             | The family is poor.                           | singular, "lacking money"          |
| Ese pobre hombre perdió su trabajo.              | That poor man lost his job.                   | singular, "unfortunate," before noun |
