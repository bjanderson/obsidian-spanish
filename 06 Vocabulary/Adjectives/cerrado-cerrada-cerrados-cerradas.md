# cerrado / cerrada / cerrados / cerradas

## Meaning

*Cerrado* means "closed" or "shut." It's also the past participle of the
verb *cerrar* ("to close"), used here as a standalone adjective describing
a state. It agrees in gender and number: *cerrado*, *cerrada*, *cerrados*,
*cerradas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form     |
| ------------------- | -------- |
| Masculine singular   | cerrado  |
| Feminine singular    | cerrada  |
| Masculine plural     | cerrados |
| Feminine plural      | cerradas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                  | Notes                       |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| La tienda está cerrada los lunes.                     | The store is closed on Mondays.                  | feminine singular                  |
| El restaurante está cerrado por reparaciones.         | The restaurant is closed for repairs.            | masculine singular                 |
| ¿Están cerrados los caminos por la tormenta?          | Are the roads closed because of the storm?       | masculine plural, question         |
| No visites las playas cerradas al público.            | Don't visit beaches closed to the public.        | feminine plural, negative command, "al" contraction |
| Encontramos cerrado el consultorio del médico.        | We found the doctor's office closed.             | masculine singular, "del" contraction |
