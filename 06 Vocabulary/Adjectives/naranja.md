# naranja

## Meaning

*Naranja* means "orange" (the color), taken directly from the fruit's
name. In careful usage it's invariable — it stays *naranja* even in the
plural (*unas cortinas naranja*) — though *naranjas* is common in everyday
speech.

## Full Paradigm

| Number     | Form                        |
| ----------- | --------------------------- |
| Singular     | naranja                     |
| Plural       | naranja (careful) / naranjas (everyday) |
