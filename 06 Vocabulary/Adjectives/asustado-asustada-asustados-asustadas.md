# asustado / asustada / asustados / asustadas

## Meaning

*Asustado* means "scared" or "frightened" — the past participle of *asustar*
used with *estar*. Agrees in gender and number: *asustado*, *asustada*,
*asustados*, *asustadas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form        |
| ------------------- | ----------- |
| Masculine singular   | asustado    |
| Feminine singular    | asustada    |
| Masculine plural     | asustados   |
| Feminine plural      | asustadas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                    | Notes           |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| Estoy asustado por el ruido.                              | I'm scared because of the noise.                    | masculine singular      |
| La niña está asustada de la oscuridad.                     | The girl is scared of the dark.                     | feminine singular          |
| Los turistas estaban asustados durante la tormenta.        | The tourists were scared during the storm.          | masculine plural              |
| Mis primas están asustadas con la película.                | My cousins are scared by the movie.                 | feminine plural                 |
| ¿Estás asustado?                                          | Are you scared?                                     | question                         |
