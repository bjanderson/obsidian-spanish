# educado / educada / educados / educadas

## Meaning

*Educado* means "polite, well-mannered" when describing behavior — it's a false
friend for "educated" in the academic sense, which is *culto* or *instruido* instead.
It agrees in gender and number: *educado*, *educada*, *educados*, *educadas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form        |
| ------------------ | ----------- |
| Masculine singular  | educado     |
| Feminine singular   | educada     |
| Masculine plural    | educados    |
| Feminine plural     | educadas    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                       | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| El niño es muy educado con los mayores.           | The boy is very polite with older people.         | masculine singular                |
| Su hija es educada y respetuosa.                  | Her daughter is polite and respectful.            | feminine singular                 |
| Los meseros aquí son educados.                    | The waiters here are polite.                      | masculine plural                  |
| Sus respuestas no fueron educadas.                | His answers weren't polite.                       | feminine plural, negation         |
| ¿Te parece educado interrumpir así?               | Does it seem polite to you to interrupt like that? | question                          |
