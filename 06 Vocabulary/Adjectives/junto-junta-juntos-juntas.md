# junto / junta / juntos / juntas

## Meaning

As an adjective, *junto* means "together" and agrees in gender and number
with the subject it describes — most often seen in the plural, *juntos*/
*juntas*, since "together" usually describes more than one person or thing
(*Vivimos juntos*, "We live together"; *Ellas trabajan juntas*, "They work
together (fem.)").

Separately, the invariable phrase *junto a* means "next to" or "right
beside" — a very common way to locate something (*El banco está junto a la
farmacia*, "The bank is next to the pharmacy"). In this phrase, *junto*
never changes form regardless of gender or number. Compare with
[[cerca|cerca]], "near" — *junto a* implies being right up against
something, while *cerca de* allows more distance.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form   |
| ------------------- | ------ |
| Masculine singular   | junto  |
| Feminine singular    | junta  |
| Masculine plural     | juntos |
| Feminine plural      | juntas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                  | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Estudiamos juntos para el examen.                   | We studied together for the exam.               | masculine plural                    |
| Las hermanas crecieron juntas.                      | The sisters grew up together.                   | feminine plural                     |
| ¿Van juntos al concierto?                           | Are they going to the concert together?         | question, masculine plural          |
| No están juntos desde el año pasado.                | They haven't been together since last year.     | negation, masculine plural          |
| El parque está junto a mi casa.                     | The park is next to my house.                   | fixed phrase *junto a*, invariable |
