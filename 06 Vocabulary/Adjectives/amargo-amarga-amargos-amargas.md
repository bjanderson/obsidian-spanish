# amargo / amarga / amargos / amargas

## Meaning

*Amargo* means "bitter" — for taste, and figuratively for a harsh or
resentful feeling or experience (*un recuerdo amargo*, "a bitter memory").
It agrees in gender and number: *amargo*, *amarga*, *amargos*, *amargas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form     |
| ------------------- | -------- |
| Masculine singular   | amargo   |
| Feminine singular    | amarga   |
| Masculine plural     | amargos  |
| Feminine plural      | amargas  |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[fuerte]] — bitterness is often described alongside intensity, as in *un sabor fuerte y amargo* ("a strong, bitter flavor").
- [[ácido-ácida-ácidos-ácidas|ácido / ácida / ácidos / ácidas]] — another sharp, unpleasant taste, often confused with bitter in casual speech.
