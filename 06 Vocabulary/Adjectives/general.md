# general

## Meaning

*General* means "general" (broad, not specific). It has the same form for
masculine and feminine — it only changes for number: *general*,
*generales*. As a noun, *el general* means a military "general."

## Full Paradigm

| Number     | Form        |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Singular     | general     |
| Plural       | generales   |
