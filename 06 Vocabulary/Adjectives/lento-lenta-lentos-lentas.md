# lento / lenta / lentos / lentas

## Meaning

*Lento* means "slow." It agrees in gender and number: *lento*, *lenta*,
*lentos*, *lentas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form   |
| ------------------- | ------ |
| Masculine singular   | lento  |
| Feminine singular    | lenta  |
| Masculine plural     | lentos |
| Feminine plural      | lentas |

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                          | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El proceso es lento.                             | The process is slow.                       | masculine singular                 |
| La música era lenta.                             | The music was slow.                        | feminine singular                  |
| ¿Por qué es tan lento el internet?               | Why is the internet so slow?               | masculine singular, question       |
| El servicio no fue lento.                        | The service wasn't slow.                   | masculine singular, negation       |
| Caminaron con pasos lentos por el parque.        | They walked with slow steps through the park. | masculine plural                |
