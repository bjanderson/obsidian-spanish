# raro / rara / raros / raras

## Meaning

*Raro* means "strange" or "weird" in everyday speech, and "rare" or
"infrequent" in a more formal or scientific sense (e.g. a rare species). It
agrees in gender and number: *raro*, *rara*, *raros*, *raras*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number     | Form  |
| -------------------- | ----- |
| Masculine singular    | raro  |
| Feminine singular     | rara  |
| Masculine plural      | raros |
| Feminine plural       | raras |
