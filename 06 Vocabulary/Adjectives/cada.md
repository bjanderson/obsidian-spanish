# cada

## Meaning

*Cada* means "each" or "every." It's invariable — the same form for masculine
and feminine nouns — and only ever appears in the singular, placed before the
noun: *cada estudiante* ("each student"), *cada persona* ("each person"). The
phrase *cada uno* / *cada una* means "each one" when standing alone as a
pronoun.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Cada estudiante tiene su propio libro.      | Each student has their own book.         | masculine noun                     |
| Cada mañana salgo a caminar.                | Every morning I go out for a walk.       | time expression                    |
| ¿Cada cuánto tiempo vienes aquí?            | How often do you come here?              | question, set phrase *cada cuánto* |
| Cada persona tiene su opinión.              | Each person has their own opinion.       | feminine noun, invariable form      |
| No cada decisión es fácil.                  | Not every decision is easy.              | negation                           |
