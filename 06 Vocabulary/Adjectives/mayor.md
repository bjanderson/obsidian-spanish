# mayor

## Meaning

*Mayor* means "older" (comparing ages) or "greater" (comparing degree).
It's the comparative form of *grande*, and has the same form for masculine
and feminine — it only changes for number: *mayor*, *mayores*.

## Full Paradigm

| Number     | Form      |
| ----------- | --------- |
| Singular     | mayor     |
| Plural       | mayores   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi hermano mayor vive en La Paz.                 | My older brother lives in La Paz.             | masculine singular, age sense       |
| ¿Es ella mayor que tú?                           | Is she older than you?                        | question                          |
| No es un problema mayor.                         | It isn't a bigger problem.                    | negation, "greater" sense            |
| Los hermanos mayores cuidan a los pequeños.      | The older siblings look after the little ones.| plural                            |
| Tiene mayor experiencia que los demás.           | She has more experience than the others.      | statement, "greater" sense           |
