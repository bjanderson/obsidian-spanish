# otro / otra / otros / otras

## Meaning

*Otro* is the adjective/pronoun "other" or "another" — it agrees in gender and
number with the noun it describes: *otro*, *otra*, *otros*, *otras*. Unlike
English "another," Spanish never puts an article in front of it: *otro libro*
("another book"), never *un otro libro* — a common learner mistake.

It works as an adjective before a noun (*otra pregunta*, "another question")
and, on its own, as a pronoun standing in for the noun (*Prefiero el otro*, "I
prefer the other one").

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number   | Form  |
| -------------------- | ------- |
| Masculine singular      | otro    |
| Feminine singular        | otra    |
| Masculine plural           | otros   |
| Feminine plural              | otras   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ¿Tienes otro lápiz?                          | Do you have another pencil?              | masculine singular, question       |
| Necesito otra oportunidad.                   | I need another chance.                   | feminine singular                  |
| Los otros estudiantes ya se fueron.          | The other students already left.         | masculine plural                   |
| Compré otras dos camisas.                    | I bought two more shirts.                | feminine plural                    |
| Este libro no me gustó; prefiero el otro.    | I didn't like this book; I prefer the other one. | pronoun, standing alone     |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[el-la-los-las|el / la / los / las]] — often paired before *otro*/*otra* (*el otro*, *la otra*), instead of an indefinite article.
- [[demás]] — a related idea, "the rest/the others," but invariable where *otro* agrees in gender and number.
