# alto / alta / altos / altas

## Meaning

*Alto* means "tall" (for people) or "high" (for objects, sounds, prices).
It agrees in gender and number: *alto*, *alta*, *altos*, *altas*.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number    | Form   |
| ------------------- | ------ |
| Masculine singular   | alto   |
| Feminine singular    | alta   |
| Masculine plural     | altos  |
| Feminine plural      | altas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi hermano es muy alto.                          | My brother is very tall.                      | masculine singular                 |
| ¿Es alta esa montaña?                            | Is that mountain high?                        | feminine singular, question         |
| Los precios no son tan altos aquí.               | The prices aren't so high here.               | masculine plural, negation          |
| Las temperaturas altas dañan los cultivos.       | High temperatures damage the crops.           | feminine plural                    |
| Habló en voz alta durante la reunión.            | He spoke in a loud voice during the meeting.  | feminine singular, fixed expression |
