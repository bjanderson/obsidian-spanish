# que

## Meaning

*Que* (no accent) does two main jobs: it links a subordinate clause to a main clause
("that"), and it stands in for a noun already mentioned ("which"/"who"/"that") as a
relative pronoun. It also shows up in comparisons ("than"). It's invariant — it never
changes form for gender or number.

Don't confuse *que* with [[qué]] (accented) — *qué* is a separate word, the
interrogative/exclamative "what" (*¿Qué quieres?*, "What do you want?").

## Core Uses

| Use                       | Pattern                         | Example                            | English                          |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Subordinating conjunction        | verb + que + [clause]                | Creo que llueve.                        | I think that it's raining.             |
| Relative pronoun (thing)          | [noun] + que + [clause]               | El libro que leí es bueno.               | The book that I read is good.          |
| Relative pronoun (person)          | [noun] + que + [clause]                | La mujer que habló es mi jefa.           | The woman who spoke is my boss.        |
| Comparative                          | más/menos + [adj] + que                 | Es más alto que yo.                      | He's taller than me.                    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Dice que va a llegar tarde.                 | He says (that) he's going to arrive late. | subordinating conjunction          |
| ¿Sabes que el examen es mañana?             | Did you know (that) the exam is tomorrow? | subordinating conjunction, question |
| La casa que compramos tiene jardín.         | The house that we bought has a garden.   | relative pronoun, thing            |
| El hombre que vive al lado es médico.       | The man who lives next door is a doctor. | relative pronoun, person           |
| Este café es mejor que el otro.             | This coffee is better than the other one. | comparative                        |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[qué]] — the accented interrogative/exclamative "what," a completely different word in writing.
- [[quien]] — the relative pronoun used only for people, an alternative to *que* after a preposition.
- [[cual]] — a more formal relative pronoun, usually with an article (*el cual*, *la cual*).
- [[cuyo-cuya-cuyos-cuyas|cuyo / cuya / cuyos / cuyas]] — a formal relative possessive, "whose," often replaced by *que... su* in casual speech.
