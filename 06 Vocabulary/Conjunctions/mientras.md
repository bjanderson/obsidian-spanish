# mientras

## Meaning

*Mientras* means "while" — it connects two actions happening at the same time (*Cocino
mientras escucho música*, "I cook while I listen to music"). *Mientras tanto* means
"meanwhile," used to link two separate sentences or clauses in sequence. It can also
mean "as long as," setting a condition tied to a duration (*Mientras vivamos aquí,
seguiremos ahorrando*, "As long as we live here, we'll keep saving"). The pattern
*mientras más... (más)...* expresses "the more... the more...": *Mientras más
practicas, más aprendes* ("The more you practice, the more you learn") — common
throughout Latin America, alongside *cuanto más... más...*. Introduced alongside
[[aunque]] and [[sino]] in this batch — worth reviewing together as a set, even though
each does a different grammatical job (simultaneity vs. concession vs. correction).

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                       | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Lavo los platos mientras tú secas.             | I wash the dishes while you dry.                     | simultaneous actions                 |
| Espera aquí; mientras tanto, voy a llamar a Ana.| Wait here; meanwhile, I'll call Ana.                 | *mientras tanto*                     |
| Mientras más lo pienso, menos me convence.      | The more I think about it, the less it convinces me. | *mientras más... menos...*           |
| No hables mientras comes.                       | Don't talk while you're eating.                      | negative command                     |
| Mientras vivamos aquí, seguiremos ahorrando.    | As long as we live here, we'll keep saving.          | "as long as," condition + subjunctive |
