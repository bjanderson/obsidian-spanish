# donde

## Meaning

*Donde* (no accent) is invariant and works as a relative adverb pointing back to a
place already mentioned — "where." Don't confuse it with *dónde* (accented), the
separate interrogative word "where" used in questions (*¿Dónde vives?*, "Where do you
live?"). After a preposition, *donde* is common on its own without a noun: *la
ciudad donde nací* ("the city where I was born").

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Este es el pueblo donde crecí.                       | This is the town where I grew up.              | relative adverb, place                |
| No sé donde dejé las llaves.                          | I don't know where I left the keys.            | embedded clause                        |
| Vive donde trabaja.                                    | She lives where she works.                     | relative adverb, no explicit noun      |
| Fuimos al restaurante donde nos conocimos.              | We went to the restaurant where we met.        | relative adverb, place                 |
| Siéntate donde quieras.                                 | Sit wherever you want.                         | relative adverb, open choice           |
