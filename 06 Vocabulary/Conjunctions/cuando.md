# cuando

## Meaning

*Cuando* (no accent) is invariant and introduces a time clause — "when." Don't
confuse it with *cuándo* (accented), the separate interrogative word "when" used in
questions (*¿Cuándo llegas?*, "When are you arriving?"). With a future or hypothetical
event, *cuando* triggers the subjunctive: *Te llamo cuando llegue* ("I'll call you
when I arrive" — the arrival hasn't happened yet).

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Cuando era niño, vivía en Sucre.                     | When I was a kid, I lived in Sucre.            | past time clause                      |
| Te aviso cuando termine el proyecto.                  | I'll let you know when I finish the project.  | future/hypothetical, subjunctive       |
| Siempre canta cuando cocina.                          | She always sings when she cooks.               | habitual action                        |
| Cuando llegues, llámame.                              | When you arrive, call me.                      | future/hypothetical, subjunctive       |
| No sabía qué hacer cuando sonó la alarma.             | I didn't know what to do when the alarm went off. | past time clause                    |
