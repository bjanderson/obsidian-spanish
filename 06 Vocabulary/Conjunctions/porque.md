# porque

## Meaning

*Porque* (one word, no accent) is invariant and introduces a reason — "because." It's
easy to confuse with two similar-looking forms:

- ***por qué*** (two words, accented) — the question word "why": *¿Por qué no
  viniste?* ("Why didn't you come?")
- ***el porqué*** (one word, accented, used as a noun) — "the reason": *No entiendo
  el porqué de su decisión* ("I don't understand the reason for his decision")

*Porque* itself only ever answers a "why," it doesn't ask one.

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| No fui porque estaba enfermo.               | I didn't go because I was sick.          | reason, answers "why"              |
| Estudio español porque quiero viajar.       | I study Spanish because I want to travel. | reason                             |
| Llegó tarde porque perdió el autobús.       | She arrived late because she missed the bus. | reason                          |
| ¿Por qué lloras? — Porque estoy triste.     | Why are you crying? — Because I'm sad.   | question and answer pair           |
| Lo hice porque me lo pediste.               | I did it because you asked me to.        | reason                             |
