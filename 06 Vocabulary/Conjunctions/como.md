# como

## Meaning

*Como* (no accent) is invariant and covers several related senses: a comparison
("like/as"), a causal reason at the start of a sentence ("since/as"), and a relative
adverb of manner ("how/the way"). Don't confuse it with *cómo* (accented), the
separate interrogative/exclamative word "how" (*¿Cómo estás?*, "How are you?").

## Core Uses

| Use                     | Pattern                 | Example                        | English                          |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Comparison (like/as)              | como + [noun/clause]           | Nada como el café de mi abuela.        | Nothing like my grandmother's coffee.    |
| Causal (since/as), sentence-initial   | Como + [clause], [main clause]    | Como llovía, no salimos.                | Since it was raining, we didn't go out.  |
| Manner (relative adverb)             | [verb] + como + [clause]             | Hazlo como quieras.                     | Do it however you want.                  |
| Approximation                          | como + [amount]                        | Cuesta como cien pesos.                 | It costs about a hundred pesos.          |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Baila como su madre.                        | She dances like her mother.              | comparison                         |
| Como no tenía dinero, no fue a la fiesta.   | Since he didn't have money, he didn't go to the party. | causal, sentence-initial |
| Lo hice como me explicaste.                 | I did it the way you explained it to me. | manner                             |
| Había como veinte personas en la sala.      | There were about twenty people in the room. | approximation                    |
| Es tan alto como su padre.                  | He's as tall as his father.              | comparison of equality             |
