# yo

## Meaning

*Yo* is the first-person singular subject pronoun, "I." Like other subject
pronouns, it's usually left out in Spanish, because the verb ending already
shows who's speaking: *Trabajo aquí* ("I work here"), not *Yo trabajo aquí*,
unless there's a real need for emphasis or contrast. *Yo* also appears after a
few specific words instead of [[mí]], most notably *entre* ("between") and
[[según]] ("according to"): *entre tú y yo* ("between you and me").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Trabajo desde casa los viernes.             | I work from home on Fridays.             | verb ending shows the subject, no *yo* needed |
| Yo prefiero el té; ella prefiere el café.   | I prefer tea; she prefers coffee.        | contrast, emphatic use              |
| ¿Y yo qué hago mientras tanto?              | And what am I supposed to do meanwhile?  | emphatic use, question              |
| Según yo, esa no es la mejor idea.           | In my opinion, that's not the best idea. | after *según*, instead of *mí*      |
| Yo sí quiero ir a la fiesta.                | I do want to go to the party.            | emphatic contrast                   |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[mí]] — the form used after most other prepositions instead of *yo*.
- [[me]] — the unstressed object-pronoun form, used right before a verb.
- [[según]] — one of the words that takes *yo* instead of [[mí]].
