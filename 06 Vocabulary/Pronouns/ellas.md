# ellas

## Meaning

*Ellas* is the third-person plural subject pronoun used when every member of
the group is female. If even one member is male, Spanish switches to [[ellos]]
instead. Like other subject pronouns, it's usually left out, since the verb
ending already shows who's acting, and appears mainly for emphasis, contrast,
or after a preposition.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Ellas estudian medicina juntas.             | They study medicine together.            | statement                          |
| ¿Ellas ya llegaron?                         | Have they already arrived?               | question                           |
| Mis hermanas cocinan bien; ellas aprendieron de mi abuela. | My sisters cook well; they learned from my grandmother. | contrast, emphatic use |
| Ellas no quieren ir a la fiesta.            | They don't want to go to the party.      | negation                           |
| El regalo es para ellas.                    | The gift is for them.                    | after preposition                  |
