# tú

## Meaning

*Tú* is the second-person singular informal subject pronoun, "you." Like other
subject pronouns, it's usually left out, because the verb ending already shows
who's being addressed: *¿Vienes a la reunión?* ("Are you coming to the
meeting?"), not *¿Tú vienes a la reunión?*, unless there's a real need for
emphasis or contrast. Its formal counterpart is [[usted-ustedes|usted]] — see
that entry for when to use one over the other.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ¿Tú vienes a la reunión?                    | Are you coming to the meeting?           | emphatic use, question              |
| Tú sí que sabes cocinar.                    | You really know how to cook.             | emphatic contrast                   |
| Entre tú y yo, ese plan no me convence.     | Between you and me, that plan doesn't convince me. | after *entre*             |
| Tú no entiendes la situación.               | You don't understand the situation.      | emphatic use, negation               |
| Vienes a la reunión, ¿verdad?               | You're coming to the meeting, right?     | verb ending shows the subject, no *tú* needed |
