# conmigo

## Meaning

*Conmigo* is the fused form of *con* + [[mí]] ("with me") — Spanish never says
*con mí*. It's invariable and always stands alone as this single word.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Ven conmigo al mercado.                     | Come with me to the market.              | statement                          |
| ¿Quieres venir conmigo esta noche?          | Do you want to come with me tonight?     | question                           |
| Nadie quiso ir conmigo.                     | No one wanted to go with me.             | negation                           |
| Ella siempre practica español conmigo.      | She always practices Spanish with me.    | statement                          |
| No cuentes conmigo para ese plan.           | Don't count on me for that plan.         | negation, idiom *contar con*        |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[mí]] — the pronoun *conmigo* fuses with *con* to replace.
- [[ti]] — the parallel form for "you," fused the same way into *contigo*.
- [[contigo]] — the parallel fused form for "with you."
