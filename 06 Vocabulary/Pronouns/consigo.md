# consigo

## Meaning

*Consigo* is the fused reflexive form of *con* + *sí* ("with him-/her-/
them-/yourself"), used when the subject of the sentence acts on or carries
something with itself: *Llevó el paraguas consigo* ("She took the umbrella
with her"). It only works reflexively — when *con* refers to someone other
than the subject, use *con él*, *con ella*, *con usted*, etc. instead.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Siempre lleva su cuaderno consigo.          | He always carries his notebook with him. | reflexive, statement                |
| ¿Trajo usted sus documentos consigo?        | Did you bring your documents with you?   | reflexive, formal, question         |
| No trajeron el pasaporte consigo.           | They didn't bring their passport with them. | reflexive, negation               |
| Ella carga esa culpa consigo desde hace años. | She's carried that guilt with her for years. | reflexive, figurative use        |
| Cada quien debe traer su propio almuerzo consigo. | Everyone should bring their own lunch with them. | reflexive, statement          |
