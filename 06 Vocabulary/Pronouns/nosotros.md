# nosotros

## Meaning

*Nosotros* is the first-person plural subject pronoun, "we," used for an
all-male or mixed-gender group. An all-female group uses [[nosotras]] instead.
Like other subject pronouns, it's usually left out, because the verb ending
already shows who's acting: *Trabajamos juntos* ("We work together"), not
*Nosotros trabajamos juntos*, unless there's a real need for emphasis or
contrast.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Trabajamos juntos en el mismo proyecto.     | We work together on the same project.    | verb ending shows the subject, no *nosotros* needed |
| Nosotros preferimos el tren; ellos prefieren el bus. | We prefer the train; they prefer the bus. | contrast, emphatic use             |
| ¿Nosotros pagamos la cuenta esta vez?       | Are we paying the bill this time?        | emphatic use, question              |
| Nosotros no sabíamos nada de eso.           | We didn't know anything about that.      | emphatic use, negation              |
| Entre nosotros, esto no va a funcionar.     | Between us, this isn't going to work.    | after *entre*                       |

## Related Vocabulary
