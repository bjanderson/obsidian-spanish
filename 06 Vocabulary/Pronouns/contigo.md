# contigo

## Meaning

*Contigo* is the fused form of *con* + [[ti]] ("with you," informal) — Spanish
never says *con ti*. It's invariable and always stands alone as this single
word. It follows the same pattern as [[conmigo]] ("with me").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Quiero hablar contigo un momento.           | I want to talk with you for a moment.    | statement                          |
| ¿Puedo ir contigo al aeropuerto?            | Can I go with you to the airport?        | question                           |
| No puedo salir contigo esta noche.          | I can't go out with you tonight.         | negation                           |
| Ella siempre está de acuerdo contigo.       | She always agrees with you.              | statement                          |
| Nadie quiere discutir contigo.              | No one wants to argue with you.          | negation                           |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[ti]] — the pronoun *contigo* fuses with *con* to replace.
- [[conmigo]] — the parallel fused form for "with me."
