# nosotras

## Meaning

*Nosotras* is the first-person plural subject pronoun used when every member
of the group is female. If even one member of the group is male, Spanish
switches to [[nosotros]] instead. Like other subject pronouns, it's usually
left out — the verb ending already shows who's acting — and appears mainly for
emphasis or contrast.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Nosotras vamos al gimnasio todos los días.  | We go to the gym every day.              | statement                          |
| ¿Nosotras pagamos la cuenta?                | Are we paying the bill?                  | question                           |
| Nosotras no sabíamos del cambio de horario. | We didn't know about the schedule change. | negation                           |
| Entre nosotras, ese secreto está a salvo.   | Between us, that secret is safe.         | after *entre*                       |
| Mi hermana y yo somos las únicas invitadas; nosotras llegamos temprano. | My sister and I are the only guests; we arrived early. | emphatic use |
