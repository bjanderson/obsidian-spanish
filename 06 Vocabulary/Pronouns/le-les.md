# le / les

## Meaning

*Le* and *les* are the third-person indirect object pronouns — *le* singular
("to him/to her/to you formal"), *les* plural ("to them/to you all"). They mark
the person on the receiving end of an action, separate from the direct object:
*Le di el libro* ("I gave the book to him/her" — *el libro* is the direct
object, *le* is who received it).

This vault avoids leísmo (using *le*/*les* as a direct object pronoun in place of
[[lo]]/la/los/las). Keep *le*/*les* for indirect objects only: *Lo vi* ("I saw
him," direct object, not *Le vi*).

Right before a direct object pronoun starting with *l-* (*lo*, *la*, *los*,
*las*), both *le* and *les* change to [[se]]: *Le lo dije* is wrong; *Se lo dije*
("I told it to him/her") is correct — see B1.4.1.2.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Le mandé un mensaje a mi jefe.              | I sent a message to my boss.             | indirect object, singular          |
| ¿Le puedo hacer una pregunta, doctora?      | Can I ask you a question, doctor?        | indirect object, formal "you"       |
| No le dije la verdad.                       | I didn't tell him/her the truth.         | indirect object, negation          |
| Les mandé el contrato a los clientes.       | I sent the contract to the clients.      | indirect object, plural            |
| ¿Les puedo ofrecer algo de tomar?           | Can I offer you all something to drink?  | indirect object, "you all"          |
| No les dijimos la verdad todavía.           | We haven't told them the truth yet.      | indirect object, negation, plural   |
