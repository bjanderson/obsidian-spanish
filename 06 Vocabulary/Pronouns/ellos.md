# ellos

## Meaning

*Ellos* is the third-person plural subject pronoun for a group of men, or any
mixed-gender group — even a group of 99 women and one man still takes *ellos*.
The all-feminine counterpart is *ellas* (not yet tracked here).

Like other subject pronouns, *ellos* is usually left out, since the verb
ending already shows who's doing the action: *Llegaron tarde* ("They arrived
late"), not *Ellos llegaron tarde*, unless there's a real need for emphasis or
contrast, or the subject would otherwise be unclear. *Ellos* also appears after
prepositions: *con ellos* ("with them"), *para ellos* ("for them").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Ellos trabajan en la misma empresa.         | They work at the same company.           | subject, mixed-gender group        |
| Fuimos al cine con ellos.                    | We went to the movies with them.         | after a preposition                |
| Ellos sí quieren ir, pero nosotros no.      | They do want to go, but we don't.        | contrast, emphatic use              |
| El regalo es para ellos.                     | The gift is for them.                    | after a preposition                |
| ¿Son ellos los que llamaron?                 | Are they the ones who called?            | question, emphatic use              |
