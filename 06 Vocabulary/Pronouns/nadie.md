# nadie

## Meaning

*Nadie* means "no one" or "nobody" — the negative counterpart of [[alguien]].
Like [[nada]], it follows the standard Spanish double-negative pattern: *No
vino nadie* ("No one came," literally "Didn't come no one"). If *nadie* comes
before the verb, *no* is dropped: *Nadie vino*. Because *nadie* refers to a
person, it takes the personal *a* when used as a direct object: *No conozco a
nadie aquí* ("I don't know anyone here").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| No hay nadie en la oficina.                 | There's no one in the office.            | *no* + *nadie*, double negative     |
| ¿No vino nadie a la reunión?                | Didn't anyone come to the meeting?       | question                           |
| Nadie sabe la respuesta.                    | No one knows the answer.                 | *nadie* before the verb, no *no*   |
| No conozco a nadie en esta ciudad.          | I don't know anyone in this city.        | personal *a*, double negative       |
| Nadie me avisó del cambio.                  | No one told me about the change.         | *nadie* before the verb            |
