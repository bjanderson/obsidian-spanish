# ella

## Meaning

*Ella* is the third-person singular feminine subject pronoun, "she" — and, for
a feminine noun, "it." Like other subject pronouns, it's usually left out,
since the verb ending already shows who's doing the action: *Llegó tarde*
("She arrived late"), not *Ella llegó tarde*, unless there's a real need for
emphasis or contrast. *Ella* also appears after prepositions: *con ella* ("with
her"), *para ella* ("for her").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Ella es mi profesora de español.            | She's my Spanish teacher.                | subject, introducing someone        |
| Fui a la fiesta con ella.                    | I went to the party with her.            | after a preposition                 |
| Ella sí sabe la respuesta.                   | She does know the answer.                | contrast, emphatic use              |
| Este mensaje es para ella.                   | This message is for her.                 | after a preposition                 |
| ¿Fue ella quien te llamó?                    | Was she the one who called you?          | question, emphatic use              |
