# cual

## Meaning

*Cual* means "which" or "which one." As an interrogative, it's almost always
accented — *cuál* — and used with *ser*: *¿Cuál es tu color favorito?* ("What's
your favorite color?"). As a relative pronoun (unaccented *cual*), it usually
follows an article — *el cual*, *la cual*, *los cuales*, *las cuales* — and
adds a bit more precision or formality than plain [[que]]: *la casa en la cual
crecí* ("the house in which I grew up").

Don't confuse accented *cuál* (question word) with unaccented *cual* (relative
pronoun) — the same accent-marks-the-difference pattern as [[que|que/qué]] and
[[quien|quien/quién]].

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ¿Cuál de estos dos prefieres?               | Which of these two do you prefer?        | interrogative, accented             |
| No sé cuál es la respuesta correcta.        | I don't know which is the correct answer. | indirect question                   |
| La empresa para la cual trabajo es pequeña.  | The company I work for is small.         | relative, *para la cual*             |
| ¿Cuáles son tus planes para el fin de semana? | What are your plans for the weekend?    | interrogative, plural                |
| Ese es el motivo por el cual me fui.         | That's the reason I left.                | relative, *por el cual*              |
