# qué

## Meaning

*Qué* (accented) is the interrogative "what" — used in direct and indirect
questions (*¿Qué quieres?*, "What do you want?"; *No sé qué hacer*, "I don't
know what to do") and in exclamations (*¡Qué bonito!*, "How pretty!").

Don't confuse it with unaccented [[que]], the relative pronoun/conjunction
("that," "which") already covered separately — the accent is the only thing
that tells them apart in writing, and it changes the word's job completely:
*el libro que compré* ("the book that I bought," relative *que*) versus *¿Qué
libro compraste?* ("What book did you buy?," interrogative *qué*).

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ¿Qué hora es?                               | What time is it?                       | direct question                    |
| No entiendo qué pasó.                       | I don't understand what happened.      | indirect question                  |
| ¡Qué calor hace hoy!                        | It's so hot today!                     | exclamation                        |
| ¿Qué quieres comer esta noche?              | What do you want to eat tonight?       | direct question                    |
| Dime qué necesitas.                         | Tell me what you need.                 | indirect question                  |
