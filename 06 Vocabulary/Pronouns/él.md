# él

## Meaning

*Él* (accented) is the third-person singular masculine subject pronoun, "he" —
and, for a masculine noun, "it." The accent is what separates it from
unaccented [[el-la-los-las|el]], the masculine singular definite article
("the"); they sound the same but have completely different jobs.

Like other subject pronouns, *él* is usually left out, since the verb ending
already shows who's doing the action: *Llegó tarde* ("He arrived late"), not
*Él llegó tarde*, unless there's a real need for emphasis or contrast. *Él*
also appears after prepositions: *con él* ("with him"), *para él* ("for
him").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Él es mi hermano mayor.                     | He's my older brother.                   | subject, introducing someone       |
| Fui al partido con él.                       | I went to the game with him.             | after a preposition                 |
| Él sí terminó el proyecto a tiempo.         | He did finish the project on time.       | contrast, emphatic use              |
| Este regalo es para él.                     | This gift is for him.                    | after a preposition                 |
| ¿Fue él quien llamó?                        | Was he the one who called?               | question, emphatic use              |
