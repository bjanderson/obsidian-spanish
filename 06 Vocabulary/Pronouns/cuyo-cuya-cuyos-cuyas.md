# cuyo / cuya / cuyos / cuyas

## Meaning

*Cuyo* is a relative possessive pronoun meaning "whose." It agrees in gender
and number with the noun it introduces — the thing that's possessed — not with
the possessor: *la mujer cuya hija estudia aquí* ("the woman whose daughter
studies here") uses *cuya* because *hija* is feminine, even though *mujer* is
also feminine — the agreement is a coincidence there, not the rule.

*Cuyo* has a formal, literary register. In everyday spoken Spanish, people
often restructure the sentence instead: *el escritor cuyo libro leí* could
become *el escritor, leí su libro* in casual speech.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number   | Form   |
| ------------------ | ------ |
| Masculine singular | cuyo   |
| Feminine singular  | cuya   |
| Masculine plural   | cuyos  |
| Feminine plural    | cuyas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Ese es el escritor cuyo libro leí.          | That's the writer whose book I read.     | masculine singular                  |
| Conozco a la mujer cuya hija estudia aquí.  | I know the woman whose daughter studies here. | feminine singular, personal *a* |
| Es una empresa cuyos empleados viajan mucho. | It's a company whose employees travel a lot. | masculine plural                 |
| Vive en una casa cuyas ventanas dan al mar. | She lives in a house whose windows face the sea. | feminine plural            |
| El profesor, cuyo nombre no recuerdo, era muy exigente. | The teacher, whose name I don't remember, was very demanding. | masculine singular, embedded clause |
