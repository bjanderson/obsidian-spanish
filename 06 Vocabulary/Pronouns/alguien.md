# alguien

## Meaning

*Alguien* is the indefinite pronoun for an unspecified person — "someone." In
a question it often works like English "anyone": *¿Hay alguien en casa?* ("Is
anyone home?"). Its opposite is [[nadie]] ("no one"). Because *alguien* refers
to a person, it takes the personal *a* when used as a direct object: *¿Conoces
a alguien aquí?* ("Do you know anyone here?").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Alguien llamó mientras no estabas.          | Someone called while you were out.       | statement                          |
| ¿Hay alguien en casa?                       | Is anyone home?                          | question                           |
| Necesito que alguien me ayude.              | I need someone to help me.               | statement                          |
| ¿Conoces a alguien aquí?                    | Do you know anyone here?                 | question, personal *a*             |
| Alguien dejó la puerta abierta.             | Someone left the door open.              | statement                          |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[algo]] — the equivalent indefinite pronoun for a thing, "something."
