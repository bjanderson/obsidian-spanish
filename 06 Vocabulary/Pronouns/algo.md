# algo

## Meaning

*Algo* is the indefinite pronoun for "something" — an unspecified thing. In a
question it often works like English "anything": *¿Necesitas algo?* ("Do you
need anything?"). Its opposite is [[nada]] ("nothing").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Tengo algo para ti.                         | I have something for you.                | statement                          |
| ¿Viste algo extraño anoche?                 | Did you see anything strange last night? | question                           |
| Algo me dice que va a llover.               | Something tells me it's going to rain.   | subject position                   |
| ¿Falta algo en la lista?                    | Is anything missing from the list?       | question                           |
| Come algo antes de salir.                   | Eat something before you leave.          | statement                          |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[nada]] — the opposite, "nothing."
- [[alguien]] — the equivalent indefinite pronoun for a person, "someone."
- [[cosa]] — near-synonym — *algo* is "something" (indefinite), *cosa* is "a thing" (a concrete noun).
