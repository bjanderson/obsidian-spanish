# mí

## Meaning

*Mí* is the form of "me" used right after most prepositions: *para mí* ("for
me"), *de mí* ("of/about me"), *a mí* ("to me," often added alongside [[me]]
for emphasis: *A mí me gusta*, "I like it"). The accent mark is what separates
it from the possessive adjective *mi* ("my," no accent — *mi casa*, "my
house").

The one big exception is *con* ("with"): *con* + *mí* fuses into [[conmigo]]
("with me"), not *con mí*.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Este regalo es para mí.                     | This gift is for me.                   | after *para*                       |
| No te olvides de mí.                        | Don't forget about me.                 | after *de*                         |
| A mí me encanta esta canción.               | I love this song.                      | emphatic, before *me* + verb        |
| Se sentó cerca de mí.                       | She sat near me.                       | after *cerca de*                   |
| ¿Hay algún mensaje para mí?                 | Is there a message for me?             | after *para*, question              |
