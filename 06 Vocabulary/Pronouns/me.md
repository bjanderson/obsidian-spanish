# me

## Meaning

*Me* is the first-person singular object pronoun and covers three roles that
English splits into separate words: direct object ("me"), indirect object ("to
me"), and reflexive ("myself"). Which one it is depends on the verb.

## Core Uses

| Use                | Pattern              | Example                    | English                        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Direct object              | [verb] + me                  | Me llamó anoche.                  | She called me last night.             |
| Indirect object                | [verb] + me                    | Me dio un consejo.                | She gave me some advice.               |
| Reflexive                          | [verb] + me                      | Me levanto a las siete.           | I get up at seven.                     |
| With *gustar*-type verbs               | me + gusta/encanta/etc.             | Me gusta el café.                 | I like coffee.                         |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Me vieron en el parque ayer.                | They saw me in the park yesterday.       | direct object                      |
| ¿Me puedes explicar esto?                   | Can you explain this to me?              | indirect object, question          |
| Me ducho antes de dormir.                   | I shower before sleeping.                | reflexive                          |
| Me encanta esta canción.                    | I love this song.                        | *gustar*-type verb                 |
| No me dijeron nada sobre el cambio.         | They didn't tell me anything about the change. | indirect object, negation      |
