# firmar

## Meaning

*Firmar* means "to sign" — putting your signature on a document, contract, check,
or form. It's a regular *-ar* verb: no stem changes, no spelling changes, no
irregularities anywhere in the paradigm.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | firmo       |
| tú                       | firmas      |
| usted / él / ella        | firma       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | firmamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | firman      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                                  | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Firmo el contrato mañana.                      | I'm signing the contract tomorrow.             | *yo*                       |
| ¿Firmas aquí, por favor?                       | Will you sign here, please?                    | *tú*, question              |
| El gerente firma todos los cheques.            | The manager signs all the checks.              | *él*, noun object           |
| Firmamos el acuerdo en la oficina del abogado. | We sign the agreement at the lawyer's office.  | *nosotros*                  |
| Mis padres no firman documentos sin leerlos.   | My parents don't sign documents without reading them. | *ellos*, negation    |
