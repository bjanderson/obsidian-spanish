# preguntar

## Meaning

*Preguntar* means "to ask" in the sense of asking a question. Don't confuse it with
*pedir* ("to ask for," requesting something): *Le pregunté la hora* ("I asked him
the time") vs. *Le pedí un favor* ("I asked him for a favor"). It's a fully regular
*-ar* verb with no stem changes or spelling adjustments in any tense.

## Related Vocabulary

- [[pedir]] — "to ask for" a thing, in contrast to *preguntar*'s "to ask" a question.
- [[responder]] — the natural pair — you *preguntar* (ask) so that someone can *responder* (answer).
