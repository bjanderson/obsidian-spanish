# dibujar

## Meaning

*Dibujar* means "to draw" — creating a picture with pencil, pen, or similar tools:
*Dibujo un gato* (I'm drawing a cat). It's a completely regular *-ar* verb with no
stem changes or spelling shifts in any tense. It's specifically about drawing/
sketching, distinct from *pintar* ("to paint," which involves color and paint) —
though the two are often mentioned together as artistic activities.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | dibujo      |
| tú                       | dibujas     |
| usted / él / ella        | dibuja      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | dibujamos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | dibujan     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Dibujo animales en mi tiempo libre.       | I draw animals in my free time.       | *yo*                          |
| ¿Dibujas bien o solo por diversión?       | Do you draw well, or just for fun?    | *tú*, question                |
| Ella dibuja retratos increíbles.          | She draws incredible portraits.       | *ella*                          |
| Dibujamos un mapa del vecindario.         | We draw a map of the neighborhood.    | *nosotros*                     |
| Mis alumnos no dibujan sin inspiración.   | My students don't draw without inspiration. | *ellos*, negation          |
