# apagar

## Meaning

*Apagar* means "to turn off" or "to switch off" — lights, appliances, a phone —
and also "to put out" a fire or a cigarette: *Apaga la luz* ("Turn off the
light"), *Los bomberos apagaron el incendio* ("The firefighters put out the
fire"). It's a regular *-ar* verb with one spelling change: the *g* takes a *u*
right before an *e*, to keep the hard *g* sound — that only affects the *yo*
preterite (*apagué*, not *apagé*) and the entire present subjunctive.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | apago       |
| tú                       | apagas      |
| usted / él / ella        | apaga       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | apagamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | apagan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Apago la computadora antes de dormir.          | I turn off the computer before I sleep.        | *yo*                              |
| ¿Apagas el celular en las reuniones?           | Do you turn off your phone during meetings?    | *tú*, question                    |
| Ella apaga las luces al salir del cuarto.      | She turns off the lights when she leaves the room. | *ella*                        |
| Apagamos el aire acondicionado por la noche.   | We turn off the air conditioning at night.     | *nosotros*                        |
| No apagan la televisión ni para comer.         | They don't turn off the TV even to eat.        | *ellos*, negation                 |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                                  | Notes                       |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish   | English  |
| ---------------- | --------- | -------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | apagando  | turning off |
| Past Participle  | apagado   | turned off |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Apagando poco a poco las luces, cerró la tienda. | Turning off the lights one by one, he closed the shop. | gerund, adverbial use |
| El televisor ya está apagado.                    | The TV is already turned off.            | past participle as adjective |
