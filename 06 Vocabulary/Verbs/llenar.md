# llenar

## Meaning

*Llenar* means "to fill": *Llené el vaso de agua* ("I filled the glass with
water"). It also covers "to fill out" a form (*llenar un formulario*) and,
reflexively, *llenarse* means "to fill up" or "to get full": *El estadio se
llenó rápido* ("The stadium filled up fast"). It's a fully regular *-ar*
verb — no stem change and no spelling change.
