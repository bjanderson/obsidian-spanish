# elegir

## Meaning

*Elegir* means "to choose" or "to select" — picking one option among several: *Elijo
el vestido azul* (I choose the blue dress). It also means "to elect" in a political
sense: *El pueblo elige al presidente* (The people elect the president). Two things
change its spelling across the paradigm: it's an *e → i* stem-changing verb (like
*pedir*), and it has a *g → j* spelling change any time the ending starts with *a* or
*o*, to keep the "kh" sound of the *g* consistent (*elijo*, not *eligo*; *elija*, not
*eliga*). Its past participle is the regular *elegido*, though the irregular form
*electo* survives as a set adjective (*presidente electo* — president-elect).

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | elijo        |
| tú                       | eliges       |
| usted / él / ella        | elige        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | elegimos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | eligen       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Elijo el camino más corto. | I choose the shortest path. | *yo*, *g → j* before *o* |
| ¿Eliges el restaurante esta vez? | Are you choosing the restaurant this time? | *tú*, question |
| Ella elige a sus amigos con cuidado. | She chooses her friends carefully. | *ella*, personal *a* |
| No elegimos el clima que nos toca. | We don't choose the weather we get. | *nosotros*, negation (no stem change) |
| Los votantes eligen al alcalde cada cuatro años. | Voters elect the mayor every four years. | *ellos*, personal *a* |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[decidir]] — closely related: *decidir* is deciding in general, *elegir* is choosing among specific options.
- [[pedir]] — another *e → i* stem-changing *-ir* verb, useful for comparing the pattern.
- [[seguir]] — also has a spelling change (*g → gu*) to keep its consonant sound, a useful contrast to *elegir*'s *g → j*.
- [[escoger]] — an exact synonym; *escoger* is the fully regular *-er* verb version of the same idea (only the *g → j* spelling change, no stem change).
