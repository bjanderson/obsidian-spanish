# comprar

## Meaning

*Comprar* means "to buy" or "to purchase" — acquiring something in exchange for
money: *Compro pan todos los días* (I buy bread every day). It's a completely regular
*-ar* verb in every tense — no stem changes, no spelling changes, no irregular
participle.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | compro       |
| tú                       | compras      |
| usted / él / ella        | compra       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | compramos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | compran      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Compro verduras frescas en el mercado. | I buy fresh vegetables at the market. | *yo* |
| ¿Compras el periódico todos los domingos? | Do you buy the newspaper every Sunday? | *tú*, question |
| Ella compra regalos para toda la familia. | She buys gifts for the whole family. | *ella* |
| No compramos nada sin comparar precios primero. | We don't buy anything without comparing prices first. | *nosotros*, negation |
| Mis padres compran la casa de sus sueños. | My parents are buying the house of their dreams. | *ellos* |
