# acabar

## Meaning

*Acabar* means "to finish" or "to end." Its most common everyday use is the phrase
*acabar de + infinitive*, meaning "to have just done something": *Acabo de llegar*
("I just arrived"). It's a fully regular *-ar* verb.

## Present Indicative

| Subject | Conjugation |
| --- | --- |
| yo | acabo |
| tú | acabas |
| usted / él / ella | acaba |
| nosotros / nosotras | acabamos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas | acaban |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acabo de comer. | I just ate. | *yo*, *acabar de* |
| ¿Acabas la tarea hoy? | Are you finishing the homework today? | *tú*, question |
| Ella acaba el turno a las seis. | She finishes her shift at six. | *ella* |
| Acabamos de llegar a casa. | We just got home. | *nosotros*, *acabar de* |
| No acaban el proyecto a tiempo. | They don't finish the project on time. | negation |
