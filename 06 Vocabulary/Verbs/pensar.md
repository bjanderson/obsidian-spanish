# pensar

## Meaning

*Pensar* means "to think." *Pensar en* means "to think about" (someone or
something on your mind): *Pienso en ti* ("I think about you"). *Pensar* +
infinitive means "to plan to": *Pienso viajar en diciembre* ("I plan to
travel in December"). It's an *e → ie* stem-changing verb in the present — the
stem vowel changes in every present-tense form except *nosotros*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | pienso      |
| tú                       | piensas     |
| usted / él / ella        | piensa      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | pensamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | piensan     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Pienso en ti todos los días.               | I think about you every day.          | *yo*, *pensar en*                |
| ¿Qué piensas de esta idea?                 | What do you think of this idea?       | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella piensa mudarse el próximo año.        | She plans to move next year.          | *ella*, *pensar* + infinitive     |
| Pensamos que es una buena decisión.        | We think it's a good decision.        | *nosotros*                      |
| No piensan igual que nosotros.             | They don't think the same as us.      | *ellos*, negation                 |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
