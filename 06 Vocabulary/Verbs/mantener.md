# mantener

## Meaning

*Mantener* means "to maintain," "to keep," or "to support" (financially). It's built
from *tener* with the prefix *man-*, and it conjugates exactly like *tener* in every
tense — irregular *yo* form, irregular preterite stem, irregular future/conditional
stem.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | mantengo    |
| tú                       | mantienes   |
| usted / él / ella        | mantiene    |
| nosotros / nosotras      | mantenemos  |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | mantienen   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Mantengo mi cuarto ordenado.                | I keep my room tidy.                   | *yo*                          |
| ¿Mantienes contacto con tus amigos de la escuela? | Do you keep in touch with your school friends? | *tú*, question         |
| Ella mantiene a toda su familia.            | She supports her whole family.         | *ella*                          |
| Mantenemos la casa limpia entre todos.      | We keep the house clean together.      | *nosotros*                       |
| No mantienen las promesas que hacen.        | They don't keep the promises they make. | *ellos*, negation                |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form     | Affirmative | Negative       |
| -------- | ----------- | --------------- |
| tú       | mantén      | no mantengas   |
| usted    | mantenga    | no mantenga    |

| ustedes  | mantengan   | no mantengan   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Mantén la calma, por favor.               | Stay calm, please.                     | *tú*, affirmative                     |
| No mantenga esa distancia con el equipo.  | Don't keep that distance from the team. | *usted*, negative                     |
| Mantengamos esta rutina por un mes más.   | Let's keep this routine for one more month. | *nosotros*, affirmative            |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Gerund (-ing)    | manteniendo  | maintaining |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                          | English                                  | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Manteniendo la calma, resolvió el problema rápido.   | By staying calm, she solved the problem quickly.    | gerund, adverbial use          |
| El edificio está bien mantenido.                     | The building is well maintained.                    | past participle as adjective |
