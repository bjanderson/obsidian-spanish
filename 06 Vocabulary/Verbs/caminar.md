# caminar

## Meaning

*Caminar* means "to walk" — moving on foot, either literally (walking somewhere) or
as a general activity: *Camino al trabajo* (I walk to work), *Me gusta caminar*
(I like walking). It's a completely regular *-ar* verb with no stem changes or
spelling shifts in any tense. It largely overlaps with *andar*, though *caminar*
leans toward walking as an act of covering distance on foot, while *andar* is often
broader ("to go around," "to be").

## Related Vocabulary

- [[andar]] — a close synonym; *andar* leans more toward "to go around" or "to be [doing something]."
- [[correr]] — a natural contrast: *caminar* is walking, *correr* is running.
