# responder

## Meaning

*Responder* means "to answer," "to respond," or "to reply": *Respondí su
correo ayer* ("I answered his email yesterday"). *Responder a* is used
when the thing being answered follows as an object of the preposition:
*Respondió a la pregunta sin dudar* ("He answered the question without
hesitation"). It's a fully regular *-er* verb — no stem change and no
spelling change, including a fully regular past participle (*respondido*),
unlike the related noun *respuesta*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | respondo    |
| tú                       | respondes   |
| usted / él / ella        | responde    |
| nosotros / nosotras      | respondemos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | responden   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Respondo todos los correos antes de las diez. | I answer all the emails before ten.  | *yo*                            |
| ¿Respondes rápido a los mensajes?          | Do you reply to messages quickly?     | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella nunca responde al teléfono en clase.  | She never answers her phone in class. | *ella*, negation                 |
| Respondemos a las quejas en 24 horas.      | We respond to complaints within 24 hours. | *nosotros*                    |
| No responden a mis preguntas.              | They don't answer my questions.       | *ellos*, negation                 |
