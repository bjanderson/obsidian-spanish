# disfrutar

## Meaning

*Disfrutar* means "to enjoy" — taking pleasure in something: *Disfruto la música*
(I enjoy the music). It's very often used with the preposition *de*: *Disfruto de la
música* means the same thing, and *de* is required when the object is an activity or
experience rather than a concrete thing (*Disfrutamos del viaje* — We enjoyed the
trip). It's a completely regular *-ar* verb in every tense — no stem changes, no
spelling changes, no irregular participle.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | disfruto     |
| tú                       | disfrutas    |
| usted / él / ella        | disfruta     |
| nosotros / nosotras      | disfrutamos  |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | disfrutan    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Disfruto de un buen café por las mañanas. | I enjoy a good coffee in the mornings. | *yo*, *de* + noun |
| ¿Disfrutas tu nuevo trabajo? | Do you enjoy your new job? | *tú*, question |
| Ella disfruta mucho de la naturaleza. | She really enjoys nature. | *ella* |
| No disfrutamos el viaje por el mal clima. | We didn't enjoy the trip because of the bad weather. | *nosotros*, negation |
| Los niños disfrutan del parque los fines de semana. | The kids enjoy the park on weekends. | *ellos*, *del* contraction |

| ustedes   | disfruten   | no disfruten     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Disfruta el momento, no pienses en el trabajo. | Enjoy the moment, don't think about work. | *tú*, affirmative |
| No disfrutes tanto el descanso que se te olvide la tarea. | Don't enjoy the break so much that you forget the assignment. | *tú*, negative |
| Disfrutemos de esta noche sin prisas. | Let's enjoy tonight without rushing. | *nosotros*, affirmative |
| No disfruten el postre antes de terminar la sopa. | Don't enjoy dessert before finishing the soup. | *ustedes*, negative |
| Disfrute usted su jubilación. | Enjoy your retirement. | *usted*, affirmative |
