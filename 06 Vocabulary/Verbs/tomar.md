# tomar

## Meaning

*Tomar* is a regular *-ar* verb meaning "to take" (*Tomo el bus a las ocho*,
"I take the bus at eight") or "to drink" (*Tomo café por las mañanas*, "I
drink coffee in the mornings"). It also shows up in set phrases like *tomar
una decisión* ("to make a decision") and *tomarse un descanso* ("to take a
break").
