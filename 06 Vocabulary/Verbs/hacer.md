# hacer

## Meaning

*Hacer* covers both "to do" and "to make" — *Hago la tarea* ("I do the
homework"), *Hizo un pastel* ("She made a cake"). It also shows up in weather
expressions (*Hace calor*, "It's hot") and time expressions (*Hace dos años*,
"Two years ago"). It's irregular in the *yo* form of the present, throughout
the preterite, and has an irregular future/conditional stem and past
participle.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | hago        |
| tú                       | haces       |
| usted / él / ella        | hace        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | hacemos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | hacen       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Hago ejercicio todas las mañanas.          | I exercise every morning.             | *yo*                            |
| ¿Qué haces los fines de semana?            | What do you do on weekends?           | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella hace las compras los sábados.         | She does the shopping on Saturdays.   | *ella*                          |
| Hacemos la cena juntos.                    | We make dinner together.              | *nosotros*                      |
| No hacen suficiente ejercicio.             | They don't exercise enough.           | *ellos*, negation                 |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                       | English                                  | Notes                        |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative      |
| --------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| tú        | haz         | no hagas      |
| usted     | haga        | no haga       |
| nosotros  | hagamos     | no hagamos    |
| ustedes   | hagan       | no hagan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Haz la cama antes de salir.          | Make the bed before you leave.      | *tú*, affirmative, irregular form   |
| No hagas eso, es peligroso.          | Don't do that, it's dangerous.      | *tú*, negative                      |
| Hagan silencio, por favor.           | Please be quiet.                    | *ustedes*, affirmative               |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish  | English |
| ---------------- | -------- | ------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | haciendo | doing/making |
| Past Participle  | hecho    | done/made |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Haciendo un esfuerzo, terminó a tiempo.          | Making an effort, he finished on time.   | gerund, adverbial use         |
| Ya está hecho, no te preocupes.                  | It's already done, don't worry.          | past participle as adjective  |
