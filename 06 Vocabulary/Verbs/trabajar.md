# trabajar

## Meaning

*Trabajar* means "to work." *Trabajar de/como* + job title means "to work
as": *Trabajo de mesera* ("I work as a waitress"). *Trabajar en* + place/topic
means "to work at/on": *Trabaja en un hospital* ("She works at a hospital").
It's a fully regular *-ar* verb throughout.
