# recordar

## Meaning

*Recordar* means "to remember" (*Recuerdo tu nombre*, "I remember your
name") or "to remind" someone of something, using an indirect object pronoun:
*Recuérdame llamarlo* ("Remind me to call him"). It's an *o → ue*
stem-changing verb in the present — the stem vowel changes in every
present-tense form except *nosotros*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | recuerdo    |
| tú                       | recuerdas   |
| usted / él / ella        | recuerda    |
| nosotros / nosotras      | recordamos  |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | recuerdan   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Recuerdo mi infancia con cariño.           | I remember my childhood fondly.       | *yo*                            |
| ¿Recuerdas dónde dejaste las llaves?       | Do you remember where you left your keys? | *tú*, question              |
| Ella me recuerda a su hermana.             | She reminds me of her sister.         | *ella*, "to remind"                |
| Recordamos ese día perfectamente.          | We remember that day perfectly.       | *nosotros*                      |
| No recuerdan el nombre del hotel.          | They don't remember the hotel's name. | *ellos*, negation                 |
