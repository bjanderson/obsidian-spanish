# bailar

## Meaning

*Bailar* means "to dance" — moving to music, either as a general activity or a
specific dance: *Me encanta bailar salsa* (I love dancing salsa). It's a completely
regular *-ar* verb with no stem changes or spelling shifts in any tense. It can be
used with or without a direct object: *bailar* (to dance in general) or *bailar
[un género/una canción]* (to dance a specific style or song).

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | bailo       |
| tú                       | bailas      |
| usted / él / ella        | baila       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | bailamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | bailan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Bailo salsa desde niño.                   | I've danced salsa since I was a kid.  | *yo*                          |
| ¿Bailas tango o solo lo miras?            | Do you dance tango or just watch it?  | *tú*, question                |
| Ella baila con mucha gracia.              | She dances very gracefully.           | *ella*                        |
| Bailamos toda la noche en la fiesta.      | We dance all night at the party.      | *nosotros*                    |
| Mis hermanos no bailan en público.        | My siblings don't dance in public.    | *ellos*, negation              |
