# expresar

## Meaning

*Expresar* means "to express" — putting a thought, feeling, or idea into words or
another form: *Expreso mi opinión* (I express my opinion). It's a completely regular
*-ar* verb in every tense. It's very commonly used reflexively, *expresarse*, meaning
"to express oneself" (*Se expresa muy bien* — He expresses himself very well) — worth
recognizing even though this file covers the non-reflexive, transitive form.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | expreso      |
| tú                       | expresas     |
| usted / él / ella        | expresa      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | expresamos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | expresan     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Expreso mis dudas antes de decidir. | I express my doubts before deciding. | *yo* |
| ¿Expresas tus sentimientos fácilmente? | Do you express your feelings easily? | *tú*, question |
| Ella expresa su gratitud a sus padres. | She expresses her gratitude to her parents. | *ella* |
| No expresamos suficiente aprecio por el equipo. | We don't express enough appreciation for the team. | *nosotros*, negation |
| Los artistas expresan sus ideas a través del color. | Artists express their ideas through color. | *ellos* |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish    | English    |
| ---------------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | expresando | expressing |
| Past Participle  | expresado  | expressed  |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Expresando con calma, logró convencer a todos. | Expressing himself calmly, he managed to convince everyone. | gerund, adverbial use |
| Está expresando su punto de vista en la reunión. | She's expressing her point of view at the meeting. | gerund with *estar* |
| El deseo expresado por el cliente fue muy claro. | The wish expressed by the client was very clear. | past participle as adjective |
