# pintar

## Meaning

*Pintar* means "to paint" — a wall, a picture, or figuratively, "to paint a
picture" of a situation. It's a fully regular *-ar* verb with no stem changes or
spelling adjustments in any tense.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | pinto       |
| tú                       | pintas      |
| usted / él / ella        | pinta       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | pintamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | pintan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Pinto la casa cada dos años.                | I paint the house every two years.      | *yo*                               |
| ¿Pintas al óleo o a la acuarela?             | Do you paint in oil or watercolor?      | *tú*, question                     |
| Ella pinta retratos por encargo.             | She paints portraits on commission.     | *ella*                             |
| Pintamos la cerca de blanco.                 | We paint the fence white.               | *nosotros*                         |
| Los estudiantes pintan un mural en la escuela. | The students are painting a mural at school. | *ellos*                       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                       | English                       | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
