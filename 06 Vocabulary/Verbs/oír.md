# oír

## Meaning

*Oír* means "to hear" — the passive perception of sound, as opposed to *escuchar*
("to listen," the active act of paying attention). It's irregular: the *yo* form
adds a *g* (*oigo*), and a *y* is inserted before the ending everywhere except
*nosotros* and the infinitive (*oyes*, *oye*, *oyen*).

## Present Indicative

| Subject | Conjugation |
| --- | --- |
| yo | oigo |
| tú | oyes |
| usted / él / ella | oye |
| nosotros / nosotras | oímos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas | oyen |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No oigo bien de este oído. | I don't hear well out of this ear. | *yo*, negation |
| ¿Oyes ese ruido? | Do you hear that noise? | *tú*, question |
| Ella oye música mientras trabaja. | She listens to music while she works. | *ella* |
| Oímos las noticias todas las mañanas. | We hear the news every morning. | *nosotros* |
| Los vecinos oyen todo desde su balcón. | The neighbors hear everything from their balcony. | *ellos* |
