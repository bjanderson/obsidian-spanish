# traer

## Meaning

*Traer* means "to bring" — moving something toward the speaker, the opposite of
*llevar* ("to take/carry away"). It's irregular: the *yo* form is *traigo*, and the
preterite has an irregular stem (*traj-*) shared with *decir*- and *producir*-type
verbs — note the 3rd-person plural is *trajeron*, not *trajieron*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject | Conjugation |
| --- | --- |
| yo | traigo |
| tú | traes |
| usted / él / ella | trae |
| nosotros / nosotras | traemos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas | traen |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traigo el postre a la cena. | I'm bringing dessert to dinner. | *yo* |
| ¿Traes tu computadora mañana? | Are you bringing your laptop tomorrow? | *tú*, question |
| Ella siempre trae buenas noticias. | She always brings good news. | *ella* |
| Traemos vino para la fiesta. | We're bringing wine for the party. | *nosotros* |
| No traen los documentos necesarios. | They're not bringing the necessary documents. | negation |
