# llevar

## Meaning

*Llevar* is a regular *-ar* verb covering several everyday senses: "to carry"
(*Llevo una mochila*, "I'm carrying a backpack"), "to wear" (*Lleva un
vestido azul*, "She's wearing a blue dress"), and "to take someone/something
somewhere" (*Te llevo al aeropuerto*, "I'll take you to the airport"). *Llevar*
+ time expression means "to have been (doing something) for": *Llevo dos años
aquí* ("I've been here for two years").

## Related Vocabulary

- [[poner]] — reflexive *ponerse* means "to put on" clothing, a related but distinct sense from *llevar*'s "to wear."
- [[traer]] — the opposite direction, "to bring (toward the speaker)," vs. *llevar*'s "to take (away from the speaker)."
- [[recoger]] — related — you *recoger* (pick up) something and then *llevar* (carry) it.
