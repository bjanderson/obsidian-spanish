# abrir

## Meaning

*Abrir* means "to open" — a door, a business, an event, a conversation. It's fully
regular in every conjugated tense, but its past participle is irregular: *abierto*,
not *abrido*. That matters for the Present Perfect table and for *abierto* used as
an adjective ("open").

## Present Indicative

| Subject | Conjugation |
| --- | --- |
| yo | abro |
| tú | abres |
| usted / él / ella | abre |
| nosotros / nosotras | abrimos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas | abren |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abro la tienda a las nueve. | I open the store at nine. | *yo* |
| ¿Abres tú la ventana? | Do you open the window? | *tú*, question |
| Ella abre el correo cada mañana. | She opens her email every morning. | *ella* |
| Abrimos temprano los sábados. | We open early on Saturdays. | *nosotros* |
| No abren los domingos. | They don't open on Sundays. | negation |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form | Affirmative | Negative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tú | abre | no abras |
| usted | abra | no abra |
| nosotros | abramos | no abramos |
| ustedes | abran | no abran |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abre la puerta, por favor. | Open the door, please. | *tú*, affirmative |
| No abras esa carta todavía. | Don't open that letter yet. | *tú*, negative |
| Abra la ventana, por favor. | Please open the window. | *usted*, affirmative |
| Abramos las puertas a las ocho. | Let's open the doors at eight. | *nosotros* |
| No abran hasta que yo llegue. | Don't open until I arrive. | *ustedes*, negative |
