# hablar

## Meaning

*Hablar* means "to speak" or "to talk," both for languages (*hablo español*, I speak
Spanish) and for the act of talking with someone (*hablar con alguien*, to talk to/
with someone). It's a regular *-ar* verb — its endings follow the standard pattern
for every tense, with no stem changes or irregular forms.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | hablo       |
| tú                       | hablas      |
| usted / él / ella        | habla       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | hablamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | hablan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Hablo un poco de portugués.               | I speak a little Portuguese.           | *yo*                         |
| ¿Hablas con tu hermana todos los días?    | Do you talk to your sister every day?  | *tú*, question               |
| Ella habla muy rápido cuando está nerviosa. | She talks very fast when she's nervous. | *ella*                      |
| No hablamos de política en el trabajo.    | We don't talk about politics at work.  | *nosotros*, negation          |
| Mis colegas hablan inglés y español.      | My colleagues speak English and Spanish. | *ellos*                     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                              | English                             | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Habla más despacio, por favor.       | Speak more slowly, please.          | *tú*, affirmative              |
| No hables con la boca llena.         | Don't talk with your mouth full.    | *tú*, negative                  |
| Hable con recepción, por favor.      | Please speak with the front desk.   | *usted*, affirmative            |
| Hablemos de esto mañana.             | Let's talk about this tomorrow.     | *nosotros*, affirmative         |
| No hablen todos al mismo tiempo.     | Don't all talk at the same time.    | *ustedes*, negative             |
