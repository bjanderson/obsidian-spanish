# conseguir

## Meaning

*Conseguir* means "to get" or "to achieve" — obtaining something, often through
effort. Followed by an infinitive, it means "to manage to" do something: *Conseguí
terminar a tiempo* ("I managed to finish on time"). It's an *e → i* stem-changing
verb, like *seguir* — the present-tense stem drops the *e* everywhere except
*nosotros* (*consigo*, *consigues*... but *conseguimos*).

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation  |
| ------------------------ | -------------- |
| yo                       | consigo      |
| tú                       | consigues    |
| usted / él / ella        | consigue     |
| nosotros / nosotras      | conseguimos  |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | consiguen    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Nunca consigo estacionamiento aquí.         | I never manage to find parking here.     | *yo*, negation                |
| ¿Consigues buenos precios en ese mercado?   | Do you get good prices at that market?   | *tú*, question                 |
| Ella consigue lo que se propone.            | She achieves whatever she sets out to do. | *ella*                          |
| Conseguimos entradas para el concierto.     | We got tickets for the concert.           | *nosotros*                       |
| Muchos no consiguen empleo fácilmente.      | Many people don't easily get a job.       | *ellos*, negation                 |
