# enseñar

## Meaning

*Enseñar* means "to teach": *Enseño español* (I teach Spanish). It also means "to
show" in the sense of displaying or revealing something: *Le enseñé mis fotos* (I
showed him my photos). Followed by *a* + infinitive, it means "to teach someone how
to do something": *Le enseñé a nadar* (I taught him to swim). It's a completely
regular *-ar* verb with no stem changes or spelling shifts in any tense — the *ñ*
stays the same throughout.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | enseño      |
| tú                       | enseñas     |
| usted / él / ella        | enseña      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | enseñamos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | enseñan     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Enseño matemáticas en una escuela pública. | I teach math at a public school.      | *yo*                          |
| ¿Enseñas a los principiantes?             | Do you teach the beginners?           | *tú*, question                |
| Ella enseña a sus hijos a cocinar.        | She teaches her kids to cook.         | *ella*, *a* + infinitive       |
| Enseñamos español a extranjeros.          | We teach Spanish to foreigners.       | *nosotros*                    |
| Mis colegas no enseñan los sábados.       | My colleagues don't teach on Saturdays. | *ellos*, negation            |
