# compartir

## Meaning

*Compartir* means "to share" — food, space, time, or information with someone else.
It's a fully regular *-ir* verb throughout every tense.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation  |
| ------------------------ | -------------- |
| yo                        | comparto      |
| tú                         | compartes     |
| usted / él / ella          | comparte      |
| nosotros / nosotras         | compartimos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas      | comparten     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Comparto mi almuerzo con mis amigos.        | I share my lunch with my friends.           | *yo*                           |
| ¿Compartes tu cuarto con tu hermano?        | Do you share your room with your brother?   | *tú*, question                 |
| Diego comparte la comida con todos.         | Diego shares the food with everyone.        | *él*                            |
| Compartimos las mismas ideas.               | We share the same ideas.                    | *nosotros*                       |
| No comparten sus juguetes fácilmente.       | They don't share their toys easily.         | *ellos*, negation                 |
