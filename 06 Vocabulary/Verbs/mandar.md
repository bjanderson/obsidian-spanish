# mandar

## Meaning

*Mandar* has two common meanings. As "to send," it's largely interchangeable with
*enviar*: *Te mando el archivo* (I'll send you the file) — *mandar* is the more
everyday, spoken-register choice, while *enviar* often sounds slightly more formal
or written. As "to order" or "to command," *mandar* means telling someone what to
do, usually with authority: *El jefe me manda hacer esto* (The boss orders me to do
this). When *mandar* introduces a command indirectly with *que*, the following verb
goes in the subjunctive: *Mandó que saliéramos* (He ordered us to leave). It's a
completely regular *-ar* verb with no stem changes or spelling shifts in any tense.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | mando       |
| tú                       | mandas      |
| usted / él / ella        | manda       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | mandamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | mandan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Mando dinero a mi familia cada mes.       | I send money to my family every month. | *yo*, "to send"               |
| ¿Mandas tú los correos o los mando yo?    | Do you send the emails, or do I?      | *tú*, question                |
| Mi jefa manda en la oficina.              | My boss is in charge at the office.   | *ella*, "to be in charge"      |
| Mandamos la invitación a todos.           | We send the invitation to everyone.   | *nosotros*                    |
| Mis padres no mandan mensajes de texto.   | My parents don't send text messages.  | *ellos*, negation              |
