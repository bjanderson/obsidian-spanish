# actuar

## Meaning

*Actuar* means "to act" in the sense of taking action or behaving a certain way:
*Actuó rápido* (He acted quickly), *Actúa como si nada pasara* (He acts as if nothing
happened). It also means "to act" in the theatrical sense, performing a role: *Actúa
en una obra de teatro* (She's acting in a play). *Actuar* belongs to a small group of
*-uar* verbs (also *continuar*, *graduar*) where the stressed *u* takes a written
accent in the forms where the stress naturally falls on the stem — every present-tense
form except *nosotros* — to keep the *u* and the following vowel as two separate
syllables rather than a diphthong: *actúo*, *actúas*, *actúa*, *actuamos*, *actúan*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | actúo        |
| tú                       | actúas       |
| usted / él / ella        | actúa        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | actuamos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | actúan       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Actúo con calma en situaciones difíciles. | I act calmly in difficult situations. | *yo*, written accent on *ú* |
| ¿Actúas así con todos tus amigos? | Do you act this way with all your friends? | *tú*, question |
| Ella actúa en una película nueva. | She's acting in a new movie. | *ella* |
| No actuamos sin pensarlo primero. | We don't act without thinking it through first. | *nosotros*, no accent needed, negation |
| Los abogados actúan en nombre de sus clientes. | The lawyers act on behalf of their clients. | *ellos* |
