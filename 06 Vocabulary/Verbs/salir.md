# salir

## Meaning

*Salir* means "to leave" or "to go out": *Salgo a las ocho* ("I leave at
eight"). *Salir con* means "to date" or "go out with" someone romantically:
*Sale con Marta* ("He's dating Marta"). It's irregular in the *yo* form of the
present (*salgo*) and has an irregular future/conditional stem (*saldr-*);
otherwise it follows the regular *-ir* pattern.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | salgo       |
| tú                       | sales       |
| usted / él / ella        | sale        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | salimos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | salen       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Salgo del trabajo a las cinco.             | I get off work at five.               | *yo*, irregular *yo* form         |
| ¿Sales con alguien ahora?                  | Are you dating anyone right now?      | *tú*, question, *salir con*       |
| Ella sale a correr todas las mañanas.      | She goes running every morning.       | *ella*                          |
| Salimos juntos los viernes.                | We go out together on Fridays.        | *nosotros*                      |
| No salen mucho los fines de semana.        | They don't go out much on weekends.   | *ellos*, negation                 |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish   | English |
| ---------------- | --------- | ------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | saliendo  | leaving/going out |
| Past Participle  | salido    | left/gone out |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Saliendo del cine, empezó a llover.              | Leaving the movie theater, it started to rain. | gerund, adverbial use   |
| Ha salido de viaje esta mañana.                  | She's left on a trip this morning.       | past participle, compound tense |
