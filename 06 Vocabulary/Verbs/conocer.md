# conocer

## Meaning

*Conocer* means "to know" in the sense of being familiar with a person,
place, or thing: *Conozco Sucre bien* ("I know Sucre well"). It contrasts with
[[saber]], which means "to know" facts or how to do something. In the
preterite, *conocer* shifts meaning to "met" (for the first time): *La conocí
en una fiesta* ("I met her at a party"). Its only irregularity is a *c → zc*
change in the *yo* form of the present and throughout the subjunctive.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | conozco     |
| tú                       | conoces     |
| usted / él / ella        | conoce      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | conocemos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | conocen     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Conozco a tu hermana.                      | I know your sister.                   | *yo*, personal *a*, irregular *zc* |
| ¿Conoces bien esta ciudad?                 | Do you know this city well?           | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella conoce a mucha gente aquí.            | She knows a lot of people here.       | *ella*, personal *a*              |
| Conocemos ese restaurante.                 | We know that restaurant.              | *nosotros*                      |
| No conocen a nadie en el pueblo.           | They don't know anyone in town.       | *ellos*, negation, personal *a*    |
