# quedar

## Meaning

*Quedar* is a regular *-ar* verb meaning "to remain" or "to be left over":
*Quedan dos entradas* ("There are two tickets left"). *Quedar en* + infinitive
means "to agree to": *Quedamos en vernos a las ocho* ("We agreed to meet at
eight"). The reflexive *quedarse* means "to stay": *Me quedo en casa* ("I'm
staying home").

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | quedo       |
| tú                       | quedas      |
| usted / él / ella        | queda       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | quedamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | quedan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Me quedo en casa este fin de semana.       | I'm staying home this weekend.        | *yo*, reflexive                  |
| ¿Quedas con tus amigos los viernes?        | Do you meet up with your friends on Fridays? | *tú*, question, "to meet up" |
| Solo queda una entrada.                    | There's only one ticket left.         | impersonal, "to be left over"     |
| Quedamos en llamarnos mañana.              | We agreed to call each other tomorrow. | *nosotros*, "to agree to"          |
| No quedan asientos disponibles.            | There are no seats left available.    | impersonal, negation                |
