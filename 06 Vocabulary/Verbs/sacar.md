# sacar

## Meaning

*Sacar* means "to take out" or "to remove" something from somewhere, and by
extension "to get/obtain" (*sacar una foto*, "to take a photo"; *sacar buenas
notas*, "to get good grades"). It's a fully regular *-ar* verb in most tenses,
except the preterite *yo* form has a *c → qu* spelling change (*saqué*, not
*sacé*) to keep the hard *c* sound before *e*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | saco        |
| tú                       | sacas       |
| usted / él / ella        | saca        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | sacamos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | sacan       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Saco la basura todas las noches.            | I take out the trash every night.      | *yo*                          |
| ¿Sacas buenas notas este semestre?          | Are you getting good grades this semester? | *tú*, question              |
| Ella siempre saca fotos en sus viajes.      | She always takes photos on her trips.  | *ella*                          |
| Sacamos entradas para el partido.           | We're getting tickets for the game.    | *nosotros*                       |
| No sacan la basura los domingos.            | They don't take out the trash on Sundays. | *ellos*, negation             |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                         | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Saca la basura antes de salir.           | Take out the trash before you leave.   | *tú*, affirmative                     |

| Saquemos las sillas al patio.            | Let's take the chairs out to the yard. | *nosotros*, affirmative                |
