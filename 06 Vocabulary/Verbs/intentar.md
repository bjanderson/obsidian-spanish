# intentar

## Meaning

*Intentar* means "to try/to attempt," usually followed by an infinitive: *Intenté
llamarte* ("I tried to call you"). It's a fully regular *-ar* verb — no stem
changes, no spelling adjustments, no irregular forms anywhere in the paradigm.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | intento     |
| tú                       | intentas    |
| usted / él / ella        | intenta     |
| nosotros / nosotras      | intentamos  |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | intentan    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                   | English                                | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Intento comer más sano.                     | I try to eat healthier.                       | *yo*                        |
| ¿Intentas resolverlo tú solo?                | Are you trying to solve it by yourself?       | *tú*, question               |
| Ella intenta hablar español todos los días.  | She tries to speak Spanish every day.          | *ella*                       |
| Intentamos llegar a un acuerdo.              | We're trying to reach an agreement.            | *nosotros*                   |
| No intentan cambiar nada.                    | They aren't trying to change anything.         | *ellos*, negation             |
