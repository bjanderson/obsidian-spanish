# golpear

## Meaning

*Golpear* means "to hit" or "to strike" — a fist, a door, a table, or a ball. It's a
fully regular *-ar* verb with no stem changes or spelling adjustments in any tense.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | golpeo      |
| tú                       | golpeas     |
| usted / él / ella        | golpea      |

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
