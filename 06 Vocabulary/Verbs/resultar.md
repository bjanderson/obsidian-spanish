# resultar

## Meaning

*Resultar* means "to result" or "to turn out" — the outcome of something becomes
clear: *Resultó ser mentira* ("It turned out to be a lie"). It's a fully regular
*-ar* verb, and it's most often used in the 3rd person, describing how a situation
or thing turned out, though every person exists grammatically.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | resulto     |
| tú                       | resultas    |
| usted / él / ella        | resulta     |
| nosotros / nosotras      | resultamos  |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | resultan    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Resulto ser el único que llegó a tiempo.    | I turn out to be the only one who arrived on time. | *yo*                    |
| ¿Resultas beneficiado con este cambio?      | Do you end up benefiting from this change? | *tú*, question              |
| El plan resulta más caro de lo esperado.    | The plan turns out to be more expensive than expected. | *él*                     |
| Resultamos ganadores del concurso.          | We ended up as winners of the contest.  | *nosotros*                       |
| Los números no resultan como esperábamos.   | The numbers don't come out as we expected. | *ellos*, negation             |

## Commands (Imperative)

*Resultar* describes an outcome, not an action someone can be ordered to perform,
so it has no natural imperative use — the forms exist only for grammatical
completeness and aren't included here as a table.

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish     | English    |
| ---------------- | ----------- | ---------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | resultando  | resulting  |
| Past Participle  | resultado   | resulted   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                           | English                                  | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Resultando ganador, celebró con su equipo.          | Ending up the winner, he celebrated with his team. | gerund, adverbial use        |
| El estudio ha resultado en nuevas políticas.        | The study has resulted in new policies.           | past participle, compound tense |
