# reír

## Meaning

*Reír* means "to laugh." The reflexive *reírse (de)* is the more common
everyday form, meaning "to laugh (at)": *Se ríe de todo* ("He laughs at
everything"). *Reír* is an *e → i* stem-changing verb: the stem vowel
changes in every present-tense form except *nosotros*, and the same shift
shows up in the third-person preterite and the gerund. It also has an
orthographic quirk from the accented *í* in the infinitive: the third-person
preterite forms drop the *i* of the ending (*rio*, *rieron*, not *riió*),
and modern spelling rules (RAE, 2010) write *rio* without an accent because
it's a single syllable, unlike its close relative *sonreír* (*sonrió*, two
syllables, keeps the accent).

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | río         |
| tú                       | ríes        |
| usted / él / ella        | ríe         |
| nosotros / nosotras      | reímos      |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | ríen        |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Río mucho con sus chistes.                 | I laugh a lot at his jokes.           | *yo*                             |
| ¿Por qué te ríes tanto?                    | Why are you laughing so much?         | *tú*, question, reflexive        |
| Ella ríe con ganas en cada película.       | She laughs heartily at every movie.   | *ella*                            |
| No nos reímos de nadie en esta casa.       | We don't laugh at anyone in this house. | *nosotros*, negation, reflexive |
| Los niños ríen al ver al payaso.           | The kids laugh when they see the clown. | *ellos*                          |
