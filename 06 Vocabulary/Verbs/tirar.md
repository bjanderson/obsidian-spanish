# tirar

## Meaning

*Tirar* covers a cluster of related meanings depending on context: "to throw" (*tirar
una pelota*), "to pull" (*tirar de la cuerda*), and "to throw away" (*tirar la
basura*). It's a fully regular *-ar* verb with no stem changes or spelling
adjustments in any tense.
