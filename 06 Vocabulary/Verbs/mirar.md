# mirar

## Meaning

*Mirar* means "to look at" — a deliberate, directed action, unlike [[ver]]
("to see"), which is more passive. *Mira eso* ("Look at that") calls
attention to something; *Veo eso* just states that you perceive it. It's a
regular *-ar* verb throughout.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | miro        |
| tú                       | miras       |
| usted / él / ella        | mira        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | miramos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | miran       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Miro las estrellas por las noches.         | I look at the stars at night.         | *yo*                            |
| ¿Miras la hora, por favor?                 | Can you check the time, please?       | *tú*, question, idiom             |
| Ella mira su teléfono constantemente.      | She looks at her phone constantly.    | *ella*                          |
| Miramos el atardecer desde el balcón.      | We watch the sunset from the balcony. | *nosotros*                      |
| No miran las noticias por la mañana.       | They don't watch the news in the morning. | *ellos*, negation             |
