# vender

## Meaning

*Vender* means "to sell." It's a fully regular *-er* verb — no stem changes,
spelling changes, or irregular forms anywhere in its conjugation.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | vendo       |
| tú                       | vendes      |
| usted / él / ella        | vende       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | vendemos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | venden      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Vendo ropa usada los fines de semana.       | I sell used clothes on weekends.       | *yo*                                |
| ¿Vendes tu carro viejo?                     | Are you selling your old car?          | *tú*, question                      |
| Ella vende artesanías en el mercado.        | She sells handicrafts at the market.   | *ella*                              |
| Vendemos productos locales en la tienda.    | We sell local products in the store.   | *nosotros*                          |
| No venden alcohol después de las diez.      | They don't sell alcohol after ten.     | *ellos*, negation                   |
