# considerar

## Meaning

*Considerar* means "to consider" — to think something over, or to regard something
as having a certain quality (*La considero mi amiga*, "I consider her my friend").
It's a fully regular *-ar* verb with no stem changes or irregular forms.

| Subject | Conjugation |
| --- | --- |

| tú | consideras |
| usted / él / ella | considera |
