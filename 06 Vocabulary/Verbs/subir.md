# subir

## Meaning

*Subir* means "to go up" or "to climb": *Subo las escaleras* (I go up the stairs). It
also covers "to get on/into" a vehicle (*subir al autobús* — to get on the bus, with
the *al* contraction), "to increase" (*Los precios suben* — Prices are going up), and,
in modern usage, "to upload" (*Subí las fotos a internet* — I uploaded the photos to
the internet). It's a completely regular *-ir* verb in every tense — no stem changes,
no spelling changes, no irregular participle.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | subo         |
| tú                       | subes        |
| usted / él / ella        | sube         |
| nosotros / nosotras      | subimos      |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | suben        |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Subo al autobús en la esquina. | I get on the bus at the corner. | *yo*, *al* contraction |
| ¿Subes las fotos a la nube? | Do you upload the photos to the cloud? | *tú*, question |
| Ella sube la voz cuando está emocionada. | She raises her voice when she's excited. | *ella* |
| No subimos al segundo piso sin permiso. | We don't go up to the second floor without permission. | *nosotros*, negation |
| Los precios suben cada mes. | Prices go up every month. | *ellos/precios* |
