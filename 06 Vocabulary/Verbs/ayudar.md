# ayudar

## Meaning

*Ayudar* means "to help" or "to assist." It's a fully regular *-ar* verb, no stem
changes or spelling quirks. The person helped is usually marked with the personal
*a* when it's a specific person as a direct object (*Ayudé a mi hermano*, "I helped
my brother"), and *ayudar a + infinitive* means "to help (someone) do something."

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | ayudo       |
| tú                       | ayudas      |
| usted / él / ella        | ayuda       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | ayudamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | ayudan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Ayudo a mi madre con las compras.              | I help my mother with the groceries.          | *yo*, personal *a*                |
| ¿Ayudas a tus vecinos alguna vez?               | Do you ever help your neighbors?              | *tú*, question, personal *a*       |
| Ella ayuda a los estudiantes con matemáticas.   | She helps students with math.                 | *ella*, personal *a*               |
| Ayudamos a organizar el evento.                  | We help organize the event.                   | *nosotros*                        |
| No ayudan mucho en casa.                          | They don't help much around the house.        | *ellos*, negation                 |
