# ir

## Meaning

*Ir* means "to go." It's one of the most irregular verbs in Spanish — its
preterite and imperfect subjunctive forms are borrowed entirely from [[ser]]
(*fui*, *fuiste*...), and context is what tells them apart. *Ir* + *a* +
infinitive is the everyday way to talk about the near future: *Voy a comer*
("I'm going to eat").

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | voy         |
| tú                       | vas         |
| usted / él / ella        | va          |
| nosotros / nosotras      | vamos       |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | van         |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Voy a la tienda ahora.                     | I'm going to the store now.           | *yo*                            |
| ¿Vas al gimnasio hoy?                      | Are you going to the gym today?       | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella va a comer con sus amigas.            | She's going to eat with her friends.  | *ella*, near future             |
| Vamos a la playa este fin de semana.       | We're going to the beach this weekend. | *nosotros*, near future         |
| No van a la fiesta esta noche.             | They aren't going to the party tonight. | *ellos*, negation               |

## Present Subjunctive

| Subject                 | Conjugation |

| usted / él / ella        | vaya        |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                       | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Espero que vaya bien la entrevista.               | I hope the interview goes well.               | impersonal, wish clause          |

## Imperfect Subjunctive

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | fuera       |
| tú                       | fueras      |
| usted / él / ella        | fuera       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | fuéramos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | fueran      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                          | English                                    | Notes                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Si fuera al mercado, compraría fruta.                  | If I went to the market, I'd buy fruit.         | *yo*, *si* clause, shared with *ser* |
| Ojalá fueras conmigo esta vez.                         | I wish you'd go with me this time.              | *tú*                              |
| Ella dudaba que él fuera a tiempo.                     | She doubted he would go on time.                | *él*, past doubt clause          |
| Nos pidieron que fuéramos temprano.                    | They asked us to go early.                      | *nosotros*, request clause         |
| Querían que fueran juntos a la reunión.                | They wanted them to go to the meeting together. | *ellos*, wish clause               |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative      |
| --------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| tú        | ve          | no vayas      |
| usted     | vaya        | no vaya       |
| nosotros  | vamos       | no vayamos    |
| ustedes   | vayan       | no vayan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Ve a comprar pan, por favor.         | Go buy bread, please.               | *tú*, affirmative, irregular form   |
| No vayas solo, es peligroso.         | Don't go alone, it's dangerous.     | *tú*, negative                      |
| Vamos a comer, tengo hambre.         | Let's go eat, I'm hungry.           | *nosotros*, affirmative, "let's go" |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish | English |
| ---------------- | ------- | ------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | yendo   | going   |
| Past Participle  | ido     | gone    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Yendo por ese camino, llegarás antes.            | Going that way, you'll get there sooner. | gerund, adverbial use, rarely used compared to other gerunds |
| Se ha ido sin decir nada.                        | He's left without saying anything.       | past participle, compound tense |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[ser]] — shares the exact same preterite and imperfect-subjunctive forms with *ir*; context tells them apart.
- [[venir]] — the opposite direction, "to come," toward the speaker instead of away.
- [[ver]] — shares the same *tú* affirmative command form, *ve*.
