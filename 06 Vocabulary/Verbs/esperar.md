# esperar

## Meaning

*Esperar* covers three related senses: "to wait" (*Espero el bus*, "I'm
waiting for the bus"), "to hope" (*Espero que vengas*, "I hope you come"), and
"to expect" (*No esperaba esta respuesta*, "I wasn't expecting this answer").
Note there's no separate word for "for" — *esperar* already includes it:
*Espero a mi hermana*, not *espero para mi hermana*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | espero      |
| tú                       | esperas     |
| usted / él / ella        | espera      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | esperamos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | esperan     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Espero el bus en la esquina.               | I wait for the bus on the corner.     | *yo*, "to wait for"                |
| ¿Esperas una respuesta pronto?             | Are you expecting an answer soon?     | *tú*, question, "to expect"        |
| Ella espera a su hermana en el aeropuerto. | She's waiting for her sister at the airport. | *ella*, personal *a*         |
| Esperamos que todo salga bien.             | We hope everything goes well.         | *nosotros*, "to hope"               |
| No esperan visitas hoy.                    | They aren't expecting visitors today. | *ellos*, negation, "to expect"      |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish    | English |
| ---------------- | ---------- | ------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | esperando  | waiting/hoping |
| Past Participle  | esperado   | waited/expected |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Esperando el momento justo, no dijo nada.        | Waiting for the right moment, he said nothing. | gerund, adverbial use   |
| El resultado era esperado.                       | The result was expected.                 | past participle as adjective |
