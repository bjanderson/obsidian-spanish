# formar

## Meaning

*Formar* means "to form" or "to shape" — a team, a shape, a habit, a person's
character. Used reflexively (*formarse*), it can mean "to be trained" or "to
develop": *Se formó como ingeniera* ("She trained as an engineer"). It's a fully
regular *-ar* verb.
