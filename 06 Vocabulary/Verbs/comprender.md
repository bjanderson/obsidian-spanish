# comprender

## Meaning

*Comprender* means "to understand" — grasping the meaning of something. It's a
close synonym of *entender*, used somewhat more formally, and is a fully regular
*-er* verb with no stem changes or spelling adjustments in any tense.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation  |
| ------------------------ | -------------- |
| yo                       | comprendo    |
| tú                       | comprendes   |
| usted / él / ella        | comprende    |
| nosotros / nosotras      | comprendemos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | comprenden   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                   | English                             | Notes                  |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Comprendo tu punto de vista.                | I understand your point of view.      | *yo*                      |
| ¿Comprendes la lección de hoy?              | Do you understand today's lesson?     | *tú*, question             |
| Ella comprende varios idiomas.              | She understands several languages.    | *ella*                      |
| Comprendemos la situación.                  | We understand the situation.          | *nosotros*                  |
| Los estudiantes comprenden el ejercicio.    | The students understand the exercise. | *ellos*                     |

## Commands (Imperative)

*Comprender* is rarely commanded directly — understanding isn't something you can
just decide to do on order. It shows up mostly in soft requests.

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative          |
| --------- | ----------- | ------------------ |
| tú        | comprende   | no comprendas      |
| usted     | comprenda   | no comprenda       |
| nosotros  | comprendamos | no comprendamos   |
| ustedes   | comprendan  | no comprendan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Comprende que hago esto por ti.      | Understand that I'm doing this for you. | *tú*, affirmative                |
| No comprendas mal mis palabras.      | Don't misunderstand my words.       | *tú*, negative                       |
