# deber

## Meaning

*Deber* + infinitive expresses obligation — "must" or "should": *Debes
estudiar* ("You should study"). The conditional forms (*debería*, etc.) soften
it into friendly advice: *Deberías descansar* ("You should rest"). *Deber* can
also mean "to owe": *Me debes diez dólares* ("You owe me ten dollars"). It's a
regular *-er* verb throughout.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | debo        |
| tú                       | debes       |
| usted / él / ella        | debe        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | debemos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | deben       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Debo terminar este informe hoy.            | I must finish this report today.      | *yo*, obligation                |
| ¿Cuánto me debes?                          | How much do you owe me?               | *tú*, "to owe," question         |
| Ella debe llegar antes de las nueve.       | She must arrive before nine.          | *ella*, obligation               |
| Debemos cuidar el medio ambiente.          | We must take care of the environment. | *nosotros*, obligation           |
| No deben mucho dinero.                     | They don't owe much money.            | *ellos*, "to owe," negation       |

## Commands (Imperative)

*Deber* is rarely used as a direct command — obligation isn't something you
order someone into with an imperative the way you order an action. Spanish
speakers use *debes* + infinitive as a statement instead, or a true command
form of the action verb itself.

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish  | English |
| ---------------- | -------- | ------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | debiendo | owing/having to |
| Past Participle  | debido   | owed; due |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| El retraso se debe al mal clima.                 | The delay is due to the bad weather.     | past participle, *deberse a* |
| Debido a la lluvia, cancelaron el partido.       | Due to the rain, they canceled the game. | past participle, *debido a*  |
