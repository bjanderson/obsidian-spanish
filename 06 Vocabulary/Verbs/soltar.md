# soltar

## Meaning

*Soltar* means "to let go of" or "to release" something you were holding — a hand,
a rope, an animal, or even a piece of news ("to let slip"). It's an *o → ue*
stem-changing verb: the stem vowel changes in every present-tense form except
*nosotros*. Its past participle is regular (*soltado*), unlike some similar-looking
verbs such as *resolver* (*resuelto*).

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | suelto      |
| tú                       | sueltas     |
| usted / él / ella        | suelta      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | soltamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | sueltan     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Nunca suelto la mano de mi hijo en la calle.    | I never let go of my son's hand on the street. | *yo*                            |
| ¿Sueltas al perro en el parque?                  | Do you let the dog off the leash at the park? | *tú*, question                   |
| Ella suelta el cabello antes de dormir.          | She lets her hair down before sleeping.        | *ella*                           |
| Soltamos los globos al final de la fiesta.       | We release the balloons at the end of the party. | *nosotros*                    |
| Los niños sueltan la cuerda sin querer.          | The kids let go of the rope by accident.       | *ellos*                          |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish    | English            |
| ---------------- | ---------- | ------------------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | soltando   | letting go/releasing |
| Past Participle  | soltado    | let go/released      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Se fue soltando lágrimas sin decir nada.         | She left, letting tears fall without saying anything. | gerund, ongoing action |
| El nudo quedó soltado sin que nadie lo tocara.   | The knot came undone without anyone touching it. | past participle as adjective |
