# viajar

## Meaning

*Viajar* means "to travel" — going somewhere, usually a distance, for tourism, work,
or any other reason: *Viajo mucho por trabajo* (I travel a lot for work). It's a
completely regular *-ar* verb with no stem changes or spelling shifts in any tense.
It's often followed by *a* (destination) or *en* (mode of transportation): *viajar a
Perú*, *viajar en tren*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | viajo       |
| tú                       | viajas      |
| usted / él / ella        | viaja       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | viajamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | viajan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Viajo a Colombia dos veces al año.        | I travel to Colombia twice a year.    | *yo*                          |
| ¿Viajas mucho por tu trabajo?             | Do you travel a lot for your job?     | *tú*, question                |
| Ella viaja sola casi siempre.             | She almost always travels alone.      | *ella*                        |
| Viajamos en autobús hasta la frontera.    | We travel by bus to the border.       | *nosotros*, mode of transport |
| Mis primos no viajan fuera del país.      | My cousins don't travel outside the country. | *ellos*, negation       |

## Related Vocabulary
