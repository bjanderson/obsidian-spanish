# ver

## Meaning

*Ver* means "to see" or "to watch" — *Veo la tele* ("I watch TV"), *¿Viste
eso?* ("Did you see that?"). It's mostly regular, but keeps its stem vowel *e*
in the imperfect (*veía*, not the usual contracted pattern) and has an
irregular past participle, *visto*. Its *tú* affirmative command, *ve*, is
spelled the same as [[ir]]'s — context tells them apart.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | veo         |
| tú                       | ves         |
| usted / él / ella        | ve          |
| nosotros / nosotras      | vemos       |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | ven         |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Veo la televisión por las noches.          | I watch TV at night.                  | *yo*                            |
| ¿Ves bien desde ahí?                       | Can you see well from there?          | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella ve a sus padres cada domingo.         | She sees her parents every Sunday.    | *ella*, personal *a*             |
| Vemos una película esta noche.             | We're watching a movie tonight.       | *nosotros*                      |
| No ven el problema como nosotros.          | They don't see the problem the way we do. | *ellos*, negation             |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative      |
| --------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| tú        | ve          | no veas       |
| usted     | vea         | no vea        |
| nosotros  | veamos      | no veamos     |
| ustedes   | vean        | no vean       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Ve esa película, te va a gustar.     | Watch that movie, you'll like it.   | *tú*, affirmative, same form as *ir*'s |
| No veas tanta televisión.            | Don't watch so much TV.             | *tú*, negative                      |
| Vean el video antes de la clase.     | Watch the video before class.       | *ustedes*, affirmative               |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish | English |
| ---------------- | ------- | ------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | viendo  | seeing/watching |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Viendo la hora, decidió apurarse.                | Seeing the time, he decided to hurry.    | gerund, adverbial use         |
| No he visto esa serie todavía.                   | I haven't seen that show yet.            | past participle, compound tense |
