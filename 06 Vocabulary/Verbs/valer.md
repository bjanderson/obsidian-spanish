# valer

## Meaning

*Valer* means "to be worth" or "to cost": *Esta pintura vale mucho* ("This
painting is worth a lot"), *¿Cuánto vale?* ("How much does it cost?"). The
common idiom *valer la pena* means "to be worth it": *No vale la pena
discutir* ("It's not worth arguing"). *Valer* is irregular in two places: the
*yo* form of the present inserts a *g* (*valgo*, not *valo*), and the
future/conditional stems drop the infinitive's *e* and insert a *d*
(*valdr-*), the same pattern as *poner* → *pondr-* and *tener* → *tendr-*.

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| No valgo menos por cometer un error.       | I'm not worth less for making a mistake. | *yo*, irregular *g*             |
| ¿Cuánto vales en el mercado laboral?       | What are you worth on the job market? | *tú*, question, figurative        |
| Esta casa vale mucho más de lo que pagamos. | This house is worth much more than what we paid. | *ella* (impersonal)       |
| No valemos por lo que tenemos, sino por lo que hacemos. | We're not worth what we own, but what we do. | *nosotros*, negation |
| Esos boletos valen el doble los fines de semana. | Those tickets cost double on weekends. | *ellos* (impersonal)        |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative      |
| --------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| tú        | vale        | no valgas     |
| usted     | valga       | no valga      |
| nosotros  | valgamos    | no valgamos   |
| ustedes   | valgan      | no valgan     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Válete por ti mismo, no dependas de otros. | Fend for yourself, don't depend on others. | *tú*, affirmative, reflexive idiom *valerse* |
| No valgas menos de lo que mereces.   | Don't sell yourself short.          | *tú*, negative, figurative          |
| Valga la aclaración, no lo dije en serio. | Let me clarify, I didn't mean it seriously. | *usted*, affirmative, fixed expression |

**Note:** Commands with *valer* are rare in everyday speech — most common uses are impersonal (*vale la pena*) or reflexive (*valerse por sí mismo*, "to fend for oneself").
