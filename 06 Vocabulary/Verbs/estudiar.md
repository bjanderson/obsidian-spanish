# estudiar

## Meaning

*Estudiar* means "to study," both in the sense of studying for an exam (*Estudio para
el examen*) and in the sense of studying a field at school (*Estudia medicina en la
universidad*, She's studying medicine at university). It's a fully regular *-ar* verb
— no stem changes, no spelling changes, no irregular forms anywhere in the paradigm.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------ |
| yo                       | estudio    |
| tú                       | estudias   |
| usted / él / ella        | estudia    |
| nosotros / nosotras      | estudiamos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | estudian   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes             |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Estudio español todas las noches.             | I study Spanish every night.                 | *yo*                |
| ¿Estudias para el examen del viernes?         | Are you studying for Friday's exam?          | *tú*, question       |
| Mi hermana estudia arquitectura.              | My sister studies architecture.              | *ella*               |
| Estudiamos juntos en la biblioteca.           | We study together at the library.            | *nosotros*           |
| Mis primos no estudian mucho antes de un examen. | My cousins don't study much before an exam. | *ellos*, negation  |
