# ofrecer

## Meaning

*Ofrecer* means "to offer": *Te ofrezco mi ayuda* (I offer you my help). It works
for offering objects, help, opinions, or even apologies: *Ofreció disculpas* (He
offered an apology). Like *nacer* and *conocer*, *ofrecer* belongs to the *-zco* verb
family: its stem ends in a vowel plus *c* (*ofre-c-er*), so the *yo* form of the
present inserts a *-zc-* (*ofrezco*, not *ofreco*), and that same *-zc-* stem carries
through the entire present subjunctive and the commands built on it. Everywhere else
— preterite, imperfect, future, and so on — *ofrecer* conjugates as a fully regular
*-er* verb.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------ |
| yo                       | ofrezco    |
| tú                       | ofreces    |
| usted / él / ella        | ofrece     |
| nosotros / nosotras      | ofrecemos  |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | ofrecen    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Te ofrezco un café antes de empezar.          | I'll offer you a coffee before we start.      | *yo*, irregular *ofrezco*      |
| ¿Le ofreces algo de tomar a tu invitado?      | Do you offer your guest something to drink?  | *tú*, question                  |
| Esta tienda ofrece descuentos los lunes.      | This store offers discounts on Mondays.      | *ella/it*                       |
| Ofrecemos garantía de un año en todos los productos. | We offer a one-year warranty on all products. | *nosotros*               |
| Mis vecinos no ofrecen ayuda casi nunca.      | My neighbors almost never offer help.        | *ellos*, negation                |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative      |
| --------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| tú        | ofrece     | no ofrezcas   |
| usted     | ofrezca    | no ofrezca    |
| nosotros  | ofrezcamos | no ofrezcamos |
| ustedes   | ofrezcan   | no ofrezcan   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Ofrece tu ayuda antes de que te la pidan.    | Offer your help before they ask for it.      | *tú*, affirmative            |
| No ofrezcas más de lo que puedes cumplir.    | Don't offer more than you can deliver.       | *tú*, negative                |
| Ofrezca una disculpa sincera, por favor.     | Offer a sincere apology, please.             | *usted*, affirmative          |
| Ofrezcamos un mejor trato al cliente.        | Let's offer the client a better deal.        | *nosotros*, affirmative       |
| No ofrezcan descuentos sin autorización.     | Don't offer discounts without authorization. | *ustedes*, negative           |
