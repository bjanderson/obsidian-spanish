# escuchar

## Meaning

*Escuchar* means "to listen" or "to listen to" — paying active attention to a
sound, unlike *oír* ("to hear"), which is the passive act of sound reaching your
ears. *Escucho música* ("I listen to music") is a choice; *oigo un ruido* ("I hear a
noise") isn't. It's a fully regular *-ar* verb, no stem changes or spelling quirks.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Escucho música mientras trabajo.               | I listen to music while I work.               | *yo*                              |

| No me escuchan cuando hablo.                       | They don't listen to me when I talk.          | *ellos*, negation                 |
