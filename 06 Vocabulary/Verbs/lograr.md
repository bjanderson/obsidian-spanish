# lograr

## Meaning

*Lograr* means "to achieve" or "to manage to" — successfully reaching a goal.
Followed by an infinitive, it means "to manage to do" something: *Logré terminar a
tiempo* ("I managed to finish on time"). It's a fully regular *-ar* verb with no
stem changes or spelling adjustments in any tense.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | logro       |
| tú                       | logras      |
| usted / él / ella        | logra       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | logramos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | logran      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                   | English                             | Notes                  |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Logro terminar mis tareas a tiempo.         | I manage to finish my tasks on time.  | *yo*                      |
| ¿Logras dormir bien estos días?             | Are you managing to sleep well these days? | *tú*, question         |
| Ella logra sus metas con disciplina.        | She achieves her goals through discipline. | *ella*                  |
| Logramos ahorrar un poco cada mes.          | We manage to save a little each month. | *nosotros*                 |
| Ellos logran resultados excelentes.         | They achieve excellent results.       | *ellos*                     |
