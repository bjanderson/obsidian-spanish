# admitir

## Meaning

*Admitir* means "to admit" — accepting that something is true (admitting a
mistake or fact), or allowing someone or something entry (admitting a student,
admitting a patient). It's a regular *-ir* verb: no stem changes, no spelling
changes, no irregularities anywhere in the paradigm.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | admito      |
| tú                       | admites     |
| usted / él / ella        | admite      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | admitimos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | admiten     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes           |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
