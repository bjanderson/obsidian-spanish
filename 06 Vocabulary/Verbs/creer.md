# creer

## Meaning

*Creer* means "to believe" or "to think" (an opinion): *Creo que sí* ("I
think so"). It's a regular *-er* verb, but the unstressed *i* between two
vowels changes to *y* in the preterite third-person forms, the gerund, and the
imperfect subjunctive: *creyó*, *creyendo*, *creyera* — a common spelling
pattern in *-er/-ir* verbs with a stem ending in a vowel.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | creo        |
| tú                       | crees       |
| usted / él / ella        | cree        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | creemos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | creen       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Creo que va a llover.                      | I think it's going to rain.           | *yo*                            |
| ¿Crees en los fantasmas?                   | Do you believe in ghosts?             | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella no cree en las coincidencias.         | She doesn't believe in coincidences.  | *ella*, negation                 |
| Creemos que es la mejor opción.            | We think it's the best option.        | *nosotros*                      |
| No creen nada de lo que digo.              | They don't believe anything I say.    | *ellos*, negation                 |

**Examples:**
