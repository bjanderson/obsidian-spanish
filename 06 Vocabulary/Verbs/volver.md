# volver

## Meaning

*Volver* means "to return" or "to come back": *Vuelvo a las seis* ("I'm back
at six"). *Volver a* + infinitive means "to do something again": *Volvió a
llamar* ("He called again"). The reflexive *volverse* means "to become" (a
gradual or unexpected change): *Se volvió loco* ("He went crazy"). It's an
*o → ue* stem-changing verb in the present, and has an irregular past
participle, *vuelto*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | vuelvo      |
| tú                       | vuelves     |
| usted / él / ella        | vuelve      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | volvemos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | vuelven     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Vuelvo a casa a las siete.                 | I get home at seven.                  | *yo*                            |
| ¿Vuelves a estudiar francés?               | Are you studying French again?        | *tú*, question, *volver a*        |
| Ella vuelve del trabajo cansada.           | She comes back from work tired.       | *ella*                          |
| Volvemos a Bolivia cada verano.            | We go back to Bolivia every summer.   | *nosotros*                      |
| No vuelven hasta el domingo.               | They aren't coming back until Sunday. | *ellos*, negation                 |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish    | English |
| ---------------- | ---------- | ------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | volviendo  | returning |
| Past Participle  | vuelto     | returned |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Volviendo a casa, se puso a llover.              | On the way home, it started to rain.     | gerund, adverbial use, *volviendo a* |
| Ha vuelto a empezar desde cero.                  | He's started over from scratch again.    | past participle, compound tense |
