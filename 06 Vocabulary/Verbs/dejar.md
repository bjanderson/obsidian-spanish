# dejar

## Meaning

*Dejar* is a regular *-ar* verb meaning "to leave (something) behind" (*Dejé
las llaves en casa*, "I left my keys at home") or "to let/allow" (*Déjame
ayudarte*, "Let me help you"). *Dejar de* + infinitive means "to stop
(doing something)": *Dejó de fumar* ("He quit smoking"). Don't confuse it with
[[salir]] ("to leave/go out" yourself).

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | dejo        |
| tú                       | dejas       |
| usted / él / ella        | deja        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | dejamos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | dejan       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Dejo el auto en el garaje.                 | I leave the car in the garage.        | *yo*                            |
| ¿Me dejas usar tu teléfono?                | Will you let me use your phone?       | *tú*, question, "to let"          |
| Ella deja la puerta abierta.               | She leaves the door open.             | *ella*                          |
| Dejamos las maletas en el hotel.           | We leave the suitcases at the hotel.  | *nosotros*                      |
| No dejan entrar a nadie sin invitación.    | They don't let anyone in without an invitation. | *ellos*, negation, "to let"  |
