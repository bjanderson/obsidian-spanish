# es

## Meaning

*Es* is the *usted*/*él*/*ella* present-tense form of the verb [[ser]] ("to be") — it's
listed here on its own because it's one of the most frequent words in the language.
See [[ser]] for the full conjugation paradigm across every tense.

*Ser* (and so *es*) is used for identity, characteristics, origin, and time — things
that define what something *is*, not a temporary state. That's the core contrast
with [[estar]] ("to be" for location/condition): *Es alta* ("She's tall," a
characteristic) vs. *Está cansada* ("She's tired," a temporary state).

## Examples

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Mi hermana es doctora.                 | My sister is a doctor.                | identity/profession                |
| Este café es de Bolivia.               | This coffee is from Bolivia.          | origin                             |
| La casa es grande y luminosa.          | The house is big and bright.          | characteristic                     |
| ¿Qué hora es?                          | What time is it?                      | time, question                     |
| Hoy es lunes.                          | Today is Monday.                      | time                               |
