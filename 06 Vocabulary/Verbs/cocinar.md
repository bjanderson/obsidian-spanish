# cocinar

## Meaning

*Cocinar* means "to cook" — preparing food with heat: *Cocino todos los días* (I
cook every day), *Cocinamos la cena juntos* (We cook dinner together). It's a
completely regular *-ar* verb in every tense — no stem changes, no spelling changes,
no irregular participle. Note the related noun *cocina* means both "kitchen" and
"cuisine."

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | cocino       |
| tú                       | cocinas      |
| usted / él / ella        | cocina       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | cocinamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | cocinan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Cocino pasta casi todas las semanas. | I cook pasta almost every week. | *yo* |
| ¿Cocinas tú o cocina tu esposo? | Do you cook, or does your husband cook? | *tú*, question |
| Ella cocina platillos tradicionales los domingos. | She cooks traditional dishes on Sundays. | *ella* |
| No cocinamos carne los viernes. | We don't cook meat on Fridays. | *nosotros*, negation |
| Mis abuelos cocinan con recetas de familia. | My grandparents cook with family recipes. | *ellos* |
