# sentar

## Meaning

On its own, *sentar* means "to seat" someone (*La azafata sentó a los
pasajeros*, "The flight attendant seated the passengers") or, with an
indirect object, "to suit/agree with" — *Esa camisa te sienta bien* ("That
shirt suits you"), *La comida no me sentó bien* ("The food didn't agree with
me"). In everyday speech, though, *sentar* is used almost exclusively in its
reflexive form, *sentarse*, meaning "to sit down": *Me siento aquí* ("I sit
here"), *Siéntate, por favor* ("Sit down, please"). It's an *e → ie*
stem-changing verb in the present tense — the stem vowel changes in every
present-tense form except *nosotros*. The tables below give the base,
non-reflexive conjugation; the reflexive form simply adds *me/te/se/nos/se*
before the same conjugated verb, and the examples lean heavily on that
reflexive, everyday use.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | siento      |
| tú                       | sientas     |
| usted / él / ella        | sienta      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | sentamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | sientan     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Me siento en la primera fila siempre.      | I always sit in the front row.        | *yo*, reflexive                  |
| ¿Te sientas conmigo en el almuerzo?        | Will you sit with me at lunch?        | *tú*, question, reflexive         |
| Ese color no le sienta bien a la sala.     | That color doesn't suit the room well. | *ella* (impersonal object), negation |
| Nos sentamos juntos en las reuniones.      | We sit together in meetings.          | *nosotros*, reflexive             |
| No se sientan hasta que empieza la clase.  | They don't sit down until class starts. | *ellos*, negation, reflexive     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Siéntate aquí, hay más espacio.      | Sit here, there's more room.        | *tú*, affirmative, reflexive imperative |

| Sentémonos antes de que empiece la película. | Let's sit down before the movie starts. | *nosotros*, affirmative, reflexive |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
