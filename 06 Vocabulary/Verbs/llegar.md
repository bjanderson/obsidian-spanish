# llegar

## Meaning

*Llegar* means "to arrive." It's a regular *-ar* verb, with one spelling
change: in the *yo* preterite, *g* becomes *gu* before *é* to keep the hard
*g* sound (*llegué*, not *llegé*). *Llegar a ser* means "to become" over time.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | llego       |
| tú                       | llegas      |
| usted / él / ella        | llega       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | llegamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | llegan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Llego al trabajo a las ocho.               | I arrive at work at eight.            | *yo*                            |
| ¿A qué hora llegas a casa?                 | What time do you get home?            | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella siempre llega temprano.               | She always arrives early.             | *ella*                          |
| Llegamos juntos a la fiesta.               | We arrived together at the party.     | *nosotros*                      |
| No llegan a tiempo casi nunca.             | They almost never arrive on time.     | *ellos*, negation                 |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
