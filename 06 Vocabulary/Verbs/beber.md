# beber

## Meaning

*Beber* means "to drink." It's a regular *-er* verb: no stem changes, no spelling
changes, no irregularities anywhere in the paradigm. *Tomar* is a common everyday
alternative for "to drink" in many regions, but *beber* is the standard, unambiguous
verb and the one used in writing and formal contexts.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | bebo        |
| tú                       | bebes       |
| usted / él / ella        | bebe        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | bebemos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | beben       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes           |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| Bebo agua todos los días.                        | I drink water every day.                    | *yo*                |
| ¿Bebes café por la mañana?                       | Do you drink coffee in the morning?         | *tú*, question       |
| Ella bebe un jugo de naranja.                    | She drinks an orange juice.                 | *ella*               |
| Bebemos mucha agua cuando hace calor.            | We drink a lot of water when it's hot.      | *nosotros*           |
| Mis amigos no beben alcohol.                     | My friends don't drink alcohol.             | *ellos*, negation     |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish   | English  |
| ---------------- | --------- | -------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | bebiendo  | drinking |
| Past Participle  | bebido    | drunk    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Bebiendo despacio, disfrutó del vino.                   | Drinking slowly, he enjoyed the wine.           | gerund, adverbial use             |
| Estamos bebiendo demasiado café últimamente.            | We're drinking too much coffee lately.          | gerund, present progressive       |
| Ha bebido toda su vida solo agua natural.               | He has drunk only plain water his whole life.   | past participle w/ present perfect |
| El agua está bebida, ya no queda nada.                  | The water is drunk, there's none left.          | past participle as adjective       |
| Después de bebido el café, se sintió mejor.             | After the coffee was drunk, he felt better.     | past participle, absolute construction |
