# tratar

## Meaning

*Tratar* is a regular *-ar* verb with several senses: "to treat" (a person or
a medical condition), "to deal with" (a topic), and — with *de* + infinitive —
"to try to": *Trato de llegar a tiempo* ("I try to arrive on time"). The
impersonal reflexive *tratarse de* means "to be about": *Se trata de un
malentendido* ("It's about a misunderstanding").

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | trato       |
| tú                       | tratas      |
| usted / él / ella        | trata       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | tratamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | tratan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Trato de comer sano.                       | I try to eat healthy.                 | *yo*, *tratar de*                 |
| ¿Tratas bien a tus empleados?              | Do you treat your employees well?     | *tú*, question, "to treat"         |
| Ella trata este tema en su libro.          | She deals with this topic in her book. | *ella*, "to deal with"            |
| Tratamos de resolver el problema.          | We're trying to solve the problem.    | *nosotros*, *tratar de*             |
| No tratan el asunto con seriedad.          | They don't treat the matter seriously. | *ellos*, negation                 |
