# presentar

## Meaning

*Presentar* means "to present" (a project, an idea, a report) and, just as commonly,
"to introduce" one person to another: *Te presento a mi hermano* ("This is my
brother," literally "I present my brother to you"). It's a fully regular *-ar* verb
— no stem changes, no irregular forms anywhere in its conjugation.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | presento    |
| tú                       | presentas   |
| usted / él / ella        | presenta    |
| nosotros / nosotras      | presentamos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | presentan   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                              | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Presento mi proyecto mañana.                 | I present my project tomorrow.       | *yo*                          |
| ¿Presentas tú el informe o lo hago yo?        | Are you presenting the report, or should I? | *tú*, question          |
| Ella presenta a los nuevos empleados.         | She introduces the new employees.    | *ella*                        |
| Presentamos los resultados el viernes.        | We present the results on Friday.    | *nosotros*                    |
| Mis colegas no presentan sus dudas en público. | My colleagues don't voice their doubts in public. | *ellos*, negation      |

## Preterite

| tú                       | presentaste |
| usted / él / ella        | presentó    |
| nosotros / nosotras      | presentamos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | presentaron |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Presenté mi tesis en junio.                  | I presented my thesis in June.       | *yo*                          |
| ¿Presentaste la solicitud a tiempo?           | Did you submit the application on time? | *tú*, question             |

| No presentaron pruebas suficientes.           | They didn't present enough evidence. | negation                      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                              | English                       | Notes                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Presenta tu propuesta con confianza.       | Present your proposal with confidence. | *tú*, affirmative                   |
| No presentes el informe sin revisarlo.      | Don't submit the report without reviewing it. | *tú*, negative                |

| No presenten más quejas sin fundamento.       | Don't file more complaints without grounds. | *ustedes*, negative              |
