# entrar

## Meaning

*Entrar* means "to enter" or "to go in": *Entro a la oficina a las nueve* ("I
go into the office at nine"). It's the opposite of [[salir]] ("to leave/go
out"). It's a regular *-ar* verb throughout.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | entro       |
| tú                       | entras      |
| usted / él / ella        | entra       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | entramos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | entran      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Entro al trabajo a las nueve.              | I get to work at nine.                | *yo*                            |
| ¿Entras a la reunión ahora?                | Are you going into the meeting now?   | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella entra a la casa por la puerta trasera. | She enters the house through the back door. | *ella*                        |
| Entramos juntos al cine.                   | We go into the movie theater together. | *nosotros*                      |
| No entran sin invitación.                  | They don't go in without an invitation. | *ellos*, negation                 |
