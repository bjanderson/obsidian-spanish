# buscar

## Meaning

*Buscar* means "to look for" or "to search." Like [[esperar]], it doesn't
need a separate word for "for" — *Busco mis llaves* ("I'm looking for my
keys"), not *busco para mis llaves*. It has a spelling change (*c → qu*)
before *-é* and *-e* endings — the preterite *yo* form and the whole
subjunctive — to keep the hard *k* sound.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | busco       |
| tú                       | buscas      |
| usted / él / ella        | busca       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | buscamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | buscan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Busco trabajo desde hace un mes.           | I've been looking for a job for a month. | *yo*                          |
| ¿Buscas algo en particular?                | Are you looking for something specific? | *tú*, question                 |
| Ella busca a su gato por todo el barrio.   | She's looking for her cat all over the neighborhood. | *ella*, personal *a*    |
| Buscamos un buen restaurante cerca de aquí. | We're looking for a good restaurant nearby. | *nosotros*                    |
| No buscan problemas.                       | They aren't looking for trouble.      | *ellos*, negation                 |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Buscando en su bolso, encontró las llaves.       | Searching her bag, she found her keys.   | gerund, adverbial use         |
