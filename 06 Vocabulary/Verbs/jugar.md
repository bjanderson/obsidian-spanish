# jugar

## Meaning

*Jugar* means "to play" — a game, a sport, or with a toy. It's the only *-ar* verb
with a *u → ue* stem change (every other *-ar*/*-er* stem-changing verb shifts *o →
ue* or *e → ie*). It also has a spelling quirk: the *g* takes a *u* right before an
*e*, to keep the hard *g* sound — that shows up in the *yo* preterite and
throughout the present subjunctive.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | juego       |
| tú                       | juegas      |
| usted / él / ella        | juega       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | jugamos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | juegan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Juego fútbol todos los fines de semana.        | I play soccer every weekend.                  | *yo*, u→ue stem change            |
| ¿Juegas algún instrumento?                      | Do you play any instrument?                   | *tú*, question                    |
| Ella juega muy bien al ajedrez.                  | She plays chess very well.                    | *ella*                            |
| Jugamos cartas después de cenar.                  | We play cards after dinner.                   | *nosotros*, keeps the *u*         |
| Los niños juegan en el patio.                      | The kids play in the yard.                    | *ellos*                           |

## Present Subjunctive

| Subject                 | Conjugation |

| usted / él / ella        | juegue      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                                  | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Espero que juegues bien mañana.                     | I hope you play well tomorrow.                      | *tú*, wish clause                 |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative      |
| --------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| tú        | juega       | no juegues    |
| usted     | juegue      | no juegue     |
| nosotros  | juguemos    | no juguemos   |
| ustedes   | jueguen     | no jueguen    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                          | Notes                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Juega con cuidado, hay muebles cerca.      | Play carefully, there's furniture nearby. | *tú*, affirmative              |
| No juegues con la comida.                  | Don't play with your food.          | *tú*, negative                      |
| Juegue en la posición que le toque.        | Play the position assigned to you.  | *usted*, affirmative                |
| Juguemos otra ronda.                        | Let's play another round.           | *nosotros*, affirmative             |
| No jueguen en la calle.                     | Don't play in the street.           | *ustedes*, negative                 |
