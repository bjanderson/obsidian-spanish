# comer

## Meaning

*Comer* means "to eat." It's a regular *-er* verb — its endings follow the standard
pattern for every tense, with no stem changes or irregular forms. In several
Latin American countries, *comer* alone (without an object) can also mean "to have a
meal," usually lunch or dinner depending on the region.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | como        |
| tú                       | comes       |
| usted / él / ella        | come        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | comemos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | comen       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Como fruta todas las mañanas.             | I eat fruit every morning.             | *yo*                         |
| ¿Comes carne o eres vegetariano?          | Do you eat meat, or are you vegetarian? | *tú*, question               |
| Ella come muy poco cuando está ocupada.   | She eats very little when she's busy.  | *ella*                       |
| No comemos afuera entre semana.           | We don't eat out on weekdays.          | *nosotros*, negation           |
| Mis primos comen mucho picante.           | My cousins eat a lot of spicy food.    | *ellos*                       |
