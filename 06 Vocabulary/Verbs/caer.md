# caer

## Meaning

*Caer* means "to fall": *La hoja cae del árbol* (The leaf falls from the tree). The
reflexive *caerse* is the more common everyday form for a person tripping or losing
their balance: *Me caí en la calle* (I fell down in the street) — this file covers the
plain, non-reflexive paradigm. *Caer* is irregular in the *yo* form of the present
(*caigo*, not *caeo*), and that same *-ig-* insertion carries into the present
subjunctive (*caiga*) and commands. Like *leer* and *creer*, its stem ends in a vowel,
so it also has the *i → y* spelling change in the preterite third-person forms
(*cayó, cayeron*), the gerund (*cayendo*), and the imperfect subjunctive (*cayera*). A
common fixed expression is *caer bien/mal*, meaning to make a good or bad impression
on someone: *Me cae bien tu amigo* (I like your friend).

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------ |
| yo                       | caigo      |
| tú                       | caes       |
| usted / él / ella        | cae        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | caemos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | caen       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                   | English                             | Notes                   |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Me caigo de sueño después del turno de noche.     | I'm falling-down tired after the night shift. | *yo*, irregular *caigo* |
| ¿Por qué caes tan seguido cuando patinas?         | Why do you fall so often when you skate?   | *tú*, question               |
| La lluvia cae fuerte esta tarde.                  | The rain is falling hard this afternoon.   | *ella*, inanimate subject    |
| No caemos en esa trampa dos veces.                | We don't fall for that trick twice.        | *nosotros*, negation, idiomatic *caer en* |
| Las hojas caen por todo el patio en otoño.        | The leaves fall all over the yard in fall. | *ellas*                      |

## Commands (Imperative)

*Caer* is rarely used as a direct command on its own — you don't usually order
someone to "fall." The forms exist mainly as negative warnings.

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative     |
| --------- | ----------- | ------------- |
| tú        | cae        | no caigas    |
| usted     | caiga      | no caiga     |
| nosotros  | caigamos   | no caigamos  |
| ustedes   | caigan     | no caigan    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                              | English                          | Notes                   |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| No caigas en esa trampa otra vez.          | Don't fall for that trap again.         | *tú*, negative               |
| No caiga en el mismo error, por favor.     | Don't fall into the same mistake, please. | *usted*, negative          |
| No caigamos en el pesimismo.               | Let's not fall into pessimism.          | *nosotros*, negative         |
| No caigan en provocaciones.                | Don't fall for provocations.            | *ustedes*, negative          |
