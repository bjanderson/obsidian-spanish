# dar

## Meaning

*Dar* means "to give" — *Le di el libro* ("I gave him the book"). It's
irregular mainly in the *yo* form of the present (*doy*) and throughout the
preterite, which borrows *-er/-ir* endings without a written accent (*di*,
*not* *dí*). The present subjunctive *dé* keeps its accent to distinguish it
from the preposition [[de]].

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | doy         |
| tú                       | das         |
| usted / él / ella        | da          |
| nosotros / nosotras      | damos       |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | dan         |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Le doy de comer al perro cada mañana.      | I feed the dog every morning.         | *yo*                            |
| ¿Me das tu número de teléfono?             | Will you give me your phone number?   | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella da clases de español.                 | She teaches Spanish classes.          | *ella*                          |
| Damos gracias por la comida.               | We give thanks for the food.          | *nosotros*                      |
| No dan suficiente información.             | They don't give enough information.   | *ellos*, negation                 |

## Present Subjunctive

| Subject                 | Conjugation |

| tú                       | des         |
| usted / él / ella        | dé          |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                       | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Espero que le dé el trabajo.                      | I hope he gives him the job.                  | *usted/él/ella*, wish clause, accented *dé* |

| Dudo que le den el premio a él.                   | I doubt they'll give him the award.           | *ellos*, doubt clause             |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative      |
| --------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| tú        | da          | no des        |
| usted     | dé          | no dé         |
| nosotros  | demos       | no demos      |
| ustedes   | den         | no den        |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Dame tu mano.                        | Give me your hand.                  | *tú*, affirmative                   |
| No des tu opinión sin que te la pidan. | Don't give your opinion unless asked. | *tú*, negative                    |
| Denle una oportunidad, por favor.    | Please give him a chance.           | *ustedes*, affirmative               |
