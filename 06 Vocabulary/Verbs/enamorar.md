# enamorar

## Meaning

*Enamorar* means to make someone fall in love with you — winning them over
romantically (*Lo enamoró con su sentido del humor*, "She won him over with her
sense of humor"). In everyday speech, when talking about *you* falling in love,
Spanish uses the reflexive form *enamorarse de* ("to fall in love with"): *Me
enamoré de ella al instante* ("I fell in love with her instantly"). It's a fully
regular *-ar* verb; the reflexive form simply adds *me/te/se/nos/se* before the same
conjugated verb.

## Commands (Imperative)

*Enamorar* is rarely used as a direct command in the reflexive sense — falling in
love isn't something you order someone to do. The non-reflexive forms appear more
naturally, as encouragement to charm someone.

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative        |
| --------- | ----------- | ---------------- |
| tú        | enamora     | no enamores      |
| usted     | enamore     | no enamore       |
| nosotros  | enamoremos  | no enamoremos    |
| ustedes   | enamoren    | no enamoren      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                              |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Enamórala con detalles pequeños.               | Win her over with small gestures.       | *tú*, affirmative                          |
| No te enamores tan rápido esta vez.            | Don't fall in love so fast this time.   | *tú*, negative, reflexive                   |
