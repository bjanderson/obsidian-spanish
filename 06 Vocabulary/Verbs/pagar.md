# pagar

## Meaning

*Pagar* means "to pay" — money for a purchase, a bill, or figuratively, a
consequence (*pagar las consecuencias*, "to pay the consequences"). It's a regular
*-ar* verb with one spelling quirk: the *g* takes a *u* right before an *e*, to
keep the hard *g* sound — that only affects the *yo* preterite and the entire
present subjunctive.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | pago        |
| tú                       | pagas       |
| usted / él / ella        | paga        |
| nosotros / nosotras      | pagamos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | pagan       |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Pago el alquiler el primero de cada mes.       | I pay rent on the first of every month.       | *yo*                              |
| ¿Pagas tú la cuenta o la pago yo?               | Are you paying the bill, or am I?             | *tú*, question                    |
| Ella paga sus deudas a tiempo.                  | She pays her debts on time.                   | *ella*                            |
| Pagamos la mitad cada uno.                       | We each pay half.                             | *nosotros*                        |
| No pagan impuestos en ese país.                   | They don't pay taxes in that country.         | *ellos*, negation                 |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                                  | Notes                       |

## Commands (Imperative)

| --------- | ----------- | -------------- |

| usted     | pague       | no pague      |

| ustedes   | paguen      | no paguen     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                             | English                          | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Paga la cuenta antes de irte.                | Pay the bill before you leave.      | *tú*, affirmative                   |
| No pagues más de lo acordado.                | Don't pay more than agreed.         | *tú*, negative                      |
| Pague en la caja, por favor.                 | Please pay at the register.         | *usted*, affirmative                |
| Paguemos entre todos.                         | Let's all chip in to pay.           | *nosotros*, affirmative             |
| No paguen sin revisar el recibo.             | Don't pay without checking the receipt. | *ustedes*, negative              |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                    |
