# limpiar

## Meaning

*Limpiar* means "to clean" — removing dirt or mess: *Limpio la casa los sábados* (I
clean the house on Saturdays). It's a regular *-ar* verb in every tense. Unlike
*-uar* verbs such as *actuar*, the *i* in *limpiar* never takes a written accent — the
*ia* stays together as a diphthong, and stress falls naturally on the ending in every
form (*limpio*, not *límpio*), so the conjugation follows the ordinary regular pattern
throughout.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | limpio       |
| tú                       | limpias      |
| usted / él / ella        | limpia       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | limpiamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | limpian      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Limpio mi cuarto todos los sábados. | I clean my room every Saturday. | *yo* |
| ¿Limpias la cocina después de cocinar? | Do you clean the kitchen after cooking? | *tú*, question |
| Ella limpia las ventanas una vez al mes. | She cleans the windows once a month. | *ella* |
| No limpiamos el garaje desde hace meses. | We haven't cleaned the garage in months. | *nosotros*, negation |
| Los empleados limpian la oficina cada noche. | The employees clean the office every night. | *ellos* |
