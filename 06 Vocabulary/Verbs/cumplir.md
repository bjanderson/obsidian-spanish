# cumplir

## Meaning

*Cumplir* means "to fulfill" or "to carry out" a promise, duty, or obligation:
*Cumplió su promesa* (He kept his promise). *Cumplir con* is the common
prepositional pattern for meeting an obligation: *Cumplo con mi trabajo* (I fulfill
my job duties). It also has a very common everyday sense with ages: *cumplir años*
means "to turn [a certain age]" or "to have a birthday" — *Cumplo veinte años el
sábado* (I turn twenty on Saturday). *Cumplir* is a fully regular *-ir* verb — no
stem changes, no spelling changes, no irregular forms anywhere in the paradigm.
