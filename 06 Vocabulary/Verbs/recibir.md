# recibir

## Meaning

*Recibir* means "to receive" — a letter, a gift, a guest, or news: *Recibí tu
mensaje* ("I received your message"). It's a fully regular *-ir* verb
throughout every tense.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | recibo      |
| tú                       | recibes     |
| usted / él / ella        | recibe      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | recibimos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | reciben     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Recibo correos todos los días.             | I receive emails every day.           | *yo*                            |
| ¿Recibes el periódico en casa?             | Do you get the newspaper delivered to your house? | *tú*, question         |
| Ella recibe a sus invitados con cariño.    | She welcomes her guests warmly.       | *ella*, "to receive/welcome"       |
| Recibimos buenas noticias hoy.             | We received good news today.          | *nosotros*                      |
| No reciben visitas los lunes.              | They don't receive visitors on Mondays. | *ellos*, negation               |
