# recoger

## Meaning

*Recoger* means "to pick up," "to collect," or "to gather" — lifting something off
the ground, gathering scattered items, tidying up, or picking up a person from a
place: *Recojo a mi hija de la escuela* (I pick my daughter up from school),
*Recogimos las hojas del jardín* (We picked up the leaves from the yard). When the
object is a specific person, it takes the personal *a*. *Recoger* has a spelling
change, not a stem change: the *g* becomes *j* whenever it's followed by *a* or *o*,
to keep the soft "h" sound of the *g* consistent — so the *yo* form is *recojo* (not
*recogo*), and the entire present subjunctive uses *recoja, recojas...* since those
endings all start with *-a*. Everywhere else (before *e* or *i*), the spelling stays
*recog-*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | recojo      |
| tú                       | recoges     |
| usted / él / ella        | recoge      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | recogemos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | recogen     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Recojo a mi hijo del colegio a las tres.  | I pick my son up from school at three. | *yo*, *g→j*, personal *a*     |
| ¿Recoges la basura los martes?            | Do you take out the trash on Tuesdays? | *tú*, question                |
| Ella recoge sus juguetes antes de dormir. | She picks up her toys before bed.     | *ella*                          |
| Recogemos la mesa después de cenar.       | We clear the table after dinner.      | *nosotros*                     |
| Mis compañeros no recogen su desorden.    | My roommates don't pick up their mess. | *ellos*, negation              |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[buscar]] — related but distinct: *buscar* is looking for something, *recoger* is picking it up once found.
- [[llevar]] — often paired: *recoger* to pick something up, then *llevar* to carry or take it somewhere.
