# almorzar

## Meaning

*Almorzar* means "to have lunch" or "to eat lunch." It's an *o → ue* stem-changing
verb in the present tense — the stem vowel changes in every present-tense form
except *nosotros*. It also has a spelling change, separate from the stem change:
*z* becomes *c* right before *-é* and *-e* endings, to keep the sound consistent —
that affects only the *yo* preterite (*almorcé*, not *almorzé*) and the entire
present subjunctive (*almuerce, almuerces...*).

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | almuerzo    |
| tú                       | almuerzas   |
| usted / él / ella        | almuerza    |
| nosotros / nosotras      | almorzamos  |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | almuerzan   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Almuerzo con mis compañeros de trabajo.    | I have lunch with my coworkers.       | *yo*                            |
| ¿A qué hora almuerzas normalmente?         | What time do you usually eat lunch?   | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella casi nunca almuerza en casa.          | She almost never has lunch at home.   | *ella*                          |
| Almorzamos juntos los domingos.            | We have lunch together on Sundays.    | *nosotros*                      |
| No almuerzan antes de la una.              | They don't have lunch before one.     | *ellos*, negation                 |

| Gerund (-ing)    | almorzando  | having lunch |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Almorzando rápido, llegó a tiempo a la clase.        | Eating lunch quickly, he arrived on time to class. | gerund, adverbial use  |
