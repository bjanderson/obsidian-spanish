# sonreír

## Meaning

*Sonreír* means "to smile." It conjugates like *reír* ("to laugh"), with an *e → i*
stem change: the stem vowel changes in every present-tense form except *nosotros*,
and the same shift shows up in the preterite third-person forms and the gerund.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | sonrío      |
| tú                       | sonríes     |
| usted / él / ella        | sonríe      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | sonreímos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | sonríen     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Siempre sonrío cuando la veo.              | I always smile when I see her.         | *yo*                            |
| ¿Por qué sonríes tanto hoy?                | Why are you smiling so much today?     | *tú*, question                  |
| Ella sonríe cuando está nerviosa.          | She smiles when she's nervous.         | *ella*                          |
| No sonreímos mucho en las fotos.           | We don't smile much in photos.         | *nosotros*, negation            |
| Los niños sonríen al abrir sus regalos.    | The kids smile when they open their gifts. | *ellos*                    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                       | English                     | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Sonríe, que hoy es un buen día.     | Smile, today's a good day.         | *tú*, affirmative              |
| No sonrías si no lo sientes.        | Don't smile if you don't mean it.  | *tú*, negative                  |
| Sonría para la foto, por favor.     | Smile for the photo, please.       | *usted*, affirmative            |
| Sonriamos y sigamos adelante.       | Let's smile and keep going.        | *nosotros*, affirmative         |
