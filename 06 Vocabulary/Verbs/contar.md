# contar

## Meaning

*Contar* means "to count" (*Cuento hasta diez*, "I count to ten") or "to
tell" a story: *Cuéntame qué pasó* ("Tell me what happened"). *Contar con*
means "to count on/rely on": *Cuento contigo* ("I'm counting on you"). It's an
*o → ue* stem-changing verb in the present — the stem vowel changes in every
present-tense form except *nosotros*.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | cuento      |
| tú                       | cuentas     |
| usted / él / ella        | cuenta      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | contamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | cuentan     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Cuento con tu apoyo.                       | I'm counting on your support.         | *yo*, *contar con*                |
| ¿Cuentas los días para las vacaciones?     | Are you counting down to the vacation? | *tú*, question                   |
| Ella cuenta historias increíbles.          | She tells amazing stories.            | *ella*, "to tell"                  |
| Contamos con poco tiempo.                  | We have little time to work with.     | *nosotros*, *contar con*            |
| No cuentan con suficiente dinero.          | They don't have enough money to work with. | *ellos*, negation, *contar con* |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative      |
| --------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| tú        | cuenta      | no cuentes    |
| usted     | cuente      | no cuente     |
| nosotros  | contemos    | no contemos   |
| ustedes   | cuenten     | no cuenten    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                          | English                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Cuéntame qué pasó.                   | Tell me what happened.              | *tú*, affirmative, "to tell"        |
| No cuentes con eso todavía.          | Don't count on that yet.            | *tú*, negative, *contar con*         |
| Cuenten conmigo para lo que sea.     | Count on me for whatever you need.  | *ustedes*, affirmative, *contar con* |

## Gerund and Past Participle

| Form             | Spanish   | English |
| ---------------- | --------- | ------- |
| Gerund (-ing)    | contando  | counting/telling |
| Past Participle  | contado   | counted/told |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Contando cada peso, logró ahorrar.               | Counting every peso, she managed to save. | gerund, adverbial use     |
| Nos ha contado toda la historia.                 | She's told us the whole story.           | past participle, compound tense |
