# confirmar

## Meaning

*Confirmar* means "to confirm" — verifying that something is certain, true, or
going ahead as planned: a reservation, an appointment, a piece of information. It's
a regular *-ar* verb built from *firmar* ("to sign") plus the prefix *con-*: no
stem changes, no spelling changes, no irregularities.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | confirmo    |
| tú                       | confirmas   |
| usted / él / ella        | confirma    |
| nosotros / nosotras      | confirmamos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | confirman   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                       | English                                     | Notes           |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Confirmo mi asistencia a la boda.                       | I confirm my attendance at the wedding.               | *yo*                |

| Mis compañeros no confirman su llegada todavía.         | My coworkers haven't confirmed their arrival yet.     | *ellos*, negation     |
