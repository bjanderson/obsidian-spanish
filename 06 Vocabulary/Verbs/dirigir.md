# dirigir

## Meaning

*Dirigir* means "to direct" — running a project, a company, an orchestra, or
pointing something toward a target. Reflexively, *dirigirse a* means "to head
toward" or "to address (someone)": *Se dirigió a la puerta* ("He headed toward the
door"). It's a regular *-ir* verb with one small spelling quirk: the *g* softens to
*j* right before an *o* or *a*, to keep the soft sound consistent — that only
affects the *yo* present form and the entire present subjunctive.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | dirijo      |
| tú                       | diriges     |
| usted / él / ella        | dirige      |
| nosotros / nosotras      | dirigimos   |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | dirigen     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| Dirijo el equipo de ventas.                    | I direct the sales team.                    | *yo*, g→j spelling                |
| ¿Diriges tú la reunión de mañana?               | Are you running tomorrow's meeting?         | *tú*, question                    |
| Ella dirige una escuela de música.              | She runs a music school.                    | *ella*                            |
| Dirigimos el proyecto entre los dos.            | We're managing the project between the two of us. | *nosotros*                  |
| No dirigen bien la empresa.                     | They don't manage the company well.         | *ellos*, negation                 |
