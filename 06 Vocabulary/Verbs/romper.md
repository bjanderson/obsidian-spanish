# romper

## Meaning

*Romper* means "to break." Its conjugation is fully regular except for one thing:
the past participle is irregular — *roto*, not *rompido*. That irregular
participle is also used as an adjective: *un vaso roto* ("a broken glass").
*Romper con alguien* means "to break up with someone."

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | rompo       |
| tú                       | rompes      |
| usted / él / ella        | rompe       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | rompemos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | rompen      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Siempre rompo algo cuando cocino.           | I always break something when I cook.  | *yo*                                |
| ¿Por qué rompes las reglas?                 | Why do you break the rules?            | *tú*, question                      |
| Ella rompe con facilidad los platos.        | She breaks dishes easily.              | *ella*                              |
| No rompemos las promesas fácilmente.        | We don't break promises easily.        | *nosotros*, negation                |
| Rompen los vasos si no tienen cuidado.      | They break the glasses if they're not careful. | *ellos*                     |
