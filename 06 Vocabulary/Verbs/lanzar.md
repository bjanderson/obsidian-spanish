# lanzar

## Meaning

*Lanzar* means "to throw" or "to launch" — often with more force or intent than
[[tirar]] (a rocket, a product, an attack, a ball with a wind-up). It's a regular
*-ar* verb except for one spelling change: the *z* becomes *c* before *e*, most
noticeably in the preterite *yo* form (*lancé*, not *lanzé*) and throughout the
present subjunctive.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | lanzo       |
| tú                       | lanzas      |
| usted / él / ella        | lanza       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | lanzamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | lanzan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Lanzo la pelota lo más fuerte que puedo.        | I throw the ball as hard as I can.            | *yo*                              |
| ¿Lanzas la moneda para decidir?                  | Do you flip the coin to decide?               | *tú*, question                    |
| La empresa lanza un nuevo producto cada año.     | The company launches a new product every year.| *ella*                             |
| Lanzamos cohetes de juguete en el patio.         | We launch toy rockets in the yard.            | *nosotros*                        |
| Los niños lanzan globos de agua.                 | The kids throw water balloons.                | *ellos*                            |

## Present Subjunctive

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | lance       |
| tú                       | lances      |
| usted / él / ella        | lance       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | lancemos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | lancen      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Espero que lance bien el balón.                 | I hope he throws the ball well.           | *él*, wish clause                  |
| Quiero que lances tu propuesta ya.              | I want you to launch your proposal now.   | *tú*, request, figurative           |
| No creo que yo lance esa acusación sin pruebas. | I don't think I'll make that accusation without proof. | *yo*, doubt clause         |
| Es mejor que lancemos el producto en otoño.     | It's better if we launch the product in the fall. | *nosotros*                    |
| Ojalá lancen el cohete sin problemas.            | I hope they launch the rocket without any issues. | *ellos*, *ojalá*               |

## Commands (Imperative)

| Form      | Affirmative | Negative    |
| --------- | ----------- | ------------- |
| tú        | lanza       | no lances   |
| usted     | lance       | no lance    |
| nosotros  | lancemos    | no lancemos |
| ustedes   | lancen      | no lancen   |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                        | English                       | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Lanza el balón hacia mí.              | Throw the ball toward me.              | *tú*, affirmative                    |
| No lances la pelota tan fuerte.       | Don't throw the ball so hard.          | *tú*, negative                       |
| Lance la propuesta cuanto antes.       | Launch the proposal as soon as possible. | *usted*, affirmative               |
