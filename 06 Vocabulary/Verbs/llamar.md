# llamar

## Meaning

*Llamar* means "to call" — by phone (*Te llamo mañana*, "I'll call you
tomorrow") or to get someone's attention (*Llamó a la puerta*, "He knocked on
the door," literally "called at the door"). The reflexive *llamarse* means "to
be named": *Me llamo Ana* ("My name is Ana," literally "I call myself Ana").
It's a regular *-ar* verb throughout.

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | llamo       |
| tú                       | llamas      |
| usted / él / ella        | llama       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | llamamos    |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | llaman      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Me llamo Sofía.                            | My name is Sofía.                     | *yo*, reflexive                  |
| ¿Cómo te llamas?                           | What's your name?                     | *tú*, question, reflexive         |
| Ella llama a su madre todos los días.      | She calls her mother every day.       | *ella*, personal *a*              |
| Llamamos al médico si empeora.             | We'll call the doctor if it gets worse. | *nosotros*                      |
| No llaman a la puerta a esta hora.         | They don't knock on the door at this hour. | *ellos*, negation             |
