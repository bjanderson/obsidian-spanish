# negar

## Meaning

*Negar* means "to deny" — refusing to admit that something is true, or refusing
to grant something (*negar un permiso*, "to deny a permit"). It's an *e → ie*
stem-changing verb (like *pensar*), so the stem vowel changes in every present-tense
form except *nosotros*. It also has a *g → gu* spelling change before endings that
start with *e* — in the *yo* preterite (*negué*) and throughout the present
subjunctive (*niegue, niegues...*) — to keep the hard "g" sound of the infinitive.
The reflexive *negarse a* + infinitive means "to refuse to do something."

## Present Indicative

| Subject                 | Conjugation |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| yo                       | niego       |
| tú                       | niegas      |
| usted / él / ella        | niega       |
| nosotros / nosotras      | negamos     |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | niegan      |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                     |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Niego haber dicho eso.                          | I deny having said that.                    | *yo*, *e → ie*                 |
| ¿Niegas la acusación?                           | Do you deny the accusation?                 | *tú*, question                  |
| Él niega todo lo que hizo.                      | He denies everything he did.                | *él*                             |
| Negamos cualquier responsabilidad en el asunto. | We deny any responsibility in the matter.   | *nosotros*, no stem change       |
| Mis vecinos niegan haber escuchado el ruido.    | My neighbors deny having heard the noise.   | *ellos*                          |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                       | English                                    | Notes                        |

| Negando la realidad, empeoró las cosas.                 | By denying reality, he made things worse.            | gerund, adverbial use             |
