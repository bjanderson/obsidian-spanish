# descubrir

## Meaning

*Descubrir* means "to discover" — finding something that already existed but wasn't
known before: *Descubrí un restaurante nuevo* (I discovered a new restaurant). It also
covers "to find out" about a fact or secret: *Descubrimos la verdad* (We found out the
truth). It's a regular *-ir* verb in every tense except one spot: its past participle
is irregular, **descubierto**, not the expected *descubrido* — the same irregular
pattern as *cubrir → cubierto*.

## Present Indicative

| ------------------------ | ------------- |

| usted / él / ella        | descubre     |
| nosotros / nosotras      | descubrimos  |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | descubren    |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| ------- | ------- | ----- |
| Descubro algo nuevo cada vez que viajo. | I discover something new every time I travel. | *yo* |
| ¿Descubres errores fácilmente en tu propio trabajo? | Do you easily spot mistakes in your own work? | *tú*, question |
| Ella descubre a los culpables al final de la novela. | She discovers who's guilty at the end of the novel. | *ella*, personal *a* |
| No descubrimos nada raro en la inspección. | We don't find anything unusual in the inspection. | *nosotros*, negation |
| Los científicos descubren una especie nueva cada año. | Scientists discover a new species every year. | *ellos* |
