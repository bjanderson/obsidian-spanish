# reconocer

## Meaning

*Reconocer* means "to recognize" — identifying someone or something already known,
or acknowledging a fact: *reconocer un error* ("to admit a mistake"). Like *conocer*
with the *re-* prefix, it has an irregular *yo* form (*reconozco*) with a *-zc-*
pattern before *o*/*a*; everything else follows the regular *-er* pattern.

## Present Indicative

| ------------------------ | --------------- |
| yo                       | reconozco     |
| tú                       | reconoces     |
| usted / él / ella        | reconoce      |

| ustedes / ellos / ellas  | reconocen     |

**Examples:**

| Spanish                                   | English                             | Notes                  |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |

| Ellos reconocen sus limitaciones.           | They recognize their limitations.     | *ellos*                          |
