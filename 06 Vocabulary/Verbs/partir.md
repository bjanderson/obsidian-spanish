# partir

## Meaning

*Partir* has two everyday senses: "to leave/depart" (a more formal alternative to
*salir*) and "to split/cut into pieces" (*partir el pan*, "to break the bread"). It's
a fully regular *-ir* verb.

## Present Indicative

| Subject | Conjugation |
| --- | --- |
| yo | parto |
| tú | partes |
| usted / él / ella | parte |
| nosotros / nosotras | partimos |
| ustedes / ellos / ellas | parten |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parto mañana a las seis. | I leave tomorrow at six. | *yo* |
| ¿Partes el pastel o lo hago yo? | Are you cutting the cake, or should I? | *tú*, question |
| El tren parte a las diez en punto. | The train departs at ten sharp. | *él* |
| Partimos temprano para evitar el tráfico. | We leave early to avoid traffic. | *nosotros* |
| No parten hasta confirmar el vuelo. | They don't leave until they confirm the flight. | negation |

**Examples:**

| Spanish | English | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parte el pan en trozos pequeños. | Cut the bread into small pieces. | *tú*, affirmative |
| No partas sin avisarme. | Don't leave without telling me. | *tú*, negative |
