# nervio

## Meaning

*Nervio* means "nerve," the anatomical structure. It's masculine: *el
nervio*. Like in English, the plural is also used figuratively for feeling
anxious or on edge, e.g. *tener los nervios de punta* ("to have one's nerves
on edge").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el nervio     |
| Plural   | los nervios   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| El nervio ciático le causa mucho dolor.    | The sciatic nerve causes him a lot of pain.  | singular, subject                |
| Los médicos dañaron un nervio durante la operación. | The doctors damaged a nerve during the surgery. | singular, object          |
| ¿Qué nervio controla el movimiento de la mano? | Which nerve controls the hand's movement? | question, singular              |
| No tiene ningún nervio afectado.           | He doesn't have any nerve affected.          | negation, singular                |
| Esa noticia le puso los nervios de punta.  | That news put her nerves on edge.            | plural, figurative idiom          |
