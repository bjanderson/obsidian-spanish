# factura

## Meaning

*Factura* means "invoice" or "bill" — an itemized document requesting payment
for goods or services (a utility bill, a store receipt/invoice). It's a feminine
noun. Compare [[cuenta]], which more often means a restaurant bill or a bank
account.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | la factura     |
| Plural   | las facturas   |

| Spanish                                       | English                              | Notes    |
