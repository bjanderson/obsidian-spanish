# chaqueta

## Meaning

*Chaqueta* means "jacket," a lighter outer garment than a coat. It's
feminine: *la chaqueta*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form             |
| -------- | ---------------- |
| Singular | la chaqueta      |
| Plural   | las chaquetas    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                | Notes    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| La chaqueta está en el respaldo de la silla.                | The jacket is on the back of the chair.      | statement  |
| ¿Me prestas tu chaqueta un momento?                          | Can you lend me your jacket for a moment?    | question   |
| No necesito chaqueta, todavía hace calor.                    | I don't need a jacket, it's still warm out.  | negation   |
| Las chaquetas de cuero duran muchos años.                    | Leather jackets last many years.             | plural     |
| Se quitó la chaqueta al entrar al restaurante.               | He took off his jacket upon entering the restaurant. | statement |
