# celular

## Meaning

*Celular* means "cell phone." It's masculine: *el celular*, and it's the
standard term for a mobile phone across most of Latin America.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el celular      |
| Plural   | los celulares   |
