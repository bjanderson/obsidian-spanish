# tierra

## Meaning

*Tierra* means "earth," "land," "ground," or "soil," depending on context —
*la tierra está mojada* ("the ground is wet"), *tierras fértiles* ("fertile lands").
Capitalized as *la Tierra*, it means "Earth," the planet. It's feminine: *la tierra*.
No article irregularity applies (the stress falls on the first syllable but it's not
a stressed *a-/ha-* sound, so regular *la/una* are used, unlike *agua*).
