# punto

## Meaning

*Punto* means "point" in several related senses: a physical or geometric
point, a scored point (*marcó tres puntos*, "he scored three points"), a
punctuation mark (a period, *punto final*), or an abstract point in an
argument (*ese es un buen punto*, "that's a good point"). It's masculine:
*el punto*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el punto    |
| Plural   | los puntos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                             | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Este es un punto importante.                  | This is an important point.              | singular, subject              |
| Marcó tres puntos en el partido.              | He scored three points in the game.      | plural, object                  |
| ¿Cuál es tu punto de vista?                   | What's your point of view?               | question                        |
| No entiendo tu punto.                         | I don't understand your point.           | negation, object                 |
| Llegamos al punto de encuentro.               | We arrived at the meeting point.         | after a preposition, "al"         |
