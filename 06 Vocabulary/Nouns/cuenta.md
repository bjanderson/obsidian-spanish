# cuenta

## Meaning

*Cuenta* has several common senses: a bank account (*cuenta bancaria*), a
restaurant bill (*la cuenta, por favor*), and — in the fixed phrase *tomar en
cuenta* — "to take into account." It's a feminine noun, from the verb *contar*
(to count). The examples below cover the account and bill senses.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la cuenta     |
| Plural   | las cuentas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                | Notes             |
| -----------------------------------------------------| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Abrí una cuenta de ahorros en el banco.                | I opened a savings account at the bank.      | bank account            |
| ¿Me traes la cuenta, por favor?                        | Could you bring me the bill, please?         | question, restaurant bill |
| No tengo dinero en la cuenta.                          | I don't have money in the account.           | negation, bank account  |
| Pedimos la cuenta al final de la cena.                 | We asked for the bill at the end of dinner.  | restaurant bill         |
| Debemos tomar en cuenta su opinión.                    | We should take their opinion into account.   | idiom                   |
