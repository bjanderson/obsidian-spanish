# cielo

## Meaning

*Cielo* means "sky," and also "heaven" in a religious sense. It's a
masculine noun, mostly used in the singular.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el cielo    |
| Plural   | los cielos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                               | English                             | Notes             |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| El cielo está muy despejado hoy.                 | The sky is very clear today.                 | statement              |
| ¿Viste el cielo lleno de estrellas anoche?       | Did you see the sky full of stars last night? | question             |
| No hay ni una nube en el cielo.                  | There isn't a single cloud in the sky.       | negation               |
| Los cielos de esa región son espectaculares.     | That region's skies are spectacular.         | plural                 |
| Creen que sus abuelos están en el cielo.         | They believe their grandparents are in heaven. | secondary sense      |
