# doctor

## Meaning

*Doctor* means "doctor," a title used for a physician or anyone holding a
doctorate. The feminine form is *doctora*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el doctor     |
| Plural   | los doctores  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| El doctor me revisó la garganta.                    | The doctor checked my throat.                | statement              |
| ¿Ya viste al doctor esta semana?                    | Have you seen the doctor this week?          | question, personal a   |
| No confío en ese doctor.                            | I don't trust that doctor.                   | negation               |
| La doctora recomendó descanso total.                | The doctor recommended complete rest.        | feminine               |
| Los doctores del hospital trabajan muchas horas.    | The hospital's doctors work many hours.      | plural, del            |
