# sorpresa

## Meaning

*Sorpresa* means "surprise" — an unexpected event or gift, often used in the
phrase *de sorpresa* ("as a surprise," "unexpectedly").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Singular | la sorpresa       |
| Plural   | las sorpresas     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                   | English                             | Notes            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Le organizamos una fiesta de sorpresa.              | We threw her a surprise party.               | statement                |
| ¿Te gustó la sorpresa?                              | Did you like the surprise?                   | question                  |
| No esperaba esa sorpresa.                           | She wasn't expecting that surprise.          | negation                   |
| Las sorpresas de cumpleaños son mis favoritas.       | Birthday surprises are my favorite.          | plural                      |
| Llegó de sorpresa a la fiesta.                      | He showed up at the party unexpectedly.      | idiomatic                   |
