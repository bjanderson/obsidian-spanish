# piso

## Meaning

*Piso* primarily means "floor" — either a **story/level of a building** (*el tercer
piso*, "the third floor") or the **floor surface of a room** (*el piso de la cocina*,
"the kitchen floor"). It's masculine: *el piso*. This file covers both of those common
senses.

**Secondary sense — usage note:** In Spain and in some Latin American countries (e.g.,
Colombia, Uruguay), *piso* can also mean "apartment/flat" (*alquilar un piso*, "to rent
an apartment"). That usage is regional; for neutral Latin American Spanish, prefer
*departamento* or *apartamento* for "apartment."

| Vivimos en el segundo piso del edificio.                       | We live on the second floor of the building.                 | singular, story sense, "del"     |
| ¿Cuántos pisos tiene ese hotel?                                | How many floors does that hotel have?                        | plural, question                  |
| El piso de la cocina es de cerámica.                           | The kitchen floor is ceramic tile.                            | singular, floor-surface sense     |
| No hay alfombra en el piso de la sala.                         | There's no rug on the living room floor.                      | singular, negation, floor-surface |
| Subimos al piso de arriba por la escalera.                     | We went up to the upper floor by the stairs.                  | singular, story sense, movement   |
