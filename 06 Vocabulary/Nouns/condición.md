# condición

## Meaning

*Condición* means "condition" — either a physical/situational state (*en buena
condición*, "in good condition") or a requirement/stipulation (*bajo esta
condición*, "under this condition"). It's feminine: *la condición*. The plural drops
the written accent: *condiciones* (the stress falls naturally on the new syllable,
so the accent mark is no longer needed).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Singular     | la condición   |
| Plural         | las condiciones |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| La condición del auto es excelente.          | The car's condition is excellent.             | singular, subject                  |
| ¿Cuáles son las condiciones del contrato?    | What are the conditions of the contract?      | plural, question                   |
| Acepto, pero con una condición.              | I accept, but with one condition.             | singular                            |
| No cumplió las condiciones del préstamo.     | He didn't meet the conditions of the loan.    | plural, negation                    |
| Vive en condiciones muy difíciles.           | She lives in very difficult conditions.       | plural, after a preposition          |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[situación]] — near-synonym — a *situación* is a state of affairs, a *condición* is a specific requirement or state within it.
