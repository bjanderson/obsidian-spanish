# hijo / hija

## Meaning

*Hijo* means "son" (*el hijo*) and *hija* means "daughter" (*la hija*). Like
*padres*, the masculine plural *hijos* usually means "children" (sons and daughters
together), not just "sons" — context tells you which sense is meant. *Hijas* is used
specifically when all the children are daughters.

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form       |
| ------------------- | ---------- |
| Masculine singular   | el hijo    |
| Feminine singular    | la hija    |
| Masculine plural     | los hijos  |
| Feminine plural      | las hijas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Mi hijo estudia medicina.                  | My son is studying medicine.               | singular, subject                  |
| Mi hija estudia medicina.                  | My daughter is studying medicine.          | singular, subject                  |
| Llevo a mi hijo a la escuela.              | I take my son to school.                   | object, personal *a*               |
| ¿Conoces a la hija de Marta?               | Do you know Marta's daughter?              | question, personal *a*             |
| ¿Cuántos hijos tienes?                     | How many children do you have?             | plural = "children," question      |
| Está muy orgullosa de sus hijas.           | She's very proud of her daughters.         | after a preposition, plural        |
