# estómago

## Meaning

*Estómago* means "stomach." It's masculine: *el estómago*. Common in idioms
like *revolver el estómago* ("to turn one's stomach") and *tener buen
estómago* ("to have a strong stomach," able to tolerate unpleasant things).

Like other body parts, *estómago* pairs with a definite article and a
dative construction rather than a possessive: *Le duele el estómago* ("His
stomach hurts"), not *Le duele su estómago*.
