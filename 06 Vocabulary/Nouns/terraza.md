# terraza

## Meaning

*Terraza* means "terrace" — a flat, often paved outdoor area, sometimes on a rooftop
(*terraza en la azotea*) or at ground level attached to a house or restaurant. It's
feminine: *la terraza*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la terraza    |
| Plural   | las terrazas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                 | English                                         | Notes                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| La terraza del restaurante da al mar.                              | The restaurant's terrace looks out onto the sea.               | singular, subject                |
| ¿Cuántas terrazas hay en ese hotel?                                 | How many terraces are there in that hotel?                     | plural, question                  |
| Cenamos en la terraza bajo las estrellas.                          | We had dinner on the terrace under the stars.                   | singular, object of preposition   |
| No hay sombrillas en la terraza hoy.                                | There are no umbrellas on the terrace today.                     | singular, negation                |
| Subimos a la terraza para ver los fuegos artificiales.             | We went up to the terrace to see the fireworks.                 | singular, purpose clause          |
