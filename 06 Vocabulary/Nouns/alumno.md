# alumno

## Meaning

*Alumno* means "student" or "pupil" — specifically someone enrolled in a
class under a teacher, in a school or course setting. It's masculine (*el
alumno*); its feminine counterpart is *la alumna*. Compare [[estudiante]],
the broader, more general word for "student" at any level — *alumno*
specifically emphasizes the pupil-teacher relationship within a given class,
while *estudiante* is the more common, all-purpose term.
