# patio

## Meaning

*Patio* means "patio" or "yard" — an open outdoor area attached to a house, often at
the back, or a central courtyard in traditional-style homes. It's masculine: *el patio*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el patio    |
| Plural   | los patios  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                        | Notes                     |
