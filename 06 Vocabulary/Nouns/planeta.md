# planeta

## Meaning

*Planeta* means "planet." Despite ending in *-a*, it's masculine, like
other Greek-origin *-ma/-ta* nouns.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el planeta    |
| Plural   | los planetas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                 | English                              | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| La Tierra es nuestro planeta.                       | Earth is our planet.                         | statement          |
| ¿Cuántos planetas tiene el sistema solar?           | How many planets does the solar system have?  | question           |
| No hay vida conocida en ningún otro planeta.        | There's no known life on any other planet.   | negation           |
| Los planetas giran alrededor del sol.               | The planets revolve around the sun.          | plural             |
