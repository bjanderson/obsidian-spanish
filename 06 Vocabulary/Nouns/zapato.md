# zapato

## Meaning

*Zapato* means "shoe." It's masculine: *el zapato*. Like in English, it's
almost always used in the plural (*los zapatos*) since shoes come in pairs.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el zapato     |
| Plural   | los zapatos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                              | Notes    |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Este zapato me aprieta un poco.                             | This shoe is a bit tight on me.             | statement, singular form |
| ¿Dónde compraste esos zapatos?                              | Where did you buy those shoes?              | question   |
| No encuentro mis zapatos negros.                            | I can't find my black shoes.                | negation   |
| Los zapatos deportivos son muy cómodos para correr.         | Athletic shoes are very comfortable for running. | plural |
| Se puso los zapatos antes de salir.                         | He put on his shoes before leaving.         | statement, plural |
