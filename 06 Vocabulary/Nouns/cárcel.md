# cárcel

## Meaning

*Cárcel* means "jail" or "prison." It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la cárcel    |
| Plural   | las cárceles |

## Examples

| Spanish                                 | English                              | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Pasó cinco años en la cárcel.                       | He spent five years in jail.                 | statement          |
| ¿Cuándo salió de la cárcel?                         | When did he get out of jail?                 | question           |
| No quiere volver a la cárcel nunca más.             | He never wants to go back to jail again.     | negation           |
| Las cárceles del país están sobrepobladas.          | The country's jails are overcrowded.         | plural, del        |
| Lo mandaron a la cárcel por robo.                   | They sent him to jail for theft.             | statement          |
