# pez

## Meaning

*Pez* means "fish" as a living animal. It's masculine: *el pez*. Once
caught and prepared as food, it becomes *pescado*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el pez     |
| Plural   | los peces  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                               | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Ese pez nada muy rápido.                            | That fish swims very fast.                     | statement                             |
| ¿Cuántos peces hay en la pecera?                    | How many fish are in the fish tank?            | question, plural (z → c)               |
| No pescamos ningún pez hoy.                         | We didn't catch a single fish today.           | negation                              |
| Los peces de este río son pequeños.                 | The fish in this river are small.              | plural                                 |
| Mi hijo tiene un pez como mascota.                  | My son has a fish as a pet.                    | statement                              |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[río]] — a common habitat for freshwater fish.
