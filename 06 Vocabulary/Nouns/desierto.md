# desierto

## Meaning

*Desierto* means "desert." It's masculine: *el desierto*. As an adjective
it can also mean "deserted/empty," but here it functions as a noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el desierto     |
| Plural   | los desiertos   |

## Examples
