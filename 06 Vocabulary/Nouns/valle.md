# valle

## Meaning

*Valle* means "valley," the low land between mountains or hills. It's
masculine: *el valle*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el valle    |
| Plural   | los valles  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                  | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El valle está lleno de viñedos.                    | The valley is full of vineyards.               | statement                      |
| ¿Conoces ese valle cerca de la frontera?           | Do you know that valley near the border?       | question                       |
| No cruzamos el valle a pie.                        | We didn't cross the valley on foot.            | negation                       |
| Los valles de esta región son muy fértiles.        | The valleys of this region are very fertile.   | plural                         |
| Vivimos en un pueblo dentro del valle.             | We live in a town inside the valley.           | statement                      |
