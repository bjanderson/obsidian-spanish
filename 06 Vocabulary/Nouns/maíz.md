# maíz

## Meaning

*Maíz* means "corn" (the crop/grain). It's a masculine noun, and it's normally
treated as a mass/uncountable noun — you talk about *maíz* the way you'd talk about
"corn" in English, without a plural in everyday speech. The plural *maíces* exists
but is rare, used mainly to talk about different varieties/types of corn (e.g. in
discussions of Mexican agriculture).

## Singular / Plural

| Number             | Form                                  |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Singular (standard) | el maíz                                |
| Plural (rare — "varieties of corn") | los maíces           |
