# agosto

## Meaning

*Agosto* is August, the eighth month of the year. Like all months in
Spanish, it's lowercase and masculine, and it's rarely used with an
article — say *en agosto*, not *en el agosto*. An article does appear
before a specific date: *el seis de agosto*. Bolivia's Independence Day
falls on agosto 6, and the month is typically cold, dry, and windy on the
altiplano.
