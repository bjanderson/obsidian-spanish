# inversión

## Meaning

*Inversión* means "investment" — money put into something with the expectation
of a future return. It's a feminine noun, from the verb *invertir* (to invest).
The plural drops the accent (*inversiones*) since the stress lands naturally on
that syllable once another syllable is added.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Singular | la inversión       |
| Plural   | las inversiones    |
