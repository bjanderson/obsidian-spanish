# revista

## Meaning

*Revista* means "magazine," a periodical publication. It's a feminine
noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la revista   |
| Plural   | las revistas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                             | Notes         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| Compré una revista de moda.                       | I bought a fashion magazine.                | statement          |
| ¿Todavía lees esa revista?                        | Do you still read that magazine?            | question           |
| No encontré la revista que buscaba.               | I couldn't find the magazine I was looking for. | negation      |
| Hay muchas revistas en la sala de espera.         | There are many magazines in the waiting room. | plural           |
| Salió su foto en la portada de la revista.        | Her photo appeared on the magazine's cover. | statement          |
