# planta

## Meaning

*Planta* means "plant." It's feminine: *la planta*. It can also mean the
"floor" of a building (*planta baja*, "ground floor") or the "sole" of a
foot.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la planta    |
| Plural   | las plantas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                          |
