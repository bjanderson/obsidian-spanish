# ayuda

## Meaning

*Ayuda* means "help" or "assistance." It's feminine: *la ayuda*. It's the noun form
of the verb *ayudar* ("to help").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la ayuda     |
| Plural   | las ayudas (rare — usually treated as uncountable) |

## Examples

| Spanish                                      | English                                   | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Necesito tu ayuda con la tarea.                  | I need your help with the homework.                | statement, singular                |
| ¿Puedo pedirte una ayuda?                        | Can I ask you for a favor/some help?               | question                          |
| No aceptó la ayuda de su hermano.                | She didn't accept her brother's help.              | negation                          |
| Hace la tarea con la ayuda de su hermano.        | She does her homework with her brother's help.     | statement, object of preposition   |
| Le dieron mucha ayuda durante el proyecto.       | They gave him a lot of help during the project.    | indirect object                    |
