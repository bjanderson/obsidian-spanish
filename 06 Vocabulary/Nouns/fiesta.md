# fiesta

## Meaning

*Fiesta* means "party" or "celebration" — a social gathering, and by
extension a holiday or festival (*día de fiesta*).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | la fiesta       |
| Plural   | las fiestas     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                      | English                              | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| La fiesta empieza a las ocho.                        | The party starts at eight.                   | statement            |
| ¿Vas a la fiesta de Carla?                           | Are you going to Carla's party?              | question              |
| No quiso ir a la fiesta anoche.                      | She didn't want to go to the party last night. | negation           |
| Las fiestas de fin de año son muy animadas.           | End-of-year parties are very lively.         | plural                 |
| Invitó a todos sus amigos a la fiesta.                | She invited all her friends to the party.    | personal a             |
