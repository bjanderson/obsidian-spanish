# traje

## Meaning

*Traje* means "suit," the formal outfit of jacket and matching pants (or
skirt). It's masculine: *el traje*. It also extends to "outfit" or
"costume" more generally — *traje de baño* ("swimsuit") and *traje típico*
("traditional costume") are both common compounds worth knowing.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el traje     |
| Plural   | los trajes   |
