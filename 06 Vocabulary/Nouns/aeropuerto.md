# aeropuerto

## Meaning

*Aeropuerto* means "airport." It's masculine: *el aeropuerto*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el aeropuerto   |
| Plural   | los aeropuertos |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                  | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Llegamos al aeropuerto dos horas antes del vuelo. | We arrived at the airport two hours before the flight. | singular, *al*, *del* |
| Recogieron a mi hermano en el aeropuerto.      | They picked up my brother at the airport.       | singular, personal *a*           |
| ¿A qué aeropuerto vuelas?                      | Which airport are you flying to?                | question                          |
| No hay taxis disponibles en el aeropuerto ahora. | There are no taxis available at the airport right now. | negation                    |
| Los aeropuertos grandes tienen varias terminales. | Large airports have several terminals.        | plural, subject                    |
