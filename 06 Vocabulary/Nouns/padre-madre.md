# padre / madre

## Meaning

*Padre* means "father" (*el padre*) and *madre* means "mother" (*la madre*). In the
plural, *los padres* usually means "parents" (mother and father together), not just
"fathers" — Spanish uses the masculine plural to cover a mixed-gender pair, the same
pattern you'll see with *hijos* ("children") and *hermanos* ("siblings").

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form        |
| ------------------- | ----------- |
| Masculine singular   | el padre    |
| Feminine singular    | la madre    |
| Masculine plural     | los padres  |
| Feminine plural      | las madres  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                          | Notes                             |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Mi padre trabaja en un hospital.              | My father works at a hospital.                       | singular, subject                     |
| Mi madre trabaja en un hospital.              | My mother works at a hospital.                       | singular, subject                     |
| Veo a mi padre todos los domingos.            | I see my father every Sunday.                        | singular, object, personal *a*        |
| ¿Conoces a mi madre?                          | Do you know my mother?                               | question, personal *a*                |
| Mis padres viven en Bolivia.                  | My parents live in Bolivia.                          | plural = "parents," subject           |
| Las madres del barrio se conocen bien.        | The neighborhood mothers know each other well.       | plural, subject, *del*                |
