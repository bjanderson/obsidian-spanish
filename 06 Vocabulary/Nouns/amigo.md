# amigo

## Meaning

*Amigo* means "friend" (male, or a mixed-gender group by default). It's
masculine, following the regular -o ending pattern: *el amigo*. Its feminine
counterpart is [[amiga]].

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Singular      | el amigo       |
| Plural           | los amigos     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi amigo vive en Cochabamba.                | My friend lives in Cochabamba.         | subject                            |
| Ayer vi a mi amigo en el centro.            | I saw my friend downtown yesterday.    | direct object, personal *a*         |
| Tengo muchos amigos en esta ciudad.         | I have a lot of friends in this city.  | plural                             |
| ¿Vas a la fiesta con tus amigos?            | Are you going to the party with your friends? | after a preposition, question |
| La casa de mi amigo es enorme.              | My friend's house is huge.             | possessive *de*-phrase              |
