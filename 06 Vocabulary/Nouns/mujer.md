# mujer

## Meaning

*Mujer* means "woman," an adult female person. It's feminine: *la mujer*. Depending
on context it can also mean "wife" (*mi mujer*, "my wife"), similar to how English
sometimes uses "woman" informally the same way.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la mujer     |
| Plural   | las mujeres  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Esa mujer trabaja en el banco.               | That woman works at the bank.              | subject                            |
| Conocí a una mujer muy amable ayer.          | I met a very kind woman yesterday.         | direct object, personal *a*        |
| ¿Viste a la mujer que pasó por aquí?         | Did you see the woman who passed by here?  | question, personal *a*             |
| Las mujeres del equipo llegaron temprano.    | The women on the team arrived early.       | plural, possessive *de*-phrase     |
| Hablamos con la mujer de la tienda.          | We spoke with the woman from the store.    | after a preposition                |
