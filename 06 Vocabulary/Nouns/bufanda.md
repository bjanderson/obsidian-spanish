# bufanda

## Meaning

*Bufanda* means "scarf," the accessory worn around the neck for warmth. It's
feminine: *la bufanda*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | la bufanda     |
| Plural   | las bufandas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                             | English                                         | Notes                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| Me puse la bufanda porque hace frío.                | I put on the scarf because it's cold.           | singular, statement  |
| ¿De qué color es tu bufanda?                        | What color is your scarf?                       | question              |
| No traje bufanda hoy.                               | I didn't bring a scarf today.                   | negation              |
| Compró dos bufandas para el viaje a la montaña.     | She bought two scarves for the trip to the mountains. | plural           |
| Las bufandas de lana abrigan mucho.                 | Wool scarves keep you very warm.                | plural                |
