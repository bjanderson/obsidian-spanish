# supermercado

## Meaning

*Supermercado* means "supermarket" — a large store selling groceries and
household goods. It's masculine: *el supermercado*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form               |
| -------- | ------------------ |
| Singular | el supermercado    |
| Plural   | los supermercados  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                    | English                                             | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Hacemos las compras en el supermercado los sábados.              | We do the shopping at the supermarket on Saturdays.       | singular, after a preposition    |
| Fui al supermercado después del trabajo.                         | I went to the supermarket after work.                    | singular, *al* contraction        |
| ¿Está abierto el supermercado los domingos?                      | Is the supermarket open on Sundays?                       | singular, question               |
| No encontré leche en el supermercado.                            | I didn't find milk at the supermarket.                    | singular, negation                |
| Los supermercados del centro suben los precios en diciembre.     | Downtown supermarkets raise prices in December.           | plural, *del* contraction         |
