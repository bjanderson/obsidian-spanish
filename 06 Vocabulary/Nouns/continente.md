# continente

## Meaning

*Continente* means "continent," one of Earth's large landmasses. It's a
masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form             |
| -------- | ---------------- |
| Singular | el continente    |
| Plural   | los continentes  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Sudamérica es un continente enorme.              | South America is a huge continent.              | statement          |
| ¿En qué continente queda Bolivia?                | What continent is Bolivia in?                    | question           |
| No he viajado a ningún otro continente.          | I haven't traveled to any other continent.       | negation           |
| Hay siete continentes en el mundo.               | There are seven continents in the world.         | plural             |
| Cruzaron el continente en tren.                  | They crossed the continent by train.             | statement          |
