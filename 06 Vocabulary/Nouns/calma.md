# calma

## Meaning

*Calma* means "calm" — a state of stillness or composure, free of stress or
agitation. It's a feminine noun. Common phrases: *con calma* ("calmly,"
"take it easy"), *mantener la calma* ("to keep calm").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la calma    |
| Plural   | las calmas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                 | Notes                 |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Trabaja con calma, no hay prisa.                   | Work calmly, there's no rush.                | statement/imperative     |
| ¿Cómo mantienes la calma en momentos así?          | How do you keep calm in moments like that?   | question                 |
| No perdió la calma ni un solo momento.             | She didn't lose her calm for a single moment. | negation                 |
| Se respira una calma extraña en el pueblo.         | An odd calm hangs over the town.             | statement                 |
| Las calmas del mar duraron toda la mañana.         | The sea's lulls lasted all morning.          | plural, nautical sense    |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[alegría]] — both describe pleasant inner states, though *calma* is stillness and *alegría* is joy.
- [[diversión]] — a contrasting state: *diversión* is active fun, *calma* is quiet stillness.
