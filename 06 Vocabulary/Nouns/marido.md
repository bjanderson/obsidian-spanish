# marido

## Meaning

*Marido* means "husband." It's masculine: *el marido*. It's a common synonym of
*[[esposo-esposa|esposo]]* — the two are largely interchangeable in everyday speech, though
*esposo* leans slightly more neutral/formal and *marido* is the word heard most
often for "husband" in casual conversation across much of Latin America.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el marido   |
| Plural   | los maridos |
