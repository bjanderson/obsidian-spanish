# mejilla

## Meaning

*Mejilla* means "cheek." It's feminine: *la mejilla*. Common in the everyday
greeting custom *dar un beso en la mejilla* ("to give a kiss on the cheek").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | la mejilla     |
| Plural   | las mejillas   |
