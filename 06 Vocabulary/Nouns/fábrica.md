# fábrica

## Meaning

*Fábrica* means "factory" — a building or complex where goods are
manufactured. It's feminine: *la fábrica*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la fábrica    |
| Plural   | las fábricas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                        | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Mi padre trabaja en una fábrica de zapatos.                | My father works at a shoe factory.                    | singular, after a preposition    |
| La fábrica contrató a cien empleados nuevos.               | The factory hired a hundred new employees.            | singular, personal *a*            |
| ¿Visitaste alguna vez esa fábrica?                         | Have you ever visited that factory?                   | singular, question               |
| No cerraron la fábrica a pesar de la crisis.               | They didn't close the factory despite the crisis.      | singular, negation                |
| Las fábricas de la zona contaminan el río.                 | The factories in the area pollute the river.           | plural, subject                   |
