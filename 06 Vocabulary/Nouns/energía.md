# energía

## Meaning

*Energía* means "energy," whether physical power, the kind that fuels
machines and homes, or a person's personal vigor. It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | la energía     |
| Plural   | las energías   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes         |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Esta casa usa energía solar.                            | This house uses solar energy.                  | statement          |
| ¿De dónde viene la energía de esta ciudad?              | Where does this city's energy come from?       | question           |
| No tengo energía para salir hoy.                        | I don't have the energy to go out today.       | negation, secondary sense |
| Hay varias energías renovables disponibles ahora.       | Several renewable energies are available now.  | plural             |
| Los niños tienen mucha energía por la mañana.           | Children have a lot of energy in the morning.  | secondary sense    |
