# martes

## Meaning

*Martes* is Tuesday. Days of the week are lowercase in Spanish and masculine:
*el martes*. Like the rest of Monday through Friday, it's invariable in the
plural — *los martes* uses the same form as *el martes* (compare [[sábado]]
and [[domingo]], which pluralize regularly).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el martes  |
| Plural   | los martes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                    | Notes                            |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| El martes vamos al médico.                   | On Tuesday we're going to the doctor.        | *el* + day, specific occurrence      |
| Los martes hago ejercicio por la tarde.      | On Tuesdays I exercise in the afternoon.     | *los* + day, habitual                |
| ¿Nos reunimos el martes o el miércoles?      | Shall we meet on Tuesday or Wednesday?       | question, comparing days             |
| No tengo clases los martes.                  | I don't have classes on Tuesdays.            | negative, habitual plural            |
| El martes que viene empieza el curso.        | Next Tuesday the course starts.              | future specific                      |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[miércoles]] — the day after Tuesday in the week.
