# ropa

## Meaning

*Ropa* means "clothing" or "clothes" — the umbrella term for any garment.
It's feminine: *la ropa*. Unlike English "clothes," *ropa* is grammatically
singular and treated as a mass noun (like "furniture" or "money"); it's
almost never pluralized as *ropas* in everyday speech (that plural form
survives mainly in older or literary usage, e.g. "las ropas del rey").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form      |
| -------- | --------- |
| Singular | la ropa   |
| Plural   | (rare — mass noun; use *ropas* only in literary/archaic contexts) |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                | Notes    |

| ¿Ya lavaste la ropa?                                        | Did you already wash the clothes?           | question   |
| No tengo ropa limpia para mañana.                           | I don't have clean clothes for tomorrow.    | negation   |

| La ropa de invierno cuesta más que la de verano.            | Winter clothing costs more than summer clothing. | statement |
