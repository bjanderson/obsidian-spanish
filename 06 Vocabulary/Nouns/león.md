# león

## Meaning

*León* means "lion." It's masculine: *el león* (the feminine form *la
leona* refers specifically to a lioness).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el león     |
| Plural   | los leones  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| El león rugió muy fuerte.                        | The lion roared very loudly.                  | statement                             |
| ¿Viste al león en el zoológico?                  | Did you see the lion at the zoo?              | question, personal *a*                 |
| No nos acercamos al león por seguridad.          | We didn't get close to the lion for safety.   | negation, personal *a*                 |
| Los leones descansan gran parte del día.         | Lions rest most of the day.                   | plural                                 |
| El guía nos mostró la manada de leones.          | The guide showed us the pride of lions.       | statement                              |
