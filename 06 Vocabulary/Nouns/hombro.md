# hombro

## Meaning

*Hombro* means "shoulder." It's masculine: *el hombro*. Common in idioms like
*encogerse de hombros* ("to shrug") and *hombro con hombro* ("side by side").

As with other body parts, *hombro* takes a definite article with a
reflexive/dative construction instead of a possessive: *Me duele el hombro*
("My shoulder hurts"), not *Me duele mi hombro*.
