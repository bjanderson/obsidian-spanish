# vez

## Meaning

*Vez* is feminine (*la vez*) and means "time" in the sense of "an occurrence" —
*una vez* ("once"), *dos veces* ("twice"), *otra vez* ("again," literally "another
time"). Don't confuse it with [[tiempo]] (time in general/duration) or *hora* (clock
time) — *vez* only counts occurrences.

The plural is irregular in spelling: *z* changes to *c* before *-es* (*vez* →
*veces*), a general Spanish spelling rule, not specific to this word.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Singular       | la vez       |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Te lo dije una vez.                         | I told you once.                        | direct object                      |
| ¿Cuántas veces has viajado a Bolivia?       | How many times have you traveled to Bolivia? | plural, question               |
| A veces no entiendo nada.                   | Sometimes I don't understand anything.  | fixed expression, "sometimes"       |
| Cada vez que llueve, me da sueño.           | Every time it rains, I get sleepy.      | fixed expression                    |
| Fue la última vez que lo vi.                | It was the last time I saw him.         | subject                             |
