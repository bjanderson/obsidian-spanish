# baño

## Meaning

*Baño* means "bathroom" or "restroom." It's masculine: *el baño*. It also means "bath"
as in the act of bathing — e.g., *tomar un baño* ("to take a bath").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el baño    |
| Plural   | los baños  |
