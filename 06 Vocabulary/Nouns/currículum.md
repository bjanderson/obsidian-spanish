# currículum

## Meaning

*Currículum* means "resume" or "CV" — a document summarizing someone's
education and work experience, short for *currículum vitae*. The alternate
spelling *currículo* is also common and means the same thing.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                |
| -------- | ------------------- |
| Singular | el currículum       |
| Plural   | los currículums     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                    | Notes            |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Envié mi currículum a varias empresas.                      | I sent my resume to several companies.           | statement               |
| ¿Actualizaste tu currículum recientemente?                  | Did you update your resume recently?             | question                 |
| No incluyó su experiencia más reciente en el currículum.    | She didn't include her most recent experience on her resume. | negation      |
| Los currículums de los candidatos llegaron por correo.       | The candidates' resumes arrived by mail.          | plural                   |
| Revisó el currículum antes de la entrevista.                | He reviewed the resume before the interview.      | statement                |
