# tigre

## Meaning

*Tigre* means "tiger." It's masculine: *el tigre* (the feminine form *la
tigresa* refers specifically to a tigress).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el tigre    |
| Plural   | los tigres  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                                | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| El tigre camina en silencio.                        | The tiger walks silently.                        | statement                             |
| ¿Has visto un tigre de cerca?                       | Have you seen a tiger up close?                  | question                              |
| No hay tigres en estado salvaje aquí.               | There are no tigers in the wild here.            | negation                              |
| Los tigres son animales muy territoriales.          | Tigers are very territorial animals.             | plural                                 |
| Fotografiamos al tigre desde lejos.                 | We photographed the tiger from far away.         | statement, personal *a*                |
