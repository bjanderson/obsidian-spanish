# precio

## Meaning

*Precio* means "price," the cost of something. It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el precio   |
| Plural   | los precios |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| El precio de la gasolina subió otra vez.         | Gas's price went up again.                  | statement, *de*    |
| ¿Cuál es el precio de esta camisa?               | What's this shirt's price?                  | question           |
| No pago ese precio por un café.                  | I don't pay that price for a coffee.        | negation           |
| Los precios bajan mucho en enero.                | Prices drop a lot in January.               | plural             |
| Compré la casa al precio que pedían.             | I bought the house at the price they wanted.| *al*               |
