# lengua

## Meaning

*Lengua* means "tongue," the body part, and also "language," used
somewhat interchangeably with *idioma*. It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la lengua   |
| Plural   | las lenguas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Me quemé la lengua con el café.                   | I burned my tongue with the coffee.          | statement              |
| ¿Cuál es tu lengua materna?                       | What's your native language?                 | question, secondary sense |
| No entiendo esa lengua extranjera.                | I don't understand that foreign language.    | negation               |
| Bolivia tiene varias lenguas indígenas oficiales. | Bolivia has several official indigenous languages. | plural            |
| Sacó la lengua para hacer reír al bebé.           | He stuck out his tongue to make the baby laugh. | statement           |
