# paz

## Meaning

*Paz* means "peace," the absence of conflict, and also inner calm. It's a
feminine noun, mostly used in the singular. The idiom *hacer las paces*
("to make peace/make up") uses a fixed plural.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | la paz     |
| Plural   | las paces  |

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                             | Notes             |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Por fin llegó la paz al país.                   | Peace finally came to the country.           | statement              |
| ¿Crees en la paz mundial?                       | Do you believe in world peace?               | question               |
| No hay paz mientras haya guerra.                | There's no peace while there's war.          | negation               |
| Hicieron las paces después de la pelea.         | They made up after the fight.                | idiomatic, plural       |
| Necesito un poco de paz y silencio.             | I need a bit of peace and quiet.             | secondary sense        |
