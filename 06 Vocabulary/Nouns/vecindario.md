# vecindario

## Meaning

*Vecindario* also means "neighborhood," near-synonymous with *barrio* (see
[[barrio]]). It's masculine: *el vecindario*. It can feel slightly more formal than
*barrio* and often emphasizes the community of neighbors (*vecinos*) living in the
area, rather than just the geographic district. *Barrio* is the more common, neutral
choice in everyday speech.

## Singular / Plural
