# tenis

## Meaning

*Tenis* means "tennis," the sport. Its form doesn't change between
singular and plural. In some Latin American countries, *los tenis* also
means "sneakers" — a different, plural-only sense.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el tenis   |
| Plural   | los tenis  |
