# pijama

## Meaning

*Pijama* means "pajamas." Unlike English, which treats "pajamas" as
inherently plural, Spanish treats it as one singular garment/set: *el
pijama*. It's masculine across most of Latin America, though a few
countries (notably Mexico) use it as feminine, *la pijama* — a regional
variation worth knowing but not the neutral form used here.
