# dinero

## Meaning

*Dinero* means "money." It's masculine: *el dinero*. It's normally used as a
mass noun (uncountable, like "money" in English) rather than in the plural —
you talk about *el dinero* in general, not *los dineros*, though the plural
form does technically exist for stylistic or emphatic use.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el dinero   |
| Plural   | los dineros |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                  | Notes                        |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| No tengo suficiente dinero.                   | I don't have enough money.                | singular, object                |
| El dinero no compra la felicidad.             | Money doesn't buy happiness.              | singular, subject                |
| ¿Necesitas dinero para el viaje?              | Do you need money for the trip?           | singular, question                |
| Ahorré el dinero para pagar el alquiler.      | I saved the money to pay the rent.        | singular, object, al/del contraction* |
| Vive del dinero que gana en el trabajo.       | She lives off the money she earns at work.| singular, after a preposition (*del*) |
