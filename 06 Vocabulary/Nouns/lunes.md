# lunes

## Meaning

*Lunes* is Monday. Days of the week are lowercase in Spanish (never
capitalized like in English) and masculine: *el lunes*. Monday through
Friday are invariable in the plural — *los lunes* uses the exact same form
as *el lunes* (compare [[sábado]] and [[domingo]], which pluralize
regularly).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form      |
| -------- | --------- |
| Singular | el lunes  |
| Plural   | los lunes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                   | Notes                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| El lunes tengo una reunión importante.      | I have an important meeting on Monday.      | *el* + day, specific occurrence        |
| Los lunes suelo llegar temprano a la oficina. | On Mondays I usually get to the office early. | *los* + day, habitual, invariable plural |
| ¿Trabajas los lunes?                        | Do you work on Mondays?                     | question, habitual plural              |
| El lunes pasado no fui a clase.             | Last Monday I didn't go to class.           | negative, past specific                |
| Nos vemos el próximo lunes.                 | See you next Monday.                        | future specific                        |
