# hora

## Meaning

*Hora* means "hour," and it's also the standard word for "time" in the clock sense —
*¿Qué hora es?* ("What time is it?"). It's feminine: *la hora*. No article
irregularity applies: the stressed syllable is *ho-*, not a stressed *a-* sound, so
regular *la/una* are used (unlike *agua* or *hacha*).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la hora     |
| Plural   | las horas   |
