# básquetbol

## Meaning

*Básquetbol* means "basketball." It's a masculine noun, more common in
Latin America than the Spain-favored *baloncesto*, and used almost always
in the singular.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el básquetbol   |
| Plural   | los básquetboles |
