# fiebre

## Meaning

*Fiebre* means "fever," an elevated body temperature. It's a feminine
noun, usually used in the singular.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la fiebre   |
| Plural   | las fiebres |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| El niño tiene fiebre desde anoche.               | The boy has had a fever since last night.    | statement          |
| ¿Le tomaste la temperatura por la fiebre?        | Did you take his temperature because of the fever? | question       |
| No baja la fiebre con el medicamento.            | The fever isn't going down with the medicine. | negation          |
| Tuvo varias fiebres altas esa semana.            | She had several high fevers that week.       | plural             |
| Está en cama con mucha fiebre.                   | He's in bed with a high fever.               | statement          |
