# cuarto

## Meaning

*Cuarto* has two common, unrelated senses:

1. As a masculine noun (*el cuarto*), it means "room," often specifically
   "bedroom": *mi cuarto* ("my room"). It also appears in the fixed phrase *un
   cuarto de hora* ("a quarter of an hour").
2. As an ordinal adjective, it means "fourth," agreeing in gender and number with
   the noun it describes: *cuarto*, *cuarta*, *cuartos*, *cuartas* — *el cuarto
   piso* ("the fourth floor"). Unlike *primero* and *tercero*, *cuarto* does
   **not** shorten before a masculine singular noun.
