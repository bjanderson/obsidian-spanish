# clínica

## Meaning

*Clínica* means "clinic," a smaller or private medical facility. It's a
feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la clínica    |
| Plural   | las clínicas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                             | Notes         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| La clínica abre a las siete de la mañana.         | The clinic opens at seven in the morning.    | statement          |
| ¿Fuiste a la clínica por el dolor de espalda?     | Did you go to the clinic for your back pain? | question         |
| No atienden emergencias en esa clínica.           | That clinic doesn't handle emergencies.      | negation           |
| Hay tres clínicas privadas en el barrio.          | There are three private clinics in the neighborhood. | plural       |
| Pidió cita en la clínica del centro.               | She made an appointment at the downtown clinic. | statement, del  |
