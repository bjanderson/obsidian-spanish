# lágrima

## Meaning

*Lágrima* means "tear," the drop of liquid produced when crying. It's a feminine
noun, following the regular -a pattern.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | la lágrima      |
| Plural   | las lágrimas    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Una lágrima cayó por su mejilla.                     | A tear rolled down her cheek.                  | subject                 |
| No pudo contener las lágrimas al escuchar la noticia. | She couldn't hold back the tears when she heard the news. | plural, direct object |
| ¿Por qué tienes lágrimas en los ojos?                | Why do you have tears in your eyes?            | question                |
| Sus lágrimas de alegría sorprendieron a todos.       | Her tears of joy surprised everyone.           | possessive, subject      |
| Lloró en silencio, sin derramar ni una lágrima.      | He cried silently, without shedding a single tear. | negation                |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[risa]] — the emotional opposite: *lágrima* for crying, *risa* for laughing.
