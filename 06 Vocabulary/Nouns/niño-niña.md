# niño / niña

## Meaning

*Niño* means "boy" or, more generally, "child" (*el niño*). *Niña* means "girl"
(*la niña*), used specifically for a female child. As with other masculine nouns for
people, the masculine plural *los niños* can mean either "boys" specifically or
"children" in general (a mixed group of boys and girls).

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form       |
| ------------------- | ---------- |
| Masculine singular   | el niño    |
| Feminine singular    | la niña    |
| Masculine plural     | los niños  |
| Feminine plural      | las niñas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                    | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El niño juega en el parque.                   | The boy is playing in the park.                | singular, subject                  |
| La niña juega en el parque.                   | The girl is playing in the park.               | singular, subject                  |
| Vi a un niño llorando en la calle.            | I saw a boy crying in the street.              | object, personal *a*               |
| ¿Conoces a la niña que vive al lado?          | Do you know the girl who lives next door?      | question, personal *a*             |
| Los niños no pueden entrar sin un adulto.     | Children can't enter without an adult.         | plural = "children," negation      |
| La maestra habló con las niñas del salón.     | The teacher spoke with the classroom girls.    | after a preposition                |
