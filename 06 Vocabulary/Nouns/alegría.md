# alegría

## Meaning

*Alegría* means "joy" or "happiness" — a felt, often expressive gladness,
stronger than plain contentment. It's a feminine noun. Common phrases: *con
alegría* ("joyfully"), *dar alegría* ("to bring joy").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la alegría    |
| Plural   | las alegrías  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes            |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Siento mucha alegría cuando visito a mi familia.   | I feel a lot of joy when I visit my family.   | statement, personal *a* |
| ¿Qué te da alegría estos días?                     | What brings you joy these days?               | question           |
| No siento alegría hoy, solo cansancio.             | I don't feel joy today, just tiredness.       | negation           |
| Las pequeñas alegrías de la vida diaria importan.  | Life's small joys matter.                     | plural             |
| Recibió la noticia con alegría.                    | She received the news joyfully.               | idiomatic phrase   |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[diversión]] — a more active, entertainment-driven kind of enjoyment, compared to *alegría*'s inner feeling of joy.
- [[calma]] — a contrasting state: *alegría* is an energized feeling, *calma* is stillness.
