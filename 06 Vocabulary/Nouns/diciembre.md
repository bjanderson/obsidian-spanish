# diciembre

## Meaning

*Diciembre* means "December," the twelfth and last month of the year. It's
masculine: *el diciembre*. Like other month names, it's lowercase and normally used
without an article — *en diciembre*, not *en el diciembre* — except when pointing to
one specific occurrence, like *el diciembre pasado* ("last December").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                                       |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Singular | diciembre (no article in general use)        |
| Plural   | los diciembres (rare — recurring instances)  |
