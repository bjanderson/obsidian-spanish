# piel

## Meaning

*Piel* means "skin." It's feminine: *la piel*. It also means "fur" or "hide" when
talking about animals or leather goods (*un abrigo de piel*, "a fur coat"); the
plural *las pieles* is common in that animal/leather sense.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la piel       |
| Plural   | las pieles    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                       | Notes                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Tiene la piel muy sensible al sol.                   | Her skin is very sensitive to the sun.                | statement                                  |
| Se puso crema en la piel después de bañarse.         | She put cream on her skin after bathing.              | reflexive                                   |
| ¿Por qué se te irritó la piel?                       | Why did your skin get irritated?                      | question, reflexive dative                  |
| No vendas pieles de animales en peligro de extinción.| Don't sell the hides of endangered animals.           | secondary sense, negation                    |
| El sol le quemó la piel de los hombros.              | The sun burned the skin on his shoulders.              | indirect object                              |
