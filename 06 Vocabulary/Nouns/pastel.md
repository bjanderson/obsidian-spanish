# pastel

## Meaning

*Pastel* means "cake" — the traditional birthday cake, or a cake in general. It's
masculine: *el pastel*. In some countries the word *torta* is used instead;
*pastel* is understood everywhere and is the neutral choice.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el pastel      |
| Plural   | los pasteles   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                             | Notes                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| El pastel está en la mesa.                    | The cake is on the table.            | statement, singular        |
| ¿Quién hizo este pastel?                      | Who made this cake?                  | question                   |
| No compró un pastel para la fiesta.           | She didn't buy a cake for the party. | negation                   |
| Los pasteles de esa panadería son deliciosos. | The cakes from that bakery are delicious. | plural                |
| Comió un pedazo de pastel con su familia.     | She ate a piece of cake with her family. | + pedazo               |
