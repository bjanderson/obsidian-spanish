# puente

## Meaning

*Puente* means "bridge" — a structure that crosses a river, road, or valley.
It's masculine: *el puente*. Worth knowing: *hacer puente* is a common idiom
meaning to take a long weekend by "bridging" a holiday to the closest weekend.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el puente    |
| Plural   | los puentes  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                             | English                                        | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| El puente une los dos lados de la ciudad.                | The bridge connects the two sides of the city.      | singular, subject                |
| Cruzamos el puente a pie.                                | We crossed the bridge on foot.                      | singular, direct object          |
| ¿Cuántos puentes hay sobre el río?                       | How many bridges are there over the river?          | plural, question                 |
| No permiten camiones pesados en el puente viejo.         | They don't allow heavy trucks on the old bridge.     | singular, negation                |
| Vamos a hacer puente este fin de semana.                 | We're going to take a long weekend this weekend.    | idiom, no article                 |
