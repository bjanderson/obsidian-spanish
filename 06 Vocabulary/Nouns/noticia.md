# noticia

## Meaning

*Noticia* means "a piece of news" in the singular. Unlike English "news" (which is
grammatically uncountable), Spanish *noticia* is a regular countable feminine noun —
*una noticia* is one news item, and *las noticias* ("the news") is the normal way to
refer to news coverage collectively (e.g. a news broadcast).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | la noticia      |
| Plural   | las noticias    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                      | English                                | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Tengo una noticia importante para ti.                 | I have important news for you.                | direct object, countable      |
| ¿Viste las noticias de anoche?                        | Did you see last night's news?                | question, plural (broadcast)   |
| La noticia se difundió rápidamente.                   | The news spread quickly.                      | subject                         |
| Nos dieron malas noticias en el hospital.             | They gave us bad news at the hospital.        | plural, indirect object *nos*    |
| Leí la noticia en el periódico esta mañana.           | I read the news story in the paper this morning. | direct object                 |
