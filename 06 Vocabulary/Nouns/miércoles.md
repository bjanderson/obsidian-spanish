# miércoles

## Meaning

*Miércoles* is Wednesday. Days of the week are lowercase in Spanish and
masculine: *el miércoles*. Like the rest of Monday through Friday, it's
invariable in the plural — *los miércoles* uses the same form as *el
miércoles* (compare [[sábado]] and [[domingo]], which pluralize regularly).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el miércoles  |
| Plural   | los miércoles |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                     | Notes                            |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| El miércoles entrego el informe.             | On Wednesday I'm turning in the report.        | *el* + day, specific occurrence      |
| Los miércoles ceno con mi familia.           | On Wednesdays I have dinner with my family.    | *los* + day, habitual                |
| ¿Qué haces los miércoles por la noche?       | What do you do on Wednesday nights?            | question                              |
| El miércoles no pude venir.                  | I couldn't come on Wednesday.                  | negative, past                        |
| El curso empieza un miércoles.               | The course starts on a Wednesday.              | indefinite article *un*               |
