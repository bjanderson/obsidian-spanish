# vino

## Meaning

*Vino* means "wine." It's masculine: *el vino*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el vino     |
| Plural   | los vinos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| El vino de esta región es reconocido.            | The wine from this region is well known.      | statement                             |
| ¿Prefieres vino tinto o blanco?                  | Do you prefer red or white wine?              | question                              |
| No sirvieron vino en la cena.                    | They didn't serve wine at dinner.             | negation                              |
| Los vinos bolivianos han mejorado mucho.         | Bolivian wines have improved a lot.           | plural                                 |
| Compramos una botella de vino para la fiesta.    | We bought a bottle of wine for the party.     | statement                              |
