# riñón

## Meaning

*Riñón* means "kidney." It's masculine: *el riñón*. As with *pulmón*, the
written accent on the singular drops in the plural (*riñones*) because the
stress stays on the same syllable.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el riñón     |
| Plural   | los riñones  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Le trasplantaron un riñón el año pasado. | He got a kidney transplant last year.   | singular, object            |
| Los riñones filtran la sangre.          | The kidneys filter the blood.           | plural, subject              |
| ¿Qué función cumplen los riñones?       | What function do the kidneys serve?     | question, plural             |
| No tiene problemas de riñón.            | He doesn't have kidney problems.        | negation, singular            |
| Donó uno de sus riñones a su hermano.   | He donated one of his kidneys to his brother. | plural, personal *a* |
