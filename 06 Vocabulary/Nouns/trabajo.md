# trabajo

## Meaning

*Trabajo* means "work" in the general sense (effort, labor) and also "a job"
(a specific position of employment). It's masculine: *el trabajo*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el trabajo   |
| Plural   | los trabajos |

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                          | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Mi trabajo es interesante.            | My job is interesting.             | singular, subject              |
| Busco un trabajo nuevo.               | I'm looking for a new job.         | singular, object                |
| ¿Cuántos trabajos tienes?             | How many jobs do you have?         | question, plural                |
| No tengo trabajo esta semana.         | I don't have work this week.       | negation, singular               |
| Salió del trabajo tarde.              | She left work late.                | after a preposition, "del"       |
