# muñeca

## Meaning

*Muñeca* has two common, unrelated senses that share the same form: the body
part "wrist," and the toy "doll." Both are feminine: *la muñeca*, *las
muñecas*. Context (an injury vs. a toy) tells them apart.

As a body part, *muñeca* follows the usual pattern: a definite article with a
reflexive/dative construction instead of a possessive — *Se torció la muñeca*
("He sprained his wrist"), not *Torció su muñeca*. As a toy, a possessive is
perfectly natural: *su muñeca favorita* ("her favorite doll").
