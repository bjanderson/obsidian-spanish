# juez

## Meaning

*Juez* means "judge." The feminine form is *jueza* in most of Latin
America, though *la juez* (invariant) also appears in some regions.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el juez    |
| Plural   | los jueces |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes             |
