# niño

## Meaning

*Niño* means "boy" or, more generally, "child." It's masculine: *el niño*. As with
other masculine nouns for people, the masculine plural *los niños* can mean either
"boys" specifically or "children" in general (a mixed group of boys and girls).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el niño      |
| Plural   | los niños    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                             | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| El niño juega en el parque.                | The boy is playing in the park.            | singular, subject                  |
| Vi a un niño llorando en la calle.         | I saw a boy crying in the street.          | object, personal *a*               |
| ¿Conoces al niño que vive al lado?         | Do you know the boy who lives next door?   | question, personal *a* + *al*      |
| Los niños no pueden entrar sin un adulto.  | Children can't enter without an adult.     | plural = "children," negation      |
| La maestra habló con los niños del salón.  | The teacher spoke with the classroom kids. | after a preposition                |
