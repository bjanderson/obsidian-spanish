# estadio

## Meaning

*Estadio* means "stadium" — a large venue for sports events with seating for
spectators. It's masculine: *el estadio*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el estadio    |
| Plural   | los estadios  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                            | Notes                        |

| Fuimos al estadio a ver el partido.                            | We went to the stadium to watch the match.                 | singular, *al* contraction        |
