# viernes

## Meaning

*Viernes* is Friday. Days of the week are lowercase in Spanish and
masculine: *el viernes*. Like the rest of Monday through Friday, it's
invariable in the plural — *los viernes* uses the same form as *el viernes*
(compare [[sábado]] and [[domingo]], which pluralize regularly).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el viernes  |
| Plural   | los viernes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                    | Notes                            |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| El viernes salimos temprano del trabajo.     | We leave work early on Friday.               | *el* + day, *del* contraction        |
| Los viernes son mis días favoritos.          | Fridays are my favorite days.                | subject, habitual plural              |
| ¿Vienes a la fiesta el viernes?              | Are you coming to the party on Friday?       | question                              |
| No trabajo los viernes por la tarde.         | I don't work on Friday afternoons.           | negative                              |
| El viernes pasado conocí a Laura.            | Last Friday I met Laura.                     | past specific, personal *a*           |
