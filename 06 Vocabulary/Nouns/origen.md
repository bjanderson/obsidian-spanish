# origen

## Meaning

*Origen* means "origin," the source or starting point of something. It's
masculine, and its plural shifts the accent: *orígenes*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el origen    |
| Plural   | los orígenes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                      | English                                | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Nadie conoce el origen de esa costumbre.               | No one knows that custom's origin.             | statement           |
| ¿Cuál es el origen de tu apellido?                     | What's the origin of your last name?           | question           |
| No hay origen claro para esta palabra.                 | There's no clear origin for this word.         | negation           |
| Los orígenes de la familia están en el sur.            | The family's origins are in the south.         | plural             |
| Su origen es humilde pero orgulloso.                    | Her origin is humble but proud.                | statement          |
