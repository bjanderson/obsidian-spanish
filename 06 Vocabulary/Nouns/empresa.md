# empresa

## Meaning

*Empresa* means "company" or "business" (a commercial enterprise). It's feminine: *la
empresa*. Regular *-a* → feminine noun, no irregularities.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la empresa    |
| Plural   | las empresas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| La empresa contrató a diez personas nuevas.        | The company hired ten new people.                | subject, personal a                |
| Fundamos la empresa hace cinco años.               | We founded the company five years ago.           | object                              |
| ¿En qué empresa trabajas?                          | What company do you work for?                    | question, after a preposition       |
| Esa empresa no paga bien.                          | That company doesn't pay well.                   | negation                            |
| Las empresas pequeñas enfrentan muchos retos.      | Small companies face many challenges.            | plural, subject                     |
