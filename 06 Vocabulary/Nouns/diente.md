# diente

## Meaning

*Diente* means "tooth." It's masculine: *el diente*. Common in the idiom
*hablar entre dientes* ("to mutter, talk under one's breath").

Like other body parts, teeth are normally referred to with a definite article
and a reflexive construction rather than a possessive adjective: *Se cepilla
los dientes* ("He brushes his teeth"), not *Cepilla sus dientes*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el diente     |
| Plural   | los dientes   |
