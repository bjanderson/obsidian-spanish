# correo

## Meaning

*Correo* means "mail." In tech contexts it's shorthand for *correo
electrónico* ("email") — Spanish speakers often just say *correo* alone to
mean "email." It's masculine: *el correo*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el correo    |
| Plural   | los correos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                             | English                                        | Notes                |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Te mandé un correo con los detalles.                | I sent you an email with the details.          | singular, statement  |
| ¿Revisaste el correo hoy?                           | Did you check your email today?                | question              |
| No recibí el correo que me prometiste.              | I didn't receive the email you promised me.    | negation              |
| Los correos de trabajo llegan muy temprano.         | Work emails arrive very early.                 | plural                |
| Envió el correo electrónico antes de la reunión.    | She sent the email before the meeting.         | full compound form    |
