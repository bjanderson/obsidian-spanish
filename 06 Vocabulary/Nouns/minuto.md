# minuto

## Meaning

*Minuto* means "minute" (the unit of time). It's masculine: *el minuto*. Regular *-o*
→ masculine noun, no irregularities.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el minuto    |
| Plural   | los minutos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                                | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| El minuto pasó muy rápido.                | The minute went by very fast.              | singular, subject                |
| Espera un minuto, por favor.              | Wait a minute, please.                     | object                            |
| ¿Cuántos minutos faltan?                  | How many minutes are left?                 | plural, question                  |
| No tengo ni un minuto libre hoy.          | I don't have even one free minute today.   | negation                          |
| Llegó después de veinte minutos.          | He arrived after twenty minutes.           | plural, after a preposition        |
