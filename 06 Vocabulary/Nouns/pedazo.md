# pedazo

## Meaning

*Pedazo* means "piece" or "chunk" — a part broken or cut off from something whole.
It's masculine: *el pedazo*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el pedazo      |
| Plural   | los pedazos    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                             | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Valentina come un pedazo de pastel.           | Valentina eats a piece of cake.               | statement, singular              |
| ¿Me das un pedazo de pan?                     | Will you give me a piece of bread?            | question, indirect object          |
| No quedó ni un pedazo del pastel.             | Not even one piece of the cake was left.      | negation, del                     |
| Cortó el papel en pedazos pequeños.           | She cut the paper into small pieces.          | plural                             |
| Guardó un pedazo para su hermano.             | She saved a piece for her brother.            | statement                          |
