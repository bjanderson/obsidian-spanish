# salón

## Meaning

*Salón* means "hall" or "classroom" — a large room used for a specific purpose:
*salón de clases* (classroom), *salón de belleza* (beauty salon), *salón de fiestas*
(event hall). It's a masculine noun. Note: *salón* overlaps closely with *aula*
("classroom") in the "classroom" sense — *aula* is the more formal/institutional
word for a classroom specifically, while *salón* is broader and also covers halls
and function rooms.

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes                    |

| El salón estaba lleno de gente.                          | The hall was full of people.                   | subject                            |

| Hay treinta salones en esta escuela.                     | There are thirty classrooms in this school.    | plural, impersonal *hay*            |
