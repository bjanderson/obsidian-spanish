# dolor

## Meaning

*Dolor* means "pain," physical or emotional. It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el dolor     |
| Plural   | los dolores  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Tengo un dolor de cabeza terrible.                | I have a terrible headache.                  | statement          |
| ¿Sientes dolor cuando presiono aquí?              | Do you feel pain when I press here?          | question           |
| No siento dolor en el brazo ahora.                | I don't feel pain in my arm now.             | negation           |
| Los dolores musculares desaparecieron con el masaje. | The muscle pains disappeared with the massage. | plural         |
| Sintió un dolor profundo tras la pérdida.         | She felt a deep pain after the loss.         | secondary sense    |
