# pizarra

## Meaning

*Pizarra* means "whiteboard" or "chalkboard" — the board a teacher writes on in a
classroom. It's feminine: *la pizarra*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | la pizarra     |
| Plural   | las pizarras   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| La maestra explica los números en la pizarra.    | The teacher explains the numbers on the whiteboard. | statement, singular       |
| ¿Puedes leer lo que dice la pizarra?             | Can you read what the whiteboard says?       | question                        |
