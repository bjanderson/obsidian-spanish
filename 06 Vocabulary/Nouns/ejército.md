# ejército

## Meaning

*Ejército* means "army," a nation's organized military force. It's a
masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el ejército    |
| Plural   | los ejércitos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                 | English                              | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Su hermano sirve en el ejército.                    | His brother serves in the army.              | statement          |
| ¿Cuántos soldados tiene el ejército?                | How many soldiers does the army have?        | question           |
| No confían en el ejército de ese país.              | They don't trust that country's army.        | negation           |
| Los ejércitos se retiraron de la frontera.          | The armies withdrew from the border.         | plural             |
| Se unió al ejército a los dieciocho años.           | He joined the army at eighteen.              | statement          |
