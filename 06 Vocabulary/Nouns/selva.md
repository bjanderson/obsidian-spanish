# selva

## Meaning

*Selva* means "jungle" or dense tropical rainforest — thicker and warmer
than a *bosque*. It's feminine: *la selva*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la selva    |
| Plural   | las selvas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                   | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| La selva amazónica cubre gran parte de Bolivia.    | The Amazon jungle covers a large part of Bolivia. | statement                      |
| ¿Han visitado la selva alguna vez?                 | Have you all ever visited the jungle?             | question                       |
| No es fácil viajar por la selva.                   | It isn't easy to travel through the jungle.       | negation                       |
| Las selvas tropicales tienen mucha humedad.        | Tropical jungles have a lot of humidity.          | plural                         |
| El guía conoce bien esta selva.                    | The guide knows this jungle well.                 | statement                      |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[bosque]] — a drier, more temperate relative of *selva*.
