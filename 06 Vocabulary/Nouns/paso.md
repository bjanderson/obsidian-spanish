# paso

## Meaning

*Paso* means "step" — both a physical footstep and a step in a process or sequence.
It's a masculine noun, following the regular -o pattern. It also appears in the
fixed phrase *a paso lento/rápido* ("at a slow/fast pace").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el paso      |
| Plural   | los pasos    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                  |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Dio un paso hacia adelante.                          | She took a step forward.                      | direct object                 |
| Sigue estos pasos para resolver el problema.         | Follow these steps to solve the problem.      | command, plural                |
| Caminaba a paso lento por el parque.                 | He was walking at a slow pace through the park. | fixed phrase, *a paso lento*  |
| El bebé dio sus primeros pasos ayer.                 | The baby took his first steps yesterday.      | plural, direct object            |
| Cada paso cuenta en este proyecto.                   | Every step counts in this project.            | subject                         |
