# parte

## Meaning

*Parte* means "part" — a piece or portion of a whole. It's feminine: *la parte*.
In legal or formal contexts it also means "party," as in *las partes del contrato*
("the parties to the contract").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Singular     | la parte     |
| Plural         | las partes   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                     | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Esta es la parte más difícil del examen.                        | This is the hardest part of the exam.                  | singular, subject                 |
| ¿Cuáles son las partes del cuerpo humano?                       | What are the parts of the human body?                  | plural, question                   |
| Leí solo una parte del libro.                                   | I read only a part of the book.                        | singular, object                   |
| No entendí ninguna parte de la explicación.                     | I didn't understand any part of the explanation.       | singular, negation                  |
| Repartieron el premio entre las partes involucradas.            | They split the prize among the parties involved.       | plural, after preposition, "party" sense |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[partir]] — the verb *partir* ("to divide, to depart") shares the same root as *parte*.
