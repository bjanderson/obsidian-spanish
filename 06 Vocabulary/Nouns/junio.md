# junio

## Meaning

*Junio* is June, the sixth month of the year. Like all months in Spanish,
it's lowercase and masculine, and it's rarely used with an article — say
*en junio*, not *en el junio*. An article does appear before a specific
date: *el veintiuno de junio*. In Bolivia, junio marks the start of winter
in the Southern Hemisphere.
