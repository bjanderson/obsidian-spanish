# oso

## Meaning

*Oso* means "bear." It's masculine: *el oso* (the feminine form *la osa*
refers specifically to a female bear).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form      |
| -------- | --------- |
| Singular | el oso    |
| Plural   | los osos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| El oso buscaba comida cerca del río.             | The bear was looking for food near the river. | statement                             |
| ¿Se puede ver un oso en este parque?             | Can you see a bear in this park?              | question                              |
| No nos acercamos al oso, era peligroso.          | We didn't get close to the bear, it was dangerous. | negation, personal *a*          |
| Los osos duermen durante el invierno.            | Bears sleep during the winter.                | plural                                 |
| El osito de peluche era un regalo del abuelo.    | The teddy bear was a gift from grandpa.       | diminutive, "teddy bear"                |
