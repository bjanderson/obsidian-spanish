# estilo

## Meaning

*Estilo* means "style," a distinctive manner of dressing, writing, or
doing something. It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el estilo   |
| Plural   | los estilos |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Me gusta el estilo de esta casa.                | I like this house's style.                   | statement, de      |
| ¿Cuál es tu estilo de baile favorito?           | What's your favorite dance style?            | question           |
| No tiene estilo propio todavía.                 | She doesn't have her own style yet.          | negation           |
| Los distintos estilos musicales se mezclaron.   | The different musical styles blended.        | plural             |
| Escribe siempre en el mismo estilo.             | He always writes in the same style.          | statement          |
