# sueldo

## Meaning

*Sueldo* means "salary" or "wage" — a near-synonym of [[salario]], and the two
are largely interchangeable. *Sueldo* is arguably the more everyday,
conversational choice (*me subieron el sueldo*, "I got a raise"), while
*salario* shows up more in formal or administrative contexts. It's a masculine
noun.
