# garaje

## Meaning

*Garaje* means "garage." It's masculine: *el garaje*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el garaje    |
| Plural   | los garajes  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                        | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El garaje está a la izquierda de la casa.                      | The garage is to the left of the house.                    | singular, subject                |
| ¿Cuántos carros caben en ese garaje?                             | How many cars fit in that garage?                          | singular, question                |
| Guardamos las herramientas en el garaje.                         | We keep the tools in the garage.                           | singular, object of preposition   |
| No hay espacio en el garaje para otro carro.                     | There's no room in the garage for another car.             | singular, negation                |
| Estacionamos la bicicleta al lado del garaje.                    | We parked the bike next to the garage.                     | singular, "del" contraction       |
