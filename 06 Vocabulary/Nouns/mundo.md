# mundo

## Meaning

*Mundo* is masculine (*el mundo*) and means "world," both literally (the planet) and
figuratively (a sphere of activity: *el mundo del arte*, "the art world"). *Todo el
mundo* is a common fixed expression meaning "everyone," literally "the whole world."

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| ---------- | ------------- |
| Singular       | el mundo      |
| Plural           | los mundos    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El mundo está cambiando rápido.             | The world is changing fast.              | subject                            |
| Quiero conocer el mundo.                    | I want to see the world.                 | direct object                      |
| Todo el mundo sabe la noticia.              | Everyone knows the news.                 | fixed expression                   |
| Viajó por muchos mundos diferentes en su carrera. | She moved through many different worlds in her career. | plural, figurative       |
| Es una experta en el mundo de la música.    | She's an expert in the world of music.   | possessive *de*-phrase, figurative  |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[país]] — related — a *país* (country) is part of the *mundo* (world).
- [[tierra]] — near-synonym in some contexts — *tierra* is the physical earth/land, *mundo* is the world as a whole.
