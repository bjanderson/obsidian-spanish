# amistad

## Meaning

*Amistad* means "friendship," the bond between friends. It's a feminine
noun. The plural *amistades* often means "friendships" or "acquaintances"
collectively — *hacer amistades* means "to make friends."

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | la amistad     |
| Plural   | las amistades  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                | Notes            |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Su amistad ha durado más de veinte años.           | Their friendship has lasted over twenty years. | statement    |
| ¿Todavía mantienes esa amistad?                    | Do you still keep up that friendship?      | question           |
| No rompas una amistad por algo tan pequeño.        | Don't break a friendship over something so small. | negation      |
| Hizo muchas amistades en la universidad.           | She made a lot of friends in college.      | plural, idiomatic  |
| La amistad verdadera no necesita explicaciones.    | True friendship doesn't need explanations. | statement          |
