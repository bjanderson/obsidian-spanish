# delito

## Meaning

*Delito* means "offense" or "crime" in the legal sense — any act
punishable by law, from minor to serious. It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el delito    |
| Plural   | los delitos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Cometió un delito menor.                        | He committed a minor offense.                | statement          |
| ¿Qué delito le imputan?                         | What offense is he charged with?             | question           |
| No es un delito estacionar aquí.                | It's not a crime to park here.               | negation           |
| Los delitos financieros son difíciles de probar.| Financial crimes are hard to prove.          | plural             |
| Fue arrestado por un delito grave.              | He was arrested for a serious offense.       | statement          |
