# deporte

## Meaning

*Deporte* means "sport," any organized physical activity or game played
for competition or exercise. It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el deporte   |
| Plural   | los deportes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Mi deporte favorito es el fútbol.                | My favorite sport is soccer.                 | statement          |
| ¿Practicas algún deporte?                        | Do you play any sport?                       | question           |
| No hago deporte desde hace meses.                | I haven't done any sport for months.         | negation           |
| Los deportes de equipo enseñan a trabajar juntos.| Team sports teach people to work together.   | plural             |
| Vamos al parque a hacer deporte.                 | We're going to the park to exercise.         | *al*               |
