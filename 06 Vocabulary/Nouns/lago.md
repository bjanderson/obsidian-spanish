# lago

## Meaning

*Lago* means "lake." It's masculine: *el lago*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el lago    |
| Plural   | los lagos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El lago Titicaca es el más alto del mundo.           | Lake Titicaca is the highest in the world.     | statement                      |
| ¿Fueron al lago este fin de semana?                  | Did you all go to the lake this weekend?       | question                       |
| No había botes en el lago hoy.                       | There were no boats on the lake today.         | negation                       |
| Los lagos de esta zona son muy profundos.            | The lakes in this area are very deep.          | plural                          |
| Nadamos en el lago toda la tarde.                    | We swam in the lake all afternoon.             | statement                       |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[río]] — a body of moving water that can feed a lake.
- [[isla]] — sometimes found within a lake.
