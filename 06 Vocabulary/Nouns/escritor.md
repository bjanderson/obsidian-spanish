# escritor

## Meaning

*Escritor* means "writer" — someone who writes, especially as a profession
(novels, poetry, articles). Like *autor*, it changes form for gender: a male
writer is *el escritor* and a female writer is *la escritora*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number             | Form            |
| ------------------- | ---------------- |
| Singular (masc.)     | el escritor       |
| Singular (fem.)      | la escritora      |
| Plural (masc.)       | los escritores    |
| Plural (fem.)        | las escritoras    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Ese escritor publicó su primera novela joven.      | That writer published his first novel young.     | singular masc., subject               |
| Admiro a la escritora que dio la charla ayer.      | I admire the writer who gave the talk yesterday. | personal *a*, direct object            |
| ¿Conoces al escritor de este cuento?               | Do you know the writer of this story?            | question, "al" + personal *a*          |
| No leyó nada de esa escritora todavía.             | She hasn't read anything by that writer yet.     | negation                              |
| Sus alumnos llegarían a ser grandes escritores.    | Her students would grow up to be great writers.  | plural masc., subject                  |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[autor]] — related — an *escritor* who publishes a work becomes its *autor*.
