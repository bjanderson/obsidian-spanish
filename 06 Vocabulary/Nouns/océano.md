# océano

## Meaning

*Océano* means "ocean." It's masculine: *el océano*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el océano     |
| Plural   | los océanos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El océano Pacífico está al oeste del continente. | The Pacific Ocean is west of the continent.   | statement                      |
| ¿Habías visto el océano antes?                  | Had you seen the ocean before?                | question                       |
| No nos bañamos en el océano por el frío.        | We didn't swim in the ocean because of the cold. | negation                    |
| Los océanos cubren la mayor parte del planeta.  | Oceans cover most of the planet.              | plural                          |
| Miramos el océano desde el acantilado.          | We looked at the ocean from the cliff.        | statement                       |
