# bicicleta

## Meaning

*Bicicleta* means "bicycle." It's feminine: *la bicicleta*. Often shortened
in casual speech to *bici*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form             |
| -------- | ---------------- |
| Singular | la bicicleta     |
| Plural   | las bicicletas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                 | English                              | Notes    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| La bicicleta está en el garaje.                             | The bicycle is in the garage.            | statement  |
| ¿Sabes andar en bicicleta?                                  | Do you know how to ride a bike?          | question   |
| No traje mi bicicleta hoy.                                  | I didn't bring my bicycle today.         | negation   |
| Las bicicletas de montaña son más resistentes.              | Mountain bikes are sturdier.             | plural     |
