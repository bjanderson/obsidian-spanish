# soldado

## Meaning

*Soldado* means "soldier." It's common gender — the article changes
(*el soldado* / *la soldado*) but the noun itself usually doesn't.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el/la soldado |
| Plural   | los/las soldados |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| El soldado montó guardia toda la noche.            | The soldier stood guard all night.           | statement              |
| ¿Conoces a ese soldado?                            | Do you know that soldier?                    | question, personal a   |
| No dejaron pasar al soldado sin identificación.    | They didn't let the soldier through without ID. | negation, personal a |
| La soldado recibió una medalla por su valor.       | The soldier received a medal for her bravery. | feminine              |
| Los soldados regresaron a casa después de la guerra.| The soldiers returned home after the war.    | plural                 |
