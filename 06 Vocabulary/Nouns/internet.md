# internet

## Meaning

*Internet* means "internet." Its gender varies: it's most often used as
masculine (*el internet*), though some regions treat it as feminine (*la
internet*) — both are heard across Latin America. Like in English, it's
normally a mass noun (uncountable) and rarely takes a plural form.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                                                  |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Singular | el internet (also *la internet* in some regions)      |
| Plural   | rarely used — treated as uncountable, like in English |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| No tengo internet en casa.                       | I don't have internet at home.               | negation                        |
| ¿Hay buen internet en el hotel?                  | Is there good internet at the hotel?         | question                        |
