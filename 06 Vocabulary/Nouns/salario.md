# salario

## Meaning

*Salario* means "salary" or "wage" — the money someone earns for their work,
usually paid on a regular schedule. It's a masculine noun. Near-synonym:
[[sueldo]] — the two words are largely interchangeable in everyday use, but
*salario* leans slightly more formal/administrative (e.g. *salario mínimo*,
"minimum wage," is the standard fixed phrase), while *sueldo* is the word people
reach for more often in casual conversation.
