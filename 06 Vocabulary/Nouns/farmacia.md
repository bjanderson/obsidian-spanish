# farmacia

## Meaning

*Farmacia* means "pharmacy" or "drugstore" — a shop where medicine is sold.
It's feminine: *la farmacia*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la farmacia   |
| Plural   | las farmacias |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                            | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Compré las pastillas en la farmacia.                           | I bought the pills at the pharmacy.                       | singular, after a preposition    |
| ¿Hay una farmacia cerca de aquí?                               | Is there a pharmacy near here?                            | singular, question               |
| No venden ese medicamento en esta farmacia.                    | They don't sell that medication at this pharmacy.          | singular, negation                |
| La farmacia de turno atiende toda la noche.                    | The on-duty pharmacy is open all night.                    | singular, subject                 |
| Visitamos varias farmacias buscando ese jarabe.                | We visited several pharmacies looking for that syrup.      | plural, object                    |
