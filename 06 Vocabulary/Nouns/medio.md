# medio

## Meaning

*Medio* covers two related senses. As "middle," it shows up in the set phrase *en
medio de* ("in the middle of"). As "means" — a way of doing or reaching
something — it shows up both for a single means (*el mejor medio para viajar*, "the
best means to travel") and, in the plural, for *los medios* ("the media," i.e. the
means of mass communication). It's masculine: *el medio*. Plural: *los medios*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el medio    |
| Plural   | los medios  |
