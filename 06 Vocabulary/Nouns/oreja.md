# oreja

## Meaning

*Oreja* means "ear" (the outer, visible ear — *oído* refers to the inner ear
and the sense of hearing). It's feminine: *la oreja*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la oreja    |
| Plural   | las orejas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                               | Notes           |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| Tiene una oreja perforada.                         | She has a pierced ear.                      | statement            |
| Me tapé las orejas por el ruido.                   | I covered my ears because of the noise.     | reflexive, plural    |
| ¿Por qué te duele la oreja?                        | Why does your ear hurt?                     | question             |
| No escucho bien de esta oreja.                     | I can't hear well out of this ear.          | negation             |
| El perro mueve las orejas cuando oye algo.         | The dog moves its ears when it hears something. | plural, subject |
