# boca

## Meaning

*Boca* means "mouth." It's feminine: *la boca*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | la boca    |
| Plural   | las bocas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes           |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Abre la boca, por favor.                        | Open your mouth, please.                     | command              |
| Se tapó la boca al toser.                       | She covered her mouth when she coughed.      | reflexive            |
| ¿Por qué respiras por la boca?                  | Why are you breathing through your mouth?    | question             |
| No hables con la boca llena.                    | Don't talk with your mouth full.              | negation             |
| Los niños abrieron la boca sorprendidos.        | The children opened their mouths in surprise.| plural               |
