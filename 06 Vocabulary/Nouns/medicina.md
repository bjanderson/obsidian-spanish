# medicina

## Meaning

*Medicina* means "medicine," either the medical field or a specific
remedy/drug. It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la medicina   |
| Plural   | las medicinas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                   | English                                | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Estudia medicina en la universidad.                 | She's studying medicine at the university.     | statement          |
| ¿Tomaste la medicina que te recetó el doctor?       | Did you take the medicine the doctor prescribed? | question        |
| No hay medicina para esa enfermedad todavía.        | There's no medicine for that illness yet.      | negation           |
| Compramos varias medicinas en la farmacia.          | We bought several medicines at the pharmacy.   | plural             |
| Practica la medicina desde hace veinte años.        | She's practiced medicine for twenty years.     | statement          |
