# techo

## Meaning

*Techo* means "roof" of a building, and also "ceiling" of a room, depending on
context. It's masculine: *el techo*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el techo    |
| Plural   | los techos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                          | English                                              | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El techo de la casa es de tejas rojas.                                    | The roof of the house is made of red tiles.                       | singular, "de" possession        |
| ¿Cuántos techos necesitan reparación después de la tormenta?              | How many roofs need repair after the storm?                       | plural, question                  |
| Pintamos el techo de la sala la semana pasada.                            | We painted the living room ceiling last week.                     | singular, past tense              |
| No hay goteras en el techo todavía.                                       | There are no leaks in the roof yet.                                | singular, negation                |
| Vimos las estrellas desde el techo del edificio.                          | We saw the stars from the roof of the building.                   | singular, "del" contraction       |
