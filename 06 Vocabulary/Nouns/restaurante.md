# restaurante

## Meaning

*Restaurante* means "restaurant." It's masculine: *el restaurante*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Singular | el restaurante    |
| Plural   | los restaurantes  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                  | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Cenamos en un restaurante italiano anoche.       | We had dinner at an Italian restaurant last night. | singular, after a preposition |
| Reservé una mesa en el restaurante para las ocho. | I booked a table at the restaurant for eight.  | singular, after a preposition     |
| ¿Conoces algún restaurante bueno cerca del hotel? | Do you know any good restaurant near the hotel? | question, *del*                 |
| No hay mesas libres en el restaurante.           | There are no free tables at the restaurant.    | negation                          |
| Los restaurantes del centro están llenos los fines de semana. | Downtown restaurants are full on weekends. | plural, subject, *del*   |
