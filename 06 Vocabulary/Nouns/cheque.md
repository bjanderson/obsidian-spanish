# cheque

## Meaning

*Cheque* means "check" — a written order to a bank to pay a specific amount. It's
a masculine noun, one of the three common payment methods alongside
[[efectivo]] (cash) and [[tarjeta]] (card).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el cheque     |
| Plural   | los cheques   |
