# número

## Meaning

*Número* means "number" — a numeral, a quantity, or an item's position in a
sequence (like a house or phone number). It's masculine, following the regular
-o ending pattern: *el número*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Singular      | el número      |
| Plural           | los números    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ¿Cuál es tu número de teléfono?             | What's your phone number?              | subject, question                  |
| Marqué el número equivocado.                | I dialed the wrong number.             | direct object                      |
| Vivo en el número 45 de esa calle.          | I live at number 45 on that street.    | after a preposition                 |
| Los números pares son fáciles de recordar.  | Even numbers are easy to remember.     | plural                             |
| El número de la casa está en la puerta.     | The house number is on the door.       | possessive *de*-phrase              |
