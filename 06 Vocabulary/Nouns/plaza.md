# plaza

## Meaning

*Plaza* means a public square — an open, usually paved area in the center of a
town or neighborhood, often surrounded by shops, a church, or government
buildings. It's feminine: *la plaza*. A secondary sense worth knowing:
*plaza* can also mean an available "spot" or "opening," as in *una plaza de
trabajo* (a job opening) or *una plaza de parqueo* (a parking spot).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                        |

| La plaza principal está llena de gente los domingos. | The main square is full of people on Sundays. | singular, subject                |
| Nos encontramos en la plaza a las seis.              | We're meeting at the square at six.           | singular, after a preposition    |

| No hay bancos libres en la plaza hoy.                | There are no free benches in the square today. | singular, negation                |
| Las plazas de este barrio son muy tranquilas.        | The squares in this neighborhood are very quiet. | plural, subject                   |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[avenida]] — a main *avenida* often leads directly into the town's *plaza*.
- [[puente]] — street-infrastructure cluster; a *puente* may connect a *plaza* to another part of town.
- [[banco]] — a *plaza* is a common place to find *bancos* (benches) to sit on.
- [[calle]] — a *plaza* is where several *calles* typically converge.
