# letra

## Meaning

*Letra* means "letter," a character of the alphabet, and also a song's
lyrics or a person's handwriting. It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | la letra   |
| Plural   | las letras |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| La primera letra de mi nombre es J.               | My name's first letter is J.                 | statement              |
| ¿Sabes la letra de esa canción?                   | Do you know that song's lyrics?              | question, secondary sense |
| No entiendo tu letra, escribes muy rápido.        | I can't read your handwriting, you write too fast. | negation, secondary sense |
| El alfabeto tiene veintisiete letras.             | The alphabet has twenty-seven letters.       | plural                 |
| Cantaron la letra completa sin equivocarse.       | They sang the whole lyrics without a mistake. | statement              |
