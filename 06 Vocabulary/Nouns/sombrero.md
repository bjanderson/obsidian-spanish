# sombrero

## Meaning

*Sombrero* means "hat." It's masculine: *el sombrero*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el sombrero     |
| Plural   | los sombreros   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                             | Notes    |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| El sombrero lo protege del sol.                             | The hat protects him from the sun.          | statement  |
| ¿Por qué te quitaste el sombrero?                           | Why did you take off your hat?              | question   |
| No traje sombrero y me quemé la cara.                       | I didn't bring a hat and I got sunburned.   | negation   |
| Los sombreros de paja son ideales para el verano.           | Straw hats are ideal for summer.            | plural     |
| Compró un sombrero nuevo para la playa.                     | She bought a new hat for the beach.         | statement  |
