# calor

## Meaning

*Calor* means "heat" — warmth, especially of weather or temperature. It's a
masculine noun. Two common idioms use different verbs depending on what's
hot: *hacer calor* ("to be hot," describing the weather) versus *tener
calor* ("to feel hot," describing a person) — don't mix them up.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el calor     |
| Plural   | los calores  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                | Notes                  |
