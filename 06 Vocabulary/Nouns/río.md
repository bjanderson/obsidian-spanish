# río

## Meaning

*Río* means "river." It's masculine: *el río*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form      |
| -------- | --------- |
| Singular | el río    |
| Plural   | los ríos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                               | English                          | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| El río bajó mucho este año.                    | The river's water level dropped a lot this year. | statement                |
| ¿Se puede nadar en este río?                   | Can you swim in this river?               | question                       |
| No cruzamos el río a pie.                      | We didn't cross the river on foot.        | negation                       |
| Los ríos de esta región alimentan el lago.     | The rivers in this region feed the lake.  | plural                          |
| Construyeron un puente sobre el río.           | They built a bridge over the river.       | statement                       |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[lago]] — a body of water a river often feeds into.
- [[agua]] — what a river is made of.
