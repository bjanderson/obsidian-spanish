# siglo

## Meaning

*Siglo* means "century" — a period of one hundred years. It's masculine: *el siglo*.
Centuries are usually named with Roman numerals but read aloud as ordinal or cardinal
numbers: *el siglo XXI* is read *"el siglo veintiuno."*

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el siglo     |
| Plural   | los siglos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                        | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| El siglo XXI trajo muchos cambios tecnológicos.         | The 21st century brought many technological changes.   | statement, subject               |
| Esta iglesia fue construida en el siglo XVI.            | This church was built in the 16th century.              | after preposition *en*           |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[época]] — a *siglo* can span several distinct *épocas*.
