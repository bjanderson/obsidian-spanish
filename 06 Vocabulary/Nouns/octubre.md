# octubre

## Meaning

*Octubre* means "October," the tenth month of the year. It's masculine: *el octubre*.
Spanish month names are lowercase (unlike English) and are normally used without an
article — *en octubre*, not *en el octubre* — except when specifying a particular
occurrence of it, like *el octubre pasado* ("last October").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                                     |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Singular | octubre (no article in general use)      |
| Plural   | los octubres (rare — recurring instances) |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                  | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Octubre es un mes lluvioso aquí.                  | October is a rainy month here.             | statement, subject, no article    |
| Vamos a viajar a Perú en octubre.                 | We're going to travel to Peru in October.  | after preposition *en*, no article |
| ¿Qué planes tienes para octubre?                  | What plans do you have for October?        | question                          |
| No trabajé mucho el octubre pasado.                | I didn't work much last October.           | negation, article, specific instance |
| Cada octubre celebramos el aniversario de la empresa. | Every October we celebrate the company's anniversary. | recurring, no article        |
