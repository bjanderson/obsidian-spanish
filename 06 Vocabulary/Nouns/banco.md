# banco

## Meaning

*Banco* has two common, unrelated senses, and context tells them apart:

1. **Bank** — a financial institution, e.g. *el banco donde deposito mi dinero*.
2. **Bench** — a long seat, usually outdoors in a park or plaza, e.g. *un banco de parque*.

It's masculine in both senses: *el banco*.
