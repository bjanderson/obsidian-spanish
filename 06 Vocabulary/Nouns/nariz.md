# nariz

## Meaning

*Nariz* means "nose." It's feminine: *la nariz*, and its plural is irregular:
*las narices* (not *narizes*).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la nariz     |
| Plural   | las narices  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                  | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Le sangra la nariz.                                    | His nose is bleeding.                          | statement                        |
| Se tapó la nariz por el mal olor.                      | She covered her nose because of the bad smell. | reflexive                         |
| ¿Te duele la nariz?                                    | Does your nose hurt?                           | question                          |
| No puedo respirar bien por la nariz.                   | I can't breathe well through my nose.          | negation                          |
| A los niños les sangran las narices con el calor.      | Children's noses bleed in the heat.            | plural, irregular, personal *a*   |
