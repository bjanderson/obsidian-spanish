# abrigo

## Meaning

*Abrigo* means "coat," a heavier outer garment worn for warmth. It's
masculine: *el abrigo*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el abrigo     |
| Plural   | los abrigos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                             | English                                  | Notes    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| El abrigo está colgado junto a la puerta.                   | The coat is hanging by the door.                | statement  |
| ¿Necesito abrigo para salir hoy?                            | Do I need a coat to go out today?               | question   |
| No traje abrigo y hace mucho frío.                          | I didn't bring a coat and it's very cold.       | negation   |
| Los abrigos de lana abrigan mejor que los de algodón.       | Wool coats keep you warmer than cotton ones.    | plural     |
| Se puso el abrigo antes de salir a la calle.                | He put on his coat before going out.            | statement  |
