# arte

## Meaning

*Arte* means "art." It's masculine in the singular (*el arte*), but
commonly feminine in the plural, especially in the fixed phrase *las
bellas artes* ("the fine arts").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form      |
| -------- | --------- |
| Singular | el arte   |
| Plural   | las artes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                   | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| El arte moderno no me gusta mucho.                 | I don't like modern art much.                   | statement          |
| ¿Estudiaste arte en la universidad?                | Did you study art at university?                | question           |
| No entiendo el arte abstracto.                     | I don't understand abstract art.                | negation           |
| Las bellas artes incluyen la pintura y la música.  | The fine arts include painting and music.       | plural, fem.       |
| El museo de arte abre los martes.                  | The art museum opens on Tuesdays.               | *de*-phrase        |
