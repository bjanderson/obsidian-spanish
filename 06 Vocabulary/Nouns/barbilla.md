# barbilla

## Meaning

*Barbilla* means "chin" — the body part. It's feminine: *la barbilla*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la barbilla   |
| Plural   | las barbillas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                    | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Tiene un lunar en la barbilla.                 | She has a mole on her chin.                 | singular, after a preposition |
| Se rascó la barbilla pensando en la respuesta. | He scratched his chin thinking of the answer. | reflexive, singular object   |
| ¿Te lastimaste la barbilla?                    | Did you hurt your chin?                     | question                      |
| No me gusta esa barbilla partida.              | I don't like that cleft chin.               | negation                      |
| Las barbillas de los hermanos son idénticas.   | The brothers' chins are identical.          | plural, subject, *de*         |
