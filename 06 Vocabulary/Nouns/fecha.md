# fecha

## Meaning

*Fecha* means "date" — the specific day, month, and year of an event (as opposed to
*hora*, which is the time of day). It's feminine: *la fecha*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la fecha     |
| Plural   | las fechas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                   | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| ¿Cuál es la fecha de hoy?                             | What's today's date?                              | question, subject                   |
| La fecha límite es el viernes.                        | The deadline (date) is Friday.                    | statement, subject                  |
| No recuerdo la fecha exacta de su cumpleaños.          | I don't remember the exact date of her birthday.  | negation, object, personal context  |
| Anotamos la fecha de la reunión en el calendario.     | We wrote down the meeting date on the calendar.    | object, *en*-phrase                 |
| Todavía no elegimos una fecha para la boda.            | We still haven't chosen a date for the wedding.    | negation, object                    |
