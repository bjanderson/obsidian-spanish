# perro

## Meaning

*Perro* means "dog." It's masculine: *el perro* (the feminine form *la
perra* refers specifically to a female dog).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el perro    |
| Plural   | los perros  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Mi perro duerme todo el día.                     | My dog sleeps all day.                        | statement                             |
| ¿Cómo se llama tu perro?                         | What's your dog's name?                       | question                              |
| No dejamos entrar al perro a la cocina.          | We don't let the dog into the kitchen.        | negation, personal *a*                 |
| Los perros de la vecina ladran mucho.            | The neighbor's dogs bark a lot.               | plural                                 |
| Sacamos a pasear al perro por la mañana.         | We take the dog for a walk in the morning.    | statement, personal *a*                |
