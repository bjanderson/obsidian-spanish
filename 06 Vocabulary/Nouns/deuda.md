# deuda

## Meaning

*Deuda* means "debt" — money that's owed. It's a feminine noun, closely related
to [[préstamo]] (loan): the *préstamo* is the money borrowed; the *deuda* is what
remains owed on it.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la deuda      |
| Plural   | las deudas    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes    |
| ------------------------------------------------| ---------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Tiene una deuda grande con el banco.              | They have a big debt with the bank.      | statement|
| ¿Cuánto es la deuda total?                        | How much is the total debt?              | question |
| No quiero más deudas.                             | I don't want any more debts.             | negation, plural |
| Las deudas crecen con los intereses.              | Debts grow with interest.                | plural   |
| Pagó la deuda del préstamo antes de tiempo.       | They paid off the loan's debt early.     | *del*    |
