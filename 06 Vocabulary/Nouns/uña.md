# uña

## Meaning

*Uña* means "fingernail" (also "toenail" as *la uña del pie*). It's feminine:
*la uña*. Common in the idiom *ser uña y carne* ("to be inseparable," literally
"to be nail and flesh").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la uña      |
| Plural   | las uñas    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                   | Notes                          |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Se rompió una uña abriendo la lata.                  | She broke a nail opening the can.                  | reflexive                              |
| Se pinta las uñas de rojo cada semana.               | She paints her nails red every week.               | reflexive, no possessive                |
| ¿Te cortaste las uñas hoy?                           | Did you cut your nails today?                      | question                               |
| No te muerdas las uñas.                              | Don't bite your nails.                             | negation, imperative                   |
| Son uña y carne desde la infancia.                   | They've been inseparable since childhood.          | idiom                                   |
