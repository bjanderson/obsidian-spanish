# panadería

## Meaning

*Panadería* means "bakery" — a shop where bread and other baked goods are
made and sold. It's feminine: *la panadería*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | la panadería    |
| Plural   | las panaderías  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                            | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| El pan de esa panadería es delicioso.                          | The bread from that bakery is delicious.                   | singular, subject                |
| Pasamos por la panadería antes de ir al trabajo.               | We stopped by the bakery before going to work.             | singular, after a preposition    |
