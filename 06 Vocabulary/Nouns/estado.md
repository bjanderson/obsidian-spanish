# estado

## Meaning

*Estado* covers two related senses: a political "state" (a country, or a state
within a federal country) and a "state/condition" something is in (*el estado del
auto*, "the condition of the car"). It's masculine, following the regular -o
ending pattern: *el estado*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Singular      | el estado      |
| Plural           | los estados    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Bolivia es un estado soberano.              | Bolivia is a sovereign state.            | subject                            |
| ¿En qué estado está el edificio?            | What condition is the building in?       | after a preposition, question      |
| Los estados de esta región son muy diversos. | The states in this region are very diverse. | plural                          |
| Revisamos el estado del contrato.           | We reviewed the state of the contract.   | possessive *de*-phrase              |
| No conozco el estado exacto del proyecto.   | I don't know the exact status of the project. | direct object                  |
