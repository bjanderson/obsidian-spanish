# enfermero

## Meaning

*Enfermero* means "nurse." The feminine form is *enfermera*, which is the
more commonly heard form since nursing is a majority-female profession in
much of Latin America.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el enfermero   |
| Plural   | los enfermeros |
