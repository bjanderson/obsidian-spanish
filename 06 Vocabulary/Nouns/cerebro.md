# cerebro

## Meaning

*Cerebro* means "brain." It's masculine: *el cerebro*. It's also used
figuratively for "the brains" behind a plan, much like in English.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el cerebro    |
| Plural   | los cerebros  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| El cerebro humano tiene miles de millones de neuronas. | The human brain has billions of neurons.       | singular, subject                 |
| Los médicos examinaron el cerebro del paciente.  | The doctors examined the patient's brain.        | singular, object, *del*           |
| ¿Cómo funciona la memoria en el cerebro?          | How does memory work in the brain?               | question, after a preposition      |
| No entienden bien el cerebro todavía.             | They don't fully understand the brain yet.       | negation                          |
| Los cerebros de los delfines son sorprendentemente grandes. | Dolphins' brains are surprisingly large.  | plural, subject, *de*             |
