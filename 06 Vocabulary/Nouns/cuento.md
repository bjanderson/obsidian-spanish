# cuento

## Meaning

*Cuento* means "story" or "tale," typically a short narrative — fiction, a
fable, or an anecdote (as opposed to *novela*, "novel"). It's a masculine
noun. Common phrase: *cuento de hadas* ("fairy tale").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el cuento   |
| Plural   | los cuentos |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Le leyó un cuento a su hija antes de dormir.       | She read her daughter a story before bed.  | statement             |
| ¿Conoces algún cuento de terror bueno?             | Do you know a good horror story?           | question               |
| No me gustan los cuentos tristes.                  | I don't like sad stories.                  | negation, plural       |
| Escribió varios cuentos durante el verano.         | She wrote several stories over the summer. | plural                 |
| Ese cuento de hadas es mi favorito.                | That fairy tale is my favorite.            | idiomatic phrase        |
