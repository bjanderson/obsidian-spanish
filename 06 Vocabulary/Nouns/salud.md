# salud

## Meaning

*Salud* means "health." It's feminine: *la salud*. It's also the standard toast
Spanish speakers say when raising a glass: *¡Salud!* ("Cheers!"). It's rarely used
in the plural — health is treated as a single, uncountable concept.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Singular     | la salud      |
| Plural         | las saludes (rare — *salud* is almost always used in the singular) |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Su salud ha mejorado mucho.                 | His health has improved a lot.           | singular, subject                  |
| ¿Cómo está tu salud?                        | How's your health?                       | singular, question                 |
| Cuido mi salud con ejercicio.               | I take care of my health with exercise.  | singular, direct object             |
| No es bueno para la salud.                  | It's not good for your health.           | singular, after a preposition       |
| ¡Salud, por el nuevo año!                    | Cheers, to the new year!                 | toast, fixed expression             |
