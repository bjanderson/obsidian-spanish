# tobillo

## Meaning

*Tobillo* means "ankle." It's masculine: *el tobillo*.

Like other body parts, *tobillo* takes a definite article with a
reflexive/dative construction instead of a possessive: *Se torció el tobillo*
("He sprained his ankle"), not *Torció su tobillo*.
