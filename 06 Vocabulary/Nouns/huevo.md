# huevo

## Meaning

*Huevo* means "egg." It's masculine: *el huevo*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el huevo     |
| Plural   | los huevos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| El huevo se rompió en la bolsa.                  | The egg broke in the bag.                     | statement                             |
| ¿Cómo quieres el huevo, frito o hervido?         | How do you want your egg, fried or boiled?    | question                              |
| No compramos huevos esta semana.                 | We didn't buy eggs this week.                 | negation                              |
| Los huevos de esta granja son muy frescos.       | The eggs from this farm are very fresh.       | plural                                 |
| Batió los huevos antes de cocinarlos.            | She beat the eggs before cooking them.        | statement                              |
