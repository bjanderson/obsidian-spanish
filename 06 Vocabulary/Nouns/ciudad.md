# ciudad

## Meaning

*Ciudad* means "city." It's feminine: *la ciudad*. Like most Spanish nouns
ending in *-dad* (a suffix related to English *-ty*, as in *ciudad* ~ "city,"
*verdad* ~ "truth"), it's feminine even though it ends in a consonant rather
than the more typical feminine *-a*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la ciudad     |
| Plural   | las ciudades  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                      |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| La ciudad crece cada año.                           | The city grows every year.                    | singular, subject                |
| Visitamos varias ciudades el verano pasado.         | We visited several cities last summer.        | plural, object                    |
| ¿Conoces bien esta ciudad?                          | Do you know this city well?                   | singular, question                 |
| No encontramos ciudades tan bonitas como esta.      | We didn't find cities as pretty as this one.  | plural, negation                    |
| Vivimos cerca de la ciudad.                         | We live near the city.                        | singular, after a preposition        |
