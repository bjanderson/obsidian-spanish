# mano

# mano

## Meaning

*Mano* is feminine (*la mano*) even though it ends in *-o* — a well-known exception
to the usual *-o* = masculine / *-a* = feminine pattern. It shows up in common
expressions like *darse la mano* ("to shake hands") and *a mano* ("by hand").
