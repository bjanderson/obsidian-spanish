# universo

## Meaning

*Universo* means "universe," all of space and everything in it. It's a
masculine noun, almost always used in the singular.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el universo   |
| Plural   | los universos |

## Examples

| Spanish                                 | English                              | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| El universo es infinito, según los científicos.     | The universe is infinite, according to scientists. | statement       |
| ¿Cómo se formó el universo?                         | How was the universe formed?                 | question           |
| No entendemos todo el universo todavía.             | We don't understand the whole universe yet.  | negation           |
| Los universos paralelos son solo teoría.            | Parallel universes are just theory.          | plural             |
| Estudia el origen del universo.                     | She studies the universe's origin.           | statement, del     |
