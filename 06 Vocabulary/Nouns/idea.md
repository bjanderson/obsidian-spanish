# idea

## Meaning

*Idea* means "idea," a thought or plan. It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | la idea    |
| Plural   | las ideas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                   | English                             | Notes         |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Tengo una idea para el proyecto.                 | I have an idea for the project.              | statement          |
| ¿Qué idea propones?                              | What idea are you proposing?                 | question           |
| No tengo ni idea de la respuesta.                | I have no idea what the answer is.           | negation           |
| Compartimos varias ideas en la reunión.          | We shared several ideas at the meeting.      | plural             |
| Cambió de idea a última hora.                    | She changed her mind at the last minute.     | idiomatic phrase   |
