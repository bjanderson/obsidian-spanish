# empleado

## Meaning

*Empleado* means "employee" — someone who works for a company or employer.
The feminine form is *empleada*: la empleada.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                             |
| -------- | --------------------------------- |
| Singular | el empleado / la empleada         |
| Plural   | los empleados / las empleadas     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| La empresa contrató a un nuevo empleado.                | The company hired a new employee.                | statement, personal a           |
| ¿Cuántos empleados tiene la empresa?                    | How many employees does the company have?         | question                        |
| No conozco a la empleada del turno de la tarde.         | I don't know the employee on the afternoon shift. | negation, personal a, del       |
