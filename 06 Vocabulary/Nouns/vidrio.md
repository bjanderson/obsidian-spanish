# vidrio

## Meaning

*Vidrio* means "glass" as in the material (not a drinking glass, which is *vaso*).
It's a masculine noun, following the regular -o pattern. As a material it's normally
uncountable (*el vidrio*), but the plural *vidrios* is common when talking about
individual panes or pieces of glass.

## Singular / Plural

| Number                          | Form             |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Singular (material)                | el vidrio        |
| Plural (panes/pieces of glass)     | los vidrios      |
