# frontera

## Meaning

*Frontera* means "border," the line separating two countries or regions.
It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | la frontera    |
| Plural   | las fronteras  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                 | English                              | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Cruzamos la frontera sin problemas.                 | We crossed the border without problems.      | statement          |
| ¿A qué hora cierra la frontera?                     | What time does the border close?             | question           |
| No dejaron cruzar la frontera sin pasaporte.        | They didn't let anyone cross the border without a passport. | negation |
| Las fronteras entre esos países están cerradas.     | The borders between those countries are closed. | plural           |
| El pueblo queda cerca de la frontera con Perú.      | The town is near the border with Peru.       | statement          |
