# escalera

## Meaning

*Escalera* means "stairs" or "staircase." It's feminine: *la escalera*. It's often
used in the plural (*las escaleras*) to talk about a flight of stairs, though the
singular *la escalera* is standard for "the staircase" as a unit. It also means
"ladder" (*escalera de mano*).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | la escalera    |
| Plural   | las escaleras  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                        | Notes                     |
