# globo

## Meaning

*Globo* means "balloon" — the party decoration. It's masculine: *el globo*. The same
word also means "globe" (a map of the Earth), but the party-balloon sense is far more
common in everyday speech.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el globo     |
| Plural   | los globos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                          | Notes                |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Hay un globo azul en la puerta.               | There's a blue balloon on the door.      | statement, singular         |
| ¿Compraste globos para la fiesta?             | Did you buy balloons for the party?      | question, plural            |
| No infló el globo porque no tenía tiempo.     | She didn't inflate the balloon because she didn't have time. | negation |
| Los globos de muchos colores decoran la casa. | The balloons in many colors decorate the house. | plural, subject       |
| Los niños jugaron con los globos toda la tarde. | The kids played with the balloons all afternoon. | plural, del not needed |
