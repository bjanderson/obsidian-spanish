# verdad

## Meaning

*Verdad* means "truth." It's feminine, ending in the consonant -d, and forms its
plural by adding -es: *verdades*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| ---------- | ---------------- |
| Singular      | la verdad         |
| Plural           | las verdades      |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| La verdad siempre sale a la luz.            | The truth always comes out.            | subject                            |
| Necesito saber la verdad.                   | I need to know the truth.              | direct object                      |
| Hay varias verdades incómodas en este caso. | There are several uncomfortable truths in this case. | plural                |
| ¿Me estás diciendo la verdad?               | Are you telling me the truth?          | direct object, question            |
| No dudo de la verdad de sus palabras.       | I don't doubt the truth of his words.  | after a preposition                 |
