# marco

## Meaning

*Marco* means "frame" — most often a picture or door/window frame. It's a masculine
noun, following the regular -o pattern. It also has a common figurative sense,
"framework" or "context" (*el marco legal*, "the legal framework").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el marco     |
| Plural   | los marcos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                 | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Puso la foto en un marco de madera.                    | She put the photo in a wooden frame.          | direct object                |
| El marco de la puerta está roto.                       | The door frame is broken.                     | possessive *de*-phrase        |
| Dentro del marco legal, esto no es posible.            | Within the legal framework, this isn't possible. | figurative sense, *del*    |
| Compré marcos nuevos para mis fotos.                   | I bought new frames for my photos.            | plural, direct object          |
| El marco de la ventana necesita pintura.               | The window frame needs paint.                  | possessive *de*-phrase          |
