# línea

## Meaning

*Línea* means "line" — a straight mark, a boundary, or figuratively a
course of action or connection (a phone line, a product line). It's a
feminine noun. Common phrase: *en línea* ("online").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la línea    |
| Plural   | las líneas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                              | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Dibujó una línea recta con la regla.               | She drew a straight line with the ruler. | statement              |
| ¿Está ocupada la línea?                            | Is the line busy?                        | question, phone sense   |
| No cruces esa línea.                               | Don't cross that line.                   | negation, command        |
| Las líneas del tren se retrasaron por la lluvia.   | The train lines were delayed because of the rain. | plural           |
| Prefiero comprar en línea.                         | I prefer to buy online.                  | idiomatic phrase          |
