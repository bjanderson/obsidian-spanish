# amiga

## Meaning

*Amiga* means "friend" (female). It's feminine, following the regular -a ending
pattern: *la amiga*. Its masculine counterpart is [[amigo]], which also covers a
mixed-gender group by default.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Singular      | la amiga       |
| Plural           | las amigas     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi amiga trabaja en un hospital.            | My friend works at a hospital.         | subject                            |
| Llamé a mi amiga anoche.                    | I called my friend last night.         | direct object, personal *a*         |
| Ella tiene amigas en varios países.         | She has friends in several countries.  | plural                             |
| ¿Sales con tus amigas este fin de semana?   | Are you going out with your friends this weekend? | after a preposition, question |
| El cumpleaños de mi amiga es en marzo.      | My friend's birthday is in March.      | possessive *de*-phrase              |
