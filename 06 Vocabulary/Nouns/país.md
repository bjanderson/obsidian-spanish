# país

## Meaning

*País* means "country" (a nation). It's masculine: *el país*. Both the singular
*país* and the plural *países* carry a written accent on the *í* — it's not
dropped in the plural. The tilde breaks the *a-í* hiatus (forcing two separate
syllables, *pa-ís*, *pa-í-ses*) rather than letting Spanish default to reading
*ai* as a single diphthong syllable, which would shift the stress to the wrong
vowel. Both forms need the accent for the same reason.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el país     |
| Plural   | los países  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                            | Notes                    |

| ¿Cuál es tu país favorito?                      | What's your favorite country?         | question                      |

| Viajamos por muchos países de Latinoamérica.    | We traveled through many Latin American countries. | after a preposition, plural |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[gobierno]] — related — a *gobierno* (government) governs a *país* (country).
