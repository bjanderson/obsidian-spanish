# descanso

## Meaning

*Descanso* means "rest" or "break" — a pause from work or activity, whether
a short break during the day or a longer period of rest.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Singular | el descanso       |
| Plural   | los descansos     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                              | Notes            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Necesito un descanso después de tanto trabajo.     | I need a break after so much work.           | statement                |
| ¿Tomaste un descanso hoy?                          | Did you take a break today?                  | question                  |
| No tuvo descanso durante toda la semana.            | She didn't get any rest all week.            | negation                   |
| Los descansos cortos ayudan a concentrarse.         | Short breaks help you concentrate.           | plural                      |
| Después de las vacaciones, volvió con más energía.  | After vacation, he came back with more energy. | statement                 |
