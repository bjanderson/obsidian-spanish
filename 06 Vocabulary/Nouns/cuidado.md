# cuidado

## Meaning

*Cuidado* means "care" or "caution." It's masculine: *el cuidado*. The phrase *con
cuidado* means "carefully," and *¡Cuidado!* on its own is a warning — "Watch out!" or
"Careful!" It's the noun form of the verb *cuidar* ("to take care of").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                                              |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Singular | el cuidado                                          |
| Plural   | los cuidados (used for specific instances of care)  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                          | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Lucas escribe letra por letra, con cuidado.   | Lucas writes letter by letter, carefully. | fixed phrase "con cuidado"     |
| ¡Cuidado con el perro!                        | Watch out for the dog!                  | warning, fixed expression       |
| No tuvo cuidado al cruzar la calle.           | He wasn't careful crossing the street.  | negation, al                    |
| Los cuidados del bebé toman mucho tiempo.     | Taking care of the baby takes a lot of time. | plural                     |
| El cuidado de las plantas requiere paciencia. | Caring for plants requires patience.    | statement, del not needed        |
