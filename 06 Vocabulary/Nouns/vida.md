# vida

## Meaning

*Vida* is feminine (*la vida*) and means "life," both biologically and in the
everyday sense ("my life," "daily life"). It shows up in common expressions like
*de por vida* ("for life") and *así es la vida* ("that's life").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Singular       | la vida      |
| Plural           | las vidas    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
