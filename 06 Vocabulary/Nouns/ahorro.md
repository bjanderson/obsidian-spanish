# ahorro

## Meaning

*Ahorro* means "saving" or "savings." In the singular it often refers to the act
or habit of saving (*el ahorro*), while the plural *los ahorros* refers to the
actual money saved. It's a masculine noun, from the verb *ahorrar* (to save
money).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el ahorro      |
| Plural   | los ahorros    |
