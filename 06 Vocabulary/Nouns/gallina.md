# gallina

## Meaning

*Gallina* means "hen." It's feminine: *la gallina*. It's also used
colloquially to mean "coward" or "chicken" (as an insult).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la gallina    |
| Plural   | las gallinas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| La gallina puso tres huevos hoy.                    | The hen laid three eggs today.                | statement                             |
| ¿Cuántas gallinas tienen en la granja?              | How many hens do they have on the farm?       | question                              |
| No dejamos salir a la gallina del corral.           | We didn't let the hen out of the pen.         | negation, personal *a*                 |
| Las gallinas corrieron cuando llegó el perro.       | The hens ran when the dog arrived.            | plural                                 |
| No seas gallina, inténtalo.                         | Don't be a chicken, try it.                   | colloquial, "coward" sense              |
