# pan

## Meaning

*Pan* means "bread." It's masculine: *el pan*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el pan     |
| Plural   | los panes  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| El pan recién horneado huele delicioso.          | Freshly baked bread smells delicious.         | statement                             |
| ¿Compraste pan esta mañana?                      | Did you buy bread this morning?               | question                              |
| No queda pan para el desayuno.                   | There's no bread left for breakfast.          | negation                              |
| Los panes de esta panadería son muy buenos.      | This bakery's breads are very good.           | plural                                 |
| Cortó el pan en rebanadas finas.                 | She cut the bread into thin slices.           | statement                              |
