# paciente

## Meaning

*Paciente* means "patient," someone receiving medical care. It's common
gender — the noun itself doesn't change; only the article does: *el
paciente* / *la paciente*. It's also an adjective meaning "patient"
(the personality trait).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form             |
| -------- | ---------------- |
| Singular | el/la paciente   |
| Plural   | los/las pacientes |
