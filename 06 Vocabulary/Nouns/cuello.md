# cuello

## Meaning

*Cuello* means "neck" — the body part. It's masculine: *el cuello*. It also means
the "neckline" or "collar" of a piece of clothing (*el cuello de la camisa*, "the
shirt collar").

Like most body parts, *cuello* normally takes a definite article with a reflexive
or dative construction instead of a possessive adjective: *Me duele el cuello*
("My neck hurts"), not *Me duele mi cuello*.
