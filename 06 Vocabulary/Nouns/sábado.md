# sábado

## Meaning

*Sábado* is Saturday. Days of the week are lowercase in Spanish and
masculine: *el sábado*. Unlike Monday through Friday (e.g. *el lunes* / *los
lunes*, unchanged in the plural), *sábado* pluralizes regularly: *el sábado*
→ *los sábados*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el sábado   |
| Plural   | los sábados |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes                            |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| El sábado hay un partido de fútbol.          | There's a soccer match on Saturday.       | *el* + day, specific occurrence      |
| Los sábados dormimos hasta tarde.            | On Saturdays we sleep in.                 | *los* + day, habitual, regular plural |
| ¿Qué planes tienes para el sábado?           | What plans do you have for Saturday?      | question                              |
| No salimos los sábados por la noche.         | We don't go out on Saturday nights.       | negative                              |
| Trabajé dos sábados este mes.                | I worked two Saturdays this month.        | plural count, regular plural form     |
