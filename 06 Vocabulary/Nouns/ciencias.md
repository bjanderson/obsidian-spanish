# ciencias

## Meaning

*Ciencias* means "science" — the school subject. It's feminine and almost always used
in the plural: *las ciencias*. The singular *ciencia* refers to science as a general
concept or field.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                                                    |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Singular | la ciencia (the general concept/field of science)        |
| Plural   | las ciencias (standard, everyday form — the school subject) |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                   | Notes                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Emma tiene la clase de ciencias por la tarde.   | Emma has science class in the afternoon.           | plural, modifier                   |
| ¿Te gustan las ciencias o las matemáticas?       | Do you like science or math?                       | question, plural                   |
| No le interesaban las ciencias antes.            | Science didn't interest her before.                | negation, plural                   |
| Las ciencias son interesantes y ella aprende mucho. | Science is interesting and she learns a lot.    | plural, subject change requires pronoun |
| Aprende sobre los animales en la clase de ciencias. | She learns about animals in science class.      | modifier                            |
