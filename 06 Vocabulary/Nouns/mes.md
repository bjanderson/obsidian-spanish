# mes

## Meaning

*Mes* means "month." It's masculine: *el mes*. It's irregular in the plural: even
though it ends in *-s*, the stress falls on that final syllable (it's a one-syllable
word), so it doesn't stay the same like *el lunes → los lunes* — it takes *-es*:
*los meses*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el mes       |
| Plural   | los meses    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                   | Notes                        |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| El mes de enero fue muy frío.                | The month of January was very cold.           | singular, subject              |
| Vamos a viajar en dos meses.                 | We're going to travel in two months.          | plural, after a preposition    |
| ¿Cuántos meses faltan para el examen?        | How many months are left until the exam?      | question, plural               |
| No trabajé el mes pasado.                    | I didn't work last month.                     | negation, object               |
| Recibimos el pago a fin del mes.             | We get paid at the end of the month.          | after a preposition, *del*     |
