# tema

## Meaning

*Tema* means "topic" or "subject" (of a conversation, essay, or discussion).
Even though it ends in *-a*, it's masculine: *el tema* — not *la tema*. It
belongs to a small group of Greek-origin *-ma* nouns that are masculine
despite the usual "-*a* = feminine" pattern, alongside words like *el
problema* ("the problem"), *el sistema* ("the system"), *el programa* ("the
program"), *el idioma* ("the language"), and *el clima* ("the climate"). Any
article or adjective modifying *tema* agrees masculine: *el tema
interesante*, not *la tema interesante*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el tema     |
| Plural   | los temas   |
