# dedo

## Meaning

*Dedo* means "finger" — and, in the phrase *dedo del pie*, "toe." Without
further context, *dedo* alone is usually understood as "finger." It's
masculine: *el dedo*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el dedo    |
| Plural   | los dedos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Me corté el dedo cocinando.                       | I cut my finger while cooking.              | statement              |
| Señaló la puerta con el dedo.                     | He pointed at the door with his finger.     | object                 |
| ¿Cuántos dedos tienes en cada mano?               | How many fingers do you have on each hand?  | question, plural       |
| No puedo mover el dedo meñique.                   | I can't move my pinky finger.               | negation                |
| Los dedos del pie también se llaman dedos.        | Toes are also called *dedos*.               | plural, *del* contraction |
