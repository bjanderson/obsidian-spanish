# pierna

## Meaning

*Pierna* means "leg" (the whole limb, from hip to foot — not to be confused
with *[[pie]]*, the foot itself). It's feminine: *la pierna*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la pierna    |
| Plural   | las piernas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                            | Notes            |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Se rompió la pierna esquiando.               | She broke her leg skiing.                | statement             |
| Levanté la pierna para estirarme.            | I raised my leg to stretch.              | object                |
| ¿Por qué cojeas de la pierna?                | Why are you limping on your leg?         | question               |
| No siento la pierna izquierda.               | I can't feel my left leg.                | negation               |
| Tiene las piernas largas.                    | She has long legs.                       | plural                 |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[pie]] — the foot at the end of the *pierna*.
- [[brazo]] — the corresponding limb on the upper body.
