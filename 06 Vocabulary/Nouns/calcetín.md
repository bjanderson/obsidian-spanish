# calcetín

## Meaning

*Calcetín* means "sock." It's masculine: *el calcetín*. Like in English,
it's usually talked about in the plural (*los calcetines*) since socks
normally come and are worn in pairs.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form             |
| -------- | ---------------- |
| Singular | el calcetín      |
| Plural   | los calcetines   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                       | Notes                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Perdí un calcetín en la lavadora.                | I lost a sock in the washing machine.         | singular, statement  |
| ¿Tienes calcetines limpios para mañana?          | Do you have clean socks for tomorrow?         | question, plural     |
| No me gustan los calcetines de lana.             | I don't like wool socks.                      | negation, plural     |
| Se puso los calcetines antes de los zapatos.     | He put his socks on before his shoes.         | plural                |
| Los calcetines rotos van a la basura.            | Torn socks go in the trash.                   | plural                |
