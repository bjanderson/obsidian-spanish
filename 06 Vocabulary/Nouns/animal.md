# animal

## Meaning

*Animal* means "animal." It's masculine: *el animal*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el animal     |
| Plural   | los animales  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                   | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Ese animal parece asustado.                        | That animal looks scared.                          | statement                             |
| ¿Qué animal es tu favorito?                        | What's your favorite animal?                       | question                              |
| No vimos ningún animal en el bosque.               | We didn't see any animal in the forest.            | negation                              |
| Los animales de esta granja están bien cuidados.   | The animals on this farm are well cared for.       | plural                                 |
| Cuidamos a los animales todos los días.            | We take care of the animals every day.             | statement, personal *a*                |
