# autobús

## Meaning

*Autobús* means "bus" — the vehicle that carries passengers around a city or
between towns. It's masculine: *el autobús*. Many countries shorten it in
everyday speech (*el bus*), and some use a local word instead (*camión* for
"bus" in Mexico, *guagua* in the Caribbean) — *autobús* itself is understood
everywhere.
