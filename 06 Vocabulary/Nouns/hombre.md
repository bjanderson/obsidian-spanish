# hombre

## Meaning

*Hombre* is masculine (*el hombre*) and means "man," an adult male person. It also
appears as an interjection in casual speech, similar to "man!"/"come on!" in
English (*¡Hombre, no exageres!*, "Come on, don't exaggerate!").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Singular       | el hombre      |
| Plural           | los hombres    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Ese hombre trabaja en el banco.             | That man works at the bank.            | subject                            |
| Conocí a un hombre muy amable ayer.         | I met a very kind man yesterday.       | direct object, personal *a*        |
| ¿Viste al hombre que pasó por aquí?         | Did you see the man who passed by here? | direct object, personal *a* + *al* |
| Los hombres del equipo llegaron tarde.      | The men on the team arrived late.       | plural, possessive *de*-phrase       |
| Hablamos con el hombre de la tienda.        | We spoke with the man from the store.   | after a preposition                  |
