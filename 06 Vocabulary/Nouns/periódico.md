# periódico

## Meaning

*Periódico* means "newspaper." It's a masculine noun, following the regular -o
pattern.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| ---------- | ------------------- |
| Singular      | el periódico        |
| Plural           | los periódicos      |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El periódico llega todas las mañanas.       | The newspaper arrives every morning.     | subject                            |
| ¿Compraste el periódico de hoy?             | Did you buy today's newspaper?           | direct object, question           |
| No leo el periódico en papel, solo en línea. | I don't read the newspaper on paper, only online. | direct object, negation    |
| Hay varios periódicos en el quiosco.        | There are several newspapers at the stand. | plural                            |
| La noticia salió en la portada del periódico. | The news came out on the newspaper's front page. | possessive *de*-phrase, *del*  |
