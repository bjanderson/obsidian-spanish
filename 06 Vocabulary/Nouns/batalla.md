# batalla

## Meaning

*Batalla* means "battle," an armed fight, or figuratively any hard
struggle. It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la batalla    |
| Plural   | las batallas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                 | English                              | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| La batalla duró varios días.                        | The battle lasted several days.              | statement              |
| ¿Quién ganó esa batalla histórica?                  | Who won that historic battle?                | question               |
| No hubo batalla, el ejército se rindió antes.       | There was no battle, the army surrendered before. | negation           |
| Las batallas de esa guerra fueron muy sangrientas.  | That war's battles were very bloody.         | plural                 |
| Perdió la batalla contra la enfermedad.             | She lost the battle against the illness.     | figurative sense       |
