# rodilla

## Meaning

*Rodilla* means "knee." It's feminine: *la rodilla*. Common in the fixed
expression *de rodillas* ("kneeling," "on one's knees").

Like other body parts, *rodilla* takes a definite article with a
reflexive/dative construction instead of a possessive: *Se lastimó la rodilla*
("He hurt his knee"), not *Lastimó su rodilla*.
