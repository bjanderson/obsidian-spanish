# café

## Meaning

*Café* means "coffee," and also "café" (the establishment). It's
masculine: *el café*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el café     |
| Plural   | los cafés   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| El café está muy caliente.                       | The coffee is very hot.                       | statement                             |
| ¿Quieres café con leche?                         | Do you want coffee with milk?                 | question                              |
| No tomamos café por la noche.                    | We don't drink coffee at night.               | negation                              |
| Los cafés de esta calle son excelentes.          | The cafés on this street are excellent.       | plural, "café" sense                    |
| Nos vemos en el café de la esquina.              | We'll meet at the café on the corner.         | statement, "café" sense                 |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[leche]] — commonly added to coffee.
- [[té]] — the other common hot beverage.
