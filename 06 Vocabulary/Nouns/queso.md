# queso

## Meaning

*Queso* means "cheese." It's masculine: *el queso*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el queso    |
| Plural   | los quesos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Este queso es de la región.                      | This cheese is from the region.               | statement                             |
| ¿Qué queso le pongo al sándwich?                 | What cheese should I put on the sandwich?     | question                              |
| No queda queso en el refrigerador.               | There's no cheese left in the fridge.         | negation                              |
| Los quesos bolivianos son muy variados.          | Bolivian cheeses are very varied.             | plural                                 |
| Compramos queso fresco en el mercado.            | We bought fresh cheese at the market.         | statement                              |
