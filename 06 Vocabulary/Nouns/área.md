# área

## Meaning

*Área* means "area" — a region, zone, or field of activity (*área rural*,
"rural area"; *área de trabajo*, "work area"). It's grammatically feminine —
but in the singular it takes the masculine-looking article *el* (*el área*,
not *la área*). This is the same exception as *agua*: feminine nouns that
start with a stressed *á-* or *ha-* sound take *el* in the singular purely to
avoid two stressed "a" sounds clashing back to back. The noun is still
feminine — any adjective modifying it agrees in the feminine, e.g. *el área
pequeña* ("the small area"), not *el área pequeño*. The plural reverts to the
normal feminine article: *las áreas*. See [[agua]] for the same pattern with
another common word.

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                              | Notes                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| El área verde del parque es muy grande.           | The park's green area is very large.     | singular, subject, feminine adjective *verde/grande* |
| Trabajo en el área de finanzas.                   | I work in the finance area.              | singular, after a preposition, "en"         |
| ¿Cuál es el área total del terreno?               | What is the total area of the land?      | question                                     |
| No conozco bien esa área de la ciudad.            | I don't know that area of the city well.  | negation, feminine adjective *esa*           |
| Las áreas rurales necesitan más inversión.        | Rural areas need more investment.         | plural, subject                              |
