# forma

## Meaning

*Forma* means "shape" (physical form) or "form/way" (a manner of doing something,
as in *de esta forma*, "this way" / "like this"). It's feminine: *la forma*.
Related to the verb *formar* ("to form, to shape").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Singular     | la forma     |
| Plural         | las formas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                       | English                                           | Notes                     |
