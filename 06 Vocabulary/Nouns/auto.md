# auto

## Meaning

*Auto* means "car." It's masculine: *el auto*. It's a third common word for
"car" in Latin American Spanish, alongside *carro* and *coche* — regional
preference varies (*auto* is especially common in the Southern Cone countries
like Argentina and Chile), but any of the three is understood everywhere.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el auto    |
| Plural   | los autos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                  | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| El auto está estacionado frente a la casa.     | The car is parked in front of the house.  | statement                  |
| ¿De quién es ese auto rojo?                    | Whose is that red car?                    | question                   |
| No tengo auto, así que voy a pie.              | I don't have a car, so I go on foot.      | negation                   |
| Los autos nuevos son más caros cada año.       | New cars are more expensive every year.   | plural                     |
| Llevé el auto al taller para revisarlo.        | I took the car to the shop to get it checked. | statement, personal a with "el auto" not needed (not a person) |
