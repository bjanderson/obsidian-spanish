# certeza

## Meaning

*Certeza* means "certainty" — the state of being sure about something. It's
a feminine noun. Common phrases: *con certeza* ("with certainty," "for
sure"), *tener la certeza de que...* ("to be certain that...").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | la certeza     |
| Plural   | las certezas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                | Notes           |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| No sé con certeza cuándo llega el tren.            | I don't know for certain when the train arrives. | negation, idiom |
| ¿Tienes la certeza de que esto va a funcionar?     | Are you certain this is going to work?     | question           |
| Habló con total certeza sobre el resultado.        | She spoke with total certainty about the result. | statement       |
| No hay certeza absoluta en la ciencia.             | There's no absolute certainty in science.  | negation            |
| Sus certezas cambiaron después del viaje.          | Her certainties changed after the trip.    | plural               |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[esfuerzo]] — *certeza* often comes only after sustained *esfuerzo*.
- [[equilibrio]] — both are qualities that take time and effort to build.
