# vecino

## Meaning

*Vecino* means "neighbor." It has both a masculine form (*vecino*) and a feminine
form (*vecina*), like most person-nouns ending in -o/-a. A mixed group of neighbors
uses the masculine plural *vecinos*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Masculine       | Feminine          |
| -------- | --------------- | ------------------ |
| Singular | el vecino       | la vecina          |
| Plural   | los vecinos     | las vecinas         |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                  | Notes                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Mi vecino saluda a todos por las mañanas.                   | My neighbor greets everyone in the mornings.       | masculine, personal *a*            |
| La vecina de al lado tiene un perro muy ruidoso.            | The neighbor next door has a very noisy dog.       | feminine                           |
| Nuestros vecinos organizan una fiesta cada año.             | Our neighbors organize a party every year.         | masculine plural                    |
| Hablé con la vecina sobre el ruido.                          | I talked to the neighbor about the noise.          | feminine                            |
| Los vecinos del edificio se conocen bien.                    | The building's neighbors know each other well.     | masculine plural, reciprocal *se*    |
