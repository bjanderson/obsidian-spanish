# amigo / amiga

## Meaning

*Amigo* means "friend" (male, or a mixed-gender group by default); it's masculine,
following the regular -o ending pattern: *el amigo*. *Amiga* means "friend" (female);
it's feminine, following the regular -a ending pattern: *la amiga*.

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form        |
| ------------------- | ----------- |
| Masculine singular   | el amigo    |
| Feminine singular    | la amiga    |
| Masculine plural     | los amigos  |
| Feminine plural      | las amigas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi amigo vive en Cochabamba.                | My friend lives in Cochabamba.                      | subject                            |
| Mi amiga trabaja en un hospital.            | My friend works at a hospital.                      | subject                            |
| Ayer vi a mi amigo en el centro.            | I saw my friend downtown yesterday.                 | direct object, personal *a*        |
| Llamé a mi amiga anoche.                    | I called my friend last night.                      | direct object, personal *a*        |
| Tengo muchos amigos en esta ciudad.         | I have a lot of friends in this city.               | plural                             |
| ¿Sales con tus amigas este fin de semana?   | Are you going out with your friends this weekend?   | after a preposition, question      |
