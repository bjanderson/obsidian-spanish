# hermano / hermana

## Meaning

*Hermano* means "brother" (*el hermano*) and *hermana* means "sister" (*la
hermana*). Like *hijos* and *padres*, the masculine plural *hermanos* usually means
"siblings" (brothers and sisters together), not just "brothers" — context tells you
which sense is meant. *Hermanas* is used specifically when all the siblings are
sisters.

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form          |
| ------------------- | ------------- |
| Masculine singular   | el hermano    |
| Feminine singular    | la hermana    |
| Masculine plural     | los hermanos  |
| Feminine plural      | las hermanas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                    | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi hermano vive en Chile.                        | My brother lives in Chile.                    | singular, subject                  |
| Mi hermana estudia en la universidad.            | My sister is studying at the university.      | singular, subject                  |
| Llamo a mi hermano todos los domingos.           | I call my brother every Sunday.               | object, personal *a*               |
| ¿Conoces a la hermana de Marcos?                 | Do you know Marcos's sister?                  | question, personal *a*             |
| No tengo hermanos.                               | I don't have any siblings.                    | negation, plural = "siblings"      |
| Tiene dos hermanas mayores que ella.             | She has two older sisters.                    | plural, object                     |
