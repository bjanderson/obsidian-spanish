# música

## Meaning

*Música* means "music." It's a feminine noun, and it's usually used in the
singular since music is treated as a mass noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la música    |
| Plural   | las músicas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes            |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Me gusta mucho la música clásica.                | I really like classical music.               | statement             |
| ¿Qué música escuchas cuando estudias?            | What music do you listen to when you study?  | question              |
| No pongas la música tan alta.                    | Don't play the music so loud.                | negation, command     |
| Las músicas de esas dos regiones son muy distintas. | Those two regions' musics are very different. | plural, rare use |
| Bailamos toda la noche al ritmo de la música.    | We danced all night to the music.            | prepositional phrase  |
