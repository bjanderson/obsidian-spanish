# comedor

## Meaning

*Comedor* means "dining room." It's masculine: *el comedor*. It's also used for a
"cafeteria" or "dining hall" in a school or workplace — e.g., *comedor escolar*
("school cafeteria").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el comedor    |
| Plural   | los comedores |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| El comedor está entre la cocina y la sala.                    | The dining room is between the kitchen and living room.  | singular, subject                |
| ¿Cuántos comedores tiene la escuela?                            | How many cafeterias does the school have?                 | plural, question                  |
| Comemos juntos en el comedor cada noche.                        | We eat together in the dining room every night.           | singular, habitual                |
| No hay sillas suficientes en el comedor.                        | There aren't enough chairs in the dining room.             | singular, negation                |
| Pusimos flores sobre la mesa del comedor.                        | We put flowers on the dining room table.                   | singular, "del" contraction       |
