# centro

## Meaning

*Centro* means "center" generally, and specifically "downtown" or "city center" when
talking about a city (*el centro de la ciudad*, often shortened to just *el centro*).
It's masculine: *el centro*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el centro    |
| Plural   | los centros  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                        | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Trabajamos en el centro de la ciudad.                           | We work downtown.                                            | singular, "de" phrase             |
| ¿Cuántos centros comerciales hay cerca de aquí?                  | How many shopping centers are there near here?                | plural, question                  |
| Vamos al centro este sábado para comprar ropa.                   | We're going downtown this Saturday to buy clothes.             | singular, "al" contraction        |
| No hay estacionamiento gratis en el centro.                      | There's no free parking downtown.                              | singular, negation                |
| Caminamos hasta el centro desde nuestro barrio.                  | We walked downtown from our neighborhood.                       | singular, "hasta"/"desde"         |
