# cuaderno

## Meaning

*Cuaderno* means "notebook" — a bound set of blank or lined pages for
writing. It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el cuaderno    |
| Plural   | los cuadernos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                | Notes           |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Escribió la tarea en su cuaderno.                  | She wrote the homework in her notebook.    | statement            |
| ¿Dónde dejaste el cuaderno de matemáticas?         | Where did you leave the math notebook?     | question              |
| No encuentro mi cuaderno por ningún lado.          | I can't find my notebook anywhere.         | negation              |
| Compró varios cuadernos para el semestre.          | He bought several notebooks for the semester. | plural            |
| El cuaderno del alumno estaba lleno de notas.      | The student's notebook was full of notes.  | *del*                |
