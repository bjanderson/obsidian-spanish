# pariente

## Meaning

*Pariente* means "relative" — any family member, often used loosely for
extended family rather than immediate family (for which Spanish usually uses
*familia* or a specific term like *hermano* or *primo*). It's common gender —
the noun itself doesn't change; only the article does: *el pariente* /
*la pariente*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Singular | el/la pariente    |
| Plural   | los/las parientes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                     |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Ese hombre es un pariente lejano.            | That man is a distant relative.           | statement                   |
| Visitamos a un pariente en Bolivia.          | We visited a relative in Bolivia.         | object, personal *a*        |
| ¿Es ella pariente tuya?                      | Is she a relative of yours?               | question, feminine          |
| No tengo parientes en esta ciudad.           | I don't have any relatives in this city.  | negation, plural            |
| El regalo del pariente llegó tarde.          | The relative's gift arrived late.         | *del* contraction           |
