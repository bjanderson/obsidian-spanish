# servicio

## Meaning

*Servicio* means "service" — customer service, a service industry, military
service, a church service, etc. It's masculine: *el servicio*. Note: in the plural,
*los servicios* commonly refers to public utility services (water, electricity, gas)
rather than restrooms — the "restroom" sense of *servicio(s)* is a Spain usage; neutral
Latin American Spanish uses *el baño* instead.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el servicio    |
| Plural   | los servicios  |
