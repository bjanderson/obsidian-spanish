# barrio

## Meaning

*Barrio* means "neighborhood" — a residential area or district within a city. It's
masculine: *el barrio*. It's the everyday, neutral, pan-regional word for this idea.
Near-synonym: *vecindario* (see [[vecindario]]) — *vecindario* can feel slightly more
formal and tends to emphasize the community of neighbors, while *barrio* is the more
common, unmarked choice in everyday speech.
