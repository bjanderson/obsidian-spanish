# efectivo

## Meaning

*Efectivo* means "cash" — physical money, as opposed to a card or check. It's a
masculine noun, most often used in the singular (it names cash as a category
rather than countable units), frequently in the phrase *en efectivo* ("in
cash").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el efectivo   |
| Plural   | (rarely used) |
