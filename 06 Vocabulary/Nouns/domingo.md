# domingo

## Meaning

*Domingo* is Sunday. Days of the week are lowercase in Spanish and
masculine: *el domingo*. Unlike Monday through Friday (e.g. *el lunes* /
*los lunes*, unchanged in the plural), *domingo* pluralizes regularly: *el
domingo* → *los domingos*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el domingo   |
| Plural   | los domingos |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes                            |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| El domingo visitamos a mis padres.           | On Sunday we visit my parents.            | *el* + day, personal *a*             |
| Los domingos vamos a la iglesia.             | On Sundays we go to church.               | *los* + day, habitual, regular plural |
| ¿Trabajas los domingos?                      | Do you work on Sundays?                   | question                              |
| El domingo no hay clases.                    | There's no class on Sunday.               | negative                              |
| Pasamos tres domingos seguidos en la playa.  | We spent three Sundays in a row at the beach. | plural count, regular plural form |
