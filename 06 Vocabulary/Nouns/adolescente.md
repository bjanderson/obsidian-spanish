# adolescente

## Meaning

*Adolescente* means "teenager." It's common gender — the noun itself doesn't
change; only the article does: *el adolescente* / *la adolescente*. It's also
used as an adjective meaning "adolescent" or "teenage."

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                 |
| -------- | -------------------- |
| Singular | el/la adolescente    |
| Plural   | los/las adolescentes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| El adolescente pasa horas en su teléfono.           | The teenager spends hours on his phone.     | statement                   |
| Ayudo a la adolescente con su tarea.                | I help the teenager with her homework.      | object, personal *a*        |
| ¿Recuerdas cuando eras adolescente?                 | Do you remember when you were a teenager?   | question                    |
| No es fácil ser adolescente.                        | It's not easy being a teenager.             | negation                    |
| Muchos adolescentes usan redes sociales.            | Many teenagers use social media.            | plural                      |
