# playa

## Meaning

*Playa* means "beach." It's feminine: *la playa*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la playa    |
| Plural   | las playas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Fuimos a la playa el sábado.                    | We went to the beach on Saturday.            | statement                      |
| ¿Cuál es tu playa favorita?                     | What's your favorite beach?                  | question                       |
| No hay mucha gente en esta playa.               | There aren't many people at this beach.      | negation                       |
| Las playas del norte son más tranquilas.        | The beaches in the north are quieter.        | plural                         |
| Caminamos descalzos por la playa.               | We walked barefoot along the beach.          | statement                      |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[costa]] — the broader coastline a beach is part of.
- [[océano]] — the water bordering a beach.
