# moneda

## Meaning

*Moneda* means "coin" — a physical piece of metal money — and, more broadly,
"currency" (e.g. *moneda nacional*, "national currency"). It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la moneda     |
| Plural   | las monedas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                               | Notes    |
