# guante

## Meaning

*Guante* means "glove." It's masculine: *el guante*. Usually talked about in
the plural (*los guantes*) since gloves come in pairs. It also shows up in
the common phrase *guante de béisbol* ("baseball glove/mitt").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el guante     |
| Plural   | los guantes   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Necesito guantes nuevos para el invierno.          | I need new gloves for winter.                  | plural, statement    |
| ¿Viste mis guantes de cuero?                       | Have you seen my leather gloves?               | question, plural     |
| No encuentro el guante derecho.                    | I can't find the right glove.                  | negation, singular   |
| Se puso los guantes antes de tocar la nieve.       | She put her gloves on before touching the snow.| plural                |
| El guante de béisbol de mi hermano es viejo.       | My brother's baseball glove is old.            | secondary sense      |
