# tienda

## Meaning

*Tienda* means "store" or "shop." It's feminine: *la tienda*. It also means
"tent" (*tienda de campaña*) in an outdoor-camping context.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la tienda    |
| Plural   | las tiendas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                      | English                             | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Fue a la tienda a comprar pan.               | She went to the store to buy bread. | singular, after a preposition    |
| Compré esta camisa en una tienda del centro. | I bought this shirt at a downtown store. | singular, object, *del*     |
| ¿A qué hora cierra la tienda?                | What time does the store close?     | question                        |
| No hay leche en esa tienda.                  | There's no milk at that store.      | negation                        |
| Abrieron dos tiendas nuevas en el barrio.    | They opened two new stores in the neighborhood. | plural, object       |
