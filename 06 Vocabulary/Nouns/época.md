# época

## Meaning

*Época* means "era" or "time period" — a stretch of time defined by its particular
events, conditions, or characteristics, rather than a fixed length like *década* or
*siglo*. It's feminine: *la época*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la época     |
| Plural   | las épocas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Esa fue una época muy difícil para la familia.           | That was a very hard time for the family.             | statement, subject                  |
| En esa época no había teléfonos celulares.               | Back then there were no cell phones.                   | after preposition *en*              |
| ¿En qué época viviste en Bolivia?                        | During what period did you live in Bolivia?            | question                            |
| No extraño esa época de mi vida.                         | I don't miss that period of my life.                    | negation, object                    |
| Esta canción es típica de la época colonial.              | This song is typical of the colonial era.               | possessive *de*-phrase              |
