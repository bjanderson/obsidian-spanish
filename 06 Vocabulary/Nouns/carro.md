# carro

## Meaning

*Carro* means "car." It's masculine: *el carro*.

**Regional note:** Spanish has more than one common word for "car," and none
of them is "the" neutral term — usage is regional. *Carro* is the standard
word across most of Latin America (Mexico, Central America, Venezuela,
Colombia). *Coche* is more common in some South American countries
(Argentina, Chile) and is the standard term in Spain. A third option, *auto*,
is widely understood across the Spanish-speaking world regardless of
country (covered in a separate vocabulary file). When in doubt about a
learner's target region, any of the three will be understood, but *carro*
and *coche* carry the strongest regional association.
