# enfermedad

## Meaning

*Enfermedad* means "illness" or "disease." It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form             |
| -------- | ---------------- |
| Singular | la enfermedad    |
| Plural   | las enfermedades |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Esa enfermedad es muy contagiosa.                | That illness is very contagious.                | statement          |
| ¿Qué enfermedad tiene tu abuelo?                 | What illness does your grandfather have?        | question           |
| No conocemos la causa de la enfermedad.          | We don't know the illness's cause.              | negation, de       |
| Hay varias enfermedades comunes en invierno.     | There are several common illnesses in winter.   | plural             |
| Se recuperó rápido de la enfermedad.             | He recovered quickly from the illness.          | statement          |
