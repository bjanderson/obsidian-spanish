# atención

## Meaning

*Atención* means "attention" — the act of focusing on something. It's a
feminine noun. Common phrases: *prestar atención* ("to pay attention"),
*llamar la atención* ("to attract attention" / "to stand out"). The plural
*atenciones* has a secondary sense of "acts of kindness or care" (e.g. shown
to a guest or a patient).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | la atención     |
| Plural   | las atenciones  |
