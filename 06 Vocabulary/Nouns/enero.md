# enero

## Meaning

*Enero* is January, the first month of the year. Like all months in
Spanish, it's lowercase (never capitalized like in English) and masculine,
though it's rarely used with an article — say *en enero*, not *en el
enero*. An article does appear before a specific date: *el primero de
enero*. In Bolivia and the rest of the Southern Hemisphere, enero falls in
the middle of summer.

| Spanish                                        | English                                       | Notes                            |
