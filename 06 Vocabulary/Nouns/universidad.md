# universidad

## Meaning

*Universidad* means "university," an institution of higher education. It's
a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Singular | la universidad    |
| Plural   | las universidades |

## Examples

| Spanish                                         | English                                   | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Mi hermana estudia en la universidad.                | My sister studies at the university.           | statement          |
| ¿A qué universidad vas?                              | Which university do you go to?                 | question           |
| Todavía no terminé la universidad.                   | I still haven't finished university.           | negation           |
| Hay tres universidades importantes en la ciudad.     | There are three important universities in the city. | plural        |
| Conocí a mi esposo en la universidad.                | I met my husband at the university.            | personal *a*       |
