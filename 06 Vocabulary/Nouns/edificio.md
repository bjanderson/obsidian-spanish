# edificio

## Meaning

*Edificio* means "building." It's masculine: *el edificio*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el edificio   |
| Plural   | los edificios |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                  | Notes                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Vive en un edificio de diez pisos.               | She lives in a ten-story building.        | singular, after a preposition   |
| Construyeron el edificio en menos de un año.     | They built the building in less than a year. | singular, object              |
| ¿Cuántos pisos tiene ese edificio?               | How many floors does that building have? | question                        |
| No hay ascensor en el edificio antiguo.          | There's no elevator in the old building. | negation                        |
| Los edificios del centro histórico son muy viejos. | The buildings in the historic center are very old. | plural, subject, *del*  |
