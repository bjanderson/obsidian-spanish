# baile

## Meaning

*Baile* means "dance," either the art form or a specific social event.
It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el baile   |
| Plural   | los bailes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                             | Notes         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| El baile empieza a las diez de la noche.          | The dance starts at ten at night.           | statement          |
| ¿Vas al baile del pueblo este sábado?             | Are you going to the town dance this Saturday? | question, al   |
| No sé bailar ese tipo de baile.                   | I don't know how to do that type of dance.  | negation           |
| Los bailes tradicionales varían de región a región. | Traditional dances vary from region to region. | plural         |
| Aprendió el baile con mucha paciencia.            | She learned the dance with a lot of patience. | statement          |
