# luz

## Meaning

*Luz* means "light," either illumination or electric power. It's feminine,
and its plural is irregular: *luces*, not *luzes*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | la luz     |
| Plural   | las luces  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                    | Notes             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| La luz de la cocina no funciona.                 | The kitchen light doesn't work.               | statement              |
| ¿Puedes apagar la luz?                           | Can you turn off the light?                   | question               |
| No hay luz en el edificio.                       | There's no power in the building.             | negation, secondary sense |
| Las luces de la calle se encienden al anochecer. | The streetlights turn on at nightfall.        | plural, *al*           |
| Vimos las luces del pueblo desde la montaña.     | We saw the town's lights from the mountain.   | *del*, plural          |
