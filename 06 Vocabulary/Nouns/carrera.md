# carrera

## Meaning

*Carrera* means "career" — a person's professional path over time. It also
means a university degree or major (*la carrera de medicina*) and, in a
different context, "a race" (running or racing); context makes the sense
clear.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | la carrera      |
| Plural   | las carreras    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                             | English                                     | Notes            |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Tiene una carrera exitosa en finanzas.                     | She has a successful career in finance.           | statement              |
| ¿Qué carrera estudiaste en la universidad?                 | What did you major in in college?                  | question                |
| No abandonó su carrera a pesar de las dificultades.        | She didn't give up her career despite the difficulties. | negation          |
