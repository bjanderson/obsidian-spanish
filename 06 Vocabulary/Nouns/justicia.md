# justicia

## Meaning

*Justicia* means "justice," fair and moral treatment under the law. It's
a feminine noun, mostly used in the singular as an abstract concept.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la justicia   |
| Plural   | las justicias |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| La familia pidió justicia para la víctima.          | The family asked for justice for the victim. | statement          |
| ¿Crees en la justicia de este país?                 | Do you believe in this country's justice system? | question       |
| No hay justicia para los pobres aquí.               | There's no justice for the poor here.        | negation           |
| Buscan justicia ante los tribunales.                | They're seeking justice before the courts.   | statement          |
| Trabaja en el sistema de justicia.                  | She works in the justice system.             | statement          |
