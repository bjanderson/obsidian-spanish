# embajada

## Meaning

*Embajada* means "embassy." It's feminine: *la embajada*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la embajada   |
| Plural   | las embajadas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                                   | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| Tramité la visa en la embajada de Estados Unidos.  | I processed the visa at the U.S. embassy.        | singular, after a preposition      |
| Llamó a la embajada para pedir información.        | She called the embassy to ask for information.   | singular, object                   |
| ¿Dónde queda la embajada de México?                | Where is the Mexican embassy located?            | question                            |
| No atienden sin cita en esa embajada.              | They don't serve you without an appointment at that embassy. | negation              |
| Las embajadas de varios países están en esa avenida. | Several countries' embassies are on that avenue. | plural, subject, *de*               |
