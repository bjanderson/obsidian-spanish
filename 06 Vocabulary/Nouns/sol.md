# sol

## Meaning

*Sol* means "sun," the star at the center of our solar system, and by
extension "sunshine." It's a masculine noun, mostly used in the singular.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form      |
| -------- | --------- |
| Singular | el sol    |
| Plural   | los soles |

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                             | Notes         |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| El sol sale muy temprano en verano.             | The sun rises very early in summer.          | statement          |
| ¿Hace mucho sol hoy?                            | Is it very sunny today?                      | question           |
| No hay sol, está muy nublado.                   | There's no sun, it's very cloudy.            | negation           |
| Los soles de esa temporada fueron muy fuertes.  | That season's sun days were very intense.    | plural, rare       |
| Nos sentamos al sol toda la tarde.              | We sat in the sun all afternoon.             | statement          |
