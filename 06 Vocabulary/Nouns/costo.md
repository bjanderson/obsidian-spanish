# costo

## Meaning

*Costo* means "cost," the amount of money needed for something. It's a
masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el costo   |
| Plural   | los costos |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| El costo de vida subió este año.                | The cost of living rose this year.             | statement          |
| ¿Cuál es el costo total del viaje?              | What's the trip's total cost?                  | question, del      |
| No cubrimos el costo completo del arreglo.      | We didn't cover the repair's full cost.        | negation           |
| Los costos de producción bajaron.               | Production costs went down.                    | plural             |
| Aceptó el trabajo a costo de su tiempo libre.   | She took the job at the cost of her free time. | idiomatic phrase   |
