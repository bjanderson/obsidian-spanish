# barco

## Meaning

*Barco* means "boat" or "ship," covering anything from a small fishing boat
to a large ocean vessel. It's masculine: *el barco*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el barco    |
| Plural   | los barcos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                                | Notes    |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| El barco zarpa a las seis de la mañana.                | The boat sets sail at six in the morning. | statement  |
| ¿Ese barco cruza hasta la isla?                        | Does that boat cross to the island?       | question   |
| No subimos al barco por el mal clima.                  | We didn't board the boat due to bad weather. | negation |
| Los barcos pesqueros salen antes del amanecer.         | The fishing boats leave before dawn.      | plural     |
| Cruzamos el río en barco.                              | We crossed the river by boat.             | statement  |
