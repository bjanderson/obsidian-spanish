# policía

## Meaning

*Policía* has two related senses. *La policía* (always feminine) means
"the police," the force as an institution. *El policía* / *la policía*
(common gender) means "police officer," a specific person.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                          |
| -------- | ----------------------------- |
| Singular | la policía (force) / el/la policía (officer) |
| Plural   | los/las policías (officers)   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes             |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Llamamos a la policía por el ruido.              | We called the police about the noise.           | statement, institution |
| ¿Ya llegó el policía que llamaste?               | Has the police officer you called arrived yet?  | question, officer      |
| No confío en esa policía corrupta.               | I don't trust that corrupt police force.        | negation               |
| Los policías detuvieron el tráfico.              | The police officers stopped traffic.            | plural, officers        |
| La policía investiga el robo del banco.          | The police are investigating the bank robbery.  | statement, del          |
