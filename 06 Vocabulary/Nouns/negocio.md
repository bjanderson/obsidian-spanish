# negocio

## Meaning

*Negocio* means "business" — either a company/establishment (*un negocio de
comida*, a food business) or a deal/transaction (*es un buen negocio*, "it's a
good deal"). It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el negocio     |
| Plural   | los negocios   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                              | Notes    |
| ---------------------------------------------------| ---------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Abrió un negocio de comida.                         | They opened a food business.             | statement|
| ¿Cómo va el negocio este mes?                       | How's the business doing this month?     | question |
| No es un buen negocio para nosotros.                | It's not a good deal for us.             | negation |
| Los negocios pequeños luchan por sobrevivir.        | Small businesses struggle to survive.    | plural   |
| Cerraron el negocio después de la crisis.           | They closed the business after the crisis.| statement|
