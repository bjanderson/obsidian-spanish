# fuente

## Meaning

*Fuente* means "source," as in where information comes from, and also
"fountain," the water structure. It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la fuente   |
| Plural   | las fuentes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| No revelaron la fuente de la noticia.            | They didn't reveal the news's source.         | negation           |
| ¿Cuál es la fuente de esta información?          | What's this information's source?             | question, de       |
| Nos sentamos cerca de la fuente del parque.      | We sat near the park's fountain.              | statement, del     |
| Consultamos varias fuentes antes de escribir.    | We consulted several sources before writing.  | plural             |
| El agua de esa fuente es potable.                | That fountain's water is drinkable.            | statement          |
