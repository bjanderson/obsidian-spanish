# brazo

## Meaning

*Brazo* means "arm." It's masculine: *el brazo*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el brazo    |
| Plural   | los brazos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Me duele el brazo derecho.                 | My right arm hurts.                     | statement                     |
| Cargó al niño en sus brazos.               | She carried the child in her arms.      | object, personal *a*, plural  |
| ¿Te fracturaste el brazo?                  | Did you fracture your arm?              | question                      |
| No puedo levantar el brazo.                | I can't lift my arm.                    | negation                      |
| Cruzó los brazos y esperó.                 | He crossed his arms and waited.         | plural                        |
