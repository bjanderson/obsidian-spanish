# información

## Meaning

*Información* means "information." It's feminine: *la información*. Like its English
counterpart, it's a mass/uncountable noun — it's normally used only in the singular
(*mucha información*, "a lot of information"), never with a plural counted the way
*condiciones* or *meses* would be. A plural form, *informaciones*, does exist in some
formal or journalistic writing (roughly "reports" or "news items"), but it's uncommon
in everyday neutral speech — stick to the singular unless you're specifically
referencing separate formal reports.

## Singular / Plural

| Number                | Form                |
| ---------------------- | -------------------- |
| Singular                | la información        |
| Plural (rare, formal)   | las informaciones     |

| Spanish                                                | English                                          | Notes                        |
