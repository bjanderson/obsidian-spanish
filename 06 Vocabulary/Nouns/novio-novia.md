# novio / novia

## Meaning

*Novio* means "boyfriend" (*el novio*) and *novia* means "girlfriend" (*la novia*).
In a wedding context, *novio* means "groom" and *novia* means "bride." The masculine
plural *novios* can mean "the couple" (boyfriend and girlfriend, or bride and groom)
collectively.

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form       |
| ------------------- | ---------- |
| Masculine singular   | el novio   |
| Feminine singular    | la novia   |
| Masculine plural     | los novios |
| Feminine plural      | las novias |
