# hija

## Meaning

*Hija* means "daughter." It's feminine: *la hija*. It's the feminine counterpart of
*hijo*; the masculine plural *hijos* covers "children" of mixed gender, so *hijas*
is used specifically when all the children are daughters.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la hija     |
| Plural   | las hijas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                             | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Mi hija estudia medicina.                  | My daughter is studying medicine.          | singular, subject                  |
| Llevo a mi hija a la escuela.              | I take my daughter to school.              | object, personal *a*               |
| ¿Conoces a la hija de Marta?               | Do you know Marta's daughter?              | question, personal *a*             |
| No veo a mi hija desde hace un año.        | I haven't seen my daughter in a year.      | negation, personal *a*             |
| Está muy orgullosa de sus hijas.           | She's very proud of her daughters.         | after a preposition, plural        |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[hijo]] — the male counterpart; together, *los hijos* means "children."
- [[madre]] — a parent of the *hija*.
