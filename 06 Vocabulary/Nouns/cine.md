# cine

## Meaning

*Cine* means "movie theater" — a venue for watching films. It's masculine:
*el cine*. It can also refer to "cinema" as an art form in general, similar
to English.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el cine     |
| Plural   | los cines   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                        | Notes                        |

| Vimos la nueva película en el cine del centro.             | We saw the new movie at the downtown theater.           | singular, after a preposition    |
