# bebé

## Meaning

*Bebé* means "baby." It's traditionally masculine and invariable — *el bebé*
is used even for a baby girl — though *la bebé* is also commonly heard in
casual Latin American speech when referring specifically to a girl.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                          |
| -------- | ----------------------------- |
| Singular | el bebé (la bebé, colloquial) |
| Plural   | los bebés                     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                        |
