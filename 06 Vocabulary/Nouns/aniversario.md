# aniversario

## Meaning

*Aniversario* means "anniversary" — the yearly recurrence of a date, most
often a wedding anniversary, but also used for company or event anniversaries.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                 |
| -------- | -------------------- |
| Singular | el aniversario       |
| Plural   | los aniversarios     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes            |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Celebramos nuestro aniversario de bodas el sábado.       | We're celebrating our wedding anniversary on Saturday. | statement       |
| ¿Cuándo es tu aniversario de trabajo?                    | When is your work anniversary?                | question                 |
| No se olvidó del aniversario este año.                   | He didn't forget the anniversary this year.   | negation, del             |
| Los aniversarios importantes merecen una gran celebración. | Important anniversaries deserve a big celebration. | plural              |
| Le regalé algo especial por nuestro aniversario.          | I gave him something special for our anniversary. | indirect object       |
