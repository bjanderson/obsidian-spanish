# médico

## Meaning

*Médico* means "doctor" or "physician," used interchangeably with
*doctor* in most contexts. The feminine form is *médica*. As an
adjective, *médico/médica* also means "medical."

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el médico    |
| Plural   | los médicos  |
