# hueso

## Meaning

*Hueso* means "bone." It's masculine: *el hueso*. It also refers to a fruit
pit/stone (*el hueso del durazno*, "the peach pit").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el hueso     |
| Plural   | los huesos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Se rompió el hueso al caer.                    | He broke his bone when he fell.        | singular, object                |
| Los huesos del brazo tardan semanas en sanar.  | The arm bones take weeks to heal.      | plural, subject, *del*           |
| ¿Cuántos huesos tiene el cuerpo humano?        | How many bones does the human body have? | question, plural               |
| No muevas el hueso hasta que llegue el médico. | Don't move the bone until the doctor arrives. | negative command             |
| El perro enterró el hueso en el jardín.        | The dog buried the bone in the yard.   | singular, object                |
