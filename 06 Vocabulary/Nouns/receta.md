# receta

## Meaning

*Receta* means "recipe." It's feminine: *la receta*. It can also mean a
medical "prescription."

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la receta    |
| Plural   | las recetas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Esta receta es de mi abuela.                     | This recipe is from my grandmother.           | statement                             |
| ¿Me pasas la receta del pastel?                  | Can you send me the cake recipe?              | question                              |
| No seguí la receta al pie de la letra.           | I didn't follow the recipe exactly.           | negation                              |
| Las recetas de este libro son fáciles.           | The recipes in this book are easy.            | plural                                 |
| El médico le dio una receta para el dolor.       | The doctor gave him a prescription for the pain. | statement, secondary sense           |
