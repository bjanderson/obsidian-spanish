# familia

## Meaning

*Familia* means "family" — the group of people related by blood or marriage. It's
feminine: *la familia*. There's nothing irregular about it — it follows the regular
*-a* → feminine pattern and pluralizes normally.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la familia    |
| Plural   | las familias  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                    | Notes                          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Mi familia es muy unida.                       | My family is very close-knit.               | singular, subject                |
| Conocí a la familia de mi novio ayer.          | I met my boyfriend's family yesterday.      | singular, object, personal a     |
| ¿Cuántas familias viven en este edificio?      | How many families live in this building?    | plural, question                 |
| No hablo con esa parte de mi familia.          | I don't talk to that part of my family.     | singular, negation                |
| El regalo es para toda la familia.             | The gift is for the whole family.           | singular, after a preposition     |
