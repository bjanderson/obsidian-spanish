# padre

## Meaning

*Padre* means "father." It's masculine: *el padre*. In the plural, *los padres*
usually means "parents" (mother and father together), not just "fathers" — Spanish
uses the masculine plural to cover a mixed-gender pair, the same pattern you'll see
with *hijos* ("children") and *hermanos* ("siblings").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el padre     |
| Plural   | los padres   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                        |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Mi padre trabaja en un hospital.          | My father works at a hospital.             | singular, subject                  |
| Veo a mi padre todos los domingos.        | I see my father every Sunday.              | singular, object, personal *a*     |
| ¿Conoces al padre de Elena?               | Do you know Elena's father?                | question, personal *a* + *al*      |
| Mis padres viven en Bolivia.              | My parents live in Bolivia.                | plural = "parents," subject        |
| Hablé con el padre de la novia en la boda. | I spoke with the bride's father at the wedding. | after a preposition          |
