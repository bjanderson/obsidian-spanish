# reunión

## Meaning

*Reunión* means "meeting" — most often a work meeting, but also any
gathering of people, such as a family reunion.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Singular | la reunión        |
| Plural   | las reuniones     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| La reunión empieza a las nueve.                    | The meeting starts at nine.               | statement           |
| ¿Asististe a la reunión del lunes?                 | Did you attend Monday's meeting?          | question, del       |
| No pudo llegar a la reunión a tiempo.               | He couldn't get to the meeting on time.   | negation             |
| Las reuniones semanales duran una hora.             | The weekly meetings last an hour.         | plural               |
| El jefe convocó una reunión urgente.                | The boss called an urgent meeting.        | statement             |
