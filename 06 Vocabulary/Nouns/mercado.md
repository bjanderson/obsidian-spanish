# mercado

## Meaning

*Mercado* means "market" — a place to buy and sell goods, or the broader
economic system of trade. It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el mercado    |
| Plural   | los mercados  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                                        | Notes            |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| El mercado abre a las seis de la mañana.               | The market opens at six in the morning.            | statement             |
| ¿Fuiste al mercado hoy?                                | Did you go to the market today?                    | question, *al*        |
| No encontré tomates frescos en el mercado.             | I didn't find fresh tomatoes at the market.        | negation              |
| Los mercados de la ciudad venden fruta muy barata.     | The city's markets sell very cheap fruit.          | plural                |
| El precio del café subió en el mercado internacional.  | Coffee's price rose in the international market.   | *del*                 |
