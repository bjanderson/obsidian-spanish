# hospital

## Meaning

*Hospital* means "hospital." It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el hospital    |
| Plural   | los hospitales |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Llevaron al niño de emergencia al hospital.       | They rushed the boy to the hospital.         | statement, personal a, al |
| ¿En qué hospital naciste?                         | What hospital were you born in?              | question           |
| No hay camas libres en el hospital.               | There are no free beds at the hospital.      | negation           |
| Los hospitales públicos están saturados.          | Public hospitals are overwhelmed.            | plural             |
| Pasó una semana en el hospital.                    | She spent a week in the hospital.            | statement          |
