# corazón

## Meaning

*Corazón* means "heart" — both the physical organ and the figurative seat of
emotion, as in English. It's masculine: *el corazón*. Common in fixed
expressions like *de todo corazón* ("wholeheartedly") and *no tener corazón*
("to be heartless").
