# mediodía

## Meaning

*Mediodía* means "noon" or "midday" (12:00 p.m.). It's a masculine noun (built from
*medio* + *día*, and *día* itself is masculine despite ending in -a). It's almost
always used in the singular — there's rarely a reason to pluralize a specific time
of day.

## Singular / Plural

| Number             | Form              |
| ------------------- | ------------------ |
| Singular (standard) | el mediodía        |
| Plural (rare)        | los mediodías      |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                              | Notes               |

| Nos vemos al mediodía.                                | See you at noon.                            | *al* contraction         |
| El sol está más fuerte al mediodía.                   | The sun is strongest at noon.               | *al* contraction         |
| Llegó justo a mediodía.                               | He arrived right at noon.                   | subject omitted, past      |
| ¿Almorzamos al mediodía o más tarde?                  | Should we have lunch at noon or later?      | question                  |
