# costa

## Meaning

*Costa* means "coast" or "coastline." It's feminine: *la costa*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la costa    |
| Plural   | las costas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| La costa peruana tiene playas muy largas.            | The Peruvian coast has very long beaches.     | statement                      |
| ¿Vives cerca de la costa?                            | Do you live near the coast?                   | question                       |
| No conocemos bien la costa del país.                 | We don't know the country's coast well.       | negation                       |
| Las costas del Pacífico son frías en esta zona.      | The Pacific coasts are cold in this area.     | plural                         |
| Manejamos toda la mañana a lo largo de la costa.     | We drove all morning along the coast.         | statement                      |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[playa]] — a specific stretch of coast for swimming.
- [[océano]] — the body of water a coast borders.
