# marzo

## Meaning

*Marzo* is March, the third month of the year. Like all months in Spanish,
it's lowercase and masculine, and it's rarely used with an article — say
*en marzo*, not *en el marzo*. An article does appear before a specific
date: *el veintiuno de marzo*. In Bolivia, the school year typically starts
in marzo, and the month marks the shift from summer into autumn in the
Southern Hemisphere.
