# niña

## Meaning

*Niña* means "girl." It's feminine: *la niña*. It's the feminine counterpart of
*niño*; use it specifically for a female child, while *niños* (masculine plural)
covers a mixed group of children.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la niña      |
| Plural   | las niñas    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| La niña juega en el parque.                  | The girl is playing in the park.             | singular, subject                  |
| Vi a una niña llorando en la calle.          | I saw a girl crying in the street.           | object, personal *a*               |
| ¿Conoces a la niña que vive al lado?         | Do you know the girl who lives next door?    | question, personal *a*             |
| Las niñas no pueden entrar sin un adulto.    | Girls can't enter without an adult.          | plural, negation                    |
| La maestra habló con las niñas del salón.    | The teacher spoke with the classroom girls.  | after a preposition                 |
