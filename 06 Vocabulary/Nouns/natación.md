# natación

## Meaning

*Natación* means "swimming," the sport/activity. It's a feminine noun,
used almost always in the singular.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | la natación     |
| Plural   | las nataciones  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| La natación es muy buena para la salud.             | Swimming is very good for your health.          | statement          |
| ¿Practicas natación en el club?                     | Do you practice swimming at the club?           | question           |
| No sabía nadar antes de tomar clases de natación.   | I didn't know how to swim before taking swim lessons. | negation      |
| Ganó dos medallas en natación.                      | She won two medals in swimming.                 | statement          |
