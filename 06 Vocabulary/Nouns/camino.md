# camino

## Meaning

*Camino* means "path" or "road" — a route or way, literal or figurative (a
"path" in life or career). It's a masculine noun. Common phrases: *en el
camino* ("on the way"), *abrir camino* ("to blaze a trail").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el camino     |
| Plural   | los caminos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                              | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| El camino al pueblo está lleno de curvas.          | The road to town is full of curves.      | statement, *al*       |
| ¿Conoces un camino más corto?                      | Do you know a shorter path?              | question               |
| No hay camino directo hasta la cima.               | There's no direct route to the summit.   | negation               |
| Recorrieron varios caminos antes de llegar.        | They traveled several roads before arriving. | plural             |
| Ese fue un momento clave en su camino profesional. | That was a key moment in his professional path. | figurative sense |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[línea]] — both describe a path from one point to another, though *línea* is straight and *camino* can wind.
- [[cuerda]] — both physically extend from one point to another.
