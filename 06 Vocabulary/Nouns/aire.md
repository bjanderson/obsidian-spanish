# aire

## Meaning

*Aire* means "air." It's a masculine noun, normally used in the singular
as a mass noun, though the plural *aires* exists in some idiomatic senses
(e.g. "airs," as in pretension).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | el aire    |
| Plural   | los aires  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| El aire de la montaña es muy fresco.             | The mountain air is very fresh.              | statement, *de*        |
| ¿Sientes el aire frío?                           | Do you feel the cold air?                    | question               |
| No hay aire acondicionado en esta oficina.       | There's no air conditioning in this office.  | negation, secondary sense |
| Se dio aires de importante toda la fiesta.       | He put on airs of importance all party long. | idiomatic plural       |
| Salimos a respirar aire puro.                    | We went out to breathe fresh air.            | statement              |
