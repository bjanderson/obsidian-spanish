# tío / tía

## Meaning

*Tío* means "uncle" (*el tío*) and *tía* means "aunt" (*la tía*). Like other
family-pair nouns in Spanish, the masculine plural *tíos* usually means "aunts and
uncles" collectively, not just "uncles."

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form      |
| ------------------- | --------- |
| Masculine singular   | el tío    |
| Feminine singular    | la tía    |
| Masculine plural     | los tíos  |
| Feminine plural      | las tías  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                        | Notes                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------|
| Mi tío trabaja como ingeniero.                    | My uncle works as an engineer.                       | singular, subject                       |
| Mi tía vive cerca del centro.                     | My aunt lives near downtown.                         | singular, subject, *del*                |
| Saludé a mi tío en la fiesta.                     | I greeted my uncle at the party.                     | object, personal *a*                    |
| ¿Conoces a la tía de Andrea?                      | Do you know Andrea's aunt?                           | question, personal *a*                  |
| No veo a mi tío desde la boda.                    | I haven't seen my uncle since the wedding.           | negation, personal *a*                  |
| Mis tíos vienen a cenar esta noche.               | My aunt and uncle are coming for dinner tonight.     | plural = "aunt(s)/uncle(s)," subject    |
