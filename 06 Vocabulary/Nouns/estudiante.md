# estudiante

## Meaning

*Estudiante* means "student" — the general, most common word for anyone who
studies, at any level (school, university, or on their own). It's a common-
gender noun: the word itself doesn't change, only the article does — *el
estudiante* / *la estudiante*. Compare [[alumno]], the narrower term for a
pupil specifically enrolled in a teacher's class — *estudiante* is the
broader, everyday word.
