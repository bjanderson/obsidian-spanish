# palabra

## Meaning

*Palabra* means "word" — a unit of language, spoken or written. It's feminine:
*la palabra*. It also appears in set phrases like *dar la palabra* ("to give
one's word," i.e. promise) and *tener la palabra* ("to have the floor," in a
discussion).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la palabra    |
| Plural   | las palabras  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                      |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Esa palabra es difícil de pronunciar.      | That word is hard to pronounce.          | singular, subject                |
| No entiendo estas palabras.                | I don't understand these words.          | negation, plural, object          |
| ¿Cómo se escribe esa palabra?              | How do you write that word?              | question                          |
| Habló sin decir una palabra.               | He spoke without saying a word.          | after a preposition, singular      |
| Le di mi palabra de honor.                 | I gave her my word of honor.             | object, indirect object *le*       |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[hablar]] — "to speak"; *palabra* is the unit that speech is made of.
- [[decir]] — "to say"; closely tied to *palabra* in phrases like *no dijo ni una palabra* ("he didn't say a single word").
