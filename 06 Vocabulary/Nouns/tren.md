# tren

## Meaning

*Tren* means "train." It's masculine: *el tren*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el tren     |
| Plural   | los trenes  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                             | English                                  | Notes    |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| El tren sale de la estación a las ocho.                | The train leaves the station at eight.      | statement  |
| ¿Este tren para en la próxima ciudad?                  | Does this train stop in the next city?      | question   |
| No perdimos el tren, por suerte.                       | We didn't miss the train, luckily.          | negation   |
| Los trenes en esa región son muy puntuales.            | The trains in that region are very punctual. | plural     |
| Viajamos en tren hasta la frontera.                    | We traveled by train to the border.         | statement  |

## Related Vocabulary
