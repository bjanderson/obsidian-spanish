# lugar

## Meaning

*Lugar* means "place" — a location or spot. It's masculine: *el lugar*. It also
appears in set expressions: *en lugar de* ("instead of") and *tener lugar* ("to
take place").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Singular     | el lugar     |
| Plural         | los lugares  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                          | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Este es un lugar muy tranquilo.                                  | This is a very quiet place.                                | singular, subject                   |
| ¿Conoces buenos lugares para comer por aquí?                     | Do you know good places to eat around here?                | plural, question                     |
| Visitamos varios lugares interesantes en el viaje.               | We visited several interesting places on the trip.         | plural, object                       |
| No hay ningún lugar libre en el estacionamiento.                 | There's no free spot in the parking lot.                    | singular, negation                    |
| La reunión tendrá lugar en la oficina central.                   | The meeting will take place at the central office.         | singular, fixed expression "tener lugar" |
