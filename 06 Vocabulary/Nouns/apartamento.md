# apartamento

## Meaning

*Apartamento* means "apartment." It's masculine: *el apartamento*. In Spain
the more common word is *piso*, but *apartamento* is standard across Latin
America.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Singular | el apartamento    |
| Plural   | los apartamentos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                         | English                                    | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Alquilamos un apartamento cerca de la universidad. | We rented an apartment near the university. | singular, object                  |
| Pintó las paredes del apartamento el mes pasado. | He painted the apartment's walls last month. | singular, *del*                   |
| ¿Cuánto pagas de renta por el apartamento?      | How much rent do you pay for the apartment? | question                          |
| No cabe todo ese mueble en el apartamento.      | All that furniture doesn't fit in the apartment. | negation                    |
| Los apartamentos de ese edificio son pequeños.  | The apartments in that building are small.  | plural, subject, *de*             |
