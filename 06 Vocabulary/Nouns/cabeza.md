# cabeza

## Meaning

*Cabeza* means "head" — the body part, and also figuratively "the head/leader"
of a group or the "top" of something (*la cabeza de la familia*, "the head of
the family"). It's feminine: *la cabeza*, following the regular "-*a* =
feminine" pattern.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la cabeza    |
| Plural   | las cabezas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                   | Notes                     |

| Me duele la cabeza.                              | My head hurts.                              | singular, subject             |
