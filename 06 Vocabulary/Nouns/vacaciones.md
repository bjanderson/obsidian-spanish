# vacaciones

## Meaning

*Vacaciones* means "vacation" or "time off." Spanish almost always uses this
word in the plural, even for a single vacation period — you say *estar de
vacaciones* ("to be on vacation"), not *estar de vacación*. The singular
*vacación* exists but is rare in everyday speech.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                | Note                                              |
| -------- | ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Singular | la vacación (rare)  | Rarely used; Spanish normally uses the plural.      |
| Plural   | las vacaciones      | The standard form: *estar de vacaciones*, *tomar vacaciones*. |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                             | Notes            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Estamos de vacaciones esta semana.                 | We're on vacation this week.                | idiomatic, plural       |
| ¿Cuándo empiezan tus vacaciones?                   | When does your vacation start?              | question                 |
| No tomó vacaciones el año pasado.                  | She didn't take vacation last year.         | negation                 |
| Las vacaciones de verano duran dos meses.           | Summer vacation lasts two months.           | plural                    |
| Planeamos las vacaciones con mucha anticipación.    | We plan our vacation well in advance.       | statement                 |
