# regalo

## Meaning

*Regalo* means "gift" or "present."

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el regalo       |
| Plural   | los regalos     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                          | Notes            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Le compré un regalo a mi mamá.                     | I bought a gift for my mom.              | indirect object          |
| ¿Qué regalo le vas a dar?                          | What gift are you going to give her?     | question                  |
| No sabe qué regalo comprarle.                       | She doesn't know what gift to buy him.  | negation                   |
| Los regalos están debajo del árbol.                | The gifts are under the tree.            | plural, del                |
| Recibió una sorpresa junto con el regalo.           | She got a surprise along with the gift.  | statement                  |
