# aula

## Meaning

*Aula* means "classroom." It's grammatically feminine — but like [[agua]],
it takes the masculine-looking article *el* in the singular (*el aula*, not
*la aula*), and the indefinite article *un* instead of *una* (*un aula*, not
*una aula*). This is the same vowel-clash exception: a feminine noun that
starts with a stressed *a-* sound takes *el*/*un* in the singular purely to
avoid two stressed "a" sounds colliding back to back. The noun is still
feminine — any adjective modifying it agrees in the feminine, e.g. *el aula
pequeña* ("the small classroom"), not *el aula pequeño*. The plural reverts
to the normal feminine articles: *las aulas*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
