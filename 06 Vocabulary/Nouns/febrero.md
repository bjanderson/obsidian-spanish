# febrero

## Meaning

*Febrero* is February, the second month of the year. Like all months in
Spanish, it's lowercase and masculine, and it's rarely used with an
article — say *en febrero*, not *en el febrero*. An article does appear
before a specific date: *el catorce de febrero*. Febrero is also when
Bolivia's Carnaval de Oruro, a major UNESCO-recognized festival, usually
takes place.
