# día

## Meaning

*Día* is masculine (*el día*) even though it ends in *-a* — a well-known exception
to the usual *-o* = masculine / *-a* = feminine pattern. It shows up constantly in
greetings and fixed expressions: *buenos días* ("good morning," literally "good
days"), *todos los días* ("every day"), *hoy en día* ("nowadays").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Singular       | el día       |
| Plural           | los días     |
