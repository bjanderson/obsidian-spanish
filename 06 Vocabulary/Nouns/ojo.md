# ojo

## Meaning

*Ojo* means "eye." It's masculine: *el ojo*. As a standalone exclamation, *¡Ojo!*
means "Watch out!" or "Be careful!" — a very common everyday warning.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Singular     | el ojo       |
| Plural         | los ojos     |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Tiene los ojos verdes.                              | He/She has green eyes.                          | plural, subject/possession      |
| ¿De qué color son tus ojos?                         | What color are your eyes?                       | plural, question                 |
| Cerró el ojo derecho para guiñar.                    | He closed his right eye to wink.                | singular, object                 |
| No abrió los ojos hasta las diez.                    | He/She didn't open his/her eyes until ten.      | plural, negation                  |
| Tenía algo en el ojo izquierdo.                      | He/She had something in his/her left eye.       | singular, after preposition "en" |
