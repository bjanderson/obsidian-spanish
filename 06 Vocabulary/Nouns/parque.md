# parque

## Meaning

*Parque* means "park," a public green space. It's masculine: *el parque*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el parque    |
| Plural   | los parques  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                              | Notes                   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Los niños juegan en el parque todas las tardes. | The children play at the park every afternoon. | singular, after a preposition |
| Caminamos por el parque después de cenar.     | We walked through the park after dinner. | singular, after a preposition   |
| ¿Vamos al parque este fin de semana?          | Shall we go to the park this weekend?    | question, *al*                   |
| No dejan entrar perros sin correa al parque.  | They don't let dogs into the park without a leash. | negation, *al*             |
| Hay varios parques cerca de mi casa.          | There are several parks near my house.   | plural, subject                   |
