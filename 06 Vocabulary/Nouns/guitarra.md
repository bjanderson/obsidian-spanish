# guitarra

## Meaning

*Guitarra* means "guitar." It's a feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la guitarra   |
| Plural   | las guitarras |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                              | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Toca la guitarra todas las tardes.                 | He plays guitar every afternoon.         | statement              |
| ¿Sabes tocar la guitarra?                          | Do you know how to play guitar?          | question                |
| No tengo una guitarra propia todavía.              | I don't have my own guitar yet.          | negation                |
| Compró dos guitarras nuevas para la banda.         | She bought two new guitars for the band. | plural                  |
| Reemplazó una cuerda rota de la guitarra.          | He replaced a broken guitar string.      | *de la*, musical sense   |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[cuerda]] — a *guitarra*'s strings are called *cuerdas*.
- [[música]] — a *guitarra* is a common instrument for making *música*.
