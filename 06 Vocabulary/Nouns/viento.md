# viento

## Meaning

*Viento* means "wind." It's masculine: *el viento*. Common in weather
expressions: *hace viento* ("it's windy").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el viento    |
| Plural   | los vientos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                              | Notes                          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Hace mucho viento hoy.                          | It's very windy today.                 | weather expression, statement      |
| ¿Sientes el viento en la cara?                  | Do you feel the wind on your face?     | question                           |
| El viento no paró en toda la noche.             | The wind didn't stop all night.        | negation                           |
| Los vientos del norte traen frío.               | The winds from the north bring cold.   | plural                             |
| Cerré la ventana porque entraba viento.         | I closed the window because wind was coming in. | subordinate clause      |
