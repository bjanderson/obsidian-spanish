# noviembre

## Meaning

*Noviembre* means "November," the eleventh month of the year. It's masculine:
*el noviembre*. Like other month names, it's lowercase and normally used without an
article — *en noviembre*, not *en el noviembre* — except when pointing to one
specific occurrence, like *el noviembre pasado* ("last November").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                                       |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Singular | noviembre (no article in general use)        |
| Plural   | los noviembres (rare — recurring instances)  |
