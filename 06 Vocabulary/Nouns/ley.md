# ley

## Meaning

*Ley* means "law," a rule created by a government or authority. It's a
feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form      |
| -------- | --------- |
| Singular | la ley    |
| Plural   | las leyes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| Esa ley cambió el año pasado.                    | That law changed last year.                  | statement          |
| ¿Conoces bien la ley de tránsito?                | Do you know the traffic law well?            | question           |
| No respetan la ley en este lugar.                | They don't respect the law in this place.    | negation           |
| Aprobaron varias leyes nuevas este mes.          | They passed several new laws this month.     | plural             |
| Actuó siempre dentro de la ley.                  | He always acted within the law.              | statement          |
