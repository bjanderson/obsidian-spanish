# hotel

## Meaning

*Hotel* means "hotel." It's masculine: *el hotel*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el hotel    |
| Plural   | los hoteles |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Nos quedamos en un hotel cerca del aeropuerto.   | We stayed at a hotel near the airport.  | singular, after a preposition, *del* |
| Reservaron la habitación del hotel con dos meses de anticipación. | They booked the hotel room two months in advance. | singular, *del* |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta una noche en ese hotel?           | How much does a night at that hotel cost? | question                       |
| No recomiendo ese hotel; el servicio es malo.    | I don't recommend that hotel; the service is bad. | negation                    |
| Los hoteles de la playa suben los precios en verano. | Beach hotels raise their prices in summer. | plural, subject, *de*          |
