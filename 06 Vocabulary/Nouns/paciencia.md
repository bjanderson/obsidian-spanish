# paciencia

## Meaning

*Paciencia* means "patience." It's a feminine noun, following the regular -a pattern,
and it's an uncountable/abstract noun — it doesn't take a natural plural in everyday
use.

## Singular / Plural

| Number             | Form              |
| ------------------- | ------------------ |
| Singular (standard) | la paciencia        |
| Plural (not used)    | —                   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                         | Notes                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Hay que tener paciencia con los niños.                     | You have to be patient with children.               | impersonal *hay que*            |
| Se le acabó la paciencia esperando el autobús.             | Her patience ran out waiting for the bus.           | indirect object *le*             |
| ¿Tienes paciencia para explicarme esto otra vez?           | Do you have the patience to explain this to me again? | question                       |
| La maestra tiene mucha paciencia con sus alumnos.          | The teacher has a lot of patience with her students. | subject                         |
| Perdió la paciencia después de tres horas de espera.       | He lost his patience after three hours of waiting.  | direct object                    |
