# pie

## Meaning

*Pie* means "foot." It's masculine: *el pie*. It shows up in common
expressions like *a pie* ("on foot") and *de pie* ("standing up").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form      |
| -------- | --------- |
| Singular | el pie    |
| Plural   | los pies  |

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                          | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Me duele el pie derecho.                 | My right foot hurts.                 | statement                   |
| Caminamos a pie hasta la estación.       | We walked to the station on foot.    | fixed expression            |
| ¿Te duele el pie?                        | Does your foot hurt?                 | question                    |
| No puedo ponerme de pie.                 | I can't stand up.                    | negation, fixed expression  |
| Tiene los pies muy fríos.                | His feet are very cold.              | plural                      |
