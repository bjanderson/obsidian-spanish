# elefante

## Meaning

*Elefante* means "elephant." It's masculine: *el elefante* (the feminine
form *la elefanta* refers specifically to a female elephant).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el elefante    |
| Plural   | los elefantes  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| El elefante caminaba lentamente.                     | The elephant walked slowly.                    | statement                             |
| ¿Cuánto pesa un elefante adulto?                     | How much does an adult elephant weigh?         | question                              |
| No habíamos visto un elefante tan grande.            | We hadn't seen such a big elephant.            | negation                              |
| Los elefantes viajan en manadas.                     | Elephants travel in herds.                     | plural                                 |
| Los niños alimentaron al elefante en el zoológico.   | The kids fed the elephant at the zoo.          | statement, personal *a*                |
