# crimen

## Meaning

*Crimen* means "crime," usually a serious one like murder. It's a
masculine noun, and its plural shifts the accent: *crímenes*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el crimen    |
| Plural   | los crímenes |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Investigan el crimen desde hace un mes.           | They've been investigating the crime for a month. | statement     |
| ¿Quién cometió ese crimen?                        | Who committed that crime?                    | question           |
| No hay pruebas del crimen todavía.                | There's no evidence of the crime yet.        | negation, del      |
| Los crímenes violentos bajaron este año.          | Violent crimes went down this year.          | plural             |
| Fue condenado por un crimen que no cometió.       | He was convicted of a crime he didn't commit. | statement          |
