# matemáticas

## Meaning

*Matemáticas* means "math" or "mathematics" — the school subject. It's feminine and
almost always used in the plural: *las matemáticas*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                                                          |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Singular | la matemática (rare — used mostly for the field of study)       |
| Plural   | las matemáticas (standard, everyday form — the school subject)  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                    | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Las matemáticas son difíciles para Emma.         | Math is difficult for Emma.                         | plural, subject                    |
| ¿Te gustan las matemáticas?                      | Do you like math?                                   | question, plural                   |
| No entendió el problema de matemáticas.          | She didn't understand the math problem.             | negation, singular use as modifier |
| Su amigo la ayuda con las matemáticas.           | Her friend helps her with math.                     | plural, object of preposition       |
| Hace la tarea de matemáticas con su hermano.     | She does her math homework with her brother.        | plural, singular use as modifier    |
