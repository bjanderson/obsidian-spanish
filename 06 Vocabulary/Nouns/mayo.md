# mayo

## Meaning

*Mayo* is May, the fifth month of the year. Like all months in Spanish, it's
lowercase and masculine, and it's rarely used with an article — say *en
mayo*, not *en el mayo*. An article does appear before a specific date: *el
primero de mayo*. In Bolivia, mayo is noticeably cooler than the months
before it, as the dry season sets in.
