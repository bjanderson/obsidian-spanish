# abril

## Meaning

*Abril* is April, the fourth month of the year. Like all months in Spanish,
it's lowercase and masculine, and it's rarely used with an article — say
*en abril*, not *en el abril*. An article does appear before a specific
date: *el veintidós de abril*. In Bolivia, abril sits in the middle of
autumn, and the rains that mark the wet season begin to taper off.
