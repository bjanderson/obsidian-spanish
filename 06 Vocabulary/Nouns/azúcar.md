# azúcar

## Meaning

*Azúcar* means "sugar." Its gender is ambiguous in everyday speech — most
commonly masculine (*el azúcar*), though *la azúcar* is also heard,
especially in set phrases like *azúcar blanca* ("white sugar").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el azúcar      |
| Plural   | los azúcares   |
