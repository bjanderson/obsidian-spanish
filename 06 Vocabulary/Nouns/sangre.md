# sangre

## Meaning

*Sangre* means "blood." It's feminine: *la sangre*. Like its English
counterpart, it's normally a mass noun used only in the singular; the plural
*sangres* exists grammatically but is rare, appearing mainly in figurative
writing about mixed ancestry or bloodlines.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la sangre   |
| Plural   | las sangres |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                 | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Le sacaron sangre en el laboratorio.           | They drew his blood at the lab.         | singular, object                      |
| La sangre circula por todo el cuerpo.          | Blood circulates through the whole body. | singular, subject                     |
| ¿Tienes miedo a la sangre?                     | Are you afraid of blood?                 | question                              |
| No hay sangre en la herida.                    | There's no blood on the wound.           | negation                              |
| Esa familia real mezcla sangres nobles de varios países. | That royal family mixes noble bloodlines from several countries. | plural, figurative, rare |
