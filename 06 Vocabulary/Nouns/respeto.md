# respeto

## Meaning

*Respeto* means "respect." It's a masculine noun, following the regular -o pattern,
and it's normally used as an uncountable/abstract noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number             | Form           |
| ------------------- | -------------- |
| Singular (standard) | el respeto     |
| Plural (rare)        | los respetos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                                     | Notes                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Le tengo mucho respeto a mi profesor.                        | I have a lot of respect for my teacher.              | indirect object *le*, personal *a*  |
| Trátalos con respeto, por favor.                             | Treat them with respect, please.                     | command, *con* + noun               |
| El respeto mutuo es clave en cualquier relación.             | Mutual respect is key in any relationship.           | subject                              |
| Perdió el respeto de sus compañeros.                          | He lost his classmates' respect.                     | direct object, *de*-phrase           |
| Habla con respeto cuando te dirijas a los mayores.            | Speak respectfully when you address your elders.     | command, personal *a*                |
