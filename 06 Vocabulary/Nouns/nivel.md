# nivel

## Meaning

*Nivel* means "level" — a height, degree, rank, or standard something is measured
against (a water level, a level of difficulty, a level of a building). It's
masculine: *el nivel*. Plural: *los niveles*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el nivel     |
| Plural   | los niveles  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                        | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El nivel del agua subió después de la lluvia.        | The water level rose after the rain.                 | singular, subject                  |
| Los niveles de contaminación son altos en la ciudad.  | Pollution levels are high in the city.                | plural, subject                    |
| ¿En qué nivel de español estás?                        | What level of Spanish are you at?                      | question, after a preposition       |
| No alcanzó el nivel necesario para aprobar.               | He didn't reach the level needed to pass.               | negation, object                    |
| Subimos al nivel superior del edificio.                     | We went up to the upper level of the building.           | after a preposition, *al*/*del* contractions |
