# costumbre

## Meaning

*Costumbre* means "custom," a habitual practice or tradition. It's a
feminine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | la costumbre    |
| Plural   | las costumbres  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Es costumbre cenar tarde en este país.          | It's a custom to eat dinner late in this country. | statement     |
| ¿Cuál es esa costumbre tan curiosa?             | What's that odd custom?                        | question           |
| No tengo costumbre de levantarme temprano.      | I'm not used to getting up early.              | negation, idiom     |
| Las costumbres cambian de una región a otra.    | Customs change from one region to another.     | plural             |
| Mantienen la costumbre por respeto a sus abuelos. | They keep the custom out of respect for their grandparents. | statement |
