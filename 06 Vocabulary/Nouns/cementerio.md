# cementerio

## Meaning

*Cementerio* means "cemetery" — a place where the dead are buried. It's
masculine: *el cementerio*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Singular | el cementerio     |
| Plural   | los cementerios   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                            | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Visitamos el cementerio el Día de los Muertos.                 | We visited the cemetery on the Day of the Dead.             | singular, direct object          |
| Enterraron a mi abuelo en ese cementerio.                      | They buried my grandfather at that cemetery.                | singular, personal *a*            |
| ¿Está lejos el cementerio del pueblo?                          | Is the town's cemetery far away?                            | singular, question, *del* contraction |
| No permiten entrar mascotas al cementerio.                     | They don't allow pets into the cemetery.                     | singular, negation, *al* contraction |
| Hay varios cementerios antiguos en esta región.                | There are several old cemeteries in this region.             | plural, subject                   |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[museo]] — both are places tied to a town's history and memory.
- [[iglesia]] — a *cementerio* is often located near an *iglesia*.
