# cara

## Meaning

*Cara* means "face." It's feminine: *la cara*. Regular *-a* → feminine noun, no
irregularities. Note the usual Spanish pattern with body parts: use the definite
article (*la cara*) with an indirect-object pronoun rather than a possessive — *le lavé
la cara al niño*, not *lavé su cara*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | la cara    |
| Plural   | las caras  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                  | Notes                            |

| ¿Qué tienes en la cara?                      | What do you have on your face?             | question, after a preposition        |

| Las caras de los niños mostraban alegría.    | The children's faces showed joy.           | plural, subject                       |
