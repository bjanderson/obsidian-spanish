# comida

## Meaning

*Comida* means "food" or, depending on the region, "a meal" (in many
Latin American countries, the main midday meal). It's feminine: *la
comida*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la comida   |
| Plural   | las comidas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| La comida está lista.                            | The food is ready.                            | statement                             |
| ¿A qué hora es la comida?                        | What time is the meal?                        | question, "meal" sense                  |
| No queda comida en el refrigerador.              | There's no food left in the fridge.           | negation                              |
| Las comidas típicas de la región son picantes.   | The region's typical dishes are spicy.        | plural, "dishes" sense                  |
| Preparamos la comida para toda la familia.       | We made the food for the whole family.        | statement                              |
