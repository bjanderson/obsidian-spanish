# escuela

## Meaning

*Escuela* means "school," usually a primary or general school. It's feminine: *la
escuela*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Singular     | la escuela    |
| Plural         | las escuelas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi hija va a esa escuela.                   | My daughter goes to that school.         | singular, subject                  |
| ¿A qué hora empieza la escuela?             | What time does school start?             | singular, question                 |
| Construyeron dos escuelas nuevas este año.  | They built two new schools this year.    | plural, direct object               |
| No hay clases en la escuela hoy.            | There's no class at school today.        | singular, after a preposition       |
| Los libros de la escuela son caros.         | The school's books are expensive.        | possessive *de*-phrase              |
