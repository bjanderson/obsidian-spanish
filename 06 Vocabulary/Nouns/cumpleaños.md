# cumpleaños

## Meaning

*Cumpleaños* means "birthday." It's masculine (*el cumpleaños*) and invariable — the
singular and plural forms are identical, since it comes from *cumplir años* ("to
complete years"). The standard greeting is *¡Feliz cumpleaños!* ("Happy birthday!").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                                             |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Singular | el cumpleaños                                     |
| Plural   | los cumpleaños (same form — invariable)           |

## Examples

| Spanish                                      | English                                | Notes                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Hoy es el cumpleaños de Valentina.            | Today is Valentina's birthday.          | singular, subject             |
| ¿Cuándo es tu cumpleaños?                     | When is your birthday?                  | question                      |
| No olvidé su cumpleaños este año.             | I didn't forget her birthday this year. | negation, direct object       |
| Sus cumpleaños siempre caen en verano.        | Their birthdays always fall in summer.  | plural = same form            |
| ¡Feliz cumpleaños!                            | Happy birthday!                         | fixed expression              |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[fiesta]] — a *cumpleaños* is often celebrated with a *fiesta*.
- [[pastel]] — a *pastel* is the traditional birthday cake.
- [[vela]] — *velas* on the cake mark the years turned.
- [[globo]] — *globos* are common birthday-party decorations.
- [[pedazo]] — a *pedazo* of *pastel* is eaten during the celebration.
- [[regalo]] — guests bring a *regalo* to a *cumpleaños*.
