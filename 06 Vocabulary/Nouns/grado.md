# grado

## Meaning

*Grado* means "degree" — for temperature, angles, or an academic level.
It's masculine: *el grado*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el grado    |
| Plural   | los grados  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                       | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Hoy hace treinta grados.                             | It's thirty degrees today.                            | statement, temperature               |
| ¿Cuántos grados tiene el ángulo?                     | How many degrees does the angle have?                 | question                             |
| No alcanzamos ni un grado bajo cero.                 | We didn't even reach one degree below zero.            | negation                             |
| Los grados de dificultad aumentan cada nivel.        | The difficulty levels increase each level.             | plural, figurative sense             |
| Terminó su grado en ingeniería el año pasado.        | He finished his engineering degree last year.          | secondary sense, academic degree     |
