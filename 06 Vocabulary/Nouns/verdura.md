# verdura

## Meaning

*Verdura* means "vegetable" (the food, as eaten). It's feminine: *la
verdura*, often used in the plural (*verduras*) to mean "vegetables" in
general.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la verdura    |
| Plural   | las verduras  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Esta verdura se ve muy fresca.                   | This vegetable looks very fresh.              | statement                             |
| ¿Comes verdura todos los días?                   | Do you eat vegetables every day?              | question                              |
| No le gustan las verduras a mi hijo.             | My son doesn't like vegetables.               | negation                              |
| Las verduras de la huerta son orgánicas.         | The vegetables from the garden are organic.   | plural                                 |
| Cocinó las verduras al vapor.                    | She steamed the vegetables.                   | statement                              |
