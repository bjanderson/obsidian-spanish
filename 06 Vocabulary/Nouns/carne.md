# carne

## Meaning

*Carne* means "meat." It's feminine: *la carne*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la carne    |
| Plural   | las carnes  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| La carne está muy bien cocida.                   | The meat is very well cooked.                 | statement                             |
| ¿Prefieres carne de res o de pollo?              | Do you prefer beef or chicken?                | question                              |
| No comemos carne los viernes.                    | We don't eat meat on Fridays.                 | negation                              |
| Las carnes rojas son más caras últimamente.      | Red meats are more expensive lately.          | plural                                 |
| Compramos carne fresca en el mercado.            | We bought fresh meat at the market.           | statement                              |
