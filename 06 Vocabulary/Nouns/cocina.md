# cocina

## Meaning

*Cocina* means "kitchen." It's feminine: *la cocina*. It also has a secondary sense of
"cuisine" or "cooking style" — e.g., *la cocina mexicana* ("Mexican cuisine").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la cocina    |
| Plural   | las cocinas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| La cocina está al lado del comedor.                     | The kitchen is next to the dining room.             | singular, subject                  |
| ¿Cuántas cocinas tiene ese restaurante?                 | How many kitchens does that restaurant have?        | plural, question                    |
| Preparamos la cena en la cocina.                        | We make dinner in the kitchen.                      | singular, object of preposition     |
| No hay platos sucios en la cocina hoy.                  | There are no dirty dishes in the kitchen today.     | singular, negation                  |
| Mi abuela pasa horas en la cocina los domingos.         | My grandmother spends hours in the kitchen on Sundays. | singular, habitual              |
