# material

## Meaning

*Material* means "material," either a physical substance or the general
supplies/equipment needed for a task. It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el material    |
| Plural   | los materiales |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes         |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Este material es muy resistente.                     | This material is very sturdy.                  | statement          |
| ¿Qué material usaron para el techo?                  | What material did they use for the roof?       | question           |
| No tenemos material suficiente para el proyecto.     | We don't have enough material for the project.  | negation           |
| Compramos los materiales en la ferretería.           | We bought the materials at the hardware store.  | plural             |
| Trajo todo el material escolar para sus hijos.       | She brought all the school supplies for her kids. | secondary sense |
