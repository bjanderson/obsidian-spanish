# adulto

## Meaning

*Adulto* means "adult." The masculine form *el adulto* is also used generically
for "an adult" regardless of gender in many contexts; the feminine form is
*la adulta* when referring specifically to a woman.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el adulto   |
| Plural   | los adultos |
