# cinturón

## Meaning

*Cinturón* means "belt," the accessory worn around the waist to hold up pants
or complete an outfit. It's masculine: *el cinturón*. It also shows up in the
common compound *cinturón de seguridad* ("seatbelt") — worth knowing since
that phrase comes up constantly outside of clothing contexts.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el cinturón     |
| Plural   | los cinturones  |
