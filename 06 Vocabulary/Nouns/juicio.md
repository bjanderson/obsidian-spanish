# juicio

## Meaning

*Juicio* is masculine — *el juicio*. It covers both a legal "trial" (*el juicio
comenzó ayer*, "the trial began yesterday") and, more broadly, "judgment" as in
sound reasoning or common sense (*tener buen juicio*, "to have good judgment").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Singular     | el juicio      |
| Plural         | los juicios    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El juicio empieza el lunes.                 | The trial starts on Monday.              | subject                            |
| ¿Viste el juicio en las noticias?           | Did you see the trial on the news?       | direct object, question            |
| El abogado habló sobre el juicio.           | The lawyer spoke about the trial.        | after a preposition                |
| Los juicios de ese tipo son raros aquí.     | Trials of that kind are rare here.       | plural                             |
| Perdió el juicio por falta de pruebas.      | He lost the trial for lack of evidence.  | direct object                      |
