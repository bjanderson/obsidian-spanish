# coche

## Meaning

*Coche* means "car." It's masculine: *el coche*.

**Regional note:** Spanish has more than one common word for "car," and none
of them is "the" neutral term — usage is regional. *Coche* is more common in
some South American countries (Argentina, Chile) and is the standard term in
Spain. *Carro* is the standard word across most of Latin America (Mexico,
Central America, Venezuela, Colombia). A third option, *auto*, is widely
understood across the Spanish-speaking world regardless of country (covered
in a separate vocabulary file). When in doubt about a learner's target
region, any of the three will be understood, but *carro* and *coche* carry
the strongest regional association.

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                        | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Compramos un coche nuevo la semana pasada.                 | We bought a new car last week.                          | singular, preterite               |
| Estacioné el coche frente a la panadería.                  | I parked the car in front of the bakery.                | singular, after a preposition    |
| ¿Tienes coche o usas el transporte público?                | Do you have a car or use public transportation?         | singular, question               |
| No enciende el coche con este frío.                        | The car won't start in this cold.                       | singular, negation                |
| Los coches eléctricos son cada vez más comunes.             | Electric cars are becoming more and more common.        | plural, subject                   |
