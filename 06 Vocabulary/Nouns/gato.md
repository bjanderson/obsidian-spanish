# gato

## Meaning

*Gato* means "cat." It's masculine: *el gato* (the feminine form *la gata*
refers specifically to a female cat).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el gato     |
| Plural   | los gatos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| El gato duerme en la ventana.                    | The cat sleeps in the window.                 | statement                             |
| ¿Tienes un gato en casa?                         | Do you have a cat at home?                    | question                              |
| No encontramos al gato en toda la tarde.         | We couldn't find the cat all afternoon.       | negation, personal *a*                 |
| Los gatos de esta calle son muy curiosos.        | The cats on this street are very curious.     | plural                                 |
| Alimentamos al gato dos veces al día.            | We feed the cat twice a day.                  | statement, personal *a*                |
