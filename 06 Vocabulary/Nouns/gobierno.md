# gobierno

## Meaning

*Gobierno* means "government" — the group or system that governs a country, state,
or other territory. It's masculine: *el gobierno*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el gobierno     |
| Plural   | los gobiernos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                 | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El gobierno anunció nuevas leyes.                  | The government announced new laws.             | singular, subject                  |
| No confío en el gobierno actual.                   | I don't trust the current government.          | singular, object, negation         |
| ¿Qué piensas del gobierno?                         | What do you think about the government?        | question, *del* contraction        |
| Los gobiernos de la región firmaron el acuerdo.    | The region's governments signed the agreement. | plural, subject                    |
| Trabaja para el gobierno desde hace diez años.     | She's worked for the government for ten years. | after a preposition                |

## Related Vocabulary
