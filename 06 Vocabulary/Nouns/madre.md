# madre

## Meaning

*Madre* means "mother." It's feminine: *la madre*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | la madre    |
| Plural   | las madres  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                             | Notes                        |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi madre trabaja en un hospital.                | My mother works at a hospital.            | singular, subject                   |
| Visito a mi madre cada domingo.                 | I visit my mother every Sunday.           | singular, object, personal *a*        |
| ¿Conoces a mi madre?                            | Do you know my mother?                    | question, personal *a*                |
| No veo a mi madre desde hace un mes.            | I haven't seen my mother in a month.      | negation, personal *a*                |
| Las madres del barrio se conocen bien.          | The neighborhood mothers know each other well. | plural, subject, "del"           |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[padre]] — the other parent — *padre* and *madre* pair together, and *padres* (plural) means "parents."
- [[hija]] — parent/child pair.
- [[familia]] — a *madre* is part of a *familia* (family).
