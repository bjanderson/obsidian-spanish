# década

## Meaning

*Década* means "decade" — a period of ten years. It's feminine: *la década*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | la década      |
| Plural   | las décadas    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                        | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Esta década ha traído grandes cambios.                  | This decade has brought great changes.                 | statement, subject                  |
| Vivimos en el extranjero durante una década.             | We lived abroad for a decade.                          | object, after a preposition sense   |
| ¿Cuántas décadas llevas trabajando aquí?                 | How many decades have you been working here?           | question, plural                    |
| No cambió mucho en la última década.                     | It didn't change much in the last decade.               | negation, *en la última década*     |
| La música de esa década sigue siendo popular.            | Music from that decade is still popular.                | possessive *de*-phrase              |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[siglo]] — ten *décadas* make up a *siglo*.
- [[año]] — a *década* is ten *años*.
- [[época]] — a *década* is often thought of as its own *época*.
