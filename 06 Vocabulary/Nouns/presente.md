# presente

## Meaning

*Presente* means "present," the current moment, or "gift" in some contexts.
It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el presente    |
| Plural   | los presentes  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Vive en el presente, no en el pasado.            | Live in the present, not in the past.        | statement              |
| ¿Qué opinas del presente de la empresa?          | What do you think of the company's present state? | question, del    |
| No tengo un presente para ella.                  | I don't have a gift for her.                 | negation, secondary sense |
| Todos los presentes votaron a favor.             | Everyone present voted in favor.             | plural, adjectival use |
| Recibió varios presentes por su cumpleaños.      | She received several gifts for her birthday. | plural, secondary sense |
