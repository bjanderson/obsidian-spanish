# caballo

## Meaning

*Caballo* means "horse." It's masculine: *el caballo* (the feminine form
*la yegua* refers specifically to a mare).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el caballo    |
| Plural   | los caballos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| El caballo corrió por el campo.                  | The horse ran through the field.              | statement                             |
| ¿Sabes montar a caballo?                         | Do you know how to ride a horse?              | question, fixed expression             |
| No pudimos acercarnos al caballo.                | We couldn't get close to the horse.           | negation, personal *a*                 |
| Los caballos de esa granja son muy fuertes.      | The horses on that farm are very strong.      | plural                                 |
| Compraron un caballo nuevo el mes pasado.        | They bought a new horse last month.           | statement                              |
