# luna

## Meaning

*Luna* means "moon," Earth's natural satellite. It's a feminine noun,
mostly used in the singular.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | la luna    |
| Plural   | las lunas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                             | Notes         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| La luna se ve enorme esta noche.                 | The moon looks huge tonight.                 | statement          |
| ¿Viste la luna llena anoche?                     | Did you see the full moon last night?        | question           |
| No hay luna visible por las nubes.               | There's no visible moon because of the clouds. | negation         |
| Las lunas de Júpiter son fascinantes.            | Jupiter's moons are fascinating.             | plural, secondary sense |
| Caminamos bajo la luz de la luna.                | We walked under the moonlight.               | statement          |
