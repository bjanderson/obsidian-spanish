# esposo / esposa

## Meaning

*Esposo* means "husband" (*el esposo*) and *esposa* means "wife" (*la esposa*). Like
other family-pair nouns, the masculine plural *esposos* can refer to a married couple
collectively ("husband and wife"), not just multiple husbands.

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form         |
| ------------------- | ------------ |
| Masculine singular   | el esposo    |
| Feminine singular    | la esposa    |
| Masculine plural     | los esposos  |
| Feminine plural      | las esposas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                                              | Notes                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Mi esposo trabaja desde casa.                         | My husband works from home.                               | singular, subject                    |
| Mi esposa es doctora.                                 | My wife is a doctor.                                      | singular, subject                    |
| Presenté a mi esposo a mis compañeros de trabajo.     | I introduced my husband to my coworkers.                  | object, personal *a*                 |
| ¿Conoces a la esposa de Julián?                       | Do you know Julián's wife?                                | question, personal *a*               |
| No viajo sin mi esposo.                               | I don't travel without my husband.                        | negation                             |
| Los esposos celebraron su aniversario en la playa.    | The husband and wife celebrated their anniversary at the beach. | plural = married couple        |
