# túnel

## Meaning

*Túnel* means "tunnel" — an underground or underwater passage. It's
masculine: *el túnel*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el túnel     |
| Plural   | los túneles  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                          | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| El túnel pasa por debajo de la montaña.                    | The tunnel goes underneath the mountain.              | singular, subject                |
| Manejamos por el túnel para evitar el tráfico.             | We drove through the tunnel to avoid traffic.         | singular, after a preposition    |
| ¿Es seguro caminar por ese túnel de noche?                 | Is it safe to walk through that tunnel at night?      | singular, question               |
| No hay luces suficientes en el túnel.                      | There aren't enough lights in the tunnel.              | singular, negation                |
| Construyeron varios túneles nuevos el año pasado.          | They built several new tunnels last year.             | plural, preterite                 |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[puente]] — street-infrastructure cluster; a *túnel* passes under an obstacle while a *puente* crosses over one.
- [[carretera]] — a *carretera* sometimes passes through a *túnel* to cross a mountain.
