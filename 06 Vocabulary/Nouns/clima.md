# clima

## Meaning

*Clima* means "climate." Despite ending in *-a*, it's masculine: *el clima*
— a Greek-origin exception, like *el problema* or *el sistema*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el clima    |
| Plural   | los climas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                          | Notes                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| El clima de Bolivia varía mucho según la región.            | Bolivia's climate varies a lot by region.               | statement, masculine agreement    |
| ¿Cómo es el clima en tu ciudad?                             | What's the climate like in your city?                   | question                         |
| Este clima no me gusta nada.                                | I don't like this climate at all.                       | negation                         |
| Los climas fríos afectan los cultivos.                      | Cold climates affect crops.                              | plural                           |
| El cambio climático altera los climas del planeta.          | Climate change is altering the planet's climates.        | plural                           |
