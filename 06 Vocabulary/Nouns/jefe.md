# jefe

## Meaning

*Jefe* means "boss" or "manager" — the person in charge at a job. The feminine
form is *jefa*: la jefa.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form                   |
| -------- | ---------------------- |
| Singular | el jefe / la jefa      |
| Plural   | los jefes / las jefas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                       | Notes                            |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Mi jefe llegó tarde a la oficina.                | My boss arrived late to the office.             | statement                          |
| ¿Conociste a la jefa nueva?                      | Did you meet the new boss?                      | question, personal a, feminine     |
| No quiero molestar al jefe con este problema.    | I don't want to bother the boss with this problem. | negation, al contraction        |
| Los jefes se reunieron el viernes.               | The bosses met on Friday.                       | plural                             |
| Le pedí permiso a mi jefa para salir temprano.   | I asked my boss for permission to leave early.  | indirect object                    |
