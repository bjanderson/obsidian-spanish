# hijo

## Meaning

*Hijo* means "son." It's masculine: *el hijo*. Like *padres*, the masculine plural
*hijos* usually means "children" (sons and daughters together), not just "sons" —
context tells you which sense is meant.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form        |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Singular | el hijo     |
| Plural   | los hijos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                             | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Mi hijo estudia medicina.                  | My son is studying medicine.               | singular, subject                  |
| Llevo a mi hijo a la escuela.              | I take my son to school.                   | object, personal *a*               |
| ¿Cuántos hijos tienes?                     | How many children do you have?             | plural = "children," question      |
| No veo a mi hijo desde hace un año.        | I haven't seen my son in a year.           | negation, personal *a*             |
| Está muy orgullosa de sus hijos.           | She's very proud of her children.          | after a preposition, plural        |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[hija]] — the female counterpart; together, *los hijos* means "children."
- [[padre]] — a parent of the *hijo*.
- [[familia]] — a *hijo* is part of a *familia* (family).
