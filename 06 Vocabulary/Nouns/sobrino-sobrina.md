# sobrino / sobrina

## Meaning

*Sobrino* means "nephew" (*el sobrino*) and *sobrina* means "niece" (*la sobrina*).
Like *hijos* and *hermanos*, the masculine plural *sobrinos* usually means "nieces
and nephews" collectively, not just "nephews" — context tells you which sense is
meant.

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form          |
| ------------------- | ------------- |
| Masculine singular   | el sobrino    |
| Feminine singular    | la sobrina    |
| Masculine plural     | los sobrinos  |
| Feminine plural      | las sobrinas  |

| Mi sobrina estudia en la universidad.       | My niece studies at the university.                  | statement                                   |
| Cuido al sobrino de mi vecina los viernes.  | I look after my neighbor's nephew on Fridays.        | object, personal *a* + *al*                 |

| ¿Cuántos sobrinos tienes?                    | How many nieces and nephews do you have?             | question, plural = "nieces and nephews"     |
| No tengo sobrinas todavía.                   | I don't have any nieces yet.                         | negation                                    |
