# septiembre

## Meaning

*Septiembre* is September, the ninth month of the year. Like all months in
Spanish, it's lowercase and masculine, and it's rarely used with an
article — say *en septiembre*, not *en el septiembre*. An article does
appear before a specific date: *el veintitrés de septiembre*. In Bolivia,
septiembre marks the start of spring in the Southern Hemisphere.
