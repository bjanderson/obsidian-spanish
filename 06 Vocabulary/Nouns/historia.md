# historia

## Meaning

*Historia* covers two related senses: "history" (the record/study of past
events, e.g. *la historia de un país*) and "a story" or "tale" (e.g. *contar
una historia*, "to tell a story"). Context makes clear which sense is meant.
It's feminine: *la historia*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la historia   |
| Plural   | las historias |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                  | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| La historia de este pueblo es fascinante.           | This town's history is fascinating.         | singular, subject, "history" sense |
| Mi abuela me contó muchas historias.                | My grandmother told me many stories.        | plural, object, "story" sense |
| ¿Conoces la historia de ese edificio?               | Do you know that building's history?        | question                       |
| No recuerdo bien esa historia.                      | I don't remember that story well.           | negation                        |
| Aprendimos mucho sobre la historia de Latinoamérica. | We learned a lot about Latin America's history. | after a preposition          |
