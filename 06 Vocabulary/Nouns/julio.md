# julio

## Meaning

*Julio* is July, the seventh month of the year. Like all months in Spanish,
it's lowercase and masculine, and it's rarely used with an article — say
*en julio*, not *en el julio*. An article does appear before a specific
date: *el nueve de julio*. In Bolivia, julio is typically the coldest and
driest month of the year.
