# orgullo

## Meaning

*Orgullo* means "pride." It's a masculine noun, following the regular -o pattern, and
it's normally used as an uncountable/abstract noun — the plural exists but is rare in
everyday speech.

## Singular / Plural

| Number             | Form            |
| ------------------- | --------------- |
| Singular (standard) | el orgullo      |
| Plural (rare)        | los orgullos    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                  | Notes                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Siente mucho orgullo por sus hijos.                     | He feels a lot of pride in his children.        | direct object                |
| El orgullo no la deja pedir perdón.                      | Her pride won't let her apologize.              | subject                       |
| Con orgullo, mostró su diploma a la familia.             | With pride, she showed her diploma to the family. | *con* + noun phrase           |
| Su orgullo fue herido por el comentario.                 | His pride was hurt by the comment.              | subject, passive-like            |
| El equipo ganó con orgullo y esfuerzo.                   | The team won with pride and effort.             | *con* + noun phrase              |
