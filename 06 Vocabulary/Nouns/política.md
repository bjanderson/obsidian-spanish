# política

## Meaning

*Política* covers two related senses. As "politics," it names the field of
government and public affairs, usually used in the singular without an article
in general statements (*hablar de política*). As "policy," it names a specific
course of action an organization or government adopts (*una política de la
empresa*, "a company policy") — this sense pluralizes normally. It's feminine:
*la política*. Plural: *las políticas*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | la política     |
| Plural   | las políticas   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                       | Notes                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| La política del país está muy dividida.                | The country's politics are very divided.           | singular, subject, "politics" sense    |
| No estoy de acuerdo con esta política de la empresa.      | I don't agree with this company policy.             | negation, singular, "policy" sense      |
| ¿Te interesa la política?                                  | Are you interested in politics?                       | question, object                        |
| Las políticas migratorias han cambiado mucho.                 | Immigration policies have changed a lot.               | plural, subject                          |
| Habló sobre política durante toda la cena.                       | He talked about politics through the whole dinner.       | after a preposition                       |
