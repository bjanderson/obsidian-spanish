# pueblo

## Meaning

*Pueblo* has two common senses: a small "town," and "the people" of a nation as a
collective body (*el pueblo boliviano*, "the Bolivian people"). It's masculine,
following the regular -o ending pattern: *el pueblo*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Singular      | el pueblo      |
| Plural           | los pueblos    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Crecí en un pueblo pequeño.                  | I grew up in a small town.               | after a preposition                |
| El pueblo exigió elecciones justas.          | The people demanded fair elections.      | subject, collective sense          |
| Visitamos varios pueblos en la sierra.       | We visited several towns in the highlands. | plural                          |
| ¿Conoces bien ese pueblo?                    | Do you know that town well?              | direct object, question            |
| La historia de este pueblo es fascinante.    | This town's history is fascinating.      | possessive *de*-phrase              |

## Related Vocabulary
