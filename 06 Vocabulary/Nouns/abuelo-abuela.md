# abuelo / abuela

## Meaning

*Abuelo* means "grandfather" (*el abuelo*) and *abuela* means "grandmother" (*la
abuela*). Like *padres* and *hermanos*, the masculine plural *abuelos* usually means
"grandparents" (grandfather and grandmother together), not just "grandfathers."

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form         |
| ------------------- | ------------ |
| Masculine singular   | el abuelo    |
| Feminine singular    | la abuela    |
| Masculine plural     | los abuelos  |
| Feminine plural      | las abuelas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                         | English                                         | Notes                              |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------|
| Mi abuelo cuenta muy buenas historias.              | My grandfather tells very good stories.           | singular, subject                     |
| Mi abuela cocina los mejores tamales.               | My grandmother makes the best tamales.            | singular, subject                     |
| Visitamos a mi abuelo todos los domingos.           | We visit my grandfather every Sunday.             | object, personal *a*                  |
| Llevo a mi abuela al médico los martes.             | I take my grandmother to the doctor on Tuesdays.  | object, personal *a* + *al*           |
| Mis abuelos viven en el campo.                      | My grandparents live in the countryside.          | plural = "grandparents," subject      |
| Su abuela está muy orgullosa de sus nietos.         | Her grandmother is very proud of her grandchildren. | after a preposition                |
