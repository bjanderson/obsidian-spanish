# nombre

## Meaning

*Nombre* means "name" — a person's, place's, or thing's name, and also
grammatically "noun" (*el nombre* is the traditional term for a noun in
Spanish grammar, alongside *sustantivo*). It's masculine: *el nombre*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el nombre     |
| Plural   | los nombres   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                    | Notes                        |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Mi nombre es Carlos.                               | My name is Carlos.                            | singular, subject                  |
| No recuerdo los nombres de todos.                  | I don't remember everyone's names.            | plural, negation                    |
| ¿Cuál es el nombre de esa ciudad?                  | What's the name of that city?                 | singular, question                   |
| Escribió su nombre en la lista.                    | She wrote her name on the list.               | singular, object                      |
| Reconozco a esa persona por su nombre.             | I recognize that person by her name.          | singular, after a preposition, personal *a* |
