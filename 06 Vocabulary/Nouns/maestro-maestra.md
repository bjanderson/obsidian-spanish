# maestro / maestra

## Meaning

*Maestro* means "teacher" (male) and *maestra* means "teacher" (female) — most often
a schoolteacher, especially at the elementary level. For secondary or university
level, *profesor/profesora* is more common, but *maestro/maestra* is understood
everywhere.

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form            |
| ------------------- | --------------- |
| Masculine singular   | el maestro       |
| Feminine singular    | la maestra       |
| Masculine plural     | los maestros     |
| Feminine plural      | las maestras     |
