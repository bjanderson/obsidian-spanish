# zoológico

## Meaning

*Zoológico* means "zoo" — a park where live animals are kept on display.
It's masculine: *el zoológico*. In casual speech it's often shortened to
*el zoo*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el zoológico    |
| Plural   | los zoológicos  |
