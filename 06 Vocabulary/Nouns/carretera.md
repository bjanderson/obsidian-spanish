# carretera

## Meaning

*Carretera* means "highway" or "road" — specifically a road that connects
towns or cities, as opposed to a street inside a city. It's feminine: *la
carretera*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | la carretera    |
| Plural   | las carreteras  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                  | English                                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| La carretera conecta las dos ciudades.                       | The highway connects the two cities.                | singular, subject                |
| Viajamos por la carretera durante toda la noche.             | We traveled along the highway all night.            | singular, after a preposition    |
| ¿Está cerrada la carretera hacia el norte?                   | Is the highway toward the north closed?             | singular, question               |
| No tomamos esa carretera porque hay mucho tráfico.           | We didn't take that highway because there's a lot of traffic. | singular, negation |
| Las carreteras de la región están en mal estado.             | The highways in the region are in bad condition.    | plural, subject                   |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[avenida]] — street-infrastructure cluster; an *avenida* is the city equivalent of a *carretera*.
- [[puente]] — a *carretera* frequently crosses a *puente* over a river or ravine.
- [[túnel]] — a *carretera* may pass through a *túnel* to cross a mountain.
