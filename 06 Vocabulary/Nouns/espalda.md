# espalda

## Meaning

*Espalda* means "back" — the body part. It's feminine: *la espalda*. The plural
*espaldas* shows up in the fixed idiom *a espaldas de alguien* ("behind
someone's back," i.e., without their knowledge).

Body parts like *la espalda* normally take a definite article with a
reflexive/dative construction rather than a possessive adjective: *Me duele la
espalda* ("My back hurts"), not *Me duele mi espalda*.
