# horario

## Meaning

*Horario* means "schedule" — used for work, school, or transportation
schedules alike.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form              |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Singular | el horario        |
| Plural   | los horarios      |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                              | Notes         |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| Mi horario de trabajo cambió este mes.                 | My work schedule changed this month.        | statement            |
| ¿Cuál es tu horario esta semana?                       | What's your schedule this week?             | question              |
| No respeta el horario establecido.                     | He doesn't respect the set schedule.        | negation               |
| Los horarios de los empleados están en la pizarra.      | The employees' schedules are on the board.  | plural                 |
| Ajustó el horario para asistir a la reunión.            | She adjusted her schedule to attend the meeting. | statement          |
