# teatro

## Meaning

*Teatro* means "theater" — a venue for live stage performances (plays,
concerts). It's masculine: *el teatro*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el teatro    |
| Plural   | los teatros  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                            | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Fuimos al teatro a ver una obra clásica.                       | We went to the theater to see a classic play.              | singular, *al* contraction        |
| Compramos las entradas para el teatro con anticipación.        | We bought the theater tickets ahead of time.                | singular, after a preposition    |
| ¿Te gusta el teatro?                                           | Do you like theater?                                        | singular, question               |
| No quedaban asientos disponibles en el teatro.                 | There were no available seats left at the theater.          | singular, negation                |
| Los teatros de la ciudad presentan obras nuevas cada mes.      | The city's theaters put on new plays every month.            | plural, subject                   |
