# hombre / mujer

## Meaning

*Hombre* means "man," an adult male person; it's masculine (*el hombre*). *Mujer*
means "woman," an adult female person; it's feminine (*la mujer*). *Hombre* also
appears as an interjection in casual speech, similar to "man!"/"come on!" in English
(*¡Hombre, no exageres!*, "Come on, don't exaggerate!"). *Mujer* can also mean "wife"
depending on context (*mi mujer*, "my wife"), similar to how English sometimes uses
"woman" informally the same way.

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form         |
| ------------------- | ------------ |
| Masculine singular   | el hombre    |
| Feminine singular    | la mujer     |
| Masculine plural     | los hombres  |
| Feminine plural      | las mujeres  |

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                               |

| Ese hombre trabaja en el banco.         | That man works at the bank.               | subject                                |
| Esa mujer trabaja en el banco.          | That woman works at the bank.             | subject                                |
| Conocí a un hombre muy amable ayer.     | I met a very kind man yesterday.          | direct object, personal *a*            |
| ¿Viste a la mujer que pasó por aquí?    | Did you see the woman who passed by here? | question, personal *a*                 |
| Los hombres del equipo llegaron tarde.  | The men on the team arrived late.         | plural, possessive *de*-phrase         |
| Las mujeres del equipo llegaron temprano. | The women on the team arrived early.    | plural, possessive *de*-phrase         |
