# gimnasio

## Meaning

*Gimnasio* means "gym," a place to exercise. It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form           |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Singular | el gimnasio    |
| Plural   | los gimnasios  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Voy al gimnasio tres veces por semana.            | I go to the gym three times a week.          | statement, al      |
| ¿Tienes membresía en algún gimnasio?              | Do you have a membership at any gym?         | question           |
| No me gusta hacer ejercicio en el gimnasio.       | I don't like exercising at the gym.          | negation           |
| Abrieron dos gimnasios nuevos en el barrio.       | They opened two new gyms in the neighborhood.| plural             |
| El gimnasio de la escuela está cerrado hoy.       | The school gym is closed today.              | statement, de      |
