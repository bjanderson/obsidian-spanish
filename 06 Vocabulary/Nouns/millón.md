# millón

## Meaning

*Millón* ("million") is masculine (*el millón*) and, unlike *cien* or *mil*, behaves
like a regular noun rather than a plain number word: it pluralizes (*millones*) and
needs *de* before the noun it's counting — *un millón de personas* ("a million
people"), *dos millones de dólares* ("two million dollars"), never *un millón
personas*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el millón       |
| Plural   | los millones    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Ganó un millón de dólares en la lotería.    | She won a million dollars in the lottery.       | singular, *de* + noun               |
| La ciudad tiene más de un millón de habitantes. | The city has more than a million residents. | singular, *de* + noun            |
| El video tiene dos millones de vistas.      | The video has two million views.                | plural, *de* + noun                 |
| Faltan varios millones de personas por vacunar. | Several million people still need to be vaccinated. | plural, *de* + noun          |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta la casa? — Casi un millón de dólares. | How much does the house cost? — Almost a million dollars. | standalone answer  |
