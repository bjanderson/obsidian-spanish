# avenida

## Meaning

*Avenida* means "avenue" — a wide street, often the main thoroughfare through
a city or neighborhood, sometimes lined with trees or divided by a median.
It's feminine: *la avenida*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la avenida    |
| Plural   | las avenidas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                               | English                                          | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| La avenida principal cruza toda la ciudad.               | The main avenue crosses the whole city.             | singular, subject                |
| Caminamos por la avenida hasta el museo.                 | We walked along the avenue to the museum.           | singular, after a preposition    |
| ¿Vives cerca de esta avenida?                            | Do you live near this avenue?                       | singular, question               |
| No cerraron la avenida durante el desfile.               | They didn't close the avenue during the parade.     | singular, negation                |
| Muchas avenidas del centro tienen ciclovías.             | Many downtown avenues have bike lanes.              | plural, subject                   |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[plaza]] — street-infrastructure cluster; an *avenida* often leads into a town's *plaza*.
- [[carretera]] — an *avenida* is a city street, while a *carretera* connects towns.
- [[calle]] — an *avenida* is a wider, more major kind of *calle*.
