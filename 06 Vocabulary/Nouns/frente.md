# frente

## Meaning

*Frente* is one of the small set of Spanish nouns whose gender changes its
meaning. As a feminine noun, *la frente*, it means "forehead" — the body part,
and the sense used in this batch. As a masculine noun, *el frente*, it means
"front" — a weather front, a military front line, or the front/façade of
something. The two are not interchangeable: always check which article
accompanies it to know which sense is meant.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Feminine — forehead | Masculine — front |
| -------- | -------------------- | ------------------ |
| Singular | la frente            | el frente          |
| Plural   | las frentes          | los frentes        |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                   | Notes                                  |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Tiene la frente amplia.                       | She has a broad forehead.                   | singular, feminine sense, subject          |
| Le tocaron la frente para ver si tenía fiebre. | They touched his forehead to check for a fever. | singular, feminine sense, indirect object |
| ¿Por qué frunces la frente?                   | Why are you frowning (wrinkling your forehead)? | question, feminine sense                 |
| No te toques la frente con las manos sucias.  | Don't touch your forehead with dirty hands. | negative command, feminine sense           |
| El ejército avanzó hacia el frente.           | The army advanced toward the front.         | masculine sense, contrast — "front," not "forehead" |
