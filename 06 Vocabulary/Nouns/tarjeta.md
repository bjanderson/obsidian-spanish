# tarjeta

## Meaning

*Tarjeta* means "card" — a general word for any small rectangular card. It's a
feminine noun that commonly appears in compounds: *tarjeta de crédito* (credit
card), *tarjeta de débito* (debit card), *tarjeta de identificación* (ID card).
