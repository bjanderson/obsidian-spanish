# bombero

## Meaning

*Bombero* means "firefighter." The feminine form is *bombera*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el bombero    |
| Plural   | los bomberos  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                   | English                              | Notes             |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| El bombero apagó el incendio en minutos.            | The firefighter put out the fire in minutes. | statement              |
| ¿Cómo llamas a los bomberos en tu país?             | How do you call the fire department in your country? | question, plural |
| No pudimos localizar al bombero de guardia.         | We couldn't find the firefighter on duty.    | negation, personal a   |
| La bombera rescató a dos personas del edificio.     | The firefighter rescued two people from the building. | feminine, personal a |
| Los bomberos llegaron rápido a la emergencia.       | The firefighters arrived quickly to the emergency. | plural              |
