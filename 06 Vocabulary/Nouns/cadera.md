# cadera

## Meaning

*Cadera* means "hip." It's feminine: *la cadera*.

Like other body parts, *cadera* pairs with a definite article and a
dative construction rather than a possessive: *Le duele la cadera* ("His hip
hurts"), not *Le duele su cadera*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | la cadera     |
| Plural   | las caderas   |
