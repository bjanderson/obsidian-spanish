# cargador

## Meaning

*Cargador* means "charger" — the device used to charge a phone, laptop, or other
battery-powered device. It's a masculine noun, related to the verb *cargar* (to
charge/load).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form            |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Singular | el cargador     |
| Plural   | los cargadores  |
