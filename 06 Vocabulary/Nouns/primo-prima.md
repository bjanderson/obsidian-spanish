# primo / prima

## Meaning

*Primo* means "cousin" (male); it's masculine: *el primo*. *Prima* means "cousin"
(female); it's feminine: *la prima*. Like other family-pair nouns, the masculine
plural *primos* usually means "cousins" in general (mixed gender), not just male
cousins; *primas* is used specifically when all the cousins are female.

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form        |
| ------------------- | ----------- |
| Masculine singular   | el primo    |
| Feminine singular    | la prima    |
| Masculine plural     | los primos  |
| Feminine plural      | las primas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                       | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Mi primo juega fútbol los sábados.                     | My cousin plays soccer on Saturdays.                 | singular, subject                   |
| Mi prima estudia arquitectura.                         | My cousin studies architecture.                      | singular, subject                   |
| Invité a mi primo a la fiesta.                          | I invited my cousin to the party.                    | object, personal *a*                |
| Llevé a mi prima al aeropuerto.                         | I took my cousin to the airport.                     | object, personal *a* + *al*         |
| Tengo muchos primos por parte de mi madre.              | I have a lot of cousins on my mother's side.         | plural, object                      |
| Fui de vacaciones con mis primas el verano pasado.       | I went on vacation with my cousins last summer.      | plural, after a preposition         |
