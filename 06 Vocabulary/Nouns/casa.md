# casa

## Meaning

*Casa* means "house" or "home." It's feminine: *la casa*. It appears in common
fixed expressions: *en casa* ("at home") and *a casa* ("home," as a destination
with verbs of motion, e.g., *ir a casa*, "to go home").

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Singular     | la casa      |
| Plural         | las casas    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                          | Notes                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Mi casa está cerca del parque.                                    | My house is near the park.                                 | singular, subject                   |
| ¿Cuántas casas hay en esta calle?                                 | How many houses are there on this street?                  | plural, question                     |
| Compramos una casa nueva el año pasado.                           | We bought a new house last year.                            | singular, object                     |
| No hay nadie en casa ahora mismo.                                 | There's no one home right now.                              | singular, negation, "en casa"         |
| Los niños corrieron hacia la casa cuando empezó a llover.         | The kids ran toward the house when it started to rain.     | singular, after preposition "hacia" |
