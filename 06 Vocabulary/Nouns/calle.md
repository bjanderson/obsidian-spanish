# calle

## Meaning

*Calle* means "street." It's feminine: *la calle*. No irregularities — it follows the
regular *-e* noun pattern, adding *-s* for the plural. Note the idiom *en la calle*
("outside," "out and about," not just "on the street" literally).

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | la calle     |
| Plural   | las calles   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                              | Notes                        |

| La calle está muy oscura de noche.          | The street is very dark at night.        | singular, subject              |
