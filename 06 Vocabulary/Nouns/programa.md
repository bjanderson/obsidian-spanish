# programa

## Meaning

*Programa* means "program," whether a TV show, a software application, or
a planned schedule. Despite ending in *-a*, it's masculine — most Spanish
nouns of Greek origin ending in *-ma* follow this pattern.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form          |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Singular | el programa   |
| Plural   | los programas |

## Examples

| Spanish                                      | English                               | Notes         |
