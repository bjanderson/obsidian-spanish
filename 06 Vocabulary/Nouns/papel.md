# papel

## Meaning

*Papel* means "paper," the material, and also "role," as in a part played
in a movie or a situation. It's a masculine noun.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el papel     |
| Plural   | los papeles  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes             |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Necesito una hoja de papel.                     | I need a sheet of paper.                    | statement              |
| ¿Qué papel tiene ella en la película?           | What role does she have in the movie?       | question, secondary sense |
| No encuentro los papeles del carro.             | I can't find the car's papers.              | negation, *del*        |
| Los papeles están sobre el escritorio.          | The papers are on the desk.                 | plural                 |
| El actor hizo un papel muy difícil.             | The actor played a very difficult role.     | secondary sense        |
