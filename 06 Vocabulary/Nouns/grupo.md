# grupo

## Meaning

*Grupo* means "group" — a number of people or things gathered or classified
together. It's masculine: *el grupo*. Plural: *los grupos*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form         |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Singular | el grupo     |
| Plural   | los grupos   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                    | Notes                          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El grupo llegó tarde a la reunión.              | The group arrived late to the meeting.         | singular, subject                  |
| Los grupos indígenas preservan sus tradiciones.  | Indigenous groups preserve their traditions.    | plural, subject                    |
| ¿Conoces a ese grupo de músicos?                  | Do you know that group of musicians?             | question, personal *a*, object     |
| No pertenezco a ese grupo.                          | I don't belong to that group.                     | negation, after a preposition       |
| Trabajamos en un grupo pequeño.                       | We work in a small group.                           | after a preposition                 |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[familia]] — a *familia* (family) is one common kind of *grupo* (group).
