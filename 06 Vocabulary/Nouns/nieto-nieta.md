# nieto / nieta

## Meaning

*Nieto* means "grandson" (*el nieto*) and *nieta* means "granddaughter" (*la
nieta*). Like *hijos* and *hermanos*, the masculine plural *nietos* usually means
"grandchildren" collectively, not just "grandsons" — context tells you which sense is
meant.

## Forms

| Gender / Number    | Form        |
| ------------------- | ----------- |
| Masculine singular   | el nieto    |
| Feminine singular    | la nieta    |
| Masculine plural     | los nietos  |
| Feminine plural      | las nietas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                            | English                                            | Notes                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------|
| Mi nieto me visita cada sábado.                        | My grandson visits me every Saturday.                  | statement                               |
| Mi nieta aprende a leer.                               | My granddaughter is learning to read.                  | statement                               |
| Llevé al nieto de mi vecina al parque.                 | I took my neighbor's grandson to the park.             | object, personal *a* + *al*             |
| Llamo a mi nieta todos los domingos.                   | I call my granddaughter every Sunday.                  | object, personal *a*                    |
| ¿Cuántos nietos tiene tu abuela?                       | How many grandchildren does your grandmother have?      | question, plural = "grandchildren"     |
| No paso suficiente tiempo con mi nieta.                | I don't spend enough time with my granddaughter.       | negation                                |
