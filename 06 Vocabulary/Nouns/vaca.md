# vaca

## Meaning

*Vaca* means "cow." It's feminine: *la vaca*.

## Singular / Plural

| Number   | Form       |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Singular | la vaca    |
| Plural   | las vacas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                 | English                              | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| La vaca pasta cerca del río.                        | The cow grazes near the river.                | statement                             |
| ¿Cuántas vacas tiene tu tío?                        | How many cows does your uncle have?           | question                              |
| No ordeñamos la vaca esta mañana.                   | We didn't milk the cow this morning.          | negation                              |
| Las vacas de la granja dan leche fresca.            | The farm's cows give fresh milk.              | plural                                 |
| Vimos a las vacas caminando hacia el establo.       | We saw the cows walking toward the barn.      | statement, personal *a*                |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[granja]] — where cows are typically raised.
- [[leche]] — what a cow produces.
