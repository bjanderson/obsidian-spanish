# préstamo

## Meaning

*Préstamo* means "loan" — money borrowed, usually from a bank, that has to be
paid back. It's a masculine noun, from the verb *prestar* (to lend). Closely
related to [[deuda]] (debt): a *préstamo* is the borrowed money itself; the
*deuda* is what's still owed on it.
