# Vocabulary Outline

Tracks which vocabulary words have a full deep-dive file (conjugation tables +
example sentences) under `06 Vocabulary/`, built with the `new-vocabulary` skill from
`00 Templates/Vocabulary Template.md`.

This is separate from `01 Lessons/` vocabulary-category lessons (tracked in
`02 MOCs/Vocabulary.md`) — those teach a themed vocabulary set inside the CEFR
curriculum; files here are single-word conjugation/usage references that lessons and
flashcards can link out to.

- **Status** — ✅ built, otherwise blank (planned).
- **POS** — part of speech: `art` article, `prep` preposition, `conj` conjunction,
  `pron` pronoun, `adv` adverb, `num` number, `v` verb, `n` noun, `adj` adjective.
- Ranking is a best-effort blend of standard Spanish frequency data (function words
  first, then core verbs, then everyday nouns/adjectives/adverbs/topical vocabulary).
  Beyond roughly the top 200–300 words, exact rank order is approximate — treat this
  as a solid learning-priority order, not a precise corpus count.
- List excludes _vosotros_/_vuestro_/_os_ and other Spain-specific forms, per
  `.claude/rules/spanish-language.md` — this vault teaches neutral Latin American
  Spanish.
- Only word lemmas are listed (e.g. verb infinitives, singular nouns) — not
  individual conjugated/inflected forms.
- Words that share the same core meaning across gender/number forms (e.g. _el_,
  _la_, _los_, _las_ — all "the") share a single row and a single file, ranked at
  the group's most frequent form. This also covers gender-paired concept nouns
  whose masculine/feminine forms are different core meanings rather than pure
  grammatical agreement (e.g. _hijo/hija_, _padre/madre_). Ranks are renumbered
  sequentially after merging.
- Words found in vault content (lessons, stories, etc.) that fall outside the Top
  1000 frequency list are woven into the ranked list below at a best-effort
  frequency position, per the same approximate-beyond-300 rule above — they
  aren't tracked in a separate unranked section.
- A row with a rank but blank Status and File was appended by the `new-story`
  skill for a word it needed but found no vocabulary file for — its rank number
  is just "next in sequence," not a real frequency position. Running
  `new-vocabulary` for that word builds the file, fills in Status/File, and can
  reposition it to its proper frequency rank.
