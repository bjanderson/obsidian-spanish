# un / una

## Meaning

*Un* and *una* are the masculine and feminine singular forms of the Spanish
indefinite article — "a" or "an." *Un* is actually the number [[uno]] ("one")
shortened (apocopated) right before a masculine singular noun, the same way *bueno*
shortens to *buen* and *grande* to *gran* in that position — away from a noun, the
full form *uno* comes back: *Tengo uno* ("I have one"). *Una*, by contrast, doesn't
shorten in front of a noun; it keeps its full form.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender / Number   | Form  |
| -------------------- | ------- |
| Masculine singular      | un      |
| Feminine singular        | una     |
| Masculine plural           | unos    |
| Feminine plural              | unas    |

The plural forms *unos*/*unas* mean "some" or "a few," not "ones": *unos libros*
("some books").

**Note:** a feminine singular noun that starts with a stressed *a-*/*ha-* sound
takes *un* instead of *una*, for the same pronunciation reason a feminine noun
takes *el* instead of *la* (see [[el-la-los-las|el / la / los / las]]) — e.g. *un
área grande*, "a big area" (*área* is still grammatically feminine).

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Necesito un vaso de agua.                    | I need a glass of water.               | masculine singular                |
| ¿Tienes un minuto?                            | Do you have a minute?                  | masculine singular, question       |
| Compré un regalo para mi hermano.             | I bought a gift for my brother.        | masculine singular, direct object   |
| Quiero una taza de café.                     | I want a cup of coffee.                | feminine singular                 |
| ¿Tienes una hoja de papel?                    | Do you have a sheet of paper?          | feminine singular, question       |
| Fue una experiencia inolvidable.              | It was an unforgettable experience.    | feminine singular                 |
| Compré unos libros en el mercado.             | I bought some books at the market.     | masculine plural, "some"           |
