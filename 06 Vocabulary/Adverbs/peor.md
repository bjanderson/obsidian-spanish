# peor

## Meaning

*Peor* is the irregular comparative and superlative of *malo/mal* ("bad"/"badly") —
"worse" and "worst." It works as both an adjective (*Es peor estudiante que su
hermano* — "He's a worse student than his brother") and an adverb (*Canta peor que
antes* — "She sings worse than before"). It never changes for gender, only for
number: *peor* (singular) / *peores* (plural).

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                       | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Esta película es peor que la anterior.      | This movie is worse than the previous one.            | adjective, comparative               |
| Es el peor día de mi vida.                  | It's the worst day of my life.                         | adjective, superlative                 |
| Hoy dormí peor que ayer.                    | I slept worse today than yesterday.                     | adverb                                  |
| Sus notas son cada vez peores.              | His grades are getting worse and worse.                  | adjective, plural                         |
| Podría haber sido peor.                     | It could have been worse.                                 | standalone                                  |
