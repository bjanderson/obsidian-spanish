# fácilmente

## Meaning

*Fácilmente* means "easily" — the *-mente* adverb from *fácil* ("easy"), describing how
easily an action happens. It commonly pairs with *poder* (*se puede hacer fácilmente*,
"it can be done easily") and with comparisons (*fácilmente el mejor*, "easily the
best"). Related in this batch to [[claramente]] as another common manner *-mente*
adverb.

| Spanish                                          | English                                   | Notes            |
| ----------------------------------------------------| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
