# casi

## Meaning

*Casi* means "almost" or "nearly" — it describes something that came close to
happening or being true but didn't quite. Common fixed pairings: *casi siempre*
("almost always"), *casi nunca* ("almost never"), *casi nada* ("almost nothing").

## Core Uses

| Use                          | Pattern                | Example                       | English                       |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| With a verb                        | casi + verb                 | Casi me caigo.                      | I almost fell.                       |
| With siempre/nunca                     | casi siempre / casi nunca         | Casi siempre llego a tiempo.          | I'm almost always on time.             |
| With an adjective/quantity                | casi + [adj/number]                     | Son casi las diez.                       | It's almost ten o'clock.                 |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                    | Notes                    |
