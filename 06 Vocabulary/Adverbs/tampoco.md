# tampoco

## Meaning

*Tampoco* is the negative counterpart of *también* ("also/too") — it agrees with a
negative statement instead of a positive one, meaning "neither" or "not... either."
Like *nunca*, it can go before the verb without *no*, or after the verb with *no*
placed before the verb.

## Core Uses

| Use              | Pattern             | Example                                        | English                                     |
| ------------------ | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Before verb           | tampoco + verb          | Tampoco quiero ir.                                  | I don't want to go either.                       |
| After verb             | no + verb ... tampoco   | No quiero ir tampoco.                               | I don't want to go either.                       |
| Short agreement          | — ... Yo tampoco.          | — No fui a la fiesta. — Yo tampoco.                    | "I didn't go to the party." "Me neither."          |
| With nunca                 | nunca ... tampoco              | Nunca voy al cine, y tampoco veo series.                | I never go to the movies, and I don't watch shows either. |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                        | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| No me gusta el pescado, y a mi hermano tampoco. | I don't like fish, and neither does my brother.    | agreeing with a negative     |
| Tampoco sé la respuesta.                        | I don't know the answer either.                    | before verb                  |
| — No fui a la fiesta. — Yo tampoco.             | "I didn't go to the party." "Me neither."          | short agreement               |
| Ella no habla francés, y yo tampoco.            | She doesn't speak French, and neither do I.        | after verb, comparison        |
| Nunca ha estado en Chile, y tampoco en Argentina. | He's never been to Chile, and not to Argentina either. | pairs with *nunca*         |
