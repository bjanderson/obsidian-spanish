# ahí

## Meaning

*Ahí* means "there" — closer than [[allá]], typically near the person being spoken to or
a spot already understood from context. *Por ahí* means "around there"/"somewhere"
(*Lo dejé por ahí*, "I left it around there somewhere"), and *ahí mismo* emphasizes the
exact spot, "right there." Don't confuse *ahí* (this adverb of place) with *hay* (from
*haber*, "there is/are") or *ay* (the exclamation "ouch"/"oh") — all three are
pronounced alike but spelled and used differently. Part of this batch's here/there
system with [[acá]] and [[allá]].

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                                | Notes                          |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Deja el paquete ahí, por favor.         | Leave the package there, please.           | location near listener              |
| ¿Qué hay ahí dentro?                    | What's in there?                           | combined with *dentro*              |
| Lo encontré ahí mismo, donde lo dejaste.| I found it right there, where you left it. | *ahí mismo*, exact spot              |
| Por ahí vive un amigo mío.              | A friend of mine lives around there.       | *por ahí*, general area              |
| Ahí viene el autobús.                   | There comes the bus.                       | location near listener              |

- [[allá]] — "there" (far), part of the here/there system.
