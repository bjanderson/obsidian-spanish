# más

## Meaning

*Más* is invariant and builds comparisons and superlatives — "more" and "most."
Don't confuse it with *mas* (no accent, an old-fashioned literary word for "but,"
rarely used in everyday speech).

## Core Uses

| Use                  | Pattern                          | Example                         | English                          |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Comparative                | más + [adj/adv] + [[que]]                | Es más rápido que el otro.              | It's faster than the other one.          |
| Superlative                    | el/la + más + [adj]                        | Es el más caro de todos.                | It's the most expensive of all.          |
| "More" with a noun                | más + [noun]                                | Necesito más tiempo.                     | I need more time.                        |
| "No more/no longer"                  | no + [verb] + más                             | No fumo más.                             | I don't smoke anymore.                    |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Ella es más alta que su hermano.            | She's taller than her brother.           | comparative                          |
| Este es el restaurante más popular del barrio. | This is the most popular restaurant in the neighborhood. | superlative              |
| ¿Quieres más café?                          | Do you want more coffee?                 | "more" with a noun, question         |
| Ya no vivo más en esa ciudad.               | I don't live in that city anymore.       | "no more/no longer"                   |
| Cuanto más practicas, más aprendes.         | The more you practice, the more you learn. | correlative comparison             |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[que]] — pairs with *más* to form comparisons: "more... than."
