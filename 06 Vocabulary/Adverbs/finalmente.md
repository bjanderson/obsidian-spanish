# finalmente

## Meaning

*Finalmente* means "finally" or "at last" — it marks the last item in a sequence, or
the end of a wait or process. It's more neutral than *por fin*, which leans harder
into relief that something long-awaited finally happened; *finalmente* works equally
well in narration and in everyday speech.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *final* + *-mente*. No standalone vocabulary file exists
  yet for *final* in `06 Vocabulary/Adjectives/`.
- Common as the last step in a list alongside *primero* and *después*: *primero...,
  después..., finalmente...*.

## Examples

| Spanish                                                    | English                                            | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Finalmente, terminamos el proyecto.                            | We finally finished the project.                       | sentence-initial            |
| Después de tres horas de espera, el avión finalmente despegó.  | After three hours of waiting, the plane finally took off. | mid-sentence          |
| Primero lavamos los platos y finalmente limpiamos la cocina.   | First we washed the dishes and finally cleaned the kitchen. | sequencing a list      |
| ¿Finalmente decidiste qué carrera vas a estudiar?               | Did you finally decide what major you're going to study? | question                |
| No lo esperábamos, pero finalmente todo salió bien.             | We weren't expecting it, but in the end everything turned out fine. | after a contrast |
