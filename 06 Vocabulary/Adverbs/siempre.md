# siempre

## Meaning

*Siempre* means "always, all the time." *Siempre que* means "whenever" or "provided
that," introducing a condition (often followed by the subjunctive).

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                     | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Siempre llega temprano a clase.              | He always arrives early to class.                    | statement                         |
| ¿Siempre comes aquí?                         | Do you always eat here?                              | question                          |
| No siempre tengo tiempo para cocinar.        | I don't always have time to cook.                    | negation                          |
| Puedes venir siempre que quieras.            | You can come whenever you want.                      | conditional ("siempre que")       |
| Siempre fue así entre nosotros.              | It was always like that between us.                  | statement                         |
