# tarde

## Meaning

As an adverb, *tarde* means "late": *Llegué tarde a la reunión* ("I arrived late to
the meeting"). The same word, preceded by an article, is also a common feminine noun
meaning "afternoon/evening" — *la tarde* ("the afternoon") — a completely different
grammatical role worth not confusing with the adverb.

## Core Uses

| Use                        | Pattern                     | Example                          | English                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Adverb "late"                     | verb + tarde                       | Siempre llega tarde.                    | He always arrives late.                |
| "Better late than never"                 | más vale tarde que nunca                    | Más vale tarde que nunca.                    | Better late than never.                    |
| Noun "afternoon"                                | la tarde                                             | Nos vemos por la tarde.                            | See you in the afternoon.                        |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                      |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Nos acostamos muy tarde anoche.             | We went to bed very late last night.          | adverb                              |
| ¿Por qué llegaste tan tarde hoy?            | Why did you arrive so late today?              | adverb, question                     |
| Es tarde para cambiar de opinión.           | It's late to change your mind.                  | adverb                                |
| Tarde o temprano vas a tener que decidir.   | Sooner or later you'll have to decide.           | fixed phrase, pairs with *temprano*     |
| Trabajo todas las tardes.                   | I work every afternoon.                          | noun use, for contrast                    |
