# sinceramente

## Meaning

*Sinceramente* means "sincerely" or "honestly" — it marks a statement as an honest
opinion, often placed at the very start of a sentence, much like English "honestly, ...".

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *sincero* + *-mente*. No standalone vocabulary file
  exists yet for *sincero* in `06 Vocabulary/Adjectives/`.
- Part of the same "stance adverb" family as [[verdaderamente]] and [[seguramente]].

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                    | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Sinceramente, no me gustó la película.               | Honestly, I didn't like the movie.                  | sentence-initial            |
| Te lo digo sinceramente: necesitas descansar más.     | I'm telling you sincerely: you need to rest more.   | mid-sentence                |
| ¿Sinceramente crees que va a funcionar?               | Do you sincerely believe it's going to work?        | question                    |
| Sinceramente, no esperaba esa respuesta.              | Honestly, I wasn't expecting that answer.           | sentence-initial, negated   |
| Ella habló sinceramente sobre sus errores.            | She spoke sincerely about her mistakes.             | after the verb              |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[verdaderamente]] — related, marks genuineness rather than honesty
- [[seguramente]] — related, marks confidence rather than honesty
