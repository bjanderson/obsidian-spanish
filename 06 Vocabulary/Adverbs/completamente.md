# completamente

## Meaning

*Completamente* means "completely" or "entirely" — a near-synonym of [[totalmente]],
slightly more neutral/formal in register but used constantly in everyday speech too.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *completo* + *-mente*. No standalone vocabulary file
  exists yet for *completo* in `06 Vocabulary/Adjectives/`.
- Opposite of [[parcialmente]] on the totality scale.

## Examples

| Spanish                                             | English                                | Notes             |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| El tanque está completamente vacío.                         | The tank is completely empty.                  | with *estar*            |
| Me olvidé completamente de la reunión.                       | I completely forgot about the meeting.          | before *de*              |
| ¿Leíste el libro completamente o solo un capítulo?            | Did you read the book completely, or just one chapter? | question       |
| La ciudad quedó completamente cubierta de nieve.              | The city was completely covered in snow.        | with a past participle  |
| No estoy completamente convencido de su plan.                | I'm not completely convinced by his plan.       | with a negative          |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[totalmente]] — near-synonym, slightly more casual register
- [[parcialmente]] — antonym, the low end of the totality scale
