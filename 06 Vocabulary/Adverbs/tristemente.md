# tristemente

## Meaning

*Tristemente* means "sadly" — it works both as a manner adverb (describing how
someone did something, e.g. smiled sadly) and as a sentence adverb expressing
regret about a fact, much like English "sadly, ...".

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *triste* ([[triste]]) + *-mente*.
- Antonym of [[felizmente]] in the manner sense.

## Examples

| Spanish                                                | English                                       | Notes                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Tristemente, el restaurante cerró para siempre.                 | Sadly, the restaurant closed for good.                  | sentence adverb, sentence-initial |
| Ella sonrió tristemente antes de despedirse.                     | She smiled sadly before saying goodbye.                 | manner, after the verb      |
| Tristemente, muchos animales pierden su hábitat cada año.        | Sadly, many animals lose their habitat every year.       | sentence adverb, sentence-initial |
| Nos miró tristemente sin decir una palabra.                       | He looked at us sadly without saying a word.             | manner, after the verb      |
| ¿Por qué hablas tan tristemente de tu pasado?                     | Why do you speak so sadly about your past?               | question, intensified       |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[felizmente]] — antonym in the manner sense (sadly vs. happily)
