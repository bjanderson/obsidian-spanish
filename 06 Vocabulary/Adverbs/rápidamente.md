# rápidamente

## Meaning

*Rápidamente* means "quickly" or "rapidly" — the *-mente* adverb built from
*rápido/rápida* ("fast," adjective), describing how fast an action happens. It's a bit
more formal or descriptive than the everyday phrase where *rápido* itself is used as an
adverb (*Ven rápido*, "Come quick"), which is extremely common in spoken Spanish — but
*rápidamente* is standard in both writing and neutral speech. Opposite of [[despacio]]
and [[lentamente]] ("slowly").
