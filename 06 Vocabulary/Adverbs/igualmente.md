# igualmente

## Meaning

*Igualmente* has two common uses. As a plain adverb it means "equally," comparing two
things treated the same way: *Los trató a todos igualmente* ("He treated everyone
equally"). As a courtesy reply it means "likewise" — echoing a wish or greeting back to
the other person: *Que tengas buen día. — Igualmente.* Near-synonym [[asimismo]] for the
"likewise/also" sense, though *asimismo* doesn't work as a stand-alone courtesy reply the
way *igualmente* does.

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Repartieron el trabajo igualmente entre todos.    | They divided up the work equally among everyone. | "equally" sense           |
| Fue un placer conocerte. — Igualmente.            | It was a pleasure to meet you. — Likewise.    | courtesy reply                |
| Que descanses. — Igualmente.                      | Sleep well. — You too.                        | courtesy reply                |
| Los dos equipos jugaron igualmente bien.          | Both teams played equally well.               | "equally" sense, before adjective |
| Igualmente te deseo mucho éxito.                  | I likewise wish you a lot of success.         | "likewise" sense, clause-initial |
