# claramente

## Meaning

*Claramente* means "clearly" — the *-mente* adverb from *claro* ("clear"), used for how
understandably or visibly something comes across, whether it's speech, an explanation,
or a sight. It also works as a stand-alone response meaning "clearly"/"obviously"
(*—¿Vas a ir? —Claramente.*, "Are you going?" "Clearly."). Related in this batch to
[[fácilmente]] as another common manner *-mente* adverb.
