# abajo

## Meaning

*Abajo* means "down" or "below" — a lower position or a downward direction. Standalone,
it's the everyday word for "downstairs" (*Está abajo*, "She's downstairs"). Followed by
*de*, it means "under"/"below" a specific thing (*abajo de la mesa*, "under the table") —
a common, colloquial construction throughout Latin America. It also appears in the
exclamation *¡Abajo...!* ("Down with...!") to reject or protest something. Part of the
spatial/directional cluster in this batch along with [[dentro]], [[fuera]], [[adelante]],
and [[atrás]].
