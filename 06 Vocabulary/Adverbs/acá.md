# acá

## Meaning

*Acá* means "here," close to the speaker — very close in meaning to *aquí*, but *acá* is
the more common, everyday choice in spoken Latin American Spanish, especially with verbs
of motion (*Ven acá*, "Come here"). It also tends to point to a looser, more general
area around the speaker rather than one exact spot: *por acá* ("around here"). Together
with [[allá]] (there, far) and [[ahí]] (there, near the listener), it forms this batch's
three-way "here/there" system, which runs parallel to *este/ese/aquel*.

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                                | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Ven acá un momento.                    | Come here for a moment.                   | motion verb                      |
| Por acá no ha llovido en semanas.      | Around here, it hasn't rained in weeks.   | *por acá*, general area          |
| Siéntate acá, a mi lado.               | Sit here, next to me.                     | location near speaker            |
| De acá para allá, no paro de trabajar. | I don't stop working, running back and forth. | idiom, with *allá*            |
| Acá se vive tranquilo.                 | Life is peaceful around here.             | general area                     |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[allá]] — "there" (far), part of the here/there system.
- [[ahí]] — "there" (near the listener), part of the here/there system.
