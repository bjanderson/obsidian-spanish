# probablemente

## Meaning

*Probablemente* means "probably" — the *-mente* adverb from *probable* ("probable,"
"likely"), used to express a likely outcome. Placed before the verb, it commonly
triggers the subjunctive when the speaker wants to emphasize doubt (*Probablemente
llueva mañana*, "It will probably rain tomorrow"); placed after the verb, or when the
speaker treats the outcome as a near-certainty, the indicative is also natural and common
(*Va a llover probablemente*). Both patterns are heard in everyday speech; the
subjunctive right after *probablemente* is the safer, more textbook-correct choice.
Slightly stronger in certainty than [[posiblemente]] ("possibly") in this batch.

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                  | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Probablemente llegue tarde a la reunión.        | I'll probably arrive late to the meeting.      | subjunctive, before the verb        |
| Ella probablemente ya lo sabe.                  | She probably already knows.                    | indicative                          |
| Probablemente no vengan hoy.                    | They probably won't come today.                | subjunctive, negative                |
| Va a llover probablemente esta tarde.           | It's probably going to rain this afternoon.    | indicative, adverb after the verb    |
| —¿Vendrás a la fiesta? —Probablemente.          | "Will you come to the party?" "Probably."      | stand-alone response                 |
