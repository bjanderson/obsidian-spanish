# antes

## Meaning

*Antes* means "before" or "earlier" in time. On its own, it works like a plain
adverb: *Ya nos conocíamos de antes* ("We already knew each other from
before"). Followed by *de* + a noun or infinitive, it means "before" that
thing: *antes de la cena* ("before dinner"), *antes de salir* ("before
leaving"). Followed by *que* + a conjugated verb, it means "before" that event
happens: *antes de que llegues* ("before you arrive").

Don't confuse *antes* with [[ante]] (preposition, "faced with"/"in the
presence of," not about time).

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Nunca había visto una nevada antes.         | I had never seen snow before.          | standalone adverb                  |
| Lávate las manos antes de comer.            | Wash your hands before eating.         | *antes de* + infinitive             |
| Llegamos antes de las ocho.                 | We arrived before eight o'clock.       | *antes de* + noun (time)            |
| Avísame antes de que sea tarde.             | Let me know before it's too late.      | *antes de que* + conjugated verb    |
| Prefiero llegar antes que tarde.            | I'd rather arrive early than late.     | comparison                          |
