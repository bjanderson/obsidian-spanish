# solo

## Meaning

*Solo* has two different jobs that share one spelling. As an **adjective**, it agrees
in gender and number — *solo*, *sola*, *solos*, *solas* — and means "alone" or
"lonely": *Vive solo* ("He lives alone"), *Se siente sola* ("She feels lonely"). As an
**adverb**, it's invariable and means "only," a synonym of *solamente*: *Solo quiero
descansar* ("I only want to rest"). Older style sometimes accented the adverb (*sólo*)
to tell it apart from the adjective, but the current spelling standard (RAE, 2010)
drops that accent in both cases — context makes the meaning clear.

## Full Paradigm (adjective use)

| Gender / Number   | Form  |
| -------------------- | ------- |
| Masculine singular      | solo    |
| Feminine singular        | sola    |
| Masculine plural           | solos   |
| Feminine plural              | solas   |

| Mi abuela vive sola desde hace diez años.       | My grandmother has lived alone for ten years. | adjective, feminine singular      |
| Los niños se quedaron solos en casa.            | The kids stayed home alone.                  | adjective, masculine plural         |
| Solo hablo español en casa.                     | I only speak Spanish at home.                | adverb                               |
| Tengo solo diez minutos antes de la reunión.    | I only have ten minutes before the meeting.  | adverb                               |
| No quiero estar sola esta noche.                | I don't want to be alone tonight.            | adjective, feminine singular          |
