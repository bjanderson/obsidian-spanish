# arriba

## Meaning

*Arriba* means "up," "above," or "upstairs" — invariable, it describes a position
higher than a reference point, or motion in an upward direction with *hacia/para
arriba*. It's also used on its own as an exclamation of encouragement (*¡Arriba!*,
"Come on!"/"Up!").

## Core Uses

| Use                | Pattern             | Example                        | English                    |
| --------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Position                    | verb + arriba              | El gato está arriba.                  | The cat is upstairs.                |
| Motion upward                       | hacia/para arriba                      | Miró hacia arriba.                        | He looked up.                          |
| Exclamation                                 | ¡Arriba!                                       | ¡Arriba México!                                | Go Mexico!                                 |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                            | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Los niños duermen arriba.                   | The kids sleep upstairs.                   | position                           |
