# asimismo

## Meaning

*Asimismo* means "likewise" or "also" — a formal connector that adds a further point,
similar to *también* but more common in written or formal speech than in casual
conversation. Near-synonym [[igualmente]] in its "likewise/also" sense.

## Examples

| Spanish                                                  | English                                              | Notes                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| El informe señala, asimismo, varios problemas de seguridad. | The report also points out several safety problems.     | mid-sentence, formal          |
| Asimismo, se recomienda revisar el contrato.                 | Likewise, it's recommended to review the contract.       | clause-initial, formal        |
| El director agradeció al equipo y, asimismo, anunció nuevos proyectos. | The director thanked the team and, likewise, announced new projects. | joining two clauses    |
| Asimismo, quiero mencionar que el plazo se extendió.         | I'd also like to mention that the deadline was extended. | formal announcement           |
| Los precios subieron; asimismo, la demanda bajó.             | Prices went up; likewise, demand went down.              | cause/effect pairing          |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[igualmente]] — a more casual near-synonym for "likewise/also," and the only one of
  the two that also works as a stand-alone courtesy reply.
