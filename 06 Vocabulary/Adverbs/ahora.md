# ahora

## Meaning

*Ahora* means "now, at this moment." *Ahora mismo* emphasizes immediacy ("right
now"), and *ahora bien* is a more formal connector meaning "now then/however."

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                       | Notes                        |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Ahora no puedo hablar.                               | I can't talk right now.                             | statement                         |
| ¿Qué haces ahora?                                    | What are you doing now?                             | question                          |
| Antes vivía en México; ahora vive aquí.              | He used to live in Mexico; now he lives here.       | contrast with past                |
| Hazlo ahora mismo, por favor.                        | Do it right now, please.                            | urgency                           |
| No entiendo por qué cambiaste de opinión ahora.      | I don't understand why you changed your mind now.   | negation                          |
