# apenas

## Meaning

*Apenas* means "barely," "hardly," or "just barely" — it marks that something
happened to a minimal degree or with difficulty. As a temporal conjunction, it can
also mean "as soon as": *Apenas llegó, empezó a llover* ("As soon as he arrived, it
started to rain").

## Core Uses

| Use                    | Pattern              | Example                          | English                        |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Barely/hardly              | apenas + verb            | Apenas duermo estos días.             | I barely sleep these days.           |
| Just (a small amount)          | apenas + [quantity]           | Tiene apenas quince años.               | She's barely fifteen years old.        |
| As soon as                        | apenas + [verb], ...              | Apenas llegué, sonó el teléfono.        | As soon as I arrived, the phone rang.   |

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                       | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Apenas puedo caminar después de la carrera.     | I can barely walk after the race.                    | barely, difficulty             |

| Apenas tengo dinero para el autobús.            | I barely have money for the bus.                       | barely, small quantity           |
