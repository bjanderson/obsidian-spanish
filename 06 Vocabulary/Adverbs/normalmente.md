# normalmente

## Meaning

*Normalmente* means "normally" or "usually" — virtually interchangeable with
*generalmente* in everyday speech. If there's a nuance, *normalmente* leans slightly
toward "what's expected/typical," while *generalmente* leans toward "what happens in
most cases" — but native speakers use them freely as synonyms.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *normal* ([[normal]]) + *-mente*.
- Often used to contrast a routine with an exception: *Normalmente hago X, pero hoy...*

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes                  |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Normalmente me despierto a las seis.                 | I normally wake up at six.                    | before the verb            |
| ¿A qué hora normalmente cierran la tienda?           | What time do they normally close the store?  | question                   |
| Normalmente no como carne los viernes.                | I don't normally eat meat on Fridays.          | with a negative            |
| Ella normalmente llega temprano al trabajo.           | She normally arrives early at work.            | before the verb            |
| Las clases normalmente empiezan en agosto.            | Classes normally start in August.              | with a routine/schedule    |
