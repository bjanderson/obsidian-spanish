# adelante

## Meaning

*Adelante* means "forward" or "ahead" — movement or position toward the front. It's
also the word for "Come in!"/"Go ahead!" when someone knocks or asks permission
(*¡Adelante!*). *De aquí en adelante* means "from now on," and *más adelante* means
"later on"/"further ahead," in time or space. Its opposite in this batch is [[atrás]]
("behind"); together with [[abajo]], [[dentro]], and [[fuera]] it forms this batch's
spatial/directional cluster.
