# cerca

## Meaning

*Cerca* means "near" or "close by" — invariable, it describes physical (or sometimes
emotional) distance. Followed by *de* + a noun, it means "near" that specific place
or thing: *cerca de la escuela* ("near the school").

## Core Uses

| Use                     | Pattern                | Example                                | English                             |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Standalone                       | verb + cerca                    | Vivo cerca.                                    | I live nearby.                               |
| With a specific place                    | cerca de + [noun]                        | Está cerca del parque.                              | It's near the park.                              |
| Emotional closeness                              | cerca de + [person]                                | Siempre se siente cerca de su familia.                   | She always feels close to her family.                  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| La farmacia queda cerca de aquí.             | The pharmacy is near here.                     | standard use                           |
| Vivimos cerca del centro de la ciudad.       | We live near downtown.                          | *cerca del* (contraction)                |
| ¿Hay un banco cerca?                         | Is there a bank nearby?                          | question, standalone                      |
| Se sentaron cerca de la ventana.             | They sat near the window.                         | with a place                               |
| Aunque viven lejos, se sienten cerca.        | Even though they live far apart, they feel close.  | contrast with *lejos*                        |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[arriba]] — another common spatial adverb, though describing height rather than distance.
