# constantemente

## Meaning

*Constantemente* means "constantly" or "continuously" — a repeated or ongoing action
without real interruption. It's stronger than [[frecuentemente]]: something frequent
happens often, something constant barely stops.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *constante* + *-mente*. No standalone vocabulary file
  exists yet for *constante* in `06 Vocabulary/Adjectives/`.
- Frequently pairs with the present progressive (*está + gerundio*) to emphasize an
  ongoing habit: *está cambiando constantemente*.

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                     | Notes              |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Mi teléfono suena constantemente durante el día.             | My phone rings constantly during the day.         | before the verb        |
| Ella constantemente revisa su correo electrónico.            | She constantly checks her email.                  | before the verb        |
| Los precios están cambiando constantemente.                  | Prices are constantly changing.                   | with present progressive |
| ¿Por qué interrumpes constantemente cuando hablo?             | Why do you constantly interrupt when I'm talking? | question                |
| No podemos vivir preocupados constantemente por el futuro.    | We can't live constantly worried about the future. | with an adjective     |
