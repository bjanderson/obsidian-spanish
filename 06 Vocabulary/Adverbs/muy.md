# muy

## Meaning

*Muy* is invariant and intensifies an adjective or another adverb — "very." It's
easy to confuse with *mucho* ("a lot," "much"), but they're not interchangeable:
*muy* goes directly before an adjective or adverb (*muy bonito*, "very pretty"),
while *mucho* goes with a noun or a verb (*mucho dinero*, "a lot of money"; *come
mucho*, "he eats a lot"). *Muy* is never used alone before a noun or a verb.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Este café está muy caliente.                | This coffee is very hot.               | before an adjective                  |
| Habla muy rápido.                            | He speaks very fast.                   | before an adverb                     |
| Estamos muy contentos con los resultados.    | We're very happy with the results.     | before an adjective                  |
| Ella corre muy bien.                          | She runs very well.                    | before an adverb                     |
| Vive muy cerca de aquí.                       | He lives very close to here.           | before an adverb                     |
