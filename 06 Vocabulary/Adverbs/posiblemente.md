# posiblemente

## Meaning

*Posiblemente* means "possibly" — the *-mente* adverb from *posible* ("possible"),
expressing a weaker degree of likelihood than [[probablemente]] ("probably"). Like
*probablemente*, placing it before the verb commonly triggers the subjunctive to
emphasize the uncertainty (*Posiblemente sea muy tarde*, "It might possibly be too
late"), while the indicative is also heard, especially after the verb or in casual
speech.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                | Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Posiblemente cambien los planes.           | Plans might possibly change.                 | subjunctive, before the verb      |
| Es posiblemente la mejor opción que tenemos.| It's possibly the best option we have.       | indicative                        |
| Posiblemente no haya suficiente tiempo.    | There possibly won't be enough time.         | subjunctive, negative              |
| Iré a la fiesta, posiblemente con un amigo.| I'll go to the party, possibly with a friend.| indicative, after the verb         |
| —¿Crees que ganará? —Posiblemente.         | "Do you think he'll win?" "Possibly."        | stand-alone response               |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[probablemente]] — stronger degree of certainty.
- [[quizás]] — near-synonym, slightly more informal
