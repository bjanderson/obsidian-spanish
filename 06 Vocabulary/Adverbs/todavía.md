# todavía

## Meaning

*Todavía* means "still" (an ongoing state or action) in affirmative sentences, and
"yet" in negative sentences (*todavía no*, "not yet"). The close synonym *aún* is
interchangeable in most contexts.

## Core Uses

| Use                                  | Pattern                    | Example                          | English                            |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Still (ongoing, affirmative)               | todavía + [verb]                    | Todavía vive en Bogotá.                     | She still lives in Bogotá.                   |
| Not yet (negative)                         | todavía no + [verb]                 | Todavía no ha llegado.                      | He hasn't arrived yet.                       |
| Emphasis with comparatives                 | todavía + más/menos                 | Es todavía más difícil que antes.           | It's even more difficult than before.        |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                        | Notes                        |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| ¿Todavía trabajas en esa empresa?                    | Do you still work at that company?                      | question                          |
| Todavía no hemos decidido qué hacer.                 | We haven't decided what to do yet.                      | negation                          |
| Mi abuela todavía cocina para toda la familia.       | My grandmother still cooks for the whole family.        | statement                         |
| Era tarde y todavía no habíamos comido.              | It was late and we still hadn't eaten.                  | negation                          |
| Este problema es todavía más complicado.             | This problem is even more complicated.                  | comparative emphasis              |
