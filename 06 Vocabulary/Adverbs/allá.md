# allá

## Meaning

*Allá* means "there" — farther away than [[ahí]] and vaguer than *allí*, pointing to a
distant or general area rather than one exact spot (*Vive allá, en el norte*, "He lives
over there, in the north"). *Más allá* means "beyond"/"further on" (*más allá de la
montaña*, "beyond the mountain"), and *el más allá*, used as a noun, means "the
afterlife"/"the beyond." The dismissive phrase *allá tú* ("that's your problem"/"suit
yourself") hands responsibility back to the other person. Part of this batch's
here/there system with [[acá]] and [[ahí]].

## Examples

| Spanish                                  | English                                  | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Mis abuelos viven allá, en el campo.        | My grandparents live over there, in the countryside. | distant location    |
| Vamos para allá en un rato.                 | We're heading over there in a bit.           | motion                       |
| Su casa queda más allá del río.             | His house is further on, beyond the river.   | *más allá de* + noun          |
| —No pienso disculparme. —Allá tú.           | "I'm not going to apologize." "Suit yourself." | idiom, dismissive           |
| Nadie sabe qué hay en el más allá.          | Nobody knows what's in the afterlife.        | *el más allá* as a noun       |
