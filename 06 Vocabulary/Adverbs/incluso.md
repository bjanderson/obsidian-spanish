# incluso

## Meaning

*Incluso* means "even" — it highlights a surprising or extreme case within a broader
statement. Right before a noun, it can also mean "including." Don't confuse it with
*además*, which simply adds more information without implying anything surprising.

## Examples

| Spanish                                                  | English                                                   | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Incluso los niños entendieron la explicación.                | Even the children understood the explanation.                    | emphasizing an extreme case      |
| Trabaja los fines de semana, incluso los domingos.            | He works on weekends, even Sundays.                               | "including"                       |
| Todos vinieron a la fiesta, incluso mi jefe.                  | Everyone came to the party, even my boss.                          | surprising inclusion               |
| Incluso si llueve, vamos a salir.                             | Even if it rains, we're going out.                                  | *incluso si* + conditional          |
| Invité a todos los vecinos, incluso al que nunca habla con nadie. | I invited all the neighbors, even the one who never talks to anyone. | personal *a* (*al que*)             |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[además]] — a milder way to add information, without the "surprising" edge of *incluso*.
