# evidentemente

## Meaning

*Evidentemente* means "evidently" or "clearly" — it points to visible evidence or a
logical conclusion that's plain to see. It comes from *evidente* + *-mente*. Close in
meaning to [[obviamente]], though a touch more formal, and often used to introduce a
conclusion drawn from evidence.

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Evidentemente, alguien olvidó cerrar la puerta.    | Evidently, someone forgot to close the door.  | clause-initial                |
| El plan, evidentemente, no funcionó.               | The plan evidently didn't work.               | mid-sentence                  |
| Evidentemente no leyeron las instrucciones.        | They evidently didn't read the instructions.  | no comma                      |
| ¿Está enojado? — Evidentemente.                    | Is he angry? — Evidently.                     | short answer                  |
| Evidentemente, hace falta más práctica.            | Evidently, more practice is needed.           | impersonal statement          |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[obviamente]], [[ciertamente]] — near-synonyms expressing certainty/evidentness.
