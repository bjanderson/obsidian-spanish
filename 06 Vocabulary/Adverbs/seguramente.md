# seguramente

## Meaning

*Seguramente* means "surely" or "most likely" — it expresses strong probability, not
absolute certainty, despite looking like it should mean "for sure." It's a sentence
adverb: it comments on how likely the whole statement is, and can sit at the start,
middle, or end of a sentence.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *seguro* ([[seguro-segura-seguros-seguras]]) + *-mente*.
- Don't confuse the strength of *seguramente* with *seguro que* ("for sure that") —
  *seguramente* is a notch softer, closer to "probably" than a flat guarantee.

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                     | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Seguramente Ana ya llegó a casa.                    | Ana has surely already arrived home.           | sentence-initial                 |
| Él seguramente ya sabe la noticia.                  | He surely already knows the news.              | before the verb                  |
| Va a llover, seguramente, antes de la noche.        | It's surely going to rain before nightfall.    | mid-sentence, set off by commas  |
| Seguramente no fue fácil para ella.                 | It surely wasn't easy for her.                 | with a negative                  |
| Vamos a llegar tarde, seguramente, por el tráfico.  | We're surely going to arrive late because of the traffic. | mid-sentence |
