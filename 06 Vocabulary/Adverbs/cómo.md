# cómo

## Meaning

*Cómo* (accented) is the interrogative/exclamative "how" — used in direct
and indirect questions (*¿Cómo estás?*, "How are you?"; *No sé cómo
llegó*, "I don't know how he got here") and in exclamations (*¡Cómo
llueve!*, "It's really pouring!"). It's also the standard way to ask
someone to repeat themselves (*¿Cómo?*, "What/Sorry?").

Don't confuse it with unaccented [[como]], the separate conjunction/adverb
covering comparison ("like/as"), causal reason ("since"), and manner
("the way") — the accent is the only thing that tells them apart in
writing: *Hazlo como quieras* ("Do it however you want," unaccented
manner) versus *¿Cómo lo quieres?* ("How do you want it?," accented
question). This puts *cómo* in the same interrogative cluster as
[[cuánto-cuánta-cuántos-cuántas|cuánto]] and [[quién]] — all three always
carry the accent when used to ask or exclaim.

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                            | Notes                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| ¿Cómo te llamas?                          | What's your name?                        | direct question                  |
| No entiendo cómo funciona esto.           | I don't understand how this works.       | indirect question                |
| ¡Cómo ha crecido tu hijo!                 | Your son has grown so much!              | exclamation                     |
| ¿Cómo llegaste tan rápido?                | How did you get here so fast?            | direct question                  |
| Dime cómo lo hiciste.                     | Tell me how you did it.                  | indirect question                |
