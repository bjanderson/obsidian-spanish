# temprano

## Meaning

As an **adverb**, *temprano* is invariable and means "early": *Se despierta temprano*
("She wakes up early"). As an **adjective**, it agrees in gender and number —
*temprano*, *temprana*, *tempranos*, *tempranas* — and describes something that
arrives or happens ahead of the usual time: *una fruta temprana* ("an early-ripening
fruit"), *síntomas tempranos* ("early symptoms"). The adverb use is far more common
in everyday speech.
