# luego

## Meaning

*Luego* means "then/afterward" (sequencing events) or "later" (at a future point).
*Desde luego* is a fixed phrase meaning "of course," and *hasta luego* is the common
farewell "see you later."

## Core Uses

| Use                              | Pattern                | Example                        | English                        |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Sequence ("then, next")               | primero..., luego...           | Primero como, luego trabajo.             | First I eat, then I work.               |
| Later (future time)                   | luego + [time reference]       | Te llamo luego.                          | I'll call you later.                    |
| Fixed phrase ("of course")            | desde luego                    | Desde luego que puedes venir.            | Of course you can come.                 |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                       | Notes                        |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Terminamos la reunión y luego almorzamos.        | We finished the meeting and then had lunch.          | sequence                          |
| ¿Puedo llamarte luego?                           | Can I call you later?                                | question                          |
| No lo hagas ahora; hazlo luego.                  | Don't do it now; do it later.                        | negative command                  |
| Desde luego que iremos a la fiesta.              | Of course we'll go to the party.                     | fixed phrase                      |
| Luego de eso, no volvimos a hablar.              | After that, we didn't speak again.                   | negation                          |
