# ciertamente

## Meaning

*Ciertamente* means "certainly" — it affirms that something is true or confirms a point,
often in a reply or to underline agreement. It comes from *cierto* + *-mente*.
Near-synonym of [[obviamente]] and [[evidentemente]], but leans toward affirmation/
agreement rather than pointing at evidence.

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                    | Notes                        |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Ciertamente, este ha sido un año difícil.            | Certainly, this has been a difficult year.    | clause-initial                |
| Es, ciertamente, una de las mejores películas del año. | It's certainly one of the best movies of the year. | mid-sentence            |
| ¿Estás de acuerdo? — Ciertamente.                    | Do you agree? — Certainly.                    | short answer                  |
| Ciertamente no esperaba esa respuesta.               | I certainly wasn't expecting that answer.     | no comma                      |
| Ciertamente, hay mucho por mejorar.                  | Certainly, there's a lot to improve.          | impersonal statement          |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[obviamente]], [[evidentemente]] — near-synonyms expressing certainty/evidentness.
