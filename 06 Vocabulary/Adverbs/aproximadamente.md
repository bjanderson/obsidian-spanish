# aproximadamente

## Meaning

*Aproximadamente* means "approximately" or "about" — the *-mente* adverb from
*aproximado* ("approximate"), used to estimate a quantity, time, or distance rather than
state it precisely. It's the estimating counterpart to [[exactamente]] ("exactly") in
this batch.

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes            |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Llegamos en aproximadamente una hora.             | We'll arrive in about an hour.               | time                  |
| Cuesta aproximadamente doscientos dólares.        | It costs approximately two hundred dollars.  | quantity               |
| Había aproximadamente cien personas en la reunión.| There were approximately a hundred people at the meeting. | quantity  |
| Vivo aproximadamente a diez minutos de aquí.      | I live approximately ten minutes from here.  | distance               |
| El vuelo dura aproximadamente tres horas.         | The flight takes approximately three hours.  | time                   |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[exactamente]] — contrast: estimation vs. precision.
