# allí

## Meaning

*Allí* means "there," referring to a place away from both speaker and listener — a
middle distance between *ahí* (near the listener) and *allá* (farther away, more
vague). Often used with *estar* or verbs of location and movement.

## Examples

| Spanish                              | English                              | Notes                        |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| El banco está allí, cruzando la calle.     | The bank is there, across the street.        | statement                         |
| Nos vemos allí a las ocho.                 | We'll see each other there at eight.         | statement                         |
| ¿Quién dejó las llaves allí?               | Who left the keys there?                     | question                          |
| No dejes tus cosas allí.                   | Don't leave your things there.               | negative command                  |
| Vivieron allí muchos años.                 | They lived there for many years.             | statement                         |
