# debajo

## Meaning

*Debajo* means "underneath" or "below." On its own it works as a plain adverb of place:
*El perro duerme debajo* ("The dog sleeps underneath"). Followed by *de* + a noun, it
means "underneath"/"under" that thing: *debajo de la mesa* ("under the table"). Opposite:
[[encima]].

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Los zapatos están debajo, cerca de la puerta.       | The shoes are underneath, near the door.    | standalone adverb                  |
| El perro se escondió debajo de la cama.            | The dog hid under the bed.                  | *debajo de* + noun                 |
| Guardamos las cajas debajo de la escalera.          | We store the boxes under the stairs.        | *debajo de* + noun                 |
| Firmó su nombre debajo de la fecha.                 | He signed his name below the date.          | *debajo de*, figurative/spatial    |
| Hay un río que pasa por debajo del puente.          | There's a river that runs beneath the bridge. | *por debajo de*                 |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[encima]] — the opposite: "on top/above."
