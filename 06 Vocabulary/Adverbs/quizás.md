# quizás

## Meaning

*Quizás* means "maybe" or "perhaps" — it expresses uncertainty about a statement. It
can go at the start of a sentence or right before the verb. The verb after *quizás*
can be indicative (when the speaker leans toward it being true) or subjunctive (when
the doubt is stronger) — both are common in everyday speech. The shorter variant
*quizá* (without the *s*) means exactly the same thing.

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                  | Notes                          |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Quizás llueva mañana.                            | Maybe it will rain tomorrow.                    | subjunctive, stronger doubt             |
| Quizás viene más tarde.                          | Maybe he's coming later.                        | indicative, more confident               |
| — ¿Vas a la fiesta? — Quizás.                    | "Are you going to the party?" "Maybe."           | standalone reply                          |
| Quizás deberías hablar con ella primero.         | Maybe you should talk to her first.               | suggestion                                 |
| Llegaremos quizás a las ocho.                    | We'll arrive maybe around eight.                  | mid-sentence                                |
