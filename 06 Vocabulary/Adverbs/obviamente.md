# obviamente

## Meaning

*Obviamente* means "obviously" — it signals that what follows is self-evident or expected
given the context. It comes from *obvio* + *-mente*. Near-synonym of [[evidentemente]] and
[[ciertamente]], though *obviamente* leans more casual/conversational than the other two.

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                        | Notes                        |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Obviamente, no sabía que ibas a venir.              | Obviously, I didn't know you were coming.          | clause-initial                |
| Ella obviamente no está de acuerdo.                 | She's obviously not in agreement.                  | mid-sentence, before verb     |
| ¿Vienes a la fiesta? — Obviamente.                  | Are you coming to the party? — Obviously.          | short answer                  |
| No pudimos terminar el proyecto, obviamente, sin tu ayuda. | We obviously couldn't finish the project without your help. | mid-sentence, commas |
| Obviamente hay cosas que todavía no entiendo.       | Obviously there are things I still don't understand. | no comma                   |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[evidentemente]], [[ciertamente]] — near-synonyms expressing certainty/evidentness.
- [[lamentablemente]] — same family of sentence-initial *-mente* adverbs, though a
  different meaning (regret rather than certainty).
