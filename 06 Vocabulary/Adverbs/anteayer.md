# anteayer

## Meaning

*Anteayer* is an invariable adverb meaning "the day before yesterday" — a
fusion of *ante-* ("before") and *ayer* ("yesterday"). *Antes de ayer* means
exactly the same thing and is just as common; the two are interchangeable.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Anteayer visité a mis abuelos.              | The day before yesterday I visited my grandparents. | statement, personal *a*     |
| ¿Nos vimos anteayer o antes?                | Did we see each other the day before yesterday or earlier? | question              |
| Anteayer no trabajé.                        | I didn't work the day before yesterday. | negative                           |
| Desde anteayer no he hablado con él.        | Since the day before yesterday I haven't spoken with him. | after a preposition, present perfect |
| Compré esto anteayer, no ayer.              | I bought this the day before yesterday, not yesterday. | contrast with *ayer*         |
