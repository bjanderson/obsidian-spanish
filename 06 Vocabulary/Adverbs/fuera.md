# fuera

## Meaning

*Fuera* means "outside" — the opposite of [[dentro]]. Followed by *de*, it locates
something outside a place: *fuera de la casa* ("outside the house"). It also covers
"away"/"out of town" (*Está fuera esta semana*, "She's out of town this week") and shows
up in set phrases like *fuera de sí* ("beside oneself," extremely upset) and the sports
term *fuera de juego* ("offside"). Usage note: in everyday Latin American speech, a
standalone location ("outside," as a place you'd point to) is more often said with
*afuera*; *fuera* is the safe, universal choice in the *fuera de* + noun construction and
in idioms — that's the pattern to lean on. Part of this batch's spatial/directional
cluster with [[abajo]], [[adelante]], and [[atrás]].
