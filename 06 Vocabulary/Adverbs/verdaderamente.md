# verdaderamente

## Meaning

*Verdaderamente* means "truly" or "really" — it emphasizes the genuineness or high
degree of whatever it modifies. It's a bit more formal/emphatic than plain *muy* or
*realmente*.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *verdadero* + *-mente*. No standalone vocabulary file
  exists yet for *verdadero* in `06 Vocabulary/Adjectives/`.
- Part of the same "stance adverb" family as [[seguramente]] and [[sinceramente]] —
  all three comment on how the speaker stands behind a statement.

## Examples

| Spanish                                             | English                                  | Notes            |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Fue verdaderamente una sorpresa verte aquí.                    | It was truly a surprise to see you here.          | before a noun           |
| El paisaje es verdaderamente hermoso.                           | The landscape is truly beautiful.                | before an adjective     |
| Verdaderamente no sé qué decir.                                 | I truly don't know what to say.                  | sentence-initial, negated |
| ¿Estás verdaderamente contento con la decisión?                  | Are you truly happy with the decision?           | question                |
| Nos ayudó verdaderamente cuando más lo necesitábamos.            | He truly helped us when we needed it most.       | before the verb          |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[seguramente]] — related, marks confidence rather than genuineness
- [[sinceramente]] — related, marks honesty rather than genuineness
- [[realmente]] — near-synonym, also intensifies with a sense of genuineness
