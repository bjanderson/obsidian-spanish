# ayer

## Meaning

*Ayer* is an invariable adverb meaning "yesterday." Combine it with other time
words for more precision: *ayer por la mañana* ("yesterday morning"), *ayer por
la noche* ("last night"). *Antes de ayer* is an alternate way to say "the day
before yesterday," alongside the single word [[anteayer]].

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                          | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Ayer fui al mercado.                        | Yesterday I went to the market.        | statement, preterite               |
| ¿Qué hiciste ayer?                          | What did you do yesterday?             | question                           |
| Ayer no vi a Marta.                         | I didn't see Marta yesterday.          | negative, personal *a*              |
| Ayer por la mañana llovió mucho.            | Yesterday morning it rained a lot.     | combined time expression            |
| Hasta ayer no sabía la noticia.             | Until yesterday I didn't know the news. | after a preposition                |
