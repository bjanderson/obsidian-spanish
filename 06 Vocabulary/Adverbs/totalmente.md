# totalmente

## Meaning

*Totalmente* means "totally" or "completely" — full degree, no partial version. It's
a near-synonym of [[completamente]] and is very common in casual speech to intensify
agreement, disagreement, or a strong reaction.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *total* + *-mente*. No standalone vocabulary file exists
  yet for *total* in `06 Vocabulary/Adjectives/`.
- Opposite of [[parcialmente]] on the totality scale.

## Examples

| Spanish                                             | English                                    | Notes             |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Estoy totalmente de acuerdo contigo.                        | I totally agree with you.                          | before an adjective    |
| La casa quedó totalmente destruida después de la tormenta.  | The house was left totally destroyed after the storm. | with a past participle |
| No es totalmente cierto lo que dijo el periódico.           | What the newspaper said isn't totally true.        | with a negative        |
| ¿Estás totalmente seguro de esto?                            | Are you totally sure about this?                   | question                |
| El plan cambió totalmente a última hora.                     | The plan totally changed at the last minute.       | after the verb          |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[completamente]] — near-synonym, slightly more formal register
- [[parcialmente]] — antonym, the low end of the totality scale
