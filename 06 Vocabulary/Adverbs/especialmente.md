# especialmente

## Meaning

*Especialmente* means "especially" — it singles out one case for emphasis within a
broader statement. It's a near-synonym of [[particularmente]].

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *especial* ([[especial]]) + *-mente*.
- Usually comes right before the thing being singled out: *especialmente los fines
  de semana*, *especialmente en julio*.

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                        | Notes           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Me gusta la comida picante, especialmente el mole.             | I like spicy food, especially mole.                      | end of sentence      |
| Hace calor todo el año, especialmente en julio.                | It's hot all year, especially in July.                    | end of sentence      |
| Especialmente los fines de semana, el tráfico es terrible.      | Especially on weekends, traffic is terrible.               | sentence-initial     |
| No confío en nadie, especialmente en los desconocidos.          | I don't trust anyone, especially strangers.                | end of sentence      |
| ¿Por qué te preocupa especialmente este examen?                  | Why does this exam especially worry you?                   | question             |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[particularmente]] — near-synonym, slightly more formal register
- [[principalmente]] — related, but marks the *main* case rather than a *notable* one
