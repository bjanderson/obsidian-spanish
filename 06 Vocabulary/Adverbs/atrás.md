# atrás

## Meaning

*Atrás* means "behind" or "backward." *Hacia atrás* means "backward" as a direction
(*dar un paso hacia atrás*, "take a step backward"). After a time expression, it means
"ago" (*años atrás*, "years ago") — *hace* + time is the more common everyday way to say
"ago," but *atrás* is heard the same way, placed after the time expression. *Quedarse
atrás* means "to fall behind." Opposite of [[adelante]]; part of this batch's
spatial/directional cluster with [[abajo]], [[dentro]], and [[fuera]].

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                               | Notes            |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------ |
| Dejamos el pueblo atrás.                     | We left the town behind.                   | "behind"            |
| Dio un paso hacia atrás antes de responder.  | She took a step backward before answering. | *hacia atrás*        |
| Eso pasó varios años atrás.                  | That happened several years ago.           | time, "ago"          |
| No quiero quedarme atrás en la clase.        | I don't want to fall behind in class.      | idiom                |
| Mira hacia atrás antes de cruzar.            | Look behind you before crossing.           | *hacia atrás*        |
