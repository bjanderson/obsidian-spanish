# generalmente

## Meaning

*Generalmente* means "generally" or "usually" — it describes what typically happens,
as a habit or a pattern, rather than what's happening right now. It's essentially
interchangeable with *normalmente* in everyday speech.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *general* ([[general]]) + *-mente*.
- Pairs naturally with time expressions like *los domingos*, *entre semana*, or *por
  la mañana* to describe a routine.

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                     | Notes                |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Generalmente como pan y café en el desayuno.             | I generally eat bread and coffee for breakfast.   | before the verb         |
| Los domingos, generalmente, vamos a la casa de mis abuelos. | On Sundays we generally go to my grandparents' house. | mid-sentence     |
| ¿Generalmente trabajas los fines de semana?               | Do you generally work on weekends?               | question                |
| Generalmente no salgo de noche entre semana.              | I generally don't go out at night during the week. | with a negative       |
| El clima aquí es generalmente cálido.                     | The weather here is generally warm.               | with *ser*               |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[normalmente]] — near-synonym, both describe a habitual pattern
- [[frecuentemente]] — related frequency adverb, describes how often rather than what's typical
