# exactamente

## Meaning

*Exactamente* means "exactly" — the *-mente* adverb from *exacto* ("exact"), used for
precise amounts, times, or agreement (*—¿Estás de acuerdo? —Exactamente.*, "You agree?"
"Exactly."). It's the precise counterpart to [[aproximadamente]] ("approximately") in
this batch — the two are often used together to bracket a range or to contrast
precision with estimation.

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes            |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------ |
| Son exactamente las tres de la tarde.           | It's exactly three in the afternoon.       | time                  |
| No sé exactamente dónde vive.                   | I don't know exactly where he lives.       | precision              |
| Eso es exactamente lo que quería decir.         | That's exactly what I meant to say.        | precision              |
| Cuesta exactamente cien pesos.                  | It costs exactly one hundred pesos.        | quantity               |
| —¿Entonces estamos de acuerdo? —Exactamente.    | "So we agree?" "Exactly."                  | stand-alone response    |
