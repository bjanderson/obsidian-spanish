# simplemente

## Meaning

*Simplemente* means "simply" or "just" — it strips away complexity from an
explanation, or means "nothing more than" before a reason or a description.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *simple* ([[simple]]) + *-mente*.
- Close in meaning to [[únicamente]] when it means "just" in a restrictive sense
  (*simplemente porque quise* ≈ "only/just because I wanted to"), but *simplemente*
  is broader and also works as a plain intensifier (*es simplemente delicioso*).

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                    | Notes             |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| No quiero discutir, simplemente quiero descansar.        | I don't want to argue, I simply want to rest.       | before the verb          |
| Simplemente no tuve tiempo de llamarte.                   | I simply didn't have time to call you.              | sentence-initial, negated |
| ¿Por qué lo hiciste? Simplemente porque quise.             | Why did you do it? Simply because I wanted to.      | short answer              |
| La receta es simplemente deliciosa.                        | The recipe is simply delicious.                    | intensifying an adjective |
| No es complicado, simplemente sigue las instrucciones.     | It's not complicated, just follow the instructions. | with a command            |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[únicamente]] — near-synonym in the restrictive "only/just" sense
