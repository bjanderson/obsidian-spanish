# raramente

## Meaning

*Raramente* means "rarely" or "seldom" — a degree between *nunca* ("never") and
*a veces* ("sometimes"). It's more common in writing than in casual speech, where
*casi nunca* is more typical, but it's perfectly natural in either.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *raro* ([[raro-rara-raros-raras]]) + *-mente*. Note the
  meaning shift: *raro* usually means "strange" or "unusual," but *raramente* keeps
  only the "unusual [frequency]" sense — "rarely," not "strangely."
- Antonym of [[frecuentemente]] and [[constantemente]].

## Examples

| Spanish                                                 | English                                              | Notes            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Raramente salgo sin paraguas cuando está nublado.              | I rarely go out without an umbrella when it's cloudy.        | sentence-initial     |
| Mi abuelo raramente se queja de algo.                           | My grandfather rarely complains about anything.              | before the verb      |
| ¿Raramente vas al cine, o simplemente no te gusta?              | Do you rarely go to the movies, or do you simply not like them? | question          |
| Raramente hace tanto frío aquí en diciembre.                    | It's rarely this cold here in December.                       | with weather verbs   |
| Ese problema raramente ocurre en el sistema nuevo.               | That problem rarely occurs in the new system.                | before the verb      |
