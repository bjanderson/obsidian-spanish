# dentro

## Meaning

*Dentro* means "inside" — something contained within a space, or, with a time
expression, within a stretch of time still to come. Followed by *de* + a noun, it
locates something inside that thing: *dentro de la caja* ("inside the box"). With a time
expression, *dentro de* means "in"/"within," counting forward from now: *dentro de una
semana* ("in a week"). Its opposite in this batch is [[fuera]] ("outside"); together with
[[abajo]], [[adelante]], and [[atrás]] it forms this batch's spatial/directional cluster.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                     |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Los documentos están dentro del cajón.  | The documents are inside the drawer.   | *dentro del* (de + el)      |
| Guardé el dinero dentro de la mochila.  | I put the money inside the backpack.   | *dentro de* + noun           |
| Volvemos dentro de dos horas.           | We'll be back in two hours.            | time expression              |
| ¿Hay algo dentro de esta caja?          | Is there something inside this box?    | question                     |
| Prefiero quedarme dentro cuando llueve. | I prefer to stay inside when it rains. | standalone                   |
