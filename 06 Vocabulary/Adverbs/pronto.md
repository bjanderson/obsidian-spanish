# pronto

## Meaning

*Pronto* means "soon" — invariable in this adverb sense, it's one of the most common
ways to talk about the near future: *Vuelvo pronto* ("I'll be back soon"). It also
appears in the common farewell *hasta pronto* ("see you soon"). As an adjective, it
can rarely describe something as "quick/prompt" (*una pronta respuesta* — "a prompt
reply"), agreeing in gender and number, but this use is far less common than
*rápido*.

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes                          |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Espero verte pronto.                         | I hope to see you soon.                        | standard adverb use                     |
| Tan pronto como termine, te aviso.           | As soon as I finish, I'll let you know.          | *tan pronto como* + verb                  |
| ¡Hasta pronto!                               | See you soon!                                    | farewell                                    |
| Se recuperará pronto de la operación.        | He'll recover soon from the surgery.               | future tense                                  |
| Necesito una respuesta pronta a esta pregunta. | I need a prompt reply to this question.           | rare adjective use, feminine singular            |
