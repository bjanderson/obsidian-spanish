# lejos

## Meaning

*Lejos* means "far" or "far away" — invariable, the direct opposite of *cerca*.
Followed by *de* + a noun, it means "far from" that specific place: *lejos de la
ciudad* ("far from the city"). It also appears in the fixed phrase *lejos de* +
infinitive, meaning "far from doing" something.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                    | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| La playa está lejos de aquí.                | The beach is far from here.                         | standard use                       |
| Vive lejos de su familia por el trabajo.    | She lives far from her family because of work.        | with a person                        |
| No quiero ir tan lejos hoy.                 | I don't want to go so far today.                       | intensified with *tan*                 |
| Lejos de ayudar, complicó todo.             | Far from helping, he complicated everything.             | *lejos de* + infinitive                  |
| Desde lejos, la casa parece más pequeña.    | From far away, the house looks smaller.                   | *desde lejos*                              |
