# además

## Meaning

*Además* adds extra information to what was just said — "besides," "also,"
"moreover," "on top of that." It's used standalone at the start or end of a sentence,
or with *además de* + a noun or infinitive to mean "besides/in addition to" something
specific.

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                            | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| El apartamento es barato; además, está cerca del metro. | The apartment is cheap; besides, it's near the subway. | standalone, adding a point    |
| Además de cocinar, ella limpia toda la casa.        | Besides cooking, she cleans the whole house.            | *además de* + infinitive      |
| No tengo tiempo, y además no tengo dinero.          | I don't have time, and besides I don't have money.       | joining two reasons            |
| Además del inglés, habla portugués.                 | Besides English, she speaks Portuguese.                  | *además del* + noun (contraction) |
| ¿Vas a invitar a alguien más además de tus primos? | Are you going to invite anyone else besides your cousins? | question                       |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[incluso]] — a stronger way to add an unexpected or extreme example ("even"), where *además* just adds more information.
