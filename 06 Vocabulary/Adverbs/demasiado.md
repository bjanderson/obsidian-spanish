# demasiado

## Meaning

As an **adverb**, *demasiado* is invariable and means "too" — it intensifies an
adjective or another adverb past a reasonable point: *Es demasiado caro* ("It's too
expensive"). As an **adjective/quantifier**, it agrees in gender and number with the
noun it describes — *demasiado*, *demasiada*, *demasiados*, *demasiadas* — and means
"too much" or "too many": *demasiada gente* ("too many people"), *demasiados
problemas* ("too many problems").

## Full Paradigm (adjective use)

| Gender / Number   | Form       |
| -------------------- | ------------ |
| Masculine singular      | demasiado    |
| Feminine singular        | demasiada    |
| Masculine plural           | demasiados   |
| Feminine plural              | demasiadas   |
