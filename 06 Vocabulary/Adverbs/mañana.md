# mañana

## Meaning

*Mañana* has two common senses. As an invariable adverb, it means "tomorrow":
*Nos vemos mañana* ("See you tomorrow"). As a feminine noun (*la mañana*), it
means "morning" and pluralizes regularly (*las mañanas*): *Trabajo por la
mañana* ("I work in the morning"). Context tells them apart — the adverb
stands alone or times a verb, while the noun takes an article (*la*, *esta*,
*toda la*) or a preposition (*por la mañana*, *de la mañana*).
