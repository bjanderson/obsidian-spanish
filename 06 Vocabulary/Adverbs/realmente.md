# realmente

## Meaning

*Realmente* means "really" or "truly" — it intensifies an adjective, a verb, or an
entire sentence, often to express genuine surprise or emphasis. It can also soften a
reply into a question of disbelief: "¿Realmente?" ("Really?"). Formed regularly from
the adjective *real* + *-mente*, like most Spanish manner adverbs.

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                   | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Esta película es realmente increíble.         | This movie is really incredible.               | intensifying an adjective       |
| Realmente no sé qué hacer.                    | I really don't know what to do.                | intensifying a whole statement  |
| ¿Realmente crees que va a funcionar?           | Do you really think it's going to work?         | question                         |
| Ella trabaja realmente duro.                  | She works really hard.                          | intensifying a verb phrase        |
| — Me ofrecieron el puesto. — ¿Realmente?      | "They offered me the position." "Really?"       | short reaction                     |
