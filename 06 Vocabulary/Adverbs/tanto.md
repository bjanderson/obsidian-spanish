# tanto

## Meaning

As an adverb, *tanto* means "so much" — it intensifies a verb: *Trabaja tanto
que nunca descansa* ("He works so much that he never rests"). It also drives
the comparison pattern *tanto... como...* ("as much... as..."/"both... and..."):
*Estudia tanto como trabaja* ("She studies as much as she works").

*Tanto* is also an adjective (*tanto*, *tanta*, *tantos*, *tantas*, "so much"/
"so many"), agreeing with the noun it describes: *tanta gente* ("so many
people"). Right before an adjective or another adverb, it shortens to *tan*:
*tan bonito* ("so pretty"), not *tanto bonito*.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| No hables tanto durante la clase.           | Don't talk so much during class.         | adverb, modifying a verb            |
| Me gusta tanto como a ti.                    | I like it as much as you do.             | *tanto... como...* comparison       |
| Había tanta gente en la plaza.              | There were so many people in the square. | adjective, feminine singular         |
| ¿Por qué te preocupas tanto?                | Why do you worry so much?                | adverb, question                    |
| Es tan tarde que ya no vale la pena salir.   | It's so late that it's not worth going out anymore. | shortened form *tan* + adjective |
