# felizmente

## Meaning

*Felizmente* has two senses depending on position. Right after a verb, it's a manner
adverb meaning "happily" (*jugaban felizmente*, "they were playing happily"). At the
start of a sentence, it works as a sentence adverb meaning "fortunately," a synonym
of [[afortunadamente]] (*Felizmente, nadie salió herido*, "Fortunately, no one was
hurt").

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *feliz* ([[feliz]]) + *-mente*.
- Antonym of [[tristemente]] in the "manner" sense (happily vs. sadly).

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                    | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Los niños jugaban felizmente en el parque.                | The children were playing happily in the park.    | manner, after the verb          |
| Felizmente, nadie salió herido en el accidente.           | Fortunately, no one was hurt in the accident.      | sentence adverb, "fortunately"  |
| Vivieron felizmente durante muchos años.                   | They lived happily for many years.                 | manner, after the verb          |
| Felizmente, encontramos las llaves antes de irnos.         | Fortunately, we found the keys before we left.      | sentence adverb, "fortunately"  |
| Recuerdo felizmente aquellos veranos en la playa.          | I happily remember those summers at the beach.      | manner, before the object       |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[tristemente]] — antonym in the manner sense (happily vs. sadly)
- [[afortunadamente]] — synonym in the sentence-adverb sense ("fortunately")
