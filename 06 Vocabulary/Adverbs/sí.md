# sí

## Meaning

*Sí* (with an accent) is the everyday word for "yes." Don't confuse it with *si*
(no accent), which means "if" and introduces a conditional clause — the accent mark
is the only thing that tells them apart in writing; they sound identical.

*Sí* is also, separately, a reflexive prepositional pronoun ("himself/herself/
themselves," used after a preposition): *Habla de sí mismo* ("He talks about
himself"). That's a different word functioning as a different part of speech, even
though the spelling is identical.

## Core Uses

| Use                    | Pattern              | Example                     | English                       |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Standalone answer                | ¿...? — Sí.                  | ¿Te gusta? — Sí.                    | Do you like it? — Yes.                 |
| Emphatic agreement                  | Sí, [statement]                | Sí, claro que sí.                    | Yes, of course.                        |
| Emphasis on the verb                   | [subject] sí + [verb]              | Yo sí quiero ir.                     | I do want to go.                       |

| Spanish                            | English                          | Notes                        |

| Creo que sí.                             | I think so.                             | fixed expression                   |
