# parcialmente

## Meaning

*Parcialmente* means "partially" or "partly" — the low end of the totality scale,
the opposite of [[totalmente]] and [[completamente]].

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *parcial* + *-mente*. No standalone vocabulary file
  exists yet for *parcial* in `06 Vocabulary/Adjectives/`.
- Common with weather (*parcialmente nublado*) and with coverage/completion
  (*cubierto parcialmente*, *terminado parcialmente*).

## Examples

| Spanish                                              | English                                    | Notes             |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| El edificio quedó parcialmente destruido por el incendio.   | The building was left partially destroyed by the fire. | with a past participle |
| Estoy parcialmente de acuerdo con tu idea.                  | I partially agree with your idea.                | before *de acuerdo*      |
| El cielo está parcialmente nublado esta tarde.               | The sky is partially cloudy this afternoon.        | with weather              |
| La empresa cubre parcialmente los gastos médicos.            | The company partially covers medical expenses.     | before the verb           |
| ¿El proyecto está parcialmente terminado o falta mucho?      | Is the project partially finished, or is there a lot left? | question         |
