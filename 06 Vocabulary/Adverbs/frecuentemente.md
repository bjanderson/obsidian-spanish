# frecuentemente

## Meaning

*Frecuentemente* means "frequently" or "often." It's a more formal/written register
than the everyday *a menudo* or *seguido*, but means the same thing.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *frecuente* + *-mente*. No standalone vocabulary file
  exists yet for *frecuente* in `06 Vocabulary/Adjectives/`.
- Sits on the same frequency scale as [[raramente]] and [[constantemente]] — from
  "almost never" to "constantly."

## Examples

| Spanish                                          | English                                      | Notes               |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Viajo frecuentemente a Bolivia por trabajo.            | I travel frequently to Bolivia for work.          | before the verb         |
| Él frecuentemente olvida las llaves en la oficina.     | He frequently forgets his keys at the office.     | before the verb         |
| ¿Vienes frecuentemente a este café?                     | Do you come to this café frequently?              | question                |
| No la veo frecuentemente desde que se mudó.             | I don't see her frequently since she moved.        | with a negative         |
| Los estudiantes frecuentemente preguntan sobre los exámenes. | Students frequently ask about the exams.     | before the verb         |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[generalmente]] — related frequency adverb, describes what's typical rather than how often
- [[raramente]] — antonym, the low end of the frequency scale
- [[constantemente]] — the high end of the frequency scale, stronger than *frecuentemente*
