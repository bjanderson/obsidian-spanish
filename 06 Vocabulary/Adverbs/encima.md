# encima

## Meaning

*Encima* means "on top" or "above." On its own it works as a plain adverb of place: *Pon
el libro encima* ("Put the book on top"). Followed by *de* + a noun, it means "on top
of"/"above" that thing: *encima de la mesa* ("on top of the table"). It also appears in
the figurative expression *por encima de* ("above/beyond," used non-literally), and
*encima* on its own can mean "on top of that"/"besides," adding an extra complaint.
Opposite: [[debajo]].

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                    | Notes                            |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Deja las llaves encima, por favor.                 | Leave the keys on top, please.                | standalone adverb                  |
| El gato está encima de la cama.                    | The cat is on top of the bed.                 | *encima de* + noun                 |
| Llegamos tarde y, encima, se puso a llover.        | We arrived late and, on top of that, it started to rain. | figurative, "on top of that" |
| El puente pasa por encima del río.                 | The bridge goes above the river.              | *por encima de*                    |
| Puso la manta encima de sus hombros.               | She put the blanket over her shoulders.       | *encima de* + body part            |
