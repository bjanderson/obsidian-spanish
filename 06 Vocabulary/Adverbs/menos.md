# menos

## Meaning

*Menos* means "less," "fewer," or "least" — invariable, it builds comparisons and
superlatives the opposite way from *más*. It also appears in fixed expressions like
*a menos que* ("unless") and *al menos* ("at least").

## Core Uses

| Use                | Pattern                    | Example                             | English                             |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Comparative               | menos + [adj/noun] + que          | Tengo menos tiempo que tú.                   | I have less time than you.                   |
| Superlative                     | el/la menos + [adj]                      | Es el menos caro de la tienda.                  | It's the least expensive in the store.          |
| At least                            | al menos                                        | Al menos llegamos a tiempo.                        | At least we arrived on time.                       |
| Unless                                  | a menos que + subjunctive                              | No voy, a menos que me acompañes.                     | I'm not going, unless you come with me.               |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                     | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Hoy hace menos calor que ayer.                     | Today it's less hot than yesterday.                  | comparative                        |
| Es el estudiante menos puntual de la clase.         | He's the least punctual student in the class.         | superlative                          |
| Necesito al menos una hora para terminar.           | I need at least an hour to finish.                     | *al menos*                             |
| No saldremos a menos que deje de llover.            | We won't go out unless it stops raining.                | *a menos que* + subjunctive             |
| Cada vez tengo menos paciencia con esto.            | I have less and less patience with this.                | ongoing comparison                        |
