# únicamente

## Meaning

*Únicamente* means "only" or "solely" — a restrictive adverb, essentially a more
formal synonym of *solo*/*solamente*.

## Usage Notes

- Formed from the adjective *único* ([[único-única-únicos-únicas]]) + *-mente*.
- Near-synonym of [[simplemente]] in its restrictive "just/only" sense.

## Examples

| Spanish                                       | English                                | Notes           |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Únicamente acepto pagos en efectivo.                    | I only accept cash payments.                    | sentence-initial     |
| Este descuento es únicamente para estudiantes.           | This discount is only for students.             | with *ser*              |
| No lo hice por dinero, únicamente por amistad.           | I didn't do it for money, only out of friendship. | contrastive         |
| ¿Únicamente trabajas los martes?                           | Do you only work on Tuesdays?                    | question              |
| La tienda abre únicamente hasta las seis.                  | The store is only open until six.                | before *hasta*          |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[simplemente]] — near-synonym in the restrictive "only/just" sense
