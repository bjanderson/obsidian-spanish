# cuatro

## Meaning

*Cuatro* is the number "four," invariable: *cuatro sillas* ("four chairs"). Common in
time expressions (*las cuatro* = "four o'clock") and dates (*el cuatro de julio* = "the
Fourth of July").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Hay cuatro sillas alrededor de la mesa.     | There are four chairs around the table.     | counting objects            |
| Nos reunimos a las cuatro de la tarde.      | We're meeting at four in the afternoon.     | telling time                |
| Mi cumpleaños es el cuatro de mayo.         | My birthday is May fourth.                  | date                        |
| El bebé tiene cuatro meses.                 | The baby is four months old.                | age                         |
| Compramos cuatro kilos de manzanas.         | We bought four kilos of apples.             | quantity                    |
