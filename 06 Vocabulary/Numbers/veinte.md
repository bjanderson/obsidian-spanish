# veinte

## Meaning

*Veinte* is the number "twenty." It's invariable. Numbers 21–29 attach to it as a
single word (*veintiuno*, *veintidós*...) rather than with *y* the way 31+ do
(*treinta y uno*) — worth knowing even though those compounds aren't covered here.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Tiene veinte años.                          | She's twenty years old.                         | age expression                     |
| Son las ocho y veinte.                      | It's eight-twenty.                              | telling time                       |
| Hay veinte alumnos en la clase.             | There are twenty students in the class.         | quantity                           |

| ¿Cuántas personas vinieron? — Como veinte.  | How many people came? — About twenty.           | standalone answer                  |
