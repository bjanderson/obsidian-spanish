# dos

## Meaning

*Dos* is the number "two." It's invariable — it doesn't change for gender or agree with
the noun it counts: *dos libros* ("two books"), *dos casas* ("two houses"). It also
appears in time expressions (*las dos* = "two o'clock") and set phrases like *cada dos
días* ("every two days").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Tengo dos hermanos y una hermana.           | I have two brothers and one sister.         | counting people            |
| Son las dos de la tarde.                    | It's two in the afternoon.                  | telling time                |
| Compré dos boletos para el concierto.       | I bought two tickets for the concert.       | counting objects            |
| Mi hija tiene dos años.                     | My daughter is two years old.               | age                         |
| Nos vemos cada dos semanas.                 | We see each other every two weeks.          | recurring interval           |
