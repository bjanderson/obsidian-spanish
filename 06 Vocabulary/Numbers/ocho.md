# ocho

## Meaning

*Ocho* is the number "eight," invariable: *ocho horas* ("eight hours"). Common in time
expressions (*las ocho* = "eight o'clock") and everyday counting.

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                    | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Duermo ocho horas todas las noches.                 | I sleep eight hours every night.                | duration                    |
| La tienda cierra a las ocho.                        | The store closes at eight.                      | telling time                |
| Mi abuela tiene ochenta y ocho años.                | My grandmother is eighty-eight years old.       | age (compound number)       |
| Compramos ocho botellas de agua para el viaje.      | We bought eight bottles of water for the trip.  | counting objects            |
| Faltan ocho minutos para que empiece la película.   | There are eight minutes left until the movie starts. | countdown              |
