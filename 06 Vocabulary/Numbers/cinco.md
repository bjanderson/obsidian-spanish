# cinco

## Meaning

*Cinco* is the number "five," invariable: *cinco años* ("five years"). Common in time
expressions (*las cinco* = "five o'clock") and everyday counting.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Tengo cinco dólares en el bolsillo.         | I have five dollars in my pocket.           | counting money              |
| La reunión empieza a las cinco.             | The meeting starts at five.                 | telling time                |
| Vivo aquí desde hace cinco años.            | I've lived here for five years.             | duration                    |
| Compré cinco boletos para toda la familia.  | I bought five tickets for the whole family. | counting objects            |
| El equipo ganó cinco partidos seguidos.     | The team won five games in a row.           | counting events             |
