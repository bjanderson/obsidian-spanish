# tres

## Meaning

*Tres* is the number "three," invariable like all the numbers 2 through 11: *tres perros*
("three dogs"). It appears in time expressions (*las tres* = "three o'clock") and common
phrases like *a la de tres* ("on the count of three").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Necesito tres huevos para la receta.        | I need three eggs for the recipe.           | counting objects            |
| El tren sale a las tres en punto.           | The train leaves at three o'clock sharp.    | telling time                |
| Vivimos a tres cuadras de aquí.             | We live three blocks from here.             | distance/quantity           |
| Tiene tres hijos: dos niñas y un niño.      | She has three children: two girls and one boy. | counting people          |
| Saltamos a la de tres.                      | We jumped on the count of three.            | set phrase                  |
