# quince

## Meaning

*Quince* is the number "fifteen." It's invariable. Culturally, *los quince años* (or
*la quinceañera*) is a major celebration across Latin America marking a girl's
fifteenth birthday, roughly comparable to a "sweet sixteen" in the U.S.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Faltan quince minutos para las cinco.       | It's fifteen minutes until five.                | telling time                       |
| Celebraron los quince años de su hija.      | They celebrated their daughter's *quinceañera*. | cultural note                      |

| Compré quince rosas para el jardín.         | I bought fifteen roses for the garden.          | quantity                           |
| Nos quedan quince días de vacaciones.       | We have fifteen days of vacation left.          | quantity                           |
