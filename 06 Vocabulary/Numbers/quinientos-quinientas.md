# quinientos / quinientas

## Meaning

*Quinientos* ("five hundred") is irregular — it doesn't come from *cinco* + *cientos*
the way *doscientos* comes from *dos* + *cientos*. Like the other hundreds from
*doscientos* on, it agrees in gender with the noun it counts: *quinientos dólares*
(masculine), *quinientas personas* (feminine).

## Full Paradigm

| Gender    | Form        |
| --------- | ----------- |
| Masculine | quinientos  |
| Feminine  | quinientas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El estadio tiene capacidad para quinientas personas. | The stadium holds five hundred people. | feminine (agrees with *personas*)  |
| Gastamos quinientos dólares en la reparación. | We spent five hundred dollars on the repair. | masculine                          |
| Hay quinientas especies diferentes en la selva. | There are five hundred different species in the jungle. | feminine                |
| Recorrimos quinientos kilómetros en un día.  | We covered five hundred kilometers in one day.  | masculine                          |
| Vendieron quinientas copias del libro en un mes. | They sold five hundred copies of the book in a month. | feminine                    |
