# noventa

## Meaning

*Noventa* is the number "ninety." It's invariable.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Cumplió noventa años el mes pasado.         | She turned ninety last month.                   | age expression                     |
| Sacó noventa puntos en el examen.           | He got a ninety on the exam.                    | quantity                           |
| El termómetro marca noventa grados.         | The thermometer reads ninety degrees.           | quantity                           |
| Hay noventa alumnos matriculados.           | There are ninety students enrolled.             | quantity                           |
| Faltan noventa kilómetros para llegar.      | There are ninety kilometers left to go.         | quantity                           |
