# ochenta

## Meaning

*Ochenta* is the number "eighty." It's invariable.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi bisabuela tiene ochenta años.            | My great-grandmother is eighty years old.       | age expression                     |
| El hotel tiene ochenta habitaciones.        | The hotel has eighty rooms.                     | quantity                           |
| Corrió ochenta metros en diez segundos.     | He ran eighty meters in ten seconds.            | quantity                           |
| Pagamos ochenta dólares por el taxi.        | We paid eighty dollars for the taxi.            | quantity                           |
| Hay ochenta páginas en el informe.          | There are eighty pages in the report.           | quantity                           |
