# diez

## Meaning

*Diez* is the number "ten," invariable: *diez minutos* ("ten minutes"). Common in time
expressions (*las diez* = "ten o'clock") and everyday counting; also a common grading
score in Spanish-speaking schools, where a "perfect ten" (*un diez*) means full marks.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                     | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Llego en diez minutos.                      | I'll arrive in ten minutes.                   | duration                        |
| La tienda abre a las diez de la mañana.     | The store opens at ten in the morning.        | telling time                    |
| Mi hijo cumple diez años el sábado.         | My son turns ten on Saturday.                 | age                             |
| Compré diez manzanas en el mercado.         | I bought ten apples at the market.            | counting objects                |
| Le puse un diez a su examen.                | I gave her exam a ten (a perfect score).      | grading/scoring use              |
