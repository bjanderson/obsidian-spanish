# seis

## Meaning

*Seis* is the number "six," invariable: *seis meses* ("six months"). Common in time
expressions (*las seis* = "six o'clock") and everyday counting.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| El curso dura seis meses.                   | The course lasts six months.                | duration                    |
| Nos vemos a las seis en punto.              | See you at six o'clock sharp.               | telling time                |
| Tiene seis años y ya sabe leer.             | She's six years old and already knows how to read. | age                   |
| Compramos seis huevos en el mercado.        | We bought six eggs at the market.           | counting objects            |
| Faltan seis días para el examen.            | There are six days left until the exam.     | countdown/duration          |
