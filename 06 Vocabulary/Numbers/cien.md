# cien

## Meaning

*Cien* is the invariable form of "one hundred" used when counting exactly to
one hundred or placed directly before a noun: *cien dólares*, *cien personas*.
When followed by a smaller number instead of a noun, it becomes *ciento*: *ciento
uno* ("one hundred one"), *ciento veinte* ("one hundred twenty").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Hay cien estudiantes en la escuela.         | There are a hundred students at the school.     | quantity, before noun               |
| Cuento del uno al cien.                     | I'm counting from one to a hundred.             | counting                           |
| Pagué cien dólares por el boleto.           | I paid a hundred dollars for the ticket.        | quantity, before noun               |
| Vivió más de cien años.                     | She lived more than a hundred years.            | quantity                           |
| Ganó cien pesos en la lotería.              | He won a hundred pesos in the lottery.          | quantity, before noun               |
