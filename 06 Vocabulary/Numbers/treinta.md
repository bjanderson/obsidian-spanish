# treinta

## Meaning

*Treinta* is the number "thirty." It's invariable. From here on, compound numbers
use *y*: *treinta y uno* ("thirty-one"), *treinta y dos* ("thirty-two").

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Trabaja treinta horas a la semana.          | She works thirty hours a week.                  | quantity                           |
| Hoy cumplo treinta años.                    | Today I turn thirty.                            | age, birthday                      |
