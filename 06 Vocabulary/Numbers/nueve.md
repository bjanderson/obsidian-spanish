# nueve

## Meaning

*Nueve* is the number "nine," invariable: *nueve meses* ("nine months," a pregnancy).
Common in time expressions (*las nueve* = "nine o'clock") and everyday counting.

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                | Notes                    |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| El embarazo dura nueve meses.                       | Pregnancy lasts nine months.                | general fact                |
| La clase empieza a las nueve en punto.              | Class starts at nine o'clock sharp.         | telling time                |
| Tiene nueve años y juega fútbol.                    | He's nine years old and plays soccer.       | age                         |
| Compramos nueve entradas para el cine.              | We bought nine tickets for the movies.      | counting objects            |
| Nos quedamos en la habitación número nueve del hotel. | We stayed in room number nine of the hotel. | labeling/numbering use    |
