# setenta

## Meaning

*Setenta* is the number "seventy." It's invariable.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Mi abuelo cumple setenta años este mes.     | My grandfather turns seventy this month.        | age expression                     |
| El examen tiene setenta preguntas.          | The exam has seventy questions.                 | quantity                           |
| Gastamos setenta dólares en la cena.        | We spent seventy dollars on dinner.             | quantity                           |
| Hay setenta personas en la lista de espera. | There are seventy people on the waiting list.   | quantity                           |
| La caja pesa setenta libras.                | The box weighs seventy pounds.                  | quantity                           |
