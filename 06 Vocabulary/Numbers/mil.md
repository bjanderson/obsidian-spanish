# mil

## Meaning

*Mil* means "one thousand." It's invariable as a counting number — it never takes a
plural *-es* when it's multiplying something: *dos mil dólares* ("two thousand
dollars"), not *dos miles*. The separate word *miles* ("thousands") does exist as a
plural noun for a vague large quantity: *miles de personas* ("thousands of people").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Vinieron mil personas al concierto.         | A thousand people came to the concert.          | quantity                           |
| Cuesta mil dólares.                         | It costs a thousand dollars.                    | price                               |
| Nací en el año dos mil.                     | I was born in the year two thousand.            | date                                |
| Hay miles de razones para viajar.           | There are thousands of reasons to travel.       | *miles de* + noun (plural form)     |
| Ahorramos mil pesos este mes.               | We saved a thousand pesos this month.           | quantity                           |
