# doce

## Meaning

*Doce* is the number "twelve." Like all numbers from *doce* through *quince*, it's
invariable — the same form regardless of the gender of the noun it counts: *doce
libros*, *doce casas*. It's used for counting, telling time, dates, and ages.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Hay doce meses en el año.                   | There are twelve months in a year.             | counting                           |
| Nos vemos a las doce.                       | See you at twelve.                             | telling time                       |
| Tiene doce años.                            | She's twelve years old.                        | age expression                     |
| Necesito doce sillas para la fiesta.        | I need twelve chairs for the party.            | quantity                           |
| ¿Cuántos invitados vienen? — Doce.          | How many guests are coming? — Twelve.          | standalone answer                  |
