# catorce

## Meaning

*Catorce* is the number "fourteen." It's invariable, like the other numbers in
this range.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Catorce estudiantes se inscribieron en la clase. | Fourteen students enrolled in the class.  | quantity, subject                  |
| Hoy es el catorce de febrero.               | Today is February 14th.                         | date                                |
| Tengo catorce mensajes sin leer.            | I have fourteen unread messages.                | quantity                           |
| Vivimos aquí desde hace catorce años.       | We've lived here for fourteen years.            | after "hace" (duration)            |
| ¿Cuántas horas duró el viaje? — Catorce.    | How many hours did the trip take? — Fourteen.   | standalone answer                  |
