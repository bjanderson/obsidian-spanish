# doscientos / doscientas

## Meaning

Unlike smaller numbers, the hundreds from *doscientos* ("two hundred") up agree in
gender with the noun they count: *doscientos hombres* (masculine), *doscientas
mujeres* (feminine). The agreement follows the grammatical gender of the noun, not
any real-world gender — *doscientas personas* is feminine because *personas* is a
feminine noun, even when some of the people are men.

## Full Paradigm

| Gender    | Form        |
| --------- | ----------- |
| Masculine | doscientos  |
| Feminine  | doscientas  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Compramos doscientos boletos para el partido. | We bought two hundred tickets for the game.  | masculine                          |
| Había doscientas personas en la fiesta.     | There were two hundred people at the party.     | feminine (agrees with *personas*)  |
| El libro tiene doscientas páginas.          | The book has two hundred pages.                 | feminine                           |
| Ahorré doscientos dólares este mes.         | I saved two hundred dollars this month.         | masculine                          |
| Se vendieron doscientas entradas en una hora. | Two hundred tickets sold in an hour.          | feminine                           |
