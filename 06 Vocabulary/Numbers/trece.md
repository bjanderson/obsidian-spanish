# trece

## Meaning

*Trece* is the number "thirteen." It's invariable, like *doce*. In Latin American
culture, *martes trece* ("Tuesday the 13th") plays the role that "Friday the 13th"
plays in English — it's considered an unlucky day, not the Friday.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                  | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Hay trece personas en la sala.              | There are thirteen people in the room.          | counting                           |
| Mi cumpleaños es el trece de mayo.          | My birthday is May 13th.                        | date                                |
| Dicen que el martes trece trae mala suerte. | They say Tuesday the 13th brings bad luck.      | cultural note                      |
| Compramos trece boletos para el concierto.  | We bought thirteen tickets for the concert.     | quantity                           |
| ¿Tienes trece dólares?                      | Do you have thirteen dollars?                   | question                           |
