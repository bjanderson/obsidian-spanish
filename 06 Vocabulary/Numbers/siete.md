# siete

## Meaning

*Siete* is the number "seven," invariable: *siete días* ("seven days," a week). Common in
time expressions (*las siete* = "seven o'clock") and everyday counting.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                                | Notes                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Hay siete días en una semana.               | There are seven days in a week.             | general fact                |
| El vuelo sale a las siete de la mañana.     | The flight leaves at seven in the morning.  | telling time                |
| Mi sobrino cumple siete años mañana.        | My nephew turns seven tomorrow.             | age                         |
| Trabajé siete horas seguidas hoy.           | I worked seven hours straight today.        | duration                    |
| Necesitamos siete sillas más para la cena.  | We need seven more chairs for the dinner.   | counting objects            |
