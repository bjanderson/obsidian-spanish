# durante

## Meaning

*Durante* is invariant and marks that something happens across a span of time —
"during" an event, or "for" a stretch of time. It overlaps with [[por]] in the
"for a duration" sense (*Estudié por dos horas* / *Estudié durante dos horas* are
both fine), but only *durante* covers "during [an event]": *durante la clase*
("during class"), not *por la clase*.

## Core Uses

| Use                    | Pattern                | Example                          | English                          |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| During an event                | durante + [event]              | No hables durante la película.          | Don't talk during the movie.             |
| Span of time                     | durante + [time period]          | Vivimos allí durante tres años.         | We lived there for three years.          |

## Examples

| Spanish                                        | English                                 | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Durante la reunión, nadie dijo nada.                | During the meeting, no one said anything.      | during an event                    |
| El bebé lloró durante toda la noche.                | The baby cried all night long.                 | span of time                       |
| ¿Qué hiciste durante las vacaciones?                | What did you do during vacation?               | during an event, question          |
| Estudió medicina durante seis años.                 | She studied medicine for six years.            | span of time                       |
| Durante el invierno, hace mucho frío aquí.          | During winter, it's very cold here.            | during an event                    |
