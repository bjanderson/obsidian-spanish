# con

## Meaning

*Con* is invariant and means "with" — accompaniment, means/tool, or manner. It has
three special contracted forms after the first-person, second-person informal, and
reflexive prepositional pronouns: [[conmigo]] ("with me"), [[contigo]] ("with you,"
informal), and [[consigo]] ("with him/her/them/self"). Those are separate, higher-
frequency vocabulary entries in their own right and aren't covered here.

## Core Uses

| Use                | Pattern                | Example                     | English                       |
| -------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Accompaniment           | con + [person]              | Voy con mi hermana.               | I'm going with my sister.           |
| Means/tool                | con + [tool]                  | Escribió con un lápiz.            | She wrote with a pencil.             |
| Manner                       | con + [manner]                 | Habló con cuidado.                | He spoke carefully.                  |
| Ingredient/contents             | con + [ingredient]               | Café con leche, por favor.        | Coffee with milk, please.            |
