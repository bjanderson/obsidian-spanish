# por

## Meaning

*Por* is invariant and covers cause, exchange, movement through a space, duration,
and the agent of a passive sentence. Learners often confuse it with [[para]] — the
short version: *por* looks back at a cause or reason, *para* looks forward to a
purpose or destination (see B1.4.2.1 for the full contrast).

## Core Uses

| Use                      | Pattern                     | Example                          | English                        |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Cause/reason                  | por + [reason]                  | Llegó tarde por el tráfico.          | She arrived late because of traffic.  |
| Exchange                        | por + [price/thing]              | Pagué diez dólares por el libro.     | I paid ten dollars for the book.      |
| Movement through                  | por + [place]                     | Caminamos por el parque.             | We walked through the park.            |
| Duration                            | por + [time span]                  | Estudié por dos horas.               | I studied for two hours.               |
| Agent of the passive                  | ser + [participle] + por + [agent]  | El libro fue escrito por ella.       | The book was written by her.           |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Gracias por tu ayuda.                       | Thanks for your help.                    | cause/reason                       |
| ¿Cuánto pagaste por esos zapatos?           | How much did you pay for those shoes?    | exchange, question                 |
| Pasamos por la plaza antes de llegar.       | We passed through the plaza before arriving. | movement through               |
| Trabajó por toda la tarde sin descansar.    | He worked all afternoon without resting. | duration                           |
| La cena fue preparada por mi abuela.        | Dinner was prepared by my grandmother.   | agent of the passive               |
