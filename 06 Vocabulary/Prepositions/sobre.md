# sobre

## Meaning

*Sobre* is invariant as a preposition, covering both physical position ("on,"
"over," "on top of") and topic ("about," "concerning"). *Sobre* is also, separately,
a noun — *el sobre* means "envelope." That's a different word functioning as a
different part of speech, even though the spelling is identical.

## Core Uses

| Use               | Pattern              | Example                         | English                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Physical position (on)   | sobre + [surface]           | El libro está sobre la mesa.            | The book is on the table.               |
| Over/above                | sobre + [thing]               | El avión voló sobre la ciudad.           | The plane flew over the city.           |
| Topic (about)                | sobre + [topic]                 | Hablamos sobre el proyecto.              | We talked about the project.            |

For approximate time, neutral Latin American Spanish more commonly uses *a eso de*
or *como a* rather than *sobre* — see [[como]] for *como* used the same way with
quantities.

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Dejé las llaves sobre el escritorio.        | I left the keys on the desk.             | physical position                  |
| Escribió un libro sobre la historia de Bolivia. | She wrote a book about the history of Bolivia. | topic                       |
| Vamos a hablar sobre tus planes.            | We're going to talk about your plans.    | topic                              |
| El puente pasa sobre el río.                | The bridge goes over the river.          | over/above                         |
| Nos vemos a eso de las siete.               | See you around seven.                    | approximate time (not *sobre*)     |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[como]] — used the same way for approximate quantities ("*como cien pesos*").
