# al

## Meaning

*Al* is the mandatory contraction of [[a]] + *el* ("to the," masculine singular).
Whenever *a* is immediately followed by the article *el*, Spanish always contracts
them to *al* — it's never optional and never written as *a el*. This works the same
way as *de + el → [[del]]*.

The same exception applies here as with *del*: when *el* is part of a proper noun's
name rather than a plain article — like the country *El Salvador* — *a* and *El* stay
separate and *El* keeps its capital letter: *Viajamos a El Salvador* ("We're
traveling to El Salvador").

*Al* also combines with an infinitive to mean "upon/when doing something": *Al
llegar, llamó a su madre* ("Upon arriving, he called his mother").

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                                | Notes                             |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Vamos al mercado los sábados.               | We go to the market on Saturdays.              | a + el → al, destination                |
| Llevé a mi hijo al médico.                  | I took my son to the doctor.                   | a + el → al + personal *a*              |
| El gato subió al techo.                     | The cat climbed onto the roof.                 | a + el → al                             |
| Al terminar la clase, salimos juntos.       | Upon finishing class, we left together.        | al + infinitive                          |
| Viajamos a El Salvador el próximo mes.      | We're traveling to El Salvador next month.     | exception, proper noun                    |
