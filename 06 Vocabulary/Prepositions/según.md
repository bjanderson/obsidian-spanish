# según

## Meaning

*Según* means "according to." Unlike most prepositions, it takes subject
pronouns rather than the stressed prepositional forms — *según yo* ("according
to me"), not *según mí* — the same exception [[yo]]'s entry mentions.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Según el pronóstico, va a llover mañana.    | According to the forecast, it's going to rain tomorrow. | statement            |
| ¿Según quién dijiste eso?                   | According to whom did you say that?      | question                           |
| Según yo, esa no es la mejor idea.          | In my opinion, that's not the best idea. | subject pronoun, not *mí*           |
| Según ella, el vuelo sale a las ocho.       | According to her, the flight leaves at eight. | statement                     |
| No todos piensan igual, según tengo entendido. | Not everyone thinks the same, as far as I understand. | statement, set phrase    |
