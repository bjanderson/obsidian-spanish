# desde

## Meaning

*Desde* is invariant and marks a starting point — in time or in space. It often
pairs with [[hasta]] ("until"): *desde... hasta...* ("from... until..."). With a
present-tense verb, *desde* signals an action that started in the past and
continues now: *Vivo aquí desde 2020* ("I've been living here since 2020").

## Core Uses

| Use                | Pattern                  | Example                          | English                              |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Time starting point         | desde + [time]                  | Trabajo aquí desde enero.               | I've worked here since January.              |
| Place starting point           | desde + [place]                   | Llamó desde el aeropuerto.              | She called from the airport.                 |
| Point of view                     | desde + [perspective]                | Desde mi punto de vista, es un error.   | From my point of view, it's a mistake.       |
| Range (with *hasta*)                 | desde... hasta...                       | Abierto desde las nueve hasta las cinco. | Open from nine until five.                  |

## Examples

| Spanish                                     | English                                | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Estudio español desde hace dos años.         | I've been studying Spanish for two years.     | time starting point, ongoing action |
| Nos escribimos desde que nos conocimos.      | We've written to each other since we met.     | time starting point, subordinate clause |
| Se puede ver el mar desde la ventana.        | You can see the sea from the window.          | place starting point                |
| Desde aquí hasta el centro hay poca distancia. | From here to downtown it's not far.         | range, with *hasta*                 |
| Desde niño, quiso ser doctor.                | Since he was a boy, he wanted to be a doctor. | time starting point                 |
