# mediante

## Meaning

*Mediante* means "by means of" or "through" — it introduces the tool, method,
or process used to achieve something. It's more formal than [[con]], and
common in written or professional Spanish: *mediante un contrato* ("by means
of a contract").

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Resolvieron el conflicto mediante el diálogo. | They resolved the conflict through dialogue. | statement                     |
| ¿Se puede pagar mediante transferencia?     | Can you pay by bank transfer?            | question                           |
| El acuerdo se firmó mediante un notario.    | The agreement was signed through a notary. | statement                        |
| No se puede resolver todo mediante la fuerza. | Not everything can be solved through force. | negation                       |
| Aprendió inglés mediante un programa en línea. | She learned English through an online program. | statement                    |
