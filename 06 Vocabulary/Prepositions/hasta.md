# hasta

## Meaning

*Hasta* is invariant and marks an endpoint — in time, in space, or in a scale. It
often pairs with [[desde]] ("from"): *desde... hasta...* ("from... until..."). It
also shows up in everyday farewells: *hasta luego*, *hasta mañana*, *hasta pronto*.

## Core Uses

| Use                | Pattern                  | Example                        | English                          |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Time endpoint             | hasta + [time]                  | Trabajo hasta las seis.               | I work until six.                       |
| Place endpoint               | hasta + [place]                   | Caminamos hasta la plaza.             | We walked as far as the plaza.          |
| Even (emphatic)                 | hasta + [surprising thing]          | Hasta mi abuela usa celular.          | Even my grandmother uses a cell phone.  |
| Farewell expressions               | hasta + [time]                        | Hasta luego.                          | See you later.                          |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El museo está abierto hasta las ocho.       | The museum is open until eight.          | time endpoint                      |
| Desde aquí hasta tu casa hay diez cuadras.  | From here to your house it's ten blocks. | place endpoint, with *desde*       |
| Hasta los niños entendieron la broma.       | Even the kids got the joke.              | even (emphatic)                    |
| Nos vemos, ¡hasta mañana!                   | See you, until tomorrow!                 | farewell expression                |
| No lo va a firmar hasta que lo revise.      | He won't sign it until he reviews it.    | time endpoint, subordinate clause  |
