# sin

## Meaning

*Sin* is invariant and means "without" — the opposite of [[con]]. Followed by an
infinitive, it means "without doing" something.

## Core Uses

| Use                  | Pattern                | Example                        | English                          |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Absence of a thing         | sin + [noun]                  | Tomo el café sin azúcar.               | I drink coffee without sugar.           |
| Absence of an action          | sin + [infinitive]              | Salió sin decir nada.                   | He left without saying anything.        |
| Fixed expressions                | —                                 | sin embargo, sin duda                    | however, without a doubt                |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Prefiero el té sin azúcar.                  | I prefer tea without sugar.               | absence of a thing                 |
| No puedo vivir sin música.                  | I can't live without music.               | absence of a thing                 |
| Terminó el examen sin revisar las respuestas. | He finished the exam without checking his answers. | absence of an action     |
| Sin embargo, decidimos quedarnos.            | However, we decided to stay.             | fixed expression                   |
| Es, sin duda, la mejor opción.               | It's, without a doubt, the best option.  | fixed expression                   |
