# ante

## Meaning

*Ante* means "before" in the sense of "in the presence of" or "faced with" —
not time. It shows up in formal or legal contexts: *ante la ley* ("before the
law"), *ante el juez* ("before the judge"). It can also mean "faced with" a
situation: *Ante tantos problemas, decidió pedir ayuda* ("Faced with so many
problems, he decided to ask for help").

Don't confuse *ante* with [[antes]] (adverb, "before" in time — *antes de la
cena*, "before dinner"). *Ante* never refers to time; *antes* almost always
does.

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| El acusado se presentó ante el juez.        | The accused appeared before the judge.   | formal/legal context               |
| Todos somos iguales ante la ley.            | We are all equal before the law.         | set phrase                         |
| Ante la duda, es mejor preguntar.           | When in doubt, it's better to ask.       | "faced with"                       |
| Se quedó callado ante la pregunta.          | He stayed silent in the face of the question. | "faced with"                  |
| Ante todo, quiero agradecerles su ayuda.     | Above all, I want to thank you for your help. | set phrase "ante todo"        |
