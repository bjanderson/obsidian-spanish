# en

## Meaning

*En* covers a lot of ground that English splits across "in," "on," and "at." It's
invariant — it never changes form. The core idea is containment or contact: something
is located inside, on top of, or at a point in space or time.

## Core Uses

| Use                    | Pattern                | Example                    | English                       |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Location (in/inside)      | en + [place]               | Está en la cocina.             | She's in the kitchen.              |
| Location (on/at a surface) | en + [surface]              | El libro está en la mesa.      | The book is on the table.          |
| Time                        | en + [month/year/season]    | Nací en enero.                 | I was born in January.             |
| Mode/means of transport      | en + [vehicle]               | Vamos en carro.                | We're going by car.                |
| Manner                        | en + [manner]                 | Habló en voz alta.             | He spoke in a loud voice.          |
| Figurative/verb pairing        | pensar en, confiar en          | Pienso en ti.                  | I think about you.                 |

## Examples

| Spanish                                    | English                              | Notes                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Los niños están en el parque.                  | The kids are at the park.                  | location                     |

| No confío en ese vendedor.                      | I don't trust that salesman.               | figurative, verb pairing     |
| Llegamos en tren, no en carro.                  | We arrived by train, not by car.           | mode of transport             |
