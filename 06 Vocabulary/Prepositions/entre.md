# entre

## Meaning

*Entre* is invariant and covers both "between" (two things) and "among" (a group) —
Spanish doesn't split these the way English does. Unlike most prepositions, *entre*
is followed by subject pronouns rather than the special prepositional forms *mí*/*ti*
in two fixed expressions: *entre tú y yo* ("between you and me"), not *entre ti y mí*.

## Core Uses

| Use                | Pattern                     | Example                        | English                           |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Between two things       | entre + [x] y + [y]              | El banco está entre la farmacia y la escuela. | The bank is between the pharmacy and the school. |
| Among a group               | entre + [group]                    | Se lo dividieron entre todos.        | They split it among everyone.           |
| Collaboration                  | entre + [people]                     | Lo hicimos entre los dos.            | We did it between the two of us.        |
| Time range                       | entre + [time] y + [time]              | Llego entre las tres y las cuatro.   | I'll arrive between three and four.     |
