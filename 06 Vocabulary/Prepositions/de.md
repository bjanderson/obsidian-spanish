# de

## Meaning

*De* is one of the most common words in Spanish. On its own it doesn't change form —
it's invariant, unlike a verb or an adjective. Its main job is to link two nouns, and
what that link means depends on context: possession, origin, material, or a starting
point.

When *de* is directly followed by the article *el*, the two words always contract to
[[del]] — *de + el → del*. This is mandatory, not optional (with one exception: proper
nouns that include "El" as part of their name, like *El Salvador*, don't contract —
see [[del]]).

## Core Uses

| Use               | Pattern                    | Example                          | English                        |
| ------------------ | --------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Possession          | [thing] + de + [owner]      | el carro de Marta                   | Marta's car                       |
| Origin               | ser + de + [place]          | Somos de Bolivia.                   | We're from Bolivia.               |
| Material             | [thing] + de + [material]   | una mesa de madera                  | a wooden table                    |
| Starting point       | de + [place] a + [place]    | de aquí a la estación               | from here to the station          |
| Fixed expressions    | —                            | de repente, de acuerdo, de nuevo    | suddenly, agreed, again           |

## Examples

| Spanish                                         | English                                   | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| El libro de mi hermana está en la mesa.           | My sister's book is on the table.            | possession                   |
| ¿De dónde eres?                                   | Where are you from?                          | origin, question             |
| No es de aquí; es de Cochabamba.                  | She's not from here; she's from Cochabamba.  | origin, negation              |
| Necesito una taza de café.                        | I need a cup of coffee.                      | contents/material             |
| De repente, empezó a llover.                      | Suddenly, it started to rain.                | fixed expression              |

## Related Vocabulary

- [[del]] — the mandatory contraction of *de* + *el*.
- [[dar]] — its present-subjunctive form *dé* keeps a written accent specifically to stay distinct from *de*.
