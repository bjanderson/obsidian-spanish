# contra

## Meaning

*Contra* means "against" — physical contact or pressure against something
(*apoyado contra la pared*, "leaning against the wall"), or opposition (*luchar
contra la injusticia*, "fight against injustice"). The set phrase *en contra de*
works the same way and is just as common: *Estoy en contra de esa idea* ("I'm
against that idea").

Combined with the pronoun *mí*, *contra* becomes *contra mí* — no special fused
form like *conmigo*, unlike [[con]].

## Examples

| Spanish                                | English                              | Notes                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Dejó la bicicleta contra la pared.          | She left the bike against the wall.      | physical contact                   |
| Jugamos contra el mejor equipo del torneo.  | We're playing against the tournament's best team. | opposition, sports         |
| Muchos vecinos están en contra del proyecto. | Many neighbors are against the project.  | *en contra de*, opinion            |
| No tengo nada contra ti.                    | I don't have anything against you.       | personal opposition                |
| Votaron en contra de la nueva ley.           | They voted against the new law.          | *en contra de*, formal context     |
