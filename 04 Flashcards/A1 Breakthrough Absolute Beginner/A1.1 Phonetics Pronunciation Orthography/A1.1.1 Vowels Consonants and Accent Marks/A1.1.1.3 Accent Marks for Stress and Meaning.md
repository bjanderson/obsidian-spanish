| Why does "tú" need a tilde but "tu" does not? |
| -------------------------------------------------- |
| "Tú" ("you") and "tu" ("your") are different words; the tilde tells them apart.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786322578323-->|

| What is the difference between "él" and "el"? |
| -------------------------------------------------- |
| "Él" means "he"; "el" means "the".<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786322578325-->|

| What is the difference between "sí" and "si"? |
| -------------------------------------------------- |
| "Sí" means "yes"; "si" means "if".<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786322578327-->|

| Translate: Ana lava el carro. |
| --------------------------------- |
