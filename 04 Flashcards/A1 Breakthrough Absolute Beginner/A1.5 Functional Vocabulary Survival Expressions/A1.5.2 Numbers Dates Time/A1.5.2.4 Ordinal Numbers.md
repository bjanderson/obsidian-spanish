| Why is "el primero piso" incorrect? |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _Primero_ shortens to _primer_ directly before a masculine singular noun — it should be _el primer piso_.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786726005739-->       |

| Why is "la primero vez" incorrect? |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _Primero_ must agree in gender with the noun it describes — before feminine _vez_ it's _primera_: _la primera vez_.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786726005741--> |
