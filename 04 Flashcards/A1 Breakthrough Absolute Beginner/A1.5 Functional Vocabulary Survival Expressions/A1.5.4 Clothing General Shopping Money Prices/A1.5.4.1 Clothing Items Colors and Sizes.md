| ---------------------------------------------------------- |

| What's the difference between "talla" and "número" for sizes? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| talla = clothing size; número = shoe size — Spanish uses two different words where English uses "size" for both.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786727720651--> |
