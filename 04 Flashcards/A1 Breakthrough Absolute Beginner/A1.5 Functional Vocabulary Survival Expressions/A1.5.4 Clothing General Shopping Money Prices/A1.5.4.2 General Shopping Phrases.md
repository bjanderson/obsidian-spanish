| Why is "usted" the default register when talking to store staff, not "tú"? |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| It's a service interaction, and Spanish defaults to the formal "usted" in that context, not familiarity-based "tú".<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786727753390--> |
