| What gender are most nouns ending in *-ción, -sión, -dad,* or *-tad*? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Feminine, like *la nación, la ciudad, la libertad*.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786407688180--> |

| What gender are most nouns ending in *-or*? |
| -------------------------------------------------- |
| Masculine, like *el color, el amor*.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786407688183--> |

| Why is it a good habit to learn a new noun together with its article? |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Because learning the article alongside the noun makes the noun's gender automatic instead of something you have to guess later.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786407688190--> |
