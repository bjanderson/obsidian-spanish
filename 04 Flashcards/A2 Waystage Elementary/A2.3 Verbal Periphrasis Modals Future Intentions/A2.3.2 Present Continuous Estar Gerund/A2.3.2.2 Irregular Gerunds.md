| Why does *leer* become *leyendo* instead of *leiendo*? |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| When the stem ends in a vowel, the *i* of *-iendo* becomes *y* to avoid an awkward vowel cluster.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786496962244--> |

| Why does *dormir* become *durmiendo* instead of *dormiendo*? |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| *Dormir* is a stem-changing *-ir* verb (*o→u*), and that change carries into the gerund.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786496962248--> |
