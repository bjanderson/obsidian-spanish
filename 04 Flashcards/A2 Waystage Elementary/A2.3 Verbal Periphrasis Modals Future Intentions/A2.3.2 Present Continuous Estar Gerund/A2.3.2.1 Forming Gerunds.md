| Why is "Estoy habla" incorrect? |
| -------------------------------------- |
| The main verb must be in the gerund form (*hablando*), not conjugated.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786496783788--> |

| Why do Spanish speakers often say "¿Qué haces?" instead of "¿Qué estás haciendo?"? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Spanish uses the present progressive less than English — the simple present is often used instead, understood the same way in context.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786496783792--> |

| Why is "Estoy yendo a la tienda" unnatural in Spanish? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The present progressive is avoided with verbs of motion like *ir*; Spanish uses the simple present instead: *Voy a la tienda*.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786496783796--> |
