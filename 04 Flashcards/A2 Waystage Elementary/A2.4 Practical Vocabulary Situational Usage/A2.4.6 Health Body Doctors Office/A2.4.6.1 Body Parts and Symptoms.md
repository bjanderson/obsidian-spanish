| Translate: My head hurts. |
| ---------------------------- |
| Me duele la cabeza. |

<!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786742984372-->

| Why is "Me duele mi cabeza" wrong? |
| --------------------------------------- |
| Spanish uses the definite article (la cabeza), not a possessive, for body parts when the owner is already clear. |

<!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786742984383-->

| Fill in the blank: Me ______ los pies. (my feet hurt, plural) |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| duelen |

<!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786742984386-->

| Why does "duele" become "duelen" in "Me duelen los pies"? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Doler" agrees with the body part (the grammatical subject), like "gustar" — plural "los pies" needs "duelen." |
