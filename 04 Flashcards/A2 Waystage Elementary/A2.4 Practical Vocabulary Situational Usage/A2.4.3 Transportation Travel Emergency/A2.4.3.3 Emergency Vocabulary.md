| What's the difference between *auxilio* and *ayuda*? |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| *Auxilio* is reserved for true emergencies (calling out when in danger); *ayuda* is used more broadly, including everyday requests.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786534675482--> |

| Why does "*Me robaron la cartera*" not need to name who did the robbing? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| It uses the impersonal "they" construction — it states what happened to you without needing to know or say who did it.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786534675485--> |

| Why should you drop the subject pronoun in "*Yo perdí mi pasaporte*"? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| *Perdí* already shows the subject is *yo* through its conjugation — the pronoun is unnecessary. Just say *Perdí mi pasaporte*.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786534675489--> |
