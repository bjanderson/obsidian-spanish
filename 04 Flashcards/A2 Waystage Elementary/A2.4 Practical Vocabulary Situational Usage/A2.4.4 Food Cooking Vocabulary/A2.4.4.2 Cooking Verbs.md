| Why does "Se hornea el pastel" not need a subject pronoun? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The impersonal *se* already carries the "it is done" meaning — adding a subject pronoun like *ello* would be redundant and wrong. |

<!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786728797422-->

| What's the difference between "cocinar" and "cocer"? |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| *Cocinar* means "to cook" in general (the whole activity); *cocer* specifically means "to boil/cook in water," overlapping with *hervir*. |
<!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786728797425-->

| Why is "yo hervo" wrong? |
| ------------------------------ |
| *Hervir* is a stem-changing verb (e→ie) — the correct form is *yo hiervo*, not *yo hervo*. |
