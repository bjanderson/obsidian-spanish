| Why is "medio hermano" the correct term for a half-brother, not "hermanastro"? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| *Hermanastro* means stepbrother (no blood relation, connected through a parent's marriage). A half-brother, who shares one biological parent, is *medio hermano*.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786532745776--> |

| Why is it wrong to translate "father-in-law" as "padre-en-ley" or similar? |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Spanish doesn't build in-law terms with a suffix like English "-in-law" — each in-law relationship has its own dedicated word, here *suegro*.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786532745779--> |
