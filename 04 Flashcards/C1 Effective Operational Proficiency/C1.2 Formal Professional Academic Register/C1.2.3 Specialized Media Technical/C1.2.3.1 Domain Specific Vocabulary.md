| Why is it "el electorado decidió" and not "decidieron"? |
| ------------------------------------------------------- |
| El electorado is a singular collective noun (like el pueblo) — it takes a singular verb even though it refers to a group of people.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786638517061--> |

| True or false: "Actualmente" means "actually" in the sense of correcting or emphasizing something. |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| False — actualmente means "currently/at present." "Actually" (to correct or emphasize) is en realidad or de hecho.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786638517076--> |

| What does "asistir a algo" mean, and how is it different from the English "to assist"? |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Asistir a algo means "to attend something" — it does not mean "to help." "To help/assist" is ayudar.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786638517078--> |

| Why can't "demanda" always be translated the same way? |
| ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Demanda means "lawsuit" in a legal context (presentar una demanda) but "demand" in an economic context (oferta y demanda) — the meaning depends on the domain.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786638517080--> |

| Is "la sentencia" a grammatical sentence or a legal ruling? |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| A legal ruling/judgment — not a grammar sentence (that's la oración).<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786638517082--> |
