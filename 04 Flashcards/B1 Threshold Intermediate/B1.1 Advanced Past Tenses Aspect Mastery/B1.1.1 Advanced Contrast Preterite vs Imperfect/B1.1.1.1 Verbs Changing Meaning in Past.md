| Why is "*Conocía a Juan en la fiesta*" wrong if it's the first time meeting him? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A one-time event (meeting someone for the first time) needs the preterite, not the imperfect. Correct: *Conocí a Juan en la fiesta*.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786537421160--> |

| Why does "*no pude*" mean more than just "I didn't want to"? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| *No pude* means "I tried and failed" — a stronger, more specific meaning than a simple inability.<br><br><!--ANKI_NOTE_ID:1786537421163--> |
