#### **AL Cognate Trick**

Many English words ending in "AL" have the same form in Spanish. These are known as cognates.

Animal, legal, social, criminal, digital, final, formal, individual, normal, hospital, judicial, brutal, fundamental, general, monumental, natural, principal, rural, sentimental, total, universal, vocal.

# **Spanish Pronunciation Guidelines**

#### **Vowels**

Spanish has 5 vowel sounds:

**A**: as in "animal" – like the 'a' in "father". **E**: as in "central" – like the 'e' in "met". **I**: as in "digital" – like the 'ee' in "see". **O**: as in "hospital" – like the 'o' in "note". **U**: as in "musical" – like the 'oo' in "food".

## **Key Consonant Pronunciations:**

**C**:Hard sound (like K): Capital, Musical Soft sound (like S) before E or I: Central (Latin America: 's', Spain: 'th') **G**:Hard sound (like in "gone"): Global, Gradual Soft sound (like H) before E or I: Digital, General pronounced SH **LL**: Pronounced like Y: ¿Cómo te llamas?

**Y**: Can be Y or J through most areas. In the Southern Cone (e.g. Argentina) it will be

**Z**: Like 's' in Latin America and 'th' in Spain

**H**: Silent: Hospital **J**: Like English H: Justificación **Ñ**: Like 'n' in "onion": Mañana (tomorrow) **Q**: Like K, always followed by silent U: Qué **B and V**: Both sound like a soft B: El vino (wine)

**Pronunciation of R and RR:**

#### **Single R**:

At the beginning of a word: Strong trill, e.g., Rosa [ˈro.sa]

Between vowels: Light tap, e.g., Caro [ˈka.ɾo]

Elsewhere: Single tap, e.g., Alrededor [al.re.deˈðoɾ]

## **Double RR**:

Anywhere in a word: Strong, vibrant trill, e.g., Perro [ˈpe.rro]

## **Stress Rules in Spanish**:

*Words ending in a vowel*, N, or S: Stress the next-to-last syllable.

Examples: Camisas (shirts), Pelo (hair), Anillo (ring), Perro (dog), Edificio (building)

*Words ending in other letters*: Stress the last syllable.

Examples: Bailar (to dance), Hospital (hospital), Legal (legal), Verdad (truth)

*Accent Marks*: Stress the syllable with the accent mark.

Examples: Justificación, Pájaros
