### **Part 4: Sentences 26–30** — **Mucho** as an Adjective

**Instructions:** Translate each sentence into Spanish.

**Remember:** When **mucho / mucha / muchos / muchas** modifies a noun, it acts like an adjective and **must agree in gender and number** with that noun.

- 27. There are a lot of chairs in the classroom.
