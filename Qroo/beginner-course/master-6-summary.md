### **Lesson Summary: Going Places with the Spanish Mega Verb "IR"**

#### **Introduction:**

Hey everybody, Qroo Paul here! Welcome to lesson 5 in my Spanish Master Series. In today's lesson, we're going places with the Spanish mega verb **IR** – to go. We'll explore how to use this verb to talk about going to various places and incorporate it into sentences using expressions of desire, ability, and obligation.

### **Key Points:**

- **IR (to go)** is a fundamental verb in Spanish.
- To express going to a place, use **IR A** (e.g., "ir a la tienda" to go to the store).
- This verb integrates seamlessly with constructions expressing desire, ability, and obligation.

### **Vocabulary:**

- **Store:** La tienda
- **Pharmacy:** La farmacia
- **Park:** El parque
- **School:** La escuela, el colegio
- **Hospital:** El hospital
- **Library:** La biblioteca
- **Restaurant:** El restaurante
- **Supermarket:** El supermercado
- **Bank:** El banco
- **Cinema, Movies:** El cine
- **Gym:** El gimnasio
- **Beach:** La playa

- **Airport:** El aeropuerto
- **Office:** La oficina

#### **Example Sentences:**

- **I want to go to the store.** <sup>o</sup> Quiero ir a la tienda.
- **Can I go to the store?** <sup>o</sup> ¿Puedo ir a la tienda?
- **Can you go to the store?** <sup>o</sup> ¿Puedes ir a la tienda?
- **I have to go to the store.** <sup>o</sup> Tengo que ir a la tienda.
- **I want to go to the store. Do you want to go to the store with me?** <sup>o</sup> Quiero ir a la tienda. ¿Quieres ir a la tienda conmigo?
- **I want to, but I can't. I have to go to the bank.** <sup>o</sup> Quiero, pero no puedo. Tengo que ir al banco.
- **Do you want to go to the beach with me on Monday?** <sup>o</sup> ¿Quieres ir a la playa conmigo el lunes?
- **No, I have to work on Monday but I can go to the beach with you on Saturday.** <sup>o</sup> No, tengo que trabajar el lunes, pero puedo ir a la playa contigo el sábado.

### **Verb Conjugations (Present Indicative):**

- **Yo:** voy
- **Tú:** vas
- **Él, ella, usted:** va
- **Nosotros, nosotras:** vamos
- **Vosotros, vosotras:** vais (Used in Spain)
- **Ellos, ellas, ustedes:** van

- **Vos:** vas (Used in certain areas of Latin America, not Spain)

#### **Usage Tips:**

- **IR** is often used with the plain present tense to describe where someone is going. <sup>o</sup> Example: "Voy a la tienda." (I am going to the store.)
- Use **IR A (place) A (infinitive)** to express purpose: <sup>o</sup> Example: "Voy a la tienda a comprar manzanas." (I am going to the store to buy apples.)
- Although **para + infinitive** can be used to express purpose, it's less common with verbs of motion like **IR**.

## **Additional Examples:**

- **Juan is going to the store to buy milk.** <sup>o</sup> Juan va a la tienda a comprar leche.
- **I have to go to the bank to take out money.** <sup>o</sup> Tengo que ir al banco a sacar dinero.
- **Do you have to go to the bank to take out money?** <sup>o</sup> ¿Tienes que ir al banco a sacar dinero?

# **Practice Sentences:**

- **I have to go to the store to buy bread.** <sup>o</sup> Tengo que ir a la tienda a comprar pan.
- **Maria has to go to the store to buy sugar.** <sup>o</sup> María tiene que ir a la tienda a comprar azúcar.
- **Do you want to go to the store?** <sup>o</sup> ¿Quieres ir a la tienda?

- **I want to go to the park, but I can't. I have to go to the store to buy more coffee.** <sup>o</sup> Quiero ir al parque, pero no puedo. Tengo que ir a la tienda a comprar más café.
