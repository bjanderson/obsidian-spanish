## **Exercise 6: English → Spanish Translation**

- 1. I went to the store yesterday. **Fui a la tienda ayer.**
- 2. Did you speak (formal) with your sister? **¿Habló usted con su hermana?**
- 3. We ate tacos last night. **Comimos tacos anoche.**
- 4. She told me the truth. **Ella me dijo la verdad.**

- 5. They came to the party. **Vinieron a la fiesta.**
- 6. I was able to finish the work. **Pude terminar el trabajo.**
- 7. He gave the keys to his friend. **Le dio las llaves a su amigo.**
- 8. Where were you (informal) last night? **¿Dónde estuviste anoche?**
