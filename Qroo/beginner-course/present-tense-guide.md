# **Qroo Spanish Crew Conjugating Verbs in the Present Tense**

#### **Introduction**

The present tense in Spanish is used to express actions happening right now, habitual actions, and even some future actions. In this guide, we will start with regular verbs, explaining the breakdown of -AR, -ER, and -IR endings. Then, we will cover irregular verbs and group them by similar patterns, with a more complete section on common irregular verbs.

# **Regular Verbs**

Regular verbs in Spanish follow predictable patterns based on their infinitive endings: **-AR**, **- ER**, and **-IR**. Here's how to conjugate them in the present tense:

## **Conjugation Endings for Regular Verbs**

| Subject Pronoun                | -AR Ending | -ER Ending | -IR Ending |
|--------------------------------|------------|------------|------------|
| yo                             | -o         | -o         | -o         |
| tú                             | -as        | -es        | -es        |
| él/ella/usted                  | -a         | -e         | -e         |
| nosotros/nosotras              | -amos      | -emos      | -imos      |
| vosotros/vosotras (Spain only) | -áis       | -éis       | -ís        |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes            | -an        | -en        | -en        |

## **Examples of Regular Verbs**

#### • **Hablar (to speak):**

#### **Irregular Verbs**

While regular verbs follow the patterns shown above, irregular verbs can deviate in different ways. Below, these verbs are grouped by their similarities and presented in more detail.

# **Verbs Irregular in the First-Person Singular ("Yo")**

Some verbs are irregular only in the "yo" form, but are regular in other conjugations.

| hacer | hago   | hago, haces, hace, hacemos, hacéis, hacen   |
|-------|--------|---------------------------------------------|
| poner | pongo  | pongo, pones, pone, ponemos, ponéis, ponen  |
| salir | salgo  | salgo, sales, sale, salimos, salís, salen   |
| traer | traigo | traigo, traes, trae, traemos, traéis, traen |
| valer | valgo  | valgo, vales, vale, valemos, valéis, valen  |
| saber | sé     | sé, sabes, sabe, sabemos, sabéis, saben     |
| ver   | veo    | veo, ves, ve, vemos, veis, ven              |

### **Verbs with Stem Changes**

Some verbs change their stem vowels in all forms except **nosotros** and **vosotros**. These changes are categorized as **e > ie**, **o > ue**, or **e > i**.

**e > ie**

| querer   | quiero, quieres, quiere, queremos, queréis, quieren             |
|----------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| pensar   | pienso, piensas, piensa, pensamos, pensáis, piensan             |
| entender | entiendo, entiendes, entiende, entendemos, entendéis, entienden |

**o > ue**

| poder  | puedo, puedes, puede, podemos, podéis, pueden       |
|--------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| volver | vuelvo, vuelves, vuelve, volvemos, volvéis, vuelven |
| dormir | duermo, duermes, duerme, dormimos, dormís, duermen  |

**e > i**

# **Verbs with Irregular Stems**

Certain verbs have unique stem changes across all forms:

| tener | tengo, tienes, tiene, tenemos, tenéis, tienen |
|-------|-----------------------------------------------|
| venir | vengo, vienes, viene, venimos, venís, vienen  |
| decir | digo, dices, dice, decimos, decís, dicen      |

#### **Completely Irregular Verbs**

A small group of verbs have completely irregular conjugations that must be memorized.

| ser   | soy, eres, es, somos, sois, son            |
|-------|--------------------------------------------|
| estar | estoy, estás, está, estamos, estáis, están |
| ir    | voy, vas, va, vamos, vais, van             |
| haber | he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han            |

#### **Summary**

Irregular verbs in the present tense often follow specific patterns, such as changes only in the "yo" form, stem changes, or complete irregularity. This section provides detailed conjugations for the most common irregular verbs, giving a clear overview of their patterns. Practice with these verbs is key to mastering the present tense in Spanish.
