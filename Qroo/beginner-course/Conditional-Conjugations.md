# **Qroo Spanish Crew Conjugating Verbs in the Conditional Tense**

## **Introduction**

The conditional tense in Spanish is used to express actions that would happen under certain conditions. It is also used to make polite requests, give advice, or express hypothetical situations. This guide covers the conjugation of regular and irregular verbs in the conditional tense.

#### **Regular Verbs**

Regular verbs in the conditional tense follow a simple pattern. The endings are the same for **-AR**, **-ER**, and **-IR** verbs and are added directly to the infinitive form of the verb.

# **Conjugation Endings for Regular Verbs**

| Subject Pronoun                | Ending |
|--------------------------------|--------|
| yo                             | ía     |
| tú                             | ías    |
| él/ella/usted                  | ía     |
| nosotros/nosotras              | íamos  |
| vosotros/vosotras (Spain only) | íais   |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes            | ían    |

## **Irregular Verbs**

Irregular verbs in the conditional tense have irregular stems but use the same endings as regular verbs. The irregular stems are the same as those in the future tense.

# **Irregular Stems**

| Infinitive | Stem   | Example Conjugation (yo form) |
|------------|--------|-------------------------------|
| decir      | dir-   | diría                         |
| hacer      | har-   | haría                         |
| poder      | podr-  | podría                        |
| poner      | pondr- | pondría                       |
| querer     | querr- | querría                       |
| saber      | sabr-  | sabría                        |
| salir      | saldr- | saldría                       |
| tener      | tendr- | tendría                       |

#### **Summary**

The conditional tense is straightforward, with regular verbs using the same endings for **-AR**, **-ER**, and **-IR** verbs. Irregular verbs modify their stems but still use these endings. With consistent practice, you'll master this versatile tense to discuss hypothetical situations and polite requests effortlessly.
