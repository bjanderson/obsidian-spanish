# **Qroo Paul's Spanish Master Course Lesson 15 Summary Skool.com/qroo**

This lesson introduces **direct object pronouns** in Spanish and shows how to use them naturally in conversation. These pronouns replace nouns that directly receive the action of the verb and help make sentences shorter and more fluid.

#### **Direct Object Pronouns**

me – me

te – you (informal)

lo – him / it (masculine)

la – her / it (feminine)

nos – us

os – you all (informal – *vosotros*, used in Spain)

los – them (masculine or mixed group), or you all (formal – *ustedes*)

las – them (feminine), or you all (formal – *ustedes*, all women)

#### **Example with a person (personal A required):**

¿Conoces a Juan? – Do you know Juan?

No, no lo conozco. – No, I don't know him.

## **Example with a thing (no personal A):**

¿Conoces la canción? – Do you know the song?

Sí, la conozco. – Yes, I know it.

#### **Word Order with Direct Object Pronouns**

If there is **only a conjugated verb**, the pronoun goes **before** the verb:

Lo veo. – I see him.

La conozco. – I know her.

If there is a **conjugated verb + infinitive**, the pronoun can go **before the conjugated verb** or **attached to the infinitive**:

¿Quieres verla? – Do you want to see it?

La quiero ver. – I want to see it.

Both are correct. Choose whichever sounds more natural to you.

#### **More Practice Examples**

¿Tienes las llaves? – Do you have the keys? Sí, las tengo. – Yes, I have them. ¿Conoces a María? – Do you know María? No, no la conozco. – No, I don't know her. ¿Quieres conocerla? – Do you want to meet her? La quiero conocer. – I want to meet her. ¿Ves a los hombres? – Do you see the men? Sí, los veo. – Yes, I see them. ¿Ves a las mujeres? – Do you see the women? Sí, las veo. – Yes, I see them. ¿Nos ves? – Do you see us? Sí, los veo. – Yes, I see you all. (in Spain: **¿Os veo? – Do I see you all? Sí, nos ves.** – Yes, you see us.)

#### **The Verb VER for Watching**

Use *ver* (not *mirar*) when referring to watching TV or movies: ¿Quieres ver la película? – Do you want to watch the movie? Sí, quiero verla. – Yes, I want to watch it.

### **Special Use of "Lo" as a Neuter Pronoun**

*Lo* is also used to refer to ideas, facts, or general concepts—not people or objects:

No lo sé. – I don't know (it / that fact / that situation). Lo único que quiero saber es cuándo comemos. – The only thing I want to know is when we eat. Use *lo* when referring to something vague or previously mentioned, like: ¿Qué quieres hacer? – No lo sé. – I don't know (what I want to do). ¿Qué dijo ella? – No lo entendí. – I didn't understand it.
