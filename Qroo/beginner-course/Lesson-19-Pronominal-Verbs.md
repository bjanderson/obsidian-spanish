## **5. Pseudo-Reflexive Verbs**

Here are several **Spanish pronominal verbs** (verbs that use reflexive pronouns) that express emotions, mental states, or changes in condition:

# **Ideas…**

## **Emotions:**

- 1. **Alegrarse (de)** to be happy (about)
  - **Me alegro de verte.** (I'm happy to see you.)
- 2. **Preocuparse (por)** to worry (about)
  - **Se preocupa por sus hijos.** (He/She worries about his/her children.)
- 3. **Enfadarse (con)** to get angry (with)
  - **Te enfadaste con él por llegar tarde.** (You got angry with him for being late.)
- 4. **Sofocarse** to get overheated or flustered
  - **Me sofoco fácilmente en situaciones tensas.** (I get flustered easily in tense situations.)
- 5. **Sentirse** to feel (emotionally or physically)
  - **Me siento triste hoy.** (I feel sad today.)
- 6. **Lamentarse (de)** to lament or regret (about)
  - **Se lamentó de no haber estudiado más.** (He/She regretted not studying more.)
- 7. **Divertirse** to have fun

- **Nos divertimos mucho en la fiesta.** (We had a lot of fun at the party.)

## **Mental States:**

- 1. **Olvidarse (de)** to forget (about)
  - **Me olvidé de tu cumpleaños.** (I forgot about your birthday.)
- 2. **Darse cuenta (de)** to realize
  - **Me di cuenta de que no tenía mi teléfono.** (I realized that I didn't have my phone.)
- 3. **Enamorarse (de)** to fall in love (with)
  - **Se enamoró de ella a primera vista.** (He fell in love with her at first sight.)
- 4. **Pensar (en)** to think (about)
  - **Siempre me pienso en lo que podría haber hecho mejor.** (I'm always thinking about what I could have done better.)
- 5. **Asustarse (de)** to get scared (of)
  - **Me asusté mucho con la película de terror.** (I got scared by the horror movie.)
- 6. **Cansarse (de)** to get tired (of)
  - **Me cansé de esperar.** (I got tired of waiting.)
- 7. **Despertarse** to wake up (from sleep)
  - **Me desperté temprano esta mañana.** (I woke up early this morning.)

## **Changes in Condition:**

- 1. **Enfermarse** to get sick
  - **Se enfermó después de la fiesta.** (He/She got sick after the party.)
- 2. **Ponerse** to become (a state, usually temporary)
  - **Se puso triste al escuchar la noticia.** (He/She became sad upon hearing the news.)
- 3. **Quedarse** to remain, stay (in a state)
  - **Me quedé sin palabras al escuchar la noticia.** (I was left speechless upon hearing the news.)
- 4. **Caerse** to fall (down)
  - **Se cayó al intentar correr.** (He/She fell while trying to run.)
- 5. **Hacerse** to become (a change that involves effort, often a profession)
  - **Se hizo médico después de estudiar muchos años.** (He/She became a doctor after studying for many years.)
- 6. **Volverse** to become (a dramatic change)
  - **Se volvió muy antipático después del incidente.** (He/She became very unfriendly after the incident.)
- 7. **Convertirse (en)** to turn into, to become

- **El agua se convirtió en hielo.** (The water turned into ice.)
- 8. **Desarrollarse** to develop (in a certain way)
  - **El niño se desarrolló rápidamente.** (The child developed quickly.)
- 9. **Romperse** to break (a body part or an object)
  - **Me rompí el brazo jugando al fútbol.** (I broke my arm playing soccer.)
- 10. **Recuperarse** to recover
  - **Ella se recuperó rápidamente de la enfermedad.** (She recovered quickly from the illness.)
- 11. **Acordarse (de)** to remember (something)
  - **Me acordé de tu nombre.** (I remembered your name.)

## **6. Meaning-Changing Verbs**

A list of **Spanish verbs** that **change meaning** when used in their **pronominal** form (with reflexive pronouns **me**, **te**, **se**, etc.), compared to their **non-pronominal** form:

## **1. Acercar (to bring closer) vs. Acercarse (to approach)**

- **Acercar**: *Voy a acercar la silla.* (I'm going to bring the chair closer.)
- **Acercarse**: *Nos acercamos al borde del río.* (We approached the edge of the river.)

## **2. Ir (to go) vs. Irse (to leave)**

- **Ir**: *Voy al supermercado.* (I'm going to the supermarket.)
- **Irse**: *Me voy ahora.* (I'm leaving now.)

## **3. Dormir (to sleep) vs. Dormirse (to fall asleep)**

- **Dormir**: *Voy a dormir ocho horas.* (I'm going to sleep for eight hours.)
- **Dormirse**: *Me dormí durante la película.* (I fell asleep during the movie.)

## **4. Levantar (to lift) vs. Levantarse (to get up)**

- **Levantar**: *Voy a levantar la caja.* (I'm going to lift the box.)
- **Levantarse**: *Me levanto a las siete.* (I get up at seven.)

## **5. Llamar (to call) vs. Llamarse (to be called, to name oneself)**

- **Llamar**: *Voy a llamar a mi amigo.* (I'm going to call my friend.)
- **Llamarse**: *¿Cómo te llamas?* (What's your name?)

- **Caer**: *Las hojas caen en otoño.* (The leaves fall in the autumn.)
- **Caerse**: *Me caí en las escaleras.* (I fell down the stairs.)

## **7. Comer (to eat) vs. Comerse (to eat up, to finish eating)**

- **Comer**: *Comí una manzana.* (I ate an apple.)
- **Comerse**: *Me comí todo el pastel.* (I ate the whole cake.)

## **8. Ir (to go) vs. Irritarse (to get irritated)**

- **Ir**: *Voy a la tienda.* (I'm going to the store.)
- **Irritarse**: *Me irrité con su actitud.* (I got irritated with his/her attitude.)

## **9. Poner (to put) vs. Ponerse (to put on, to become)**

- **Poner**: *Voy a poner la mesa.* (I'm going to set the table.)
- **Ponerse**: *Me puse un abrigo porque hacía frío.* (I put on a coat because it was cold.)

## **10. Quedar (to remain, to stay) vs. Quedarse (to stay, to remain in a place)**

- **Quedar**: *Nos quedan tres días de vacaciones.* (We have three days of vacation left.)
- **Quedarse**: *Me quedé en casa todo el día.* (I stayed at home all day.)

## **11. Sentir (to feel) vs. Sentirse (to feel a state or emotion)**

- **Sentir**: *Siento una gran felicidad.* (I feel great happiness.)
- **Sentirse**: *Me siento cansado.* (I feel tired.)

## **12. Ver (to see) vs. Verse (to see oneself, to look like)**

- **Ver**: *Vi una película ayer.* (I watched a movie yesterday.)
- **Verse**: *Me veo en el espejo.* (I see myself in the mirror.)

## **13. Burlar (to mock, to deceive) vs. Burlarse (to mock, to make fun of)**

- **Burlar**: *El niño burló la seguridad.* (The boy deceived the security.)
- **Burlarse**: *Se burlaron de mí.* (They made fun of me.)

## **14. Encontrar (to find) vs. Encontrarse (to meet, to be situated)**

- **Encontrar**: *Encontré las llaves.* (I found the keys.)
- **Encontrarse**: *Me encontré con un amigo en la calle.* (I ran into a friend on the street.)

## **15. Mudar (to change, to move) vs. Mudarse (to move house)**

- **Mudar**: *Mudaré los muebles de lugar.* (I'll move the furniture.)
- **Mudarse**: *Nos vamos a mudar a otra ciudad.* (We're going to move to another city.)

- **Acostar**: *Voy a acostar a los niños.* (I'm going to put the kids to bed.)
- **Acostarse**: *Me acuesto a las diez.* (I go to bed at 10.)

## **17. Despertar (to wake up someone) vs. Despertarse (to wake up oneself)**

- **Despertar**: *Desperté a mi hermano a las siete.* (I woke up my brother at seven.)
- **Despertarse**: *Me desperté tarde.* (I woke up late.)

## **18. Cortar (to cut) vs. Cortarse (to cut oneself)**

- **Cortar**: *Corté el pan.* (I cut the bread.)
- **Cortarse**: *Me corté el dedo.* (I cut my finger.)

## **19. Morir (to die) vs. Morirse (to die of a particular cause)**

- **Morir**: *El animal murió ayer.* (The animal died yesterday.)
- **Morirse**: *Me muero de hambre.* (I'm dying of hunger.)
