#### **Lesson Summary: The Verb** *Ir* **in the Preterite Tense**

In this lesson, we explored the verb *ir* (to go) in the preterite tense, a critical aspect of Spanish for discussing past events. The focus was on conjugations, practical examples, and integrating new vocabulary and time expressions to enhance fluency.

## **Key Conjugations**

The preterite conjugations of *ir* were presented in the standard order, noting the inclusion of the *vosotros* form for those studying European Spanish. The forms are:

- Yo fui
- Tú fuiste
- Él/Ella/Usted fue
- Nosotros/Nosotras fuimos
- Vosotros/Vosotras fuisteis (Spain only)
- Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes fueron

Students were encouraged to repeat the conjugations until they became second nature.

# **Example Sentences**

The lesson included various sentences to illustrate how to use *ir* in the preterite tense:

- "¿Fuiste a la tienda?" (Did you go to the store?)
- "Sí, fui a la tienda." (Yes, I went to the store.)
- "No, no fui a la farmacia." (No, I didn't go to the pharmacy.)

## **Time Expressions**

A new time-related structure was introduced using *hace* (from the verb *hacer*):

- "Hace dos horas" (Two hours ago)
- "Hace dos días" (Two days ago)

- "Fui a la tienda hace dos horas." (I went to the store two hours ago.)
- "Ya fui a la tienda hace dos días." (I already went to the store two days ago.)

#### **Vocabulary Integration**

To enhance comprehension and retention, the lesson revisited key vocabulary and expressions:

- Days of the week: "Fui el lunes" (I went on Monday), "Fui el martes" (I went on Tuesday), etc.
- Time phrases: "Hace una semana" (One week ago), "Hace un año" (One year ago).

# **Practical Dialogue Practice**

Students practiced forming questions and answers:

- "¿Adónde fuiste?" (Where did you go?)
- "Fui a la playa." (I went to the beach.)
- "¿Cuándo fuiste al parque?" (When did you go to the park?)
- "Fui hace dos horas." (I went two hours ago.)

### **Usage of** *Ya*

The lesson introduced *ya* to indicate "already" in past contexts:

- "No, ya fui." (No, I already went.)
- "Ya fuiste a la tienda?" (You already went to the store?)

# **Encouragement to Practice**

The lesson concluded with advice to practice creating sentences using these patterns, such as narrating daily activities:

- "Fui al baño hace una hora." (I went to the bathroom an hour ago.)
- "Fui a la playa hace dos días." (I went to the beach two days ago.)

The goal was to empower students to internalize the conjugations and expressions, enabling them to communicate effectively about past events.
