# **Qroo Paul's Spanish Master Course Lesson 36 Exercise Skool.com/qroo**

### **Part 1: ¿Qué tal…? (How is/was…?)**

Translate the following questions into Spanish using *¿Qué tal…?*

- 1. How are the tacos?
- 2. How's the movie?
- 3. How was your trip?
- 4. How's the hotel?
- 5. How was the class?
- 6. How is the soup?
- 7. How was the concert?
- 8. How's your day going?
- 9. How was the dinner?
- 10. How's the coffee?

#### **Part 2: ¿Qué tal si…? (How about we…?)**

Translate the following suggestions into Spanish using *¿Qué tal si…?* Use the present tense.

- 11. How about we go to the beach?
- 12. How about we stay home today?
- 13. How about we watch a movie?
- 14. How about we eat tacos?
- 15. How about we practice Spanish?
- 16. How about we invite your friend?
- 17. How about we take a taxi?
- 18. How about we cook something?

- 19. How about we go to the store?
- 20. How about we cancel the reservation?
