# **Qroo Spanish Crew Conjugating Verbs in the Preterit Tense**

### **Introduction**

The preterit tense in Spanish is used to describe actions that are completed and occurred at a specific point in the past. This guide will explain how to conjugate regular verbs in the preterit tense, followed by irregular verbs grouped by similar patterns.

## **Regular Verbs**

Regular verbs in the preterit tense follow distinct patterns based on their infinitive endings: **-AR**, **-ER**, and **-IR**. Here are the conjugation endings for each type:

## **Conjugation Endings for Regular Verbs**

| Subject Pronoun                | -AR Ending | -ER Ending | -IR Ending |
|--------------------------------|------------|------------|------------|
| yo                             | -é         | -í         | -í         |
| tú                             | -aste      | -iste      | -iste      |
| él/ella/usted                  | -ó         | -ió        | -ió        |
| nosotros/nosotras              | -amos      | -imos      | -imos      |
| vosotros/vosotras (Spain only) | -asteis    | -isteis    | -isteis    |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes            | -aron      | -ieron     | -ieron     |

#### **Irregular Verbs**

Unlike regular verbs, irregular verbs in the preterit tense do not follow standard patterns. Below, these verbs are grouped by their irregularities.

## **Irregular Stem Verbs**

Many irregular verbs in the preterit have unique stems. The endings, however, remain consistent for all these verbs:

## Subject Pronoun Ending

| yo                  | -e      |
|---------------------|---------|
| tú                  | -iste   |
| él/ella/usted       | -o      |

#### **Verbs Ending in -car, -gar, and -zar**

These verbs have spelling changes in the **yo** form to maintain pronunciation:

- **-car:** The "c" changes to "qu" (e.g., buscar > yo busqué)
- **-gar:** The "g" changes to "gu" (e.g., pagar > yo pagué)
- **-zar:** The "z" changes to "c" (e.g., comenzar > yo comencé)

## **Irregular Verbs: Ser and Ir**

The verbs **ser** and **ir** share the same conjugation in the preterit:

Subject Pronoun Conjugation

| Subject Pronoun     | Conjugation |
|---------------------|-------------|
| yo                  | fui         |
| tú                  | fuiste      |
| él/ella/usted       | fue         |
| nosotros            | fuimos      |
| vosotros            | fuisteis    |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | fueron      |

#### **Stem-Changing -IR Verbs**

Stem-changing **-IR** verbs undergo a vowel change in the third person (singular and plural):

#### • **pedir (to ask for):**

#### **Summary**

This guide covers the formation of the preterit tense for both regular and irregular verbs. Regular verbs follow predictable patterns, while irregular verbs often require memorization. With practice, mastering the preterit tense will become much easier and allow you to express past events effectively.
