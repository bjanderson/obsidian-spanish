# **Qroo Paul's Spanish Master Course Lesson 10 Exercise Skool.com/qroo**

In this exercise, you'll practice using *este / ese / aquel* and their forms. You will be given the noun with its article to show the gender. Pay attention to whether it says *this*, *that*, or *that (over there)* to help you choose the correct form.

Remember:

*este / esta / estos / estas* = this / these

*ese / esa / esos / esas* = that / those

*aquel / aquella / aquellos / aquellas* = that / those (over there — more distant)
