#### **TION – CION Cognate Trick**

In this lesson, we explored the powerful TION – CION cognate trick. This trick involves taking English words that end in TION and changing the T to C while adding an accent over the last O. Examples include: creación, invitación, documentación, información, verificación.

This trick is highly effective, although not all words follow this pattern. For instance, "translation" becomes "traducción," not "translacion."

#### **Spelling Changes in Cognates**

**S-ES**: English words beginning with S followed by a consonant change by adding an E at the beginning: Speculation – especulación, station – estación, stipulation – estipulación, stigmatize – estigmatizar.

**Double letters** in English that don't change the sound of the word: Recommendation – recomendación, communication – comunicación, illumination – iluminación.

For words where double letters are important for pronunciation, they remain unchanged: Cooperation – cooperación.

**Double RR words** tend to stay the same and require you to make the trill: Narration – narración, irritation – irritación.

Some cognates may have spelling changes in the root of the word: Demonstration – demostración, immigration – inmigración, humiliation – humillación.

One more spelling change: TH becomes T: Authorization – autorización.

### **Gender of Nouns in Spanish**

Every noun in Spanish has a gender, either masculine or feminine. This assignment seems random for inanimate objects and must be learned with each new word. Masculine nouns often end in O: libro (book), carro (car), vino (wine), cuento (story), barco (boat). However, some do not: árbol (tree), mar (sea), sol (sun), dolor (pain).

Feminine nouns often end in A: casa (house), escuela (school), mesa (table), isla (island). Some exceptions include: lección (lesson), flor (flower), ciudad (city), imagen (image).

All nouns created with the TION – CION cognate trick are feminine, making it easier to know which definite and indefinite articles to use. For example, "option" becomes "opción": La opción (the option), las opciones (the options), una opción (an option), unas opciones (some options).

When pluralizing, add S after a vowel and ES after a consonant: La decoración, una decoración, las decoraciones, unas decoraciones.

#### **Definite and Indefinite Articles**

Masculine Nouns:

- **Definite Articles:** <sup>o</sup> Singular: el (the) ▪ Example: el libro (the book) <sup>o</sup> Plural: los (the) ▪Example: los libros (the books)
- **Indefinite Articles:** <sup>o</sup> Singular: un (a) ▪ Example: un libro (a book) <sup>o</sup> Plural: unos (some) ▪Example: unos libros (some books)

# **Making Verbs from Nouns**

Nouns created using the TION – CION trick can be transformed into verbs if they end in ACION by dropping CION and adding R:

Observation – observación – observar (to observe)

Recommendation – recomendación – recomendar (to recommend)

If the CION word does not end in ACION, it cannot be made into a verb: Opción, ficción, acción.

#### **Practice Sentences with Verbs**

Using the verb "querer" (to want), you can form sentences by adding the infinitive (unconjugated verb): Quiero + Infinitive (I want to do something)

Examples:

- 1. I want to memorize the information Quiero memorizar la información.
- 2. I want to participate Quiero participar.
- 3. I want to examine the documentation Quiero examinar la documentación.
- 4. I want to organize the information Quiero organizar la información.
- 5. I want to celebrate Quiero celebrar.
- 6. I want to certify the information Quiero certificar la información.
- 7. I want to cooperate Quiero cooperar.
- 8. I want to collaborate Quiero colaborar.
- 9. I want to confirm the description Quiero confirmar la descripción.
- 10. I want to continue the conversation Quiero continuar la conversación.
- 11. I want to prepare the documentation Quiero preparar la documentación.
- 12. I want to verify the information Quiero verificar la información.
- 13. I want to decorate the house Quiero decorar la casa.

Negative form using "no quiero" (I don't want): I don't want to speculate – No quiero especular. I don't want to decorate the house – No quiero decorar la casa.
