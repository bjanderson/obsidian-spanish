Languatalk (Transcript 04-25-2025):

1. \*\*In English: "The soup tastes good."\*\* - \*Tastes\* is a \*\*linking verb\*\*, because it connects the subject (\*the soup\*) with the predicate adjective (\*good\*), which describes the subject. \*Good\* is placed on the horizontal line as an attribute of the subject.

2. \*\*In Spanish: "La sopa sabe bien."\*\* - \*Sabe\* is a \*\*complete intransitive verb\*\*, because it does not connect the subject (\*la sopa\*) with an adjective. Instead, \*bien\* is an adverb that modifies the verb \*sabe\*. Therefore, \*bien\* is placed on a diagonal line under the verb.
