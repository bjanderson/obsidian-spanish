# **Qroo Paul's Spanish Master Course Lesson 47 Summary Skool.com/qroo**

In this lesson, Paul teaches six of the most common verbs that **trigger the subjunctive** when followed by a different subject in the sentence. These are verbs of **desire, influence, hope, command, or request.**

## **Subjunctive Trigger Pattern:**

![](_page_0_Picture_3.jpeg)

When there's a **subject change**, you must use:

**[Trigger verb in indicative] + que + [verb in the subjunctive]**

#### **Core Trigger Verbs:**

![](_page_0_Picture_7.jpeg)

- 1. **querer que** to want that
- 2. **esperar que** to hope that
- 3. **desear que** to wish that
- 4. **preferir que** to prefer that
- 5. **recomendar que** to recommend that
- 6. **insistir en que** to insist that (*note: always appears with "en"*)

*These are typically used in the first person: "quiero," "espero," etc.*

### **Subject Change Required**

Use **subjunctive** only when the subject of the second verb is different.

Examples:

- *Quiero que estudies español.* (I want **you** to study Spanish — 2 subjects → use subjunctive.)
- *Quiero estudiar español.* (I want **to study** Spanish — 1 subject → use infinitive.)

### **Example Constructions:**

| Trigger Verb    | English Translation                  | Subjunctive Use Example            |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Querer que      | I want you to study.                 | Quiero que estudies.               |
| Esperar que     | I hope you get better soon.          | Espero que te mejores pronto.      |
| Desear que      | I hope you have a good day.          | Deseo que tengas un buen día.      |
| Preferir que    | I prefer that you stay home.         | Prefiero que te quedes en casa.    |
| Insistir en que | I insist that you tell me the truth. | Insisto en que me digas la verdad. |

### **More examples**

- 1. Do you want me to clear the table? → *¿Quieres que limpie la mesa?*
- 2. Do you want me to go to the store? → *¿Quieres que vaya a la tienda?*
- 3. Do you want me to pay in cash? → *¿Quieres que pague en efectivo?*
- 4. Do you want me to wash the clothes? → *¿Quieres que lave la ropa?*
- 5. Do you want me to take you to the office? → *¿Quieres que te lleve a la oficina?*
- 6. Do you want me to bring you a blanket? → *¿Quieres que te traiga una cobija?*
- 7. Do you want me to help you set the table? → *¿Quieres que te ayude a poner la mesa?*
- 8. Do you want me to tell Dan we can't go to his party? → *¿Quieres que le diga a Dan que no podemos ir a su fiesta?*
