# **Qroo Spanish Crew Conjugating Verbs in the Present Subjunctive**

## **Introduction**

The present subjunctive in Spanish is formed by starting with the **yo** form of the present indicative, dropping the final **-o**, and then adding the subjunctive endings. These endings vary depending on whether the verb ends in **-AR**, **-ER**, or **-IR**. This guide focuses exclusively on the conjugation patterns for regular and irregular verbs.

## **Regular Verbs**

Regular verbs in the present subjunctive follow a predictable pattern once the base is established. Below are the endings for **-AR**, **-ER**, and **-IR** verbs.

# **Conjugation Endings for Regular Verbs**

| Subject Pronoun                | -AR Ending | -ER/-IR Ending |
|--------------------------------|------------|----------------|
| yo                             | -e         | -a             |
| tú                             | -es        | -as            |
| él/ella/usted                  | -e         | -a             |
| nosotros/nosotras              | -emos      | -amos          |
| vosotros/vosotras (Spain only) | -éis       | -áis           |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes            | -en        | -an            |

#### **Irregular Verbs**

Irregular verbs in the present subjunctive typically have irregular stems or forms that need to be memorized. These include verbs with stem changes, spelling changes, and completely irregular forms.

# **Verbs with Stem Changes**

Stem-changing verbs follow the same pattern as in the present indicative, except **-IR** verbs also change in the **nosotros** and **vosotros** forms.

## **Spelling Changes in -car, -gar, -zar Verbs**

Verbs ending in **-car**, **-gar**, and **-zar** undergo spelling changes to maintain pronunciation.

- **Buscar (to look for):** yo busque, tú busques, él/ella/usted busque, nosotros busquemos, vosotros busquéis, ellos/ellas/ustedes busquen
- **Pagar (to pay):** yo pague, tú pagues, él/ella/usted pague, nosotros paguemos, vosotros paguéis, ellos/ellas/ustedes paguen
- **Empezar (to start):** yo empiece, tú empieces, él/ella/usted empiece, nosotros empecemos, vosotros empecéis, ellos/ellas/ustedes empiecen

#### **Summary**

The present subjunctive involves consistent endings for regular verbs and some predictable changes for irregular verbs. It is essential to start from the **yo** form of the present indicative and adjust the endings as needed. With practice, the patterns will become familiar and manageable.
