# **Qroo Spanish Crew Conjugating Verbs in the Future Tense**

#### **Introduction**

The future tense in Spanish is used to describe actions that will happen. It is one of the simplest tenses to conjugate because both regular and irregular verbs use the same set of endings, and the endings are added directly to the infinitive form of the verb.

#### **Regular Verbs**

Regular verbs in the future tense follow a straightforward pattern. The endings are the same for **-AR**, **-ER**, and **-IR** verbs. These endings are added to the entire infinitive form of the verb.

## **Conjugation Endings for Regular Verbs**

| Subject Pronoun                | Ending |
|--------------------------------|--------|
| yo                             | é      |
| tú                             | ás     |
| él/ella/usted                  | á      |
| nosotros/nosotras              | emos   |
| vosotros/vosotras (Spain only) | éis    |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes            | án     |

### **Irregular Verbs**

Irregular verbs in the future tense do not use the infinitive as the base for conjugation. Instead, they have modified stems, but the endings remain the same as for regular verbs.
