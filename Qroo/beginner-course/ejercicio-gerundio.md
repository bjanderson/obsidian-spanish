### **Spanish Gerundio Practice Exercise**

### **Section 1: ESTAR + Gerundio (Present Progressive)**

**Verbs:** comer (to eat), estudiar (to study), ver (to watch), dormir (to sleep), escribir (to write)

- 1. I am eating.
- 2. She is studying.
- 3. We are watching a movie.
- 4. They are sleeping.
- 5. He is writing an email.

# **Section 2: Past Progressive (Imperfect or Preterite)**

**Verbs:** dormir (to sleep), leer (to read), hablar (to talk), trabajar (to work), cocinar (to cook)

- 6. I was sleeping when you arrived. *(background)*
- 7. She was reading all afternoon. *(set time)*
- 8. They were talking when I got there. *(background)*
- 9. We were working all day. *(set time)*
- 10. He was cooking when it started to rain. *(background)*

# **Section 3: LLEVAR or TENER + Gerundio (Time Doing Something)**

**Verbs:** vivir (to live), trabajar (to work), estudiar (to study), esperar (to wait)

- 11.María has been living in Spain for two years.
- 12. I've been working here for five months. 13.We've been studying Spanish for a long time.
- 14. He's been waiting for the bus for thirty minutes.

# **Section 4: SEGUIR + Gerundio (To keep doing something)**

**Verbs:** seguir, estudiar, llamar, preguntar, quejarse, trabajar

- 15. I keep studying every night.
- 16. She keeps calling me. 17.We keep asking the same thing.

- 18.Why do you keep complaining?
- 19. They keep working even when it's late.

### **Section 5: TERMINAR / ACABAR + Gerundio (To end up doing something)**

**Verbs:** comprar, llamar, comer, quedarse

- 20. I ended up buying the blue one. (referring to a dress, el vestido) 21.We ended up eating at home.
- 22. She ended up calling him.
- 23. They ended up staying all night.

## **Section 6: PASAR + Gerundio (Spending Time Doing Something)**

**Verbs:** estudiar, limpiar, caminar, hablar

- 24. I spend the whole day studying.
- 25. She spent the afternoon cleaning. 26.We spent the morning walking.
- 27. They spent the night talking.

## **Section 7: ANDAR + Gerundio (Going Around Doing Something)**

**Verbs:** decir, quejarse, buscar

- 28. He goes around telling lies.
- 29. She's always going around complaining.
- 30. I go around looking for my keys.

### **Section 8: Method or Means (How something is done)**

**Verbs:** cortar, aprender, mejorar, encontrar

- 31. I cut the bread using a sharp knife.
- 32. They learned by watching the experts.
- 33. She improved her Spanish by practicing every day.
- 34. I found it by looking under the table.

### **Verbs:** ver, bailar, correr, llorar, comprar

- 35. I saw her dancing. 36.We saw Juan running.
- 37. He saw my friend crying.
- 38. I saw my boss buying something.

### **Section 10: Simultaneous Actions (With Motion Verbs)**

**Verbs:** pensar, cantar, llorar, reír

- 39. I woke up thinking about the problem.
- 40. He left crying. *(Use: irse)* 41.We entered singing.
