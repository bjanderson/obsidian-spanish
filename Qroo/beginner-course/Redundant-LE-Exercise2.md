- 2. We told the truth to our friend. (decir)

## ANSWER KEY

- 1. I gave the book to Marta. (dar) → **Le di el libro a Marta.**
- 2. We told the truth to our friend. (decir) → **Le dijimos la verdad a nuestro amigo.**
- 3. He bought a new phone for his brother. (comprar) → **Le compró un teléfono nuevo a su hermano.**
- 4. I brought flowers to my parents. (traer) → **Les traje flores a nuestros padres.**
- 5. They sent a message to Juan. (enviar) → **Le enviaron un mensaje a Juan.**
- 6. I asked the teacher for help. (pedir) → **Le pedí ayuda al profesor.**
- 7. He stole Luis's wallet. (robar) → **Le robó la cartera (billetera) a Luis.**
- 8. I took the tablet away from the child. (quitar) → **Le quité la tableta al niño.**
- 9. We lent the car to our neighbors. (prestar) → **Les prestamos el carro a nuestros vecinos.** 10.Did you return the money to your cousin? (devolver) → **¿Le devolviste el dinero a tu primo?**
- 11. I told a story to my daughter. (contar) → **Le conté un cuento a mi hija.** 12.Juan brought me tacos. (traer) → **Juan me trajo tacos.**
- 13. They sent a letter to the company. (mandar) → **Le mandaron una carta a la empresa.** 14.Did you ask your mom for a favor? (pedir) → **¿Le pediste un favor a tu mamá?**
- 15. I lent my book to Andrea. (prestar) → **Le presté mi libro a Andrea.**

- 16. They stole Pedro's cell phone. (robar) → **Le robaron el celular a Pedro.**
- 17. I gave flowers to my grandma. (dar) → **Le di flores a mi abuela.** 18.Did you tell your boss the truth? (decir) → **¿Le dijiste la verdad a tu jefe?**
- 19. He returned the keys to his roommate. (devolver) → **Le devolvió las llaves a su compañero de cuarto.** 20.We bought gifts for the kids. (comprar) → **Les compramos regalos a los niños.**
- 21. I gave her the book. (dar) **→Le di el libro.**
- 22. We told him the truth. (decir) **→Le dijimos la verdad.**
- 23. She bought him a new phone. (comprar) **→Le compró un teléfono nuevo.**
- 24. They brought us flowers. (traer) **→Nos trajeron flores.**
- 25. They sent him a message. (enviar) **→Le enviaron un mensaje.**
