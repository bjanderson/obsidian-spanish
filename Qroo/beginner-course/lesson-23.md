#### **Part 1: Sentences 1-5**

**Instruction:** Write each sentence using the formula hace + time expression + verb in the preterite to say how long ago something happened.

# **Formula: Using hace + time expression + verb in the preterite**

- 1. I came here three years ago.
- 2. She started her job six months ago.
- 3. I met her two years ago.
- 4. He bought that car a long time ago.
- 5. He saw his friend a few days ago.

# **Part 2: Sentences 6-10**

**Instruction:** Use present tense + desde + a specific point in time to show when the action began.

# **Formula: Using present tense verb + desde + specific point in time**

- 6. We have been friends since high school.
- 7. Since when have you lived here?
- 8. I have lived here since 2020.
- 9. He has had that car since last summer.
- 10. She has worked at that school since January.

#### **Part 3: Sentences 11-15**

**Instruction:** Write each sentence using the formula present‑tense verb + desde hace + time expression to express how long the action has been happening.

# **Formula: Using present tense verb + desde hace + time expression**

- 11. I have lived here for three years.
- 12. She has worked at that school for six months.
- 13. We have studied Spanish for two hours.
- 14. He has had that car for a long time.
- 15. I have worked here for ten years.

## **Part 4. Sentences 16-20**

**Instruction:** Write each sentence using the formula hace + time expression + que + present tense verb to express how long something has been going on.

# **Formula: hace + time expression + que + present tense verb**

- 16. They have been friends for many years.
- 17. We have been studying for the exam for two hours.
- 18. She has been working at that school for six months.
- 19. My parents have lived here for three years.
- 20. I've been buying grapes at that store for a month.

# **Part 5: Sentences 21-25**

**Instruction:** Write each sentence using the formula llevar + time expression + gerund to say how long someone has been doing an activity.

# **Formula: Using llevar + time expression + gerund**

- 21. They have been talking all morning.

- 22. I have been studying Spanish for three years.
- 23. We have been waiting for the bus for twenty minutes.
- 24. She has been working at that school for six months.
- 25. They have been working on that project for years (**formula: tener + time expression + gerundio,** which you may hear in Mexico/Latin America).
