- 5. Can I make a reservation?
- 6. I want to work on Monday.

- 9. Do you want to collaborate with me?

- 14. I want to confirm the information. 15.Can you study Spanish with me today? 16.Can you help me study on Thursday? 17.Can I speak with you?
- 18. I can make a reservation. 19.Do you want to eat now?

- 20. I want to read a book. 21.Can you help me with my computer?
