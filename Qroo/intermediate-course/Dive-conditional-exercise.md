# **Section 1: Using "¿Podría...?" and "¿Podrías...?" for Politeness**

The conditional tense is often used to make polite requests, helping to soften commands or suggestions. Use "¿Podría...?" (formal) or "¿Podrías...?" (informal) based on the level of formality indicated.

- 1. Could you tell me the time, please? (formal)
- 2. Could you bring me a glass of water? (informal)
- 3. Could you help me with this project? (formal)
- 4. Could you show me where the restroom is? (informal)
- 5. Could you help us with our luggage? (formal)
- 6. Could you open the door for me? (informal)
- 7. Could you explain this exercise again? (formal)
- 8. Could you help my friend with her suitcase? (informal)
- 9. Could you tell us what time the train arrives? (formal) 10.Could you recommend a good restaurant? (informal)

# **Section 3: Expressing Actions Someone Would Do (or Would Have Done) in the Past**

The conditional tense can be used to talk about actions someone would do (or would have done) in the past, often in reported speech. Use the conditional tense to translate these sentences into Spanish.

- 1. She told me she would call me later.
- 2. He promised he would bring the documents.
- 3. They said they would help us with the work.
- 4. I thought we would arrive on time.
- 5. He mentioned he would clean the house today.

- 7. They said they would wait for us at the restaurant.
- 8. I told her I would visit her this weekend.

## **Section 4: Talking About Hypothetical Situations**

The conditional tense is used to discuss hypothetical situations, often paired with the imperfect subjunctive. Use the "si + imperfect subjunctive + conditional" structure to translate these sentences into Spanish.

- 1. If she had more time, she would travel more.
- 2. If we had a bigger house, we would invite more people.
- 3. If I knew how to cook, I would make dinner tonight.
- 4. If they lived closer, they would visit us more often.
- 5. If he had a car, he would drive to work.
- 6. If you (informal) studied more, you would pass the exam.
- 7. If I had enough money, I would buy that computer.
- 8. If she were the boss, she would change many things.
- 9. If you (formal) went to Spain, you would love the food.
- 10. If we could take a vacation, we would go to the beach.
