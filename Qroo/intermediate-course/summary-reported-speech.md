# **Qroo Spanish Crew Lesson Summary / Reported Speech**

#### **1. Direct vs. Indirect Speech**

**Direct speech**: a literal quote.

**Indirect speech**: a paraphrase of what was said.

Examples:

- Direct: María dijo: «Quiero ir a la playa.» *María said, "I want to go to the beach."*
- Indirect: María dijo que quería ir a la playa. *María said that she wanted to go to the beach.*

## **2. The Word "que" = "that" (when it fits)**

In English, we can often drop "that," but in Spanish you must include **que** whenever you could put "that" in English.

## Examples:

- María dice que quiere ir a la playa. *María says (that) she wants to go to the beach.*
- La casa que compré es verde. *The house (that) I bought is green.*
- La pizza que comí anoche estaba deliciosa. *The pizza (that) I ate last night was delicious.*

If you **cannot** naturally add "that" in English, then you don't use **que** in Spanish.

## **3. Reporting Verb in the Present**

If the reporting verb is in the present, there is no tense shift in the quote.

Examples:

- Direct: «Quiero ir a la playa.» *"I want to go to the beach."*

- Indirect: María dice que quiere ir a la playa. *María says that she wants to go to the beach.*

#### **4. Reporting Verb in the Past**

When the reporting verb is past, tense shifts occur. Here are the main patterns:

#### **a) Present → Imperfect**

- Direct: Juan dijo: «Estoy cansado.» *Juan said, "I'm tired."*
- Indirect: Juan dijo que estaba cansado. *Juan said that he was tired.*

## **b) Future → Conditional**

- Direct: María dijo: «Iré a la playa mañana.» *María said, "I will go to the beach tomorrow."*
- Indirect: María dijo que iría a la playa al día siguiente. *María said that she would go to the beach the next day.*

### **c) Preterite → Preterite / Past Perfect**

- Direct: Juan dijo: «Visité a mis padres ayer.» *Juan said, "I visited my parents yesterday."*
- Indirect (option 1): Juan dijo que visitó a sus padres ayer. *Juan said that he visited his parents yesterday.*
- Indirect (option 2): Juan dijo que había visitado a sus padres ayer. *Juan said that he had visited his parents yesterday.*

## **d) Present Perfect → Past Perfect**

- Direct: María dijo: «He terminado mi tarea.» *María said, "I have finished my homework."*

- Indirect: María dijo que había terminado su tarea. *María said that she had finished her homework.*

#### **e) Imperfect → Imperfect (no change)**

- Direct: Juan dijo: «Cuando era niño, jugaba en el parque todos los días.» *Juan said, "When I was a child, I used to play in the park every day."*
- Indirect: Juan dijo que cuando era niño, jugaba en el parque todos los días. *Juan said that when he was a child, he used to play in the park every day.*

### **f) Past Perfect → Past Perfect (no change)**

- Direct: María dijo: «Ya había estudiado el tema.» *María said, "I had already studied the topic."*
- Indirect: María dijo que ya había estudiado el tema. *María said that she had already studied the topic.*

## **g) Imperative → Imperfect Subjunctive**

Commands in direct speech become imperfect subjunctive in indirect speech.

- Direct: Juan dijo: «Cierra la puerta.» *Juan said, "Close the door."*
- Indirect: Juan dijo que cerrara la puerta. *Juan said to close the door.*
- Direct: María me dijo: «No abras la ventana.» *María told me, "Don't open the window."*
- Indirect: María me dijo que no abriera la ventana. *María told me not to open the window.*

## **5. Exceptions: Keeping the Present**

Even with a past reporting verb, sometimes the present tense is kept:

- If the statement is still true now.

- If repeating what was just said in conversation.

#### Examples:

- María dijo que vive en México. *María said that she lives in Mexico.*
- Él dijo: «Tengo hambre.» → Él dijo que tiene hambre. *He said, "I'm hungry." → He said that he is hungry.*

#### **6. Reported Questions**

## **a) With Question Words (keep accent marks)**

- Direct: María preguntó: «¿Cuándo llegó Juan a casa?» *María asked, "When did Juan get home?"*
- Indirect: María preguntó cuándo llegó Juan a casa. *María asked when Juan got home.*

#### **b) Yes/No Questions (use si, no accent)**

- Direct: Juan preguntó: «¿Quieres venir a la fiesta?» *Juan asked, "Do you want to come to the party?"*
- Indirect: Juan preguntó si quería ir a la fiesta. *Juan asked if I wanted to go to the party.*
