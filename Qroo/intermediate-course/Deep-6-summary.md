**Describing Unspecified Items or People** The subjunctive is used when describing people or things that are not specific or certain.

## **Examples:**

- *Quiero contratar al cantinero que sabe hacer 50 bebidas distintas.* (I want to hire the bartender who knows how to make 50 different drinks.)
- *Quiero contratar un cantinero que sepa preparar 50 bebidas distintas.* (I want to hire a bartender who knows how to make 50 different drinks.)
- *Quiero comprar la casa que tiene cuatro recámaras.* (I want to buy the house that has four bedrooms.)
- *Quiero comprar una casa que tenga cuatro recámaras.* (I want to buy a house that has four bedrooms.)

Another example:

- *Busco alguien que sepa pintar una casa.* (I'm looking for someone who knows how to paint a house.)

# **Verbs That Trigger the Subjunctive in This Context:**

- **Buscar** (to look for)
- **Contratar** (to hire)

- **Necesitar** (to need)
- **Querer** (to want)

**Usage Tip:** With certain verbs like *buscar* (to look for), *contratar* (to hire), and *querer* (when used to look for someone), the personal "a" may or may not be used. When in doubt, use the personal "a" before objects that are people or pets.

**Negation** The subjunctive is required in subordinate clauses after "que" when the main clause is negative.

**Pattern:** Negative Clause + "que" + Subjunctive

#### **Examples:**

- *Creo que Juan está en casa.* (I think Juan is at home.)
- *No creo que Juan esté en casa.* (I don't think Juan is at home.)

Additional examples:

- *Dudo que María quiera ir a la tienda.* (I doubt that Maria wants to go to the store.)
- *No parece que Linda quiera comprar el carro.* (It doesn't seem like Linda wants to buy the car.)

# **Verbs That Trigger the Subjunctive in This Context:**

- **No creer** (to not believe)
- **Dudar** (to doubt)
- **No parecer** (to not seem)
- **Negar** (to deny)

**Verbs of Influence, Command, or Desire** Verbs that express wanting, permitting, forbidding, ordering, persuading, and recommending trigger the subjunctive.

# **Key Examples:**

- *Quiero que vayas a la tienda.* (I want you to go to the store.)
- *Mi mujer deja que nuestros hijos vayan solos al parque.* (My wife lets our children go to the park alone.)

#### Additional examples:

- *Prefiero que te quedes en casa.* (I prefer you stay home.)
- *Mi madre insiste en que yo coma más verduras.* (My mother insists that I eat more vegetables.)
- *Quiero evitar que Juan se entere del incidente.* (I want to avoid that Juan finds out about the incident.)
- *Juan me dijo que fuera a la tienda.* (Juan told me to go to the store.)

#### **Verbs That Trigger the Subjunctive in This Context:**

- **Querer** (to want)
- **Dejar** (to let/allow)
- **Preferir** (to prefer)
- **Insistir en** (to insist on)
- **Evitar** (to avoid)
- **Mandar** (to order)
- **Pedir** (to ask/request)
- **Decir** (to tell, when giving a command)

**Emotional Reactions or Value Judgments** The subjunctive is used after emotional reactions or value judgments.

**Pattern:** Emotional Reaction/Value Judgment + "que" + Subjunctive

## **Key Examples:**

- *Me gusta que Juan siempre llegue temprano.* (I like that Juan always arrives early.)
- *Me entristece que no quieras pasar tiempo conmigo.* (It saddens me that you don't want to spend time with me.)
- *Me alegra que hayas aprendido mucho viendo mis videos.* (I'm happy that you have learned a lot watching my videos.)
- *Me pone triste que Julio y Juan no se lleven bien.* (It makes me sad that Julio and Juan do not get along.)

- *Me sorprende mucho que Julio se negara a venir a la fiesta.* (It surprises me a lot that Julio refused to come to the party.)

#### **Verbs That Trigger the Subjunctive in This Context:**

- **Gustar** (to like)
- **Entristecer** (to sadden)
- **Alegrar** (to make happy)
- **Alegrarse de** (to become happy)
- **Poner(se)** (to make someone feel a certain way/to become)
- **Sorprender** (to surprise)
- **Molestar** (to bother)
- **Temer** (to fear)
